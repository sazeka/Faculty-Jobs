Inclusion Criteria

Faculty Atlas targets U.S. postsecondary institutions using a policy-safe, transparent inclusion standard. Last reviewed: February 22, 2026.

Core Eligibility (Required)

  • Institution is in the United States.
  • Institution is postsecondary and classified as 2-year or 4-year.
  • Institution is degree-granting (associate level or higher).
  • Institution publishes faculty hiring information through a reachable public-facing jobs or faculty search page.

Institution Categories Tracked

  • Control: public and private nonprofit institutions are included.
  • Private for-profit institutions are excluded from coverage.
  • Mission/type overlays where available: doctoral, master's, baccalaureate, associate-focused.
  • Special-focus and mission-designated schools where applicable (for example, HBCU, HSI, tribal, religiously affiliated).

Operational Criteria

  • Faculty-relevant postings only (tenure-track, tenured, lecturer/instructor, postdoc, fellow, and related academic roles).
  • Non-faculty roles are excluded by title and content filters.
  • Private for-profit institutions are excluded by scope policy before scraping output is published.
  • Institutions are excluded when platform terms indicate automated extraction is disallowed.

Reference Universe

Target universe is based on federal postsecondary framing (IPEDS/NCES): U.S. 2-year and 4-year institutions, with a practical emphasis on degree-granting schools and policy-compliant data access.
