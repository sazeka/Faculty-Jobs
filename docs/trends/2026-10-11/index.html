Faculty Atlas is currently tracking 23,599 open faculty positions — +177 compared to last week. Among listings matched to institution type, 70.7% are at public institutions and 29.3% are at private nonprofit institutions. Adjunct roles represent the largest category with 8,953 listings. Among positions with a known appointment track, 23.2% are tenure-track and 76.8% are non-tenure-track. 1,004 listings (4.3%) explicitly reference artificial intelligence or a core AI method. 85 listings (0.4%) address responsible technology, such as AI ethics, algorithmic fairness, or technology policy. Nursing is the most in-demand discipline among classified listings this week, with 997 open positions.
