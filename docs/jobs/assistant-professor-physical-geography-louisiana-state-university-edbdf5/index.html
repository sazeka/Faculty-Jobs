We

We encourage those who participate in community-engaged or stakeholder-driven research and/or those who assist in advancing science communication and emergency management. Candidates should have a promising program of

research, publication, and extramural funding. Expectations are to teach and advise graduate and undergraduate students. The department offers BS, MS, and PhD degrees in Geography and BA, MA, and PhD degrees in Anthropology.
