Position summary

JOB DESCRIPTION: Pediatric Nephrology Clinician Educator (Open Rank) Opportunity: The Department of Pediatrics at the University of Texas Medical Branch (UTMB) John Sealy School of Medicine in Galveston is seeking a full-time Pediatric Nephrology Clinician Educator at the rank of Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor. This is a unique opportunity to join a nationally recognized academic health system and a

collaborative team of clinicians dedicated to advancing care for children. UTMB is building a world-class kidney program, anchored by a robust research and clinical enterprise in adult nephrology. While pediatric nephrology is currently a smaller division, this position offers exciting opportunities to collaborate with leading kidney experts and contribute to the growth of pediatric kidney care. This position offers

the opportunity to work in a multidisciplinary environment, contribute to the education of pediatric residents and medical students, and engage in meaningful clinical care across UTMB’s Galveston Island and Clear Lake campuses. Primary clinical responsibilities include providing comprehensive services for children with renal diseases in both outpatient and inpatient settings. The position also involves active

participation in medical education, including teaching and mentoring pediatric residents and medical students. Living and Working in South Houston and Galveston, Texas: South Houston and Galveston offer a unique combination of urban convenience, coastal living, and rich history just a short drive from Houston. South Houston is a diverse, industrial city with affordable housing and close proximity to major highways,

making it ideal for professionals seeking easy access to Houston’s medical and business centers. Galveston, a vibrant coastal city, is known for its beautiful beaches, historic architecture, and thriving tourism and healthcare sectors. Residents enjoy a relaxed lifestyle with plenty of recreational opportunities, including boating, fishing, festivals, and waterfront dining. Learn more about Galveston by visiting this

link: Things to do in Galveston. Both areas provide affordable living options, strong community ties, and access to quality healthcare facilities, making them attractive places for healthcare professionals looking to build a career and enjoy a balanced lifestyle near the Gulf Coast.
