Position summary

DEPARTMENT of ENGINEERING Instructor in Electrical and Electronics Engineering POSITION: The College of Science, Engineering, and Technology invites applications for an instructor position in Electrical and Electronics Engineering RESPONSIBILITIES: The Department of Engineering is accepting applications for full-time instructor to be hired for the Spring 2027 semester. The successful candidate will teach

undergraduate courses and laboratories in Electrical and Optical Engineering. The maximum teaching load is four courses per semester. Responsibilities include, but are not limited to, teaching in-person courses, participating in data collection for student learning and program evaluation, and participating in department faculty and staff meetings. QUALIFICATIONS: Minimum qualifications include a master of science or

higher degree in Electrical Engineering, or a related discipline. Candidates should have prior experience teaching undergraduate coursework, and/or supporting Electrical Engineering laboratory activities. Candidates are encouraged to demonstrate strong communication skill through their application materials. Preferred qualifications include an ability to teach undergraduate-level Electrical Engineering courses in

systems science, including control systems, communications systems, and signals and systems, embedded systems, or microcontrollers. Prior experience administering online instruction is also preferred. SALARY/RANK: Instructor rank with salary commensurate with experience. EFFECTIVE DATE: The position start date is January 10, 2027. NSU Teaching Philosophy for Underrepresented Populations: Norfolk State University is

particularly interested in candidates who have experience working with students from diverse backgrounds and a demonstrated commitment to improving access to higher education for all students, regardless of background. Candidates must include a statement that reflects their philosophy and experience working with initiatives or with students and communities from underrepresented populations. EEO STATEMENT: NSU is

committed to providing equal employment opportunities for all persons and applicants, without regard to age, color, disability, gender, national origin, political affiliation, genetic information, race, religion, sexual orientation, sex (including pregnancy) or veteran status. NSU encourages and invites minorities, women, individuals with disabilities, and veterans to apply. About Engineering at Norfolk State
