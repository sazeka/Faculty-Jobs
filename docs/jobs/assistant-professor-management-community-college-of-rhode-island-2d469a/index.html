Academic Affairs · Business, Science, Tech and Math · Liston Campus (Providence) Assistant Professor, Management JOB SUMMARY:The successful candidate will teach business courses within the Business Administration management concentrations. In addition to providing high-quality instruction, the successful candidate will contribute to the continued growth and success of the department and College through curriculum

development, assessment, accreditation activities, program review, student success initiatives, and other departmental and institutional priorities. Successful candidates will have a desire and demonstrated ability to work with undergraduate students from diverse cultures, disciplines, and academic backgrounds.Commitment to CCRI’s Mission:Demonstrate a commitment to the philosophy and mission of a comprehensive

community college.Work collaboratively with others in a diverse and inclusive environment.Work collaboratively in a diverse, inclusive and student-centered environment, students of various learning styles, cultures, identities, and life-experiences.Apply strong communication, organizational, and interpersonal skills, and a collaborative approach to working with students and colleagues.Instructional Skills:Teach

courses required to earn an Associate Degree in Business Administration with a major concentration in Management (i.e., courses with BUSN prefixes).Use innovative instructional strategies that support student success and recognize diverse learning styles.Contribute to a dynamic teaching/learning environment requiring adaptability, creativity, and teamwork.Course Management:Use technology to enhance instruction,

complete administrative tasks, and communicate effectively with the students and the campus community.Create assessments that evaluate student learning outcomes.Curriculum Development:Use a student-focused approach to create meaningful educational experiences.Work collaboratively to develop curriculum in Management, with suggested areas including, but not limited to, Banking, Insurance, Healthcare Administration,
