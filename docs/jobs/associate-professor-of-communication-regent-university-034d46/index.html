Associate Professor of Communication Apply now Job no: 50000562 Work type: Full time Location: Virginia Beach Categories: Faculty & Adjunct, School of Communications & the Arts Regent University’s School of Communication and the Arts invites applications for a full-time, tenure-track Associate Professor of Communication in the Department of Journalism and Communication Studies. Tenure at appointment may be available

for candidates who qualify. This faculty member will teach across the curriculum, with a special focus on graduate communication studies courses, and will coordinate the PhD program in partnership with the department chair and dean. The successful candidate will hold a PhD in communication or a related field by the start date and maintain an active research program engaged with current conversations in the

discipline, whether social-scientific, computational, qualitative, or critical in method. Candidates must bring an established record of peer-reviewed scholarship and evidence of successful direction of doctoral and master’s students. Areas of current interest include artificial intelligence and human-machine communication, misinformation and epistemic trust, family communication, health communication, and religion

and media, though we welcome strong scholars working in any active area of the field. Dissertation/thesis supervision and program direction of the PhD are part of this appointment’s assigned load rather than an addition to it. Workload is set with the department chair and dean to reflect the mix of teaching and doctoral supervision the faculty member carries. Regent University is one of the nation’s leading academic

centers for Christian thought and action, offering over 150 associate, bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral degrees and enrolling more than 13,000 students worldwide. Regent offers high-quality, in-demand, and affordable degree programs while remaining committed to its foundational mission to educate Christian leaders to change the world. The salary range for this position is $80,000 to $89,000 annually, posted in good

faith based on the budgeted amount available for the position. Salary within this range is commensurate with academic experience, a record of scholarship adjudicated in juried forums, and demonstrated capacity to teach and serve across the curriculum. Work Location: Virginia Beach, VA Expectations include 1) a dedication to Christ-centered teaching and learning; 2) an active program of scholarship in the discipline;
