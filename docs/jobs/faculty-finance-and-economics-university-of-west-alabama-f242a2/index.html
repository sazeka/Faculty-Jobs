University of West Alabama: Academic Affairs: College of Business: Department of Accounting, Finance, and Economics Location University of West Alabama Open Date May 05, 2025 Description The University of West Alabama College of Business is seeking to hire a full-time faculty member in Finance and Economics (open rank, tenure-track, full-time, 9-month) to teach finance and economics courses. Responsibilities: Teach

undergraduate and graduate finance and economics courses on-campus and online. Fulfill other duties expected of a faculty member, such as student advising, committee assignments, appropriate public and professional service, research, professional development, and college recruitment and retention activities. Perform other duties as assigned by the Department Chairperson and/or Dean. Qualifications Essential: A PhD or

DBA in Finance and/or Economics from an accredited institution. Consideration will be given to ABD candidates. A minimum of 18 hours of graduate course work in Finance and Economics. Ability to work effectively with administration, faculty, staff, and students. Desired: Successful college teaching experience. Experience and willingness to teach both on-campus and online courses. Before hiring, the final candidate(s)

must successfully pass a pre-employment background investigation, including information obtained from social media and other internet sources. Application Instructions To Apply: When you select "Apply Now," you will be prompted to provide the following: Cover Letter. A current resume or CV. Transcripts of all college work. Unofficial transcripts will suffice for application. Official transcripts must be submitted

immediately upon employment. Email and phone number of three professional references. Reference letters may be requested later.
