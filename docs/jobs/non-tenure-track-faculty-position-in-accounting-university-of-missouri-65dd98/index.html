Previous JobJob TitleNon-Tenure-Track Faculty Position in Accounting Next JobApply for JobJob ID60791 LocationColumbia Full/Part TimeFull-Time Add to My Favorite JobsEmail this Job Hiring Department The School of Accountancy in the Trulaske College of Business at the University of Missouri, Columbia Job Description The School of Accountancy in the Trulaske College of Business at the University of Missouri invites

applications for a full-time, non-tenure-track (NTT) faculty position beginning no later than Fall 2027. The appointment is expected to be made at the rank of Assistant Teaching Professor, although exceptionally qualified candidates may be considered for appointment at a higher teaching rank. Qualifications Applicants must possess a graduate degree in accounting or a closely related field and be willing to teach four

course sections per semester. Professional accounting experience and/or certification are preferred. Candidates with a doctoral degree in accounting or a closely related field are also encouraged to apply.Evaluation Criteria:Candidates will be evaluated on the following dimensions:• Teaching: Demonstrated ability or strong potential to provide high-quality instruction at the undergraduate and graduate levels across

one or more areas of accounting. The School has teaching needs in areas including financial accounting and managerial and cost accounting, but candidates with expertise in any area of accounting are encouraged to apply. The ability to incorporate data analytics, emerging technologies, and artificial intelligence into courses will also be considered.• Student Engagement: Commitment to student success through advising,

mentoring, experiential learning, student organizations, or other activities that enhance the student experience. • Service: Ability and willingness to contribute to curriculum development, committee service, and outreach that supports the School of Accountancy, the College, and the accounting profession. • Professional Experience: Relevant professional accounting experience and professional certifications.

Anticipated Hiring Range Compensation will be competitive and commensurate with experience. This position is eligible for University benefits. As part of your total compensation, the University offers a comprehensive benefits package, including medical, dental and vision plans, retirement, and educational fee discounts for all four UM System campuses.
