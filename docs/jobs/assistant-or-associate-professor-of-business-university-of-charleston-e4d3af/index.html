Assistant or Associate Professor of Business Posted September 24, 2026Assistant or Associate Professor of Business Entrepreneurship Program Director School of Business, University of Charleston Position Summary: The University of Charleston invites applications for a faculty position in Business and Entrepreneurship at the rank of Assistant or Associate Professor. This position also serves as Program Director for the

Entrepreneurship major and is responsible for providing academic leadership, curriculum oversight, assessment coordination, student recruitment and retention initiatives, and engagement with the entrepreneurial and business communities. The faculty member will teach undergraduate courses in Management, Entrepreneurship, and related areas, as well as selected courses within the School of Business core curriculum. The

successful candidate will bring expertise that supports the broader management curriculum as well as entrepreneurship education more specifically. The University is teaching-focused and student-centered. The School of Business serves undergraduate students across multiple majors, including Accounting, Business Analytics, Digital Marketing, Entrepreneurship, Financial Planning, Management, Marketing, Professional

Selling, Sport Business, Sport Analytics, and Sport Media. Our students come from diverse backgrounds and are primarily full-time and residential. The School is accredited by the Accreditation Council for Business Schools & Programs (ACBSP). The position reports to the Dean of the School of Business.
