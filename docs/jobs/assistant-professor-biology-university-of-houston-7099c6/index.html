Assistant Professor, Biology 497312 Full-Time Biological Sciences Department: Biological & Environmental Sciences Salary: Commensurate with Experience/Education Description: The University of Houston-Clear Lake (UHCL) invites applications for the position of Assistant Professor of Biology, effective August 2027. UHCL is a comprehensive university with over 8,000 students and a diverse population of traditional and

non-traditional students. UHCL is in southeast Houston, the fourth largest city adjacent to NASA Johnson Space Center and within driving distance of the Texas Medical Center and UTMB Medical Center in Galveston. The region is one of the most diverse in the United States, home to vibrant communities such as the historic East End and Third Ward. Note: Applications received by January 15, 2027 will receive full

consideration. Duties: Teaching courses at the undergraduate and graduate level to support the biology program related to the candidate’s field of study. Teaching load of 6 courses per semester (3 fall and 3 spring) with the opportunity to teach courses in the summer term. Workload will be divided among teaching (60%), research (20%) and service (20%). Qualifications Required: PhD in biology or a closely related

field with concentration in whole organism biology Preferred: Experience teaching organismal biology Required Attachments by Candidate: 1) Curriculum Vitae, 2) a Letter of Application that addresses the required and preferred qualifications, and highlights teaching philosophy and experience, 3) unofficial graduate transcripts, 4) copies of educator certifications, and 5) three letters of referenceNote: Please combine

all supporting documents into one file for attachment to your application. Official transcripts will be requested for the finalists who are invited for on-campus interviews, and a background check will be required. This position is not eligible for H-1B sponsorship. For any questions on this posting, please contact FacultyAffairs@uhcl.edu. All positions at the University of Houston-System are security sensitive and

will require a criminal history check. The University of Houston System and its universities are Equal Opportunity Institutions. Everyone is encouraged to apply.
