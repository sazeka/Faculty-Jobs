Assistant Professor Physician Assistant Assistant Professor, Physician Assistant Fort Wayne, Indiana Summary The position of PA Faculty requires an individual who can provide excellence in teaching, with interest in both their students and their subject matter. Faculty must be committed to the centrality of a Trine University education and possess the ability to support the program in achieving excellence through

teaching, scholarship, outreach, and engagement. Under the direction of the MPAS Program Director, program faculty will be responsible for creating and implementing program curriculum and will participate in the ongoing ARC-PA accreditation process. PA Faculty will provide service to the university, community and the PA profession, while fully supporting Trine's mission of preparing students to succeed, lead, and

serve. and competencies required for entry into the profession Evaluating student performance assess and supervise the student's progress in achieving competencies and in fulfilling course goals, learning outcomes, and instructional objectives Academic counseling of students providing both academic and professionalism mentoring Student Success Committee activities assuring the availability of remedial instruction, Assisting
