Florida Transportation Institute (UFTI). We welcome expertise in any area of multi-modal transportation research including but not limited to planning, operations, safety, systems analysis, emerging technologies (e.g., connected / autonomous vehicles, AI, digital twins, edge computing), and economics. The UFTI vision statement is “To lead the profession in shaping a better transportation future by functioning as a

preeminent center of multidisciplinary transportation research, student’s top choice for transportation education, and a provider of state-of-the-art analysis and decision-support tools.” UFTI is home to auxiliary programs McTrans, Technology Transfer (T2), and I-STREET, each of which plays a distinctive but complementary role within the transportation profession. McTrans develops and supports industry-leading

software for highway capacity design and traffic simulations. T2 provides training and safety program support to industry throughout Florida and beyond, while I-STREET is a living lab that leverages advanced technologies to improve mobility and safety and serves as a resource for facilitating deployment for the entire state. The UFTI director will strengthen Florida’s national leadership by shaping how university-led

research is conducted and managed.
