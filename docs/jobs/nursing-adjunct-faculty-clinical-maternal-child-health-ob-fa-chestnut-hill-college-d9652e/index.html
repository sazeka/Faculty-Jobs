Nursing Adjunct Faculty, Clinical Maternal Child Health (OB Fall 2026) Nursing Adjunct Faculty, Clinical Maternal Child Health (OB Fall 2026) Chestnut Hill College – School of Nursing Accelerated Second Degree Program in Nursing The Chestnut Hill College Department of Nursing invites applications for Adjunct Faculty positions for the Fall 2026 semester within its new Accelerated Second Degree Program in Nursing. We

seek dynamic educators committed to excellence in teaching and student success. Adjunct Nursing Faculty will deliver high-quality clinical instruction in both the direct care (clinical setting) and participate in clinical simulation scenarios. Faculty will engage students in active learning, and contribute to the academic success of second-degree nursing students. About Chestnut Hill College Founded by the Sisters of

Saint Joseph in 1924, Chestnut Hill College is an independent, Catholic institution rooted in a strong liberal arts tradition that fosters equality through holistic education. Distinguished by its strong mission, collegial environment, and dedication to personalized instruction through a small faculty-to-student ratio, the College promotes the spiritual, academic, social, ethical, and moral development of the whole

person. Located in the charming Chestnut Hill neighborhood of Northwest Philadelphia, it serves a culturally diverse student body. Qualifications Master’s or doctoral degree in Nursing or a related field Unencumbered license to practice as a Registered Nurse in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania Demonstrated experience in classroom teaching and course management Experience teaching adult or accelerated learners

preferred Strong communication, organizational, and interpersonal skills Familiarity with Canvas (recommended) Demonstrated understanding of and commitment to the College’s Mission and Core Values, including a shared dedication to inclusive, mission-driven education that supports the development of the whole person.
