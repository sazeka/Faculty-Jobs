Assistant or Associate Professor/Director of Medical Laboratory Sciences 533325 Anchorage, Alaska, United States Faculty Full-time On Campus UNAC Grade 30 UAA College of Health The Program Director position is a tenure track assistant professor position in the Medical Laboratory Science department. In this leadership role, you will have a balanced workload of teaching MLS courses along with administrative duties,

student advising and opportunities for research. The NAACLS accredited MLS program at UAA is essential for Alaska’s healthcare. We are looking for a dynamic leader, passionate about laboratory medicine with a strong desire to provide innovative, industry level, research-based education towards the continued development and growth of Alaska’s clinical laboratory scientists. A great candidate will have exceptional

laboratory skills supported by an advanced degree in clinical laboratory science and research. A leader, this position requires excellent communication skills with students, faculty, and clinical partners, maintaining a strong network within the laboratory community of Alaska. Organized with experience in assessment towards curriculum development and understanding of NAACLS accreditation. Minimum Qualifications:
