SB2298 (1990) Appointment Type Faculty - Tenure-Track Full Time/Part Time Full Time Start Date 08/12/2027 End Date Position Description In collaboration with the Department Chair, the College-Wide Tutoring Faculty Coordinator provides leadership and oversight for CCSF’s Student Tutoring and Resource Centers (STAR Centers) across all District locations, community partner sites, and online platforms. This position

ensures that credit and noncredit students have access to high-quality academic support that advances their success. Responsibilities include supervising STAR Center operations, overseeing the peer tutoring program, instructing Learning Assistance courses, managing program budgets, fostering campus-wide collaboration, and coordinating program assessment and continuous improvement. Job Duties This is an in-person
