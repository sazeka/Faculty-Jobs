Seward County Community College  ·  Liberal, KS
Instructor

Position summary

Seward County Community College is a two-year institution offering 45 programs of study, including Adult Basic Education, certificate programs and Associate of Arts, Science, General Studies and Applied Sciences degrees. Ranked in the top 5% of more than 1,100 accredited community colleges in the U.S. by the Aspen Institute, SCCC serves a student body of 2,500+ students ranging in age from 15 to 85, in a county with

the highest foreign-born percentage population in the U.S., and a rural service area of about 50,997 in seven Southwest Kansas counties as well as neighboring counties in southeastern Colorado, the Oklahoma and Texas Panhandles. Responsibilities Responsibilities include preparation, teaching, and conducting courses as assigned including but not limited to laboratory courses and clinicals supervision. Duties may

include but not limited to develop syllabi; facilitate student learning in classroom and lab settings, recruit, advise, tutor, and evaluate students. Teaching assignments may include some evenings and occasional travel. Seward County Community College is a comprehensive institution which places primary emphasis on excellence in classroom instruction and is currently seeking a highly motivated and knowledgeable

individual to instruct in the Allied Health Division. Classes will include but are not limited to: Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) and Medical Terminology. Qualifications Minimum Required: Must be a Registered Nurse with a minimum of three (3) years experience in long-term care/clinical experience required. Must meet requirements as determined by the Kansas Department of Aging and Disability Services. Current

Kansas Register Nursing License and BLS certification. Preferred: Bachelor’s degree or Master’s Degree in Nursing or subfield. Previous teaching instruction in community college or secondary education preferred. Salary/Benefits Competitive salary. Participation in college Flexible Benefits program which includes options for single and family health and dental coverage, optional insurances, and reimbursement accounts.

Other benefits include: income protection insurance; matching annuity; SCCC tuition reimbursement for employee and dependents; annual and sick leave allowances and participation in social security (FICA) and state retirement programs (KPERS).
