Skills Location Bloomington Position Summary The Kelley School of Business at Indiana University Bloomington (IUB) invites applications for full-time, open-rank Lecturer positions in Communication Skills beginning Fall 2027. These are 10-month, non-tenure-track appointments with an 18-credit-hour annual teaching assignment.Communication Skills faculty teach core undergraduate courses in business writing and business

presentations. The curriculum emphasizes audience, purpose, rhetorical situation, and the effective use of written and oral genres in professional contexts. Faculty typically have a primary assignment in either Business Writing or Business Presentations, with the possibility of teaching both. Communication Skills Courses Business Presentations (3 credits) Business Presentations introduces students to oral

communication in professional contexts through theory-based skill development. Students learn to analyze audiences, construct and deliver persuasive messages, develop oral arguments, and work effectively in teams. The course includes a team persuasive presentation addressing a real problem for a corporate client. Business Writing (3 credits) Business Writing develops students’ ability to produce strategic, ethical,

audience-centered professional documents individually and in teams. Students analyze communication situations, make rhetorical choices across business genres, and develop recommendations for authentic professional problems.
