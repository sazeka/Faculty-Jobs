Job Opening: Assistant/Associate Professor in Healthcare Management and Leadership Location: Montreat, North Carolina (residential undergraduate) Start Date: August 2027 Summary The School of Arts and Sciences (SAS) at Montreat College seeks a full-time faculty member in Healthcare Management and Leadership to serve in our Christ-centered traditional residential undergraduate liberal arts setting, with plans for

future graduate studies in the program. The successful candidate will be an outstanding, creative, entrepreneurial teacher and scholar with a heart for student success. The College desires an individual who can help further shape and collaboratively lead a developing program that integrates business acumen, health policies and practices broadly understood, and basic biology/medical science, with a variety of

professional applications. Interdisciplinary ability to operate cooperatively in the context of the College’s strengths and forward momentum around data and information security in the healthcare industry is also expected. This appointment is in the Faculty of Technology and Human Industry, Studies in Business, with teaching and service in Business/Technology and Natural Science (Biology), plus administrative load

(details under “Duties” below). Qualifications: The successful candidate will affirm and support the Mission, Vision, Statement of Faith, and Community Life Covenant of Montreat College. The candidate will also demonstrate support for Christian liberal arts education across the arts and sciences and the specific ability to integrate faith with the disciplines in a compelling, thoughtful manner. Teaching excellence

and a willingness to serve the broader college community through service and committee work are also expected. Additional specific qualifications and expectations include: Earned terminal degree, preferably in either business or a health-related natural science field; at least one graduate degree in business or equivalent, and one or more degrees or professional experience in the healthcare industry, with

professional credentials and ability to teach basic undergraduate biology. The College’s local network includes a number of experienced healthcare professionals and leaders that can contribute to delivery of the program.
