Job Description SummaryLincoln Land Community College is seeking qualified individuals to provide instruction and planning for adult education courses.Instructors will be responsible for the following: Teach assigned adult education course(s) (Adult Basic Education, Adult Secondary Education (High School Equivalency preparation), English as a Second Language, and bridge/career pathway courses. Bridge /pathway courses

include adult education activities and workforce preparation skills in conjunction with workforce training contextualized to various industry sectors such as healthcare, IT, hospitality, automotive, etc. Plan and facilitate explicit teaching activities for whole group and small group lessons. Establish and maintain a classroom climate that is conducive to learning. Utilize ICCB approved curriculum and adhere to the

Illinois content standards. Maintain and submit attendance and testing records in a timely and accurate manner. Administer standardized assessments as required. Assist students in defining, implementing and accomplishing educational, training and career goals. Complete 6 hours of New Teacher Orientation upon hire. Complete at least 12 hours of appropriate and approved professional development per year. Complete ICCB

Standards Proficiency training after 1 year of employment. Attend meetings and conferences. Participate in evaluation process. Work schedule may require teaching day and/or evening classes. Pay will be $33/hr. SURS is a state funded pension program specified for university and community college employees. SURS requires 8% of the employee's pay to be deducted on a pre-tax basis and contributed to the system.
