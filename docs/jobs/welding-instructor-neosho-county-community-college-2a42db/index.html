Welding Instructor

Neosho County Community College  ·  Chanute, KS
InstructorWelding

Position summary

WELDING INSTRUCTOR - OTTAWA Classification: Faculty Pay Status: Instructor Scale Fringe Benefits per Negotiated Agreement Starting Salary: Instructor Scale Purpose of Position The Ottawa Welding Instructor reports to the Dean of Outreach and Workforce Development and is responsible for providing effective welding instruction resulting in student success in weld qualifications and achievement of work-ready welding

skills. Essential Functions • Provide premiere quality service to all constituencies of the College. • Engage in continuous quality improvement and professional development. • Teach welding courses as scheduled. • Complete reporting requirements. • Participate in recruitment efforts. • Comply with the negotiated agreement and college policies and procedures. • Other duties assigned by the Dean of Outreach and

Workforce Development. Required Knowledge, Skills and Abilities • Welding qualifications. • Excellent organization and management skills. • Excellent interpersonal skills. • Excellent computer skills. • Effective communication, both orally and written Education and Experience • Practical welding experience required (minimum 4,000 hours). • Certified Welding Inspector (CWI) strongly preferred. • Other education and

experience will be considered. • Valid driver’s license required, with the ability to be insured by the institution’s insurance carriers. Working Conditions • Normal office, classroom, and welding laboratory working environment. Ability to sit, stand, bend, and utilize welding equipment for the majority of the workday. • Specific vision abilities required by this job include close vision and the ability to adjust

focus. • Physical effort required. Occasionally lift and transport items during the workday weighing 10-20 pounds is required. • Ability to move throughout the building and between buildings that may be at different locations. • Ability to use hands/fingers repetitively to operate, activate, or use office and welding machinery. • Ability to lawfully operate and/or drive a motor vehicle during the course of employment

is required. Created July 2026 Application: Please submit a letter of application, resume with names and phones numbers of five references unofficial transcripts, and employment application to: Human Resources Director, Welding Instructor – Ottawa, Neosho County Community College, 800 West 14th Street, Chanute, KS 66720 or Email to hr@neosho.edu. Employment application available at www.neosho.edu/careers. Background
