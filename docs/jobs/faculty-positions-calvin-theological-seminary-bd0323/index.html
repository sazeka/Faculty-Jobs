Academic Services Coordinator Calvin Seminary has an open position for a full-time Academic Services Coordinator serving in the Academic Office. The person in this position will provide administrative support for the Dean of the Faculty and the Dean of Academic Programs and Registrar, the directors of academic

programs, Doctoral programs and faculty. Additionally, this position provides customer service to students in all programs. This position is a year-round full time on-site position with flexibility for some hours of remote work. To apply, please send a cover letter and resume to semhr@calvinseminary.edu.
