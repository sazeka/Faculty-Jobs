The work will involve applying state-of-the-art

machine learning approaches to classify organisms despite cluttered imagery, marine snow, suspended particles, and subtle morphological differences among taxa. conferences and journals Contribute to the intellectual development of students and junior researchers Required Qualifications PhD in Computer Science or Electrical Engineering received within the last 3 years.
