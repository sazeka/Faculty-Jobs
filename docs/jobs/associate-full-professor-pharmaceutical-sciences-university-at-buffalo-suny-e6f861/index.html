Faculty Bargaining Unit Posting Detail Information Fiscal Year 2025-2026 Position Summary The University at Buffalo (UB) School of Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences (SPPS) is seeking a tenure track faculty member at the associate or full professor level to expand the UB Drug Discovery, Development, and Evaluation Hub (DDDE). in drug discovery/development as well as the development of a translational therapeutic core to support clinical pharmacology, policy development and evaluative clinical sciences.
