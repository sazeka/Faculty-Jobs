Summary:The University of Illinois Springfield (UIS) invites applications for the Founding Director of its planned School of Applied Engineering. This is a unique opportunity for an entrepreneurial academic leader to build a new School focused on preparing students for engineering and technology careers through an innovative, work-based learning model.The School will launch with a Bachelor of Applied Science in

Engineering Technology that integrates academic instruction with meaningful workplace experience. Through partnerships with employers and community colleges, the School aims to create accessible educational pathways that connect students, industry, and the region’s evolving workforce needs.The Founding Director will play a central role in shaping the School’s academic identity, building its faculty and partnerships,

and establishing the systems needed for long-term success. The Director will lead curriculum implementation, faculty recruitment, student pathway development, industry engagement, and planning for ABET accreditation.We seek a collaborative, forward-thinking leader who combines academic credibility with practical engineering experience and a commitment to educational innovation. The successful candidate will be

comfortable building new initiatives, working across institutional boundaries, and translating an ambitious vision into a sustainable academic enterprise.This is a full-time, on-campus tenure-system appointment at the rank of Associate or Full Professor, with the potential for a tenure waiver subject to university policies and approval. The position carries a nine-month academic appointment with an administrative

stipend for year-round leadership responsibilities.Visa sponsorship is not available for this position.Duties & Responsibilities:Strategic Leadership and School DevelopmentProvide the vision, leadership, and direction needed to establish and grow the School of Applied Engineering.Develop and implement a strategic plan that supports academic quality, enrollment growth, financial sustainability, and industry
