Number of Positions In Requisition 1&#xa;&#xa; Job Details The Division of Biostatistics & Bioinformatics, Department of Pharmacology, Physiology, and Cancer Biology at Thomas Jefferson University in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, invites applications for a full-time faculty position in biostatistics at the Assistant Professor level. The mission of the Division of Biostatistics & Bioinformatics is to further the

advancement of biomedical and population health science through collaboration and support of research, to promote understanding of study design, data analysis, and statistical literacy among researchers and trainees, and to develop applied statistical and computational methods and tools for biomedical and population health research. Desired areas of research focus include, but are not limited to, electronic health

record (EHR) and/or administrative health big data analyses, causal inference, clinical trials, analysis of sequencing and ‘omics data, survival analysis, and biomarker studies. Job Description We are seeking a colleague with excellent written and verbal communication skills, motivation to collaborate with investigators, and strong interest in applying or developing statistical methods to solve problems in

observational and experimental biomedical research. Candidates must have a doctoral degree in biostatistics or statistics. Experience with collaborative biomedical projects is preferred. The University provides substantial continual support of salary to encourage independent and collaborative research, contributions to extramural grant applications, and some teaching. The Division is committed to diversity and

applications from women and minority candidates are encouraged. Interested candidates should apply online or submit 1) their curriculum vitae, 2) a cover letter that describes their interest in and qualifications for the position, and 3) the names and contact information for three references to: scott.keith@jefferson.edu Scott W.
