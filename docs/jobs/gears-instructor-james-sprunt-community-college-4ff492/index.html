GEARS Instructor Print GEARS Instructor Salary Depends on Qualifications Location 133 James Sprunt Drive, Kenansville, NC Job Type Full Time Permanent Job Number COS-CE-CCR-3 Division Continuing Education Department College and Career Readiness Opening Date 08/31/2026 Closing Date 9/14/2026 5:00 PM Eastern Description Benefits SUMMARY The GEARS Instructor (Giving Everyone a Role in Society) provides instruction,

coaching, and support to adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities to enhance their independence, employability, and community participation. The instructor delivers curriculum in workplace readiness, career exploration, social and communication skills, functional academics, technology, self-determination, and independent living skills. Responsibilities include instructional planning, student progress

monitoring, documentation and recordkeeping, employer and community engagement, and collaboration with college staff and external support agencies. The position is committed to helping students achieve their educational, personal, and employment goals through inclusive and individualized learning experiences. The GEARS Instructor reports directly to the Director of College and Career Readiness. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND

RESPONSIBILITIES The following duties are representative of the responsibilities assigned to this position and are not intended to be all-inclusive. Employees may be assigned additional duties as required.
