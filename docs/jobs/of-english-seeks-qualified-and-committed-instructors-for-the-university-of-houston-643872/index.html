Adjunct, English 496319 Part-Time Closing on: Jun 7 2027 English Department: English Salary: Commensurate with Experience/Education Description: The University of Houston-Downtown (UHD) Department of English seeks qualified and committed instructors for the position of Adjunct Faculty in our First-Year Composition (FYC) or Technical Communication (TC) programs. Depending on experience and departmental needs, hired

instructors will teach one of the following schedules: Two or more in-person sections of ENG 1301: Composition I and/or ENG 1302: Composition II throughout a 16-week fall/spring semester Two or more in-person or online sections of TCOM 3302: Business and Technical Writing throughout a 16-week fall/spring semester Adjunct faculty are eligible for benefits if they teach three or more 16-week sections in a semester.

About University of Houston Downtown The second-largest university in Houston, the University of Houston-Downtown (UHD) is a comprehensive, four-year, urban university in the heart of downtown. UHD is a community of diverse faculty, staff, students, and regional partners dedicated to nurturing talent, generating knowledge, and driving socioeconomic mobility for a just and sustainable future. UHD is recognized as a

Hispanic-Serving Institution and a Minority-Serving Institution. While the student population is largely Hispanic and African American, more than 64 countries are represented. Furthermore, UHD has the most affordable tuition among four-year universities in Houston, and 70% of its first-year students are the first in their families to attend college.
