Position summary

Rockhurst University's School of Education invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Education position. This is an 11-month, tenure-track position with a start date of January 4, 2027 . Responsibilities include teaching doctoral courses and supervising doctoral students&rsquo; research projects as well as supporting undergraduate and graduate-level teacher candidates. Applicants must have

demonstrated an active research agenda. A specialization in areas pertaining to Special Education or Teacher Education is preferred. The School of Education supports undergraduate educator preparation programs, graduate certificates, Master of Education programs, and EdD in Education and Leadership programs. Graduate programs are delivered using online modalities and the Ed.D. in Education and Leadership is an

online, cohort-based program with four concentration areas: K-12 Education, Higher Education, Healthcare Education, and Organizational Leadership. This Jesuit - inspired Ed.D. program provides a distinctive approach to developing educational leaders and health care educators prepared to face the challenges of todays accelerated rate of change. The faculty teaching load will include courses such as Foundations of

Special Education, Research Methodology I and II, and Research Capstone I and II. The tenure-track faculty member will engage in scholarship activities; provide service to the university, department, program, and community; and advise and mentor students.
