Job Purpose: The Department of School Psychology within Gonzaga University’s School of Education is seeking a part-time instructor to teach a course in the Cognitive Bases of Behavior (3 credits) for its Psy.D. program in School Psychology during the Fall 2026 semester. This course exposes

students to both classic and recent empirical research in various areas of cognition and discuss the underlying theories that account for those empirical findings. The areas of cognition that are discussed include perception, attention, mental imagery, memory, problem solving, reasoning and decision making, and language.
