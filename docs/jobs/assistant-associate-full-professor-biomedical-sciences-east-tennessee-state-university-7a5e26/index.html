Assistant/Associate/Full Professor - Biomedical Sciences TN, United States and 1 more Job Description The Department of Biomedical Sciences (DBMS) at the Quillen College of Medicine, East Tennessee State University (ETSU), invites applications for multiple full-time, tenure-track, open-rank faculty positions. We encourage applications from individuals with research expertise in one or more of the following areas of

major strength within the department:CancerCardiovascular/renalImmunology/infectious diseaseNeurosciencePromising candidates in other areas of biomedical research will also be considered. Applicants with extramural funding through the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) or who are eligible to apply for VA funding are encouraged to apply. Consideration will also be given to cluster or group hiring in the areas of

departmental strength. Applicants are expected to engage in basic and translational research, sustain independent research funding, publish in high quality peer-reviewed journals, and participate in teaching of medical and/or graduate students. Administrative duties may include service on departmental, college and university committees. This is an excellent opportunity as the university offers state-funded positions,

competitive starting salary, ample laboratory space, multiple core facilities, opportunity to mentor B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. students, and a collaborative environment. Our collegial and collaborative environment also extends to the clinical departments and other colleges at ETSU. Our department’s faculty have a strong history of extramural funding from the NIH, AHA, and the U.S. Departments of VA and Defense. Johnson
