KVCC Heat Pump Installation Instructor

Kennebec Valley Community College  ·  Fairfield, ME
InstructorHVAC

Position summary

Kennebec Valley Community College (KVCC) Workforce Training & Professional Development is seeking an experienced HVAC professional to provide instruction for the College's Heat Pump Installer Training program and related EPA Section 608 Refrigerant Certification training. The instructor will provide classroom and hands-on laboratory instruction designed to prepare participants to safely and professionally install

ductless mini-split heat pump systems. The instructor will also facilitate EPA Section 608 refrigerant certification review sessions and serve as an approved proctor for EPA 608 certification examinations. This is a part-time/contract instructional position with courses offered periodically throughout the year based on enrollment and workforce training needs. KVCC is an Efficiency Maine Registered Trainer for Heat

Pump Installer Training. The program combines classroom instruction with hands-on laboratory experience to prepare participants to professionally install heat pump indoor units, outdoor units, and line sets. Students completing the Heat Pump Installer Training and the separately offered EPA Section 608 Refrigerant Certification training gain skills and credentials applicable to employment with Maine HVAC and heat

pump installation contractors. Responsibilities: Heat Pump Installer Training Deliver KVCC's 40-hour Heat Pump Installer Training using established curriculum and instructional materials. Provide a combination of classroom instruction, demonstrations, and hands-on laboratory activities. Teach proper installation of ductless mini-split heat pump systems, including indoor and outdoor units, line sets, electrical

connections, condensate management, evacuation, commissioning, and system start-up. Provide instruction in heat pump operation, refrigeration cycle fundamentals, basic electrical theory, troubleshooting, safe work practices, and proper use of HVAC tools and equipment. Provide instruction related to current refrigerant requirements and practices, including Low GWP/A2L refrigerants. Demonstrate proper installation

techniques and supervise students as they complete hands-on installation and troubleshooting activities. Reinforce applicable manufacturer specifications, codes, industry standards, and workplace safety practices. Maintain a safe, organized, and professional learning environment. Evaluate student participation, skill development, and successful completion of required training activities. EPA Section 608 Refrigerant
