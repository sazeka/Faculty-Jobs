Assistant/Associate Professor of Microbiology

Corban University  ·  Salem, OR
Assistant ProfessorAssociate ProfessorMicrobiology

Position summary

MISSION: To educate Christians who will make a difference in the world for Jesus Christ. SUMMARY: The Department of Math and Science seeks a full-time faculty member with expertise in microbiology to support growing biology, kinesiology, agriculture, and nursing programs. The successful candidate will have primary responsibility for teaching microbiology lecture and laboratory courses and will contribute to the

broader science curriculum based on departmental needs and the candidate's areas of expertise. ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS: Teach undergraduate microbiology lecture and laboratory courses. Develop and maintain high-quality laboratory and classroom experiences in microbiology. Teach additional courses within the Math and Science Department based on departmental needs and the candidate's areas of expertise. Develop course

schedules, syllabi, and instructional materials. Identify and meet the learning needs of students. Develop and administer formative and summative assessments of student learning. Advise and mentor undergraduate students. Support undergraduate research and/or other experiential learning opportunities as appropriate. Participate in departmental and university service and contribute to the continued growth and

development of Math and Science programs. Perform biblical integration in alignment with Corban University's Statement of Faith. Work closely and maintain consistent communication with the department chair to ensure instructional alignment with Math and Science Department outcomes. Perform other duties as assigned. KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES: Strong knowledge of microbiology and contemporary microbiological

laboratory methods. Ability to effectively teach microbiology at the undergraduate level in both lecture and laboratory settings. Ability to teach across multiple areas of the undergraduate science curriculum. Commitment to excellent undergraduate teaching, advising, and mentoring. Ability to engage students with a range of academic backgrounds and career goals. Ability to integrate Christian faith and a biblical

worldview thoughtfully within the sciences. Strong communication, organizational, and interpersonal skills. Ability to collaborate effectively with faculty across Biology, Chemistry, Kinesiology, Agriculture, Nursing, and related disciplines.
