Assistant Professor of Computer Science

Muskingum University  ·  New Concord, OH
Assistant ProfessorComputer Science

Position summary

Assistant Professor of Computer Science Muskingum University invites applications for an Assistant Professor of Computer Science, beginning August 2027. The successful candidate will join Muskingum’s Computer Science program and contribute to teaching across the undergraduate computer science curriculum. We are particularly interested in candidates with expertise in cybersecurity, computer networks, systems, or

closely related areas. Anticipated teaching responsibilities may include courses in cybersecurity, computer networks, operating systems, programming, and other undergraduate computer science courses based on program needs and the successful candidate’s areas of expertise. The successful candidate will also contribute to the continued development of Muskingum’s computer science curriculum, including opportunities to

strengthen offerings in cybersecurity and networking. Additional opportunities may include supporting interdisciplinary programs and initiatives in areas such as artificial intelligence and data analytics, mentoring student projects and internships, and developing relationships with employers and other external partners. Faculty members at Muskingum are expected to demonstrate a strong commitment to undergraduate

teaching and student learning; advise and mentor students; maintain an appropriate program of scholarly and professional activity; and contribute to departmental and university service. A doctorate in computer science or a comparable field is preferred. Candidates with a master’s degree in computer science or a comparable field will also be considered. Candidates should demonstrate the ability to teach undergraduate

computer science courses and an interest in working closely with students in a small university environment. Professional or academic experience in cybersecurity, computer networking, systems, or related areas is particularly desirable. HOW TO APPLYApplicants should submit a cover letter, CV, statements of teaching philosophy, unofficial graduate transcripts, and a list of three professional references with contact

information and relationship to the applicant to careers@muskingum.edu.Questions may be directed to Dr. Richard Taylor, Chair of the Computer Science Department at richardt@muskingum.edu.Review of applications will begin on December 14, 2026 and will continue until the position is filled.
