Position Description The Department of World Languages and Cultures at Spelman College seeks a dynamic scholar-teacher for the Fall 2027 academic year specializing in Global Francophone Studies, whose research engages the French-speaking world through transnational, comparative, or interdisciplinary perspectives. We are particularly interested in candidates whose scholarship expands the intellectual and

geographic reach of French and Francophone Studies and fosters connections across disciplines.The successful candidate will teach French language courses at multiple levels and advanced courses in French and/or English aligned with their research expertise. The ability to develop interdisciplinary courses and innovative curricula, including French for the Professions, community-engaged learning, or digital

humanities, is desirable.Areas of specialization may include, but are not limited to, Francophone Africa and the Caribbean; African and African diasporic studies; Black European studies; migration and diaspora; colonialism, postcolonialism, and decoloniality; race and ethnicity; gender and sexuality; environmental humanities; visual and digital cultures; and media and performance studies.
