maintain an active and productive program of research in physical education, physical education pedagogy, sport pedagogy, physical education teacher education, or a related area, resulting in high-quality scholarly publications and contributions to the field. The faculty member will teach undergraduate and graduate courses, mentor students, and contribute to the continued excellence of the Department’s academic
