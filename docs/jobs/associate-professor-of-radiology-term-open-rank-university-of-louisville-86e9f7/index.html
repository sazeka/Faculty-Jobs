Department: Diagnostic Radiology Location: Health Sciences Center Time Type: Full time Worker Type: Regular Term (Fixed Term) Job Req ID: R108085 Position Description: The University of Louisville, Department of Radiology, Vascular Interventional Section, is seeking a full-time academic Interventional radiologist. The position is for an ABR certified/eligible Interventional Radiologist at the proposed rank Assistant

or Associate Professor. Job responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Clinical Duties and Training of Diagnostic Radiology Residents: We are a growing and busy practice. Our division performs the entire spectrum of IR procedures with a strong emphasis on oncologic, transplant, and trauma interventions. We cover three hospitals, including a Level One Trauma Center and a hospital that performs solid organ

transplants. We are also affiliated with the Brown Cancer Center. Call cases are limited to emergent cases overnight (e.g. traumatic pelvic bleeds) and urgent/emergent interventions over the weekend. We have 27 residents with at least 2 residents rotating in IR each month, as well as an IR Fellow. We also have two PAs to assist with daily responsibilities. Procedural Expertise : Vascular and interventional procedures

include but are not limited to angiography, angioplasty and stent placements, oncologic interventions, GI/GU interventions, kyphoplasties, TIPS, CT-guided and US-guided biopsies and drainages, lumbar punctures, pain interventions and central venous access. Conference Participation: Multidisciplinary tumor boards and trauma conferences, resident case and core didactic lecture conferences; medical school lectures.

Required Qualifications: Medical Degree or equivalent, eligibility for Kentucky Medical License, and ABR certified or eligible. Required Application Materials.
