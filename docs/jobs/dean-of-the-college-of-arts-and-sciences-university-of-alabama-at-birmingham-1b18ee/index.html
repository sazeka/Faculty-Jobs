Posting Details Position Information School/College Office of the Provost Title Dean of the College of Arts and Sciences Assignment Category Full-Time Rank Professor Tenure Status Tenured Payroll Status Faculty 12 Job Description The University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB, or the University) seeks an accomplished, collaborative, and forward-looking dean to lead the College of Arts and

Sciences (CAS, or the College). The College of Arts and Sciences encompasses the arts, humanities, social and behavioral sciences, natural sciences, and mathematics across 19 departments and 10 interdisciplinary programs, including five joint programs with the Heersink School of Medicine. As the largest academic unit at UAB, the College offers more than 41 baccalaureate, master’s, and doctoral degrees, as well as a

wide range of certificates and minors. The College of Arts and Sciences is home to approximately 6,000 students, 307 faculty members, and 157 staff. The Dean of the College of Arts and Sciences serves as its chief academic and executive officer, reporting to the University’s Senior Vice President for Academic Affairs and Provost. The next Dean will unite the College’s departments, programs, and perspectives behind a

compelling shared vision that celebrates their individual strengths while fostering a strong collective identity and purpose. This individual will build strong teams, support departmental leaders, promote interdisciplinary engagement, and serve as a persuasive advocate for the liberal arts and sciences within a university widely recognized for excellence in health care, medicine, and research. Working closely with

faculty, staff, students, alumni, University leadership, and community partners, the Dean will advance the priorities of the College’s Strategic Plan 2024–2028 and UAB’s broader Forging Ahead Strategic Plan.
