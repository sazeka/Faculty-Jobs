Position summary

Welding Technology Instructor ASU Mid-South, a comprehensive community college located just minutes from downtown Memphis, Tennessee, seeks a progressive, student-centered individual to provide instruction in its Welding Technology program to train individuals to fill the growing demand for skilled welding professionals. ASU Mid-South’s Welding Technology program offers students an opportunity to earn four separate

certificates of proficiency in four welding processes, a certificate of proficiency in fabrication/fitting, and an Associate of Applied Science Degree that will prepare them to seek rapid entry to employment. This is a full-time, 12-month position with full college benefits. Qualifications: Candidate must hold an associate’s degree in welding technology or related field and at least 5 years of related industry

experience. Candidates without an associate’s degree may be considered, but must agree to obtain an appropriate credential within a mutually agreed upon timeframe. Consideration will be given to candidates with greater experience and relevant industry-recognized certifications (i.e. AWS or NCCER certifications). Candidate must have the ability to read/analyze and interpret blueprints and schematics related to the

profession. Previous use of Canvas or another Learning Management System (LMS) is a plus. Preference will be given to candidates with prior teaching or industry training experience. A compelling combination of educational background, work experience, and professional credentials may be considered. Other qualifications include excellent interpersonal, written and communication skills; a working knowledge of Microsoft

applications; and evidence of ongoing commitment to professional development. Responsibilities: Reports to the Dean for Advanced Manufacturing and Transportation and has responsibility for curriculum, instruction, lab oversight, and industry alignment of the Welding Technology program. Teaching schedule may include evening or weekend hours. Teach courses for the Welding Technology program during the fall, spring, and

summer semesters. Deliver instruction to secondary and postsecondary students that aligns with approved ASU Mid-South syllabi and academic standards, supporting student attainment of course- and program-level learning outcomes.
