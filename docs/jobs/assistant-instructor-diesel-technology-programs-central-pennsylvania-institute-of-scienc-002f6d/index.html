Prior instructional experience helpful. Send letter of interest, resume,

and 3 professional references to Executive Secretary, 540 N. Harrison Road, Pleasant Gap, PA 16823. Information can also be emailed to tbrickley@cpi.edu. Clearances are required from successful applicant. Posting is open until position is filled. EOE.
