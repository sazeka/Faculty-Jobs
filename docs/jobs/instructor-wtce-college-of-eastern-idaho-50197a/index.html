Instructor, WTCE Print Instructor, WTCE Salary $20.00 - $40.00 Hourly Location 1600 S 25th E, Idaho Falls, ID Job Type Part Time Job Number 1905203 Department Workforce Training Non-Credit Opening Date 07/01/2026 Closing Date Continuous FLSA Non-Exempt Description Benefits Job Summary Workforce Training & Continuing Education (WTCE) Instructors are generally considered Subject Matter Experts (SME) in their particular

field of expertise. WTCE instructors are required to provide high quality instruction for predominantly adult learners. Instructor work hours are based upon an as-needed schedule and are determined by program requirements, but will not exceed part-time hourly employment. Instructors are expected to keep their course material relevant to their assigned subject matter and maintain standard classroom protocols and

procedures. Instructors will coordinate with WTCE staff to develop course content and schedule classes according to instructor availability and regional need. At times, additional technical expertise or certification/licensure may be required to teach particular courses. Instructors will adhere to State and Federal guidelines for proper procedures, practices, and operations when required by subject matter. Typical

Job Functions Provides technical support and/or instruction for assigned WTCE courses related to candidate's particular field of expertise.May be contracted to develop training material (course outlines, lesson plans, hands-on activities, etc.) according to regional needs and educational learning outcomes. Provides community education and/or professional development instruction and/or contracted training as experts

in their field.Regularly updates and maintains instructional material.Tracks and records student outcomes such as grades and attendance in WTCE management systems as needed per course. Performs other job-related duties as assigned.
