to serve as the next Head of the Department of Sustainable Earth Systems Sciences (SESS). The successful candidate will be a highly accomplished scholar and academic leader who will strengthen SESS’s mission of advancing the understanding of Earth and planetary sciences as interconnected, dynamic systems to inform sustainability, resilience, and stewardship of the planet. The research specialty is open, although

preference will be given to candidates with expertise that can complement the department’s existing strengths. Reporting to the Dean, the Department Head provides academic, administrative, financial, operational, and strategic leadership for the department; partners with the Dean and university leaders to grow the department’s research and educational impact; and helps develop a vibrant, people-centered academic

culture that supports the success and professional development of faculty, staff, and students. The Department Head will play a key role in shaping the department’s next phase of growth and enhancing its impact in sustainable Earth and environmental science disciplines. They will develop, with input from stakeholders, consensus around a strategic vision and plan for the department that aligns with the strategic

priorities of NSM and of UT Dallas. Likewise, the new Head will build connections to attract opportunities and resources from across the university, supporting a creative research environment, particularly in support of the development of our large cohort of early career faculty.
