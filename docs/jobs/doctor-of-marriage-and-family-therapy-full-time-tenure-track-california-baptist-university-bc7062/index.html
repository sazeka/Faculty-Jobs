Doctor of Marriage and Family Therapy: Full-Time Tenure-Track Faculty Position Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Details Position Information Position Title Doctor of Marriage and Family Therapy: Full-Time Tenure-Track Faculty Position Position Summary The College of Behavioral and Social Sciences at California Baptist University invites applications for a full-time, 12-month,

tenure-track position in the DMFT Program at any rank. The primary duties include teaching 24 units for the academic year, research/scholarship, and service to the community. The anticipated start date is August 2026. Pay Range The compensation for this appointment will range from $95,000 – $110,000 annually and reflects what California Baptist University reasonably expects to pay for this faculty appointment. Actual

compensation may vary based on the qualifications and experience of the applicant, as well as market conditions. In addition to wages, CBU offers a robust suite of employee benefits. Qualifications The successful candidate should possess a Doctoral degree in the field of Marriage and Family Therapy, have a background in marriage and family therapy and have an active license (e.g., LMFT) in the state of California.

Teaching Responsibilities Duties include teaching in the Doctor of Marriage and Family Therapy program. Candidates are expected to be actively engaged in research and/or scholarship and serve with purpose and distinction within the community. A strong interest in and commitment to the integration of faith and learning is required. Preference will be given to applicants with a history of quality teaching, notable

scholarship, and demonstrated relational skills. This position will remain open until filled. Nondiscrimination Statement State and Federal law permit California Baptist University to discriminate on the basis of religion in order to fulfill its purpose.
