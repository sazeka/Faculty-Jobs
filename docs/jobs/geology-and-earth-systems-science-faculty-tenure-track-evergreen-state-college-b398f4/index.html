Geology and Earth Systems Science Faculty (Tenure Track) Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Position Details Position Information Position Title Geology and Earth Systems Science Faculty (Tenure Track) Job Summary We seek a broadly trained Earth scientist committed to field-based undergraduate education. The successful candidate will engage students directly with the landscapes, watersheds, and

geologic history of the Pacific Northwest through local and extended field experiences. Areas of expertise may include geology, hydrology, geomorphology, climate science, Earth surface processes, environmental geochemistry, or related fields. Evergreen’s location provides exceptional opportunities for field-based teaching and research, with access to volcanic landscapes, mountain watersheds, glacial landforms, river

systems, coastlines, estuaries, and diverse ecosystems. The successful candidate will teach introductory and advanced Earth systems science through interdisciplinary programs that emphasize observation, field methods, quantitative analysis, and scientific inquiry. They will help students understand the interactions among Earth’s physical processes, climate, water, landscapes, ecosystems, and human activities,

including how these systems respond to natural variability and climate change. The successful candidate will lead field experiences ranging from local day trips to extended excursions throughout the Pacific Northwest and engage undergraduate students in original research through field, laboratory, and community-based projects that foster scientific inquiry and collaborative scholarship. Committed to interdisciplinary

education, The Evergreen State College values faculty who teach across traditional disciplinary boundaries. This position offers opportunities to collaborate with colleagues in environmental studies, ecology, oceanography, arts, humanities, and other fields to create innovative undergraduate programs. Experience with GIS, spatial analysis, remote sensing, quantitative methods, or related analytical approaches is

desirable. Preference will be given to candidates who demonstrate a strong commitment to undergraduate education, field-based learning, and inclusive teaching practices.Regular is equivalent to tenure-track at other colleges.
