Home Position: Assistant Professor of Biology Location: Yankton, SD Job Id: 336 # of Openings: 1 Overview: Mount Marty University, a Catholic, Benedictine liberal arts institution sponsored by the Sisters of Sacred Heart Monastery, invites applications for a full-time, in-person, tenure-track position in Biology. Located in Yankton, South Dakota, Mount Marty seeks a broadly trained biologist who will serve as the

primary faculty member for teaching human anatomy and physiology. Appointment is anticipated at the rank of Assistant Professor, though candidates with substantial prior academic experience may be considered for appointment at a higher rank. The successful candidate will demonstrate a primary commitment to undergraduate teaching, the ability to teach across the biology curriculum, and sustained professional or

scholarly activity that enriches classroom instruction. MMU’s biology professors serve biology majors, students preparing for pre-professional careers and students in the university’s nursing program. The department also contributes substantially to the General Education program, so the successful candidate will teach students at a wide range of preparation levels and career intentions. Mount Marty welcomes

applications from candidates early in their academic careers. New faculty are supported by the university’s Teaching and Learning Center, which provides mentoring, instructional design assistance, and ongoing development for colleagues new to the classroom. Review of applications begins immediately. Position is open until filled. Key Responsibilities: Teach undergraduate courses in human anatomy and physiology,

including the accompanying laboratory sections with an Anatomage table, as the primary teaching assignment for this position. Teach additional courses across the biology curriculum as departmental need requires, with microbiology and genetics as the most likely assignments. Contribute to the General Education program. Provide academic advising. Work collaboratively with colleagues in Biology and across the university

to support mission integration in the curriculum. Contribute actively to the intellectual and communal life of the university through departmental and institutional service. Qualifications: Education: An earned doctorate in Biology or a closely related field is preferred by the time of appointment. Candidates who are ABD will be considered provided all doctoral requirements are completed prior to the fall of 2027.
