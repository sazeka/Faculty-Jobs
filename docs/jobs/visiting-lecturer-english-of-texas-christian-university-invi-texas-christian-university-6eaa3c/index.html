Position Details: This full-time faculty position is on-campus and in-person. The English Department of Texas Christian University invites applicants for full-time Visiting Lecturer positions for on-campus, in-person teaching. This is one-year,

full-time, non-renewable position. The teaching load is 4 courses per semester, primarily in composition, but also possibly in literature, creative writing, or any combination of the three. We invite applicants to demonstrate in their application materials how they might contribute to the department's core mission of providing a socially just literacy education.A successful applicant will have at minimum a PhD in

Composition and Rhetoric, English, or Creative Writing or a related field or an MFA in Creative Writing or will have completed all requirements for the PhD except the final defense from a regionally accredited institution at the start of employment. Teaching experience in one or more of the indicated fields is required; applicants are also requested to demonstrate commitment to and excellence in undergraduate

instruction. Department Details: Students and faculty in the Department of English at TCU are committed to a variety of intellectual and creative pursuits fostered by the exploration of literature, rhetoric and writing. The department includes a cohort of rhetoric and composition faculty, and both tenure-stream and non-tenure-stream faculty who work together to schedule courses, organize curriculum, and serve on

various committees. School/College Description: Named for TCU’s founders, Addison and Randolph Clark, the AddRan College of Liberal Arts is home to the core academic disciplines of the humanities and social sciences. The College includes approximately 155 full-time faculty members, 13 departments (Comparative Race & Ethnic Studies, Criminology & Criminal Justice, Economics, English, Geography, History, Modern

Language Studies, Philosophy, Political Science, Religion, Sociology & Anthropology, Spanish and Hispanic Studies, and Women & Gender Studies), and two units of Reserve Officers’ Training Corps (Army and Air Force).
