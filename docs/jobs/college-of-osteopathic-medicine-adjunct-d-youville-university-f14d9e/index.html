Share: College of Osteopathic Medicine - Adjunct Buffalo, NY $100 ‒ $125 Hourly D'Youville University's College of Osteopathic Medicine seeks adjunct faculty to provide hands-on laboratory instruction in osteopathic manipulative medicine (OMM) to first- and second-year osteopathic medical students. Working under the direction of the OMM chair, adjunct laboratory instructors — commonly known as table trainers —

supervise small groups of students during scheduled OMM labs, demonstrate osteopathic structural examination and manipulative treatment techniques, and coach students toward safe, competent palpatory and treatment skills grounded in osteopathic principles and practice.This is a teaching-focused, part-time appointment well suited to practicing osteopathic physicians, NMM/OMM residents and fellows, and other qualified
