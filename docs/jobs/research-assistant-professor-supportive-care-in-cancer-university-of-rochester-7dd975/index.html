Research Assistant Professor (Supportive Care in Cancer)

University of Rochester  ·  Saunders Research Building, NY
ResearchNon-TenureOther

Position summary

The Department of Surgery at the University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry is seeking a non-tenure track Research Assistant Professor for the NCI-supported T32 clinical and translational research training position in the Division of Supportive Care in Cancer.&nbsp; The program eagerly seeks highly motivated early-career stage individuals with doctorates (PhD, Dr. P.H., M.D.) in health-related areas,

including translational science, who are looking for skills training in cancer control research and grant writing towards developing a successful research career as an independent investigator.&nbsp; NIH requires candidates must be US citizens or permanent residents.&nbsp; Two years of funding with stipends are offered along with health insurance, tuition, research support and travel to up to four scientific meetings

per year.&nbsp; Applications for women and those from minority groups are strong encouraged.&nbsp; Prior post-doctoral experience is allowed.&nbsp; Doctoral degree requirements must be met prior to hire.&nbsp; Post offer drug screen is required.&nbsp;
