Emergency Radiology - Faculty (Open Rank)

Medical College of Wisconsin  ·  Faculty (Open Rank)  ·  Milwaukee, WI
FacultyEmergency Radiology

Position summary

The Emergency Radiology Section of the Department of Radiology at the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW) is seeking a Board Certified/Eligible radiologist who has successfully completed a fellowship or has experience in Emergency Radiology and is eligible for medical licensure in Wisconsin. The Emergency Radiology section is recruiting for an on-site, remote, or hybrid radiologist and are open to a variety of

staffing models, including (but not limited to) 9pm-7am (CST) shift or 5pm-2am (CST) 7 days, 14-day off or 1pm-9pm (CST) 7-days on, 7-days off. Faculty on this shift are supported by a collaborative team, including another 9p-7a faculty, neuro-specialized 5p-2a faculty, resident trainees, and a radiology liaison. Faculty will have access to cutting-edge CT, MR, and US technology with opportunities to work in

collaboration with basic research scientists and a major equipment vendor. We are dedicated to the principles of equity and diversity and particularly encourage those of diverse backgrounds to apply. Our ideal candidate will have a: Desire to excel and collaborate with a talented team of radiologists who choose to practice, learn, and innovate together. Passion for providing high-quality patient care with expertise

in Emergency Imaging for 5 emergency departments, including a Level 1 Trauma Center. Opportunities to participate in scholarly activities which could include education, quality, and/or research. Team: The Department of Radiology is recognized as a progressive and forward-thinking team that continues to expand with a dynamic multi-site hospital and clinic network in the Milwaukee metropolitan area. The Emergency

Radiology Section has dedicated full-time faculty and a rotation of other subspecialized radiologists who are highly respected, collaborative, and fellowship trained. The Department of Radiology provides post-graduate fellowships, including a one-year Emergency Radiology fellowship, a diagnostic radiology residency program, and medical student elective clinical rotations. Collaboration is the foundation for how we

work within our department and with others across the organization. Our Radiology team includes 122 clinical faculty radiologists, 12 imaging science faculty, 12 instructor/fellows, 41 residents, 20 advanced practice providers, and 64 support staff. Benefits: MCW Radiology offers a competitive salary within the academic environment with bi-annual incentives based on team-based metrics. Protected
