Social and Behavioral Sciences - Psychology Department - Professor Staten Island, NY, United States Job Description The Department of Psychology at Wagner College invites applications for an open-rank faculty position in the area of Cognitive Psychology to begin August 2027. Candidates should hold a Ph.D. at the time of appointment. Rank will be dependent upon the candidate’s experience. In addition to undergraduate

courses in cognitive psychology and research area, the successful candidate should be able to teach statistics and methodology. Candidates who can involve undergraduate students in laboratory research are highly desirable. Additionally, candidates with an interest in teaching one or more of the following will be given particular attention: history of psychology, sport psychology, and sensation and perception.

Candidates should demonstrate a strong commitment to excellence in undergraduate teaching, student mentoring, inclusive pedagogy, and scholarly research. The teaching load is 3/3, with opportunities to teach summer courses. Wagner College, located in Staten Island, NY, values diversity, equity, and inclusion and encourages applications from candidates whose experiences and perspectives will contribute to the

excellence of our academic community. Applicants should submit the following as a single PDF file under the "Resume" button: Letter of application Curriculum vitae Graduate transcript Statement of teaching philosophy and experience Evidence of teaching excellence (if available) Statement of current and future research plans Contact information for three professional references Review of applications will begin on

November 1, 2026, and continue until the position is filled.
