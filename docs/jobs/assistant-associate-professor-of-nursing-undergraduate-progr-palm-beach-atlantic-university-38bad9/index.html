Assistant/Associate Professor of Nursing, Undergraduate Program

Palm Beach Atlantic University  ·  Nursing, Undergraduate Program  ·  West Palm Beach, FL
Assistant ProfessorAssociate ProfessorNursing

Position summary

SUMMARY The School of Nursing invites applications for a full-time, on-campus faculty position. This position is dedicated to undergraduate nursing education and offers an exciting opportunity to prepare the next generation of practice-ready, Christ-centered nurse leaders to provide holistic, evidence-based, community-focused care across diverse healthcare settings. The faculty member will provide leadership in

community health nursing education, community engagement, and student mentorship, prepare graduates to address individual and population health needs through health promotion, disease prevention, and the care of vulnerable and underserved populations. The position also plays a key role in strengthening partnerships with local healthcare and community organizations to expand high-quality clinical learning experiences

while advancing Palm Beach Atlantic University's mission of preparing nurses to care for the whole person and lead the delivery of Christ-centered healthcare. Primary teaching responsibilities include didactic, laboratory, simulation, and clinical instruction in community health nursing, as well as the development, coordination, and evaluation of community-based clinical experiences. The faculty member will

collaborate with community partners to secure and maintain quality clinical placements that support student learning and service to the community. In addition to teaching, the faculty member is expected to actively engage in student advising and mentoring, scholarship and research, professional practice, and service to the School of Nursing, the University, and the nursing profession. The School of Nursing is

committed to academic excellence, innovation in nursing education, and the integration of Christian faith with nursing practice. Palm Beach Atlantic University is a comprehensive Christian university whose mission is to equip students to grow in wisdom, lead with conviction, and serve God boldly, emphasizing moral, spiritual, and intellectual development.
