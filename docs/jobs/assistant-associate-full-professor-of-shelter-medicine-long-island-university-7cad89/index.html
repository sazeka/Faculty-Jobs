Assistant/Associate/Full Professor of Shelter Medicine

Long Island University  ·  Brookville, NY
Assistant ProfessorAssociate ProfessorFull ProfessorVeterinary Medicine

Position summary

SummaryThe Long Island University College of Veterinary Medicine (LIU-CVM) in Brookville, New York, invites applications from self-directed, innovative and motivated individuals for a full-time, clinical-track or tenure-track faculty position in shelter medicine. The position may be filled as Assistant, Associate or Full Professor depending upon qualifications. This is an exciting opportunity to join a dynamic,

missiondriven team dedicated to educating the next generation of veterinary professionals. We are looking for an enthusiastic educator who thrives at the intersection of teaching, clinical service, and community engagement. The successful candidate will have the unique opportunity to work within the North Shore Animal League of America (NSALA), described as one of the world’s largest, most comprehensive no-kill

rescue and adoption organizations (https//www.animalleague.org). Supported by a team of exceptional professionals, the NSALA is a world leader in setting best practices for adoption and performs >38,000 exams annually. Between the NSALA and other shelter program partners of LIU-CVM, the successful candidate will have the opportunity to build a center of excellence in veterinary shelter medicine. Successful candidates

will be engaged in the College’s educational, research and service activities. The primary responsibility is to direct the LIU-CVM shelter medicine fourth year clinical rotation at NSALA. The typical rotation has five to six 4th year students. The chosen candidate will be responsible for educating the students in all things related to shelter medicine (practicing shelter medicine, conducting dental prophylaxis and

oral surgery, basic surgeries, wellness visits, etc.). Some didactic lectures and or case discussions are anticipated to complement clinical experience, as needed. Rotations through other NSALA services will be expected, as directed by the NSALA director, to contribute to NSALA collaborations. Successful candidates are expected to promote the LIU-CVM and NSALA missions of enhancing veterinary medical education

through a commitment to teaching, academic excellence, and service to a variety of stakeholders. The majority of time will be spent at NSALA and thus the NSALA director will be the primary director of this position on a daily basis.
