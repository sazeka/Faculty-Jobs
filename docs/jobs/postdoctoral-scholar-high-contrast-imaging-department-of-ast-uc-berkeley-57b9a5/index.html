Position description The Bottom group in the UC Berkeley Department of Astronomy develops instrumentation and techniques for imaging and characterizing exoplanets and circumstellar environments. Our work spans adaptive optics, high-contrast imaging and coronagraphy, wavefront sensing and control, and infrared detector development. We build, test, and deploy instruments,

components, and algorithms at major ground-based observatories including W. M. Keck Observatory, and work with collaborators at JPL, Caltech, the University of Hawaii, and industry partners on technology for future space missions, including the Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO). The group combines laboratory development with on-sky observing. The postdoctoral scholar will carry out research in high-contrast imaging

of exoplanets and circumstellar environments, working at the interface between instrumentation and observation. Duties and responsibilities will include some combination of: Developing, testing, and deploying adaptive optics, wavefront sensing and control, and coronagraphic techniques on ground-based telescopes and instruments (e.g., at Keck and Subaru Observatory). Proposing, planning, and carrying out high-contrast

observing programs, and reducing and analyzing the resulting data. Contributing to technology development for the Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO), including laboratory testbed work and modeling and simulation of coronagraph and wavefront control performance.
