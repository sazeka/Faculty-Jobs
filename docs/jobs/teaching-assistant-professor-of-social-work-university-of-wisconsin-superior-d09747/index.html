Position Title: Teaching Assistant Professor of Social Work Job Category: Academic Staff Employment Type: Regular Job Profile: Teaching Assistant Professor Job Duties: The University of Wisconsin-Superior has an established Bachelor of Social Work (BSW) program

and is launching a new Master of Social Work (MSW) program to start enrolling students in fall 2027. UW-Superior seeks a Teaching Assistant Professor to oversee the BSW and MSW Practicum Programs. The position requires professional knowledge, administrative and management skills, practicum policy expertise, accreditation experience, and cultural sensitivity. Additionally, a strong collaborative work ethic, commitment

to excellence in social work education and community partnership are required. The person will assist the BSW and MSW program directors with administration of the curricula and professional accreditation requirements. Key Job Responsibilities: May supervise student employees involved in development or delivery of instruction Contributes to the strategic development of curriculum and academic content through various

mediums to ensure the integrity of the educational mission of the work unit Facilitates classroom, online and/or laboratory instruction, including assessment of student performance Contributes to the development of teaching and learning scholarly work including publications and presentations Collaborates with department faculty and staff to develop teaching strategies relevant to the discipline Develops and designs

curriculum and instructional material relevant to a course of instruction Advises students on academic and career direction within a specific field of study Department: Human Behavior, Justice, and Diversity Full-Time 100%, Instructional Academic Staff, 9-month ongoing appointment, one vacancy Job Responsibilities: Direct Practicum Program (BSW and MSW) Ensure the Practicum Education program remains in compliance

with Council on Social Work Education (CSWE) standards. Develop and maintain practicum sites and affiliation agreements. Recruit, train, and mentor qualified practicum supervisors. Oversee and provide support to practicum liaisons, helping to resolve student issues. Maintain students’ practicum education records for licensure. Develop, update, and distribute the MSW Practicum Manual. Contribute to initial CSWE
