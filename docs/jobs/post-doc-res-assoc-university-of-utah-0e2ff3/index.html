the completion of a large-scale evaluation of Utah’s Reemployment Services and Eligibility Assessment (RESEA) program. This is a full-time, grant-funded position with an anticipated duration of 12–18 months, with a flexible start date beginning as early as September 2026.The successful candidate will join an interdisciplinary evaluation team and play a leading role in completing the final stages of the RESEA impact

evaluation. The position is particularly well suited for individuals with strong quantitative research skills and prior experience in program evaluation.Please direct any questions about this position to David Curtis, Lead Evaluator, at david.curtis@fcs.utah.edu, or Su Shin, Co-Evaluator, at su.shin@fcs.utah.edu*Candidates must already be authorized to work in the United States as visa sponsorship is not available

for this position. Responsibilities The Research Associate will work closely with the principal investigators and will be responsible for: Conducting statistical analyses using large administrative datasets Preparing analytic datasets and documenting data management procedures Developing statistical code and producing tables, figures, and technical appendices Contributing substantially to the preparation of the RESEA

Final Evaluation Report, including drafting technical sections and revising report chapters Collaborating with investigators to ensure timely completion of project milestones Participating in project meetings and communicating progress to the research team Although opportunities for scholarly publications may arise, the primary responsibility of this position is the completion of high-quality evaluation reports.
