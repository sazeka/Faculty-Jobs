University's Occupational Therapy Doctorate (OTD) program invites applications for an Assistant Professor of Occupational Therapy. This role will lead and facilitate dynamic experiential learning and clinical education, preparing future practitioners for transformative practice. About the Job: This is a part-time (0.65 FTE) clinical faculty position focused on clinical education, community outreach, curriculum

innovation, and program development. In addition to teaching within the curriculum, the person hired will develop opportunities for hands-on learning throughout the program to enhance OTD students' ability to develop critical and clinical thinking skills and integrate evidence-based learning into occupational therapy practice. Once hired, this person will build relationships with community organizations and fieldwork

supervisors, with a focus on developing the skills and resources needed to support the provision of high-quality clinical education to our students. The person will also design a mentorship system for GFU OTD graduates during their first one to two years of clinical practice, particularly for those who choose to work in rural and underserved communities throughout Oregon. This role will work alongside the Program

Director in innovative program development, with a focus on GFU's mission of integrating spirituality and educating the whole person. Job responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Building community outreach and relationships with fieldwork sites to support student growth and development as excellent clinicians. Serving as a primary lecturer and co-instructor for Occupational Therapy Doctorate (OTD) courses,

delivering curriculum, facilitating hands-on clinical skills labs, and adapting instructional approaches based on program needs. Supporting program development and assisting the Program Director with program administration to enhance the student experience and foster a culture of growth mindset and lifelong learning. Developing curriculum and ensuring compliance with ACOTE standards. Mentoring clinical fieldwork
