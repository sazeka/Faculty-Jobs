Adjunct Instructor

Norfolk State University  ·  Main Campus, VA
AdjunctEnglish

Position summary

ENG 101 Responsibilities: Teach Composition I, the introductory writing course in the General Education core. This course is designed to be a hands-on workshop that will provide students with an introduction to college writing, including development, argumentation, and the rhetorical situations for producing written communication for a college audience. ENG 102 Responsibilities: Teach Composition II, the second

writing course in the General Education core sequence. This course is designed to provide students with further experience in college writing, including development with sources, persuasion, logic and analytical thinking, and the transfer of written communication skills to other discipline specific needs. ENG 285 Responsibilities: Teach Public Speaking and oral communication in the General Education core. In this

course, students learn to give informal and formal speeches/oral presentations based on a variety of communication principles.
