Position Information Position Title Visiting Assistant Professor Job Description The Department of Natural Sciences and Environmental health department at Mississippi Valley State University invites applications for a full-time Visiting Assistant Professor position in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning with applications in Environmental Health Systems. The successful candidate will teach graduate-level

courses in AI and machine learning, with emphasis on environmental data, environmental health, and Environmental Public Health applications and undergraduate courses as needed.The department seeks a committed educator and scholar who can support graduate instruction, mentor graduate and undergraduate students, and contribute to interdisciplinary teaching and research in AI, environmental science, environmental

health, and public health systems. Required Qualifications Applicants must have: A Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Environmental Health, Environmental Science, Environmental Public Health, Computational Science, or a closely related discipline by the start date of appointment. Expertise in AI/ML applications related to environmental data, environmental health, or environmental

systems. Demonstrated ability or strong potential to teach graduate courses in AI, machine learning, and environmental health applications. Evidence of scholarly potential and the ability to engage students in research and applied learning experiences. Strong communication, organizational, and interpersonal skills. Commitment to excellence in teaching, advising, mentoring, research, and service. Preferred
