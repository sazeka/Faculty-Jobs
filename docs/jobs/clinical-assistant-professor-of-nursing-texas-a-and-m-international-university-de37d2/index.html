TAMIU

is a Hispanic Serving Institution with over 8,000 students located in Laredo, Texas, a vibrant and bicultural city with a population of about 250,000. The university advances knowledge through research and discovery, teaching and learning, community engagement and public service, and with an intentional focus on student success and research excellence. For more information, please visit https://www.tamiu.edu/ . This

is a full-time, non-tenure track, 9-month academic faculty appointment. Primary duties will include teaching specialty didactic and clinical courses in PMHNP; supervising and assessing student clinical competencies and theoretical knowledge while supporting comprehensive learning experiences; evaluating students' progress in course work and clinical experience; planning, developing, implementing, and evaluating the

curriculum, as appropriate; completing all administrative aspects of courses taught; and advising/mentoring the next generation of PMHNP's.
