Assistant or Associate Professor of Nursing Description of Job The Henry Predolin School of Nursing at Edgewood University is seeking a full-time, tenure track faculty member for the 2025-26 academic year. The School of Nursing fosters excellence in classroom and clinical settings in both undergraduate and graduate programs. The undergraduate program includes both a traditional and a post-baccalaureate program. Our

Master’s (MSN) program has concentration areas in nursing education and nursing administration. A Doctor of Nursing Practice in Leadership (DNP) program is offered both fully online and in a blended format. Join a talented team of nursing professionals by teaching small classes, with a 7:1 student/teacher ratio. Utilize our state-of-the-art clinical simulation lab at the Center for Healthcare Education and

Simulation, which features a realistic patient setting that includes patient simulators with high-fidelity mannequins and three real-life, fully equipped patient rooms, each viewable from a control room with one-way glass and closed-circuit monitors and in-lab cameras. Enjoy living in Madison, a vibrant capital city nestled between lakes Mendota and Monona. Located on the wooded shores of Lake Wingra, Edgewood

University is located in a friendly residential community, on a safe and scenic 55-acre campus. It is within walking distance of shopping, restaurants, and recreational and cultural opportunities. Edgewood University is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission. The School of Nursing is accredited by the Commission on Collegiate Nursing Education . Work collaboratively with Deans and Director(s) in the pre and post licensure nursing programs to teach, develop, implement, refine, revise, and/or evaluate nursing student curriculum and policies.
