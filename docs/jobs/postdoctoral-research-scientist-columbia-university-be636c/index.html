THE OPPORTUNITY This opportunity to join the Department of Pediatrics at the Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons at the rank of Postdoctoral Research Scientist is an exciting one. Columbia University Irving Medical Center, one of the nation’s foremost academic health science centers. department in the NY region in NIH research funding.&nbsp; KEY RESPONSIBILITIES The Mace Lab at Columbia University in New York is seeking a postdoctoral researcher for a collaborative cell biology project that seeks to define determinants of lymphocyte cell migration.
