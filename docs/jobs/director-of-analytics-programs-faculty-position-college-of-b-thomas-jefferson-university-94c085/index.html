Director of Analytics Programs/Faculty Position, College of Business, Thomas Jefferson University

Thomas Jefferson University  ·  College of Business  ·  Philadelphia, PA
Open RankBusiness Analytics

Position summary

Job Details Thomas Jefferson University seeks a full-time appointment at the rank of Associate or Full Professor to serve as Program Director of Graduate Analytics Programs in the College of Business, with an anticipated start date of January 2027. The college currently offers two graduate degrees: MS (Business Analytics and Artificial Intelligence) and MS (Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence). The Program

Director will provide academic and strategic leadership for graduate programs and related initiatives in Analytics and Artificial Intelligence. This is a leadership-focused faculty role that combines teaching, program administration, and scholarship, with opportunities to shape a growing portfolio of analytics programs across the College of Business and Thomas Jefferson University. Responsibilities: The Program

Director of Analytics Programs will provide strategic vision and leadership for the Data Analytics and Business Analytics graduate programs, ensuring alignment with the College’s broader Analytics and Artificial Intelligence initiatives. The Director will oversee curriculum development and revision to keep programs current with emerging technologies, industry needs, and accreditation standards, while managing course

planning, staffing, scheduling, assessment, and potential expansion into undergraduate and new certificate or concentration offerings. In addition to teaching graduate-level analytics courses (and potentially undergraduate courses as programs expand), the Director will mentor and advise students, fostering inclusive and engaging learning environments that support a diverse student population. The role also includes

cultivating strong industry, alumni, and community partnerships to enhance experiential learning, internships, and employment opportunities, as well as leading outreach, recruitment, and program promotion efforts in collaboration with Enrollment Management and the Dean’s Office. The Director will contribute to program branding and communications, oversee student orientation activities, and maintain an active

scholarly agenda in analytics, data science, artificial intelligence, or related fields, including pursuing external funding and engaging students in research. Finally, the Director will work closely with the Dean, faculty, and staff on college-wide initiatives, participate in university governance, and promote a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement within the College of Business.
