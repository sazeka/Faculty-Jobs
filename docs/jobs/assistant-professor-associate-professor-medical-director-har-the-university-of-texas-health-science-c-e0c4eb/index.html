JOB DESCRIPTION McGovern Medical School at UTHealth Houston, a leading academic health center, invites applications for a full-time faculty Medical Director position in the Department of Family & Community Medicine. The selected candidate will contribute to the department's mission to provide health care to an urban underserved population and train the next generation of physicians. The Medical Director at each site

is the physician leader of primary care services in the site clinic. The Medical Director has the ultimate responsibility for clinical and educational services/activities at the clinic. Day-to-day coordination of activities for education (site coordinator) could be the assignment of another on-site faculty member. This position is 80% Patient Care Clinical Time, 20% Director Administrative Time, and supervises clinic

site faculty, nurse practitioners, and trainees (medical students and residents). Medical Director Scope: Within established Department and/or Harris Health System policies, is responsible for overall monitoring as well as coordinating the successful day-to-day operation of clinical and educational activities at the clinic site. The Medical Director frequently interacts and is involved with the Clinic Operations

Manager and Nurse Manager in all areas of clinic operations. Together, the triad ensures that the clinic is providing the best possible patient care with maximum efficiency.
