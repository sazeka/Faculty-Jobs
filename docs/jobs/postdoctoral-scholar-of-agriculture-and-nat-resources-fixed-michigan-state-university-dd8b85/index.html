Jun 25 2028 - 23:55 EDT Salary Commensurate with Experience College Of Agriculture And Nat Resources 1143687 Position Summary The Kravchenko Lab in the Department of Plant, Soil, and Microbial Sciences at Michigan State University is seeking a Postdoctoral Scholar to conduct research focused on root characteristics of plants in annual and perennial cropping and native vegetation systems and their contribution as

drivers of processes of soil carbon sequestration. The experimental component of the position will involve field soil and root sample collections from multiple experimental sites aimed at better understanding how different plant systems contribute to soil carbon accrual and weather variations in weather patterns (such as droughts and deluges) might influence it. . The project involves 13C plant labeling, root

structure analysis, and X-ray computed micro-tomography. Job Duties: Travel to experimental sites as needed for deep core soil sampling. Analyses of root scans and measurements of soil properties. X-ray computed tomography scanning of soil samples and image analyses. Analyses of the collected data. Publication results in peer-reviewed journals and presentations at national/international conferences. Starting date is

September 2026 or as soon as possible after that. The position is for one year with a possibility of a renewal contingent upon funding and satisfactory performance.
