Adjunct Instructor, FYI/FYE

Vaughn College of Aeronautics and Technology  ·  FYI/FYE  ·  Flushing, NY
InstructorAdjunctNon-TenureFirst Year Initiatives/Experience

Position summary

Position Purpose The First Year Initiatives (FYI101) and First Year Experience (FYE101) courses provide students with the essential skillsets to positively influence their success in college. FYE101 and FYI101 will assist students in transitioning to the college environment by engaging them collaboratively with Vaughn community while developing a sense of belonging. Major Responsibilities/Duties/Functions/Tasks

Instruct a First Year Initiative/Experience course for one of the following student populations: flight majors, Arthur O. Eve HEOP, Aviation Training Institute (i.e., airframe and powerplant), or mixed-major, first year cohorts.Instruction should be engaging and involve discussions and group exercises.While some lecture is necessary, the instructor should strike an appropriate balance of in-class methodologies.

Participate in pre-semester, mid-semester and post-semester curriculum alignment / faculty meetings. Formulate lesson plans, presentations and course materials to prepare first-year students for success in college through academic and social integration.This includes customizing existing content to suit the population of students assigned. Share syllabi and curricular innovations with fellow instructors and the

Director of Student Success and Graduation Initiatives Collaborate with other departments at Vaughn and outside parties in order to bring guest speakers into the FYE/I classroom. Submit attendance information, NA census (never attended) information, midterm grades and final student grades via SONIS. **Qualifications: To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty

satisfactorily. The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill and/or ability required. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions. Other relevant higher education experience, such as working with cohorts in a relevant Opportunity Program may also be considered in lieu of teaching experience.
