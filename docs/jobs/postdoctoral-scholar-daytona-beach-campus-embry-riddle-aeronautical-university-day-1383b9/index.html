Position summary

Job Description The Opportunity Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), Daytona Beach Campus, invites applications for a full-time Postdoctoral Scholar position in the field of magnetospheric physics and space instrumentation. The successful candidate will contribute to cutting-edge research focused on the design and development of a miniature particle detector, as well as the analysis of satellite observations

and numerical simulation results related to the Earth’s magnetosphere and space plasma environment. This position offers an exciting opportunity to work in a collaborative research environment at one of the world’s leading aerospace-focused universities. Position Responsibilities: Participate in the design, development, testing, and integration of a miniature particle detector for space applications. Analyze

satellite measurements and numerical simulation results in the field of magnetospheric physics and space plasma science. Contribute to the development of data analysis tools and scientific interpretation of observational and modeling results. Prepare manuscripts for publication in peer-reviewed journals and present research findings at scientific conferences. Assist with proposal development and contribute to

externally funded research activities. Collaborate with faculty, students, and external research partners on interdisciplinary research initiatives. Mentoring and Career Development: This position includes a structured mentoring plan designed to support the professional growth of an early-career postdoctoral scientist and foster the development of independent research capabilities. Mentoring activities will include:

Weekly one-on-one meetings focused on research progress, manuscript preparation, proposal development, and career planning. Regular group meetings to encourage collaboration, scientific communication, and presentation skills. Guidance in building a competitive research portfolio, securing external funding, leading research projects, and publishing high-impact research. Career development support will include:

Periodic discussions to define long-term career goals and identify funding opportunities. Collaborative establishment of short- and medium-term milestones, including manuscript submissions and grant applications. Opportunities to take increasing ownership of research activities, including project leadership, collaboration management, and mentoring junior trainees.
