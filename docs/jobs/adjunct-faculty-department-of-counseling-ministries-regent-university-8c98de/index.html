Adjunct Faculty - Department of Counseling Ministries Apply now Job no: 50000234 Work type: Part time Location: Virginia Beach Categories: Faculty & Adjunct, College of Health and Behavioral Sciences Regent University’s School of Psychology & Counseling - Department of Counseling Ministries is seeking adjunct faculty to teach online in the following degree programs: M.A. in Human Services, M.A. in Pastoral

Counseling, and Ph.D in Counseling & Psychological Studies. An earned terminal degree from an accredited college or university appropriate for the specific discipline is required. Prior college teaching experience preferred in addition to proficiency with online instructional technology. Expectations include 1) a dedication to Christ-centered teaching and learning; 2) a record of or potential for academic

scholarship; 3) the ability to teach undergraduate-level courses; 4) facility with online instructional technology; and 5) a willingness to embrace the College and University Identity and Mission statements and subscribe to a statement of Christian faith. Salary is based on number of students enrolled and ranges from $400 - $5,000 per 3 credit hour undergraduate course; $500 - $5,200 per 3 credit hour master’s level

course; $600 - $5,500 per 3 credit hour doctoral level courses. 2 credit hour courses range from $350 - $3,150. Please submit CV, cover letter, and unofficial transcripts along with your application. Regent University does not discriminate in employment in its programs or activities on the basis of race, color, national or ethnic origin, sex, disability, age or veteran status.
