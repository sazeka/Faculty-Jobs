Position summary

Job Description Summary The Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC) Department of Urology based in scenic Charleston, South Carolina, is recruiting a Board Eligible / Board Certified GURS fellowship-trained urologist to join our Bladder and Pelvic Health team. Successful candidates will possess an interest in the diagnosis, evaluation, surgical and non-surgical conditions of female and male pelvic floor including

voiding dysfunction, incontinence (male and female), vaginal prolapse, neurogenic bladder, and urinary reconstruction and have a robust clinical background as well as research and academic interests or experience. MUSC Department of Urology delivers the region’s most comprehensive range of Urologic services, encompassing every Urologic subspecialty, with more than 42,000 outpatient encounters each year. The candidate

selected for hire will experience a busy, comprehensive office and surgical urology practice utilizing the full spectrum of technology, including robotics and laparoscopy. This Urologist’s practice week will consist of an average of 2.5- 3 days in a MUSC clinic setting and 1 to 1.5 days in the operating room and 1 day per week at the Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center. The RHJVAMC is a 5-Star facility, one of the top

10% of VA facilities nationwide, and the 6th fastest growing. Academic Rank and appointment status will be commensurate with qualifications and experience. Opportunities exist to participate in already established clinical and research programs at all locations as well as room to grow and design new programs. The candidate can expect to be working in a beautiful, family-friendly community alongside very qualified and

friendly providers. More than four million visitors each year experience our 90 miles of pristine coastline and enjoy world-class shopping and culinary arts. Our region, which covers more than 3100 square miles, boasts a growing economy, top ranked schools, and year-round outdoor activities. Charleston has been repeatedly named as one of the country’s friendliest cities. Entity Medical University of South Carolina
