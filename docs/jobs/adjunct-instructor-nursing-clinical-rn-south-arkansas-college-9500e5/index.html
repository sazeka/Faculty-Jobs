Adjunct Instructor- Nursing Clinical (RN)

South Arkansas College  ·  Nursing  ·  South Arkansas College, AR

Position summary

Adjunct Instructor- Nursing Clinical Job Summary:This position is responsible for the instruction of Nursing students in the clinical setting; should be articulate, team oriented, professional in appearance and demeanor, and display excellent interpersonal skills. The selected candidate will maintain currency within his/her field, including required licensure. This is a part-time position and the hours can vary

depending on a number of factors, including, but not limited to simulation/skills laboratory need and clinical site needs. This position is governed by state and federal laws and institution policy.Salary: $30 per hourSupervisory Responsibilities:Supervise students in the nursing skills and/or simulation lab and/or various clinical settings. No supervision of employees. Duties and Responsibilities:• Supervise

students in the nursing skills and/or simulation lab and/or various clinical settings at a ratio not to exceed 8 in an acute care setting, 10 in a non-acute care setting, or 15 in a community service setting. • Structure a learning environment in which all students are treated equally, fairly, and respectfully.• Maintain attendance records and report student deficiencies to Clinical Coordinator and/or Director of

Nursing Programs.• Facilitate effective student learning experiences that focus on achievement of the student’s current course learner outcomes.• Evaluate student performance and provide immediate feedback to the student, including advising student of areas of needed improvement if applicable.• Ensure students are receiving and providing appropriate patient report per the facility policy.• Grade clinical assignments

and enter grades in the college’s LMS (Learning Management System) in a timely manner. • Perform scheduled work hours and expected tasks as assigned.• Provide regular updates on student progress to Clinical Coordinator and/or Director of Nursing Programs.• Attend faculty meetings as scheduled by the Clinical Coordinator and/or the Director of Nursing Programs. • Maintain professionalism and effective communication on

campus, in the lab and in clinical settings.• Provide a supportive and educational learning environment.• Be knowledgeable of and uphold the program’s Student Handbook policies, program procedures, and program/course requirements.• Attend all required orientation and training.• Actively participate in annual evaluation processes related to job performance as directed by the South Arkansas College Plan for Faculty
