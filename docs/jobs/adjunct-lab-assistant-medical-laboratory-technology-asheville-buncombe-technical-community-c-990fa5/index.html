Description Summary The Medical Laboratory Technology Program is looking for someone to fulfill an assistant position for the Microbiology course.Lab Assistants support faculty by helping prepare laboratories, maintaining an organized and safe learning environment, and assisting students during hands-on activities. Duties Responsibilities Lab Preparation & Set-Up Prepare reagents, controls, media, slides, stains, and

solutions for all MLT laboratory courses. Set up equipment, instrumentation, and supplies prior to each lab session. Pipette, aliquot, label, and organize patient sample simulations as needed. Assist with prepping unknowns, QC materials, and teaching specimens. Ensure proper storage of chemicals, samples, and supplies. Lab Support & Instruction Assist faculty during lab sessions by helping students with procedures,

instrumentation, troubleshooting, and basic concepts. Reinforce safety protocols and good laboratory practices. Monitor workflow to maintain an efficient, professional learning environment. Answer student questions and guide them through step-by-step technical procedures. Support students in Hematology, Chemistry, Urinalysis, Immunology, Blood Bank, Microbiology, and related lab activities. Safety & Compliance

Maintain a clean, organized, and safe laboratory environment. Follow all institutional, OSHA, and CLIA-related safety guidelines.
