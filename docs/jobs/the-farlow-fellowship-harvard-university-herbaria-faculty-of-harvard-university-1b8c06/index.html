Explore Academic Positions at Harvard The Farlow Fellowship - Harvard University Herbaria Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Position Details Title The Farlow Fellowship - Harvard University Herbaria School Faculty of Arts and Sciences Department/Area Postdocs-Science Position Description The HUH include six collections that, together, form one of the largest university herbarium collections in

the world, and the third largest herbarium in the United States. With its state-of-the art research laboratories, computational facilities, and world class libraries, the HUH have been a centerpiece of biodiversity science. More information about the HUH can be found on its website http://huh.harvard.edu/. The Harvard University Herbaria (HUH) invites nominations and direct applications for the Farlow Fellowship, an

outstanding opportunity for an early-career scientist or collections professional to develop an innovative research program while helping shape the future of one of the world’s premier collections of fungi and other cryptogams. Based in the Farlow Herbarium, the fellowship provides access to more than 1.5 million specimens of fungi, bryophytes, algae, and lichens. These resources are complemented by exceptional

libraries and archives containing rare books, manuscripts, field notes, historical correspondence, monographs, journals, and electronic media. Together, they offer extraordinary opportunities for discovery across taxonomy, systematics, biodiversity science, collections research, and the history of natural history. Eligibility Applications are sought from early-career individuals with a research program involving

collection-based taxonomy and/or systematics in groups represented in the Farlow collection, as well as a strong interest in the curation and stewardship of the collection. Fellowship Details We seek outstanding candidates who are eager to advance collections-based research and develop new approaches to curation, stewardship, accessibility, and engagement. Applicants should have a research program involving

collection-based taxonomy and/or systematics in groups represented in the Farlow Herbarium, as well as a strong interest in the care and development of natural history collections. The Farlow Fellowship is awarded for one year, with the possibility of renewal contingent on satisfactory performance, continued funding, and required approvals.
