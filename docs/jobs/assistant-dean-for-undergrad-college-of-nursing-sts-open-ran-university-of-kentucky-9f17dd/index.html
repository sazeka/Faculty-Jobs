College of Nursing at the University of Kentucky invites exceptional candidates to apply for a 12-month, tenure-eligible, Special Title Series, faculty position at the rank of Assistant, Associate, or full Professor. This position will also serve as the Assistant Dean for Undergraduate Programs and Policy. The Assistant Dean for Undergraduate Programs provides administrative and academic leadership for assigned

academic programs within the College of Nursing, with an emphasis on effective program operations, faculty support, curriculum implementation, and student success. The role involves collaboration with the Associate Dean, faculty, track and course coordinators, and college leadership to ensure appropriate faculty resources, support faculty recruitment and development, and maintain high-quality didactic and clinical

learning experiences. Additional responsibilities include oversight of student recruitment, orientation, retention, academic progression, and student concerns, as well as coordination of curriculum planning, evaluation, clinical placements, and program assessment. The position also supports resource allocation, budget planning, accreditation and regulatory compliance, and long-range academic planning to promote

continuous program improvement and alignment with the mission and strategic priorities of the College of Nursing. Qualified candidates must possess a terminal degree in nursing and hold (or be eligible to obtain) an active Kentucky RN license.
