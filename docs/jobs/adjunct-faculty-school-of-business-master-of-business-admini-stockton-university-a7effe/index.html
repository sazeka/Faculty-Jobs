Position summary

Stockton University is ranked among the top public universities in the Northeast with more than 160 undergraduate and graduate programs, as well as continuing education opportunities. Our University, one hour from Philadelphia and two hours from New York City, offers unique learning, living, and working environments throughout southern New Jersey, including the 1,600-acre Galloway campus in the Pinelands National

Reserve and Atlantic City campus, located in the University District, just steps from the beach and iconic Atlantic City Boardwalk. Additional locations include Hammonton and Manahawkin. As a public University, Stockton University provides an environment for excellence to our student body through an interdisciplinary approach to liberal arts, sciences, and professional education. Founded in 1969, our University held

its first classes in 1971. Our University’s welcoming and vibrant community makes Stockton a great place to learn, live, and work! Learn more about the many reasons to choose Stockton at www.Stockton.edu. Our Faculty and Staff are committed to supporting Stockton University’s strong student-centered vision and mission.
