Recruitment Type: Internal/External Applicants Work Schedule: part-time/12-months Department: 201-Dean-Engineering Position Summary: The Sustainable Enterprise Executive Education and Development (SEED) program seeks qualified individuals for adjunct faculty and welcomes applicants. designing and incorporating sustainability practices into their business environments.Unlike most executive-level programs of its kind, SEED combines the leadership expertise of faculty from the highly-ranked Villanova School of Business with the technical knowledge of professors in Villanova’s first-of-its-kind Sustainable Engineering program.
