CaliforniaThe USC Caruso Department of Otolaryngology Head and Neck Surgery seeks a board-eligible or board-certified Otolaryngologist with fellowship training and expertise in Sleep Surgery, or both Sleep Medicine and Sleep Surgery, to join our department. We are seeking an innovative clinician-scholar with demonstrated expertise or strong potential in sleep surgery, including contemporary approaches to the surgical

management of obstructive sleep apnea and related sleep disorders. We seek faculty members whose career goals include excellence in patient care, research, and clinical teaching.Clinical responsibilities will include the evaluation and treatment of adult patients with sleep-related breathing disorders, including the performance of advanced sleep surgery procedures. This physician will closely collaborate with Sleep

Medicine physicians and participate in the USC Multidisciplinary Sleep Center. Clinical duties will involve seeing patients in our various USC clinics, with surgery performed at Keck Medical Center of USC and other USC-affiliated hospitals. There is a strong opportunity to start a fellowship program if the candidate desires.Our institution offers tremendous clinical, research, and educational opportunities, and we

encourage faculty to take full advantage of them. The department prioritizes mentorship, collaboration, and teamwork to promote faculty development within a supportive, intellectually curious, and collegial environment. Faculty are expected to pursue scholarly activities that advance patient care and contribute to the field of otolaryngology–head and neck surgery. Opportunities exist to participate in clinical

research, resident and medical student education, and multidisciplinary collaborations across the USC health system. Faculty rank will be commensurate with the qualifications and experience of the selected candidate.The University of Southern California (USC), founded in 1880, is one of the world's leading private research universities and the largest private employer in the City of Los Angeles.
