Assistant/Associate Professor of Professional Counseling Department: College of Theology and Ministry Location: Tulsa, OK Start Your Application ABOUT US Oral Roberts University is a non-profit organization whose staff and faculty actually make a difference in the lives of its students. We pride ourselves on our culture and the values we uphold. Our team is passionate about what we do, and we want you to make us even

better! As a globally recognized, Holy Spirit-empowered university, we develop whole leaders for the whole world through a unique Whole Person education. Students come to ORU not to "stay" in their faith but to GROW in faith and to become the Spirit-empowered leaders they are called to be. JOB DESCRIPTION The Graduate School of Counseling in the Oral Roberts University College of Theology and Ministry invites

applications for a full-time, promotional-track faculty position - the start date is upon review. We seek candidates who can integrate the Christian faith, from a Spirit-Empowered perspective, with the counseling profession and incorporate teaching, administration, and research. RESPONSIBILITIES The position requires occasional all-day, seminar-style teaching and some afternoon/evening teaching. Candidates must

demonstrate excellence in teaching courses in traditional residential, virtual, hybrid, and combination formats. REQUIREMENTS Candidate must have a PhD in Counselor Education and Supervision, preferably from a CACREP accredited program, and hold state licensure as a professional counselor, marriage and family therapist, or alcohol/drug/mental health counselor. Previous experience teaching in a counselor education

program, functioning as a mental health clinician, administrative experience, and credentialing as a board-approved supervisor is desired. INSTRUCTIONS 1. Complete the ORU faculty application 2. Please provide transcripts from all colleges and universities listed on your C.V. and application 3. Please provide names and e-mail addresses for three references Equal Employment Opportunity: Oral Roberts University
