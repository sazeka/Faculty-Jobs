Job Details Coastal Carolina University invites applications for an Assistant Professor in Communication or a related field to begin in August 2027. Candidates must have a Ph.D. in

Communication, Journalism, Mass Communication, or a closely related discipline. ABD candidates with a clear plan for degree completion prior to May 31, 2027, may be considered. Applications will be reviewed beginning on October 15, 2026.The teaching load is three courses per academic semester and may include face-to-face, hybrid, or online delivery formats. We are seeking excellent teacher-scholars who can teach the

department’s foundation courses as well as courses in the graduate program, including communication theory and communication research. Faculty will also teach courses in their areas of expertise. Preference will be given to candidates whose expertise includes one or more of the following areas: media, social media, health communication, and emerging technologies, such as artificial intelligence. Faculty are expected

to provide high-quality instruction, maintain an active scholarly agenda, mentor students, and provide service to the Department, College, and University. Interested candidates should submit electronically :(1) letter of application, (2) curriculum vitae, (3) reference list, (4) copy of transcripts, (5) research statement, and (6) teaching evaluations to https://jobs.coastal.edu/postings/122599 .
