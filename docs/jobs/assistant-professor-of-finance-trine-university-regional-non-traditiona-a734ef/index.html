Assistant/Associate Professor of Finance Assistant/Associate Professor of Finance Angola, Indiana Full-time Summary Trine University invites applications for a full-time Assistant/Associate Professor position in the Ketner School of Business beginning Fall 2026. The primary responsibility will be teaching undergraduate courses in finance. Duties and Responsibilities Trine University is a teaching-focused institution.

A standard teaching load for faculty is 12 credit hours per semester. Faculty are expected to spend 80% of their time on teaching, 10% on scholarship, and 10% on university service. Trine does not offer tenure. High-quality teaching is Trine University’s priority; however, faculty members are also expected to engage in professional development activities, develop/maintain appropriate professional contacts, and serve

as academic advisors for students. Service, in the form of committee work, advising student organizations, etc. is highly valued. The ideal candidate will have a background in finance and a passion for teaching. We are seeking an individual with experience in areas such as corporate finance, investments, financial markets, or related fields. Exceptional applicants with expertise in other areas of finance or

accounting will also be considered. The successful candidate will be able to teach undergraduate courses such as Managerial Finance, Investments, Financial Modeling, Risk & Insurance, International Finance, Money & Banking, and successfully direct and supervise internships. Other responsibilities will include playing an active role in recruiting and retention efforts and participating in Finance Club and/or

Accounting Club activities. Duties will begin in August 2026. Qualifications The ideal candidate will have: Demonstrated education or experience that would support the teaching of specified finance, accounting, or business classes; A demonstrated record of excellence and innovation in teaching; A commitment to service as a faculty advisor, student organization advisor, and active member of an undergraduate teaching

team; A strong network in the finance industry and business connections. A minimum of 3 years of professional experience in a finance and/or accounting role; A strong understanding of and commitment to the Trine culture and to the Angola community.
