Adjunct Faculty Position in Theater Adjunct Faculty Position in Theater July 10, 2026 2026-07-10 17:28 Rocky Mountain College invites applications for an in-person adjunct faculty member to teach our practicum courses (THR291 and THR391) for the Fall 2026 semester. This is a non-tenure track position, with the possibility for renewal. This position works closely with theater faculty to oversee students participating

in live production work, helping guide hands-on learning in a fast-paced, creative environment. The successful candidate will have a Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field. A background in theater production, technical theater, or a related field is required, as is knowledge of safety standards in theater production environments. Strong organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills, and the ability to mentor

and support students with varying levels of experience are also needed. Prior experience working in educational contexts, especially at the college level, while not required, is an advantage. The successful candidate will support and carry out the mission of the college as well as demonstrate a commitment to developing and enhancing a culture of rigor, excellence, and achievement. The start date is August 1, 2026;

classes start on August 17, 2026. Salary is commensurate with experience. Information about Rocky Mountain College can be found at www.rocky.edu. Questions about the courses should be directed to Jayme Green (greenj@rocky.edu) ; general questions about the position should be directed to Human Resources at humanresources@rocky.edu. Review of applications will begin immediately and continue until the position is

filled. To apply, please submit a letter of interest, curriculum vitae, and a teaching statement as a single PDF to jobs@rockv.edu. Applicants must also complete a Rocky Mountain College Faculty Employment Application. If offered the position, the candidate must also successfully complete a background check before hiring. Rocky Mountain College is an equal opportunity employer and does not discriminate on the basis

of race, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, protected veteran status, disability, age, or other legally protected status.
