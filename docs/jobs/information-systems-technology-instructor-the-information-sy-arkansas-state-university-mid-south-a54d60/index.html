Information Systems Technology Instructor The Information System Technology Instructor reports to the Interim Dean of General Education with responsibility for instruction of secondary center and college-level courses, student advising, student retention, assessment of student learning, service on college committees, and other duties as assigned. Work assignments may include evening and/or weekend hours. This is a

full-time, 10- month or 12-month position with full college benefits. Required Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in information technology related subject or in a computer science field, with appropriate professional certifications preferred; associate degree may be considered* Excellent spoken and written communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively with colleagues Working knowledge of networking,

computer maintenance, web design, Unix, Linux, Cisco, MS Office Suite applications and Canvas or other Learning Management System *A compelling combination of educational background, work experience, and professional credentials may be considered. Desired Qualifications: Teaching and/or other position-related work experience preferably at a community college Demonstrated understanding of academic needs of

underprepared, low income, first generation, and traditionally underrepresented students Evidence of the ability to work effectively to meet the educational needs of a diverse student body Preference will be given to candidates who understand active learning and divergent learning styles Essential Duties and Responsibilities: Teach courses for the Information Systems Technology program during the fall and spring

semesters. Deliver instruction to secondary and postsecondary students that aligns with approved ASU Mid-South syllabi and academic standards, supporting student attainment of course-and program-level learning outcomes. Support development of curriculum to align with initiatives in Information Systems Technology. Participate in Information Systems Technology Advisory Committee. Maintain accurate attendance and grade

records using the College’s LMS. Utilize and maintain lab areas following appropriate safety standards. Serve as an academic program advisor for students enrolled in the Information Systems Technology program. Participate in SkillsUSA activities and events.
