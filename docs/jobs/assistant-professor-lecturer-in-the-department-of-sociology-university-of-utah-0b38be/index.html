7/1/2027 Details The Department of Sociology & Criminology at the University of Utah invites applications for a career-line faculty position at the rank of Assistant Professor (Lecturer) to begin July 1, 2027. We seek an outstanding educator who demonstrates excellence in teaching, commitment to student engagement, and pedagogical innovation. Candidates should be able to teach courses that contribute to the

department’s population and health program area. Preferred candidates will be able to teach in one or more of the following areas: Medical Sociology Sociology of Mental Health Social Epidemiology Health and Social Policy Families in Society Population Studies, including Demographic Methods Technology, Social Media, Health and Wellbeing Artificial Intelligence, Society & Human Relationships The successful candidate

will teach in one or more of these areas in addition to generalist sociology courses, such as Introduction to Sociology, Research Methods, or Social Theory. Qualifications: Ph.D. in Sociology by start date. PhD in closely allied disciplines such as Criminology or Demography will be considered.
