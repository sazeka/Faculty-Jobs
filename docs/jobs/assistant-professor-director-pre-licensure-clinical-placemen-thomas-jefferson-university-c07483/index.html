Job Details The Assistant Professor and Director, Pre-Licensure Clinical Placements is a faculty leadership role that combines the academic responsibilities of an Assistant Professor with strategic and operational leadership for undergraduate clinical education and placement. The position assumes a leadership role in the planning, implementation, and evaluation of curriculum and student learning experiences in

classroom, laboratory/simulation, and clinical settings while providing oversight of clinical placement processes, clinical partnerships, adjunct clinical instructors, and related systems. The Director serves as a primary liaison among JCN faculty, staff, students, clinical partners, and the broader Jefferson enterprise and supports an environment conducive to learning, strong academic-practice partnerships, and

positive student/faculty relationships. profession through scholarly papers, presentations, publications, grantsmanship, or other appropriate scholarly activity. • Serve on College and/or University committees and demonstrate an emerging leadership role. • Represent the College in appropriate University activities and participate actively in professional organizations at the local, state, or national level. • Participate in College and University

recruitment activities and activities that support the quality of nursing care and the nursing profession.
