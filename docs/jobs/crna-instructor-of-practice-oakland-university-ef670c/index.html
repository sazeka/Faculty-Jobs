Posting Details Position Information Job Title CRNA Instructor of Practice Department 0090 - Nursing Requisition No. 0000808 Job Description Summary The Oakland University School of Nursing is seeking a highly motivated and qualified CRNA applicant to teach graduate students enrolled in the Oakland University-Beaumont Graduate Program of Nurse Anesthesia, beginning August 15,

2026.Applicants will be expected to engage in all aspects of the program, including but not limited to teaching in the classroom, clinical mentoring, guiding the development of clinical doctorate projects, and developing and evaluating curriculum. A minimum of two years in a leadership or faculty role is preferred.Candidates must be expert clinicians and able to demonstrate an ongoing commitment to service. The

ability to effectively communicate and work within a team environment is essential. First consideration will be given to applicants who apply by March 15, 2026, with the continued search until the position is filled. After March 15, it is highly recommended you reach out to the committee chair via email if you are considering applying to learn where we are in the search process. Minimum Qualifications Graduation from

an accredited School of Nursing and accredited School of Nurse Anesthesia. Doctoral degree (DNP, DNAP, PhD) in nursing or related field preferred. A Master’s in nursing or related field may be accepted if the candidate is enrolled in a doctoral program and will complete the program within a year of hire.Current certification/recertification by the National Board of Certification and Recertification for Nurse

Anesthetists. Must hold a current Michigan Registered Nurse license or be eligible for Michigan RN licensure and specialty licensure to practice in Michigan as a Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist.A minimum of two years of practice experience in the specialty of nurse anesthesia. Teaching experience in clinical or didactic areas is preferred.Expertise in academic or hospital administrative leadership
