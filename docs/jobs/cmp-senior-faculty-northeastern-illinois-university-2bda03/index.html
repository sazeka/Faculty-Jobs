Anthony Bruno - Saxophone Acclaimed international touring and recording artist Anthony Bruno is a genre-bending saxophonist and one of Chicago's most sought-after musicians. With an impressive global performance history, he has shared the stage with legends like Wynton Marsalis, The Temptations, Bobby Watson, and Buddy Guy. Since 2020, Bruno has forged a successful path as a solo artist, blending jazz with pop, rock,

and R&B to create deeply soulful, innovative music. With 5 albums and nearly 4 million Spotify streams, his latest release, Blue Velvet, channels groove and mood into a vibrant contemporary jazz soundscape. Bruno has been featured on 14 Spotify Editorial Playlists and performed at iconic festivals like the New York City Winter Jazz Festival, Jazz Plaza Fest (Havana Jazz Festival), Chicago Blues Fest, and Chicago Jazz

Festival. Teaching Philosophy My philosophy is to encourage students to pursue what they love about music and playing the saxophone. Musical interests and goals can vary and my aim is to help each student accomplish their musical ambitions and feel fearlessly creative and confident in their musical endeavors. In lessons, we will strive for the highest playing standards focusing on technique, tone, intonation,

repertoire, reading, improvising, and musicality. With my extensive background in jazz, classical, and pop styles, our lessons can be truly catered to your musical vision. Diego Tapia - Piano Diego Tapia is currently a junior at Northeastern Illinois University and is majoring in piano performance. Diego was born in Argentina and showed musical inclination at a very young age. He learned the fundamentals from his

beloved childhood piano masters, Horo Ansata and Leandro Di Pasquantonio. After high school, Diego continued his musical journey by diving into the world of Indian Classical music where he developed a deep understanding of percussion and traditional ragas (scales) while initiating full-time spiritual practices. He performed for almost a decade in various Hindu temples in South and North America. Diego moved

permanently to the United States in 2013 and received a position as a music instructor at the International Society for Krishna Consciousness, where he founded Vamsi School of Music in an attempt to organize and inspire music education in his community.
