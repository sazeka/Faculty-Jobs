GENERAL DESCRIPTION OF POSITION This position will begin the Fall semester of 2026 meeting at the ASU Beebe Searcy campus. Beginning in the Spring semester of 2027, this class will be taught at the Jacksonville High School campus in Jacksonville, Arkansas.The successful candidate will possess a thorough knowledge of the discipline required to share in the teaching of secondary students. The Welding instructor is

responsible for teaching in the classroom and lab environment. Welding processes to be taught include, but are not limited to, GTAW (Gas Tungsten Arc Welding), GMAW (Gas Metal Arc Welding), FCAW (Fux Core Arc Welding), SMAW (Shielded Metal Arc Welding), and Metal Fabrication, both in the basic and advanced processes. NCCER and AWS D1.1 curriculum will be used in the instruction. The instructor must be able to inspect

welds to grant AWS certifications, under the direct supervision of a qualified AWS CWI, to the students according to the AWS D1.1 and ASME guidelines or be willing to obtain those certifications. It is preferred for the instructor to be able to also provide OSHA safety training. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES: 1. Provide instruction in a classroom and a lab environment.2.
