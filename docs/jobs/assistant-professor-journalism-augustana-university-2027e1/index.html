Assistant Professor, Journalism

Augustana University  ·  Sioux Falls, SD
Assistant ProfessorJournalism

Position summary

Augustana University invites applications for a 9-month, full-time assistant or associate journalism professor in the Department of English and Journalism beginning in August 2027. Responsibilities: The successful applicant will demonstrate a strong commitment to teaching excellence in a liberal arts environment and will specialize in areas including basic newswriting, sports writing, public affairs reporting, media

law, magazine writing and editing, and introduction to digital media. The candidate will be prepared to address the ethical uses of AI in journalism. In addition, the preferred candidate will be in charge of internships, advise publications such as the yearbook, and participate in the following initiatives as set forth by the AU 2030 Vision: Enhance and embolden the academic enterprise through the transformational

power of technology and data, and Keep the journalism major healthy in order to sustain excellence, facilitate growth, and cultivate innovative and impactful teaching, learning, and research. The successful candidate may contribute to courses in the First-Year Seminar program, Via Viking (the core curriculum), and the Civitas Honors Program. The typical load for members of the department is three courses per semester

plus a course in two out of every three January terms. Qualifications: Master&rsquo;s with sufficient professional experience required and completed Ph.D. with professional experience preferred. Evidence of teaching excellence and scholarly productivity expected; willingness to engage in research and service; ability to work collaboratively with colleagues and a commitment to serving the campus and broader

professional community. Salary: Salary and rank commensurate with qualifications and experience; excellent fringe benefits are included. Application Procedure The deadline is December 1st or until the position has been filled. Interested applicants are asked to submit the application materials listed below in one document to our career center portal. If assistance is needed in the application process, please reach

out to humanresources@augie.edu. Official website: augie.edu. Questions regarding the position may be directed to Dr. Daniel Gerling, Chair of the Department of English and Journalism, daniel.gerling@augie.edu.
