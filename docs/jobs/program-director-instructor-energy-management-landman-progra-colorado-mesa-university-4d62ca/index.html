Program Director/Instructor – Energy Management/Landman Program

Position summary

ANNOUNCMENT OF POSITION VACANCY ABOUT THIS POSITION Summary: The Davis School of Business at Colorado Mesa University is seeking candidates for the Program Director/Instructor – Energy Management/Landman Program. Candidates may come from diverse backgrounds, with a range of experiences, achievements, and leadership roles, either within or outside academia. Candidates should have significant industry, business, or

corporate experience relevant to the energy management industry. The position is full-time, tenure-track starting January 2027 (preferred) or August 2027. Colorado Mesa University (CMU) is a state-funded institution that emphasizes quality teaching and research, having embraced the teacher-scholar model. The standard teaching load is 12 credit hours per semester; courses are delivered on-campus, online, and in hybrid

format, though our main focus is on in-person learning on a dynamic residential campus. In addition to teaching, scholarship within your discipline is expected along with advising students and service at the school and university level. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES include the following. Other related duties may be assigned. The Program Director/Instructor of Energy Management/Landman provides strategic

leadership, administration, instruction, and continuous improvement for Colorado Mesa University's nationally recognized Energy Management/Landman Program in the Davis School of Business. The position directs all aspects of the program, including curriculum development, AAPL accreditation, student recruitment, advising, industry engagement, experiential learning, field tours of energy operations, assessment, and

instruction. The Program Director serves as the university's primary liaison to the energy industry, professional organizations, alumni, advisory board members, and community partners to ensure graduates are prepared for careers in the energy sector. The position combines responsibilities typically associated with an academic program director, faculty instructor, academic advisor, recruiter, internship coordinator,

and industry relations manager. Salary: $88,000 - $100,000 Commensurate with education and experience. Excellent health and retirement benefits package: Click here for more information! EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE: REQUIRED: Master’s degree in Business, Energy Management, Petroleum Land Management, Natural Resources, Engineering, Law, or a related field. Significant professional experience in the energy industry.
