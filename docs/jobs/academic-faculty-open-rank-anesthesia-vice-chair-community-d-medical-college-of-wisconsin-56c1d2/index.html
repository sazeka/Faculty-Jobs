Position summary

Position Summary The Department of Anesthesiology at the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW) seeks a board certified / board eligible physician anesthesiologist to serve as Vice Chair of the Community Division. This position provides operational, clinical, and strategic leadership across MCW's community-based anesthesiology practice, which spans two community hospitals and four ambulatory surgery centers (ASCs) in the

greater Milwaukee area. The Vice Chair partners closely with departmental leadership to ensure consistent, high-quality anesthesia care and operational excellence across all community sites. Position Highlights Serve as the senior physician leader for the Community Division, with full accountability for operations, physician recruitment, and budget/financial performance across all community sites. Provide direct

leadership and oversight to two Community Division Leaders: one responsible for the community hospital sites and one responsible for the ambulatory surgery center (ASC) sites. Partner with and provide mentorship to the Medical Directors at each of the two community hospitals and four ASCs, ensuring alignment with departmental and divisional goals. Lead recruitment, onboarding, and retention strategy for

community-based anesthesiology faculty and advanced practice providers (APPs). Own the divisional budget, including staffing models, capital and operating expenses, and financial performance across all sites. Clinical 50% — maintain an active clinical anesthesia practice within the community division. Teaching % and description — support resident/APP education at community sites, as applicable, and facilitate faculty

development at each of the sites. Serve as a key liaison between the Community Division and department/hospital system leadership on quality, safety, and strategic initiatives. Performs other duties as assigned by the Chair or Chair-Designate. Team Structure The Vice Chair, Community Division leads a multi-site leadership team consisting of: 2 Community Division Leaders (1 – Community Hospitals; 1 – Ambulatory

Surgery Centers) Site-based Medical Directors at each of the 2 community hospitals and 4 ASCs Division Director: Pain Division Director of Clinical Operations Community-based anesthesiology faculty and APP staff across all sites Locations This position oversees the following community sites (required): 2 Community Hospitals [Menomonee Falls/ West Bend] 4 Ambulatory Surgery Centers (ASCs) [FMF/FWB/DTS/FSC] Pain
