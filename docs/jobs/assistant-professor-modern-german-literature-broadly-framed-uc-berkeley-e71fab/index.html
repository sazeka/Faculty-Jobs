Position description The Department of German at the University of California, Berkeley, seeks applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor of German, with a focus on modern literature and/or applied language

studies. This position entails broad competence in German-language literature in its historical depth and formal complexity. The successful candidate will teach a broad range of undergraduate and graduate courses, including introductory and German-taught courses for the major. The appointee will also advise undergraduates and graduates, supervise student research, develop a successful and original research program,

and provide service contributions. The appointee will be expected to contribute to the department’s diverse and inclusive community through their research, teaching, and service. We encourage applications from individuals who bring diverse perspectives to higher education. At Berkeley, faculty have many opportunities for interdisciplinary research, teaching, and cooperation. Many faculty are affiliated with centers,

programs, and other departments across campus. As a top public research university and a California institution, Berkeley is committed to creating an environment in which faculty and students of all backgrounds can thrive. The department is committed to addressing the family needs of faculty, including dual career couples and single parents. We are also interested in candidates who have had non-traditional career

paths or who have taken time off for family reasons, or who have achieved excellence in careers outside academia.
