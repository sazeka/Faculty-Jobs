Job Description: The Department of Political Science at the University of Florida invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor in American Politics, with a focus on American institutions, beginning in August

2027. We seek a scholar whose research advances the study of American national political institutions and their development, operation, and consequences. Special preference will go to candidates whose research focuses on the executive and legislative branches. We welcome applications from scholars working across the breadth of American national institutions and especially encourage candidates whose research engages

Historical Political Economy (HPE), American Political Economy (APE), and/or American Political Development (APD). This position is part of a broader effort to strengthen the department’s capacity in historical and political economy approaches. In addition to this position in American Politics, the department anticipates making at least one additional tenure-track appointment in Historical Political Economy. The

field and subfield of subsequent positions will be open. We therefore encourage scholars working in HPE in Comparative Politics, International Relations, or other areas of Political Science to apply. The successful candidate will maintain an active and innovative research program that makes important theoretical and empirical contributions to the study of American politics. We are open to a wide range of

methodological approaches. Candidates whose research would benefit from the University of Florida’s substantial investments in high-performance computing, artificial intelligence, and data-intensive research are especially encouraged to apply. The successful candidate will contribute to the department’s undergraduate and graduate teaching and mentoring missions, including its doctoral methods curriculum. Teaching
