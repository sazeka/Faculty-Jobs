CCB - Instructor of Marketing - 530560 Alabama, United States CBA Non-Tenure-Track/Clinical Faculty Closing at: Dec 31 2026 - 22:55 CST Department/Organization206301 - Marketing RankInstructor Position SummaryThe Department of Marketing invites applications for a full-time, non-tenure track faculty position at the rank of instructor. Detailed Position InformationThe Department of Marketing invites applications for a

full-time, non-tenure track faculty position at the rank of instructor, beginning January 1, 2027. Primary duties include teaching marketing at the graduate and undergraduate levels. Teaching needs include principles of marketing, social media/digital marketing, consumer behavior, retail management, product innovation/managing innovation, marketing analytics, marketing research, sales, strategic marketing, marketing

research, and other areas. Responsibilities will focus mostly on teaching courses face-to-face. There may also be responsibilities for hybrid or online delivery modes. In addition, regularly meeting with students throughout the week related to coursework and student responsibilities is expected. Other responsibilities may be assigned as needed and as appropriate to further advance the program and its curriculum.

Service to the Department, College, and UA is also expected. Reviews of applications will occur on a rolling basis as they are received. Initial interviews will begin as soon as possible, and the position will remain open until filled. While engaging in publishable research activities is not required, for those applicants interested in doing so, the Department will provide support as available. This could take the

form of financial assistance and/or access to the Department’s Behavioral Lab. Minimum QualificationsA master’s degree in business or related field from an accredited institution is required, but some preference will be given to those with a PhD/DBA in marketing or a related field. Preferred QualificationsExperience teaching marketing at the college level is preferred. Industry experience in marketing is also

desirable. Instructions and Required Materials for ApplicationOnly online applications will be accepted. Candidates must apply online at https://careers.ua.edu and attach a curriculum vitae and three references. Required documents not attached at time of application will disqualify application. Review of applicants will begin immediately. The posting is expected to remain open until the position is filled. About the
