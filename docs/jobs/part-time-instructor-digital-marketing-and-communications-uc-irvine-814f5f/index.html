Position description The Division of Continuing Education (DCE) is a self-supporting unit within the University of California. Funding is generated through fees collected from a diverse,

complex, and ever-changing student population. Part-time instructor positions are on a course by course contracted basis. This is NOT a faculty position. This is a recruitment for classroom and online teaching position available in the Digital Marketing and Communications program. We are seeking qualified instructors with current knowledge and work experience in the marketing industry, with a special emphasis on

Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and use of AI and LLM technologies in marketing. Course topics include Business Optimization with AI and LLMs and GEO Strategies for Lead Generation. PLEASE NOTE: Classes are on-ground, online and hybrid.
