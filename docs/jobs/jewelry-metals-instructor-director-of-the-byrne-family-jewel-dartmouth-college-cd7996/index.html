Jewelry/Metals Instructor, Director of the Byrne Family Jewelry Studio (BFJS) is one of the Student Workshops at the Hopkins Center for the Arts. The Student Workshops are a mentored makerspace arts and crafts program that is open to all Dartmouth students. The Jewelry Studio Director is responsible for: safety, planning and programming, teaching design and fabrication processes and techniques, assisting/advising

students in their design and fabrication efforts, supervising student teaching assistants and other shop staff, maintaining equipment, ordering, receiving, and selling materials and all other aspects or managing the program.
