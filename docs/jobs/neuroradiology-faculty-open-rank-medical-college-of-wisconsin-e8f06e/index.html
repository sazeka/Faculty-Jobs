Position summary

The Neuroradiology Section of the Department of Radiology at the Medical College of Wisconsin is seeking a board-certified radiologist with fellowship training in Neuroradiology. Successful candidates should bring strong clinical abilities, enthusiasm, and commitment to MCW’s academic missions and field of Neuro Imaging. We are dedicated to the principles of equity and diversity and particularly encourage those of

diverse backgrounds to apply. Our ideal candidate will have: Desire to excel and collaborate with a talented team of Neuroradiology radiologists that choose to practice, learn, and innovate together. Passion for providing high-quality patient care with Neuroimaging expertise including CT, MRI, PET/CT, and fluoroscopic-guided lumbar punctures and CT myelograms (if onsite). (The position does not include invasive

Neuroangiography or pediatric Neuro responsibilities) Opportunities to participate in scholarly activities which could include education, quality, and/or research. Schedule: Full time Open to remote, hybrid, onsite, daytime or 2nd shift opportunities Team: The Department of Radiology is recognized as a progressive and forward-thinking team that continues to expand with a dynamic multi-site hospital and clinic network

in the Milwaukee metropolitan area. MCW Radiology offers a hybrid model that allows faculty to read and work in both the academic and community settings. The Neuroradiology Section includes 22 faculty who are highly respected as one of the country’s leaders in the clinical application of fMRI, DTI, MR perfusion, ASL, segmental brain volume analysis, and simultaneous integration of the techniques for regional and

whole-brain analysis. The Department of Radiology provides post-graduate fellowships (including a one-year Neuroradiology fellowship with up to five positions per year), a diagnostic radiology residency program, and medical student elective clinical rotations. Access to cutting-edge CT, MR, and US technology with opportunities to work in collaboration with basic research scientists and a major equipment vendor in the

Milwaukee metro area. Collaboration is the foundation for how we work within our department and with others across the organization. Our Radiology team includes 120+ clinical faculty radiologists, 11 imaging science faculty, 15 instructor/fellows, 45 residents, 20 advanced practice providers, and 69 support staff.
