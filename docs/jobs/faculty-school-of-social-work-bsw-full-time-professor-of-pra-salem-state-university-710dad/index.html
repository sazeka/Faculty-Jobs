Faculty, School of Social Work (BSW), Full-Time, Professor of Practice 492969 245 - Social Work Faculty: Non-Tenured Not On Track Professor of Practice On-site $80,000 - $85,000 (for the rank of assistant professor) Overview The School of Social Work at Salem State University invites applications for a Professor of Practice faculty position in our BSW program beginning in September 2027. The ideal candidate will work

as a collaborative member of a faculty dedicated to academic excellence, innovation, equity, and student success. The School of Social Work offers both an MSW and BSW education to approximately 500 students and, was recently named one of the Top 100 Schools of Social Work (#96) by U.S. News and World Report. Salem State University values, celebrates and appreciates diversity in all its forms and endeavors to create a

space that reflects the campus community population which it serves. At Salem State University, great teaching and serious scholarship go hand in hand. We seek faculty who are energized by working with a diverse student body: learners who bring real-world experiences, ambition and drive to every classroom. As a proud Hispanic and Minority-Serving Institution committed to serving all students, we believe that who we

teach is just as important as what we teach. If you value collegiality, continuous growth and the impact higher education can have on individuals and communities alike, we'd love to have you on our faculty. Duties and ResponsibilitiesCandidates are required to perform duties in accordance with the MSCA contract. Duties include teaching, advising, and demonstrated consistent and substantive effort to maintaining

modern skills and knowledge in their field of practice. The standard teaching load is 12 credit hours per semester. The position is subject to available funding. Professor of Practice faculty duties include teaching, liaising with practicum education staff, providing supervision to students in practicum, academic advising, career advising and professional development for students, participation in committee work,

program coordination, and curriculum development guided by practice experience with a focus on equity-minded teaching and transformative learning. Please Note: This is a four year, fixed term position ending on May 31, 2031.
