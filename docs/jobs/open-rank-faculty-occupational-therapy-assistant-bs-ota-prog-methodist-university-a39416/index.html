Open RankOccupational Therapy Assistant (BS-OTA) Program

Position summary

Summary Methodist University, located in Fayetteville, NC, is seeking a 9-month, full-time, open rank BS-OTA faculty member. Methodist University Occupational Therapy Assistant Program is a Bachelor of Science program. Academic rank and salary are commensurate with level of education as well as professional and academic experience. We are seeking applicants passionate about teaching who can add to our team approach

to occupational therapy education. In addition to faculty responsibilities (teaching, scholarship, service) this faculty position will serve as the Coordinator for the BS-OTA Baccalaureate Project. This is a full-time, tenure track position that requires day and occasional evening & weekend availability. Position Description OTA faculty are responsible for leveraging their expertise to develop, maintain and deliver

core education courses to students through: Preparing course plans, materials, and student assessment methods Delivering course instruction & student evaluation Monitoring academic, clinical skill, and professional behavior progress Facilitating interprofessional and intraprofessional learning activities Advising students Support and mentor students in the development, implementation, and evaluation of baccalaureate

project Maintain policies, curriculum, and assessment of baccalaureate project for the BS-OTA Program Contribute to accreditation reporting Establishing and maintaining ongoing professional scholarship and development Maintaining a record of department, university, and community service Other duties: Attend all faculty and departmental meetings. Participation in the selection of applicants for admission to the

program Attendance of educational conferences and workshops. Participation in state and national OT associations, leadership positions are encouraged. Other duties as assigned by the dean or department chair. Applications Applications will be accepted until the position is filled. Qualified candidates are invited to submit a letter of intent, curriculum vitae, teaching philosophy statement, and concise statement

about teaching or other experiences, successes, and challenges in working with a diverse student population. All applications should be submitted through http://www.methodist.edu/positions-available . For questions, contact Melissa Gibson, OTA Program Director at megibson@methodist.edu .
