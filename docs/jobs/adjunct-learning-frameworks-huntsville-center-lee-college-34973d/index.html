adjunct faculty member supports student success through direct instruction within Texas Department of Criminal Justice (TDCJ) facilities. This position requires understanding and following all TDCJ protocols and security procedures while supporting system-impacted students in developing academic skills and promoting pathways to successful reentry and career development.The ideal candidate will demonstrate a

commitment to student-centered teaching and learning, showing flexibility in meeting different student needs while maintaining high academic standards within the constraints of a correctional setting. The successful candidate must be able to adapt teaching strategies and materials to comply with TDCJ regulations and limited technology access, and must be available to travel to TDCJ units within the college's service

area, including units in Huntsville, Navasota, and Dayton. Faculty will be reimbursed for mileage between their primary campus location and TDCJ facilities, per state and college travel policies. Essential Duties & Responsibilities Complete required TDCJ security training and maintain security clearance.Teach assigned Learning Frameworks courses within TDCJ facilities in keeping with approved syllabi, outlines, and

instructional materials.Adapt teaching materials and strategies for use within TDCJ facility constraints.Maintain accurate student records in compliance with Lee College requirements.Submit required student reports to the Admissions & Records Office according to schedule.Direct, evaluate, and assess the learning experience of students in accordance with adopted curricula and approved procedures.Provide appropriate

counsel and guidance to students while maintaining professional boundaries.Create an inclusive, welcoming classroom environment that supports diverse learning needs.Monitor student progress and provide timely intervention for at-risk students.Incorporate career exploration and planning appropriate for system-impacted students.Participate in the college's early alert system.Maintain required documentation for travel
