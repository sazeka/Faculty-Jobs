Purpose of Role Pre-Teaching Assistant Instructor in Behavioral Science Department, Daemen University Daemen University is seeking applications for a Pre-Teaching Assistant Instructor position in the Behavioral

This individual will serve in a Pre-Teaching

Assistant role to a faculty member in the Department of Behavioral Science. Pre-TA Activities Under the mentorship of the course instructor, the Pre-TA will learn skills that bridge theory and online instructional delivery in a practice-based manner, including observing course structure andinstructor-student interactions. Instructor-student interactions can be synchronous, through student support groups, and

asynchronous, such as emails that are specific to course learning. Responsibility of the Pre-TA The Pre-TA may facilitate asynchronous discussion boards, provide targeted feedback on low-stakes assignments, and support small-group or individual student engagement through messaging or virtual office hours under the supervision of the primary instructor. Additional responsibilities may include monitoring student

participation, assisting with course organization and content accessibility, and engaging in structured reflection (e.g., weekly journals) to analyze effective online teaching practices.
