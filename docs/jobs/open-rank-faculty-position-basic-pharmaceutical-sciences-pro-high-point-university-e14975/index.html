Open Rank faculty position, Basic Pharmaceutical Sciences – Promotion Path

High Point University  ·  Department of Basic Pharmaceutical Sciences  ·  High Point University, NC
Basic Pharmaceutical Sciences

Position summary

The Department of Basic Pharmaceutical Sciences in the Fred Wilson School of Pharmacy at High Point University invites applications for a full-time, 12-month promotion path (non-tenure track) faculty position. The individual in this position will be primarily responsible for teaching PharmD students but will also be expected to contribute to new knowledge through scholarship and provide service to the Fred Wilson

School of Pharmacy, the University, and the greater High Point community. The estimated distribution of effort for this individual at program maturity (i.e., when a full faculty cohort is hired) will be 60-70% teaching, 20-30% service, and 10-15% scholarship. Academic rank will be commensurate with experience. Applicants with areas of expertise/interest within the broad discipline of pharmaceutical sciences are

welcome to apply. Areas of priority include physiology or pharmacology. Instructional duties may include both lecture or laboratory settings. Interested candidates should apply by submitting the following documents as a single PDF file: • Cover letter describing your professional goals, relevant academic preparation, and experience • Current curriculum vitae • Statement of teaching/mentoring philosophy (one page) •

Statement of scholarship interests and philosophy (one page) • Contact information for two professional references For additional information regarding this position, please contact Dr. Kent Stewart, Interim Chair and Associate Professor of Basic Pharmaceutical Sciences at kstewar1@highpoint.edu. QUALIFICATIONS: Education: Pharm.D., M.D., or Ph.D. in pharmacology, physiology, toxicology, biochemistry, or related

biomedical science field is required Experience and Training: Postdoctoral experience and strong academic credentials in areas of relevance to pharmacy, such as pharmacology or biochemistry is strongly preferred. Medical or pharmacy academic teaching experience is strongly preferred, and priority will be given to candidates with prior teaching experience in one of these settings. Knowledge, Skills, Ability: Strong

verbal and written communication skills are required Ability to multi-task and work cooperatively with others is required ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS: Develop, coordinate, and teach content within the Fred Wilson School of Pharmacy integrated curriculum as appointed by the Department Chair.
