Position summary

ASSISTANT PROFESSOR OF RELIGION RESPONSIBILITIES AND QUALIFICATIONS The University of the Ozarks invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Religion position, beginning in August 2027. Candidates should be ABD or have earned a Ph.D. in Religion by the start of fall classes in August 2027. Candidates with a master’s degree and a record of teaching excellence will be considered. The ideal applicant

will work as part of a strong team supporting students in our Religion major and minor, as well as in our new Rural Church Leadership minor. We seek a teacher-scholar whose work will strengthen the Religion program while contributing significantly to the University’s growing commitments to community engagement, rural vitality, and the preparation of students for leadership in churches and other community-serving

organizations. The position will have a distinctive connection to the Town Square Collaborative, an interdisciplinary initiative that brings the University’s resources into partnership with rural communities, congregations, and community organizations. Working with colleagues and external partners, the successful candidate will help develop educational experiences that prepare students for the challenges facing rural

communities and to exercise thoughtful, ethical, and collaborative leadership within them. Primary responsibilities include teaching courses focused on Religion. Candidates who can teach some or all of the following will be given preference: Bible in American Culture, Introduction to Theology, Asian Philosophies and Religions, Judaism, Christianity and Islam, and Approaches to the study of Religion. The teaching load

for the position is 24 credits per academic year. University of the Ozarks values personal attention and collegiality; thus, familiarity with and understanding of a small, residential campus are highly desirable. Candidates should demonstrate a strong interest in working with a culturally diverse student body. Excellent communication skills and support of the University's mission are also required.
