Department: Mechanical Engineering Job Summary: Performs the function of teaching with renewable contracts. Not eligible for tenure. Job Description: Position Description The Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Mississippi (Ole Miss), Oxford, Mississippi, invites applications for a full-time Instructor of Mechanical Engineering. This teaching-focused, non-tenure-track faculty appointment will

strengthen hands-on undergraduate instruction in mechanical engineering laboratories and related engineering courses. The successful candidate will teach and support hands-on undergraduate laboratories, including structures and dynamics, materials, thermal-fluids, instrumentation, design, or related mechanical engineering laboratory experiences. The Instructor will work closely with faculty course coordinators,

laboratory faculty directors, department staff, and the machine shop supervisor to support safe, effective, well-maintained, and practice-oriented instructional laboratories. Primary Responsibilities Teach, coordinate, or support hands-on undergraduate mechanical engineering laboratories, with possible assignments in Structures and Dynamics Lab, Materials Lab, Thermal-Fluids Lab, instrumentation, design, or related

laboratory courses. Teach undergraduate mechanical engineering or engineering science courses as assigned, based on departmental needs and the candidate's expertise. Develop, revise, and assess laboratory manuals, experiments, safety procedures, demonstrations, project-based activities, and course materials. Work collaboratively with faculty laboratory directors, course coordinators, department staff, and the machine

shop supervisor to support laboratory safety, equipment readiness, maintenance coordination, and continuous improvement. Mentor undergraduate students in laboratory practice, engineering problem solving, professional conduct, and safe use of equipment and facilities. Contribute to ABET-related assessment, curriculum coordination, laboratory documentation, department service, and other continuous-improvement

activities. Join the Ole Miss Family — Build Your Legacy Here! Job Responsibilities: Required Qualifications Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering or a closely related engineering field by the appointment start date. Relevant laboratory, instructional, technical, or hands-on engineering experience. Demonstrated ability or strong potential to teach and supervise hands-on undergraduate engineering laboratory
