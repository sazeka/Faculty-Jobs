Assistant, Associate or Full Professor of Nephrology, Director of Transplantation, Kidney & Pancreas 501523 Health Science Campus College Toledo, OH Academic Full-Time Healthcare Positions Faculty - Regular Job Description: UToledo Health and the University of Toledo College of Medicine and Life Sciences invites applications to the Department of Medicine, Division of Nephrology as an Assistant, Associate, or Full

Professor and the Director of Transplantation. The Transplant Nephrologist will provide comprehensive, patient-centered care for kidney and kidney-pancreas transplant candidates and recipients across the continuum of transplantation. The faculty member will join a multidisciplinary transplant program and contribute to clinical excellence, education, scholarship, quality improvement, and program growth. The successful

candidate will work collaboratively with transplant surgeons, nephrologists, advanced practice providers, coordinators, pharmacists, nurses, social workers, dietitians, histocompatibility specialists, and other institutional partners. Essential Duties & Responsibilities: Evaluate kidney and kidney-pancreas transplant candidates, including assessment of medical suitability, immunologic and cardiovascular risk, and

optimization before transplantation. Provide inpatient and outpatient care for transplant recipients, including immediate post-transplant management, long-term graft surveillance, immunosuppression management, infection prevention, and treatment of transplant-related complications. Participate in the evaluation and longitudinal care of living kidney donors in collaboration with the independent living donor team and

applicable regulatory standards. Manage complex conditions including delayed graft function, rejection, recurrent kidney disease, post-transplant malignancy, opportunistic infection, and medication-related toxicity. Participate in transplant nephrology call, ambulatory clinics, multidisciplinary selection conferences, and organ-offer consultation according to the division's coverage model. Perform or supervise

procedures within the physician's training and privileges, which may include ultrasound-guided native and transplant kidney biopsy. Coordinate transitions of care with referring nephrologists, dialysis programs, primary care clinicians, and community partners. Maintain timely, accurate documentation and meet institutional expectations for access, productivity, quality, patient experience, and professional conduct.
