We adhere closely to the NPA’s guidelines for best practices in postdoc training. We work closely with our local NPA chapter, the VCU Postdoc Association, to make this a welcoming and productive place to complete your training! The Department of

Epidemiology at Virginia Commonwealth University (VCU) School of Public Health invites applications for a highly motivated Postdoctoral Research Fellow with expertise in metabolomics and/or lipidomics to join an innovative research program focused on menopause, cardiometabolic aging, and cardiovascular disease (CVD) prevention in women. The successful candidate will work under the mentorship of Dr. Samar R. El

Khoudary, Chair of the Department of Epidemiology and an internationally recognized leader in women's cardiovascular health and menopause research. A central focus of this position will be participation in SHE-HEALS (Stopping Atherosclerotic Heart and Vessel Disease in Women), a newly established international research network bringing together investigators from 13 institutions across seven countries. SHE-HEALS

seeks to transform cardiovascular prevention in women by identifying early vascular changes associated with menopause and investigating opportunities for earlier detection and intervention. Dr. El Khoudary leads a subproject within SHE-HEALS investigating how metabolomic and lipidomic profiles in midlife women are associated with changes in vascular health across the menopause transition. The postdoctoral fellow will

play a central scientific and analytical role in this project, applying advanced methods for metabolomics and lipidomics data processing, analysis, and interpretation to identify molecular signatures associated with vascular aging and subclinical atherosclerosis.
