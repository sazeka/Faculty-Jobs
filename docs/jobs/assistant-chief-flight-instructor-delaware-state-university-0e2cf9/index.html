Assistant Chief Flight Instructor Print Assistant Chief Flight Instructor Salary $66,000.00 Annually Location DSU Main Campus, 1200 N Dupont Highway, Dover Job Type Professional Job Number 2026-00483 Division Provost Office Department Aviation Opening Date 09/23/2026 Description Benefits Questions General Description of the Position The Assistant Chief Flight Instructor assists the Chief Flight Instructor in the

safe, efficient, and compliant operation of Delaware State University's FAA Part 141 flight training program. The position provides flight instruction and evaluation, supervises and standardizes flight instructors, monitors student progression, supports regulatory compliance and records management, and actively participates in the Aviation Program's Safety Management System (SMS). Essential Functions & Required

Knowledge, Skills and Abilities The following are the functions essential to performing this job:Assist the Chief Flight Instructor in overseeing and managing flight and ground training in accordance with FAA regulations, approved Training Course Outlines, University policies, and departmental procedures.Conduct flight and ground instruction, stage checks, proficiency checks, and other evaluations ground and flight

courses as authorized.Assist with the supervision, scheduling, standardization, mentoring, and performance evaluation of Certified Instructors.Monitor student flight-training progression, training quality, lesson completion, stage-check performance, and establish departmental progression.Assist with daily flight operations, including coordination of students, instructors, aircraft, dispatch, maintenance, weather, and

other resources affecting safe and efficient training.Participate in the Aviation Program's Safety Management System (SMS), including hazard identification, safety reporting, risk assessment, safety assurance, corrective actions, safety promotion, and monitoring operational safety trends.Assist in maintaining compliance with applicable FAA regulations, including 14 CFR Parts 61, 91, and 141, and support FAA

inspections, audits, certification activities, and maintenance of required training records.Assist with instructor and student safety briefings, standardization meetings, operational meetings, and continuous improvement initiatives.Support student recruitment, retention, orientation, mentoring, and other Aviation Program and University initiatives.Perform other duties related to flight training operations as assigned
