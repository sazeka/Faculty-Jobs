HVCC offers more than 80 degree and certificate programs in three schools: Business and Liberal Arts; Health Sciences; and Science, Technology,

Engineering and Math (STEM). Hudson Valley is one of 30 community colleges in the State University of New York (SUNY) system and serves nearly 9,000 students each year. Hudson Valley has an extension center in Malta, NY known as HVCC North and

sponsors the Capital District Educational Opportunity Center in Troy, NY.
