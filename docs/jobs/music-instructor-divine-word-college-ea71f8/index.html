Music Instructor

Divine Word College  ·  Divine Word College, IA
AdjunctMusic

Position summary

Music Instructor Divine Word College, a Roman Catholic College Seminary of the Divine Word Missionaries (SVD), invites applications for a pool of Adjunct Music Instructors within the Department of Interdisciplinary Studies for in-person, individual instruction of Applied Music courses as needed, beginning in Fall 2026 Semester. Instructors in guitar, voice, or clarinet are encouraged to apply. These are 1 credit

courses, with an end-of-term recital required. Instruction must take place between 9 am and 5 pm, as arranged by the student and the instructor. The initial contract is for one semester and is renewable thereafter on a semester basis, depending on need. Applications will be considered on an as-needed basis, according to course needs/enrollment. Qualifications: Successful applicants will possess a Master’s degree or

higher in Music Performance, Pedagogy or a closely related field, from an accredited institution of higher learning, and will have previous teaching experience. Applicants must provide evidence of effective teaching, commitment to mentoring undergraduate students, ability to work collaboratively with faculty and administration, and experience in an intercultural classroom environment. Divine Word College encourages

applications from candidates of minority backgrounds who will contribute to the rich cultural diversity of the College, and who can commit themselves to the College’s Mission of educating men and women for missionary service as religious priests, brothers, sisters, and lay persons. Send a cover letter describing teaching experience, curriculum vitae, sample syllabi, and contact information for three professional

references to Dr. Cathleen Cleary email: ccleary@dwci.edu or to Divine Word College, P.O Box 380 Epworth IA 52045.​​​​​​​ Application deadline: Review of applications will begin immediately and will continue until the position is filled. Start date for Fall 2026 is 10 August, 2026. Salary is commensurate with candidate's qualifications and experience. Divine Word College is an affirmative action/equal opportunity

employer. We'd Love to Hear From You Whether you'd like to become a student here or want to support our mission Contact Us Receive Our Newsletter 563-876-3353
