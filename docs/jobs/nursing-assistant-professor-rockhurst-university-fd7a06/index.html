values of nursing. The College of Nursing is in one of two academic schools housed in Saint Luke&rsquo;s College of Nursing and Health Sciences. The College of Nursing is known for its innovative teaching using a broad range of active learning experiences, close faculty-student interactions, and our strong mission focused on service, advocacy, and leadership. The faculty have established close relationships with many
