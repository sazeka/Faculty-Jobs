Conducted under the auspices of the Dominican Friars, Providence College is a Catholic, Dominican, liberal arts institution of higher education and a community committed to academic excellence in pursuit of the truth, growth in virtue, and service of God and neighbor. We seek candidates who can affirm and contribute to our mission. Providence College is also an equal opportunity employer and is committed to fostering

a Beloved Community— an environment that reflects the rich diversity of the human family and is marked by justice, charity and the promotion of the common good and flourishing of all. We invite applications from qualified individuals who possess the experience, knowledge and commitment to develop the diverse learning environment envisioned as part of the Beloved Community. Women and minority candidates are strongly

encouraged to apply. The College’s Non-Discrimination policy can be found Notice of Non-Discrimination (PDF document) (Download PDF reader) Overview Providence College invites applications for a tenure-track position at the assistant professor level, beginning August 1, 2027, with scholarly expertise at the intersection of Communication and Public and Community Service Studies and to be jointly appointed within these

departments. We seek candidates with a Ph.D. in Communication, Cultural Studies, Media Studies, or a related discipline who also have demonstrated knowledge of, and experience in executing, community engaged research and/or participatory action research. Outstanding candidates will promote a leading vision for community-engaged research that foregrounds cutting edge developments in the media environment, bringing

innovative approaches to real-world challenges, and working with community partners on critical concerns. Engaged academics with an interest in applying theories of communication to their communities, using social media, digital imaging, artistic platforms, video and blogging channels, and similar contemporary resources to support organizational initiatives, are encouraged to apply.
