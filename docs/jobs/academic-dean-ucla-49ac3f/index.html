accepted until this date. Position description Geffen Academy seeks an experienced, collaborative, and student-centered educational leader to serve as Academic Dean. The Academic Dean is an essential part of Geffen Academy’s leadership team, works under the guidance of the Associate Head of School (AHoS), and supervises the Manager, Student Counseling and Learning Services, including oversight of the Learning

Specialist and Counseling departments. The Academic Dean’s work focuses on leading a streamlined and responsive academic experience for all students. The Academic Dean works directly with Division Directors, Dean of Faculty, and Department Chairs on academic programs; partners with AHoS on the design of the master schedule; collaborates with Educators to identify and support students in need of academic intervention;

communicates with parents/guardians on student support; creates academic support and remediation plans when necessary; serves as an academic advisor for students; and supervises UCLA BruinCorps. Anticipated start date is July 2027. Essential Responsibilities Academic Program Grades 6-12 In partnership with Heads of School, engage in strategic visioning for academic programs and experiences. In consultation with

Division Directors and Dean of Faculty, directly support the Associate Head of School and Department Chairs on departmental staffing plans and course catalog updates and revisions. In partnership with AHoS, registrar, and Director of Academic Advising & College Guidance (AACG), develop annual academic advising materials, communications, and master schedule. Serve as an Academic Advisor and partner to the Director of

AACG for issues regarding graduation requirements, prerequisites, transfer credits, and academic advising. Review transcripts of incoming students to assess past academic experiences and to ensure maximization of Geffen Academy’s course offerings and programs when appropriate.
