Position summary

Assistant Professor of Psychology Cottey College, a private liberal arts and sciences college for women, invites applications for a full- time, tenure-track Assistant Professor of Psychology position, beginning August 2027. We are seeking candidates with experience teaching student-centered courses in Developmental Psychology, with preference given to candidates who can also teach a course in Psychology of Women or

Sports Psychology. A Ph.D. In Psychology is preferred, along with a strong record of undergraduate teaching; however, ABD candidates will be considered and are encouraged to apply. This position includes opportunities to become involved with undergraduate research in our dedicated Psychology Lab space, which includes computers and an observation room. We are seeking candidates who will collaborate with our effective,

dynamic department that, in its short time, has successfully sent graduates to careers in behavioral health and counseling, as well as several graduate programs and research laboratories. In addition to teaching responsibilities, faculty advise and mentor undergraduates, sponsor student organizations, and participate in a tight-knit campus community. Cottey College, located in Nevada, Missouri, has offered young

women the opportunity to learn and grow since 1884. It encompasses a diverse group of students, faculty, and staff, including first generation students, students awarded Pell Grants, students of color, international students, faculty of color, and international faculty. The successful candidate will have demonstrated favorable results in working with diverse populations. A Cottey education is guided by three threads:

leadership, social responsibility, and global awareness. Cottey College helps each student excel as learners, leaders, and global citizens - providing a comprehensive and empowering experience in an intimate academic setting that transforms them to reach their highest potential. Review of applications begins upon receipt and continues until the position is filled. Send cover letter, curriculum vitae, statement of

teaching philosophy that also addresses teaching a diverse student body, copies of unofficial transcripts, and contact information for three references. Application materials should be submitted by email as PDF documents to Dr. Karla McCain, VPAA, Cottey College, Nevada, MO 64772. E-mail: acadaffairs@cottey.edu; Phone: 417-667-8181, ext. 2128. Applicants must be eligible to work in the U.S. without sponsorship.
