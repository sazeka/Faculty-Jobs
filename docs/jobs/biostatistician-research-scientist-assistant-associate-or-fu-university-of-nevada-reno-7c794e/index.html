Job Description The University of Nevada, Reno School of Medicine (UNR Med) seeks a doctoral-level Biostatistician (Research Scientist, Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor), non-tenure-track. to serve as a faculty member within the Translational Research Center. This position will play a critical role in advancing the

institution’s clinical, translational, and medical education research mission by providing expertise in study design, statistical analysis, and research methodology, while contributing to the education and mentorship of medical students, residents, and faculty investigators. The successful candidate will integrate biostatistical rigor into clinical, translational, and medical education research initiatives, support

student scholarship, and contribute to the delivery and continuous improvement of the biostatistics curriculum within the MD program. Primary Responsibilities: Teaching and Curriculum Development Contribute to the design, delivery, and evaluation of biostatistics instruction within the MD curriculum, including pre-clerkship and clerkship phases Participate in the Evidence and Systems-Based Practice (ESP) or

equivalent curriculum thread Develop teaching materials, case-based learning modules, and applied statistical exercises Support integration of biostatistics into clinically relevant learning activities and assessments Evaluate student performance and contribute to curriculum improvement efforts Research Collaboration and Support Provide biostatistical expertise for clinical, translational and medical education

research projects across department Collaborate with clinical faculty to: ​Design research protocols Develop statistical analysis plans Interpret findings for publication and presentation Serve as a core resource for: ​Study design and methodology consultation Data analysis and statistical modeling Facilitate manuscript and grant preparation Partner with the Translational Research Center and affiliated research
