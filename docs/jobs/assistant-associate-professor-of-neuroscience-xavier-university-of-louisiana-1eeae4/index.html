Posting Details Posting Summary Position Title Assistant/Associate Professor of Neuroscience FLSA Department Office Of Academic Affairs Job Summary Xavier Ochsner College of Medicine invites applications for a faculty position in Neuroscience at the Assistant or Associate Professor

level. This position offers an opportunity to join a growing biomedical sciences team and contribute to the development, delivery, assessment, and continuous improvement of neuroscience, behavioral health, and/or neuroanatomy instruction within an innovative, integrated medical curriculum. The successful candidate will bring relevant expertise and strong potential for growth as a medical educator across organ-based

systems courses, with enthusiasm for collaborative teaching, curriculum development, and student-centered learning. The College of Medicine welcomes applicants from diverse academic and professional backgrounds, including those with experience in undergraduate, graduate, health professions, medical education, clinical, or behavioral health settings; prior medical school teaching experience is not required. The

successful candidate will contribute to neuroscience, behavioral health, and/or neuroanatomy instruction while developing broad teaching responsibilities within an integrated biomedical sciences curriculum. Faculty will work closely with interdisciplinary colleagues to support active learning, curricular innovation, and the academic success of future physicians.
