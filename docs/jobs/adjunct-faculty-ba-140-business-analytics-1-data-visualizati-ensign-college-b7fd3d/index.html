Adjunct Faculty - BA 140 Business Analytics 1 — Data Visualization About the Role Ensign College is seeking an adjunct faculty member to teach Business Analytics 1 — Data Visualization within the Business Analytics Certificate program. This is a foundational course that introduces students to the principles and practice of data visualization for business decision-making. Students will learn to connect to data

sources, transform and model data, build meaningful visualizations, and present findings through interactive dashboards — using both Power BI and Tableau as their primary tools. Rather than training students on one platform, the goal is to develop visualization literacy: the ability to choose the right tool for the task, design for an audience, and tell a clear story with data. Students will gain hands-on proficiency

in both Power BI and Tableau, learning to evaluate the strengths of each in different business contexts. The ideal candidate brings both technical depth and teaching instinct. We’re looking for someone who can help students think critically about what their data is telling them. About Ensign College Ensign College is an institution of higher education founded and sponsored by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day

Saints. The college’s mission is to develop capable and trusted disciples of Jesus Christ. Students at Ensign learn through a “Prepare, Act, Demonstrate” framework designed to make them active agents in their own learning. Faculty are expected to teach in a way that develops both professional capability and personal character. This means creating learning experiences that are practical, engaging, and grounded in

real-world application. Teaching Responsibilities Deliver in-person instruction for Business Analytics 1 during assigned 7-week block sessions. Teach data visualization principles that transcend any single tool: choosing chart types for different data stories, designing for clarity and audience, and evaluating whether a visualization supports sound decision-making. Guide students through hands-on projects in both

Power BI and/or Tableau, including data connection, transformation, calculated fields/measures, data modeling, and interactive dashboard design. Help students understand when and why to use each tool, developing the judgment to select the right platform for a given business problem. Supplement structured learning modules with live instruction, real-world datasets, and applied exercises that deepen understanding
