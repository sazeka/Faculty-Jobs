Clinical Instructor of Anatomy & Physiology Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Details Position Information Job Title Clinical Instructor of Anatomy & Physiology Position Number 997109 Department Health Science Professions Salary $50,000 Remote No Job Summary The Department of Health Science Professions at Angelo State University in San Angelo, Texas is seeking applicants for a

non-tenure-eligible Clinical Instructor position to teach Anatomy and Physiology. Candidates must have prior college teaching experience and a minimum of 18 graduate hours in Biological Sciences or Anatomy and Physiology–related coursework; specific discipline is open. The selected candidate will collaborate with faculty on course instruction, curriculum development, and laboratory activities. Essential Duties

Teaching is the primary responsibility for this position, along with additional service and departmental duties as assigned. This is a nine-month appointment with the possibility of teaching summer courses. The role requires flexibility to teach morning, afternoon, and evening courses and labs.The selected candidate will teach undergraduate courses in Health Science Professions for both majors and non-majors, with a

focus on human anatomy and physiology. Responsibilities include collaborating with faculty on course design, curriculum development, and laboratory activities. The position also involves participation in departmental, college, and university initiatives, as well as advising students and serving on committees. Knowledge, Skills and Abilities The successful applicant will demonstrate strong written and verbal

communication skills, along with excellent interpersonal and organizational abilities. A commitment to creating engaging and effective learning experiences is essential.Candidates should show a strong dedication to excellence in teaching and service, with a commitment to maintaining high professional standards in all settings. A solid foundation in anatomy and physiology is required to effectively teach and assess

rigorous undergraduate-level courses.The position requires strong instructional skills and the ability to work collaboratively with colleagues in a professional and collegial environment. Engagement in community service and ongoing professional development is encouraged. Minimum Qualifications Earned M.S. in Biological Sciences or related field from anaccredited institution with college-level teaching experience
