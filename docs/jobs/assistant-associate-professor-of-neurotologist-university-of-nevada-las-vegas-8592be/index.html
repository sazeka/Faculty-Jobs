Job Description The Kirk Kerkorian School of Medicine at

University of Nevada, Las Vegas invites applications for Assistant/Associate/Professor of Neurotologist. Role of the Position The Department of Otolaryngology at Kirk Kerkorian School of Medicine at University of Nevada, Las Vegas, seeks a full-time Neurotologist, at the Assistant/Associate/Professor level. This is an exciting opportunity to join a growing academic Otolaryngology practice and Otolaryngology residency

training program.The successful candidate will be joining a senior neurotologist with a well-established and busy practice providing tertiary otologic/neurotologic care. The division of neurotology is supported by two full-time audiologists who perform comprehensive diagnostic hearing and vestibular testing and programming at the implant center. Responsibilities include patient care, research and teaching for

Otolaryngology residents, other residencies, and medical students; surgical practice and teaching in both inpatient and outpatient care settings, and clinical and/or basic science research. Must take calls and obtain privileges at several hospitals in the Las Vegas area. Profile of the Kirk Kerkorian School of Medicine The Kirk Kerkorian School of Medicine at UNLV has been Transforming Healthcare in Southern Nevada

since 2017. Our mission is to serve our patients, students, medical professionals, and community as a transformational force for improving healthcare in Nevada. Our faculty, students, residents, and fellows have had their research published in top-tier peer-reviewed journals.
