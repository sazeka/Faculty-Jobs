Job Description Job Details From time to time, the Department of Psychiatry & Human Behavior has openings for faculty of all rank and welcome all letters of interest. Strong senior mentorship and interesting clinical, education and research opportunities become

available in the following areas: Outpatient Psychiatry Opportunity to join a growing outpatient clinic with significant opportunities to teach medical students and supervise senior psychiatry residents. Full-time position preferred, but part time would be considered, focused primarily on outpatient medical management with some psychotherapy for a diverse population of patients. Clinic includes a thriving group of

therapists, nurse practitioners and nursing and administrative support. Inpatient Psychiatry Full-time position for an inpatient-unit attending, responsible for clinical, educational, and administrative aspects of our 16-bed Adult Inpatient Psychiatry Unit, including quality improvement and meeting accreditation standards. Daily clinical coverage of the unit is shared with multiple psychiatrists. The unit is a key

clinical training site that includes Jefferson medical students, residents and fellows. Attending faculty responsibility includes evaluation of new patients, ongoing care, management of comorbid substance abuse, treating comorbid medical issues with appropriate consultation, and coordination of discharge with social work. Outpatient work, in addition to inpatient coverage, is dependent on candidate’s blended

interests and expertise. Primary hospital site is the Thomas Jefferson University Hospital, Center City Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Addiction Psychiatry Full-time position for a Psychiatrist with interest in addictions and academic medicine. This position involves providing psychiatric care to patients with substance use disorders within a sophisticated network of established substance use disorders programs, all

with experienced multi-disciplinary staffing. Board eligibility or board certification by the ABPN or ABPM for addiction psychiatry or addiction medicine is preferred. Consultation-Liaison (CL): Hospital Psychiatric Services / Outpatient Psychiatry Full-time position focused on mainly inpatient consultation with some outpatient clinical work dependent on candidate’s interests and expertise.
