Position Type: Assistant Professor in Animal Behavior or Behavioral Ecology Position Type: Regular Salary Range: $105,122 to $116,846 Purpose: Assistant Professor in Animal Behavior or Behavioral Ecology Santa Clara University, a Jesuit, Catholic university, seeks an Assistant Professor in the area of Animal Behavior or Behavioral Ecology (specifically, the Michael and Elizabeth Valeriote Assistant Professor of

Biology). This appointment includes annual discretionary funds to support professional development and scholarly activities for the duration of the probationary period. We seek to fill this full-time tenure-track position starting September 1, 2027. SCU is a welcoming and inclusive community of teaching scholars whose work is grounded in the University’s fundamental values . Those values, which include academic

excellence, social justice and community engagement, are infused with a culture of care that defines our community, upholding the dignity of all. The University draws on the many resources of the greater Bay Area, including Silicon Valley, where we are located. The successful candidate will be expected to teach, engage in independent research, and contribute service to the Biology Department, College of Arts and

Sciences, the University, and profession. The position comes with comprehensive benefits , including medical, dental, vision, and life insurance, and University contributions toward retirement. Recognizing the high cost of housing in the local area, there are two University housing assistance programs : Rental assistance to ease the transition into the local housing market, and housing purchase assistance for

tenure-stream faculty. Required Qualifications A Ph.D. in Animal Behavior, Behavioral Ecology, Zoology, or a related field is required. Ph.D must be completed at the time of submission of application. At least 12 months of postdoctoral experience is required, as it pertains to the candidate’s ability to develop an independent research program. Evidence of high-quality research and potential for ongoing productivity

involving undergraduate students is expected. Demonstrated potential for effective and inclusive teaching and mentoring of a diverse undergraduate student population is expected. Experience with inclusive pedagogical practices that promote access and academic success for all students is encouraged. Responsibilities.
