Instructor/Lecturer Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Details Posting Details Logo Institution South Dakota State University Working Title Instructor/Lecturer Posting Number F01892P Department SDSU-Graduate Nursing Physical Location of Position (City) Unspecified Posting Text South Dakota State University is offering an exciting career opportunity to join the College of Nursing as an

Instructor/Lecturer. This position will serve as the Nurse Midwife Faculty and Director of the new midwifery program. This position will be located in either Brookings, Sioux Falls, or Rapid City. This position is a 12-month, full-time, benefits-eligible position. The SDSU College of Nursing, located in Brookings, Rapid City, and Sioux Falls, has a long history of exceptional nursing education and this is recognized

through its designation as a Center of Excellence in Nursing Education by the National League for Nursing. The college’s fully accredited Healthcare Simulation Center encompassing all three sites is further evidence of our commitment to high quality education for all students. SDSU Nursing’s history of excellence, innovation and achievement across its graduate and undergraduate programs provides an excellent

foundation for growth and innovation.Must be authorized to work in the U.S. Sponsorship is not available for this position.KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: Lead accreditation activities, such as ACME self-studies; Annual reports; Site visit preparation and follow-up (done in collaboration with Associate Dean for Academic Programs). Monitor compliance with accreditation standards. Track program outcomes such as graduation rates,

certification exam pass rates, employment outcomes. Facilitate faculty meetings and committee work. Oversee clinical experiences- assist in securing clinical placements; selection and orientation of clinical preceptors. BENEFITS: SDState offers a wide range of excellent benefits including medical, dental, and flexible benefits, retirement plans, compensation packages, paid holidays, and vacation leave. We offer a

generous retirement plan that includes 6% matching, 10 hours of annual leave each month, 9.34 hours of sick leave each month, and eleven paid holidays. Employees also have access to internal and state-sponsored training as well as reduced tuition for state-supported courses at all State of South Dakota academic institutions. Relocation benefits are available per university’s guidelines. QUALIFICATIONS MINIMUM
