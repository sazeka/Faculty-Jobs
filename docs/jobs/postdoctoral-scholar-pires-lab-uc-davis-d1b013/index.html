Position description Job characteristics: We are seeking for a Post-Doctoral Scholar that will work in veterinary epidemiology, animal health, biosecurity and food safety in the laboratory of Dr. Alda Pires, Department of Population and Health and Reproduction at the

University of California, Davis.
