POSITION SPECIFICS The Cocoziello Institute of Real Estate Innovation at The Pennsylvania State University invites applications for a three-year Assistant Research Professor position with expertise in digital twins for the built environment (buildings and

infrastructure), building information modeling, and data analytics for buildings. The position will be located on the University Park campus of Penn State. This position has a possible extension contingent on performance review and continued funding. The position has 60% research, 30% project management, and 10% research advising responsibilities. The successful candidate will manage the development of a robust

digital twin effort, focusing on creating and implementing a digital twin strategy for the Penn State University Park campus. The candidate will collaborate with Institute faculty and industry partners, and guide graduate/undergraduate students on digital twin research and development activities. The successful candidate is expected to secure external funding for their research and disseminate research findings

through peer-reviewed journals, conferences, and other effective channels. The individual should be highly motivated and knowledgeable of relevant literature, be able to lead their own individual projects, write grant proposals, and contribute to collaborative research efforts.
