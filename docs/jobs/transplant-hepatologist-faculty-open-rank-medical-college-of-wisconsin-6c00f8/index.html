Transplant Hepatologist - Faculty (Open Rank)

Medical College of Wisconsin  ·  Milwaukee, WI
FacultyHepatology

Position summary

The Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology at Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW) seeks a board certified or board eligible physician to join our expanding Division. This position is located in Milwaukee, WI at Froedtert Memorial Lutheran Hospital (FMLH). We are comprised of a nationally & internationally renowned, dedicated group of faculty and staff with a strong commitment to clinical care. This is an amazing

opportunity to enhance your academic career while mentoring others in a vibrant & growing academic transplant center and expansive transplant service line. Join us in transforming health care and connecting communities to the best of what academic medicine offers by providing exceptional hepatology and transplant care to diverse communities in the greater Milwaukee, Wisconsin region! Position Highlights: Full time

faculty position within the MCW Division of Gastroenterology & Hepatology and the Department of Medicine in conjunction with the Froedtert & MCW Transplant Service Line Fully accredited ACGME Gastroenterology & Hepatology Fellowship program with 13 fellows with intent to initiate a Transplant Hepatology Fellowship Program. Fellows allotted to Transplant/Hepatology consultative service. This is a large & vibrant

training program with transplant hepatology/general hepatology call coverage provided by fellows under faculty supervision, and fellow endoscopy call coverage of hepatology/transplant hepatology patients supervised by the gastroenterology faculty Participate in the institution's academic, research, and educational missions Opportunity to build a practice in transplant hepatology & general hepatology at FMLH, and

various other outreach locations Opportunity to lead outreach clinic efforts in collaboration with current transplant hepatologists to grow & expand patient access to hepatology services Interest in outreach clinic efforts in collaboration with the current 4 transplant hepatologists to help grow and expand hepatology access for patients Serve as a consultant on general and transplant hepatology patients Contribute to

the clinical education of fellows, residents, medical students, and other trainees Participate in multidisciplinary care involving hepatologists, hepatology APPs, gastroenterologists, and other relevant specialties Deliver patient-centered care while fostering a culture of inquiry, quality, & safety Strong APP, nutritional, & pharmacy support Serve as a consultant for transplant hepatology & hepatology patients
