Tenure-Track, Assistant Professor of American History and Women’s and Gender Studies Print Tenure-Track, Assistant Professor of American History and Women’s and Gender Studies Salary Depends on Qualifications Location Sewanee, TN Job Type Faculty Job Number 202100557 Division College of Arts & Sciences Department History Opening Date 11/11/2026 Description Benefits Position Overview The History Department and Women’s

and Gender Studies (WGS) Program at The University of the South, Sewanee, invite applications for a tenure-track assistant professor of the history of gender and/or sexuality and American history to begin in August 2027. This joint appointment in History and Women’s and Gender Studies is open to teachers and scholars in any chronological field of American history who also specialize in the history of gender and/or

sexuality. We encourage candidates whose research and teaching agendas deploy feminist theories and methodologies in their work to apply.Position Appointment Details:FacultyTenure-TrackJoint Appointment: History and Women’s and Gender Studies Position Description The successful candidate will be expected to teach undergraduate survey and upper-level courses in U.S. history, introductory courses in Women’s and Gender

Studies (WMST 100), an advanced course in Feminist Theory, Method, Praxis (WMST 360), an advanced course in WMST senior thesis-writing (WMST 400), and courses related to their research specialization in consultation with the History department and WGS program. Attributes of a Successful Candidate Ideally, candidates will have completed all requirements for a PhD in History by August 1, 2027, when the appointment

begins; however, we will consider candidates who are ABD. A certificate or focus in women’s and gender studies is strongly preferred. Supplemental Information Please submit the following application materials to https://www.schooljobs.com/careers/sewanee/: Application Instructions:A cover letter that includes a summary of current scholarship, teaching interests, and teaching experience.A curriculum vitae.The names

and contact information of three professional references who will be asked to submit letters of recommendation for candidates advancing to the second round.Copies of course syllabi for courses candidates have taught or would like to teach are encouraged but not required.Application Deadline:Applications must be received by December 30, 2026.For more information about the Department of History, please visit History |
