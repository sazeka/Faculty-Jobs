Job Title: Professor of Biology - Anatomy (Assistant/Associate/Full) Job Family: Furman Faculty

Full-Time/Part-Time: Full time Pay Type: Salary Department: Biology Job Summary: Furman University invites applications for a tenure-track faculty position in Biology at the rank of Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, or Full Professor, beginning August 2027. Job Description: We seek a broadly trained biologist who is excited to teach across the undergraduate curriculum, mentor students in authentic research,

and contribute to a collaborative liberal arts and sciences community. Teaching expectations for this position may include lecture and laboratory courses from a newly revised slate of introductory biology and data science courses. We are interested in recruiting candidates with expertise in teaching human anatomy and/or comparative anatomy. Candidates with expertise in related areas are also encouraged to apply.

Other teaching responsibilities may include opportunities to develop new upper-level courses in the candidate’s area of expertise, especially those that broaden our current course offerings. The Department of Biology currently has 15 full-time Biology faculty members and offers both a B.A. and a B.S. degree in Biology.
