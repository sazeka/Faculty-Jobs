If you need assistance or have questions regarding the application process, please contact our recruitment helpline at (775) 784-1495 or jobs@unr.edu . Job Description The

Department of Nutrition within the College of Agriculture, Biotechnology and Natural Resources (CABNR) at the University of Nevada, Reno is seeking applications for a Teaching Assistant Professor. This is a full-time position based in the Department of Nutrition at the University of Nevada, Reno. The role involves 90% teaching and 10% service. Responsibilities The Teaching Assistant Professor will serve as an

integral member of the Department faculty and help maintain existing excellence in teaching and instruction as well as best practices in collegiate instruction in nutrition. The role will also support Department programs and contribute to the development of future academic programs, including a doctoral program in Nutrition. The successful candidate will be expected to: Develop and deliver high-quality, in-person and

online instruction in department courses in Nutrition at the undergraduate and graduate level. Mentor and evaluate departmental graduate teaching assistants. Mentor and supervise graduate students, and/or serve on graduate committees. Contribute to a vibrant and inclusive academic community through collegiality, cooperation, and collaboration. Required Qualifications Ph.D. in nutrition or a closely related field. A

strong track record of effective teaching at the undergraduate and graduate level. Preferred Qualifications Experience in developing and evaluating courses at the undergraduate and graduate level. Experience with developing online and distance learning courses. Experience working in an interdisciplinary environment. Proven ability to work with diverse audiences. Demonstrate leadership skills.
