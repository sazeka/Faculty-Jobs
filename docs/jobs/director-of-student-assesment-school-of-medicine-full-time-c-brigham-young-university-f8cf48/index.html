Alignment with and commitment to the mission, values, and spiritual aims of Brigham Young University and The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Preferred: Experience within undergraduate or graduate medical education. Preferred: Demonstrated experience with competency-based medical education and

Oversee Comprehensive Assessment System Direct the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of assessment systems across all three phases of the MD curriculum. Ensure assessments measure cognitive achievement, competency attainment, and steady progression toward graduation outcomes. Maintain alignment between internal summative Phase 1 exams, clerkship assessment (primarily NBME shelf exams), and

Ensure assessment methods are valid and support competency-based progression decisions. Monitor alignment of assessments with institutional competencies, EPA frameworks, graduation objectives,

and LCME standards Provide strategic oversight for assessment planning and associated personnel. Assessment Governance and Policy Development Develop and maintain policies, procedures, and guidelines related to student assessment, grading, remediation, progression, and promotion to be approved by the curriculum committee.
