Position description

HS/ClinX Assistant/Associate Professor UCSF Department of Dermatology, Division of Dermatologic Surgery The Department of Dermatology at the University of California, San Francisco is seeking outstanding fellowship-trained applicants for a full-time position as an Assistant Professor or Associate Professor for our Dermatologic Surgery Division. The position will involve direct delivery of clinical care at our

clinical sites including our new, state-of-the art UCSF Bayfront clinic, our VA Medical Center, and other sites as needed. Clinical care will encompass Mohs surgery, cutaneous oncology, and procedural dermatology. Applicants with academic and teaching interests in surgical dermatology and cutaneous oncology are encouraged to apply. Interest in the use of technology to improve clinical operations, patient experiences,

quality improvement, and research productivity is desired. Requirements: Candidates must be eligible for licensure in California and have strong clinical skills, academic research interests, and interest in serving a range of patient population. The successful candidate will be a Mohs Surgery and Dermatologic Oncology Board-Certified, fellowship trained Mohs surgeon. Contact: Applicants should submit their curriculum

vitae and a personal statement including a 1-2 page perspective on future plans, academic interests, reprints of major publications, statement of research, and statement of teaching. 5 references will be requested through the AP recruit system. Please apply online at https://aprecruit.ucsf.edu/JPF05653 The posted UC salary scales set the minimum pay determined by rank and step at appointment. See Table 5. The minimum

base salary range for this position is $149,700-$232,900. This position includes membership in the health sciences compensation plan which provides for eligibility for additional compensation.
