Assistant Professor/Associate Professor/Professor | Department of Anesthesiology | Cardiovascular Intensive Care Unit (CVICU) Position Summary: The Department of Anesthesiology at the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW) seeks a board certified/board eligible physician to join our Cardiovascular Intensive Care Unit (CVICU) faculty. This position is located in Milwaukee, WI, with clinical practice at Froedtert Hospital.

We are comprised of a nationally recognized and dedicated group of faculty and staff with a strong commitment to excellence in clinical care, education, research, and innovation. The CVICU provides care for a complex and diverse population of critically ill cardiovascular patients and works collaboratively with multidisciplinary teams across the health system. Position Highlights: Provide comprehensive critical care

to patients in the Cardiovascular Intensive Care Unit, including complex postoperative and critically ill cardiovascular patients. Participate in the multidisciplinary management of patients in collaboration with cardiac surgeons, cardiologists, anesthesiologists, APPs, nurses, pharmacists, respiratory therapists, and other members of the care team. Participate in the education and supervision of medical students,

residents, fellows, APPs, and other learners. Opportunities to participate in clinical research, quality improvement, education, and other scholarly activities based on individual interests and expertise. Manage a high-acuity patient population with opportunities to care for patients requiring advanced cardiovascular and critical care therapies. Utilize and develop expertise in advanced critical care procedures and

technologies as appropriate to the candidate’s training and experience. Work within a collaborative, multidisciplinary team committed to providing high-quality, patient-centered care. Participate in departmental and institutional initiatives that support clinical excellence, education, research, and continuous improvement. Clinical responsibilities will primarily be within the CVICU at Froedtert Hospital, with the

potential for additional clinical responsibilities based on departmental needs. Performs other duties as assigned by Chair or Chair-Designate. Schedule: Full-time faculty position with clinical responsibilities within the CVICU. Schedule and clinical responsibilities will be determined based on departmental and CVICU needs. Participate in the CVICU call schedule as assigned. Salaried (Exempt) position.
