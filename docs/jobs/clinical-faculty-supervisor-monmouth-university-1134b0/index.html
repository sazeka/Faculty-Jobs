Clinical Faculty Supervisor

Monmouth University  ·  Certification, Field Placements & School Partners  ·  Monmouth University, NJ
ClinicalEducation

Position summary

The School of Education at Monmouth University is seeking applications for Clinical Faculty Supervisor. The candidate would be expected to: provide field supervision of student teachers in the areas of mathematics, science, Spanish, special education, early childhood, elementary education, Health/Physical Education, English, social studies, art or music. Additional information about the department can be found at:

http://www.monmouth.edu/school-of-education/about.aspx This is an in-person, on-campus/on-site position that is not remote. Additional Application Material Required: Monmouth University requires all applications and supporting documents to be submitted via the university’s career portal. In addition to the application, candidates must upload the following documents. Applications will not be considered complete for

review until all required documents are uploaded. Resume or Curriculum Vitae Cover Letter Contact Information for Three Professional References Two Letters of Recommendation Optional Documents: 1. Licenses/Certifications Duties and Responsibilities: Provide field supervision of student teachers in the areas of mathematics, science, Spanish, special education, English, social studies, art or music. Enrichment

Statement: Monmouth University values diversity and invites applications from underrepresented groups who will enrich the teaching and service missions of the university. Minimum Qualifications: Master’s degree. Three years of permanent certified experience in the area(s) of certification. Permanent certification in the content area of supervision as issued by the New Jersey Department of Education or equivalent.

Excellent interpersonal, organizational, and communication skills. Available for in-person observations during school district hours. Recent experience supervising, consulting, or otherwise working in schools in contact with classroom teachers. Preferred Qualifications: Administrative and/or Supervisor’s certification, formal training in supervision and/or supervisory experience. Special Instructions to Applicants:

We encourage candidates to include information in their cover letter that addresses their approach to mentoring and teaching and their plans to advance diversity, equity, and inclusion in their proposed role at Monmouth University.
