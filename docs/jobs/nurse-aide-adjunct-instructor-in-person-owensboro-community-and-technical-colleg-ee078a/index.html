College Department: Nursing Job Summary Owensboro Community and Technical College invites applications for the Nurse Aide Adjunct Instructor. The Nurse Aide Adjunct Instructor will teach nurse aide classes, including didactic, lab, and clinical instruction. Job Duties: •Provides didactic, lab, and clinical instruction for nurse aide students.•Follows federal and state regulations and guidelines pertaining to the

Kentucky Nurse Aide Training program.•Keeps accurate and up-to-date records for the nurse aide program.•Securely maintains student records.•Identifies supplies needed to conduct class and lab.•Schedules required clinical days with contracted long-term care facilities and attend clinical days with nurse aide students.•Completes and maintains the required files and other pertinent records in order to maintain clinical

facility compliance.•Determines clinical placement of students. Minimum Qualifications: •Valid, unencumbered licensure as a Registered Nurse in KY or another compact state (must have multi-state privileges if not licensed in KY).•2 years of nursing experience, with a minimum of one year in a long-term care setting.•Successful completion of Methods of Instruction (MOI) course (may be completed upon hire).
