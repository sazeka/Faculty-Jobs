POSITION SPECIFICS The Management and Organization (M&O)

Department in the Smeal College of Business at The Pennsylvania State University (University Park, PA) has two full-time tenure-track professor positions in the areas of Organizational Behavior (OB), Organizational Theory (OT), or Strategy. We seek to fill these positions at the rank of Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor . The desired start date for the position is Fall 2027. Those who are in the final stages of

completing a PhD program will be considered for appointments as an Assistant Professor. For consideration for Associate or Full Professor level positions, applicants must have a proven track record in conducting high-quality scholarly research and demonstrated teaching competence at a high level. Candidates for all ranks must have a PhD in Management or a related field by the appointment start date. Candidates at the

Associate or Full Professor rank must also have demonstrated both a sufficient number of publications in high-quality journals and sufficient impact of that work on the field. Job Requirement : The preferred candidate will have publications in premier journals, a research program with high-impact or such potential underway and is able to connect with a wide array of doctoral students and faculty in our department.

Faculty members in the M&O department are expected to pursue an active research program, perform undergraduate and/or graduate teaching, supervise graduate students, and engage in professional service. Onsite teaching is an essential function of the job. Candidates for the position can apply by uploading a letter of interest, CV, and three research papers.
