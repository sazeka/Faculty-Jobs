LOUIS, MO 63110&#xa;&#xa; Scheduled Hours 40 Position Summary The position is responsible for the development and implementation of a compliance education program which fosters privacy awareness and compliance with applicable regulations, standards and policies. Position also facilitates privacy incident reporting and investigation and assists in the prevention of breaches. Job Description Primary Duties

& Responsibilities: Oversees, develops and delivers initial and ongoing privacy education and training to the workforce. Facilitates and promotes activities to foster privacy awareness and compliance. Provides compliance advice and guidance to all departments on privacy matters to ensure compliance with applicable regulations, standards and policies. Manages privacy incident reporting, response, mitigation and

investigation processes; prevents breaches of protected health information. Develops and implements policies and procedures to ensure compliance with HIPAA privacy and breach notification rules; communicates changes and proposes new or revised policies and procedures as necessary. Maintains current knowledge of applicable federal and state privacy laws and monitors advancements in privacy risk mitigation strategies.

Conducts privacy risk assessments and ongoing compliance monitoring. Develops, recommends and maintains appropriate HIPAA-compliant consents, authorization forms, privacy notices and other materials consistent with current regulatory requirements. Performs other duties as assigned. Working Conditions: Job Location/Working Conditions Normal Office Environment Physical Effort Typically Sitting at a desk or table
