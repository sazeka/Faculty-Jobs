Position description The Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering

(MAE), at the University of California, San Diego, invites applications for an Assistant Professor position in Fusion Energy Engineering. We seek exceptional candidates with research interests in fusion engineering including, but not limited to: 1) Engineering of fusion energy components and systems, with particular emphasis on thermal management and thermo-fluid phenomena, and with consideration of related MAE

disciplines. Areas of interest include, but are not limited to: management of high heat and neutron fluxes on first-wall and divertor systems; tritium-breeding blankets, including neutron transport, shielding, activation, and radiation damage; and thermo-fluid engineering of liquid-metal and molten-salt systems. 2) Fusion power plant systems engineering and integration. Areas of interest include, but are not limited

to: multiphysics and techno-economic analysis of coupled thermal-fluid and mass-transport processes relevant to energy conversion and fuel-cycle processing; data and physics driven engineering approaches to plant control, safety, and risk analysis under limited instrumentation; electricity generation and thermal energy end-uses; and integration of fusion energy with other low-carbon/zero-carbon energy systems.
