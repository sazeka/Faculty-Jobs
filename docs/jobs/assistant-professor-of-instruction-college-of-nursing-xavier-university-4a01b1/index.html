Current Xavier University employees must apply to the internal career site by logging into Workday Assistant Professor of Instruction, College of Nursing ‌ The College of Nursing (CN) at Xavier University is seeking experienced, innovative, and highly motivated candidates for a full-time, non-tenure-track faculty to teach in our high-quality undergraduate and graduate nursing programs beginning Spring 2027. The CN

programs include BSN, second-degree accelerated MSN (MIDAS), and online MSN, FNP, PMHNO, AGPCNP, and DNP programs. The successful candidates are expected to teach in one or more of our nursing programs depending on program needs. Desired teaching specialty areas include but are not limited to: obstetric nursing, pre-licensure second-degree accelerated MSN clinical nurse leader track (CNL), MSN core, and fundamentals.

Candidates with expertise in more than one specialty area are highly desirable. Candidates will teach in our in-person MSN Direct Entry as Seond Degree (MIDAS) program, and may teach in the BSN and/or MSN programs depending on the college's needs. Successful candidates are expected to provide teaching instructions in a classroom, online, and/or lab/simulation. The undergraduate and accelerated programs are

traditional on-campus programs that expect the physical presence of faculty. The online graduate programs expect some physical on-campus presence. Visa sponsorship is not available for this position. The successful candidates will actively participate in the development and evaluation of curriculum, course content, clinical experiences, test construction, and assessment of students’ learning; establish effective

coordinating relationships with co-workers and clinical agencies; participate in nursing scholarly work and professional development; participate in student academic advising and guidance; and participate in the faculty organization and appropriate program, college, and university activities and committees. Clinical areas of expertise include obstetric nursing, and/or leadership. QUALIFICATIONS: Current and

unencumbered license to practice as a registered nurse in Ohio or be eligible to be licensed and practice in Ohio. A minimum of a MSN degree, with a doctoral degree in nursing or a related field preferred (With no doctoral degree the candidate must possess demonstrable expertise in the desired clinical areas).
