Position summary

The University of Nevada, Reno (UNR) appreciates your interest in employment at our growing institution. We want your application process to go smoothly and quickly. Final applications must be submitted prior to the close of the recruitment. If you need assistance or have questions regarding the application process, please contact our recruitment helpline at (775) 784-1495 or jobs@unr.edu . Job Description The

College of Liberal Arts at the University of Nevada, Reno (UNR) invites applications for a visiting professorship (rank open) in Constitutional Law to begin Fall of 2027. This is a full-time one-year appointment with the possibility of renewal based on performance. The successful candidate will have a 2-2 course load, teaching undergraduates in constitutional law, theories of justice, and related areas, which form

part of the Legal Studies major Interdisciplinary Degree in Law, Business, and Society. Both programs sit under the newly established Center for Constitutional Law, housed within the College. This Center works closely with the Boyd Law School in Las Vegas. The candidate will also work with the Executive Director and the Director of the Center for Constitutional Law in the implementation of the Center’s mission.

Prospective applicants should familiarize themselves with the mission and activities of the Center, as characterized on the Center’s website: https://www.unr.edu/constitutional-law Required Qualifications PhD in political science, law, history, philosophy, or related academic discipline; OR a JD Demonstrated excellence in teaching constitutional law or theories of justice & related at the college level or an ABA law

school For senior applicants (associate or full professor), a record of scholarly excellence in Constitutional Law, Political Science, Philosophy, or Legal Studies; for other applicants, evidence of scholarly promise in these areas Preferred Qualifications Strong communication skills to engage with faculty, administrators, and external stakeholders Expertise both in law and in an academic discipline relevant to the

focus of the Center Compensation The salary range for this position is $70,300 to $106,500. This is a 9-month contract. Salary is competitive and commensurate with related education and experience. Remarkable Retirement Our retirement plan is beyond compare. Your 19.25% contribution is generously matched by the University. With the faculty retirement plan, you are 100% vested your first day.
