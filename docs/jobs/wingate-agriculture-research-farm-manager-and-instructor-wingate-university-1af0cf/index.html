The Agricultural Food Systems Program seeks

an experienced agricultural professional with demonstrated excellence in undergraduate teaching, experiential learning, and farm management. This is a full-time University staff Farm Manager position beginning in January 2027. The successful candidate will oversee the day-to-day operations, management, and continued development of the Wingate University Research and Education Farm; provide hands-on learning

opportunities for students; and teach undergraduate courses in agricultural food systems and related areas. The Farm Manager will support agricultural research and community engagement, collaborate with faculty and community partners, and contribute to the continued growth and development of the Agricultural Food Systems Program. This position will work closely with the Agricultural Food Systems Director and other

University and community partners to advance the educational, research, and outreach missions of the program and farm. The successful candidate will be team-oriented and demonstrate a service-oriented leadership approach and growth mindset. The candidate will contribute to an inclusive, collaborative, and equitable learning and work environment. The position will remain open until filled. Duties/Responsibilities :

Farm management responsibilities will include oversight of the day-to-day operations, maintenance, safety, and continued development of the Wingate University Research and Education Farm.
