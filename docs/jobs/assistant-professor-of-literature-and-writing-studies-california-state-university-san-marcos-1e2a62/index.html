at California State University San Marcos seeks to hire a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the field of Writing, Rhetoric, and Composition, with expertise in related fields. We are looking for a colleague who will work closely with our department’s General Education Writing (GEW) Program while also making contributions to our Literature & Writing Studies curriculum. The candidate will have the opportunity to

partner with the current GEW Director in overseeing and researching particular aspects of the GEW Program. Service opportunities may also include working with faculty across CSUSM to administer the all-campus writing requirement, coordinating with campus partners such as the CSUSM Writing Center and Library, mentoring master’s-level graduate students, and chairing graduate theses. Depending on expertise and interest,

the candidate will have the opportunity to rotate in and out of the GEW Program Director position and make other contributions to Literature & Writing Studies department leadership. Tenure track faculty at CSUSM teach three 3-unit courses per semester. Located in North County San Diego, CSUSM is a diverse Hispanic and Asian American Native American Pacific Islander Serving Institution committed to access, community

engagement, and innovative teaching. CSUSM has been ranked #1 in social mobility by CollegeNET. Applicants whose degree is in a related field should explain how their doctoral training prepares them to contribute to a Literature and Writing Studies program and meet the

responsibilities of the position. Qualified candidates will have engaged in scholarly work related to one or more of the areas described above. Preferred Qualifications An interest in first-year writing instruction and writing program administration, we seek a colleague who will contribute to the larger Literature & Writing Studies department’s undergraduate and graduate programs.
