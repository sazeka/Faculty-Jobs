Physician-Scientists: All Health-Related Research Areas - A Division-wide, Open-rank Faculty Search #BSD044

University of Chicago  ·  Biological Sciences Division  ·  Chicago, IL
FacultyBiological Sciences

Position summary

Scope and Opportunity The University of Chicago's Biological Sciences Division is searching for full-time, outstanding physician-scientists in any area of health-related research who are interested in joining a premier academic medical center committed to building the next generation of integrated clinical and biomedical research programs. This search is open to physician-scientists with relevant degrees (i.e., MD,

MD/PhD or equivalent) working in any health-related research area basic, translational, clinical, computational, population, implementation, outcomes, public health and health services research. Academic rank, track, tenure status, and compensation are dependent upon qualifications. Primary appointments will be in appropriate departments or units in the Biological Sciences Division (BSD) with possible secondary

appointments in other relevant units or Divisions depending on the candidates' research. Positions will include clinical activity, typically 0.1-0.25 of clinical full-time employment (FTE), which is negotiable on an individual basis. These positions are benefits eligible. The University of Chicago offers a wide range of benefits programs and resources for eligible employees, including health, retirement, and paid

time off. Information about the benefit offerings can be found in the Benefits Guidebook . Appointees will join a highly collaborative academic ecosystem with outstanding basic and translational science and clinical departments housed within a compact urban campus, state-of-the-art core facilities, exceptional graduate and medical students, and opportunities to engage with new multidisciplinary research and

commercialization initiatives, including Hyde Park Labs , a new hybrid facility for academic research, science-based companies and the UChicago Science Incubator. Research Priorities The goal of this search is to recruit physician-scientists who will build ambitious, disease-relevant research programs and strengthen the connections among clinical care, basic discovery, translational science, and the training of

future biomedical investigators. Areas of interest include, but are not limited to: Mechanistic studies rooted in human disease Clinical and translational research linked to patient populations, biospecimens, imaging, or longitudinal cohorts Precision medicine, biomarkers, diagnostics, and therapeutic response Early-phase trials, experimental therapeutics, and investigator-initiated clinical studies Health outcomes,
