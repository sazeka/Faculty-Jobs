University, Oklahoma's only Historically Black College and University and an 1890 land-grant institution, invites applications for a full-time Instructor of Biology. This is a teaching-intensive appointment with primary responsibility for delivering the Department of Biology's foundational curriculum to both science majors and general education students. The successful candidate will teach non-majors biology lecture

and laboratory, majors biology lecture and laboratory, and Biology II lecture and laboratory. The position is central to student success at the gateway level, where retention and progression in the sciences are established, and to the University's land-grant mission of expanding access to STEM education for students from historically underserved communities.
