Employment Opportunity Detail Assistant Professor of Physical Therapy Position Overview We are on the lookout for a passionate faculty member to join our thriving Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) Program in the city of Evansville, Indiana. Imagine yourself in a full-time, 10-month position with the possibility of tenure-track, provided you hold an earned terminal degree (PhD, EdD, DSc, HSD, etc.). Our faculty and

environment are vibrant because we are passionate about creating the future generation of physical therapy leaders. The requirements for this exciting role include one of two options: 1) a terminal academic degree in neurobiology, physiology, or research methods; or 2) a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) degree or a DPT combined with an earned post-professional academic doctorate with current expertise in geriatrics,

pharmacology, acute care, or cardiopulmonary rehabilitation. If you have a DPT degree, ensure you are eligible for physical therapy licensure in Indiana and bring at least three years of clinical experience. Beyond the classroom, this role extends into student advising, focused scholarship, and service within the department, university, and the broader profession. Your journey with us would begin on August 1, 2026.

At the University of Evansville, we champion diversity and operate under a nondiscriminatory policy, embracing individuals of all backgrounds, genders, orientations, and abilities. We encourage minorities and underrepresented groups to seize this opportunity to contribute to our rich tapestry of knowledge. Join us as we make strides in education and be part of a community that values your unique perspective. Bonus

points if you appreciate collaborative colleagues, meaningful work, and the occasional Qdoba taco bar. The University of Evansville eagerly awaits your transformative presence. Are you ready to shape the future? Apply now!
