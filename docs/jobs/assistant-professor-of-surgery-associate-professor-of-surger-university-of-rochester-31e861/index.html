Position summary

Cardiothoracic Intensivist The University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry is seeking a full time cardiothoracic intensivist to join our Cardiac Surgery team at an Assistant Professor level in the Department of Surgery.&nbsp; The candidate must be board certified in Adult Critical Care Medicine. The Cardiothoracic ICU is a 22 bed unit staffed with 22 APP’s and provides medical management to patients with

complex therapies and interventions for advanced cardiac and pulmonary failure including Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation (ECMO), Left Ventricular Assist Device (LVAD), cardiac transplant, complex cardiac and vascular surgery, and all subspecialties of cardiology practice. &nbsp;Strong Memorial Hospital is an 886-bed, Level I Trauma Center performing approximately 1,100 cardiac surgical cases annually.&nbsp; The U

of R is located in Rochester, New York and is surrounded by world class culture, affordable housing and nationally ranked public schools.&nbsp;&nbsp; The City of Rochester is a mecca for the arts: home to a philharmonic orchestra, wonderful museums including the Strong National Museum of Play, internationally recognized dance companies as well as The Eastman School of Music. The Rochester International Jazz Festival

is one of the top 10 Jazz festivals in the nation. The region boasts access to great watersports both on Lake Ontario as well as the Finger Lakes. The Finger Lakes region is also home to many internationally known wineries.&nbsp;&nbsp; Rochester has a rich history and was the last step on the underground railroad and the place Frederick Douglas called home.&nbsp; It is also the location of the Susan B. Anthony House

making Rochester the epicenter of the woman’s suffrage movement.
