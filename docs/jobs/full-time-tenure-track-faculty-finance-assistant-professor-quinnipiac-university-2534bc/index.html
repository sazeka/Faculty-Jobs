We seek

candidates whose research and teaching interests contribute to the evolving intersection of Finance, Financial Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, FinTech, Healthcare Finance, Biotechnology, and data-driven decision making. We welcome candidates employing both traditional and emerging approaches to important questions in finance. Opportunities exist for interdisciplinary collaboration with faculty across the School

The successful

candidate will have opportunities to contribute to the continued development of innovative programs in Financial Technology, Financial Analytics, and AI applications in finance as well as university initiatives in Healthcare Finance. Candidates should demonstrate the potential to establish and sustain a high-impact research agenda that contributes to leading finance journals. The department is particularly interested

in candidates whose scholarship explores how emerging technologies are reshaping financial markets, institutions, firms, and investors. innovation Digital assets, blockchain, and decentralized finance Generative AI and large language models in finance AI governance, regulation, and the ethical implications for financial markets The successful candidate will play a central role in a premier finance program focused on preparing graduates for careers in a rapidly changing financial landscape.
