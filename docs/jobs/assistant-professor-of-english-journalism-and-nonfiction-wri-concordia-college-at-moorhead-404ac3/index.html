About the Role: The Department of English at Concordia College (Moorhead, Minnesota) invites applications for a full-time, tenure-eligible position in English Journalism and Nonfiction Writing at the Assistant Professor level to begin August 2027. The successful

candidate will be qualified to teach journalism and writing courses at all levels of undergraduate work. The candidate would also teach our first-year composition courses, which share common elements but leave room to be designed around each teacher’s interests. The position includes a teaching load of five courses per year, as well as serving as advisor for The Concordian, the college newspaper. The position also

requires institutional service including mentoring journalism students, academic advising of undergraduate students after the first year, assisting the department and interdisciplinary programs in curriculum development, revision, and evaluation, and contributing to the Multimedia Journalism major.
