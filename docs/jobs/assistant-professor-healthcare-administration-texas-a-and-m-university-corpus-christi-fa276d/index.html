Job Description We are seeking candidates with expertise in healthcare

administration who demonstrate teaching and/or research expertise across multiple areas relevant to the program, including healthcare information systems, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies in healthcare, project management, health law, and health services research. Tenure-track faculty are expected to teach students, pursue a robust program of research, seek external funding, and disseminate research

findings. Faculty are expected to model exemplary professional behavior through work with students, peers, administration, and community colleagues and to provide student support including mentoring, advising, recruiting, and HCAD student project supervision.
