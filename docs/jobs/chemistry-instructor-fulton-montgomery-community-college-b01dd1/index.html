Chemistry Instructor

Fulton-Montgomery Community College  ·  Johnstown, NY
InstructorChemistry

Position summary

Chemistry Instructor SUNY FMCC is seeking applicants for a full-time tenure-track position to teach chemistry beginning January 2027. Candidates should be prepared to primarily teach Introductory Chemistry as well as Introduction to Forensic Science and General Chemistry. Candidates must be committed to the mission of community colleges and teaching students who have a wide range of academic abilities. Additional

responsibilities include, but are not limited to, assessment, planning, and implementation and evaluation of curriculum. Qualifications: • Either a master’s degree in chemistry or a master’s degree in chemistry education combined with a bachelor’s degree in chemistry is required. • Teaching experience is preferred, preferably at the two-year college level. • Experience with assessment of student learning is also

preferred. Salary and Benefits: • The annual Instructor base salary is $64,259. • A comprehensive benefits package including health insurance, a NYS Pension Plan, and other perks to support your professional and personal well-being. Application Procedures: • Applications should be submitted by e-mail to Faculty2@fmcc.edu. • Applications should include a cover letter, resume, copies of transcripts (official

transcripts required for hire) and a list of five references with their current phone numbers and addresses. • The anticipated start date is January 13, 2027. Recruiting will continue until a successful candidate has been identified. • For full consideration, applications should arrive before September 18, 2026. However, the search will remain open until the position is filled. Fulton-Montgomery Community College is

an equal opportunity employer committed to providing a workplace free from unlawful discrimination. All applicants will be considered for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, veteran status, disability, or any other status protected by applicable federal or New York State law. Questions regarding the College’s non-discrimination policies

may be directed to the Civil Rights Compliance Coordinator at Human Resources, 2805 State Highway 67, Johnstown, NY 12095, 518-736-3622, Ext. 8403.
