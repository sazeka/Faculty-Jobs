Assistant Professor in Mechanical Engineering Position Overview The University of Kansas (KU) Department of Mechanical Engineering (ME) is seeking outstanding candidates for multiple tenure-track faculty positions at the rank of Assistant Professor. We seek dynamic and innovative scholars who are passionate about collaborative research, excellence in teaching, and advancing the field of mechanical engineering through

impactful research and education.The department welcomes applicants whose research complements and expands existing strengths within the department. The examples below illustrate areas of potential interest.Mechanics, materials, and manufacturing: solid and computational mechanics, advanced materials, advanced manufacturingEnergy and transport: energy systems, thermal and fluid sciences, sustainabilityRobotics and

intelligent systems: robotics, autonomous vehicles, controlsEmerging and interdisciplinary areas: research connecting mechanical engineering with health, climate, infrastructure, or other fieldsOutstanding candidates in other mechanical engineering fields may also be considered. Candidates whose work bridges traditional mechanical engineering disciplines or promotes interdisciplinary collaboration are especially

encouraged to apply.The Assistant Professor position is subject to a collective bargaining agreement between The University of Kansas and United Academics. UAKU is the exclusive representative for the bargaining unit, and it is the candidate’s right to be a member or not be a member of a union without fear of personal or professional repercussions.Applications are sought from candidates with earned doctorates in

mechanical engineering or closely related fields by the time of appointment. Successful candidates are expected to contribute to the development of academic programs and a strong research program. Successful candidates will be results-oriented, have a record of superior scholarship, have demonstrated potential to develop an externally-funded research program, and teach high-quality courses at both the undergraduate

and graduate levels. The KU ME Department currently has programs leading to bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees. Increasing research innovation and collaborations to make a difference in the world are priorities in the department.The KU School of Engineering has embarked on a strategic plan that leads to significant expansion while maintaining and improving the existing high-quality student programs.
