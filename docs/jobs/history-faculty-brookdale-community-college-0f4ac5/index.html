Dr. Ashley Zampogna-Krug Associate Professor and Department Chair azampognakrug@brookdalecc.edu Areas of Interest/Specialization: Immigration and Ethnicity, World History, Italy, American History Ashley Zampogna-Krug is an Associate Professor of History. She completed her doctorate, with a specialization in Italian immigration during the 1920s and 1930s, in 2012 at the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee. At Brookdale,

she teaches both world and U.S. history surveys as well as a 200-level course on immigration and ethnicity in U.S. history. She is actively engaged in the Global Citizenship Project and nominates one to two students each year for a Global Citizenship Project Award. Professor Zampogna-Krug’s current appointments include Co-Chair of the History and Political Science Department, Co-Chair of the Standard III Committee

for Middle States, Faculty Fellow on Immigration with the Center for Transformative Learning, and Advisor of the student club, Society of Latinos (SOL). Her former involvements at the college include Faculty Liaison to the International Education Center and Advisor of Dreamers+. Professor Zampogna-Krug’s passion is to educate others on immigration and enhance support for immigrant students at the college. She has

given numerous presentations on historical and contemporary immigration related topics, and she continues to serve as one of the founding members of UndocuAlly at Brookdale, a committee dedicated to supporting immigrant students and their families. Jess A. LeVine Professor jlevine@brookdalecc.edu Professor Le Vine is a full professor at Brookdale with a long teaching career here. He holds a B.A. from Colgate

University and an M.A. from The University of California, Santa Barbara. He is very involved in campus activities, including Governance, a multitude of committees and is an advisor to the History and Political Science Club. The Jacob W. Needle Annual Lecture in History is organized by him. He has been actively involved with the Monmouth County Historical Association and the N.J. Vietnam Memorial. Professor Le Vine

has taken students on study abroad courses to Mexico (Maya), Italy (Etruscans, Romans, Renaissance, Sicily) and Cuba. He teaches all courses in American Civilization, The American Revolution, Recent America, World Civilization including Honors, Vietnam, New Jersey History and the History of Rock and Roll.
