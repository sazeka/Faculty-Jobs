Summary of Responsibilities Plans, prepares and follows an approved course of study that includes measurable objectives for each course taught.Prepares as necessary instructional

materials (course outlines, syllabi, exams, etc.).Instructs, manages and supervises students in classrooms and computer labs using appropriate teaching methods.Evaluates instruction and learning using appropriate testing methods and in consultation with full-time faculty.Prepares and administers assessment instruments, an assessment report, and an improvement plan following department specifications.Works closely

with an assigned full-time faculty member to ensure that each class taught meets academic standards, department standards, and course/program outcomes.Maintains a student-oriented approach to education.Maintains and submits accurate and current reports and records involving student accountability, attendance, performance, and follow-up.Coordinates engaged experience programs with students as assigned.Assists

counselors, department chairperson, program coordinator, faculty, and department staff to maintain an effective program.Compliance with university policy for certification of online instructors. Qualifications / Licenses / Certifications A master’s degree in Technology Management, Mechatronics, or a related field such as Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Engineering Technology, Automation,

Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering, Engineering Management, Project Management, Operations Management, or Business Management AND 2 years of teaching experience, relevant professional experience or a combination of both.ORA bachelor’s degree in one of the above fields plus at least four years of teaching experience, relevant professional experience, or a combination of both.
