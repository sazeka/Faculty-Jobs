Center for Cellular Therapies and Cancer Immunology (CCTCI) at UT Southwestern Medical Center is recruiting tenure-track faculty at all academic ranks (Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor). We seek innovative scientists with expertise in immune engineering who are eager to develop transformative technologies and biologics to treat cancer. About the Center The CCTCI is a newly established, interdisciplinary center

dedicated to accelerating the discovery and clinical translation of next-generation immunotherapies, including first-in-human Phase I trials. The Center leverages UT Southwestern’s world-class infrastructure in cellular manufacturing, genomics, and early-phase clinical development to bridge bench and bedside. Research Areas of Interest We welcome applicants using cutting-edge tools to investigate and manipulate the

immune system for therapeutic gain.
