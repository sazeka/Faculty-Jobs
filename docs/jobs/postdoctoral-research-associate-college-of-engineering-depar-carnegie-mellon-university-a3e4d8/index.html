Carnegie Mellon University’s department of Mechanical Engineering is searching for a Postdoctoral Research Associate to join their team. This is an exciting opportunity for someone who thrives in an interesting and challenging work environment. The successful candidate will lead data collection and analysis for two mobile sampling

campaigns. We are conducting mobile measurements of air toxic VOCs in several cities in New Jersey, and we will soon begin a second project to make similar measurements in rural Pennsylvania, West Virginia, and Ohio. These measurements will help to quantify exposures in urban/suburban communities (New Jersey) and in rural communities impacted by oil and gas development (PA, WV, OH). Both the urban and rural

communities are in environmental justice areas. The ideal candidate is someone who wants the opportunity to apply innovative and creative approaches to analyzing large and complex datasets. The mobile sampling approach combines high resolution mass spectrometry with in-motion sampling. This results in a rich, chemically specific, and spatially resolved dataset that can be used to quantify both pollutant exposures and

the sources responsible for those exposures. In addition to the projects presented here, the successful candidate will have the opportunity to engage in the wider community in the Department of Mechanical Engineering and in CAPS.
