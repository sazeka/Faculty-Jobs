ProfessorThe Department of Agribusiness and Applied Economics at North Dakota State University seeks to appoint a promising early-career research economist to conduct grant-funded research, policy analysis, and market intelligence on U.S. agricultural policy and trade issues. The position will support applied research and stakeholder-oriented analysis on commodity markets, trade policy, and global market developments

affecting U.S. and North Dakota producers and agribusinesses. The appointment is a full-time, non-tenure track position at the rank of Research Assistant Professor. The initial appointment will be for two years with the possibility of extension based on performance and continued availability of external funding.The successful candidate will join a research and outreach program led by Dr. Sandro Steinbach at North

Dakota State University and affiliated with the Center for Agricultural Policy and Trade Studies. The position will help develop timely and rigorous analysis of agricultural trade, commodity market conditions, input market developments, policy changes, and global disruptions that shape opportunities and risks for U.S. and North Dakota agriculture. Work may include analysis of trade measures, tariffs, sanctions,

export restrictions, logistics disruptions, exchange-rate movements, fertilizer and energy market conditions, and international supply-and-demand shocks.This position provides an outstanding opportunity for independent research, collaboration, and stakeholder engagement. The Research Assistant Professor is expected to actively participate in the University’s intellectual life and contribute to peer-reviewed

publications, policy briefs, data products, conference presentations, and market intelligence outputs.
