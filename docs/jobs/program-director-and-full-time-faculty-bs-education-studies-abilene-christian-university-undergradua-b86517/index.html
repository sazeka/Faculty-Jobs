Position summary

ACU is affiliated with the fellowship of the Church of Christ. All qualified applicants for this position must be professing Christians and willing to support the Christian mission and purpose of the university. Churches of Christ membership is preferred for this position. ‌ Faculty Rank: College Assistant Professor, College Associate Professor, or College Professor (all non-tenured) Position Location: Remote, with

the possibility of limited travel The key principles of this job are to: Support ACU’s Mission: Educate students for Christian service and leadership throughout the world. Support ACU’s Vision: Become a premier university for the education of Christ-centered leaders. Scope: The Program Director manages all areas related to the day-to-day operations of the B.S. in Education Studies and Learning Development program.

The Program Director reports to the Dean of the College of Learning and Development. The Program Director also oversees coordination related to external educator preparation and certification partnership pathways associated with the program, including collaboration with university departments and external partners supporting Texas teacher certification opportunities. Major Responsibilities Lead and oversee the online

B.S. in Education Studies and Learning Development program. Supervise and evaluate the performance of faculty and facilitators of the program. Teach in the program. Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of full-time and adjunct faculty regularly. Administer the academic, personnel, recruitment, and administrative life of the program. Stay abreast of current trends in education, instructional practice, teacher

preparation, and workforce needs, and adjust program practices as applicable. Utilize data to inform practice in pedagogy, student engagement, student achievement, retention, and alignment to standards. Advise undergraduate students toward the completion of their degrees. Coordinate and support operational processes related to external educator preparation and teacher certification partnerships. Collaborate with

university departments and external partners regarding certification-related student progression, field experiences, and operational alignment.
