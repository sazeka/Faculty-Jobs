for a tenure-track Assistant Professor position in Organizational Learning, Innovation, and Development (OLID) within the School of Education. This is a continuing, nine-month appointment beginning January 2027. The successful candidate will join a collaborative and forward-looking program dedicated to preparing professionals who lead learning, workforce development, and organizational change across diverse contexts.

This position is represented by SIU Carbondale Faculty Association/IEA-NEA. For more information regarding salary and other details please visit: https://laborrelations.siu.edu/labor-contracts/. Due to the newly established $100,000 fee for H-1B petitions requesting consular processing outside of the U.S., Southern Illinois University is unable to provide H-1B sponsorship at this time for any prospective applicant

who is outside of the U.S. and requires consular processing. Salary is competitive and commensurate with qualifications and experience, while also considering internal equity. Examples of Duties The Assistant Professor will contribute to the OLID program through a balanced portfolio of teaching, research, and service, including: Teaching undergraduate and graduate OLID courses in both face-to-face and online formats

Advising students and chairing master’s committees Co-chairing doctoral committees Developing and sustaining an active research agenda leading to peer-reviewed publications and presentations at local, national, and international venues Providing service to the department, college, university, and professional community Pursuing external funding opportunities (encouraged) Performing other departmental responsibilities

as assigned An on-campus presence is required.
