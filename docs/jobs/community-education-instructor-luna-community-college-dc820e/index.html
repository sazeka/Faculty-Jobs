Community Education Instructor

Luna Community College  ·  Continuing Education  ·  Las Vegas, NM
Instructor

Position summary

Compensation The hourly pay rate will be between $20.00 and $22.50 per hour, depending on the certifications required for the specific class. Compensation Type: Non-Exempt Employment Type: Casual Scheduled Weekly Hours: 19 Grade: NE10 Department: Continuing Education Position Summary The Community Education Instructor is responsible for teaching and facilitating classes designed to provide non-credit, enrichment, or

skills-based instruction to community members at Luna Community College. This role is essential for delivering quality, long-term course offerings to the community. This position will be part time at 19 hours per week or less. Duties & Responsibilities ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS Develop and deliver engaging instruction in the listed subject areas. And ensure all instruction is executed safely and in compliance with

required specialty and LCC standards. Fiber Arts, Sewing, Quilting Hospitality Art/Drawing Book Study Groups Culinary or Cooking Classes Fitness/Exercise Classes KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, AND ABILITIES Expertise in the subject matter being taught (e.g., Cooking, Fitness, Arts, etc.). Strong verbal communication and instructional skills. Ability to work effectively with diverse groups of community members EDUCATION,

EXPERIENCE AND LICENSES Expertise/experience necessary to teach the specific course subject. Required Certifications for Specific Classes (Long-Term): Culinary or Cooking Classes: Must possess current ServSafe certification at time of application. Fitness/Exercise Classes : Must be certified in the specific specialty (e.g., Yoga, Zumba, etc.) AND hold a current First Aid/CPR card at time of application. Next Steps We

encourage all current and prospective instructors of long-term courses to prepare the necessary documentation, particularly the required certifications. To ensure a consistent and efficient hiring process at Luna Community College, we require all applicants to apply for any open position through our website at https://luna.edu/ . All new hires will be processed and managed within our Workday system. * If you have
