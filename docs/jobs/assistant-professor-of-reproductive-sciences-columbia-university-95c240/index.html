Position summary

The Suh Lab at Columbia University Irving Medical Center is seeking a Assistant Professor of Research to lead experimental initiatives investigating the fundamental mechanisms of human aging. We are looking for an intellectually curious scientist who can continually generate novel research hypotheses and drive the lab’s research direction. The successful candidate will spearhead projects exploring the genetic and

epigenetic drivers of systemic aging, including but not limited reproductive aging and aging-related diseases. A primary focus will be the application of advanced stem cell engineering—specifically the differentiation of stem cells into ovarian cell types, brain cell types, and other specialized lineages required for the laboratory, and advanced gene-editing tools (e.g., CRISPR/Cas9 systems) to dissect age-related

phenotypes and their underlying biological mechanisms. This leadership role requires a high degree of independence in designing experimental frameworks and overseeing the laboratory's diverse research portfolio.
