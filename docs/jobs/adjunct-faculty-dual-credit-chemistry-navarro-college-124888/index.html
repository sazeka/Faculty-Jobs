Instructors shall communicate their knowledge and experience effectively to students. Instructors shall have good rapport with students in class,

instilling an atmosphere in which students are engaged and feel comfortable asking questions. Instructors shall evaluate students regularly and fairly based on outcomes and objectives identified in course syllabi. Instructors

Instructors shall provide course averages to students at regular
