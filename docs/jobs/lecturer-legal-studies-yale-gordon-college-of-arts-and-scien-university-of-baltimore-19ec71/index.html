Job Posting: JR102207 Lecturer, Legal Studies, Yale Gordon College of Arts and Sciences (Open) Department: CAS Academic Instruction, PM Position Type: Regular Open Date: 10-08-2026 Close Date: $80,000 - $90,000 Job Description: The Yale Gordon College of Arts and Sciences (YGCAS) at the University of Baltimore invites applications for the position of Lecturer in Legal Studies. The position is a full-time, one-year

renewable contract appointment, 9.5-month faculty position, with a 4/4 teaching load. The position is available beginning Fall 2027 and is subject to final budget approval by the President of the University. Responsibilities: We seek a student-oriented colleague with a demonstrated commitment to collaboration across programs and an ability to teach all levels of graduate and undergraduate law-related courses. In

addition to instruction, this position will be responsible for co-instruction and co-supervision of legal studies related internships. The selectee will also assist in implementing our newly launched program, Law, Philosophy, and History (LPH) BA, the only humanities-based interdisciplinary pre-law program in the state of Maryland, including development and recruitment for that program, as well as providing similar

help for the existing Legal Studies MA. The position will also take a faculty advisory role for UBalt’s competitive mock trial team and engaging in other activities to enhance prelaw at UBalt. Faculty members in YGCAS contribute to program and curriculum development, maintain a professional practice or creative/research agenda, and participate in university service and other areas of faculty responsibility. Minimum

Qualifications: Successful candidates will have a JD from an accredited institution. Relevant teaching experience is required. The position requires professional accomplishments and evidence of teaching ability in both theory and process appropriate for classroom and online instruction at all levels. Preferred Qualifications: Preference will be given to candidates who possess advanced degrees in philosophy, history,

or other related fields and to those who have some experience practicing law. UBalt offers classes daytime, evening and weekend; both face-to-face and in various types of online environments (fully online, hybrid, synchronous, asynchronous).
