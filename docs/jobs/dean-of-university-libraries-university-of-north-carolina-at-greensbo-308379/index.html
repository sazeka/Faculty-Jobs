diverse, learner-centered public research university serving more than 19,000 students and employing approximately 2,500 faculty and staff across eight colleges and schools. Founded in 1891 as one of the original three UNC System institutions, UNCG offers a broad academic portfolio and is nationally recognized for its commitment to academic excellence, access, and affordability.Recognized by the Carnegie Foundation

for both high research activity and community engagement, UNCG is ranked No. 1 in North Carolina for affordability by The New York Times and social mobility by The Wall Street Journal. Designated an Innovation and Economic Prosperity University, UNCG supports more than $77.4 million in research and creative activity and contributes over $1 billion annually to the Piedmont Triad economy. Primary Purpose of the

Organizational Unit The University Libraries are the crossroads of knowledge at UNCG — at the center of teaching, learning, and research. University Libraries faculty and staff support the UNC Greensboro community by promoting learning, student success, creativity, research, and collaboration in a diverse and innovative environment. The University Libraries’ collections span print, electronic, and streaming formats,

including more than one million printed books, three million eBooks, 125,000 eJournals, and approximately 300,000 streaming films and audio files. The libraries recorded 7.6 million uses of print and electronic materials and welcomed more than 200,000 in-person visitors last year.
