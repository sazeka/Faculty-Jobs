Position Description The Department of Biology at Seattle University invites applications for a full-time Instructor position for the 2026-27 academic year. This position is a temporary replacement for current faculty on sabbatical leaves. We are seeking an instructor to teach some combination of introductory courses (BIOL 1610+1611, 1620+1621, 1630+1631), majors courses including genetics, anatomy and physiology,

virology, and cell biology, and/or non-majors courses on cancer biology or global health. Specific course assignments will be determined based on the candidate’s expertise and departmental needs. Ideal candidates will demonstrate expertise in a relevant field of biology and a commitment to undergraduate education and teaching excellence. Minimum requirements include a Masters degree, or equivalent, in a relevant

biological field and undergraduate teaching experience. Salary Range: $55,000 - $61,000, dependent on experience Application Instructions: Submit applications to https://www.seattleu.edu/careers, and include a curriculum vitae; a cover letter that includes your future teaching plans and courses of interest; also include the names and contact information for three references. Review of applications will begin August

25, 2026 and continue until the position is filled. Inquiries may be directed to Dr. Carolyn Stenbak (stenbakc@seattleu.edu).
