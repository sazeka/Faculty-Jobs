Assistant Professor, Anatomist or Physiologist Print Assistant Professor, Anatomist or Physiologist Location 1 S Grove St, Westerville, OH Job Type Faculty Job Number 00526 Division Academic Affairs Department Biology & Earth Science Opening Date 10/15/2026 Description Benefits DESCRIPTION Otterbein University invites applications for the position of a full-time, tenure track or non-tenure track Assistant Professor

of Biology with a primary specialization in Human Anatomy & Physiology. We seek candidates with a who will contribute to the department’s efforts to enhance diversity and provide an inclusive learning environment for all. RESPONSIBILITIES RESPONSIBILITIES: If non-tenure track: teach 24 workload hours in the academic year including: Anatomy & Physiology I labs, Anatomy & Physiology II lectures, Anatomy & Physiology II

labs, and an Integrative Studies course in their field of expertise. Serve as Lab Director, responsible for organizing the lab, preparing student learning materials and assessments, and directing all A&P instructors, for all Anatomy & Physiology I labs. Contribute to university life through advising pre-professional health students and service on university and departmental committees. If tenure track: teach 21

workload hours in the academic year and engage in an active research program along with the other listed responsibilities. An individual on the tenure track would teach 1 less A&P lab than in the non-tenure track route.COMPENSATION: For the rank of Assistant Professor - $62,226 (plus full University benefits).START DATE: This appointment begins in August 2027. QUALIFICATIONS QUALIFICATIONS: PhD, MD, or DO is required

for this full-time, tenure track or non-tenure track position. A strong candidate will be a broadly trained anatomist, physiologist, or in a closely related field, with expertise in human anatomy and physiology and health. All candidates need to provide evidence of teaching effectiveness in anatomy and physiology lectures and labs and display a commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion. Preference will be given

to candidates with more than 3 years’ experience teaching human anatomy and physiology labs and lectures. Candidates for the tenure track option should have a strong publication record and an active research plan that can include undergraduate students.
