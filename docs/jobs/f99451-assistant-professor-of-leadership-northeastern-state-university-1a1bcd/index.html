The Ed.D. in Community-Based Leadership is an innovative, cohort-based doctoral

program designed to prepare scholar-practitioners who create sustainable change through collaboration among educational institutions, tribal nations, nonprofit organizations, government agencies, and community partners. Through applied research and a Dissertation in Practice, doctoral students address authentic leadership challenges while developing evidence-based solutions that strengthen organizations and

communities. This position offers an exceptional opportunity for an accomplished scholar-practitioner to help shape and grow a distinctive doctoral program. The successful candidate will play a significant role in curriculum development, doctoral student success, research initiatives, community partnerships, and the continued advancement of graduate education. Faculty members are expected to contribute to a

collaborative academic culture that values innovation, applied scholarship, mentorship, and community engagement.
