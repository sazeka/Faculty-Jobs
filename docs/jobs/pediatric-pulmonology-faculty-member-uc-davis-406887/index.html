Position description UC Davis Children’s Hospital School of Medicine Department of Pediatrics Pediatric Pulmonary Faculty Member The Department of Pediatrics at the University of California, Davis School of Medicine is seeking a board-certified or board-eligible

pediatric sleep medicine specialist to join the Division of Pediatric Pulmonology and Sleep Medicine. The successful candidate will help expand a growing pediatric sleep medicine program serving children throughout Northern California while contributing to clinical care, research, and the education of medical students, residents, and fellows. Leadership opportunities are available, including potential roles within

the Pediatric Sleep Medicine Program and Pediatric Sleep Laboratory. UC Davis Children’s Hospital offers a comprehensive pediatric sleep medicine program featuring multidisciplinary sleep and CPAP clinics, accredited in-lab and home sleep testing, and access to advanced surgical therapies. Join a collaborative team of seven pediatric pulmonologists, including three sleep medicine physicians, committed to innovation,

education, research, and exceptional patient care. The position is open at the Assistant/Associate/Full Professor level in Health Science Clinical or the Clinical X Series in the Division of Pediatric Pulmonology. Candidate hired into the position will join a thriving pulmonary practice that serves a huge catchment area in Northern California. The Division has robust Cystic Fibrosis, Asthma, Technology Dependent and

Sleep programs. The division has six pulmonologists, two registered nurses, respiratory therapists, dieticians, pharmacists and social workers and provides multidisciplinary care in the areas of Severe Asthma, Aerodigestive, Sickle Cell, Pulmonary Hypertension and Neuromuscular clinics. Candidates should have an interest in the care of complex multidisciplinary patients with pulmonary and sleep medicine conditions
