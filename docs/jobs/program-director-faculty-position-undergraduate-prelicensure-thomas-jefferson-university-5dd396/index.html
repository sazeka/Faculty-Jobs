Program Director/Faculty Position, Undergraduate Prelicensure BSN Programs, Lehigh Valley Campus

Thomas Jefferson University  ·  Undergraduate Prelicensure BSN Programs, Lehigh Valley Campus  ·  Thomas Jefferson University, PA
Nursing

Position summary

Job Details Position Description: The Program Director reports directly to the Chair of Undergraduate Prelicensure BSN Programs, with secondary consultation from the Associate Dean of Academic Affairs. The Program Director is responsible for delivering a high quality academic programming for students. Under the directorship of the Program Director, it is expected that the Program will be responsible for program

compliance with all professional and accreditation standards, guidelines, and benchmarks consistent with the mission, vision, and values of the Jefferson College of Nursing. The Program Director is accountable for successful teaching and learning practices within the Program. Responsibilities: Assist with the review and selection of program candidates. Monitor formative and summative program evaluation metrics.

Review and recommend revision of program curriculum . Meet/exceed expected Program outcomes and benchmarks. Develop action plans if standards, expectations, and benchmarks are not met. Assist the Chair with academic planning and faculty deployment. Respond to student concerns to resolve conflicts, appeals, and disputes/grievances. Assist with preparation of self-study report for accrediting agencies and participation

in site evaluations. Assist with faculty recruitment, development, evaluation, and mentoring. Review and consult clinical site evaluations with the Academic Practice Integration (API) Team. Maintain strong working relationships with federal, state and local constituencies, accrediting bodies, nursing boards, clinical agencies, specialty organizations, students, preceptors, faculty and alumni. Develop initiatives to

integrate the Jefferson College of Nursing and its communities of interest. Participate in recruitment/information initiatives as required. Job Description Qualifications: Required Earned doctorate in nursing or a related field Earned Graduate degree in Nursing Eligible for appointment at the rank of Assistant Professor or higher Current, unencumbered PA RN license (or eligibility as required) Knowledge of

accreditation, curriculum design, and program evaluation Appointment and Application: This is a 12-month salaried position with a generous benefit and retirement program. Academic appointment and salary will be commensurate with experience. Interested candidates should submit on-line application/CV/Cover Letter detailing their specific teaching and clinical experience and areas of scholarship and service;
