In your cover letter please indicate which of these

courses you are interested in and qualified to teach. The successful candidate should demonstrate a strong commitment to effective undergraduate teaching utilizing engaging, data-driven pedagogies. Experience and interest in teaching primarily face-to-face or hybrid courses is a plus. As UVU’s primary mission is undergraduate education through engaged learning, applicants with experience in engaged learning

pedagogies are especially encouraged to apply. Please describe your philosophy on teaching and learning and how you put that philosophy into action to be an effective educator in a 1-2 page Teaching Philosophy.We value and promote collegial relationships and mutual respect among students, faculty, staff, and the community. applicants from all backgrounds who are eager to help construct and maintain a culture of engagement within biology education and research, and who are committed to helping students access, succeed in, and complete college.
