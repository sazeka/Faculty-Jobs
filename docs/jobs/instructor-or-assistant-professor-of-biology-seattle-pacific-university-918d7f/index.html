Position summary

Job Description: About Seattle Pacific University Founded in 1891, Seattle Pacific University has a long and distinguished history in Christian higher education. Its comprehensive academic programs serve more than 2,000 undergraduate and graduate students. Located just minutes from downtown Seattle, SPU seeks to be a premier Christian university committed to engaging the culture and changing the world by graduating

people of competence and character, becoming people of wisdom, and modeling grace-filled community. Seattle Pacific University seeks faculty who are committed to excellent teaching, meaningful scholarship, and the integration of Christian faith with their academic discipline. We value teachers who mentor students thoughtfully, engage them across varied learning contexts, and help them connect their studies to the

wider world. About Biology The Biology program is part of Seattle Pacific University’s College of Applied and Natural Sciences . Our faculty are committed to excellent undergraduate teaching, meaningful faculty-student engagement, and providing students with opportunities to connect classroom learning with scientific inquiry and hands-on experience. In addition to courses on our Seattle campus, SPU provides

distinctive opportunities for field-based teaching and research at the University’s Blakely Island Field Station in Washington’s San Juan Islands. We seek a colleague who will contribute broadly to our Biology curriculum and help students develop the knowledge, skills, and scientific curiosity necessary for their academic, professional, and vocational pursuits. What We Need Seattle Pacific University invites

applications for a regular, full-time, nine-month, non-tenure track faculty position in Biology, beginning September 1, 2027. (Possibly moving to tenure track in the future.) The position may be filled at the rank of Assistant Professor or Instructor; candidates whose highest degree is a master’s may be considered at the rank of Instructor. We seek a broadly trained biologist who can contribute to multiple areas of

our undergraduate curriculum and add depth to the program both on our Seattle campus and at the Blakely Island Field Station. We are particularly interested in candidates with expertise in marine biology or biological oceanography. Additional experience in microbiology or molecular biology would strengthen a candidate’s ability to contribute across the curriculum. What You'll Do Teach upper-division Biology courses
