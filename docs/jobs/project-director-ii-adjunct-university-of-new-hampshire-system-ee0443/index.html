Position summary

USNH Employees should apply within Workday through the Jobs Hub app Internal Candidate Identified Manage applied research projects as a member of the Institute for Health Policy and Practice to support the creation of new knowledge to be used to improve population health and the health care delivery system. A strong understanding and commitment of diversity and equity in the workplace environment and with the

implementation of projects. This is accomplished by facilitating conversations between internal and external project team members and contracted resources and overseeing progress toward deliverables with contracted resources. The position includes direct contact with clients and funders of the projects, identifying business needs in detail; analyzing and synthesizing research data and results; recommending solutions

where required; providing direct consultation and training resources to clients, and reporting and publishing results. The position is responsible for training new project team staff in areas of the project as needed. This position will support report and publication development as needed. Duties/Responsibilities Participate in and plan meetings of management, internal and external project teams and committees to

ensure that project work plans are adhered to, and ensure the project remains on schedule and program objectives are achieved. Maintain an overall project plan for the relevant IHPP focal area projects; and provide project workplan and updates to IHPP, external partners, and funders. Confer with faculty/staff members and/or external partners, to identify concepts and questions that can be addressed by appropriate

research methods and collecting of descriptive data, qualitative data, summaries, white papers, and the like. Work with campus (e.g., academic departments and Institutes) and external project partners (such as State and local health agencies), as required, to define business needs for project, and develop procedures and protocols and provide information and consultation to support project needs.
