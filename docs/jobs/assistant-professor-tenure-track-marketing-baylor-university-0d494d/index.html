publications in top journals. Candidates should also be devoted to excellence in teaching. Planned start date for this position is August 2027.&nbsp; The Marketing Department provides a very collegial environment for research and teaching, with 22 faculty members ( https://hankamer.baylor.edu/marketing/faculty-staff ).
