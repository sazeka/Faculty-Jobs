Biology Faculty Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Position Details Position Information Working Title Biology Faculty Position Status Department School of Health Sciences General Summary The in-person Biology Faculty member is responsible for delivering high-quality instruction in support of Odessa College’s mission to provide accessible, student-centered education and promote student success.

The instructor teaches assigned biology courses in traditional, hybrid, and online formats, utilizing engaging, evidence-based teaching practices that foster critical thinking, academic achievement, and student engagement. This will include teaching a variety of undergraduate biology courses, including specialized subjects such as Microbiology, Molecular Biology, and Genetics, while incorporating current disciplinary

knowledge and instructional technologies. In addition to teaching, the faculty member recruits, advises, and mentors students, supports curriculum development and assessment, collaborates with colleagues across the College, and contributes to departmental and institutional initiatives that advance academic excellence and workforce readiness. Specific Position Duties Maintains current knowledge and professional

competency in the discipline through ongoing professional development. Demonstrates the flexibility and ability to teach a variety of biology courses across multiple instructional modalities, schedules, locations, and delivery methods, including traditional, hybrid, online, and CollegeNOW® Academy (Dual Credit) courses serving high school students. Designs and delivers appropriately rigorous coursework that promotes

critical thinking, student engagement, and academic success while helping students reach their full potential. Provides students with clear course expectations, including syllabi, course policies, assignment guidelines, grading criteria, and timely, constructive feedback. Develops and administers assessments aligned with course learning outcomes, including a comprehensive final evaluation when appropriate. Creates

and maintains an inclusive, respectful, and student-centered learning environment that values diverse perspectives, encourages the free exchange of ideas, and upholds academic integrity. Prepares thoroughly for all instructional activities and arrives promptly for classes, laboratories, office hours, and other assigned responsibilities.
