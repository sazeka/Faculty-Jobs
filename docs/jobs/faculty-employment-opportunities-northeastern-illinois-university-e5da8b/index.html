Position summary

On behalf of our faculty, staff, and students, welcome to the College of Business and Technology (CBT) at Northeastern Illinois University (NEIU). NEIU’s Main Campus is located on the Northwest Side of Chicago. It is ranked as the safest college campus in Illinois and one of the safest in the nation. We’re known for being leaders in graduating our students with one of the least amounts of debt and for having the most

multicultural campus in the Midwest (U.S. News and World Report). The College of Business and Technology strives to offer accessible and relevant education in a supportive environment to help our diverse learners achieve their personal and professional goals. With reasonably sized classes, ease of access to faculty, excellent professional advisors, high retention rate, and empowered student clubs and organizations,

students have real opportunities to apply their knowledge and skills. I’ve had the privilege of leading the College since 2013, and you can also find me teaching an HR course! I am proud of your numerous and continuous accomplishments, including: Obtaining AACSB Accreditation, recognizing our business programs as being in the top 6% globally and representing the highest standard of achievement for business schools

worldwide. Evolving from the College of Business and Management to the College of Business and Technology after adding Computer Science programs. Expanding student programming, including career readiness and professional development. Establishing the first CBT Living Learning Community. Establishing the Golden Eagles Esports Team. Attaining the premier ABET accreditation for our Bachelor's degree in Computer Science.

I invite you to explore our educational offerings: (1) traditional degree programs and (2) community institute programs. The real question is, “Where do you fit in?” Traditional Degree Programs Do you see yourself as an entrepreneur, marketing professional, data analyst, accountant, computer scientist creating a new AI product for a company, or a cybersecurity analyst working to prevent hacking? The CBT offers four

bachelor’s degrees (Business, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, and Information Technology); and three master's degrees (MBA, M.S. Accounting, M.S. Computer Science), which can be completed full or part-time. Our students benefit from: A blend of qualified research faculty and practitioners that connect knowledge to practice.
