If you need assistance or have questions regarding the application process, please contact Human Resources at (775) 673-7168 or humanresources@tmcc.edu . Job Description Truckee Meadows Community College (TMCC), located in Reno, Nevada, is seeking exceptional educators for an Emergency Medical Services (EMS) Instructor position in a fast-paced, diverse

educational environment. TMCC EMS Academy is one of the premier EMS educational institutions in the region. Our team of faculty and staff is solid and collaborative, with one goal in mind: To produce the absolute best EMS providers who embody our 3 core commitments: Character, Compassion, and Competence. The major areas of responsibility are to teach and develop theory instruction, arrange appropriate learning

experiences in the clinical and practice lab settings, grade and evaluate students in theory and the clinical and field settings, and coordinate theory and clinical instruction with other faculty to ensure appropriate learning experiences. When students are in clinical/field settings, faculty will act as liaisons between the students and the clinical/internship sites. Liaison work may require travel to hospitals and

field sites. Minimum Qualifications 1. A minimum of an associate degree in Fire, EMS, or other related field from an accredited institution, or a bachelor’s degree in any science, computers, education, or a field comparable as needs of the program dictates. 2. Current state of NV paramedic certification, or ambulance attendance license, or the ability to obtain by the start of the semester they are hired. 3. Four (4)

years of experience as a full-time paramedic in EMS. 4. An EMS instructor endorsement on the Nevada EMS certification can be obtained by the start of the semester in which they are hired. 5. Two (2) years’ experience in teaching in EMS, preferably in an academy setting, or at least ten (10) years of EMS experience in place of teaching. 6. Be able to locate the region, if necessary. Preferred Qualifications 1.

Master’s Degree or higher from a regionally accredited institution preferred. 2. Eight years of experience in a full-time paid paramedic position in emergency services. 3. Three years of experience teaching in an emergency services academy setting. 4. NAEMSE I instructor completion 5. Have experience teaching in an online capacity and have familiarity with software programs and computers 6. AHA instructor in BLS,
