Bloomington Kinesiology Location Bloomington Position Summary Indiana University Bloomington (IUB) School of Public Health‐Bloomington (SPH‐B) invites applications for the position of Lecturer of Exercise Science in the Department of Kinesiology. The SPH-B seeks passionate, contemporary educators who will contribute to excellence in teaching and student learning in the study and application of human movement,

physical activity, exercise, and human performance, preparing students for continued education and professional careers in kinesiology, allied health, or related fields. This 10-month instructional faculty appointment is planned to start August 1, 2027. Appointment may be made at the rank of Lecturer, Senior Lecturer, or Teaching Professor, commensurate with qualifications and experience. Applications received by

November 15, 2026, will receive full priority consideration, and applications will be accepted until the position is filled. This position is located on the Bloomington campus of Indiana University. Successful candidates are expected to work on campus to allow full engagement with the department, school, university, and community.
