Assistant/Associate Professor and Extension Specialist in forest business/forest economics. This is a tenure-track, 12-month appointment, with 65% Extension and 35% teaching. The successful candidate will develop an extramurally funded program to expand education, outreach, and applied research in forest business resources for non-industrial private forest landowners and the forest industry. The successful candidate

will perform the educational functions of the Alabama Cooperative Extension System (ACES). In addition, the successful candidate will develop a successful extramurally funded Extension program with related applied research focusing on forest business practices, including but not limited to economics, costs of forestry practices, forest microenterprises and income-producing opportunities, forest products and markets,

economic development, and forest management practices. Exceptionally qualified candidates will be considered for the Harry E. Murphy Endowed Professorship which provides a modest salary supplement and additional operating funds. Review of applications will begin on Aug 15, 2026, and continue until a successful candidate has been identified. Applicants should combine into one document a cover letter, curriculum vitae,

names and contact information of at least three references, statements of teaching and research philosophies under the “Resume Upload” section (maximum file size: 5MB). All other documents should be uploaded under “Additional Documents.” For additional information or questions contact: Dr. Mark Smith (mds0007@auburn.edu). ResponsibilitiesThe successful candidate is expected to work independently and in collaboration

with other faculty in the College of Forestry, Wildlife, and Environment, Alabama Cooperative Extension System, and other Auburn University faculty to: (1) develop and implement undergraduate and graduate forestry and forest business programs (2) work with stakeholders to develop applied research to address the educational needs in Alabama and beyond, (3) develop and deliver niche technical training and service
