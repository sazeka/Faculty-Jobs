University of Oklahoma Norman Campus: Gaylord College of Journalism and Mass Communication Location Norman Open Date Oct 24, 2025 Description Are you someone working in or who have worked in the media and mass communication fields? Do you have a Master’s degree? Are you looking to pass on your experience and know-how to future generations of media professionals? The Gaylord College of Journalism and Mass

Communication is the No. 1 media program in the country according to the 2025 Broadcast Education Association school rankings. We are looking for adjuncts to help shape the education and future of our undergraduate students across our five majors: Advertising, Creative Media Production, Journalism, Public Relations and Professional Writing. Application Instructions Applications for these positions will be reviewed as

they are submitted and as we have a need to fill gaps in our teaching schedules. Applicants should provide 1) a cover letter describing their qualifications and their vision and plans for this position, 2) a curriculum vitae/resume that details professional experience, 3) names, addresses, phone numbers, and email of at least three references. For specific questions about the positions and application process, please

contact Associate Dean for Academic Affairs, Dr. Elanie Steyn at elanie@ou.edu. Applications should be submitted online via Interfolio: https://apply.interfolio.com/175309. Application Process This institution is using Interfolio's Faculty Search to conduct this search.
