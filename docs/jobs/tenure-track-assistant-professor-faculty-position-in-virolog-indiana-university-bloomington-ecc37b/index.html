University Bloomington (IUB) invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant in the College of Arts and Sciences, Department of Biology in the field of Virology. We seek candidates to join IU’s outstanding Microbiology research program to lead research in the areas of Cellular Responses to Virus Infection, Cell Biology of Virus Infection, and Evolution of Emerging Viruses but other areas of virology will be

considered. We encourage applications from scholars who apply interdisciplinary perspectives and share important research overlap with current IUB scholars, while also offering new dimensions that will expand and strengthen virology research at IUB. Researchers have access to state-of-the-art facilities for genomics and bioinformatics, light and electron microscopy, flow cytometry, proteomic analysis, physical

biochemistry analytical instrumentation, and crystallography. This 10‐month appointment would ideally start August 1, 2027, but start date is negotiable. Responsibilities: Tenure-track faculty in the Biology department at Indiana University Bloomington are expected to substantially contribute to the overall academic excellence of the department by developing and/or maintaining a robust research portfolio including

pursuing and obtaining major extramural research funding to conduct cutting-edge research as a principal investigator, teaching undergraduate and graduate in the classroom, mentoring graduate students in our graduate programs, participating in service at the University and actively engaging with their professional community.
