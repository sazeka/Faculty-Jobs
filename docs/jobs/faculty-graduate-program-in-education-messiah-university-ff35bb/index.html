Campus Main Campus (Grantham) Position Description Position Summary The School of Graduate and Professional Studies at Messiah University invites applications for a full-time faculty in the Graduate Program of Education (MEd and EdD). Rank is open and will be determined based on qualifications. This is a 9-month, on main campus, position with limited summer responsibilities. Responsibilities This position is

responsible for teaching, advising, and field supervision within the Graduate Program in Education. Qualifications Doctoral degree (PhD, EdD) in education. Candidate should have PK-12 teaching experience and be able to teach both introductory and mastery level content in their areas of expertise. Experience in PK-12 school leadership is preferred. Candidates with principal and/or superintendent certification and

leadership experience at the building or district level are encouraged to apply. The Program The Graduate Program in Education at Messiah University offers both MEd and EdD pathways designed to advance the field of education by developing leaders, practitioners and collaborators whose growing commitment to reconciliation, justice, and the dignity of all people compels them to pursue excellence in their professional

understanding and practice. The Department Advancing the mission of Messiah University, the School of Graduate and Professional Studies educates students for a lifetime of intellectual exploration, ethical leadership and exceptional professional practice within a complex world.
