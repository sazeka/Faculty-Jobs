Position Information Position Title Instructor of Art Requisition Number F00547P Job Description The Department of Visual Art & Design (DOVAD) at Weber State University

invites applications for a full-time, non-tenure track, instructor position to begin July 1, 2027. We seek a candidate who will contribute in meaningful ways to the department, college, and university’s continuing commitment to student success and belonging. The successful candidate will primarily teach in the department’s lower-division General Education and Foundations curricula (Foundations courses are designed to

teach art majors basic visual literacy and making skills), and will serve as the department’s Concurrent Enrollment Liaison. The person in this position will also participate in departmental service opportunities and faculty governance. The breakdown of duties is approximately 60% time teaching, 25% overseeing Concurrent Enrollment and 15% scholarship and/or service.DOVAD is committed to serving students from a

variety of backgrounds and experiences and we seek candidates with experience supporting the learning needs of these students. Excellent communication, collaboration, and organization skills are necessary.NOTE: This is a one-year Instructor appointment renewable by separate contract at the sole discretion of the University. Job Duties The successful candidate will be responsible for: Teaching a load of 8 to 11 credit

hours (three courses), primarily in Creative Arts General Education and Foundations courses each Fall and Spring semester. The Creative Arts General Education curriculum in DOVAD includes two, three-credit load hour non-majors courses and two four-credit load hour studio courses.
