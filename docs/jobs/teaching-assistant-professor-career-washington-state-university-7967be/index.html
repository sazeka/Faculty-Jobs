Available Title(s): 158-NN_FACULTY - Teaching Assistant Professor - Career Business Title: Teaching Assistant Professor – Career Employee Type: Faculty Position Term: 9 Month Position Details: The Opportunity: The School of Economics Sciences is dedicated

to high quality teaching, research, outreach, and engagement. Our program provides students with the economic knowledge, quantitative tools, and communications skills necessary to secure professional positions in a wide range of industries and public organizations. As a Teaching Assistant Professor with the School of Economic Sciences with the WSU College of Agricultural, Human, and Natural Resource Sciences (CAHNRS)

your primary responsibilities will be teaching economics courses to undergraduate students, with minimal research expectations that may focus on teaching pedagogy or any field within economics. The successful candidate will be a dynamic and engaging instructor, capable of inspiring and attracting students to the discipline of economics. Additional duties include advising students, collaborating with faculty and staff

across related disciplines, and actively participating in committees, instructional activities, and other initiatives within the School of Economic Sciences, the Pullman campus, and Washington State University. You will also have opportunities to mentor undergraduate students and supervise their research projects. Your teaching load will be three courses per semester (3/3), with 85% teaching, 5% research, and an

additional 10% devoted to services. Additional Information: This is a full-time (100% FTE), 9-month appointment position . This position works on-site the WSU Pullman campus in Pullman, WA.
