Job Description LEHIGH UNIVERSITY. The Goodman Center for Real Estate and Perella Department of Finance invite applications for a full-time, non-tenure-track faculty position in Real Estate. The appointment may be made as Teaching Assistant Professor, Teaching Associate Professor, Teaching Professor, Professor of Practice, or Senior Professor of Practice, depending on the successful candidate’s qualifications and

experience. We seek candidates with expertise in real estate, real estate finance, or closely related areas and welcome applicants from both academic and professional backgrounds. The anticipated start date is July 1, 2027. This is a renewable, fixed-term appointment with an initial term of three years.The individual hired will normally teach four courses per semester, primarily at the undergraduate level, and will

contribute to Center, Department, College, and University service. There may also be opportunities to teach at the graduate level in the future. Primary teaching responsibilities will be in real estate and real estate finance, with opportunities to teach additional finance courses depending on departmental needs. The individual will also be expected to contribute to curriculum development, student mentoring,

experiential learning activities, and engagement with the Center’s professional and alumni community.Founded in 1865, Lehigh University has combined outstanding academic and learning opportunities with leadership in fostering innovative research. Recognized among the nation's highly ranked research universities, Lehigh offers a rigorous academic community for over 7,500 students. Lehigh University has some 5,800

undergraduates, 1,800 graduate students, and nearly 600 full-time faculty members. Lehigh University is located in Bethlehem, PA, a vibrant and historic area. Over 840,000 people live in the Lehigh Valley, which is in close proximity to New York City and Philadelphia.QualificationsApplicants must have an earned graduate degree at the master’s level or higher in business, finance, real estate, or a closely related

field from an accredited institution or international equivalent by the date of employment. Candidates must demonstrate the ability to provide excellent instruction in real estate and/or real estate finance. Candidates considered for appointment as Professor of Practice or Senior Professor of Practice must have substantial professional experience in the real estate industry.
