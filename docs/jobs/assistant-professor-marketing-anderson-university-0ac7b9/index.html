Position summary

The College of Business and Economics at Anderson University cordially invites applications for a professional-track, ten-month instructional position in Marketing at the rank of assistant professor. The preferred start date for the position is Fall 2026. An earned doctorate in Marketing from a regionally accredited institution is strongly preferred, though ABD will also be considered if the doctorate is earned by

start of employment. Experience teaching at the undergraduate level is preferred but not required. Exceptionally well-qualified candidates with teaching and industry related experience will be considered for appointment at an advanced rank (associate or full professor). Only those candidates who have authorization to work in the United States can be considered for the position.We are seeking a faculty member who can

support the Marketing program within the College of Business and Economics. Potential classes primarily include Marketing principles and Sales.
