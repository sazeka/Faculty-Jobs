Position summary

Position Summary: The Department of Pediatrics , Division of Cardiology at the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW) and Children’s Wisconsin (CW), seeks a board certified / board eligible physician to join our expanding Division. We are comprised of nationally and internationally renowned, dedicated group of faculty and staff with a strong commitment to clinical care. Position Highlights: Through the Forward Pediatric

Alliance (FPA), a collaborative partnership between the Herma Heart Institute at Children's Wisconsin and UW Health Kids/American Family Children's Hospital, fellows benefit from two vibrant academic medical centers. The combined program includes five interventional cardiologists representing five distinct training backgrounds, providing a diverse and comprehensive educational experience, as well as a combined annual

catheterization volume of approximately 650-700 cases. This position will be based primarily at Children's Wisconsin in Milwaukee. However, the fellow will be credentialed at both sites, allowing participation in catheterization procedures at the affiliated University of Wisconsin site in Madison (American Family Children's Hospital) and expanding exposure to a broader range of procedures and practice approaches.

Clinical experience in the Children's Wisconsin catheterization laboratory is anticipated to average approximately three days per week. Additional catheterization laboratory experience in Madison is anticipated to average approximately one day per week. This experience may be tailored and expanded to accommodate particularly educational or complex cases, as agreed upon by the fellow and supervising physicians.

Fellows will have the opportunity to participate in a weekly pediatric cardiology continuity clinic, supported by nursing staff, with independent responsibility for the management of general cardiology patients and progressive responsibility for the care of pre- and post-catheterization patients under the supervision of the attending interventional cardiologist. Current staffing includes a highly experienced

physician assistant who is proficient in many of the clinical and procedural responsibilities assumed by the advanced interventional fellow and serves as an additional educational and clinical resource. Fellows will present cases and participate in weekly clinical care conferences and other multidisciplinary conferences as appropriate.
