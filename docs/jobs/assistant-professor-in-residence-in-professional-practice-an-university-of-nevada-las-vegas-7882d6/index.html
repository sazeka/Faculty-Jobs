ROLE of the POSITION The University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV) School of Architecture invites applications for an Assistant Professor-in-Residence position in Professional Practice and Community Engagement in Architecture. This is a non-tenure-track, full-time academic appointment within the

College of Fine Arts. This position is designed to strengthen the school’s instructional and outreach capacity in areas directly aligned with NAAB accreditation criteria, including professional practice, leadership, collaboration, and community engagement. The successful candidate will play a central role in bridging academic instruction with professional practice and community-based design initiatives.

RESPONSIBILITIES The Faculty in Residence in Professional Practice and Community Engagement will support the school’s instructional, outreach, and accreditation missions through the delivery of professionally grounded coursework in architectural practice, including project delivery, ethics, and practice operations, while providing mentorship to students pursuing internships, licensure pathways (AXP/ARE), and career

placement. The position will lead or contribute to community-engaged design studios and initiatives in partnership with local organizations, public agencies, and nonprofit stakeholders, translating real-world challenges into applied learning experiences. The successful candidate will cultivate and sustain industry and community partnerships to expand experiential learning opportunities and workforce development
