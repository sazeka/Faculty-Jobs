Description The School of Education, Social Work, and Psychological Sciences at the University of Missouri-Kansas City is continuously seeking qualified candidates for adjunct faculty teaching positions. This includes positions to teach coursework offered by SESWPS in service to UMKC Essentials, the campus-wide general education program for undergraduate students. THE INTENT OF THIS ANNOUNCEMENT IS TO IDENTIFY A POOL

OF ADJUNCT FACULTY WHO ARE QUALIFIED TO DELIVER GENERAL EDUCATION COURSES.Specifically, SESWPS staffs UMKC Essentials coursework in the following areas: Critical Thinking (in the natural sciences, social sciences, and arts and humanities), Civic and Urban Engagement, and Culture and Diverse Perspectives. UMKC instructors also contribute to teaching in the First Semester Experience course. Courses are taught in

face-to-face, online/synchronous and online/asynchronous modalities; opportunities for specific modalities depend on availability and which specific course is being taught.Responsibilities of UMKC Part-time Faculty Members:Create and maintain civil and engaging learning environmentsDeliver curriculum through high quality course instructionAssess student learning of course objectives:Use a variety of evaluation
