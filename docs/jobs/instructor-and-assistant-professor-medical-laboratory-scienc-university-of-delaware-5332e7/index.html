Molecular Sciences The Department of Medical and Molecular Sciences is seeking continuing track faculty to teach in our NAACLS-accredited Medical Laboratory Science (MLS) program. These individuals should have the skills and innovation required to effectively teach university courses for MLS students and participate in appropriate scholarly and service activities consistent with the Department's vision, mission, and

philosophy. Eligible candidates should have at least three years of clinical laboratory experience, preferably with an emphasis in hematology or microbiology. Ideal candidates should demonstrate competence in both didactic and clinical education and the ability to deliver the MLS curriculum in both the traditional classroom and online. The department is seeking two (2) qualified candidates, one at the rank of

Assistant Professor and the other at the rank of instructor or assistant professor. The anticipated start date is August 15, 2027. MMSC offers undergraduate and graduate programs in the clinical, biomedical, and pre-health sciences. Successful candidates are expected to teach lectures and student lab courses at the undergraduate and graduate level in the department, primarily in areas of medical laboratory science. A

generalist background is preferred, although experience in hematology or microbiology is desired. Selected candidates will be expected to advise graduate students, pursue scholarly activities, and perform service to the university, department, and professional community. Content knowledge in the biomedical sciences and a demonstrated commitment to using evidence-based teaching methods are required. Incumbents also

work with clinical affiliates to support students in their clinical rotations. QUALIFICATIONS: Successful candidates will have at least three years of clinical laboratory experience, teaching and professional experience.
