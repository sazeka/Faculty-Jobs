Number of Positions In Requisition 1&#xa;&#xa; Job Details Thomas Jefferson University seeks an innovative and collaborative leader to serve as the inaugural Program Director of its accelerated Hybrid Distance Learning Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) Program. This is a unique opportunity to shape and lead a new educational pathway within a nationally recognized Department of Physical Therapy while advancing

excellence in physical therapist education. The Program Director will lead the design, implementation, assessment, and continuous improvement of this new program. The distance learning pathway will operate within the Department of Physical Therapy alongside the department's well-established residential DPT program. Reporting to the Chair of the Department of Physical Therapy, the Program Director will collaborate

closely with department, college, and university leadership. The successful candidate will be an experienced physical therapist educator, relationship-builder, and leader who can cultivate university, community, and clinical partnerships while supporting faculty, staff, and students. Preference will be given to candidates with experience teaching in a distance learning environment; however, all qualified applicants

are encouraged to apply. This position is eligible for remote work within the United States and requires travel to Jefferson's Philadelphia campus approximately two to four times per trimester for on-site instructional, leadership, and community-building activities. Leadership Roles and Responsibilities Provide leadership in curriculum design, program implementation, faculty recruitment, and development of strategic

clinical partnerships. Foster a learning environment that promotes student engagement, academic success, professional formation, and a culture of belonging. Lead program review, outcomes assessment, and continuous quality improvement to ensure program excellence and sustainability. Oversee all academic and administrative operations of the program. Provide faculty evaluation, mentoring, and personnel management.

Participate in fiscal planning, budgeting, and resource management. Support student recruitment, retention, and success. Ensure ongoing compliance with CAPTE accreditation standards. Teach within areas of expertise and program need.
