Unclassified Employment Opportunities Unclassified Employment Opportunities Position: Nursing Instructor - Adjunct Department: Health & Natural Sciences / Allied Health Reports To: Director of Nursing Position Summary The Adjunct Nursing Instructor provides competent instruction in both clinical and theoretical areas of nursing. The instructor serves as a role model in professional settings, evaluates student

progress, maintains accurate academic records, assists with curriculum and program development, and supports the mission and goals of the institution through committee service, advising, and mentorship. Required Education & Experience Associate’s degree in Nursing Valid, unencumbered Registered Nurse (RN) license eligible to practice in Louisiana. Three (3) years of nursing experience, including at least one (1) year

as a medical-surgical hospital staff nurse providing direct patient care. Practice as a nurse for at least six (6) full-time months within the three years preceding application. Approval by the Louisiana State Board of Practical Nurse Examiners prior to hire. Preferred Education & Experience Bachelor’s or higher degree in Nursing Prior teaching experience in a clinical or classroom setting Duties and Responsibilities

Provides competent instruction in clinical and theoretical nursing subjects. Maintains accurate records of student performance, attendance, and academic progress. Participates in team teaching and models professional behavior for students. Promotes positive relationships with hospitals, clinics, and clinical sites. Assists the Director of Nursing in scheduling and coordinating clinical site usage. Supports ongoing

assessment of student learning and curriculum effectiveness. Contributes to student mentoring, advising, and academic support activities. Serves on department and institutional committees as needed. Performs other duties as assigned by the Director of Nursing, Dean of Nursing and Allied Health, or Vice Chancellor for Education, Training, and Student Success. Required Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities Strong knowledge

of nursing practice and subject matter expertise. Effective adult education instructional skills. Curriculum planning and program management abilities. Effective classroom management and positive learning environment facilitation. Mentorship and leadership capabilities. Strong organizational and records management skills. Conflict resolution and professional communication abilities. Commitment to institutional
