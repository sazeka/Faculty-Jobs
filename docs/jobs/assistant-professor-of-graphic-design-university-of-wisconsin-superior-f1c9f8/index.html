Position Title: Assistant Professor of Graphic Design Job Category: Faculty Employment Type: Regular Job Profile: Assistant Professor Job Duties: The Department of Art and Design at the University of Wisconsin-Superior invites applications for a tenure-track

Assistant Professor of Graphic Design. We seek an innovative educator and leader committed to excellence in undergraduate teaching, contemporary design practice, and collaborative program development. As Graphic Design Program Lead, the successful candidate will teach and advise students, provide academic leadership, and cultivate strong community and industry partnerships that expand internship opportunities,

strengthen workforce connections, and ensure the program remains responsive to evolving professional trends. The Art and Design Department offers a 60-credit Bachelor of Fine Arts (B.F.A.) in Graphic Design designed to prepare students for success in the dynamic creative communications industry. The program emphasizes concept development, technical expertise, collaborative design and project management, community

engagement, and entrepreneurial thinking. Built on a strong liberal arts foundation, the degree combines hands-on, experiential learning with real-world professional experiences, equipping graduates with the skills, adaptability, and confidence needed to thrive in diverse design careers.
