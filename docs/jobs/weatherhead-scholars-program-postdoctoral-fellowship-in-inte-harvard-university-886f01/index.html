Explore Academic Positions at Harvard Weatherhead Scholars Program – Postdoctoral Fellowship in International Security Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Position Details Title Weatherhead Scholars Program – Postdoctoral Fellowship in International Security School Faculty of Arts and Sciences Department/Area Weatherhead Center for International Affairs Position Description The Program on

International Relations and International Security (IRIS) at the Weatherhead Center for International Affairs invites applications for its 2027–2028 Postdoctoral Fellowship in International Security, a one-year, residential opportunity for early-career scholars working on international relations, international security, and related issues in the social sciences.The Postdoctoral Fellow will be part of the newly

established Program on International Relations and International Security, led by Joshua D. Kertzer, John Zwaanstra Professor of International Studies and of Government in the Department of Government at Harvard University. The position will also be affiliated with the Weatherhead Scholars Program and actively participate in its intellectual community and activities. About the Weatherhead Center for International

AffairsThe Weatherhead Center is Harvard’s largest international research center, supporting rigorous scholarship on global, comparative, transnational, and international issues. Founded in 1958, the Center brings together faculty, fellows, and students from across the University to address pressing global challenges, including conflict and governance, inequality, environmental change, migration, and international

development. It serves as an interdisciplinary hub for research, exchange, and public dialogue.About the Program on International Relations and International SecurityThe Program on International Relations and International Security (IRIS) supports rigorous research on national security, international conflict, and foreign policy decision making. As a newly established initiative at the Weatherhead Center for

International Affairs, the program encourages innovative scholarship on topics such as diplomacy, interstate conflict, nuclear security, international institutions, political violence, emerging technologies and artificial intelligence, and other pressing challenges in international security, while advancing theoretical, historical, and empirical approaches to the study of world politics.
