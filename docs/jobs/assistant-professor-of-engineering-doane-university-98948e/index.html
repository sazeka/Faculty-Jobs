Official transcripts of degree or degree in progress will be required as a condition for employment.For further inquiries on the position, please email academicaffairs@doane.edu Position Details Position Information Position Title Assistant Professor of Engineering Unit Engineering & Physics Job Location Crete, NE (68333) Description of Work Doane University, a liberal arts university with campuses

in Crete and Lincoln and an Omaha Location, invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Engineering with an anticipated start date of August 2027. The selected candidate will join the growing Engineering & Physics department, which offers an ABET-accredited Bachelor’s degree program in Engineering with specializations in mechanical, civil, electrical, and integrated engineering. Anticipated

responsibilities include teaching 24 credit hours per year, supervising engineering design projects, engaging undergraduate students in research, advising majors, and participating actively in the life of the department and university. Open to all engineering disciplines, although preference will be given to mechanical, civil, electrical, or interdisciplinary engineering candidates that would complement the expertise

and talent pool of the current faculty. The ideal candidate will be able to teach core engineering courses required of all engineering majors, physics courses, and emphasis area courses within the candidate’s discipline. Candidates with demonstrated teaching and industry experience are particularly encouraged to apply. Doane University actively works to create and sustain a welcoming learning community for all

members to fulfill their potential, feel valued, and make meaningful contributions to the University. Doane University is an equal-opportunity employer and seeks candidates who reflect the diversity of our society. Applicants must be currently authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis. Doane University does not provide employment visa sponsorship for this position.
