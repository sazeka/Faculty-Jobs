Faculty job listings Faculty Position in Nursing Full-time position, posted 09/29/26 Position John Brown University seeks a full-time Faculty member in Nursing beginning in Fall 2027. This position reports to the Nursing Department Chair. Responsibilities include participating in clinical rotations, teaching in skills labs, providing classroom instruction, and advising nursing majors. Teaching duties and courses are

determined by the needs of the department and faculty experience and/or area(s) of expertise. Additional responsibilities include, but are not limited to, recruiting students, participating in departmental governance, developing curriculum, attending and participating in public events, as appropriate, and serving on college and university committees. Nursing Program The JBU Nursing Department is a growing

CCNE-accredited program housed in a 10-year-old facility designed specifically for nursing education. The facility includes modern classrooms, a high-fidelity simulation lab, and other appropriate technology and faculty offices. Our BSN is a relationally focused program that implements a concept-based curriculum. The Nursing program is currently the largest major at John Brown University and is home to many

first-generation and minority students. The JBU Nursing Department is entering an exciting season of growth, supported by more than $35 million in grant funding and donor investment. These resources will expand health professions education at JBU, including the development of new graduate programs in nurse practitioner and physician assistant education. As part of this growth, JBU is recruiting exceptional nursing

faculty to help shape the next chapter of healthcare education for Arkansas and the surrounding region.
