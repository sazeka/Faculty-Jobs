Adjunct Nursing Clinical Faculty PositionThe Department of Nursing at North Carolina

Wesleyan University is seeking qualified, student-centered professionals for the position of Adjunct Nursing Clinical Faculty. These part-time positions are responsible for supervising nursing students in clinical settings and facilitating the integration of theory into practice. Key Responsibilities: Supervise and evaluate nursing students in assigned clinical settings. Facilitate safe, evidence-based patient care
