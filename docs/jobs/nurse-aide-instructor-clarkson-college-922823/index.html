Nurse Aide Instructor

Clarkson College  ·  Omaha, NE
InstructorNursing

Position summary

"Prepare to be the best" is not just a saying at Clarkson College; it's part of our Mission. Every member of our team plays a vital role in preparing students to professionally provide high quality, ethical and compassionate health care services and become the best in their chosen field. Clarkson College is currently seeking a Nurse Aide Instructor to teach Nurse Aide Course and/or the Medication Aide Course and

other Professional Development health care courses utilizing a college or State of Nebraska approved curriculum. This position is currently only open to current Clarkson College employees. Required Qualifications: Minimum of two (2) years nursing experience required. Minimum of six (6) months experience teaching nurse aide, medication aide or other related health care courses OR minimum of six (6) months experience

supervising nurse aides or nurses required. Associate degree in nursing required. Excellent communication and instructional skills required. Theoretical and clinical competency required. Ability to work with a varied population of individuals required. Ability to multi-task while in classroom and at clinical site required. Proficient in use of MS Word, PowerPoint and Excel required. Proficient in use of classroom

equipment (computer, projector, electronic bulletin board, TV, and DVD player) required. Proficient in use of office equipment (files, duplicating machine, fax machine, telephone) required. Proficient in use of medical facility equipment (mechanical bed, wheelchair, patient lifts, etc.) required. Current State of Nebraska RN license or compact agreement state RN license required. Current American Heart Association

certification in Basic Life Support for Health Care Providers required. Current valid motor vehicle operator's license required. Ability to meet and maintain insurability requirements as defined by the fleet automobile liability insurance carrier required. Preferred Qualifications: One year or more experience working in a long-term care facility or with a geriatric population preferred. Bachelor's degree in nursing

preferred Clarkson College is an Equal Opportunity Employer: All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, marital status, sex, age, national origin, disability, genetic information, sexual orientation, gender identity and protected veterans’ status.
