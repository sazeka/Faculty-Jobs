Assistant Professor of Psychology Elon Campus, North Carolina, 27244 Academic Affairs Faculty Full-Time Closing at: Oct 20 2026 - 23:55 EDT Title: Assistant Professor of Psychology Position Type: Faculty Full-Time VP Area: Office of the Provost and Academic Affairs Department: Elon College, the College of Arts and Sciences Position Summary The Psychology Department in the Elon College of Arts and Sciences seeks

applicants for a tenure track position at the Assistant Professor level with a focus on community or individual mental health and well-being. The position will begin mid-August 2027. Applicants should have completed or expect to complete a Ph.D. in Counseling Psychology or related field (such as community psychology with a mental-health lens, clinical, etc.) by August 2027. Licensure and experience in practice is

preferred. The department seeks to enhance teaching and research that advances diversity, equity, and inclusion, and candidates with demonstrated experience and/or scholarly interests related to these topics are especially encouraged to apply. Candidates must show evidence of high-quality teaching at the undergraduate level. Teaching will include courses in our health and well-being curriculum requirement (adult

and/or child psychopathology, community psychology, health psychology), as well as introduction to psychology, with the possibility to develop advanced special topics courses relating to one’s area of expertise and courses in the Elon Core Curriculum. Candidates interested in offering courses that align with Elon’s Advancing Equity Requirement are especially welcomed. The ability to maintain a sustained research

agenda that actively incorporates undergraduate mentees into one’s program of research at Elon is essential. Candidates must also be capable of supervising internships. Elon employs a Teacher-Scholar-Mentor model of faculty engagement, with faculty typically teaching six courses per year and teaching is a priority. Elon is committed to supporting faculty as teacher-scholar-mentors through the provision of excellent

teaching spaces, research facilities and support, and professional development resources. Candidates are encouraged to identify their experiences in contributing to a climate of inclusivity in their cover letters. Elon is a dynamic private, co-educational, comprehensive institution that is a national model for actively engaging faculty and students in teaching and learning in a liberal arts based residential campus.
