Assistant Professor - Counselor Education: Clinical Mental Health Counseling FACULTY VACANCY ANNOUNCEMENT The successful candidate will be expected to demonstrate knowledge and skills for teaching graduate-level courses (on campus and online) in multiple facets within the Counselor Education: Clinical Mental Health Counseling curriculum. Candidate may also be assigned to teach undergraduate courses. The candidate

will share responsibility for program, committee, and department assignments, including administrative, supervisory, program and course development, CACREP compliance, and other duties as assigned; advise master’s students; engage in and maintain an active program of research and scholarly work, including professional publications and presentations; maintain professional identity as a Counselor Educator and serve on

program, departmental, school, college, and university committees. Responsibilities include maintaining a teaching load of 18 credits per academic year, serving the Counselor Education: Clinical Mental Health Counseling program, the CLLSE department, the School of Education, and the CUNY Lehman College community.Faculty are expected to maintain a regular on campus presence for teaching, office hours, departmental and

other activities. The teaching will include online teaching; however, this this is not a remote position. Faculty may assist in program and clinical coordination. Other responsibilities include: Collaborate across departments and/or schools. Assist with assessment, accreditation, and program development initiatives. Seek external funding for research and program improvements. Assist with seeking articulation

agreements and educational partnerships with non-profit, community-based, and other organizations and/or institutions. Perform service to the college, school, and department through committee membership. Assist with program hiring, staffing, and mentoring part-time faculty. Participate actively in student recruitment, application, and admissions processes. The Lehman College campus is located in the Bronx, New York,

and is a senior college within the City University of New York (CUNY) system. Lehman is the only four-year public college in the Bronx and is recognized as a Hispanic-Serving Institution. The teaching load is 18 hours per academic year.
