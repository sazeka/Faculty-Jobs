Biology at the University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB) invites applications for a full-time, non-tenure-track faculty position (9-month appointment) at the rank of Assistant Professor. The successful candidate will primarily teach introductory-level human physiology and/or microbiology courses, with a particular emphasis on supporting the learning and success of first-year and first-generation college students. The

anticipated start date is August 15, 2027. Our department is committed to excellence in undergraduate and graduate education through high-quality instruction, experiential learning, and student mentorship. Faculty expertise spans molecular and biomedical sciences, physiology, evolutionary biology, ecology, and environmental sciences. We foster a collaborative academic environment that values evidence-based teaching,

curriculum innovation, and meaningful student engagement. Teaching faculty play an integral role in advancing our educational mission through effective instruction, curriculum development, and a commitment to creating supportive learning environments that promote student success. As part of a leading research university and academic medical center, the Department of Biology offers opportunities for interdisciplinary

collaboration, community engagement, and innovative teaching. Located in UAB’s Science & Engineering Complex, the department benefits from modern teaching laboratories, research spaces, and technologies that support hands-on learning and scientific discovery.
