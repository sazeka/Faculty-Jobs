(Fixed Term) Salary Range: $44,144.00 - $99,016.00 Job Description Anoka Technical College is seeking Nursing Assistant faculty members for full-time and part-time teaching. Day, evening, and some weekend courses are available, and course schedules can be modified to accommodate instructor preferences. This position instructs students using the Minnesota State Curriculum for Nursing Assistants in preparation to take

the state competency exam and register for the Minnesota Department of Health Nursing Assistant Registry. If you meet the minimum qualifications and have a desire to make a deeper impact—to shape the next generation of Nursing Assistants and help guide the future of the profession—we encourage you to apply for this rewarding position. No previous teaching experience is required, and if you’re new to higher education,

we have incredible faculty and professional development opportunities that will help you master the craft. Salary Range: Compensation will be based upon the number of credits assigned, education, and related work experience. For more information on wages: MSCF Contract Minimum Qualifications Education Requirement Diploma or associate degree in nursing. Occupational Experience Requirement Four full-time years (or

equivalent) of verified related paid work experience of direct patient care, one year must be in long term care. Recency Requirement One year of this work experience shall be within the five years immediately preceding the date of application for the credential field. The recency requirement shall be waived if the individual has two years of successful full-time (or equivalent) postsecondary teaching experience in

the credential field within the last five years. Professional Requirement Current Minnesota Registered Nurse (RN) license. Other Requirements Please upload these required documents under the Resume/Cover Section at the time of application: Resume/CV Copy of Minnesota RN license Unofficial transcript(s) NOTICE: The college regrets that it is unable to offer H-1B sponsorship.
