Job Details Coastal Carolina University, Department of Nursing and Health Sciences, within the Conway Medical Center College of Health and Human Performance, is seeking applications for a nursing clinical and laboratory coordinator with primary focus in clinical, simulation and/or laboratory instruction. This position will address instructional needs in the Nursing and

Health Sciences Department, starting in January 2027. The successful candidate will play a crucial role in coordinating the education of undergraduate students in clinical and laboratory settings. The Clinical and Laboratory Coordinator will facilitate the clinical learning experiences across all nursing pathways as a clinical liaison and through the acquisition of suitable sites, assurance of onboarding, compliance,

orientation of faculty and students to the clinical sites, and enforcement of student policies related to requirements of clinical affiliates. The position is responsible for daily operations of the nursing laboratories including preparing, maintaining, and troubleshooting equipment, supplies, and support for lab activities. Clinical and laboratory instruction as needed are expected. Candidates must possess a minimum

of a bachelors (or higher) degree in nursing, hold an unencumbered active license as a registered nurse in South Carolina or another Compact state, have a minimum of two years of clinical experience as a registered or advanced practice nurse related to the primary area of responsibility. Preferred qualifications include a record of clinical or laboratory teaching, experience in instructional settings, and experience

working with nursing students. Candidates should complete the online application to: https://jobs.coastal.edu/postings/123079and submit a letter of application, a current CV, copies of all transcripts, proof of current RN licensure, contact information for three references and statement of clinical teaching experience/teaching philosophy.
