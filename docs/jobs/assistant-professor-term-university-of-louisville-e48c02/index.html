Department: Pathology Location: Health Sciences Center Time Type: Full time Worker Type: Regular Term (Fixed Term) Job Req ID: R109656 Position Description: The Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine at the University of Louisville is experiencing a sustained period of growth and expansion and is seeking a full‑time or part‑time academic Hematopathologist to join our collegial and evolving faculty. The

successful candidate will demonstrate a strong commitment to the academic mission, including scholarly activity and participation in graduate and undergraduate medical education. Applicants must be board certified or board eligible in Anatomic Pathology and/or Clinical Pathology and have completed fellowship training in Hematopathology. Additional expertise in Molecular Pathology, Coagulation, Transfusion Medicine,

Pathology Informatics, and/or another Anatomic Pathology subspecialty is highly desirable. The position is well suited for clinical educators and physician‑scientists , including candidates with an established research program or those seeking to develop one. Faculty will participate in the mentorship of residents and fellows, with opportunities to contribute to career development, educational innovation, and

academic advancement within the department. The selected faculty member will join three experienced and collegial academic Hematopathologists who are committed to ensuring seamless onboarding and integration. The service offers a rich and diverse case mix, including bone marrow evaluations, lymph node biopsies, peripheral blood smear reviews, and flow cytometry interpretation. Leadership opportunities , including

service as a Laboratory Medical Director, are available for candidates with appropriate interest and qualifications. Prior academic experience and a record of educational and scholarly accomplishment are desirable. Applicants must be eligible for an unrestricted medical license in the Commonwealth of Kentucky . Academic rank, tenure status, and salary will be commensurate with qualifications, experience, and

scholarly achievements. The Department is a recognized leader in digital pathology and artificial intelligence , with a fully digital clinical workflow that provides faculty and trainees with unique professional opportunities and flexibility, including remote access. Faculty benefit from a robust institutional research infrastructure, including interdisciplinary collaborations across the Health Sciences Campus,
