JOB TITLE Adjunct Instructor – Biomanufacturing LOCATION Worcester DEPARTMENT NAME Biomanufacturing Education and Training Center - BETC - JM DIVISION NAME Worcester Polytechnic Institute - WPI JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY The Biomanufacturing Education & Training Center (BETC) at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) is seeking to expand its pool of adjunct instructors to support hands-on training programs in its

state-of-the-art, pilot-scale mammalian biomanufacturing facility. BETC is the first center in New England to offer intensive, immersive education and workforce training for professionals currently working in the biotechnology and biopharmaceutical industries, as well as individuals seeking to transition into biomanufacturing careers. We are looking for experienced subject matter experts who are passionate about

sharing industry knowledge and training the next generation of biomanufacturing talent. JOB DESCRIPTION Responsibilities: Adjunct instructors teach onsite at the BETC facility and facilitate both classroom instruction and hands-on laboratory training. Instructors will guide participants through core concepts and real‑world applications across the biomanufacturing workflow. Specifically, we are seeking subject matter

experts in one or more of the following areas: Upstream Processing of Mammalian (Animal) Cell Cultures Cell culture fundamentals Bioreactor operations Process monitoring and optimization Downstream Processing Principles and Techniques Harvest, clarification, and purification Chromatography and filtration Process understanding within a GMP framework Instructional assignments are flexible and may include teaching in a

specific technical area or across multiple modules, depending on expertise. Evening and flexible scheduling options are available to accommodate industry professionals. Requirements: Minimum of 5 years of professional experience working in the bioprocessing or biomanufacturing industry Demonstrated ability to operate laboratory and bioprocessing equipment in a simulated GMP manufacturing environment Prior teaching,
