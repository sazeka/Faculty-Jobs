Postdoctoral Instructor - Richardson Chair #3 Fellowship 2027Apply now DescriptionThe Department of Mathematics at the University of Texas at Austin invites applications for a Richardson Chair #3 Postdoctoral Fellowship supervised by Professor Alexis Vasseur in the research area of Partial Differential Equations. The opening is for academic year 2027-2028. We are interested in candidates who will contribute to

mentoring through teaching, research, and service. The initial appointment will be for one year, with the option to renew it for up to two additional years. The appointment will consist of a 1/1 teaching load per long semester. The annual salary is $70,000 annually with insurance and retirement benefits in addition to other programs and services. Additionally, the fellowship will provide $4,000 annually for research

travel on a departmental account. The applications review process will start on November 6, 2026.
