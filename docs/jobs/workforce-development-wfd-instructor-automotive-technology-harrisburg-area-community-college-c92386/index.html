Workforce Development (WFD) Instructor - Automotive Technology 494668 Harrisburg, PA, Pennsylvania Non exempt Job Summary: The Workforce Development (WFD) Instructor – Automotive Technology Automotive Technology Instructors are per diem instructors hired to teach on an annual contract basis. Develop and deliver Workforce Development course offerings specific to the Automotive Industry. Minimum Qualifications: 5 - 7

years of experience in the Automotive industry as a Technician (or combination of education and experience). Required certifications: PA State Vehicle Safety and Emissions Inspection and NATEF.For inspection and emission classes, PennDOT instructor certification for inspection and emissions. Licensing Requirements: Please note that all teaching faculty are required to comply with PA Act 15. You will need to present

completed clearances and training certificates at the time of hire. Please visit https://www.hacc.edu/HumanResources/NewHire/index.cfm (and scroll to PA Act 15 Clearances) for more information. Hiring Range: Varies Preferred Qualifications: Master-level Automotive Technician certified.
