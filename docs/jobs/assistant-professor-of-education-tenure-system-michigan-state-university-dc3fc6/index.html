Working/Functional Title Assistant Professor in Early/Elementary Literacy Position Summary Michigan State University actively promotes a dynamic research and learning environment in which qualified individuals of differing perspectives and cultural backgrounds pursue academic goals with mutual respect and shared inquiry. The Department of Teacher Education at Michigan State University (MSU), a national leader in

teacher preparation and research on teaching, teacher learning, curriculum, policy, and international education, seeks a tenure-system scholar in Early/Elementary Literacy. The successful candidate will join a vibrant intellectual community with longstanding strengths in literacy education and opportunities to contribute to innovative research, teaching, and teacher preparation focused on literacy learning and

instruction in early childhood and elementary settings. The Department of Teacher Education offers a rich environment for developing a sustained and nationally significant program of literacy scholarship. We welcome candidates whose research advances understanding of areas such as early and elementary literacy development and instruction, language and literacy, literacy teacher education, literacy assessment,

culturally and linguistically diverse learners, family and community engagement in literacy, and related areas of inquiry. The successful candidate will have opportunities to collaborate with faculty and students across literacy education and other areas of teacher education and to engage in interdisciplinary work throughout the College of Education and the broader university. The department has a longstanding

national reputation for excellence in teacher education. Its elementary and secondary teacher preparation programs have been ranked #1 by U.S. News & World Report and include robust urban and global education experiences that provide opportunities for examining literacy teaching and learning across diverse cultural, linguistic, community, and educational contexts.
