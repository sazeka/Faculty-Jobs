Assistant Professor of Marketing School of Business & Technology Search 5484- Posted October 12, 2026 The School of Business & Technology (SBT) at Emporia State University invites applications for an Assistant Professor of Marketing. Candidates should be prepared to teach a variety of marketing courses. Ability/willingness to teach online classes (including accelerated online classes) is required. The position is

housed in the AACSB-accredited School of Business & Technology and begins August 2027. This is a full-time, nine-month appointment. This position offers the successful candidate a choice between two appointment options: (1) a tenure-track appointment or (2) a non-tenure-track multi-year contract of up to three years. The successful candidate will be offered both options for consideration and may select the option

they believe best aligns with their professional goals and interests. RESPONSIBILITIES: Teach undergraduate and graduate courses in Marketing, Digital Marketing, Marketing Research, Strategic Marketing, Marketing Analytics, and other related subjects.Conduct and publish high-quality scholarly research in marketing.Engage in service activities to support the SBT and the university. QUALIFICATIONS: Terminal degree or

anticipation of completing a Ph.D. on or before August 2027 from an AACSB accredited business program or closely related field. ABDs may be considered.Relevant teaching and/or industry experience.Excellent teaching evaluations.Evidence of high-quality scholarly activity.Demonstrated ability to maintain Scholarly Academic status based on Faculty Qualifications policy of the School per AACSB standards. COMPENSATION:

Pay is commensurate with experience and qualifications.Compensation package includes a comprehensive benefits package. BENEFITS: Base pay is one component of Emporia State University’s total rewards package. We are dedicated to supporting the needs of the “whole you” with our holistic approach to employee benefits by offering comprehensive well-being benefits for you and your family that include: Health InsuranceFree
