Position summary

Adjunct Clinical Faculty - Mental Health Nursing The Mississippi Christian University School of Nursing invites applications for adjunct clinical faculty in Mental Health Nursing. Adjunct faculty play a vital role in preparing skilled, compassionate nurses by facilitating student learning in clinical settings and supporting their development of professional nursing competencies. Primary Responsibilities: Facilitate

student learning and clinical experiences in assigned nursing settings. Supervise, guide, and evaluate students during clinical rotations. Grade associated clinical assignments and provide timely, constructive feedback. Evaluate student performance based on established course and clinical objectives. Collaborate with full-time faculty to ensure consistency in course outcomes and clinical expectations. Minimum

Qualifications: Master’s degree in Nursing required. Active, unencumbered RN license in Mississippi. Minimum of one year of recent clinical experience in mental health nursing practice. Strong organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills. Must satisfy a criminal history background check. Must be able to meet and provide documentation of all health and compliance requirements as designated by the clinical

facility. About the School: The School of Nursing at Mississippi Christian University fosters a student-centered, collaborative environment that values clinical excellence, faith-based education, and innovation in nursing practice. Our accredited programs prepare graduates to provide compassionate, evidence-based care across diverse healthcare settings. How to apply: Mississippi Christian University uses Dynamic

Forms for the employment application process. To apply, you must create an account with Dynamic Forms. Once logged in, select the faculty application type.
