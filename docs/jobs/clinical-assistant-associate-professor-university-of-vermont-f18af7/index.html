Advertising Copy The Department of Nursing in the College of Nursing and Health Sciences (CNHS) at the University of Vermont (UVM) invites applications for a full-time, non-tenure track position at the rank of clinical assistant/associate professor to serve as a clinical

faculty member and Department of Nursing Director of Simulation Education. In addition to contributing to the teaching mission of the department, this position provides strategic, administrative, and faculty leadership for all simulation-based education within the Department of Nursing, including low-, medium-, and high-fidelity simulation. This is a 10-month appointment.Responsibilities•Teach in the undergraduate

and/or graduate Nursing programs• Provide leadership for all nursing simulation activities. • Design, implement, and evaluate simulation learning experiences aligned with curricular outcomes. • Collaborate with nursing faculty on scenario design and standardized patient training. • Serve as Department of Nursing representative on CSL Operations Team and Advisory Board. • Support faculty development in simulation
