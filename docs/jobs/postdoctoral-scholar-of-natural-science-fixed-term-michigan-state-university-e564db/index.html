1169918 Position Summary The successful candidate will work with Dr. Kunlun Qi < https://kunlun-qi.github.io> on research projects related to kinetic equation and its related multiscale model reduction, numerical method, and data-driven/machine-learning approaches, including: · Numerical simulation of kinetic equations: fast algorithms with stability/convergence analysis; · Data-driven and machine learning-assisted

methods: data assimilation, uncertainty quantification (UQ), and machine-learning-based moment closure models. · Multiscale modeling: kinetic limits of many-particle dynamical systems, hydrodynamic limits of kinetic models, and semiclassical limits of quantum systems. · Theoretical analysis for kinetic PDEs: Well-posedness and asymptotic behavior of the Boltzmann equation and related models Equal Employment
