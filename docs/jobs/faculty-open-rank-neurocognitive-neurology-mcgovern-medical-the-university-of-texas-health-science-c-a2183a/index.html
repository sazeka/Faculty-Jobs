Position summary

JOB DESCRIPTION The Department of Neurology at McGovern Medical School at UTHealth Houston is seeking an outstanding faculty physician to join the Division of Dementia and Memory Disorders at the rank of Associate Professor or Professor, commensurate with academic accomplishments and experience. McGovern Medical School at UTHealth Houston is committed to advancing innovative patient care, education, and discovery. We

invite applications from neurologists passionate about improving the lives of patients and families affected by dementia and other neurodegenerative disorders and eager to contribute to a dynamic, collaborative academic environment.Our ideal candidate brings expertise or a strong interest in cognitive neurology, dementia, and memory disorders, with particular enthusiasm for clinical trials, translational research,

neurodegenerative movement disorders, and the psychiatric manifestations of neurodegenerative disease.This is an exciting opportunity to join a growing academic program and contribute to the division's missions of clinical excellence, discovery, education, and service. The successful candidate will have the opportunity to develop and expand their clinical and academic interests while collaborating with

multidisciplinary colleagues across UTHealth Houston. Position Highlights:Clinical practice focused on dementia, memory disorders, and cognitive neurology.Opportunities to participate in and develop clinical trials and translational research.Opportunities for collaboration in neurodegenerative movement disorders and neuropsychiatric manifestations of neurodegenerative disease.Participation in the education and

mentorship of medical students, residents, fellows, and other learners.Opportunity to contribute to the growth and academic mission of a leading medical school and health sciences center. Qualifications:Medical degree (MD or DO) from an accredited medical school.Completion of an ACGME-accredited neurology residency.Completion of an ACGME-accredited fellowship in dementia, memory disorders, or cognitive neurologyABPN

board certification or eligibility in NeurologyTexas medical license or eligibility for Texas licensure.Excellent clinical skills, including comprehensive history-taking, neurological examination, and interpretation of diagnostic studies.Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to effectively communicate with patients, families, trainees, and interdisciplinary team members.Demonstrated
