Position summary

The Roosevelt School at Long Island University invites applications for a full-time Assistant or Associate Professor of International Relations & Diplomacy. This position is a Non-Tenure Track Appointment (NTTA). The successful candidate will demonstrate excellence in teaching international relations, diplomacy, and global affairs; a strong commitment to student mentorship; and the ability to integrate experiential

learning with academic instruction. Candidates should be prepared to teach undergraduate courses in areas such as international relations theory, U.S. foreign policy, international organizations, negotiation and conflict resolution, global governance, and diplomatic practice. Required Qualifications Master’s degree or Ph.D. in International Relations, Political Science, Diplomacy, Public Policy, or a closely related

field Demonstrated ability to teach effectively at the post-secondary level Strong knowledge of global political systems, international institutions, and contemporary foreign policy issues Excellent communication, organizational, and interpersonal skills Preferred Qualifications Ph.D. in International Relations, Political Science, or related discipline Professional experience in diplomacy, international

organizations, public service, policy analysis, or global non-profit leadership Experience with experiential learning models, including simulations, Model United Nations, policy labs, or diplomatic negotiations Record of scholarship, policy engagement, or applied research Demonstrated commitment to inclusive and equitable teaching practices Experience mentoring students for internships, fellowships, or careers in
