Purpose: The Core Faculty member in the Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) program is responsible for contributing to the academic, clinical, and scholarly mission of the program. This role includes teaching, scholarship, service, and active participation in program assessment and continuous

improvement. Core faculty members are essential to ensuring program quality, student success, and compliance with accreditation standards established by the Commission on Accreditation in Physical Therapy Education (CAPTE). Primary Responsibilities: Teaching and Instruction  Teach assigned courses across the DPT curriculum, including didactic, laboratory, and/or clinical components.  Utilize evidence-informed,
