Posting Information Posting Information Position Title Founding Dean, Ingram College of Engineering Job Posting Number 2027037TTL Job Location San Marcos College/Division Ingram College of Engineering Department Office of the Provost and EVPAA Position Description Texas State University (TXST) invites applications and nominations for a nationally recognized scholar and administrator

to lead the newly established Ingram College of Engineering as the founding Dean. Reporting to the Provost and Executive Vice President for Academic Affairs, the Dean is a member of the Provost’s leadership team and serves as the chief academic and administrative officer for the Ingram College of Engineering. This is a historic opportunity to launch a new college in a high-growth environment, building on more than 20

years of strong engineering development at Texas State University. The Dean is responsible for developing and implementing a strategic vision that continues the growth in the research portfolio, advances excellence in undergraduate and graduate education, promotes student success, and builds deep industry and philanthropic support for the Ingram College of Engineering, while effectively managing resources and

personnel. The Dean will oversee a team of associate deans, chairs, and directors, as well as administrative staff that manages a wide range of support functions. This opportunity comes amid extraordinary momentum across Texas State.
