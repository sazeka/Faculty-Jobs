Posting Details Position Information Job Title Dean of the School of Arts and Sciences Position Summary Serves as Chief Academic Officer for the School of Arts and Sciences, provides advocacy for, leadership to and has managerial

responsibility for the programs, budgets, course schedules, faculty and students of the School. Serves as a representative of the University administration to the faculty, staff and students of the School, as well as to the community. Reports directly to the Provost and Senior Vice President. Working with the department chairs and program directors, the Dean is responsible for the School’s academic program quality,

assessment of curriculum and instruction, fostering faculty research and scholarship, budgeting and fiscal management, fundraising, and new program development. Teaches courses in their discipline as appropriate to the needs of the School and in consultation with the Provost/Senior Vice President. Serves on appropriate University committees, task forces, search committees and in other roles as requested. Anticipated

start date of Summer 2027 Essential Duties and Responsibilities Leadership and Strategic Direction Provide leadership in support of the University’s strategic plan as well as in the development and execution of the School’s strategic plan, goals, and assessment. Provide leadership and direction to instructional and program staff, faculty, and adjunct faculty in developing and sustaining programs, instructional

strategies, and program offerings. Recruit, develop and retain quality faculty and staff members. Evaluate faculty and staff based on performance criteria that supports and advances the School and the University. Recommend employment, retention, promotion, and termination of instructors and staff to the Provost. Assist in resolving faculty and staff issues, including student grievances, academic integrity, and safety

concerns. Curriculum and Program Oversight Oversee the School’s academic programs to ensure quality and relevance. Ensure that effective program planning and assessment is carried out in the School to accomplish the goals and objectives of the School’s and University’s Strategic Plan as well as to meet accreditation standards of SACSCOC.
