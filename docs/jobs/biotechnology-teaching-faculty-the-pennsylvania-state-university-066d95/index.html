POSITION SPECIFICS The Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology (BMB) at Penn State invites applications for a Lecturer, Assistant Teaching Professor, or Associate Teaching Professor appointment with expertise in Biotechnology, Biochemistry,

Molecular Biology, Microbiology, and/or related fields. We are seeking a dynamic, collaborative, and self-motivated educator who is passionate about teaching and committed to mentoring the next generation of biotechnology scientists and entrepreneurs. The department is particularly interested in candidates with experience in the biotechnology industry who will work to strengthen and grow our Biotechnology B.S.

program. The ideal candidate will demonstrate the ability to work effectively across disciplines, fostering partnerships with industry, academia, and the community to enhance our undergraduate biotechnology offerings. A proactive collaborator who takes the lead in developing innovative curricula, cultivating industry connections, and creating opportunities for student engagement will thrive in this role. Expectations
