Leadership Profile Provost and Vice President of Academic Affairs This leadership profile is intended to provide information about Regis College and the position of Provost and Vice President of Academic Affairs. It is designed to assist qualified individuals in assessing their interest in this position. Opportunity and Position Summary The Provost and Vice President of Academic Affairs (Provost) will be an energetic

and innovative leader who will provide vision for all aspects of academic life at the university. The Provost will ensure both the high quality of academic programs and their alignment with student demand and workforce need; strengthen teaching and research; and support the success of diverse students in their academic work and post-graduation planning. The Provost will play a significant role on the

university&rsquo;s leadership team in achieving the institution's strategic goals, including expanding Regis' standing, locally and nationally, as a leader in the education of health professionals, building on the university&rsquo;s reputation in nursing to encompass its broader scope of health professions programs, grounded in Regis' mission and in the durable capacities of the liberal arts, which underpin every

major. The new Provost must be a seasoned, strategic leader with demonstrated accomplishments; the ability to embody and promote the university&rsquo;s mission; and a clear sense of the complex challenges of an institution serving traditional and non-traditional undergraduate and graduate students in professional and in liberal arts schools, with both campus based and online offerings. This is a role that depends on

partnership. The Provost will work closely with colleagues across every division &ndash; enrollment management, advancement, finance, student affairs, ministry and service, and marketing and communications &ndash; and success will come through building shared purpose rather than directing from the academic side alone. Extraordinary communication skills are essential, as is the ability to foster a culture that is both

collaborative and innovative. The successful candidate will be both empathetic and accountable. Regis is a community that knows its people by name and takes seriously the CSJ commitment to excellence tempered by gentleness.
