Visiting Assistant Professor, Healthcare Administration 496842 Full-Time Healthcare Admin Program Department: Healthcare Admin Program Salary: Commensurate with Experience/Education The Department of Healthcare Administration at the University of Houston–Clear Lake prepares students for management and leadership careers across the health sector. The department offers four degree programs - the BS in Healthcare

Administration, the BAS in Healthcare Services, the Master of Healthcare Administration (MHA), and the joint Master of Healthcare Administration / Master of Business Administration (MHA/MBA) - serving approximately 400 students. Graduate instruction is delivered at the university's Texas Medical Center location, placing students and faculty inside the largest medical complex in the world. The department maintains

deep practice ties across the Houston market, and its alumni hold executive positions regionally and nationally, including hospital and health system CEO roles. The College of Business is AACSB-accredited. Description: The Department of Healthcare Administration seeks an accomplished healthcare executive to join the faculty in a full-time instructional appointment. The position is designed for a senior leader who has

stepped back from - or is stepping between - operating roles and wants to invest that experience in the next generation of healthcare administration leaders. The successful candidate teaches across the department's undergraduate and graduate programs, mentors students moving toward fellowships and early-career leadership roles and strengthens the connective tissue between the classroom and the Houston healthcare

market. This is not a research appointment. The department seeks a leader with substantive operational responsibility, the ability to translate that experience into instruction, and a commitment to learner success.
