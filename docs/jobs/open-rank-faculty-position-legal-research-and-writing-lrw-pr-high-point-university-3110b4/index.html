Open Rank Faculty Position, Legal Research and Writing (LRW) Program, 10 months

High Point University  ·  High Point, NC
FacultyLaw

Position summary

The Kenneth F. Kahn School of Law at High Point University offers a unique opportunity to continue building a distinctive law school committed to honoring God, family, and country, while promoting free inquiry, professional excellence, and a faith-friendly community. The School of Law benefits from strong momentum; robust university support and financial resources; respected and experienced institutional leadership;

talented and motivated students; collegial faculty and staff; a brand new, first-class facility; and an innovative curriculum focused on holistic professional formation. The law faculty seeks to add one (1) full-time colleague in the Legal Research and Writing (LRW) Program who will contribute to a stimulating intellectual environment and who desires to join a community that appreciates an entrepreneurial spirit,

creative collaboration, and high aspirations. The faculty member will generally teach three (3) sections of LRW I (fall) and LRW II (spring), with approximately ten (10) students in each section. Salary and academic rank (assistant, associate, or full professor of law) will be commensurate with qualifications and experience. After an initial service period, the position is eligible for presumptively renewable 5-year

contracts (sometimes referred to as “clinical tenure” in furtherance of American Bar Association Standard 405(c) and (d)). Please direct inquiries and applications to the chair of the appointments committee, Prof. John DeStefano, at jdestef1@highpoint.edu. Applications must include a cover letter, a curriculum vitae, J.D. transcript, and three professional references. Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis.

The anticipated start date for this position is August 1, 2027. Minimum Qualifications: Juris Doctor from an ABA-accredited law school. Exemplary academic record. Bar license in at least one state or the District of Columbia (active or inactive). “Sufficient experience as a practicing attorney, with a well-rounded view of the legal profession and the settings in which legal writing occurs; demonstrated excellence in

legal analysis, research, and writing; an interest in pedagogy and scholarship related to the LRW discipline; and the ability to communicate effectively, both in the classroom and in one-on-one conferences with students” (American Bar Association ’s Legal Writing Sourcebook (3d ed. 2020)). Strong desire to contribute to the mission and vision of High Point University and the Kenneth F. Kahn School of Law. Articulated
