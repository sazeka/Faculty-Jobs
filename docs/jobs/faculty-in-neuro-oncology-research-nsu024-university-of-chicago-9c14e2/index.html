Faculty in Neuro-oncology Research #NSU024

University of Chicago  ·  Department of Neurological Surgery  ·  Chicago, IL
FacultyTenure-TrackNeurological Surgery

Position summary

The University of Chicago's Department of Neurological Surgery is rapidly growing and expanding its focus on brain tumor research and treatment. We invite applications for full-time faculty members at any rank to oversee a research program in any area of basic and/or translational research in neuro-oncology. Appointees will be based on the University of Chicago’s Hyde Park campus. Appointees will teach at the

undergraduate and/or graduate level and may participate in clinical care if appropriate. Academic rank, track (BSD tenure-track or School of Medicine track), and compensation are dependent upon qualifications. These positions are benefits-eligible. The University of Chicago offers a wide range of benefits programs and resources for eligible employees, including health, retirement, and paid time off. Information about

the benefit offerings can be found in the Benefits Guidebook . The Department of Neurological Surgery is engaged in cutting-edge neurosurgical research and development, focusing on pioneering new treatments for a range of neurological conditions. This includes ongoing investigations into treatments for brain tumors, epilepsy, CNS congenital anomalies, and neurovascular disease. While the primary faculty appointment

will be in the Department of Neurological Surgery, appointees may have an opportunity for secondary appointments in other departments of the Biological Sciences Division (BSD) dependent upon interests and qualifications. There will be multiple opportunities for appointees to collaborate with faculty in the following programs: the Ben May Department for Cancer Research, the Ludwig Center of Chicago, the Department of

Medicine’s Section of Hematology/Oncology, and The University of Chicago Medicine Comprehensive Cancer Center and its Abbvie Foundation Cancer Pavilion (opening in 2027), and the University of Chicago Neuroscience Institute. The University of Chicago is a vibrant center of scientific discovery and innovation. The Biological Sciences Division, other academic units of the University, and the University of Chicago

Medical Center are all contained within one compact campus in the Hyde Park neighborhood of Chicago. Appointees will have access to state-of-the-art core facilities, outstanding colleagues, and graduate students affiliated with numerous degree-granting programs within and outside the Biological Sciences.
