Director of the Doctor of Social Work (DSW) program and plays a pivotal leadership role in the administration and advancement of the program. Key responsibilities include marketing the program and actively recruiting prospective students, overseeing the admissions process, and assigning incoming students to appropriate cohorts. The Director will teach three doctoral seminars/undergraduate courses and summer courses

annually, evaluate comprehensive exams, and participate on all dissertation committees to ensure academic rigor and consistency. Additionally, the Director ensures timely completion of all requirements for students’ “Application for Admission to Candidacy,” manages student concerns and requests, and provides mentorship and support to faculty teaching within the DSW curriculum. This position requires a strategic and

student-centered approach to program management, academic leadership, and faculty collaboration.
