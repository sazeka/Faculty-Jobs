Successful candidates will: Have a strong record of innovation and research productivity Maintain an active,

applicants should submit the following materials: Cover letter Curriculum vitae Statement of Research Focus (Specific Request 1) Statement of Teaching Philosophy (Specific Request 2) Statement of Service Philosophy (Specific Request 3) Names and contact information of three references Applications will be reviewed as they are received and will continue to be accepted until the position is filled.
