Assistant/Associate Professor and Director of Teacher EducationDepartment: Teacher EducationEmployment Type: Full TimeSalary Range: $70,000-$85,000Reports to: Dean of Faculty, Academic Affairs Who We Are?Ferrum College is committed to providing a supportive academic environment that promotes student success, professional growth, and service to others. Guided by the College's motto, "Not Self, But Others," Ferrum

College strives to uphold academic excellence, collaborative decision-making, and meaningful partnerships with the communities it serves.The Teacher Education Department prepares future educators through undergraduate and graduate programs designed to support effective teaching, professional development, and student achievement. Through strong academic leadership, curriculum development, and partnerships with local

school divisions, the department works to prepare qualified educators who are ready to make a positive impact in the classroom and beyond.What We're Looking ForFerrum College is seeking a collaborative, experienced, and forward-thinking Assistant/Associate Professor and Director of Teacher Education to provide academic leadership and administrative oversight for the College's undergraduate and graduate teacher

education programs.This is a full-time, 12-month, hybrid faculty-administrative leadership position with a 1:1 teaching load. The Director is responsible for overseeing program operations, supervising faculty and administrative staff, managing curriculum and academic policies, leading accreditation and licensure compliance, overseeing budgets, and developing partnerships with local public school divisions and other

educational organizations.The successful candidate will demonstrate strong leadership, organizational, and communication skills, along with experience in higher education or PK-12 education management. This position requires a commitment to collaborative decision-making, shared governance, academic quality, continuous program improvement, and the successful preparation of future educators.Key

ResponsibilitiesAdministrative & Program LeadershipDevelop and refine program policies in collaboration with Teacher Education faculty, ensuring alignment with College handbooks, shared governance practices, and academic standards.Oversee, balance, and allocate program budget resources in accordance with College and departmental requirements.Manage admissions processes for undergraduate and graduate teacher education
