Mizzou Engineering invite nominations and applications for Chair of the David L. Payne Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS).The future of engineering and computing is being rewritten—and the next chair of Mizzou's David L. Payne Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) has an extraordinary opportunity to help shape what comes next. Spanning electrical engineering,

computer engineering, computer science, information technology, and data science, EECS brings together expertise in fields transforming industry and society—from AI and quantum technologies to cybersecurity, geospatial intelligence, bioinformatics, and energy. With substantial research strengths, interdisciplinary partnerships, and institutional investment, the department is poised to play a defining role in

advancing Mizzou Engineering's strategic vision to Educate Future Leaders, Drive Engineering Impact, and Foster Engineering Connections.At the convergence of rapid technological change and historic investment, EECS has an opportunity to reimagine its academic programs, strengthen student success, and develop pathways aligned with tomorrow's workforce. David L. Payne's transformational $50 million gift is supporting a

comprehensive renovation of EECS’s home (Naka Hall), significant laboratory and equipment investments, and a departmental endowment. The University's Payne Energy Innovation Center, which will house EECS faculty and research activities, further expands these possibilities. Together with Mizzou Forward and major research initiatives—including the Center for Geospatial Intelligence, Mizzou AERI, Quantum Innovation

Center, and MUMSEI—these investments provide a powerful platform to accelerate discovery, strengthen research and faculty capacity, and elevate EECS's national prominence.EECS serves approximately 1,000 undergraduate and 220 graduate students across five undergraduate and seven graduate programs.
