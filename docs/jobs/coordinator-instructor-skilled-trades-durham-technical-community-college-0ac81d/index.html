Durham Technical Community College is hiring a Coordinator/Instructor of Skilled Trades in the Building, Engineering & Skilled Trades program. The Coordinator/Instructor, Skilled Trades provides leadership, administration, and instructional support for programs within the Building, Engineering & Skilled Trades (BEST) Pathway at the Orange County Campus (OCC) extension, including HVAC, Plumbing, and Electrical

Systems. This position oversees program operations and strategic initiatives by planning, implementing, coordinating, and evaluating program activities, personnel, equipment, and services; managing cross-functional projects; coordinating course schedules; facilitating communication among stakeholders; and ensuring timely completion of program goals. Working closely with the Dean, the Coordinator/Instructor supports

program development, continuous improvement, and student success while maintaining alignment with departmental and college strategic priorities. As needed, the incumbent also develops and teaches courses within the skilled trades programs and must possess strong knowledge of industry standards and practices related to HVAC, Plumbing, and Electrical Systems.
