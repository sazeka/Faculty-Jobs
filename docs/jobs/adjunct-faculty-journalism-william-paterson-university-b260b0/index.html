Journalism The Department of Communication is seeking adjunct faculty members to develop and teach undergraduate courses in our growing

journalism program. The Department is seeking candidates with professional experience in journalism. Prior teaching experience is a plus. A master's degree (M.A., M.F.A. or M.B.A.) or commensurate professional experience is required. Areas of expertise should include some or all of the following: news reporting, news writing, feature writing, social/digital media management, and audio and television production.
