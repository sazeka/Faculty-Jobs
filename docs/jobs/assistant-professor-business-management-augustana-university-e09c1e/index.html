Assistant Professor, Business Management

Augustana University  ·  Business Management  ·  Sioux Falls, SD
Assistant ProfessorBusiness Management

Position summary

Augustana University invites applications for the position of full-time Assistant Professor of Business Management. Tenure track position. Responsibilities: Teaches graduate and undergraduate courses and conducts research in the business and management disciplines with special focus on the areas of business, management, or administration. Develops and designs curriculum to foster student learning, stimulate class

discussions, and encourage student engagement. Course offerings may be taught during the day, evening, or online, as well as taught using distance learning formats. Provides academic advising and assesses student outcomes. Provides intellectual leadership, conducts research, and makes contributions to the literature in the field of business. Engage in service to the department, university, and community Augustana

prides itself on excellence in-person classroom instruction, thus the successful applicant will demonstrate a strong commitment to teaching and mentoring students in a liberal arts environment. Qualifications: A PhD or DBA in a qualifying academic major, including Business, Business Administration, Management, or Strategic Management. ABD candidates accepted. Candidates with a discipline specific business degree

(finance, marketing) will also be considered. Post-secondary teaching experience and research accomplishment are preferred. Candidates with additional non-academic business experience are preferred. Interacting with students, faculty and leadership with different backgrounds and points of view and a deep appreciation of and respect for academic values and culture must be a top value of the candidate. If you are

looking for a rewarding and dynamic career, look no further than Augustana University in Sioux Falls, SD. Augustana offers an excellent benefit package including health insurance, dental insurance, life insurance, AD&D, short- and long-term disability, retirement plan, tuition assistance, holiday, vacation and sick pay. Application Procedure: The position will remain open until filled. Interested applicants are asked

to submit the below application materials via our Career Center Portal. If assistance is needed during the application process, please reach out to humanresources@augie.edu . cover letter statement of teaching philosophy copies of undergraduate and graduate transcripts a curriculum vita three letters of reference Augustana University is an Equal Opportunity/Affirmative Action/Title IX Employer.
