JOB DESCRIPTION - Residential Adjunct Location: Tulsa, OK The Oral Roberts University School of Liberal Arts invites applications for a residential adjunct faculty position in Arabic beginning August 2025.

RESPONSIBILITIES Our department is looking for a dynamic individual to promote our Arabic language program. The candidate duties include but are not limited to teaching elementary through advanced levels of Arabic, including Business Arabic and Arab Civilization and Culture courses. QUALIFICATIONS Candidates must have a master's degree and native or near fluency in Arabic. While a master's degree in Arabic is

preferred, candidates with Arabic-language proficiency and experience teaching Arabic may be considered. Candidates must be able to demonstrate effective teaching and leadership skills. Knowledge of educational technology and openness to expanding these skills are a must. Oral Roberts University is a private liberal arts university dedicated to educating the whole person located in Tulsa, Oklahoma, that operates in
