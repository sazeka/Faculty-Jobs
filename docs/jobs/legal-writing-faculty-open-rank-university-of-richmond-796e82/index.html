Position summary

The University of Richmond School of Law is seeking applicants for two full-time faculty members to join our first-year Legal Analysis and Writing program in the 2027-2028 school year. Both experienced and entry-level candidates are encouraged to apply. Appointments may be made at the rank of Assistant, Associate, or full Professor of Law, Legal Practice, commensurate with the candidate’s qualifications and

experience. The University of Richmond’s first-year Legal Analysis and Writing ( LAW) course is a two-semester, five-credit program (three credits in the Fall and two in the Spring) that focuses on legal analysis and core principles of communication and expository writing, such as audience and purpose analysis, organization, and presentation. In addition to the LAW course, first-year students take a one-credit legal

research course taught by library faculty. Faculty members will teach one section of LAW for first-year J.D. students. Sections typically contain between 27 and 30 students. Faculty will also teach one additional course in line with their experience, expertise, and school need. The five-member LAW faculty team coordinates learning objectives to ensure all first-year students receive instruction in the same

fundamental legal writing and analysis skills. All faculty members are committed to providing multiple opportunities for students to practice written and oral communication and significant personalized feedback on all major assignments. Faculty members have autonomy with respect to all pedagogical choices for achieving these objectives, including reading assignments, classroom exercises, teaching materials, and

problem design, selection, and implementation.
