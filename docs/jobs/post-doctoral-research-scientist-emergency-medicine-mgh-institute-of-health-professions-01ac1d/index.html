Job Summary The RESPOND Lab at MGH (https://mesk.mgh.harvard.edu/) is seeking candidates with a Ph.D. in biostatistics, applied mathematics, computer science, or a related quantitative field for a two-year Postdoctoral Research Fellow position. This position involves applying established methods from

network science to pediatric health access and outcome data. Working with a multi-disciplinary team of health services researchers, including experts in pediatric emergency medicine and network science, the successful candidate will apply techniques from network science to pediatric emergency care data, and develop expertise in project administration and applications of advanced analytic methods to health services.

Candidates should have a doctoral degree in biostatistics, statistics, or a related field. Strong computational skills and expertise in R is required. Experience in Python and network science are desired but is not required. Start date is negotiable.
