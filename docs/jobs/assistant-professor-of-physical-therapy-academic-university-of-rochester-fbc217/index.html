Position summary

The University of Rochester Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) Program, housed in the School of Nursing, invites applications for an Assistant Professor (tenure track) position. Candidates must hold a current physical therapy license and demonstrate a research interest in musculoskeletal-related physical therapy practice. The successful candidate will play a central role in advancing the research agenda at the

University of Rochester’s emerging DPT academic program — an exceptional opportunity to contribute to orthopedic rehabilitation research, education, and practice at a major academic medical center. All DPT faculty are aligned with collaborators across the University of Rochester Medical Center to "Learn, Discover, Heal, Create—and Make the World Ever Better."&nbsp; The DPT clinical faculty are expected to demonstrate

teaching excellence, develop an ongoing scholarship agenda, and serve on department and school committees.&nbsp; Faculty are encouraged to maintain clinical practice expertise through joint appointments and establish research collaborations with the University of Rochester clinical enterprise.&nbsp; Successful candidates will be part of a collaborative, solution-focused team of faculty and staff supporting the

tripartite mission of excellence in physical therapy education, research, and practice.
