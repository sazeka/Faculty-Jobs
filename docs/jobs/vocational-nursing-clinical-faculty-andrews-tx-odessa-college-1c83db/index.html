Vocational Nursing Clinical Faculty-Andrews, Tx Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Position Details Position Information Working Title Vocational Nursing Clinical Faculty-Andrews, Tx Position Status Full Time Department School of Health Sciences General Summary The Vocational Nursing Clinical Faculty member is responsible for delivering high-quality instruction with a primary emphasis on

clinical education and supervision of students in healthcare settings. This includes direct oversight of students during clinical experiences in a variety of settings, including hospitals and community agencies, as well as demonstration and reinforcement of nursing skills and clinical reasoning in simulation and laboratory environments. This position plays an essential role in supporting student success by creating

an engaging and effective learning environment that promotes the development of clinical competence, sound clinical judgment, critical thinking, and professional behavior. The instructor is responsible for teaching, modeling, and evaluating clinical judgment in real-time patient care situations, ensuring students are able to safely apply theoretical knowledge in practice. The Clinical Faculty member ensures that all

instructional and clinical activities align with program objectives, accreditation standards, and state regulatory requirements, as well as the policies and expectations outlined in the nursing program handbook. This role requires consistent enforcement of clinical standards, professional conduct, and safe patient care practices.Key responsibilities include maintaining accurate and up-to-date clinical and course

records; advising, tutoring, and counseling students to support their academic and clinical progression; and actively participating in student recruitment and selection. The instructor also collaborates with the program director in ongoing program evaluation and curriculum revision to ensure continuous improvement and alignment with current healthcare practices.
