Department: Psychology Location: Belknap Campus Time Type: Full time Worker Type: Regular Term (Fixed Term) Job Req ID: R109499 Position Description: The Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences at the University of Louisville invites applications for a Professor Term (Open Rank) and Director of the Noble H. Kelley Psychological Services Center (PSC). The start date is June 1, 2027. The PSC is a training clinic

located on campus and operated by the university’s APA-accredited Ph.D. program in Clinical Psychological Science. Clinical services are provided by Ph.D. students supervised by licensed psychologists in Kentucky with the Health Service Provider (HSP) designation. The PSC operates as a fully functioning psychological clinic, providing services for children, adolescents, and adults to the university and the larger

metropolitan community on a sliding scale basis. Services include individual, family, group, and couples psychotherapy as well as psychological assessments. More information about the PSC can be found on the website: https://artsandsciences.louisville.edu/academics-programs/academic-departments/psychological-and-brain-sciences/services/psychological-services-center The Director position is a full-time, 12-month

clinical Term faculty position and includes administrative (60%) and supervision and teaching (40%) responsibilities. The Director works collaboratively with program faculty, staff, and the department chair in overseeing clinic operations and has an essential role in training graduate students. The Director would provide clinical supervision in empirically supported interventions and assessments as well as teach

graduate courses to Ph.D. students in the program. Examples of courses include Professional and Ethical Issues in Clinical Psychology, Clinical Psychopathology, History of Modern Psychology, and Assessment.
