Department Latin American, Caribbean, and Latinx Studies Position Summary Bowdoin College’s Latin American, Caribbean, and Latinx Studies (LACLaS) Program at Bowdoin College invites applications for a full-time tenure-track faculty appointment in migration and border studies at the Assistant or Associate Professor level, beginning July 1, 2027. We seek a social or data scientist with a demonstrated interdisciplinary

approach to artificial intelligence and immigration and border policies impacting U.S. Latinx, Caribbean, and/or Latin America who will engage students in the technological, ethical, political, and/or social landscapes that intertwine these studies. The teaching load is two courses per semester, teaching across the curriculum, and participating in the teaching rotation of the program’s intro course and LACLaS major

requirements in the social sciences. Bowdoin is particularly interested in candidates dedicated to undergraduate teaching and with a promise of long-term successful research. A Ph.D. is expected by the date of the appointment.Bowdoin’s Hastings Initiative for AI and Humanity aims to critically examine, thoughtfully utilize, and ethically guide AI’s trajectory. A $50 million dollar gift by Reed Hastings funds ten new

faculty positions across disciplines; supports faculty in AI-related teaching, research, and creative work; sponsors workshops, symposia, and student research; and prepares the next generation of leaders to engage responsibly with the opportunities and challenges presented by artificial intelligence. Bowdoin College offers opportunities for professional development, a fully-funded, year-long pre-tenure sabbatical
