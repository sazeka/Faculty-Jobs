This position is primarily responsible for teaching, laboratory instruction, and coordination of selected courses

across the curriculum, as well as participation in integrated clinical education experiences The faculty member will contribute to a highly integrated, practice-oriented curriculum that combines didactic instruction with early and progressive clinical experiences, including participation in the program’s pro bono clinic. Responsibilities include course coordination, student assessment, and engagement in program

assessment and continuous improvement processes consistent with accreditation standards. The position requires maintenance of contemporary clinical expertise and engagement in scholarship appropriate to a teaching-focused faculty role.
