Description The University of Maine at Fort Kent is seeking a qualified Adjunct Clinical Instructor to provide clinical instruction at Eastern Maine Medical Center (Bangor, ME) for pediatric clinical rotations. Clinical instructors play an important role in the education of nursing students. They assist students to apply and integrate theoretical learning to client care situations. Clinical instructors enjoy flexible

scheduling and participate in student learning. Clinical instructors typically oversee 6 to 8 students at a time. The responsibilities of clinical instructors who work with our nursing students include: Supervising care provided by a group of students Coordinating with staff and supervisors at the clinical site to make student patient assignments. Teaching students how to perform duties in the clinical site and

answering their questions.
