Job Title Full-time Faculty in Business Department/School School of Business Classification Exempt (Salary) Compensation and Placement of the new hire within the salary pay grade is dependent on qualifications and Benefits professional teaching experience. The pay grade assigned to this position ranges from $67,067 – $86,279 annually. Benefits: May include medical, retirement, paid time off, and more. To learn more,

visit: wallawalla.edu/benefits. Available January 1, 2027 Description: The School of Business invites applications for a full-time faculty position in business to begin as soon as January 1, 2027. Core teaching responsibilities are in management and marketing but candidates with a range of experience including in AI/information systems, business law, and entrepreneurship are also encouraged to apply. Responsibilities

include: (a) teaching, academic advising, and mentoring students; (b) engaging in scholarly and professional activities; (c) serving on universities committees; (d) and demonstrating commitment to community service. Qualifications: Preferred qualifications include: (1) earned doctorate in business/management area; (2) other non-business doctorate, including juris doctorate, and an MBA degree or significant teaching

and work experience in the teaching area; (3) or an MBA or relevant master’s degree and significant teaching and work experience in the teaching area. Applicants must already have authorization to work in the U.S. Applicants should be able to: (a) use technology effectively in teaching and learning; (b) bring real-world experiences to the classroom; (c) work collaboratively; (d) show evidence of scholarly and

professional activity; (e) demonstrate a commitment to Christian higher education and the integration of faith and learning. Apply: Prospective candidates interested in the position are asked to complete an online faculty employment application and attach a cover letter, CV, and statement of teaching philosophy. Application materials due before September 1, 2026. Consideration of applicants will continue until the

position is filled. Questions: For further information, please contact David Lennox by phone (509) 527-2051 or email David.Lennox@wallawalla.edu. To learn more about our program visit our website at http://business.wallawalla.edu.
