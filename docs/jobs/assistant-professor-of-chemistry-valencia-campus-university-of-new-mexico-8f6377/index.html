With an emphasis on teaching excellence, Valencia Campus offers vocational and technical courses as well as academic transfer courses

to the citizens of Valencia and northern Socorro counties. The faculty and staff serve a student body of approximately 2,300 each semester.Salary: Base salary for nine-month faculty position is $57,000.
