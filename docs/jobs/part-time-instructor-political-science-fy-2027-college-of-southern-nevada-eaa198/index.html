Job Description CSN is seeking part-time instructors to teach Political Science courses, primarily in an on-ground format, with potential opportunities for online instruction. Teaching assignments will include Introduction to American

Politics (PSC 101), a four-credit course that includes a Nevada Constitution and Politics component. Core Responsibilities Teach college-level courses in the field. Develop syllabi and course materials. Evaluate student mastery of skills and competencies required by course outcomes. Provide an excellent teaching experience as well as student advisement, recruitment activities and program/curriculum development. Teach

at multiple campuses in Clark County. Be available to teach day, evening and/or weekend courses. Minimum Qualifications Master's degree in political science (Ph.D. preferred) from a regionally accredited college or university with at least 18 graduate credits in the subject being taught (also from a regionally accredited college or university). Salary $925.00 per instructional unit. Credits will vary by semester.

Applications will be considered on an as-needed basis determined by course availability.
