Position Title: Assistant Professor in Special Education Job Category: Faculty Employment Type: Regular Job Profile: Assistant Professor Job Duties: The School of Education and Human Services at the University of Wisconsin-Oshkosh invites applications for a

tenure-track Assistant Professor of Special Education beginning in Fall 2027. We seek a scholar-practitioner with expertise in inclusive special education methods for K–12 learners with disabilities, including curriculum access, assessment-informed instruction, and specially designed instruction. The successful candidate will join a collaborative faculty committed to preparing future educators through inclusive,

equitable, and evidence-based teaching and will contribute to the preparation of educators, counselors, and human service professionals who positively impact schools and communities. The successful candidate will contribute to teacher preparation in cross-categorical special education, maintain an active program of scholarship, and engage in meaningful service to the university, profession, and community. This is a

9-month academic year appointment with a standard teaching load of 12 credits per semester. During the first four years of employment, new faculty receive a 3-credit research release each semester, reducing their teaching load to 9 credits per semester while they establish their research and scholarship. Join us in Oshkosh, WI Oshkosh, Wisconsin, offers an outstanding quality of life in a vibrant and welcoming

community. Nestled along the shores of Lake Winnebago and the Fox River, Oshkosh blends natural beauty with the amenities of city living. Conveniently located just 90 miles from Milwaukee and Madison and 55 miles from Green Bay, residents enjoy easy access to larger metropolitan areas while benefiting from the charm and affordability of a smaller city. Outdoor enthusiasts will find abundant opportunities for hiking,

biking, boating, fishing, and other recreational activities nearby. The scenic Northwoods of Wisconsin, picturesque Door County, and the Wisconsin Dells are all within a short drive, making weekend adventures easy and accessible.
