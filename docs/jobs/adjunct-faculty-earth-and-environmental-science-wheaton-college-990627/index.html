Adjunct Faculty-Earth and Environmental Science Wheaton, IL, United States Job Description JOB DESCRIPTION The Earth and Environmental Science Department is seeking qualified and enthusiastic individuals as Adjunct Instructors of Fundamentals of Mineral Science and Lab. Job requirements: M.S. or Ph.D. in Geology, Environmental Science or related field. Experience teaching at the collegiate level preferred.

Applications are received on an on-going basis. Interested candidates should send a letter of inquiry and curriculum vitae to Dr. Andrew Luhmann, Chair of the Department of Earth and Environmental Science, Wheaton College, Wheaton, IL 60187-5501. Email: andrew.luhmann@wheaton.edu Please do not send transcripts or letters of recommendation, or credential files unless requested. Salary range: $935 per credit hour for a

faculty member with an MA and fewer than 3 years of experience teaching at Wheaton - $1100 per credit hour for a faculty member with a PhD.
