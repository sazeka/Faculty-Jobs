Posting Details Posting Summary Position Title Assistant/Associate Professor of Educational Leadership FLSA Department Division of Education & Counseling Job Summary The Division of Education and Counseling at Xavier University of Louisiana invites applicants for a tenure track position in Educational Leadership at the Assistant/Associate Professor level beginning Fall 2027. This position is a unique

opportunity for a dynamic individual with significant K-12 school leadership experience and expertise in educational leadership, focusing in qualitative and mixed methods research to shape the next generation of educational leaders at both the master’s and doctoral levels. The ideal candidate will be a core contributor to our doctoral program, mentoring students through their dissertation research, while also
