Teaching Assistant Professor Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Please see Special Instructions for more details. Review of applications will begin on November 1st and applicants are encouraged to submit by that date for consideration in the initial review cycle.Please submit: A cover letter summarizing your teaching experience and interests, including the areas above in which you could

contribute. A curriculum vitae detailing all courses taught, including enrollments. A two-page statement of teaching philosophy and practices, ideally including discussion of pedagogical approaches to engaging students in high-enrollment university courses.Selected applicants will be invited to submit a short teaching video responding to a common introductory-computing prompt. Further instructions will be provided

with the invitation. Videos will be evaluated for technical accuracy, clarity, and suitability for undergraduate learners. A subset of applicants will then be invited to a 30-minute Zoom interview. At this stage, candidates will be asked to provide two reference letters, including at least one addressing their teaching or potential as an educator, ideally based on direct observation. Letters must be signed, appear on

institutional letterhead, and be addressed to the Teaching Assistant Professor Search Committee. Finalists will be invited to campus to give a 50-minute talk, interviews and meals with faculty and staff, and a student lunch.Evidence of teaching effectiveness, such as student evaluations from prior courses taught as instructor of record or teaching assistant, midterm surveys of current students, or teaching

observation reports. Upload this material in "Other Document".
