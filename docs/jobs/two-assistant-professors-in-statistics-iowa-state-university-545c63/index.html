Position Title: Two Assistant Professors in Statistics Appointment Type: Faculty Job Description: Summary of Duties and Responsibilities: The Department of Statistics, at Iowa State University in Ames, Iowa, seeks two tenure-track assistant professors with expertise in statistics and data science beginning August 16, 2027. One of the positions will have a particular emphasis on sports analytics. The successful

candidates will develop and sustain a high-quality and high-impact research program, successfully compete for external funding, and collaborate with world-class researchers at Iowa State in statistics and other disciplines. Further, the successful candidates will engage in professional and institutional service, teach graduate and undergraduate students, and advise graduate students in one of the first and largest

statistics graduate programs in the country. Required Minimum Qualifications: PhD or equivalent degree in statistics or closely related discipline by appointment start date Preferred Qualifications: Foundational training in statistics and data science Ability to teach statistics and data science classes at the undergraduate and graduate levels Ability to develop and sustain a high-impact research program with the

potential to secure external research funding Ability to contribute to Iowa State University’s growing strength in data science Ability to contribute to Iowa State University's growing strength in data science and, for one position, sports analytics Department/Program & College Description: The Department of Statistics is jointly administered by the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences and the College of Liberal

Arts and Sciences. The College of Agriculture and Life Sciences is one of the world’s leading institutions of agriculture, with more than 160 years of leadership in science, education and extension. The college educates future leaders, conducts mission-oriented research, and shares new knowledge for the betterment of Iowa and the world. The college is one of eight colleges at Iowa State University, which is one of

the most influential land grant universities in the nation. Central to Iowa State University’s mission, the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences (LAS) is a collaborative, vibrant community of faculty, students and staff, committed to learning, research, and innovation.
