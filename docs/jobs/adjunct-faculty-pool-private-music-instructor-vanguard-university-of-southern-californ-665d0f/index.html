Share Apply Now Title Adjunct Faculty (Pool) - Private Music Instructor Description Position Summary: The Department of Music at Vanguard University of Southern California invites applications for its General Music Private Instructor Adjunct Pool. This pool supports the department’s need for qualified instructors to provide private or small-group instruction in general music skills and applied music areas, as

enrollment and program needs arise. Private instructors play a vital role in mentoring students’ musical, technical, and artistic development while supporting Vanguard’s commitment to excellence, faith integration, and holistic education. Appointments are made on a student-need and semester-by-semester basis. Essential Functions: Provide weekly private or small-group instruction tailored to student skill level and

academic goals Develop individualized learning plans that support technical growth, musicality, and professional preparation Prepare students for juries, performances, recitals, or competency evaluations as required Assess student progress and submit evaluations in accordance with departmental policies Maintain regular communication with students and applied faculty coordinators Support a positive, encouraging, and

faith-informed learning environment Participate in faculty meetings or professional development as requested Minimum Qualifications: Master’s degree in Music or a closely related field (Bachelor’s degree with significant professional experience may be considered) Demonstrated excellence as a performer, educator, or applied instructor Experience teaching private lessons at the collegiate or pre-collegiate level Strong

interpersonal and mentoring skills Ability to support the Christian mission and values of Vanguard University SALARY: $60 per hour APPLICATION PROCEDURE: Please complete the application and upload the required documents below. Please do not send your documents directly to the Department Chair. Accompanying Essays: 1. Personal Testimony of Relationship with Jesus Christ. Please provide a brief account of your

experience of coming to faith in Christ.
