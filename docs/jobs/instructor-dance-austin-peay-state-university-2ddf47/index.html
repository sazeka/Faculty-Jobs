Instructor - Dance Clarksville, TN, United States Be the First to Apply Job Description The Austin Peay State University Department of Theatre and Dance is seeking a three-year full-time instructor for the position of Dance to begin August 1st, 2027. This position is specifically seeking a candidate with a strong foundation in Commercial and Musical Theatre dance forms, including but not limited to jazz, hip-hop,

tap, contemporary, and diasporic dance traditions. The ideal candidate will be passionate about training versatile, industry-ready performers who are proficient across all aspects of the performance field. Additionally, the ideal candidate will also contribute to the Acting students, teaching performance-based coursework as needed, reflecting the department's commitment to developing well-rounded performers ready for

the demands of the modern industry. The Theatre and Dance program at Austin Peay State University offers a BFA with concentrations in Acting, Design, Musical Theatre, and Stage Management, and a BA with concentrations in Theatre and Dance. Minors in Theatre and Dance are popular choices for many students. An intimate proscenium theatre seating just under 200 currently houses 4-5 productions each academic year, with a

Laboratory Theatre space available for additional productions, student performances, and events. Theatre and Dance are integral parts of the Center of Excellence for the Creative Arts (www.apsu.edu/creativearts). A long established, state-funded initiative, APSU’s Center of Excellence for the Creative Arts, is designed to enhance the cultural environment of the university, community, and region through a diverse

range of imaginative arts experiences.
