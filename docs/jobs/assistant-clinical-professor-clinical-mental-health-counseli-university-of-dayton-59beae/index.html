The University of Dayton Department of Counselor Education and Human Services is seeking

applications for a full-time, 12-month, Assistant Clinical Faculty specializing in Clinical Mental Health Counseling beginning January 1, 2027. Clinical Mental Health Counseling faculty teach in a CACREP-accredited M.S.Ed. program. The OpportunityThe University of Dayton’s Clinical Mental Health Counseling program offers opportunities for faculty engagement and growth through teaching; interprofessional

collaboration; and curriculum, program, and community outreach development. We have an established partnership with two other universities for Interprofessional Education and Practice (IPE) which prepares students in health professions to work well together. Our faculty work collaboratively with students and community partners to extend learning beyond the classroom through such programs as the Community Wellness

Initiative, Brain Health Collective, Ecotherapy Certificate program, and Chi Sigma Iota. As a member of this dynamic team, this position is expected to: Teach graduate counseling courses on our Dayton and Columbus campuses (12 credits per term; fall, spring, & summer); Work primarily from our main campus in Dayton, OH with one evening per week of teaching in Columbus (mileage provided); Serve as Clinical Experience

Coordinator (including but not limited to: site development, overseeing student placements, supervisor training, & clinical documentation); Recruit, advise, and mentor graduate students; Serve as co-faculty advisor for Chi Sigma Iota chapters; Provide service to the program, department, university, community, and profession; and Demonstrate a commitment to inclusive excellence. Salary is competitive and dependent
