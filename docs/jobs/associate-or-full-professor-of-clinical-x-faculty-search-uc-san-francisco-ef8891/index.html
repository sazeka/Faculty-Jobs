description Associate or Full Professor of Clinical X Faculty Search The Department of Community Health Systems at the UCSF School of Nursing invites applications for a full-time faculty position in the Clinical X series, at the rank of Associate or Full Professor, depending on qualifications. The Department prepares nurses for leadership roles in advanced clinical practice, systems-based care, education, and

research. We seek a scholar-clinician whose independent program of research, clinical expertise, and teaching advance accessible mental health care for older adults, individuals with serious mental illness, and other underserved populations. Key Responsibilities Conduct a pioneering, independent program of research examining structural and organizational determinants of mental health care access, delivery, and

outcomes for older adults, individuals with serious mental illness, and other underserved populations. This program should attract extramural funding.
