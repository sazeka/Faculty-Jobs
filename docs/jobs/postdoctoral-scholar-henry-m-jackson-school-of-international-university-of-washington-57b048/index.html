Jackson School of International Studies – The Stroum Center for Jewish Studies Position Details Position Description The Stroum Center for

Jewish Studies (SCJS) in the Henry M. Jackson School of International Studies at the University of Washington invites applications for up to two full-time Postdoctoral Scholar positions. These Postdoctoral Scholar positions are funded by the Hazel D. Cole Endowed Fellowship in Jewish Studies, a one-year in-residence fellowship, with the opportunity for reappointment. The fellowship will support deserving postdoctoral

scholars in Jewish studies from September 16, 2027, through September 15, 2028 (a twelve-month service period). The scholars selected as a Cole Fellow will contribute to the Stroum Center’s intellectual community, provide instructional activities as appropriate, engage in research, and support the broader academic mission of The Stroum Center for Jewish Studies. The thematic scope of the position remains

intentionally flexible. Illustrative areas of engagement include, but are not limited to: Research and working on publication Contribute to the Stroum Center, including but not limited to: public outreach; participating in SCJS programs, events, and affiliated academic activities; with opportunities to give public talks and engage in other center activities Participate in the SCJS colloquia series as a speaker during

the appointment Engage in initiatives that enhance the visibility and institutional presence of Jewish Studies at the University of Washington The salary range for this position is $5,705-$6,055 per month ($68,460- $72,660 annually). University of Washington offers a wide range of benefits as part of the postdoctoral scholar compensation package, including medical, dental and vision insurance programs; tax-deferred

investing through the UW retirement options; vacation and sick time off; and life and long-term disability insurance: https://hr.uw.edu/benefits/orientation/summaries/postdoctoral-scholars/. Postdoctoral employees are represented by the UAW Local 4121 employee union and are subject to the collective bargaining agreement, unless agreed exclusion criteria apply.
