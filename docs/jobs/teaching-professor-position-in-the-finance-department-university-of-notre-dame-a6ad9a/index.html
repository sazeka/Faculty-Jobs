Teaching Professor Position in the Finance Department

University of Notre Dame  ·  Finance Department  ·  Notre Dame, IN
Finance

Position summary

University of Notre Dame: Mendoza College of Business: Finance Location Notre Dame, IN Open Date Aug 26, 2026 Deadline Jan 31, 2027 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time Description The University of Notre Dame, Department of Finance, is looking for applicants in all areas of finance to fill one or more teaching faculty positions (non-tenure-track) in the Finance Department. Positions can be at the assistant, associate, or full

teaching professor level depending on qualifications. Position duties include outstanding teaching at the undergraduate and graduate levels, and contributing to the intellectual life of the college. We offer competitive salary and benefits, and generous resources for professional development. The University of Notre Dame, located in South Bend, Indiana, is consistently ranked among the top 25 undergraduate

institutions in the U.S. The University has approximately 13,000 students, with undergraduates accounting for 70% of the student body. As part of the University’s Mendoza College of Business, the finance department contributes to a top-ranked undergraduate business program and several highly regarded graduate programs, including MBA, executive MBA, and Masters of Science in Finance programs. Finance faculty and

students also benefit from close collaboration with the Notre Dame Institute for Global Investing (NDIGI) and the Fitzgerald Institute for Real Estate (FIRE). For more information about the Mendoza College of Business or the finance department, visit https://mendoza.nd.edu/ or https://mendoza.nd.edu/research-faculty/academic-departments/finance/. Qualifications Candidates should have a demonstrated record of

excellence in teaching across a range of topics and program levels. The ideal candidate will have significant relevant industry experience and/or hold a Ph.D. in finance or a related field. Application Instructions Applications for the Teaching Professor Position must be submitted via Interfolio. Applications should include: 1) a cover letter indicating the candidate’s interest in a position at the University of

Notre Dame; 2) a curriculum vitae; 3) teaching evaluations (if available). Additional documentation, including letters of recommendation, may also be requested. Our intention is to begin holding campus interviews in November 2026. For full consideration, applications should be submitted no later than November 1, 2026. Review of applications will continue until the position is filled. Application Process This
