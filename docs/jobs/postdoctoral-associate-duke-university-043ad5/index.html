At Duke University, groundbreaking discoveries begin with talented individuals who are passionate about advancing science and improving

As a Postdoctoral Associate in the laboratory of Dr. Vadim Arshavsky at the Albert Eye Research Institute, you will join a world-class research environment dedicated to uncovering the molecular and cellular mechanisms of vision and retinal disease. This position offers an exciting opportunity to contribute to innovative research focused on the development and maintenance of the light-sensitive cilium in

vertebrate photoreceptor cells and to advance understanding of inherited retinal degenerations. Working within a collaborative and intellectually stimulating environment, you will engage in cutting-edge basic and translational research that has the potential to shape future therapies for blinding disorders. We welcome highly motivated and enthusiastic individuals who are eager to expand their scientific expertise

while contributing to meaningful discoveries in vision science. What You’ll Bring Required Qualifications PhD or MD/PhD in a relevant scientific discipline. Strong background in molecular biology and cell biology. Experience working with animal models. Demonstrated scientific curiosity, initiative, and commitment to research excellence.
