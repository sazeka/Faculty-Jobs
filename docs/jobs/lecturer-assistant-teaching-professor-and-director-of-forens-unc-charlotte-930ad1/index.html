The Department of Communications Studies at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte invited applications for a Director of Forensics and a Lecturer/Assistant Teaching Professor rank, 9-month teaching-track position beginning August 16, 2027.

Finalists will be asked to discuss how their teaching practices engage, include, and eliminate barriers to learning for UNC Charlotte students, and how their educational approach helps increase the engagement and success of all students regardless of their entry pathway into the university. The review of applications will begin October 30, 2026 and will continue until the position is filled. Applicants must apply

electronically at https://jobs.charlotte.edu/, position #005250. Please upload a cover letter responding to the qualifications listed above, a current CV, and contact information for three references. Contact Dr. Nance Riffe (nriffe@charlotte.edu, Search Committee Chair) or Dr. Erin Basinger (ebasinge@charlotte.edu, Chair of the Department of Communication Studies) with questions.The candidate chosen for this
