Explore Academic Positions at Harvard Post-Doctoral Fellow in Chemistry and Chemical Biology (Long Lab) Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Position Details Title Post-Doctoral Fellow in Chemistry and Chemical Biology (Long Lab) School Faculty of Arts and Sciences Department/Area Chemistry and Chemical Biology Position Description The Department of Chemistry and Chemical Biology at Harvard

University invites applications for a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Jeffrey Long Research Group, which focuses on the synthesis, characterization, and application of advanced nanomaterials. The group maintains active collaborations with researchers in electrical and computer engineering, materials science, and physics, and is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities for materials synthesis, structural characterization,

and optical measurements. The successful candidate will conduct independent and collaborative research on the design and synthesis of low-dimensional (two-dimensional and quasi-two-dimensional) halide perovskite materials, including but not limited to nanowire growth, crystal engineering via supramolecular and hydrogen-bonding strategies, and the development of perovskite-based lasing and light-emitting devices.

Specific responsibilities will include: Research: Designing and synthesizing novel organic spacer molecules and organic–inorganic hybrid perovskite materials; growing and characterizing single-crystal and thin-film perovskite nanomaterials; performing structural analysis using single-crystal X-ray diffraction, scanning electron microscopy (SEM), atomic force microscopy (AFM), and related techniques; conducting

optical characterization including steady-state and time-resolved photoluminescence, circular dichroism, and lasing measurements; and exploring new directions in chiroptical perovskites, perovskite nanophotonics, and related areas. Publishing: Preparing manuscripts for publication in high-impact peer-reviewed journals and presenting research findings at national and international conferences such as ACS, MRS, AIChE,

and Gordon Research Conferences. Collaboration: Working closely with the PI, graduate students, and external collaborators across disciplines including electrical engineering, photonics, and computational materials science.
