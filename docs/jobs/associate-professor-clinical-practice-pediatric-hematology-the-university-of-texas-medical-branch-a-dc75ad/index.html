Position summary

JOB DESCRIPTION: The Department of Pediatrics at the University of Texas Medical Branch (UTMB) John Sealy School of Medicine in Galveston is seeking a full-time Hematology & Oncology Physician-Scientist the rank of Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor. This is a unique opportunity to join a nationally recognized academic health system and a collaborative team of clinicians and researchers. This position offers the

opportunity to engage in clinical care, contribute to the education of pediatric residents and medical students, and develop a productive funded research program. Position Overview: This full-time academic role combines clinical care, teaching and research within a collaborative health system. The successful candidate will: • Join a multidisciplinary team to support the institutional clinical, educational and

research missions. • Provide comprehensive pediatric hematology & oncology care across inpatient and outpatient settings. • Teach and mentor medical students, residents, and fellows. • Lead the development of a clinical and/or basic research program within the Department of Pediatrics. Qualifications: Required • M.D. or D.O. degree from an LCME or COCA accredited medical school. • Completion of an ACGME-accredited

Pediatric Residency. • Completion of an ACGME-accredited Pediatric Hematology & Oncology Fellowship. • Board Certification in Pediatrics and Pediatric Hematology & Oncology. • Ability to obtain an unrestricted State of Texas Physician License. • Current U.S. DEA Practitioner registration. • U.S. Citizenship or Employment Authorization to work in the U.S. • Demonstrated commitment to patient care and medical

education. • Demonstrated clinical and/or basic science research productivity. Preferred • Three or more years of post-fellowship clinical experience in Pediatric Hematology & Oncology. • Prior academic experience in resident and medical student education. • Current transferable funded research. Income Package: Salary will be commensurate with academic rank, qualifications, and experience. The position includes a

provision for dedicated research time. In addition, UTMB offers a superior benefits package, including an excellent retirement program and a relocation allowance.
