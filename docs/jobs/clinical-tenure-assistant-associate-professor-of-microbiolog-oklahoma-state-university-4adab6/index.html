About this Position Faculty Position in Veterinary Microbiology The Department of Veterinary Pathobiology in the College of Veterinary Medicine at Oklahoma State University invites applications for a faculty position in veterinary microbiology, specifically in the field of veterinary bacteriology and mycology. We seek an innovative, collaborative educator and scientist who will contribute to

veterinary education, advance research in infectious diseases, and strengthen the College’s research and graduate training programs. Qualifications Applicants must hold a Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM or equivalent) and PhD degree. Board certification or eligibility for certification by the American College of Veterinary Microbiologists (ACVM) is preferred. Preference will be given to candidates with experience

teaching in a professional curriculum, as the successful candidate will serve as the primary instructor for the core bacteriology and mycology course in the veterinary curriculum. The successful candidate will demonstrate a commitment to excellence in teaching and mentoring and the potential to establish a competitive, extramurally funded research program. Responsibilities The successful candidate will: Teaching:

Deliver the core veterinary bacteriology and mycology course for veterinary students and contribute to other instructional activities consistent with departmental and College needs. Research: Develop and sustain a competitive, extramurally funded research program in infectious diseases, with an emphasis on molecular mechanisms of disease pathogenesis, host–pathogen interactions, and therapeutics.
