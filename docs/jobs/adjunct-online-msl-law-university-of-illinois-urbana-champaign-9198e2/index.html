Sign InCreate ProfileEnglish (US)Adjunct (Online MSL) - LawUrbana, IL, United States|1037540Apply NowPosted on: 7/17/2026 Back to Search Adjunct (Online MSL) - Law Law The University of Illinois College of Law invites applications for Course Content Developers to create multiple 8-week online courses for its innovative Master of Science in Law (MSL) program, launching in Spring 2027. The MSL program targets

professionals in business, tech, and related fields, offering concentrations in Business Compliance and Tech & Privacy. We seek dynamic experts to develop courses in AI Ethics & Bias, Individual Employee Rights, and Cybersecurity. Successful candidates may transition into lecturer roles, delivering courses and contributing to future course development. Responsibilities: • Design multiple 8-week online courses (2–3

preferred, based on expertise), including learning objectives, weekly modules, assessments (e.g., quizzes, case studies), and interactive activities. • Develop engaging, practical content for non-lawyer professionals, incorporating real-world scenarios and industry-relevant examples. • Collaborate with the MSL program team to align courses with program goals and accreditation standards. • Create accessible materials

(e.g., video lectures, readings, discussion prompts) for the program’s learning management system. • Meet project milestones, with course drafts completed by March 31, 2026. • (Optional) Serve as a lecturer for developed courses post-launch, delivering content and engaging with students online. • (Optional) Contribute to future course development for MSL program expansion. Appointment Information This is a 100%

full-time Academic position, appointed on a 12-month basis. The expected start date is as soon as possible after 8/16/2026. Adjuncts will make $10,000 for course development, with the potential for an ongoing lecturer role with additional compensation.This position is eligible for all sponsorship for work authorization options. Application Procedures & Deadline Information Applications must be received by 6:00 pm

(Central Time) on September 1, 2026. Apply for this position using the Apply Now button at the top or bottom of this posting. Applications not submitted through https://jobs.illinois.edu will not be considered. For further information about this specific position, please contact Devin Scheidemantel at wds@illinois.edu.
