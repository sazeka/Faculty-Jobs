This position also serves as the Coordinator of Rehabilitation, providing leadership and oversight for the rehabilitation counseling program. The role presents an opportunity to contribute to a dynamic and growing counseling program, preparing the next generation of professionals through teaching, mentorship, research, and program coordination. Faculty members in this role are responsible for teaching online graduate
