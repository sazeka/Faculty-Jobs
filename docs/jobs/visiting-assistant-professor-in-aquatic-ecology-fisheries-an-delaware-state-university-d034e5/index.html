FUNDED POSITIONThe Visiting Assistant Professor in Aquatic Ecology, Fisheries, and Aquaculture is a full-time position related to coastal environmental and water resources research at Delaware State University (DSU), College of Agriculture, Science and Technology. The position is a yearly appointment at the rank of Visiting Assistant Professor with 60% of time devoted to research and 40% of time devoted to teaching

and student experiential learning coordination efforts for both EPSCOR and NOAA LMRCSC Programs. This is a non-tenured professional staff position. The Visiting Assistant Professor supports the project director for EPSCOR and NOAA LMRCSC programs, focusing on coastal water quality monitoring, nutrient dynamics, ecological assessments of the various estuarine and coastal research sites, and environmental microbiology.

Proficiency in both field and laboratory research is required, including coordination with the research team (technicians, scientists, and students). The Visiting Assistant Professor has knowledge working with aquatic species (i.e. fish, oyster, crab), background proficient with environmental sampling, water and soil nutrient testing, as well as analytical skills and knowledge in environmental microbiology.
