This position plays a central role in the artistic and

educational mission of the department, providing technical leadership for UAB Theatre’s five-show mainstage season while teaching and mentoring undergraduate students in Design and Technology. The Technical Director works closely with directors, scenic designers, the Production Manager, faculty, staff, and students to realize scenic designs safely, efficiently, and collaboratively. The position works within a

production team that includes a Scene Shop Supervisor, Electrics Supervisor, Props Supervisor, Costume Director and Shop Manager, faculty designers, and undergraduate students.
