Fort Valley State University, the only 1890

land-grant university in Georgia, is a comprehensive institution that provides an education to over 2,800 students including graduate students. The university is located in the town of Fort Valley in Peach County, the original site of the state's peach industry. Its 1,365-acre (5.52 km2) campus is Georgia's second-largest public university in area. Fort Valley State University is an equal opportunity employer. The

University does not discriminate in employment on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, protected veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other status protected by applicable federal, state, or University System of Georgia policy. JOB SUMMARY The College of Agriculture, Family Sciences and Technology (CAFST) invited applications

for an energetic and student-focused faculty member with expertise in Veterinary Technology for a 10-month tenure-track appointment at the rank of Assistant Professor in the Department of Veterinary Technology pending previous experience. The successful candidate will be responsible for teaching undergraduate courses in Veterinary Technology, mentoring students and supporting programmatic activities that strengthen

the academic and experiential learning environment of the department. Student mentorship and advisement are central to the position. In addition to providing academic advising, the faculty members will guide and support the Veterinary Technology club, collaborate and assist with Research Farm and Cooperative Extension animal operations and participate in relevant university and community service activities. The

successful candidate will also be expected to engage in a focused program of scholarly activity resulting in peer-reviewed publications, professional presentations and collaborative research opportunities. Participation in professional development, clinical engagement and service to the community is expected. Specific teaching assignments will be assigned by the chair of the department.
