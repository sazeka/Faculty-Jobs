Sociology and Social Work has 13 full-time teaching faculty and staff (6 in Sociology and 7 in Social Work), approximately 165 undergraduate social work majors and approximately 150 undergraduate sociology majors in both face-to-face and online program options. learning, the Department values quality teaching/learning experiences, and emphasizes undergraduate research, community-based practicums, and service to the region.
