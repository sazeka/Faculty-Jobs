Position summary

Adjunct Faculty - Baker School of Business - Management and Entrepreneurship Mgmt and Entrepreneurship Adjunct Faculty Unclassified Job Responsibilities: The Citadel, The Military College of South Carolina is seeking applicants for adjunct faculty in Management and Entrepreneurship disciplines in the Baker School of Business. Our goal is to build long-term relationships with high-quality adjunct part-time faculty

members who share in The Citadel’s commitment to develop principled leaders in business. There are main programs where adjunct faculty may be employed to teach classes: Our traditional undergraduate program, which features face-to-face classes to the South Carolina Corps of Cadets Our part time undergraduate degree completion program, which are primarily working adults earning Bachelor’s degrees, mostly through

online classes. Minimum Qualifications: To teach at the undergraduate level, applicants must have a minimum of a Master's degree from an AACSB-Accredited Institution in the appropriate business discipline or in a closely related field. Be able to teach in-person classes, on campus in Charleston, SC. Evidence of previous effective teaching experience is required. Complete Citadel instructor training and follow rules

and regulations set by The Citadel Center for Excellence and Innovation in Teaching, Learning and Distance Education (CEITL&DE). Preference is given to applicants who meet AACSB Qualifications as a Scholarly Academic, Practice Academic, Scholarly Practitioner, or Instructional Practitioner. Official transcripts indicating education level and background check are required prior to employment. If you have questions,

please contact Dr. James Bezjian at jbezjian@citadel.edu. This is a pooled position and roles are dependent on department need. The review of applications will begin immediately and will continue until filled.
