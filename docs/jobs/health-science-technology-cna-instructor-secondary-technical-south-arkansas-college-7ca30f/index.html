Health Science Technology CNA Instructor-Secondary Technical CenterJob Summary:This nine-month academic position at the SouthArk Secondary Technical Center is responsible for delivering instruction within the Health Science Technology program. The coursework involved prepares high school juniors and seniors to learn basic health care skills and allows them the opportunity to explore a variety of health care fields.

The courses to be taught include, but are not limited to, Introduction to Healthcare, Medical Terminology, CPR & First Aid, and CNA coursework.
