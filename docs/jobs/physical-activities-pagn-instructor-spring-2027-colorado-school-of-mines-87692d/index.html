Physical Activities, PAGN Instructor Spring 2027

Colorado School of Mines  ·  Golden, CO
InstructorPhysical Activity Science

Position summary

Engineering a world of possibilities The PAGN Instructor will teach physical activity courses under the supervision of Student Wellness Promotion. The PAGN Instructor will follow all Colorado School of Mines policies, guidelines, and procedures. PAGN Instructors are expected to have worked with Student Wellness Promotion staff prior to application to propose the course(s) that they are applying to teach. PAGN courses

will be designated to a specific tier based on expected instructional effort. Instructor qualifications and pay are dependent on whether each course is being taught on a low (asynchronous), medium (hybrid), or high (in-person hands-on) instructional level. If you are a current Mines employee on a paid contract, please log into Workday to apply to the internal posting. If you are a former Mines employee and are

applying to an external posting as you are not currently active on a paid contract, do NOT use your Mines email address when creating your account to apply. Primary Responsibilities Develop and implement physical activity curriculum. Create a course syllabus, including required PAGN departmental learning outcomes and assessment. Support students in a timely manner. Meet all expectations for each course’s

instructional level. Enter final grades in a timely manner. Manage equipment, as needed. Performs other duties as assigned. Minimum Qualifications For low instructional level class instructors: Bachelor's degree or two years of relevant program, sport, recreation, or administrative experience. Ability to coordinate participation and maintain academic records. For medium instructional level class instructors:

Bachelor's degree or approved equivalent. One year of related instruction or coaching. Current CPR/AED and first aid when supervising physical activity. Technical credential when the instructor designs exercise programs or teaches higher-risk techniques. For high instructional level class instructors: Bachelor's degree or approved tested-experience equivalent. Two years of direct instruction or coaching. Current

CPR/AED and first aid when supervising physical activity. Recognized activity-specific credential where one exists. Also, for all instructors: Professional verbal and written communication skills. Ability to work in a dynamic fast paced environment and manage stressful situations. Ability to communicate with many internal and external entities in a variety of ways. Salary and Benefits $600 per low instructional level
