Open Rank Professor of Mechanical Engineering

Position summary

The Webb School of Engineering at High Point University seeks a full-time, open-rank Mechanical Engineering faculty member to teach courses in manufacturing, mechanical design, and vibrations, beginning August 2027. HPU launched new Mechanical Engineering and Mechatronics majors in Fall 2024 and opened a state-of-the-art Makerspace and Machine Shop in Fall 2026. We seek a creative, dynamic individual to play a

critical role in the development of innovative, active learning classes and laboratories, and to mentor highly motivated students in undergraduate research projects and design competitions. The successful candidate will teach a variety of courses within the Mechanical Engineering and Mechatronics programs. Additional expectations include academic advising, supporting undergraduate research, assisting the department

with ABET accreditation, and serving on college and university-wide committees. Faculty appointments are for a nine-month contract period with a normal teaching load of 12 credit hours per semester. Qualifications Education Candidates must have an earned Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering, or a closely related field like Aerospace, Manufacturing, or Materials Science. Industry experience is also desirable. Knowledge,

Skills, Ability: The successful candidate should have demonstrated promise of excellence in teaching, a record of scholarly achievement, an entrepreneurial spirit and an interest in working with highly motivated undergraduate students. The success of our undergraduate students is our primary focus. Responsibilities Teach 12 contact hours (typically 2 – 3 classes) per semester Advise and mentor students Produce

scholarly research or creative activity, especially in partnership with undergraduate students Assist with curriculum development and ABET Accreditation Assist with university recruiting efforts Participate in department, college, and university meetings and committees Perform other duties as assigned by the Department Chair and/or Dean Application Materials Applicants should upload the following documents as one

combined PDF file . A cover letter A current CV A statement of teaching philosophy A statement of research interests Names and contact information for three professional references Any questions related to this position should be sent to Dr. Brian Bernard, Department Chair of Mechanical Engineering and Mechatronics, bbernard@highpoint.edu .
