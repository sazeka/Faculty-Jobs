Posting Details Position Information Title Adjunct Professor of Public Policy - Career Support Campus Sacramento Department Public Policy, Public Administration Posting Number F01097 Full or Part Time Part Time Open Date Close Date Open Until Filled No Days Per Week Weeks Per Year Position Description Primary Purpose and Essential Functions The University of the Pacific, McGeorge School of Law

(McGeorge), an ABA-accredited law school in Sacramento, California, seeks a public policy/administration professional to teach a Career Support course on a part-time basis.The course teaches the skills and qualities that are essential to a successful career in the public sector. Topics covered include professional identity, professionalism, teamwork, resilience, integrity, self-awareness, interpersonal communication,

and best practices for finding a fulfilling career. The course also familiarizes students with the wide spectrum of careers in which a public policy/administration degree is required or desirable, and challenges them to think deeply about career paths that will be truly satisfying. The course also requires students to understand the “best practices” in job search activities, including resume writing, cover letter

writing, working with mentors, goal setting, and networking.The course will be taught on a one-on-one basis and/or via a series of workshops.
