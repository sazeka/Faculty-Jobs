Supplemental Instructor (Current Students Only)

Vaughn College of Aeronautics and Technology  ·  Flushing, NY
InstructorEnglish Literature

Position summary

Position Summary The Supplemental Instructor (SI) supports student learning, academic achievement, and student engagement by providing peer-assisted instruction, tutoring, mentoring, and classroom support for designated courses. Working closely with faculty and the Interim Learning Specialist, the SI attends assigned classes, reinforces course concepts through individual and group tutoring sessions, facilitates

supplemental instruction activities, and assists students in developing effective study strategies, time management skills, and academic confidence. The SI serves as a positive role model who promotes independent learning, academic integrity, and a welcoming, inclusive learning environment while contributing to student persistence and success. understanding course concepts, completing assignments, preparing for examinations, and developing effective study strategies. &bull; Encourage independent learning, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. &bull; Support students in developing time management, organization, and other academic success skills. &bull; Assist faculty during class activities, laboratories, workshops, and other instructional
