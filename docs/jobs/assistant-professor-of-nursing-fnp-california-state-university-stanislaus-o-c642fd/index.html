PTOC Assistant Professor of Nursing (FNP) Apply now Job no: 556444 Work type: Instructional Faculty – Tenured/Tenure-Track Location: Stanislaus - Turlock Categories: Unit 3 - CFA - California Faculty Association, Tenured/Tenure-Track, Full Time, Faculty - Health Sciences Faculty Employment Opportunity POSITION: Assistant Professor of Nursing: The School of Nursing invites applications for a full-time,

tenure-track appointment at the rank of assistant professor. The person appointed to this position will teach in either the Bachelor of Science in Nursing program or the Masters of Science in Nursing: Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP) concentration. Primary duties to include: teaching responsibilities for didactic, laboratory, and clinical courses. Effective teaching, research, creative activities, and service to the

University are required for retention, promotion, and tenure. This position is contingent upon the availability of authorized funding. Applicant must be a licensed Family Nurse Practitioner. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS: - A Master's of Science in nursing is required.- A current and unobstructed California RN license and a California Nurse Practitioner Furnishing License- A current national Nurse Practitioner

Certification, and the American Health Association Basic Life Support Health Provider Certification. - A minimum of two years of instructional experience. - A minimum of two years of full-time practice as a nurse practitioner. - Knowledge and skills related to teaching adults, teaching methods, curriculum development, evaluations, or willingness to plan for acquisition of these skills not later than the first year of

teaching. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS: In addition to required qualifications, a Doctoral degree is preferred. HOW TO APPLY & DEADLINE: A complete application must include: an application letter specifically addressing the position description and qualifications; unofficial photocopies of graduate transcripts; a statement of teaching philosophy; evidence of teaching proficiency; a sample of research; professional

curriculum vitae, including the names, addresses, and telephone numbers of three references. Official transcripts will be required at the time of hire. To apply for this position, please click the "Apply Now" button on this page. If you have questions regarding the position, please contact:Dr. Jennifer Peltier, Search Committee Chair School of Nursing College of Science California State University, StanislausOne
