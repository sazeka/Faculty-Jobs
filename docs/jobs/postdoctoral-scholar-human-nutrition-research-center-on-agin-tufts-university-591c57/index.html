Position summary

Overview The mission of the Jean Mayer USDA Human Nutrition Research Center on Aging at Tufts University (HNRCA) is to promote healthy aging through nutrition science to empower people seeking to enjoy long, active, and independent lives. HNRCA investigators conduct the world's most advanced studies on nutrition and aging. The research focuses on determining the nutrient and physical activity requirements necessary

to promote well-being for older adults. HNRCA scientists examine how nutrition and physical activity play a major role in the prevention of the major chronic degenerative conditions and diseases associated with aging. What You'll Do The HNRCA invites applications for a Postdoctoral Scholar position within the Biology of Aging research group, under the mentorship of Dr. Arpit Sharma. This role focuses on identifying

organelle pathways that regulate longevity and healthy aging. Current projects include: Role of Peroxisomes in Healthy Aging: Conducting C. elegans based lifespan assays, multi-omics analysis, pharmacological and dietary interventions, and high-resolution microscopy to access the role of peroxisome and organelle function in longevity and healthy aging. Role of Lipid Metabolism in Aging: Using cell culture and C.

elegans based assays to identify how dietary lipids regulate healthy aging and organelle function. The lab aims to use C. elegans as a model to study the effect of dietary lipids on longevity, while using cell-based methods to dissect molecular mechanisms underpinning the regulation. Novel Projects: The HNRCA offers a unique environment where several molecular aspects of healthy aging are being studied. The position

is suitable for working with collaborators and developing new hypotheses and collaborative mechanistic directions in healthy aging.
