Number of Vacancies 1 General Summary The College of Aviation at Western Michigan University invites

applications for the J. Robert Duncan Endowed Professorship. This position is a continuing tenure-track position, which will include the title and responsibilities of Endowed Professor for the first five years. The position focuses on the application of advanced technology to improve learning, development of an applied research program to expand experiential learning, and collaboration on the development of an

industry-style task card lab process. Applicants will join a supportive and collegial environment that values collaboration among students, faculty, and staff. This position represents an exceptional opportunity to contribute to a dynamic academic community and to bring professional aviation leadership and maintenance experience into the classroom and laboratory to enhance student learning. The workload is

anticipated to be 50/50 split between teaching and research/technology advancement in the program. Major Duties Teach up to 12 credits in the fall and spring for a total of no more than 24 credits of regularly scheduled courses or their equivalent workload over an academic year. Engage in scholarly activities, including research, writing, and publication appropriate to the faculty member’s discipline or

interdisciplinary work. Enhance student hands-on learning and workforce readiness by integrating advanced technologies into aviation maintenance training. Establish a task-oriented lab system that mirrors real-world environments demonstrating how maintenance work is developed and delivered. Launch a student-led, faculty-mentored research program that engages undergraduates in solving real industry challenges. Support
