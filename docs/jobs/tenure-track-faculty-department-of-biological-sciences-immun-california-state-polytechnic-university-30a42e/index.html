DESCRIPTION: Cal Poly Humboldt and the Department of Biological Sciences invites applicants for a full-time, academic year, tenure-track faculty position with an emphasis in Immunology and Hematology. DEPARTMENT: The Department of Biological Sciences at Humboldt is dedicated to excellence in undergraduate education, hands-on research, and fostering an inclusive and supportive

academic community. Our faculty expertise spans molecular biology, microbiology, physiology, ecology, and organismal biology, and we are excited to expand our strengths with a new colleague in immunology and hematology who will also contribute to the new Health and Medical Sciences degree program. As one of the academic programs included in our university’s transformation to California’s newest polytechnic

university, this position will contribute to the applied, experiential learning and active mentoring that will prepare students for graduate school, health professions, and careers in biotechnology and related fields. For more information about the department, please visit: https://www.humboldt.edu/biological-sciences PROFESSIONAL DUTIES: Candidates should be committed to teaching excellence and to building a strong

research record. Instructional assignments may include: Immunology, Hematology, and Principles of Biology.
