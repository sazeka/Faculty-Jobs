Position description The Department of Slavic Languages and Literatures at the University of California, Berkeley is hiring a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Polish Language, Literature, and Culture. Area of

specialization: Polish language, literature, and culture of any period, from the early modern to the contemporary. We welcome scholars with a supplementary comparative focus on the Slavic or Jewish languages, literatures, and cultures of Central and Eastern Europe, and candidates with interdisciplinary interests in other fields. The successful candidate will teach introductory and special topics courses in Polish and

East/Central European Literature and Culture, as well as courses designed to appeal to a wider section of undergraduates interested in the humanities, mentor undergraduate and graduate students, supervise and help grow the department’s program in Polish studies, and engage with the broader Slavic and East European studies community on campus. Tenure-track Assistant Professors at Berkeley are expected to excel in

research, teaching, and service. Elements of the position include the following: implementing a program of research that culminates in the publication of books and articles; developing and teaching undergraduate and graduate seminars and lecture courses; supervising dissertation research; overseeing and developing the department’s program in Polish language, literature, and culture; and participating in various

administrative activities in the Slavic department and across the University. UC Berkeley values the family needs of faculty, including dual career couples, single parents, or those who may have taken time off for family reasons.
