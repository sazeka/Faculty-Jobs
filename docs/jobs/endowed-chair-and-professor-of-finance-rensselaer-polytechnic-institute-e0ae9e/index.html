Endowed Chair and Professor of Finance Troy, NY Faculty Lally School of Management Full Time Job Summary: The Lally School of Management at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute seeks applicants for the Kay & Jackson Tai ’72 Endowed Chair and Professor of Finance position starting Fall 2027, at the Full Professor level. The ideal candidate will work in interdisciplinary areas at the cutting edge of finance and analytics

with a focus on risk management, quantitative methods, and the applications and implications of cybersecurity, artificial intelligence (AI), and quantum computing in finance. Candidates with a strong track record of conducting impactful research and innovative teaching in one or more of these areas are preferred. Who we are: Founded in 1824, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI) is the first technological research

university in the United States. We bring creativity, science, and technology together to address society's greatest challenges. We cultivate exceptional problem-solvers by immersing them in a culture of rigorous inquiry, disciplined practice, and hands-on application. Our strategic plan, RPI Forward, charts a pioneering course for the next era of RPI. Explore the RPI Forward plan. ↗︎ Nestled on a beautiful 275-acre

campus in upstate NY, RPI is home to five schools, 32 research centers, three makerspaces, an observatory, one of the world’s fastest supercomputers, and the IBM Quantum System One - the first university-based quantum computer in the world. With top notch employee health and retirement benefits, Rensselaer offers its full-time employees a generous paid time off program, tuition remission, and opportunities for career

growth. Minimum Qualifications: PhD degree in a relevant business discipline from an accredited university. Strong track record of producing original and innovative scholarly work. Publications in premier finance journals. Demonstrated record of effective teaching in graduate and undergraduate finance courses. Demonstrated commitment to teaching basic and advanced topics in finance, risk analytics, and emerging

financial technologies. Demonstrated ability to contribute through service to the Lally School of Management and Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. Preferred Qualifications: Research expertise in one or more of the following areas: risk management, analytics, AI and machine learning in finance, quantitative asset management, cybersecurity, quantum computing, or financial technology compliance and regulation.
