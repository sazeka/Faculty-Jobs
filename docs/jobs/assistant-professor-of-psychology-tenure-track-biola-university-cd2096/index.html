Sign InCreate ProfileEnglish (US)Assistant Professor of Psychology (Tenure-track)United States|req2446Posted on: 7/25/2025 Back to SearchAssistant Professor of Psychology (Tenure-track)Assistant Professor of Psychology (Tenure-Track)Rosemead School of Psychology, Biola University – Fall 2026The Rosemead School of Psychology at Biola University invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Psychology

position beginning August 2026. We are seeking a dynamic individual who will join our faculty in contributing to the training of future psychologists through teaching, clinical training, research mentorship, and the integration of faith and psychology. Qualifications· PhD or PsyD in Psychology from an APA-accredited program, Clinical Psychology/PhD preferred.· Licensed (or license-eligible) in the state of

California· Strong record or potential for excellence in teaching, clinical supervision, and research· Commitment to mentoring and supporting students, including undergraduate and doctoral students· Demonstrated interest in and capacity to integrate psychology and Christian theology Candidates who bring diverse perspectives and a commitment to Christian higher education are encouraged to apply. Candidate interest in

leadership and clinical training will also be viewed favorably. Role and Responsibilities· Teach courses in both graduate and undergraduate programs (area of expertise negotiable)· Contribute to the clinical training and professional development of doctoral students· Engage in scholarship that reflects both scientific rigor and a commitment to the integration of Psychology and Christian Theology· Participate in

departmental service, professional development, and spiritual community life Rosemead and Biola UniversityThe Rosemead School of Psychology is home to a vibrant academic community offering undergraduate programs and APA-accredited PhD and PsyD programs in Clinical Psychology, preparing students for both clinical and academic careers. Biola University is a Christ-centered, private university in La Mirada, California,

serving a diverse student body of approximately 3,500 undergraduates and 2,000 graduate students. Our mission is to equip men and women in mind and character to impact the world for Christ. Faculty are expected to affirm the Biola Doctrinal Statement and Standard of Conduct, and to actively engage in spiritual formation and mentorship within the community.
