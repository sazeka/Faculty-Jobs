About this Position The Department of Veterinary Clinical Sciences, Oklahoma State University (OSU) College of Veterinary Medicine (CVM) invites applications for a tenure- or clinical-track faculty position in equine surgery. If you have a passion for educating the next generation of veterinarians and veterinary specialists, providing exceptional patient care, love contributing to clinical research (welcomed, but not

required), and want to work in a collegial and supportive environment, this is the position for you. Clinical responsibilities include mentorship and training of house officers, supervision of veterinary students, and technical staff in the examination and management of equine surgery patients. You would be joining a team of four board-certified (ACVS) equine surgeons and three board-certified (ACVIM LAIM) equine

internal medicine specialists; one surgeon is also ACVSMR board-certified. The service is further supported by a busy equine theriogenology service and a growing equine field service. Tired of being on call 24/7? We are in the process of building a separate after-hours large animal emergency service. This service currently has two faculty clinicians, one ACVIM LAIM board-certified and one ACVS board-certified, with a

third position posted and waiting to be filled. Additional responsibilities include didactic teaching, contribution to clinical research if desired (not a requirement for career-track), and participation in extension and outreach efforts of the College, if desired.
