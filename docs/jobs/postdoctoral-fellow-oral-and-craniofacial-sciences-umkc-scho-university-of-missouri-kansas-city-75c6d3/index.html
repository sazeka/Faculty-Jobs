We are seeking highly motivated individuals to join the Biomaterials and Bioengineering Laboratory, under the supervision of Dr. Yong Wang

in conducting innovative research on the development of next-generation dental biomaterials for caries prevention and durable restorative therapies. We welcome candidates who are enthusiastic about scientific discovery and eager to collaborate with a diverse team of researchers and clinicians. ResponsibilitiesUtilize multiscale imaging techniques to investigate the structure-property relationships at the

biomaterial-dental tissue interface. Design and conduct experimental and molecular studies to assess the efficacy of emerging biomaterials in oral environments and uncover the mechanisms governing their biological and functional performance. Mentor junior researchers in the laboratory. Assisting with secondary projects relevant to the research goals of the lab.Present research findings in both written and oral

formats.Assist PI in the preparation of proposals, reports, and presentations.This is a 12-month, full-time, benefit eligible, unranked/non-tenure track academic position. Qualifications Ph.D. in Biomaterials, Biomedical Engineering, Dentistry, a related field.Experience in dental materials research is preferred. Experience with microbiology and oral biofilm research is desirable. Experience in cell culture and
