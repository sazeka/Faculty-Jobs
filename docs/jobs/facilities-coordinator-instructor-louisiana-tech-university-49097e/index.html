Position summary

T hank you for your interest in employment with Louisiana Tech University. The School of Biological Sciences offers undergraduate degrees in Biology, Environmental Science, Medical Laboratory Science, and Nutrition and Dietetics as well as MS degrees in Biology, Molecular Sciences and Nanotechnology (MSNT), and Nutrition and Dietetics, and a PhD degree in MSNT. In total, the School has ~650 undergraduate students and

~20-25 graduate students. The School of Biological Sciences at Louisiana Tech University invites applications for a full-time, 12-month Facilities Coordinator/Instructor position. This faculty position combines undergraduate teaching with responsibilities for maintaining the instructional facilities and equipment that support the School's teaching mission. The appointment is approximately 50% instruction and 50%

service, with instructional responsibilities typically including five undergraduate biology courses annually, including summer instruction. Responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Instruction : Teach undergraduate biology lecture and laboratory courses as assigned, appropriate to the Instructor's area of expertise. Participate in curriculum development and assessment activities. Service : Facilities,

Equipment, and Operational Support Maintain and support the instructional laboratories, teaching facilities, and shared equipment used in the School’s instructional programs. Maintain inventories of laboratory equipment, instructional supplies, and other School assets. Perform other duties assigned by the Director of the School of Biological Sciences. Minimum Qualifications Master's degree in Biology or a closely

related discipline from an accredited institution. Demonstrated ability to teach undergraduate biology lecture and laboratory courses. Experience supporting laboratory operations and maintaining scientific equipment. Ability to maintain and troubleshoot laboratory equipment and support instructional facilities. Strong organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills. Valid driver's license or ability to obtain

one consistent with University requirements. Preferred Qualifications College-level teaching experience in biology. Experience maintaining laboratory equipment and coordinating laboratory operations. Experience managing laboratory inventories, supplies, and equipment procurement. Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively with a variety of audiences, including students, faculty, staff, and the public. About
