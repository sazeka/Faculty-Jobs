Position Information Position Information Posting Number F00372 Position Title Assistant Professor of Secondary Education History/Social Studies (1-Year Limited) Division Academic Affairs

Division Department Name Educational Studies Department Position Type Faculty Position Overview The Department of Educational Studies at Rhode Island College invites a creative, collaborative, and student-centered teacher-scholar to join our dynamic faculty. We seek a candidate with strong expertise in History/Social Studies content and innovative, research-based pedagogy, as well as a demonstrated ability to engage

in interdisciplinary work across the college. The primary role of the successful candidate will be to teach in the newly redesigned Secondary Education History/Social Studies Program, which leads to Social Studies teacher certification in Rhode Island. Responsibilities include teaching History/Social Studies Methods courses and supervising candidates in both the Practicum and Student Teaching semesters. Additional

responsibilities include supporting RIDE accreditation efforts in collaboration with the Departments of Educational Studies and History, building professional relationships with local educators and school partners, teaching introductory or advanced History and Social Studies courses as needed, and advising undergraduate students in the major. Essential Duties ● Teach undergraduate courses in the Secondary Education

History/Social Studies Program, including Methods, Practicum, and Student Teaching;● Assist the History/Social Studies Secondary Education Program Coordinator;● Serve as a liaison between the Educational Studies and History Departments;● Collect data for the development of RIDE accreditation reports ● Work with the Office of Partnerships and Placements to build strong relationships with local teachers in the field;●
