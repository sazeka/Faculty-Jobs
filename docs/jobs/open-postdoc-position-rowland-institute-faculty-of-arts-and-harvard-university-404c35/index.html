Explore Academic Positions at Harvard Open Postdoc Position – Rowland Institute Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Position Details Title Open Postdoc Position – Rowland Institute School Faculty of Arts and Sciences Department/Area Optical microscopy Position Description The brain computes through distributed neural circuits on millisecond timescales. To understand how the brain operates, the Z.

Wang Lab at the Rowland Institute at Harvard develops optical tools to record and control neural activity across the entire brain at cellular and millisecond resolution. By combining advanced microscopy, genetically encoded voltage indicators, two-photon optogenetics, and zebrafish neuroscience, we aim to enable causal interrogation of brain-wide neural dynamics at single-cell and single-spike resolution. We invite

applications for a postdoctoral fellow to develop an integrated optical platform for simultaneous whole-brain voltage imaging and targeted optogenetic stimulation in larval zebrafish. The system will combine a high-speed light-sheet microscope, a two-photon holographic stimulation system, and software for instrument control, synchronization, and image acquisition. This project builds on our recent paper (Nature

Methods 2026, in press, preprint) on voltage imaging of neurons distributed across the larval zebrafish brain at over 200 Hz. The postdoctoral fellow will play a leading role in designing, building, integrating, and validating the next-generation platform.More information is available at https://www.wang-lab.science. Basic Qualifications Applicants must hold, or expect to soon receive, a Ph.D. in physics, optics,

engineering, biomedical engineering, or a related field. Candidates should have experience in one or more of the following areas:· Optical microscope design and construction;· Light-sheet or multiphoton microscopy;· Holography, spatial light modulators, or wavefront shaping;· Laser optics, high-speed imaging, or experimental control;· Microscope-control and data-acquisition software development. A strong record of

research in optical imaging, microscopy, scientific instrumentation, or related fields is preferred. Additional Qualifications Experience in neuroscience, optogenetics, or zebrafish research is helpful but not required.
