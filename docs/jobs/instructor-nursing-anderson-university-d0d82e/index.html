Position summary

Anderson University and the College of Health Professions invite applications for a full-time, twelve-month position as Instructor of Nursing. The position will start January 2027. Candidates should be prepared to teach undergraduate clinical courses focusing on patient care within the nursing curriculum. As a full-time faculty member your responsibilities include teaching, advising students, keeping office hours,

observing grading deadlines, administrative work assignments, committee assignments, professional activities and participating in University events designated as being of annual significance. Qualified candidates should have the following: Master’s degree in nursing (Doctoral degree for graduate programs) from a regionally accredited institution that is appropriate for area of teaching according to accreditation

standards on Commission on Collegiate Nursing Education (CCNE) and Southern Association of Colleges and Schools (SACS) Minimum of two years clinical experience as a registered nurse or advanced practice nurse related to area of primary responsibility Teaching experience at the appropriate level of nursing education preferred Hold an unencumbered active license as a registered nurse (advanced practice nurse, if

applicable) in the state of South Carolina or another Compact state Must be fully supportive of the goals, values and strategic direction of the institution and of the South Carolina Baptist Convention. Demonstrates effective Christian qualities and values Demonstrates current experience in client care Job duties include, but are not limited to: Teach undergraduate courses focusing on the nursing curriculum Provide

complete advising to students (e.g., offer appointments, maintain up-to-date advising folders, offer advice related to internships/careers/educational goals) Demonstrate evidence of teaching that is focused on student learning Utilize current teaching models, practices and research in the discipline Participate in continuing professional development in teaching (e.g., workshops, etc.) Show willingness to integrate

faith and learning in their discipline Demonstrate innovation and/or continuous improvement in classroom practices, seeking to engage students in the learning process Design cognitive, effective and psychomotor strategies and techniques in classroom and clinical areas to enhance learning of students in accordance with the established curriculum.
