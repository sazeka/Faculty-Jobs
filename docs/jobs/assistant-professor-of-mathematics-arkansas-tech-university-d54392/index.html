Position Summary The Department of Mathematical and Physical Sciences invites applications for the position of a 9-month tenure track Assistant Professor of mathematics. The position

carries a teaching load of 12 credit hours of lower and upper division classes in mathematics and statistics per semester, performing scholarly activities and research with undergraduate students and serving the department, institution, and community, as assigned by the department head. The department currently consists of fifteen faculty members and eleven instructors. Candidates must have a Ph. D. in mathematics or

a closely related field by the time of appointment in August 2027. Applicants must demonstrate a commitment to teaching excellence and student interaction through advising and student organizations.This position is not eligible for work authorization or visa sponsorship. Applicants must be authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis at the time of application and throughout the duration of employment

without the need for employer sponsorship, now or in the future.
