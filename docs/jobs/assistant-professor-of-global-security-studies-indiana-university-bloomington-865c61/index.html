Posting Details Position Details Title Assistant Professor of Global Security Studies Appointment Status Tenure Track Department IU Bloomington International Studies Location Bloomington Position Summary The Department of International Studies at Indiana University’s Hamilton Lugar School of Global and International Studies invites applications for an assistant professor, tenure-track faculty position in

Security Studies beginning August 1, 2027. We seek a scholar whose research and teaching focus on security studies. Areas of interest include, but are not limited to, grand strategy, the causes of war, nonproliferation, and how new technologies may affect international security and geopolitics. Scholars with regional expertise within security studies are welcome to apply, as well.The successful candidate will

contribute to the department’s strengths in international and global studies and a growing focus on security studies across IU-Bloomington. The candidate’s teaching will support existing undergraduate and graduate programs, including the growing Master of International Affairs (MIA) program.We welcome applications from candidates employing diverse disciplinary and methodological approaches. Candidates should

demonstrate a strong research agenda, evidence of or potential for excellence in teaching, and a commitment to interdisciplinary engagement within an international studies environment. Responsibilities include maintaining an active research program, teaching undergraduate and graduate courses in areas related to global security studies, mentoring students, and contributing to departmental and university service.
