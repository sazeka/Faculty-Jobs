MenuCareers HomeCurrent Openings Adjunct Faculty: Health and Exercise Science (On Campus) Department: Academic Affairs Location: Manchester Community College Pay Range: $0 - $0 Appointment Type: Adjunct Position Number:: N/A Work Schedule: N/A START YOUR APPLICATION Manchester Community College is seeking a dedicated and student-centered instructor to teach in the Health and Exercise Science program. The ideal

candidate will have a Master's degree in Kinesiology, Exercise Science or a related field and will be prepared to teach coursework focused on human movement, sports conditioning, corrective exercise, and resistance training. This course prepares students to assess movement and posture, design individualized exercise programs, and understand the scientific principles underlying physical performance. Minimum

Qualifications: Minimum qualifications are Masters Degree in the specific subject or closely related field and three years’ experience in teaching, coaching, or industry in related fields. The Vice President of Academic Affairs will determine adjunct levels. Adjunct level will be determined based upon Education and Experience. Current pay rates are: Level 1 - $850.00 per contact hour for the course Level 2 - $925.00

per contact hour for the course Level 3 - $1000.00 per contact hour for the course Application Process: Submit your application with cover letter, resume/Curriculum Vitae, statement of teaching philosophy, and copy of unofficial transcripts.
