Department research programs encompass the spectrum of modern life

sciences research from single molecule biophysics to the development of multicellular organisms. We are broadly interested in candidates who use state-of-the-art-tools, including cryoET/cryoEM, single cell genomics/proteomics, single cell imaging, and artificial intelligence and de novo protein design, to address fundamental problems in biochemistry, cell biology, and genetics. However, candidates focused on any

areas that complement the strengths of our collaborative faculty should apply. Candidates must hold a Ph.D. degree and demonstrate commitment to excellence in research and teaching.Northwestern University offers a rich intellectual environment and cutting-edge support facilities. Appointees will join an outstanding, highly interactive research community that is committed to collaboration across disciplinary

boundaries. The Department of Molecular Biosciences has close ties with other Northwestern departments and centers, including Neurobiology, Physics, Statistics, Applied Math, the NSF-Simons Institute for Theory and Mathematics in Biology, the Northwestern Institute on Complex Systems, the Chemistry of Life Processes Institute, and the Robert H. Lurie Comprehensive Cancer Center.Applicants should submit (in PDF

format) a cover letter, CV, description of research plans, and teaching statement. Please request at least three letters of recommendation. Details on preparing and submitting the application can be found on the Molecular Biosciences homepage (http://www.molbiosci.northwestern.edu). The anticipated start date is Fall of 2027. Review of applications will commence October 15, 2026. Inquiries can be sent to Michele

McDonough at molbioscisearch@northwestern.edu.SalaryThe expected base pay range for this position is $110,000 - $125,000.This base pay range is for a nine-month academic appointment, does not include summer salary, and is subject to negotiation.Northwestern University has provided a pay range representing its good faith estimate of what the university reasonably expects to pay for the position.
