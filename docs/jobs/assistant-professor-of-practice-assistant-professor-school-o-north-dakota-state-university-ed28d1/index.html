invites applicants for an Assistant/Associate Professor. We seek collaborative individuals engaged in nursing and health services research, with preference for candidates who possess or demonstrate the potential to develop a funded research portfolio that complements current faculty scholarship. Areas of interest include, but are not limited to, healthcare informatics, implementation science, quality and process

improvement, population health, rural health, health equity, chronic disease management, health systems innovation, care delivery models, and outcomes research. The successful candidate will hold a DNP or PhD in Nursing or a closely related field and be prepared to contribute to the teaching, research, and service missions of the School of Nursing. Clinical practice as a nurse practitioner is encouraged but not

required. The major foci of this position will be to establish and maintain an active, independent program of research and scholarship while teaching doctoral DNP students. Candidates should demonstrate a strong interest and track record in securing extramural funding, leading interdisciplinary research initiatives, and advancing evidence-based improvements in healthcare delivery and outcomes. Additional expectations

include developing and teaching courses in the Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) program and other graduate nursing programs; mentoring DNP students and scholarly projects; supervising graduate student research; serving on School, College, and University committees; contributing to the research, education, and practice missions of the School of Nursing; supporting accreditation standards and program assessment

activities; engaging in student recruitment and retention efforts; fostering academic-practice partnerships; and collaborating with faculty, healthcare systems, and community partners to advance nursing science, healthcare quality, and innovation.
