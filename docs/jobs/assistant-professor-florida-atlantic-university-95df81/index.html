Position Summary: The IT & Operations Management (ITOM) Department in the College of Business at Florida Atlantic University seeks to hire one tenure-track Assistant

Professor in Applied Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Business Analytics for employment starting Fall 2027. Responsibilities: The successful candidate will teach AI, Business Analytics, and Management Information Systems courses in both undergraduate and graduate programs, including MBA and doctoral programs; must maintain an active program of research and publication in Applied AI and Business Analytics; and will

participate in appropriate service to the department, college, university, community, and profession. Emphasis will be placed on producing scholarly research in top-tier peer-reviewed Information Systems and related business journals. For More Information: For questions or additional information about this position, please contact the search committee chair, Dr. Ravi Behara, at rbehara@fau.edu . Minimum

Qualifications: An earned doctorate in Applied Artificial Intelligence, Business Analytics, Management Information Systems, or a related field from a university with AACSB or similar accreditation conferred by the effective date of employment. Demonstrated record of ability to publish quality scholarly research in peer-reviewed academic journals and conferences. Demonstrated record of excellence in teaching as

instructor of record at an accredited (AACSB or similar) university. Certification or experience in online teaching preferred. The ideal candidate will have research and teaching interests focused on Applied Artificial Intelligence and Business Analytics. However, all related areas of teaching and research focus will be considered. Related industry experience is strongly desired. Salary: To be discussed with

applicant.
