Open Rank Faculty – Department of Quantitative and Qualitative Health Sciences Kate Marmion School of Public Health The Kate Marmion School of Public Health (“the School”) was founded in 2022 as a collaboration between The University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio (UT Health San Antonio) and The University of Texas at San Antonio, both designated as Hispanic-Serving Institutions by the U.S. Department

of Education. Effective September 1, 2025, the two universities merged into a single institution, now operating as The University of Texas at San Antonio. The School provides graduate-level academic instruction in public health and conducts research and community engagement activities to promote and maintain community health. The Department of Quantitative and Qualitative Health Sciences seeks faculty candidates with

demonstrable academic skills for faculty positions. The Department of Quantitative and Qualitative Health Sciences welcomes faculty with various research, teaching, and service interests in epidemiology and/or biostatistics in a variety of fields of study, including: epidemiologic research in infectious diseases; epidemiologic research in chronic diseases; cancer epidemiology; spatial, environmental, social, and/or

genetic epidemiology; aging; cardiometabolic and cardiovascular outcomes; substance use; injury prevention; mental health; maternal-fetal and women’s health; exercise and physical activity; and related fields. Faculty may have methodologic interests including cohort, case-control, or cross-sectional studies; secondary data analysis; longitudinal data analysis, meta-analysis; clinical trials; qualitative

methodologies; or mixed methods. The Department seeks to identify successful candidates with demonstrated research portfolios and scholarly publications, and experience in teaching coursework appropriate to graduate-level instruction in the domain of quantitative and qualitative health sciences and their application in public health. Faculty will be expected to contribute formally to the academic curricula, teach

graduate courses, and actively engage in forming the School through internal and external service activities. Applications for appointment at all ranks (assistant, associate, or full professor) and tracks (tenure-track, tenured, or non-tenure track) will be considered based on the candidate’s professional and academic experiences.
