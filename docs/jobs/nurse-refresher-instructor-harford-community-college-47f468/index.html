Benefits Job Description Harford Community College is seeking qualified instructors to teach nurse refresher courses, including theory, lab, and clinical

instruction. Additional duties include, but are not limited to: planning, implementing, and revising the educational program; Selecting and evaluating clinical facilities used to achieve program objectives; Developing & utilizing written examinations to measure achievement of program objectives; Developing and utilizing an evaluation tool to measure the achievement of clinical competencies. The clinical competencies

shall be based upon the RN and LPN standards of practice. Developing and utilizing a mechanism for student evaluation of the course; Assisting with set-up, inventory, and cleaning of the practice lab; Maintaining course and student records and submitting them at course completion; Assisting with the submission of reports and applications to the Maryland Board of Nursing (MBON) as indicated.
