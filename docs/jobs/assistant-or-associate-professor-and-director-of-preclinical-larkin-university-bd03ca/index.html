Assistant or Associate Professor and Director of Preclinical Education Job Title – Assistant/Associate Professor, Dental Education (Biological and Behavioral Sciences)Job Type – Full TimeLocation – Winter Garden, OrlandoDepartment – School of Dental Medicine, Biological and Behavioral SciencesPosition Reports to – Director of Biomedical and Behavioral Sciences or Associate Dean for Academic AffairsAnticipate Start

Date – January 2027Position Summary:The Assistant or Associate Professor and Director of Preclinical Education provides leadership, coordination, and continuous quality improvement of all preclinical simulation-based education within the dental curriculum. This position ensures that preclinical instruction prepares students for safe and competent entry into patient care experiences while maintaining alignment with

institutional goals and Commission on Dental Accreditation (CODA) standards.The Director collaborates with faculty, course directors, and academic leadership to oversee preclinical curriculum delivery, student progression, faculty development, and implementation of innovative evidence-based teaching practices within the simulation learning environment. Key Responsibilities • Lead and oversee all preclinical

simulation laboratory courses, instruction, and educational activities• Ensure integration of preclinical curriculum with biomedical sciences and clinical education• Develop and implement policies, procedures, and best practices for simulation-based instruction• Coordinate scheduling, facilities, equipment, and materials for preclinical laboratories and simulation spaces• Supervise preclinical faculty, course

directors, and simulation support staff• Lead faculty calibration efforts to ensure consistency in instruction, grading, and student assessment• Monitor student performance, competency development, and readiness for transition into clinical care• Deliver didactic and preclinical laboratory instruction and provide direct supervision and feedback to students• Support curriculum development, course assessment,

accreditation preparation, and continuous quality improvement initiatives• Promote innovation through integration of digital dentistry, simulation technologies, and emerging educational methods Required Qualifications • DDS/DMD or equivalent dental degree• Eligibility for Florida State dental licensure or Florida Dental Teaching License• Experience in preclinical dental education or simulation-based teaching•
