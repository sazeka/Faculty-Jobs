Assistant/Associate Professor of Accounting Assistant/Associate Professor of Accounting Angola, Indiana (In-Person Instruction) Full-time Summary Trine University invites applications for a full-time Assistant/Associate Professor position in the Ketner School of Business beginning Fall 2026. The primary responsibility will be teaching undergraduate courses in accounting full-time on main campus in seated classes.

Duties and Responsibilities Trine University is a teaching-focused institution. A standard teaching load for faculty is 12 credit hours per semester. Faculty are expected to spend 80% of their time on teaching, 10% on scholarship, and 10% on university service. Trine does not offer tenure. High-quality teaching is Trine University’s priority; however, faculty members are also expected to engage in professional

development activities, develop/maintain appropriate professional contacts, and serve as academic advisors for students. Service, in the form of committee work, advising student organizations, etc. is highly valued. The ideal candidate will have a minimum of an MBA and be a CPA, but exceptionally qualified applicants with other areas of experience and expertise may be considered. The successful candidate will be able

to teach undergraduate courses such as Accounting I & II, Personal Income Tax, Auditing, Accounting Information Systems, and be able to run an on-campus Volunteer Income Tax Assistance clinic (VITA). Expected teaching load is 12 hours per term. Duties will begin in August 2026. The successful candidate will be able to teach undergraduate courses, support accounting students through academic advising, and successfully

direct and supervise internships. Other responsibilities will include playing an active role in recruiting and retention efforts and participating in Accounting Club activities. Duties will begin in August 2026.
