The Columbia Journalism School ( www.journalism.columbia.edu ) currently seeks a full-time professor (rank open) to join the faculty of our interdisciplinary Ph.D. program in Communications. historical, economic, and technological contexts. &nbsp; The successful candidate should have an ambitious research agenda and a distinguished publishing record as well as experience in and passion for supervising Ph. D. students.
