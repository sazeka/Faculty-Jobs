Position summary

The Department of Social Work & Gerontology at Georgian Court University invites applications for a full-time, tenure-track faculty position in the Social Work program. The successful candidate will contribute to teaching, scholarship, service, and program development in a CSWE-accredited program grounded in Mercy core values, social justice, and community engagement. Responsibilities Teaching & Instruction Teach

BSW/ MSW courses (typical load: approximately 24 credit hours per academic year) Deliver instruction across foundation and advanced practice areas (e.g., HBSE, practice methods, policy, research, clinical practice, and specialization courses) Integrate evidence-based practice, cultural humility, and anti-oppressive frameworks into the curriculum Advise and mentor BSW/MSW students throughout their academic progression

Scholarship & Professional Activity Maintain an active scholarly agenda through research, publications, presentations, or community-engaged scholarship Engage in professional development aligned with social work education and practice Contribute to program visibility and academic excellence Service & Program Support Participate in departmental, college, and university committees Support CSWE accreditation activities,

assessment, and continuous program improvement Collaborate with faculty on curriculum design and review Engage in community partnerships and field education initiatives Qualifications Required PhD or DSW, and an MSW from a CSWE-accredited program Minimum of 2&ndash;5 years of post-MSW social work practice experience Strong commitment to teaching and social work education Demonstrated commitment to social justice and

culturally responsive practice Preferred New Jersey LSW or LCSW license, or eligibility for licensure in New Jersey. Experience teaching at the graduate level Prior involvement in CSWE accreditation, assessment, or curriculum development **Must be US Work Authorized**
