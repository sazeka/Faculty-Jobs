Interior Design Adjunct Instructor

Harcum College  ·  Harcum College, PA
AdjunctInterior Design

Position summary

HARCUM COLLEGE in Bryn Mawr, PA, a leader among the nation&rsquo;s independent associate degree colleges, is currently seeking adjunct instructors for the Spring 2026 semester. The Interior Design Department of Harcum College is seeking PART-TIME (adjunct) INSTRUCTORS for AutoCAD with an emphasis on the Interior Design Industry, to join our team starting in January 2026. HARCUM COLLEGE provides its students with an

opportunity for outstanding academic, career, and life preparation. We teach, mentor, and prepare students for success in their chosen profession in an experiential environment. PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES: The class is an intro level AutoCAD course with an emphasis on layer management, construction documentation, furniture, fixture, and equipment (FF&amp;E) schedules, lighting plans &amp; millwork details, This class

meets 2x/ week in person on campus in Bryn Mawr on Tuesdays &amp; Thursdays. The instructor will use a pre-planned 15-week course shell. The instructor will evaluate student performance through class work, individual progress, projects, assignments, and exams, ensuring mastery of technical skills and proper design application. Instructor will grade student work, provide detailed and timely feedback to students,

connect students to appropriate resources, and maintain accurate records of student progress. QUALIFICATIONS: Prior college-level teaching experience (full-time or adjunct) in AutoCAD is highly desirable but not required. Proficient use of AutoCAD 2021 or later is required. Familiarity with other relevant software (e.g., SketchUp, Adobe Creative Suite) is a strong plus. FOR CONSIDERATION: All interested candidates

should submit the following documents for review: Brief letter of interest Resume or curriculum vitae Contact information for two references About Harcum Harcum College is an independent, coeducational, fully accredited, residential college. The College was founded in 1915 and offers associate degrees in multiple disciplines. Today, Harcum has career-informed degree programs on its Bryn Mawr Campus, west of

Philadelphia, programs online, and at area Partnership Sites serving close to 1,000 students. Harcum College is an Equal Employment Opportunity employer.
