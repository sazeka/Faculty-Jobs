Of Business 1170112 Working/Functional Title Assistant/Associate/Full Professor Tenure System Position Summary The Marketing Department at the Broad College of Business, Michigan State University, seeks applications for an assistant, associate or full professor to begin in the Fall of 2027. We encourage applications from excellent candidates in all research areas. Candidates at the assistant, associate or full

professor levels must have a Ph.D. in marketing or related areas and have a record of high-quality, top-tier publications and demonstrated teaching excellence. Applicants must show continued research productivity and achievement of scholarly prominence and be willing to work with doctoral students and other faculty members. All applicants should demonstrate ability as a classroom instructor through summarized peer

and/or student evaluations and/or explicit commentary in letters of recommendation. The department’s Ph.D., M.S., M.B.A., and B.A. programs are highly regarded (see https://broad.msu.edu/marketing/). While candidates with expertise in all areas of specialization will be considered, we especially seek scholars with expertise in marketing strategy, empirical modeling, international business/marketing, and/or sales who

can contribute to the excellence of our current faculty. Please note that this is a union position represented by the Union of Tenure System Faculty (UTSF). The university is working with the union to establish a collective bargaining agreement. Equal Employment Opportunity Statement All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation,

gender identity, national origin, citizenship, age, disability or protected veteran status.
