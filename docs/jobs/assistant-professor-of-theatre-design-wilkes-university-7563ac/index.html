Professor of Theatre Design (Scenic and Sound) in the Division of Performing Arts, beginning Fall 2027. The Division offers a BFA in Musical Theatre, a BA in Theatre Arts, and a BFA in Theatre Design and Technology, along with minors in Theatre, Music, and Dance. The successful candidate is responsible for: Designing scenery and sound for the Division's four annual mainstage productions Teaching five courses per year

in scenic design, sound design, and other areas of expertise Helping grow and develop the BFA in Theatre Design and Technology, including recruiting and mentoring students Supervising scenic painting, set dressing, and props assistants Leading and taking part in production processes, including technical rehearsals, strikes, and shop work Maintaining an active record of scholarly and creative work Contributing to

university service The position works alongside a full-time Technical Director and Assistant Technical Director, who provide production support. An MFA in Theatre Design or a related field is required. The ideal candidate will have prior scenic and sound design experience in both professional and university settings, as well as university-level experience teaching design. Salary is commensurate with qualifications

and experience. Wilkes University is an independent, comprehensive university dedicated to academic excellence in the liberal arts, sciences, and selected professional programs. The University has approximately 2,000 students at the undergraduate level and over 1,500 full time equivalent students at the graduate and first professional levels. Its institutional focus is on developing strong mentoring relationships

with each of its students and contributing vitally to economic development of Northeastern Pennsylvania. The University is located in Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania, a revitalized city that is located on the lovely Susquehanna River and is within two and one-half hours driving distance of New York City and Philadelphia.
