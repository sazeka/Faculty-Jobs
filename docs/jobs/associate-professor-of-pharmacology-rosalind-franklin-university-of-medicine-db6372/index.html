Associate Professor of Pharmacology

Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine and Science  ·  Pharmacology  ·  Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine and Science, IL
Associate ProfessorPharmacology

Position summary

Compensation: $120,000 - $128,000 depending on experience and qualification Position Summary The Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences invites applications for a full-time, 12-month tenure-track faculty position at the rank of Associate Professor of Pharmacology. We are seeking a visionary, collaborative investigator to expand our research faculty and contribute to our educational mission. The successful candidate

will maintain an internationally recognized, competitively funded research program that complements existing strengths within the department and collaborate across the university. We are particularly interested in outstanding applicants with active, well-funded pharmacology programs and interest in Neuroscience, Cancer, Infectious Disease, Immunology and Genetics, with an interest in translational research and

experimental therapeutics involving animals or other in vivo models. An appropriate startup package and other laboratory support will be provided. The faculty effort distribution is approximately: Research / Scholarship: 70% Teaching: 25% Service: 5% Priority areas: Neuroscience: Including neurodegenerative diseases, neuropharmacology, neuroprotection, or targeted therapeutic delivery to the central nervous system

(CNS). Oncology (Cancer): Focused on biological target identification, drug mechanisms of action, mechanisms of therapeutic resistance, or innovative modalities such as targeted protein degradation. Infectious Disease: Investigating novel antimicrobial, antiviral, or antiparasitic strategies, host-pathogen interactions, or therapeutic approaches to combat resistance. Immunology: Cardio-metabolic diseases, autoimmune

diseases, inflammatory diseases Genetics & Functional Genomics: Utilizing genetic screening, gene regulation pathways, or precision medicine approaches to identify and validate novel pharmacological targets. Our generous benefits package includes medical, dental, and vision plans; paid parental leave; short term and long term disability plans; life insurance; flexible spending accounts; and a 403(b) retirement plan

which includes a 8% employer contribution once eligible. RFUMS is committed to employee wellbeing and work-life balance. This position is eligible for 24 days of vacation/ personal leave, 15 sick days, and 9 paid holidays, paid winter break, plus two floating holidays. How to Apply Interested candidates should submit a single PDF containing: A cover letter summarizing qualifications and translational research
