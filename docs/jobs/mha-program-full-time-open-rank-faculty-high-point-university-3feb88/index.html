Position summary

This is a full-time, 12-month, open-rank faculty appointment in the fully online Master of Healthcare Administration (MHA) Program. The position's primary responsibility is high-quality graduate teaching. The faculty member will also contribute to student advising, curriculum and assessment activities, CAHME accreditation-related work, scholarship, service, and program initiatives. Academic rank will be commensurate

with the candidate's education, experience, and qualifications at the time of hire.
