The Dean of Enrollment Management plans, organizes, and manages the operations of all district-wide related functional areas of the College, including enrollment management for credit instruction, recruitment, registration, admissions, international student services, student records/registrar, financial

aid, student advising, career services, and assessment testing. The Dean provides strategic and operational leadership to the enrollment management activities across the College; evaluates the effectiveness of the College’s enrollment management activities and recommends methods for continuous improvement. Collaborates with the Vice President of Enrollment Management and Student Services and other college personnel

to meet the enrollment growth and retention goals as laid out in the College’s Strategic Plan. POSITION DESCRIPTION Provides primary leadership directly or through intermediate managers in planning, implementing, evaluating and coordinating all aspects of enrollment management operations. Ensures enrollment management personnel are in compliance with college policy, administrative rules, procedures, state and federal laws and regulations.
