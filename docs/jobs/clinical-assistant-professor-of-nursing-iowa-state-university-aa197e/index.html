Position summary

Position Title: Clinical Assistant Professor of Nursing Appointment Type: Faculty Job Description: Summary of Duties and Responsibilities: The Simulation Coordinator is responsible for the planning, coordination, implementation, and evaluation of simulation-based learning experiences for Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) students. This role supports the integration of simulation throughout the nursing curriculum

to enhance clinical judgment, critical thinking, communication, and professional practice competencies. The Simulation Coordinator collaborates with faculty, students, standardized patients, and simulation staff to ensure high-quality, evidence-based simulation experiences that support program outcomes, accreditation standards, and regulatory requirements. The position will be a two-year term with renewal based on

performance and department needs. Candidates must be legally authorized to work in the U.S. on an ongoing basis without sponsorship. Immigration sponsorship is not available for this position. Required Minimum Qualifications: Doctoral degree in nursing or related field and 3 years of clinical experience 2 years simulation experience Current unencumbered RN license. Preferred Qualifications: Experience in nursing

education, simulation-based learning, or clinical education. Knowledge of healthcare simulation standards and best practices. Certification in healthcare simulation (CHSE or CHSE-A) preferred or willingness to obtain certification. Experience with high-fidelity simulation equipment, audiovisual technologies, and simulation management software. Knowledge of nursing IBON and accreditation standards and regulatory

requirements. Strong organizational, communication, collaboration, and project management skills. Demonstrated ability to work effectively with faculty, students, healthcare partners, and interdisciplinary teams. Experience with concept-based curriculum design, competency-based nursing education, and the integration of learning experiences that support the development and assessment of clinical judgment. Experience

with learning management systems and educational technologies. Department/Program & College Description: Our mission is to enhance sustainable food, nutrition and healthcare systems that promote human well-being and improve the quality of life through education, research and outreach in food science, nutritional sciences, dietetics and nursing. Our vision is to be a global leader in food science, nutritional sciences
