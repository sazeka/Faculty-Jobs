Posting Details Position Information Job Title Art & Crafts Instructor Job Summary Schoolcraft College is seeking enthusiastic and knowledgeable instructors to teach a variety of art and craft classes for adult learners such as colored pencil, Acrylic Painting, Patel Art, Cartooning, Macrame,

Jewelry making and more through its Personal & Professional Learning division. Personal & Professional Learning offers a diverse range of non-credit courses and programs designed to enrich the lives of adult and senior learners. Classes are taught by experienced professionals who bring expertise, creativity, and passion to the learning experience. Instructors may develop and teach new courses or lead existing

offerings in their area of expertise. Classes vary in format, schedule, and duration, ranging from single-session workshops to multi-week courses and full-semester programs. Successful candidates will create engaging, welcoming, and inclusive learning environments that encourage creativity, skill development, and lifelong learning. Job Duties Schoolcraft College Personal & Professional Learning is seeking an Art &

Crafts Instructor to develop new courses and/or teach existing art and crafts classes for adult learners, fostering a fun, welcoming, and engaging learning environment.
