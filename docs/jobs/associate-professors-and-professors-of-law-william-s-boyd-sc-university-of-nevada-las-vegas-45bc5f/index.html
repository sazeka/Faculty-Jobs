Job Description The University of Nevada, Las Vegas

invites applications for Associate Professors and Professors of Law within the William S. Boyd School of Law [ [R0152946]. ROLE of the POSITION The University of Nevada, Las Vegas—William S. Boyd School of Law invites applications from both entry-level and lateral candidates for tenure system positions expected to begin July 1, 2027. We aim to fill two positions. We aim to hire an entry-level tenure-track candidate,

at the rank of Associate Professor of Law (tenure-track), for our top-ranked Lawyering Process (Legal Writing) Program as well as a lateral candidate, at the rank of Associate Professor of Law (tenure-track) or Professor of Law (tenured). Once a candidate has been selected, they may be offered an initial opportunity to serve as Director of our Workplace Law Program, with the opportunity to teach courses in the areas

of labor law, disability law, and/or employment law. The Directorship will be for an initial one-year term. Any additional term(s) will be subject to satisfactory performance and may be offered at the discretion of the Dean. We boast a 3-course teaching load and generous research leaves. We have a unified tenure system for faculty teaching doctrinal, clinical, and lawyering process courses. Faculty who primarily

teach lawyering process courses may also choose to teach a doctrinal course. Additional areas of curricular focus include Contracts, Torts, Professional Responsibility and Sports/Entertainment Law, as well as faculty who will build on Boyd's academic concentrations .
