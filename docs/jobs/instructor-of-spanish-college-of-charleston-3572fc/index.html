Instructor of Spanish Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Details POSTING INFORMATION Internal Title Instructor of Spanish Position Type Instructor School/Area School of Languages, Cultures, and World Affairs Department Hispanic Studies Position Description The College of Charleston’s Department of Hispanic Studies invites applications for a full-time Instructor of Spanish beginning

August 2027. The successful candidate will contribute to the department’s commitment to excellence in language instruction and professional applications of Spanish. [charleston.edu] We seek a dynamic teacher whose primary area is Spanish for the Health Sciences, community-engaged learning, and professional Spanish. Areas of interest may include Spanish Sociolinguistics and Healthcare, Medical Humanities, Medical and

Health Communication, Disability Studies, or related fields with relevance to health contexts. A successful dossier will include either a description of a proposed class in their desired field or a sample syllabus. Responsibilities: Teach undergraduate courses in Spanish Basic Language and Spanish for the Health Professions. Support departmental and college-wide initiatives connecting language study to professional

and community needs. Ability to collaborate with programs in the College’s health sciences and pre-professional programs Teaching load is 4/4 Qualifications: Ph.D. (or ABD with degree completed by start date) in Hispanic Linguistics, Linguistics, Applied Linguistics, or a related field, with at least 18-credit hours of graduate work in Spanish. Native or near-native proficiency in Spanish and English. Demonstrated

excellence in teaching undergraduate Spanish courses at different levels both in person and online. The position is benefits-eligible and the salary is competitive. Questions concerning the position should be addressed to the department chair, Dr. Susan Divine. For full consideration for the position submit December 28, 2027.
