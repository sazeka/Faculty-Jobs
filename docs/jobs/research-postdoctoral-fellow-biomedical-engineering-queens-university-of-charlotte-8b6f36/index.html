Department: 85082 Wake Forest University Health Sciences - Academic Biomedical Engineering Status: Full time Benefits Eligible: Yes Hou rs Per Week: 40 Schedule Details/Additional Information: Job Summary The Connected Health Lab within the Center for Remote Health Monitoring at Wake Forest University School of Medicine is seeking a motivated and enthusiastic Postdoctoral Research Fellow to join a growing

interdisciplinary research program focused on developing, evaluating, and implementing technology-enabled approaches to improve mobility, physical function, and musculoskeletal health. Our research integrates biomechanics, artificial intelligence, digital health, behavioral science, implementation science, and human-centered design to create scalable solutions that improve health across the lifespan. Current and

planned projects include developing objective movement assessment tools using smartphone video, markerless motion capture, wearable sensors, and machine learning; designing and evaluating digital interventions to improve physical activity and musculoskeletal health; developing digital biomarkers of mobility and physical function; implementing technology-enabled interventions within healthcare systems; and building

next-generation digital health platforms that combine movement assessment, remote monitoring, and personalized behavioral support. Additional collaborative projects include adaptive sports, healthy aging, rehabilitation, and sports performance. Researchers will have the opportunity to contribute to ongoing studies while helping shape new research directions and develop independent projects through collaborations

across Wake Forest University School of Medicine and external academic, clinical, and industry partners.
