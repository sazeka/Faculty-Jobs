Position Information Position Title Visiting Instructor Political Science Requisition Number F00542P Job Description The Department of Political

Science and Philosophy at Weber State University invites applications for a one-year, full-time Visiting Instructor position in Political Science, beginning Fall 2027 and continuing through the 2027–2028 academic year.We seek candidates with expertise in Comparative Politics who can contribute to the department’s undergraduate curriculum in comparative and global politics. The successful candidate will be expected to

teach a combination of introductory and upper-division political science courses.The anticipated Fall 2027 teaching schedule includes two sections of POLS 1010: Politics, Power, and the State, (our Introduction to Political Science course), POLS 3140: Foreign Policy of the United States, and POLS 3210: Politics in the European Union. The Spring 2028 schedule will include POLS 1010: Politics, Power, and the State and

POLS 2200: Global Governments, Politics, and Societies. The remaining spring courses will be determined in part by the successful candidate’s areas of expertise and departmental needs. Possible upper-division offerings may include POLS 4200: Dictatorships, POLS 3290: Democratization, Violence, and Political Transitions, POLS 3200: Middle East and North Africa, or a special-topics course in the candidate’s area of

expertise.The standard teaching load is four courses per semester. Job Duties Required Qualifications A Ph.D. in Political Science or a closely related field. ABD candidates will be considered. Preferred Qualifications Candidates should demonstrate a commitment to effective undergraduate teaching and the ability to work with students from a range of backgrounds and academic interests.
