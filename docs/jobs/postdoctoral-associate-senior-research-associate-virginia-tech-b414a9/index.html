Postdoctoral Associate/Senior Research Associate 534264 Blacksburg, Virginia Physics Research Faculty College of Science Job Description We invite applications for postdoctoral associate or research associate positions in theoretical Quantum Information Science in the Physics Department of Virginia Tech. The focus of these positions will be on quantum computing and quantum simulation, quantum networks, and quantum

control. There will also be an opportunity to test theoretical results on cloud quantum processors. The successful candidates will join a vibrant research environment in the quantum information groups of Profs. Sophia Economou and Edwin Barnes at Virginia Tech. There will be plenty of opportunities for collaboration with other members of the group and with some of the leading experimental and theory groups around the

world. Our group is part of the DOE Quantum Center “Codesign for quantum advantage” (https://www.bnl.gov/quantumcenter/), and there will be opportunities for collaboration with the researchers of the center. The Postdoctoral and Senior Research Associate positions will also involve opportunities for travel to present in conferences and to collaborate with other groups. Postdoctoral and Senior Research Associates will

conduct research in the above areas and disseminate results in journal publications and conference presentations. Senior Research Associates will have additional responsibilities with mentoring the junior group members and potentially assisting the PIs with grant management. Required Qualifications - Postdoctoral associate applicants must hold a Ph.D. in either Quantum Information Science, Theoretical Condensed

Matter, AMO Physics or a closely related field. PhD must be awarded no more than four years prior to the effective date of appointment with a minimum of one year eligibility remaining. A strong background in Quantum Mechanics and programming skills are necessary.- Senior research associate applicants must have a Ph.D. in either Quantum Information Science, Theoretical Condensed Matter, AMO Physics or a closely

related field. Prior postdoctoral experience. A strong background in Quantum Mechanics and programming skills are necessary. Preferred Qualifications Knowledge of concepts and techniques from Quantum Information Science are preferred. Experience with Python and Mathematica is preferred. Overtime Status Exempt: Not eligible for overtime Appointment Type Restricted Salary Information Hours per week 40 Review Date
