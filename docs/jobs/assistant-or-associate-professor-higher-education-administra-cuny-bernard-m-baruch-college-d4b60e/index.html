Assistant or Associate Professor- Higher Education Administration FACULTY VACANCY ANNOUNCEMENT "At Baruch College we believe that student success is everyone’s responsibility” The Austin W. Marxe School of Public and International Affairs at Baruch College invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant or tenured Associate Professor of Higher Education Administration, beginning in August 2027. The position

requires teaching across the MSEd and EdD in Higher Education Administration. The candidate will also be expected to chair dissertation-in-practice committees and mentor doctoral students. The Marxe School values a faculty which represents diverse viewpoints and backgrounds and is committed to building an inclusive academic community. For more information on the values and priorities of the school, please see our

mission statement and strategic plan posted at https://marxe.baruch.cuny.edu/about/ QUALIFICATIONS Candidates must hold a PhD or other doctorate in higher education, higher education administration, educational leadership, or a closely related field or be on schedule for dissertation defense by Summer 2027. The candidate must have completed a doctorate prior to the teaching start date for the Fall 2027 semester.

Experience chairing doctoral committees is preferred. The search committee seeks candidates who maintain an active program of scholarly research in higher education or a closely related field. We are especially interested in candidates whose work draws on critical methodologies suited to practitioner-scholar doctoral research, including critical quantitative methods; policy analysis, case study methods, participatory

action research, or community-based participatory research; or critical qualitative and narrative research. We will give special consideration to candidates who have chaired Doctor of Education (EdD) or equivalent doctoral dissertation committees and who have helped develop or refine EdD curriculum. The ideal candidate brings a record of expanding access to postsecondary education and to advancing educational

justice, demonstrated through a combination of research, teaching, mentoring, and service. COMPENSATION Assistant Level: $94,909 - $102,017 Associate Level: $98,995- $105,816 CUNY is in the process of implementing salary schedule increases. Once implemented, salaries of eligible employees will be adjusted by 3.5% effective 9/1/26, in accordance with the terms of the PSC-CUNY collective bargaining agreement. CUNY
