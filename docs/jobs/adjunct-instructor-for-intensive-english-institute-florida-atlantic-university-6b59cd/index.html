Position Summary: The Intensive English Institute invites applications for adjunct instructor positions. The primary role of IEI is to prepare internationals for

university admissions to FAU. IEI also serves language learners who are seeking language training for other academic and professional goals. Adjunct instructor responsibilities include: Delivering challenging and stimulating lessons in-person; instruction is primarily in-person with hi-flex/hybrid options. Preparing materials for assigned course(s); transforming course objectives and materials into useful learning

activities for students. Holding regular office hours and meeting/conferencing with students as needed. Applying knowledge of language, culture, cross-cultural communication and language acquisition to assist learners in own language objectives. Affirming learners’ own cultural and linguistic backgrounds. Participating in faculty meetings and other departmental meetings and activities. Participating in faculty

professional development and implementing research in class. Minimum Qualifications: - Master’s degree in TESOL, Linguistics, Applied Linguistics, or English OR - Master’s degree with 18 graduate credits in linguistics, TESOL, English, Education, language instruction, or related area - Excellent English language proficiency Salary: Commensurate with Experience College or Department: Intensive English Institute
