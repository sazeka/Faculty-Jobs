Job Search Subscribe to jobs Assistant Professor Anatomy Apply now Job no: 493016 Work type: FT Location: West Des Moines Categories: Faculty At Des Moines University Medicine and Health Sciences, you will make a meaningful contribution to the dynamic future of medicine and health sciences while helping shape the next generation of healthcare leaders. DMU is seeking an Anatomy Faculty Member who is passionate about

teaching, discovery, and service. What you’ll do: In this role, you will play a vital part in delivering an exceptional educational experience while advancing scholarly work and contributing to the academic community. You will: Teach and/or coordinate courses within the prescribed curriculum, including engaging classroom and gross anatomy laboratory instruction Facilitate active learning through small-group

experiences such as problem-based and case-based sessions Support student success through mentoring, advising, and maintaining regular office hours Design and implement effective instructional strategies, assessments, and innovative teaching approaches Develop and sustain an independent, externally funded research program Prepare and submit grant proposals and disseminate research through peer-reviewed publications

Pursue ongoing professional development to stay at the forefront of anatomical sciences Actively contribute to departmental, university, and professional service, including governance and committee work Engage in community outreach and service initiatives that reflect DMU’s mission What we are looking for: We are seeking a collaborative educator and scholar who brings enthusiasm, expertise, and a commitment to

student success. Ideal candidates will have: Earned doctoral degree (or willingness to complete one within an agreed timeframe) Strong communication and presentation skills Excellent organizational and time management abilities Proficiency with computer applications and the ability to integrate technology into teaching Commitment to and alignment with DMU’s mission and values. Preferred qualifications include:

Demonstrated teaching experience in anatomical sciences A record of—or strong potential for—developing an externally funded research program Evidence of scholarly activity, including publications Ability or willingness to teach histology and/or neuroanatomy topics is preferred To see the full job description, please refer to the Position Description at the bottom of this posting.
