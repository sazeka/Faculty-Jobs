Position summary

Job Description: Berklee College of Music in Boston, Massachusetts, is actively seeking applicants for a full time faculty position within its esteemed Electronic Production and Design department. As one of the globe's leading independent music colleges, Berklee stands as a pinnacle institution dedicated to the exploration and advancement of contemporary music. At Berklee, a vibrant community thrives, of

exceptionally talented and diverse students, revered faculty members, over 500 committed staff, and a network of accomplished alumni. Berklee's commitment to arts education is reflected in the work of its students, faculty, and alumni - who have been recognized with 332 Grammy Awards, 118 Latin Grammy Awards, 31 Emmy Awards, 7 Academy Awards, and 11 Tony Awards. The Electronic Production and Design (EPD) Department

at Berklee is redefining what it means to be a musician in the 21st century. We educate artists, producers, performers, technologists, designers, and innovators who combine creativity with technological fluency to push the boundaries of music and sound. Our curriculum spans music production, sound design, immersive audio, electronic performance, AI, coding, game audio, interactive media, new musical instrument

design, and emerging creative technologies. The EPD Department invites applications for a full-time faculty position in the Electronic Digital Instrument (EDI) principal instrument performance program. We seek a visionary artist, performer, and educator with an established international reputation as an electronic music recording and performing artist. The ideal candidate will have a significant body of original

music, a distinctive artistic voice, and a sustained record of professional performances, recordings, and creative projects that have made meaningful contributions to the field. They will demonstrate exceptional musicianship, technical fluency, and performance virtuosity through their own artistic practice, with experience as a solo artist, collaborative performer, and/or a performing member of internationally

recognized touring acts. They will inspire students to develop artistry, technical mastery, and performance virtuosity while contributing to the continued evolution of a forward-thinking pedagogy for electronic music performance and the Electronic Digital Instrument as a contemporary principal instrument.
