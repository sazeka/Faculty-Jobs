Business Analytics/Finance Faculty (Open Rank) JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY Florida Polytechnic University invites applications for a tenure-equivalent faculty position in Analytical Finance at the rank of Instructor, Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, or Professor, beginning August 2027. Our Business Analytics program emphasizes a STEM-focused, quantitative approach to business, preparing students to apply data

analysis, statistical modeling, and computational methods to complex business problems. This faculty position will strengthen our business core and Analytical Finance concentration by connecting financial theory with rigorous analytical methods and data-driven financial decision-making. JOB DESCRIPTION Responsibilities Teach relevant courses in finance to support the B.S. in Business Analytics and online M.S. in

Engineering Management programs; develop and teach other courses in your area of expertise. Maintain a program of scholarship that leads to publication in the discipline (Assistant Professor and above). Instructors demonstrate a scholarly, intentional approach to pedagogy and curriculum instead. Contribute to department and University service. Areas of interest Areas of interest include, but are not limited to,

financial analytics, financial econometrics, investments and asset pricing, corporate finance, financial risk management, and financial technology (fintech). Candidates in finance, financial economics, or related fields whose teaching and, as appropriate to rank, research complement the program’s STEM and analytics focus are encouraged to apply; other areas of specialization will also be considered. Minimum

qualifications Instructor: An earned master’s degree from an accredited institution, with at least 18 graduate credit hours in finance, financial economics, or a closely related field. Assistant Professor: An earned doctorate in finance, financial economics, or a closely related field, completed before the start date. Associate Professor: An earned doctorate in finance, financial economics, or a closely related

field, plus six years at the rank of Assistant Professor or equivalent. Professor: An earned doctorate in finance, financial economics, or a closely related field, plus six years at the rank of Associate Professor or equivalent. All ranks: Evidence of effective college-level teaching and excellent oral and written communication skills. Preferred qualifications For candidates with graduate degrees in business
