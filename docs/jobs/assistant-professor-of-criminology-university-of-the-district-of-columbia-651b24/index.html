Essential Duties and Responsibilities Teaches courses in Criminal Justice: Assists with recruitment

Recommends selection of OER (Open Educational Resources) textbooks and other instructional materials. Familiarity with and conducts assessment of student learning outcomes (course and curriculum levels) in accordance with the standards of the College and Middle States Commission of Higher Education.
