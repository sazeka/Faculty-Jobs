POSITION SPECIFICS The Department of Pathology and

Laboratory Medicine at Penn State College of Medicine invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor position in the Experimental Pathology Division. This recruitment is a critical component of the department’s strategic effort to revitalize and expand the division’s research strength, extramural funding activity, and collaborative scientific impact. The successful candidate will be expected to establish

and sustain an innovative, externally funded research program in genetics, functional genomics, computational phenomics, or a closely related field. The position also includes serving as Scientific Director of the Zebrafish Functional Genomics Core, an institutional shared resource that supports investigators across the College of Medicine through genome editing, imaging, and high-throughput phenotyping capabilities.

Key Responsibilities Develop and maintain an independent, externally funded research program in genetics, genomics, computational phenomics, or a related area. Additionally, also serve as Scientific Director of the Zebrafish Functional Genomics Core, providing faculty leadership for genome editing, zebrafish husbandry, imaging, and high-throughput phenotyping services. Advance interdisciplinary and translational

research collaborations with basic science departments, the Penn State Cancer Institute, and multidisciplinary research teams. Contribute to the growth and sustainability of shared research resources and institutional scientific platforms. Mentor graduate students, postdoctoral fellows, trainees, and junior investigators.
