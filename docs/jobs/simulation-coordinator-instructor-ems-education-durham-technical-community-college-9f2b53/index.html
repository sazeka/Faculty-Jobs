Description Do Great Things! Durham Technical Community College is hiring a Coordinator/Instructor in the Emergency Medical Science Program. The EMS Simulation Coordinator/Instructor is responsible for the day-to-day operation of EMS simulation across all credentialed curriculum & continuing education programs. The coordinator/instructor oversees the preparation, maintenance, inventory and laboratory operation of all

simulation equipment/supplies and collaborates with faculty to design, plan, implement and schedule simulation activities. The coordinator/instructor ensures adherence to program policies, course objectives, program outcomes and current simulation best practice and safety measures.
