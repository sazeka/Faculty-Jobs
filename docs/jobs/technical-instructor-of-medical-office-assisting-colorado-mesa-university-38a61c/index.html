Technical Instructor of Medical Office Assisting

Colorado Mesa University  ·  Medical Office Assisting  ·  Grand Junction, CO

Position summary

ANNOUNCMENT OF POSITION VACANCY ABOUT THIS POSITION Summary: We are seeking a passionate and experienced Medical Assistant Instructor to join our faculty. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in medical assisting and a dedication to teaching and mentoring students. As a Medical Assistant Instructor, you will be responsible for delivering high-quality instruction, developing curriculum, and ensuring that

students acquire the skills and knowledge necessary to excel in the medical field. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES include the following. Other related duties may be assigned. Essential Duties and Responsibilities: Conduct both lecture and lab instruction for 15 credit hours per semester and maintain 5 hours of office time per week for student consultation and support Work independently and collaboratively with

colleagues to develop innovative curriculum that addresses the needs of our diverse student population. Demonstrate extensive knowledge and experience in medical assisting, utilizing the most current equipment and technology to enhance instruction in clinical and administrative procedures. Working knowledge and skill of up-to-date medical assisting principles, methods, and techniques. Evaluate student performance in

coursework using industry-related standards and expectations to ensure competency and readiness for the workforce. Determine program needs and manage the program budget, including purchasing and cost control, to maintain high-quality educational resources. Maintain knowledge of industry-standard medical equipment and demonstrate its functionality in instructional settings. Engage successfully with students,

healthcare professionals, other educators, and the public in a professional and courteous manner. Assist in the recruitment, advisement, placement, and development of cooperative work experience opportunities for students, and conduct follow-up with graduates. Serve as a representative of the department by participating in committees, task forces, and events at the campus, college, and/or state levels. Exhibit

excellent organizational, communication, and presentation skills, along with a positive and enthusiastic attitude towards teaching and student success Excellent organization, communication, and presentation skills. Positive and enthusiastic attitude. Advise students, maintain student files and paperwork, including job sheets, registration, and intent to graduate. Coordinate industry engagement with local employers
