PURPOSE & SCOPE The AI Initiatives Librarian provides strategic leadership and cross-organizational coordination for the Libraries’ adoption,

integration, assessment, and ethical stewardship of Artificial Intelligence technologies. In alignment with the Libraries’ mission, vision and, values and the Association of Research Libraries (ARL) Guiding Principles for Artificial Intelligence , this position builds institutional capacity for innovation across library services, internal workflows, and collections. Reporting to the Associate Dean for Digital

Strategies & Scholarly Communication, the AI Initiatives Librarian operates as a key collaborator, working across library units, including Learning & Research Services, Technical Services, Distinctive Collections, Research Data & Open Scholarship, Learning Research & Clinical Information Services, and Library IT to guide AI literacy, assess emerging tools, evaluate the effectiveness and user impact of AI-related

services, and to facilitate strategic AI adoption. Campus partners may include the UMIT AI Team, Academic Technologies, the Writing Center, and the Platform for Excellence in Teaching & Learning (PETAL). ESSENTIAL DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES Strategic Leadership & Project Coordination In collaboration with the Associate Dean for Digital Strategies & Scholarly Communication, library leadership, and key stakeholders,

design and coordinate the implementation of a strategic roadmap for AI-based innovation across library operations and services. Track, facilitate, and lead (when applicable) library-wide AI projects, establishing metrics and assessment measures to evaluate their impact on workflows and user engagement. Identify opportunities to enhance library workflows or services with the use of AI technologies. Evaluate emerging

generative and agentic AI tools, natural language processing applications, and machine learning software to recommend adoption strategies. Serve as a key representative on library and university committees, including the AI & Libraries Steering Group and the Libraries’-based UX Team.
