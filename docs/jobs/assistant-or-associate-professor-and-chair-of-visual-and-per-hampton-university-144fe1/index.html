Assistant or Associate Professor and Chair of Visual and Performing Arts Hampton University Faculty The Department of Visual and Performing Arts in the School of Liberal Arts and Education seeks to fill a chairperson position at the Associate or Assistant professor level beginning Fall 2027. The department is grounded in the University’s historic mission, offering a comprehensive arts education integrating theory,

practice, and innovation across multiple disciplines. It houses a BA in Art, BA in Dance, BA in Graphic Design and BA in Theatre. The successful candidate must be committed to managing the growth and development of the Visual and Performing Arts. They should also be committed to teaching, the advisement of students and capable of leading faculty in research and grantsmanship activity. Specific responsibilities will

include oversight of the faculty through consistent observation and evaluation of teaching effectiveness, and the work of the department such as development of the short and long-range plans for the academic unit, assurance of the quality and coordination of instruction, preparation of annual faculty and staff evaluations, overseeing the recruitment of students, preparation of a budget for the expenditure of

departmental funds, acquisition of external funding from grant sources and conducting research projects through personal initiative. Job Duties Include: Provide academic leadership for the department, including strategic planning, curriculum review, assessment, scheduling, and coordination of instruction. Teach undergraduate courses in the candidate’s area of expertise within art, dance, graphic design, theatre, or a

closely related visual or performing arts discipline. Advise Visual and Performing Arts majors and hold office hours. Supervise, mentor, and evaluate departmental faculty and staff in accordance with university policies. Prepare and manage the departmental budget; pursue grants, external funding, and other resources that support program goals. Contribute to the scholarly advancement of the department through a

productive research agenda that includes agenda that includes grantsmanship activity. Demonstrate dedication to the progress of the Visual and Performing Arts Department, the School of Liberal Arts and Education and to the University and community, through service activities.
