Academic Faculty & Community Based Physician Opportunity- Emergency Medicine

Medical College of Wisconsin  ·  Department of Emergency Medicine at the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW)  ·  Milwaukee, WI
Emergency Medicine

Position summary

The Department of Emergency Medicine at the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW) is actively recruiting community-focused physicians to join our thriving clinical practice across multiple locations in Southeast Wisconsin. Whether you're just beginning your career or are an experienced physician seeking to make a meaningful impact, MCW Emergency Medicine offers a supportive and rewarding environment where you can

thrive. We are currently seeking board-eligible or board-certified Emergency Medicine physicians to join our team in a variety of settings, including clinical academic practice, VA practice, and community-based hospital locations. We are comprised of nationally and internationally renowned, dedicated group of faculty and staff, united by a strong commitment to clinical excellence, education, and service. Join us and

be part of a mission-driven team that values community engagement, collaboration, and innovation in emergency care. Position and Department Highlights: We invite you to become part of a dynamic group of clinician educators driving a growing academic emergency medicine practice. Our department proudly hosts one of the oldest, continuously accredited Emergency Medicine residency programs in the United States. Our

Emergency Department team is nationally recognized for expertise across a broad spectrum of academic disciplines, including EMS and Disaster Medicine, Toxicology, Injury Prevention and Control, Cardiac Resuscitation, Global Health, Ultrasound, Medical Education, Emergency Department Process Improvement We are seeking candidates who bring strong clinical skills, excellent interpersonal abilities, enthusiasm for

academic medicine, a commitment to MCW’s academic missions Faculty practice in a collaborative, well-supported environment alongside experienced APPs, emergency medicine nurses, EMS professionals, and responsive consulting services. Our Emergency Department offers 24/7 access to radiology and laboratory services to support high-quality care. Locations: We provide exceptional emergency care at a variety of sites,

including: Froedtert Hospital ED – Level 1 Adult Trauma Center Children’s Wisconsin ED – Level 1 Pediatric Trauma Center Clement J. Zablocki VA Medical Center ED Froedtert Menomonee Falls Hospital ED Froedtert West Bend Hospital ED Froedtert Community Hospitals in New Berlin, Pewaukee, Oak Creek, and Mequon Why MCW?
