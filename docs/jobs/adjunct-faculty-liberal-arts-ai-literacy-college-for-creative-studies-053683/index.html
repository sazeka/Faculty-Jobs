Adjunct Faculty Liberal Arts – AI Literacy STATUS: Part TimeDIVISION: Undergraduate StudiesREPORTS TO: Chair of Liberal ArtsSUPERVISES: N/AFLSA STATUS: Non Exempt POSITION DESCRIPTION The College for Creative Studies (CCS) invites applications for adjunct faculty members to teach Understanding AI on Mondays from 12.45-3.30 in the Winter of 2027. Course description: This course examines artificial intelligence – the

aim is to understand what it is, what it does, and what it means. Students learn how AI systems generate outputs and how those outputs should be read, interpreted, and evaluated. Emphasis is placed on identifying error, bias, and hidden assumptions, as well as assessing credibility and evidence. The course situates AI within broader ethical, legal, and social contexts, including questions of responsibility, power,

and inequality. Students develop the capacity to engage AI critically and thoughtfully, treating its outputs not as authoritative answers but as texts requiring analysis and judgment. Experience teaching these or nearby topics at the college or university level is absolutely essential. Experience with Canvas or other LMS is also essential.
