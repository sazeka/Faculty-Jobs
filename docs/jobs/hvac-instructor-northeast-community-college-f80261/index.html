Under minimal supervision, the HVAC Instructor is responsible for delivering high-quality instruction in HVACR technology across a variety

of instructional formats and locations. The instructor will support student learning and success while developing and maintaining curriculum aligned with industry standards and workforce needs. Instructional emphasis includes residential, commercial, and industrial heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration (HVACR) systems; system installation, design, and maintenance; electrical components and

controls; airflow distribution and psychometrics; refrigerant handling and piping systems; and troubleshooting and repair techniques. The instructor will also integrate safety standards, energy efficiently principles, sustainability practices, and emerging technologies relevant to the HVACR industry. The instructor must be able to read and interpret technical documents such as safety regulations, HVACR codes,

blueprints, and installation manuals. Responsibilities include documenting student progress, maintaining accurate records, and utilizing instructional technologies and tools relevant to the trade. Additionally, the instructor will collaborate with faculty and staff to ensure a high-quality educational experience for a diverse student population while adhering to the College’s mission, policies, and procedures.
