Job Title Assistant Teaching Professor/Assistant Lecturer - Mathematics Department Mathematics Department Worker Type Regular Pay Type Salary Salary will be commensurate with the level of the position, education, and experience. Benefit Eligible Yes Screening Date 2026-10-05 Job Description Summary Teach undergraduate courses in mathematics, provide academic advising, and contribute to professional and institutional

service. Job Description The Department of Mathematics at Miami University invites applications for an Assistant Teaching Professor or Assistant Lecturer position starting in Fall 2027. Duties include teaching mathematics courses (primarily introductory mathematics courses, precalculus, calculus, and possibly mathematics education courses), advising, and service to the department. The position may also involve

coordinating sections of high-enrollment courses. This is an on-campus position requiring a regular on-campus presence. Based on performance, this position is eligible for subsequent annual reappointment with the possibility of promotion to the associate and, subsequently, full rank that includes longer-term continuing contracts. Responsibilities: Prepare and deliver lectures, and hands-on learning activities. Assess

student learning through assignments, projects, quizzes, and exams. Maintain accurate grade records and submit grades in accordance with university deadlines and guidelines. Hold regular office hours to support student learning. Adhere to departmental, college, and university policies. Engage in assigned academic advising and/or university and departmental service. Minimum Qualifications: Required: For appointment as

an Assistant Lecturer, an M.S. or M.A. in Mathematics or Applied Mathematics, or a closely related area by date of appointment is required. For appointment as an Assistant Teaching Professor, a Ph.D. in Mathematics or Applied Mathematics, or a closely related area by date of appointment is required. Additional Position Information (if applicable) Required Application Documents Resume/CV and Cover letter, teaching

statement, transcripts, evidence of teaching effectiveness. Special Instructions (if applicable) Inquiries may be directed to Dr. Narcisse Randrianantoanina at mathsearch@miamioh.edu. Screening of applications will begin January 5th 2027 and will continue until the position is filled. Additional Information A criminal background check is required.
