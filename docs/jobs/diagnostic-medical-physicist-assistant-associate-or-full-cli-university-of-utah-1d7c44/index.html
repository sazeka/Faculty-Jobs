Candidates must demonstrate the ability to manage radiology departmental

operations to achieve high performance and quality goals and effectively interact and communicate with others. The candidate must have experience and training in radiation safety regulations, principles, techniques, and nuclear materials handling and the ability to practically apply this knowledge to resolve safety issues. He/she must have knowledge of imaging techniques, processes, and procedures used in diagnostics

and the ability to produce images of internal body structures for clinical purposes or medical science. Knowledge of radiography tools, techniques, and applications, and of the equipment used in clinical diagnosis, treatment, and monitoring, as well as the ability to select, procure, maintain, quality check, and dispose of medical equipment in compliance with related policies is also required. The applicant must be

able to perform acceptance testing and quality control of diagnostic imaging equipment. The position requires performing and reviewing shielding calculations, implementing quality control programs, revising and optimizing imaging protocols, and managing protocol doses.
