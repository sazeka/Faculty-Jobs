Instructor - Mechanical Engineering

University of North Florida  ·  Mechanical Engineering  ·  Jacksonville, FL

Position summary

Department School of Engineering Compensation Negotiable General Description/Primary Purpose: The School of Engineering is seeking a 12-month, non-tenure earning, Instructor of Mechanical Engineering. Position responsibilities include teaching undergraduate and graduate courses with a 4/4/1 teaching load, collaboration with local industry and service to the School, College of Computing, Engineering and Construction

and the University of North Florida. Supervisor : This position will report to Dr. Alan Harris, the School of Engineering Director. Working Hours: Varies Supervision Exercised: This is not a supervisory role Required Licensure: N/A Anticipated Start Date: The position is expected to begin on August 2, 2027. Required Qualifications: The candidate must hold a Master’s Degree or higher in Mechanical Engineering, or

closely related field. A PhD is required to teach graduate courses. Preferred Qualifications / Skills: Candidates from all areas of mechanical engineering are encouraged to apply, with preference given to those who demonstrate a record of teaching experience or practice in their profession in a non-academic setting such as industry or government laboratories. Additional Application Materials Required: Applicants

should be prepared to provide the following required documents: · Current curriculum vitae · Cover letter · Statement of Teaching Philosophy Name, phone number, and email address of three professional references will be requested during the application review stage. The selected candidate will be required to submit official transcripts (and, as applicable, U.S. degree equivalency evaluations) documenting the

conferral of their qualifying academic credentials by the date of hire. Additional Requirements: Applicants may be required to upload additional documents based on their responses to the Foreign Influence Review questions. · Passport (all pages including blank pages) and · Form DS-160 (if ever submitted. If not, upload an explanation) Application Review Date : Initial review of applications will begin on October 12,

2026. Job Posting Close Date : Open until filled. How to Apply: Applicants must submit an online application that includes all required application documents. If you have any questions about this position, please contact Alan Harris at alan.harris@unf.edu About the Department: The School of Engineering at the University of North Florida provides high-quality education in a student-centered environment.
