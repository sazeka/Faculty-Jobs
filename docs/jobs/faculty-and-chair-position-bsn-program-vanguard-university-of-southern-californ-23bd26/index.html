Share Apply Now Title Faculty and Chair Position - BSN Program Description Position Summary: Vanguard University is looking for an outstanding nurse-educator would relish leading our Bachelor of Science in Nursing program, a role which includes teaching and mentoring in a warm and diverse community of people who are serious about following Jesus Christ. The BSN Program Chair is a full-time faculty member who teaches

Medical-Surgical Nursing and provides day-to-day operational leadership for our BSN pre-licensure program. The Chair supports high-quality instruction, student progression and success, faculty coordination, and operational excellence. The role ensures ongoing compliance with regulatory and accreditation standards and advances Vanguard’s mission through academic excellence, service, and Christian commitment. Essential

Functions: BSN Program Operational Leadership Provide daily coordination of BSN program operations, ensuring consistent execution of program policies, schedules, and processes. Oversee course scheduling, clinical and lab coordination (in collaboration with clinical placement coordinator), faculty teaching assignments, and coverage plans. Maintain program readiness for regulatory and accreditation expectations by

supporting documentation, compliance tracking, and continuous quality improvement. Coordinate student progression processes (e.g., progression checkpoints, remediation pathways, retention supports) in alignment with program policies. Monitor key student outcomes and help implement improvement plans. Serve as a primary point of contact for day-to-day student issues requiring program-level coordination; escalate

complex matters to the Director as appropriate. Support admissions, orientation, and student communication workflows in collaboration with Admissions and Student Services. Reports to the Director of Nursing Faculty and Course Coordination Lead BSN faculty operational coordination (e.g., course lead meetings, exam/assessment coordination, clinical evaluation consistency). Promote consistency across sections in course

delivery, grading practices, clinical evaluation tools, and student communication. Support onboarding and mentoring of new and adjunct faculty; coordinate peer support, teaching resources, and compliance training. Curriculum and Assessment Support Support implementation of the BSN curriculum, including alignment of course objectives, learning activities, clinical experiences, and program outcomes.
