Number 201104121 Position Title Clinical Assistant/Associate Professor - Periodontics State Wisconsin Employment Status Full Time Position Status Regular If Limited Term (End Date of Assignment, Project, or Grant) Position Type Faculty Job Family Faculty # Hours Per Week 37.5 Position Overview Marquette University School of Dentistry (MUSoD) seeks qualified candidates for a full-time clinical assistant/associate

faculty position in periodontics at the school’s main campus in Milwaukee. As Wisconsin’s only dental school, MUSoD is answering the call to meet workforce needs, increase access to oral health care for underserved patients, and advance dental education in Wisconsin. MUSoD serves the community on its main campus in Milwaukee and in outreach clinics across the State of Wisconsin. Duties and Responsibilities

Responsibilities include full participation in all aspects of 1) periodontic clinical instruction rotations and simulation lab instruction of predoctoral dental students, and 2) clinical instruction of post-graduate students in the Advanced Education Program in Periodontics. Teaching will include didactic courses and direct supervision of patient care. Administrative responsibilities include participation in

committee assignments and eventual directorship of predoctoral courses as teaching development progresses. Required Knowledge, Skills and Abilities Qualified applicants must have: DMD/DDS degree from a US or Canadian dental school recognized by the ADA Commission on Dental Accreditation or a comparable international degreeCompleted an Advanced Dental Education Program in Periodontics accredited by the ADA Commission
