Assistant Professor in Photography, tenure track 493383 Gambier, OH, Ohio Studio Art Faculty Kenyon College, a highly selective, nationally ranked liberal arts college in central Ohio, welcomes applications for a full-time Tenure-Track Assistant Professor of Photography in the Studio Art Department beginning July 2027. The ideal candidate will have strong conceptual and technical grounding in analog and digital

photography, alongside an understanding of photography as an evolving and interdisciplinary field. The candidate may have experience in experimental and alternative photographic processes, socially engaged and community-based art practices, or archival research. Related practices might include time-based media, photo-based installation, or generative image-making. They should demonstrate knowledge of the histories,

theories, and contemporary contexts of photography and image culture; the ability to teach a broad range of fine art photographic practices; and a strong understanding of contemporary art. Candidates should be active in their field, with a record of professional achievement and a demonstrated commitment to teaching. Preference will be given to candidates with at least three years of collegiate-level or equivalent

teaching experience. An MFA and an active professional artistic practice are required. Responsibilities include teaching five courses per year on all levels including one cross-media advanced seminar. Applicants must demonstrate strong teaching skills, with teaching experience at the college level expected. Additional responsibilities include maintaining the digital photography classroom and printers, the darkroom

facility, the lighting studio, and equipment (digital printers, cameras, lighting studio equipment), purchasing supplies, implementing and adhering to safety protocols, and supervising the student lab monitors. The Studio Art Department includes 6.0 FTE and is housed in a well-appointed new building, Horvitz Hall. Kenyon College is a highly selective, independent, highly ranked liberal arts college of 1700 students

located in the beautiful village of Gambier, 45 miles northeast of Columbus, Ohio. Kenyon offers competitive salaries and has an excellent benefits package, including provisions for a spouse or domestic partner. Faculty Development support is available to assist with pedagogical and artistic projects.
