JOB TITLE Assistant Professor of Work and Organizations/Organizational Behavior LOCATION Worcester DEPARTMENT NAME The Business School DIVISION NAME Worcester Polytechnic Institute - WPI JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY Do you want to have a greater impact inside and outside the classroom? Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI), a premier university located in New England, might be the place for you! WPI's Business School (the

Business School) seeks motivated applicants for the tenure-track position of Assistant Professor in Work and Organizations/Organizational Behavior to build on the university's strengths in the future of work, organizing, and leadership in the digital age. Successful candidates must be prepared to conduct high-impact research, publish in high-quality outlets and commit to high-quality teaching. Candidates with

interests in ethnographic and qualitative work are especially encouraged to apply. JOB DESCRIPTION A Ph.D. in management, organization studies, or other related field is required. Preference will be given to candidates with an ability to engage in high-quality scholarship and high-quality teaching, and have demonstrated interests in creativity, innovation, or leadership; how digital tools and technologies (including

artificial intelligence (AI)) are transforming workplace dynamics, organizations, careers, and industries; social impact, sustainability and ethics in the age of AI; or leading and managing cross-cultural, cross-disciplinary teams . Candidates should demonstrate ability and willingness to teach at the graduate and undergraduate level. If you are interested in joining our faculty and team of motivated and caring

colleagues, you should include: · A cover letter · A Curriculum Vitae including three references · Teaching evaluations Timing: The position will start in August 2027. We expect to interview at AOM 2026 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Review of applications to start on a rolling basis as they come in until the position is filled. For questions or clarifications, please contact Elizabeth Long Lingo, Search Committee

Chair, at ellingo@wpi.edu . About the Business School: The WPI Business School is not your average business school.
