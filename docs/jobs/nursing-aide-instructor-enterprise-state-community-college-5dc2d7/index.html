Nursing Program Coordinator, the Nurse Aide (CNA) Instructor will teach students according to the established policies and procedures to provide competent and effective classroom and laboratory instruction at varied times and places based on student demand. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Teach face to face courses, during the scheduled meeting times in the area of Certified Nursing Assistant. Prepare

instructional materials, deliver instruction, and evaluate and record student progress in relation to achieving the objectives and learning outcomes of the course.Adhere to times scheduled for class sessions. Prepare, maintain, and submit accurate student and class records in accordance with College policies and procedures.Work cooperatively with others to plan and accomplish goals of the ESCC.Assign outside learning

experiences to students that reinforce classroom instruction. Evaluate inventory of instructional materials and equipment and advise the Nursing Program Coordinator and college administration on related needs.Maintain licensure and certification as required. Maintain program certifications/accreditations as required.Provide clinical supervision at varied times and places and in varied formats based on student demand;

day, evening, or weekend, at any approved clinical site. Support the college’s mission, including the institution’s commitment to providing opportunities to students without regard to race, sex, age, income, religion, disability, or occupation. Qualifications Required Qualifications:Licensed Practical Nurse or Registered Nurse with at least two years of in field experience.An active unencumbered Alabama Board of

Nursing License with no restrictions as a Registered Nurse. Preferred Qualifications:Prior teaching experience Other Special Qualifications/Requirements of the Job:1. Ability to work flexible hours as needed.2. Communicate in a professional, effective and pleasant manner with students, college employees, clinical facilities, and the general public.
