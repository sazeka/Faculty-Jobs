Adjunct Clinical Nursing Instructor

Judson University  ·  Judson University, IL
AdjunctNursing

Position summary

Job Summary: Ambria College of Nursing at Judson University seeks applications from qualified faculty to teach maternity and pediatric clinical in Fall 2024 and Spring 2025. Join a team of nurse educators who are passionate about training future nurses. Primary Responsibilities: Plans for clinical experiences that maximizes the hands-on patient care experience on the clinical floor. Provides direct supervision and

instruction throughout the scheduled clinical day. Develops clinical assignments consistent with student’s knowledge base, skill competency and individual learning needs. Evaluates and provides documented feedback to students on level of performance based on course objectives. Qualifications: Required qualifications: LPN Program clinical instructors must hold a BSN degree. ADN Program clinical instructors must hold

an MSN degree. Unencumbered professional nurse licensure in the state of Illinois Minimum of two years’ experience as a registered nurse within the last five years Valid BLS for Healthcare Providers CPR certification Compliance with all clinical healthcare requirements Preferred qualifications: MSN with an Emphasis in Nursing Education preferred Previous clinical teaching experience Salary/Benefits: Adjunct faculty

hold the rank of instructor and are hired on a per-course basis. Contact Information: Applicants must provide a complete application packet including: Cover letter Judson Faculty Application, including answers to the three General/Faith Questions Curriculum vitae, and Unofficial transcripts of both undergraduate and graduate work (official transcripts will be required upon hiring) and Please submit all application

materials to: Malina Campos at malina.campos@judsonu.edu. Timeline: For full consideration, please submit all required materials as soon as possible. This position will remain open until filled. About Judson University: Judson University is a Christian institution representing the Church at work in higher education. Its campus is located along the picturesque Fox River in the city of Elgin just 36 miles northwest of

Chicago, Judson is home to over 1,100 students from 42 states and 28 countries. We offer more than 50 undergraduate majors, minors, and pre-professional programs; seven master’s degrees and three doctoral programs. Ambria College of Nursing was acquired by Judson University in May 2024 to enhance both institutions’ commitment to excellence in healthcare education and expanding opportunities for nursing students.
