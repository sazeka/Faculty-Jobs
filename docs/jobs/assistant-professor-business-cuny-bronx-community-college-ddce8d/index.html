Assistant Professor - Business FACULTY VACANCY ANNOUNCEMENT The Department of Business seeks a student-centered Assistant Professor of Accounting to teach undergraduate accounting courses, support curriculum development, engage in scholarly and professional activities, and contribute to Hostos Community College's mission. A candidate with strong accounting knowledge, effective teaching skills, professional accounting

experience, commitment to student success, and the ability to serve a diverse urban community college population. The ideal candidate engages in scholarly and professional activities that advance accounting knowledge, accounting pedagogy, student success, and assessment practices. Interest in research related to accounting education, artificial intelligence and technology in business education, data analytics,

curriculum assessment, retention, persistence, and transfer pathways from community colleges to four-year institutions is highly desirable. The selected candidate will teach introductory, financial, and managerial accounting; participate in departmental, college, and university service; advise and mentor students; engage in professional development and scholarly activities; and support assessment and curriculum

review efforts. Strong scholarly activities include: Research on theoretical accounting; research on teaching and learning, i.e., accounting pedagogy and active-learning techniques, AI, data analytics, and technology integration in accounting; and research into student success and transfer from community colleges to four-year colleges. Assessment activities include studies on: Assessment of curricula such as program

learning outcomes (PLO) or student learning outcomes (SLO); studies of student success, retention, persistence, and completion in accounting courses. Essential duties require in-person work on campus. experience. Also required are the ability to teach successfully, demonstrated scholarship or achievement, and ability to cooperate with others for the good of the institution.
