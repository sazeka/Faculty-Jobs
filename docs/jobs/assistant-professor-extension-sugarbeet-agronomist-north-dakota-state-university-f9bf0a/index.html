The incumbent will be based at NDSU and expected to work in both North Dakota and Minnesota. The successful candidate will lead the development and implementation of Extension and applied research programs focused on weed

management and cropping systems for sugarbeet production. Responsibilities include developing educational materials and programs for producers, industry professionals, and Extension personnel; conducting applied research; publishing results in peer-reviewed journals and Extension publications; delivering science-based recommendations to support successful sugarbeet production; and pursuing extramural funding. The

incumbent will build collaborative partnerships (including equipment and resources) with NDSU faculty and staff, specifically in the NDSU Departments of Plant Sciences and Plant Pathology, Microbiology, and Biotechnology; with the University of Minnesota; the Sugarbeet Research and Education Board; USDA sugarbeet researchers; allied industry partners in the sugarbeet industry; and sugarbeet cooperatives. Extension

programming will use a variety of delivery methods, including print and digital publications, web resources, presentations, field days, workshops, radio, television, and virtual platforms. Additional responsibilities include coordinating Extension and research activities across department and college faculty and Extension agents across a range of agronomic disciplines, and advising graduate students.Hiring Range:
