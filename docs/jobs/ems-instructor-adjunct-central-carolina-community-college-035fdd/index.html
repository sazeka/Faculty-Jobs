Join us in shaping futures, strengthening our region, and contributing to a mission-driven culture rooted in service and opportunity.The EMS Adjunct Instructor is responsible for delivering

high-quality instruction in accordance with state and national EMS education standards. This role involves teaching assigned EMS courses—ranging from EMT to Paramedic level—using both didactic and hands-on instructional methods. The instructor ensures that all course content aligns with curriculum guidelines and regulatory requirements set by the North Carolina Office of EMS and/or CoAEMSP, where applicable.

Responsibilities include preparing lesson plans, assessing student performance, maintaining accurate records, and promoting a safe and inclusive learning environment. Adjunct instructors may also assist with skills labs, simulation exercises, and clinical coordination under the direction of the Program Director. Strong communication skills, current EMS certification at or above the level being taught, and a

commitment to student success are essential. Instructors serve as professional role models and are expected to stay current with best practices and continuing education in EMS.This position may have occasional travel between campuses and for training.
