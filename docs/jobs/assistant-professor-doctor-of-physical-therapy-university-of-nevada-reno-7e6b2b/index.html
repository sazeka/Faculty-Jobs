Position summary

The University of Nevada, Reno (UNR) appreciates your interest in employment at our growing institution. We want your application process to go smoothly and quickly. Final applications must be submitted prior to the close of the recruitment. If you need assistance or have questions regarding the application process, please contact our recruitment helpline at (775) 784-1495 or jobs@unr.edu . Job Description The

University of Nevada, Reno School of Public Health invites applications for a state-funded, tenure-track Assistant Professor position to support the launch and growth of its new Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) program. We seek candidates with expertise in one or more of the following areas: Cardiopulmonary Physical Therapy, Exercise Physiology, Physiology, Musculoskeletal Physical Therapy, Neuroscience/Neurologic

Rehabilitation, Applied Clinical Pathology, and/or Anatomy. Candidates with strong research agendas in rehabilitation science, exercise physiology, neuroscience, biomechanics, applied physiology, clinical pathology, or related fields are encouraged to apply. This role offers the opportunity to help shape a developing DPT program at a Carnegie R1 research institution, build an independent research program, engage in

interdisciplinary collaboration, and contribute to academic excellence in the region. Key Responsibilities Develop and sustain an independent, extramurally fundable research program aligned with one or more of the following areas: Cardiopulmonary Physical Therapy / Exercise Physiology / Physiology, Musculoskeletal Physical Therapy, Neuroscience/Neurologic Rehabilitation, Applied Clinical Pathology, and/or Anatomy.

Teach in the DPT curriculum and/or related graduate programs in areas of expertise (e.g., physiology, exercise physiology, neuroscience, pathology, research methods, or related foundational sciences). Mentor DPT students, graduate students, and/or postdoctoral trainees in research and scholarly activities. Contribute to curriculum development, program assessment, and CAPTE accreditation processes as appropriate. Seek

and secure extramural funding to support research activities. Disseminate research findings through peer-reviewed publications, presentations, and other scholarly outlets. Foster interdisciplinary collaborations within the School of Public Health, across UNR, and with regional clinical and research partners. Participate in university, school, and professional service activities. Required Qualifications Earned
