Back to Listings Academic Professional Track (Non-Tenure): Clinical Assistant Professor Comprehensive Dentistry Location Dallas, TX Open Date 8/26/2026 Position ID 186454 Description The Department of Comprehensive Dentistry at Texas A&M University College of Dentistry invites applications for one full-time non-tenure track position with an 11-month appointment beginning Fall semester of 2026. Applicants will be

considered for the title of Clinical Assistant Professor. Responsibilities include coordinating clinical activity, supervising patient care, participating in undergraduate didactic and clinical teaching, evaluation of dental students' professionalism, clinical progress, and performance, as well as counseling students regularly. Interested individuals should have a strong commitment to teaching and service. The

opportunity for extramural private practice is available. Qualifications Candidate must have a DDS, DMD, or equivalent degree (e.g BDS) from a U.S. or recognized dental school. Advanced education certificate in prosthodontics from a U.S. or foreign equivalent accredited dental school. Completion of a Master's of Science in Dentistry degree. Be eligible for licensure in Texas. Candidate should have experience in all

fields of general dentistry. At least three (3) years of experience teaching in an academic setting At least three (3) years of experience with current dental digital technologies. All final candidates will be required to successfully pass a criminal background check prior to beginning employment. Application Instructions Interested applicants must apply through the Texas A&M University faculty job board hosted by

Interfolio at https://apply.interfolio.com/188765 and upload the following: 1) Curriculum Vitae, 2) Cover Letter 3) List of three references with full contact information including email addresses, and 4) Personal Statement include philosophy and plans for research, teaching, and service, as applicable. For questions related to this position, please contact Gracie Castillo gracie.rangel@tamu.edu Incomplete and/or

ineligible submissions will not be considered. Equal Opportunity/Veterans/Disability Employer. The University is aware that attracting and retaining exceptional faculty often depends on meeting the needs of two careers and therefore has a Dual Career Program.
