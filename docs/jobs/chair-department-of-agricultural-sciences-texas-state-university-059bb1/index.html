Chair of the Department of Agricultural Sciences is responsible for academic leadership in the department in its efforts to recruit, retain and evaluate and distinguished faculty and promote their professional development. They will support high-quality instructional programs, oversee curriculum and degree programs, guide the department in recruiting and retaining students, increase and support scholarly and grant

activity, carry out the department’s strategic planning, and budget preparation and execution. The Chair oversees and manages department facilities such as greenhouses, farms, and gardens. They will work closely with the Division of Research and the Freeman Ranch to support department activities. This position is a 12-month appointment.
