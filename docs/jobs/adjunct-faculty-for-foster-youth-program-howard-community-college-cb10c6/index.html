depending on education & experience Summary The Adjunct Faculty for the Foster Youth Program provides high-quality instruction and academic support to students participating in Howard Community College’s Foster Youth Program, in partnership with Howard County Department of Social Services Ready by 21 Independent Living Program and Youth Transitional Services. The instructor creates an inclusive, supportive, and

trauma-informed learning environment that promotes academic achievement, career readiness, and personal development for current and former foster youth.The instructor delivers course content using evidence-based instructional practices while collaborating with program staff to support student persistence, engagement, and successful completion of educational and workforce pathways. Courses may include college success,

workforce readiness, life skills, strength and career exploration, financial literacy, or discipline-specific academic instruction.
