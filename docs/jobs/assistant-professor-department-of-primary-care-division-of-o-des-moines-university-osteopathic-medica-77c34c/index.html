Job Search Subscribe to jobs Assistant Professor - Department of Primary Care, Division of Osteopathic Manual Medicine (OMM) Apply now Job no: 492994 Work type: FT Location: Des Moines Categories: Clinical Faculty and Staff Des Moines University Medicine and Health Sciences is seeking a clinically focused, mission-driven physician educator to join the Division of Osteopathic Manual Medicine (OMM). This role offers a

unique opportunity to build a meaningful clinical practice while shaping the next generation of physicians through hands-on teaching and mentorship. The position is primarily dedicated to delivering high-quality primary care, including osteopathic manual medicine, complemented by a defined and impactful commitment to academic engagement. What you’ll do: Provide comprehensive primary care, integrating osteopathic

manual medicine, while managing an active patient panel and supporting clinic operations Meet clinical productivity and quality benchmarks; participate in peer review and quality improvement initiatives Precept medical students in clinical settings and mentor OMM scholars and residents Contribute to teaching across lectures, labs, and clinical instruction, including student evaluation and assessment Engage in service

activities, including committees, admissions, community outreach, and required compliance responsibilities What we are looking for: Have earned a Doctor of Osteopathy or Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine degree from an AOA-accredited college of osteopathic medicine. Board-eligible/certified by the American Osteopathic Board of (specialty) or board-eligible/certified by the American Board of (specialty). If

board-eligible, you must obtain certification within one year of employment. Must possess significant experience in osteopathic manual medicine (e.g. completion of an undergraduate OMM fellowship/scholarship, experience teaching OMM, completion of an OMM/NMM residency, etc.) Be a member in good standing in all appropriate professional and governmental organizations. Must be eligible to obtain and maintain active

provider enrollment and credentialing with commercial, Medicare, and Medicaid insurance payers or ability to obtain within 60 days of hire. *To see the full job description go to Position Description at the bottom of this posting.
