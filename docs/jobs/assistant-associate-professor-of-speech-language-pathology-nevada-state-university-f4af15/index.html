Job Description Nevada State University invites applications for the position of Assistant or Associate Professor of Speech-Language Pathology to start either Spring 2027 or Fall 2027, negotiated upon offer . The Assistant/Associate Professor will contribute to program development and academic leadership. Qualified candidates may be considered for department chair responsibilities in the future. Our campus is

designated a Hispanic-Serving and Asian American Native American Pacific Islander Institution, and we are looking for candidates with a demonstrated commitment to and success working in diverse, multicultural, and multilingual communities. Our program seeks candidates who are passionate about fostering success among our highly diverse student population as well as preparing students to advocate in a similar manner

for their own clients/patients. Candidates should be able to teach speech-language pathology courses at both the undergraduate and graduate levels, particularly courses with a focus on adult neurological conditions. About School of Education at Nevada State: The School of Education (SOE) is an exciting place to be. More than 1,200 undergraduate, post-baccalaureate, and graduate students are pursuing licensure

programs in elementary and secondary, special education, as well as speech-language pathology and school psychology. In response to a statewide shortage of K-12 educators, speech-language pathologists and school psychologists, Nevada State University has made a strong commitment to building out the SOE to include the following academic programs: B.A. in Speech Pathology B.A. in Early Childhood and Early Childhood
