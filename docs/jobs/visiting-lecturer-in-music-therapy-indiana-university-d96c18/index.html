undergraduate and graduate degrees. This is a full time, non-tenured track, 10-month position beginning August 1, 2026.This position provides opportunities to further develop a creative and innovative music therapy program with unique access to clinical, research, and community partners, enhancing student and faculty experiences. Ideal candidates will have teaching experience and will be expected to provide teaching

coverage.The Music Therapy Program embraces the impact creative therapies have upon the health and mental well-being of those who we engage. Located on a Health and Life Science campus, this program recognizes music’s ability to contribute to healing and its unique ability to serve as a vehicle for engaging with patients, family members and other caregivers in meaningful and effective ways.
