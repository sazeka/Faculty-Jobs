TITLE: Clinical Assistant Professor JOB PURPOSE: The Clinical Assistant Professor serves as a physician educator within the University of Wyoming Family Medicine Residency Program, providing clinical supervision, teaching, mentorship, and evaluation of resident physicians while delivering high-quality patient care. This position supports the educational mission of the residency program through direct resident

instruction, curriculum development, scholarly activity, and participation in program administration and continuous improvement. The faculty member maintains an active behavioral clinical practice at the Educational Health Center Clinic and contributes to the advancement of family medicine education, patient care, and community health.
