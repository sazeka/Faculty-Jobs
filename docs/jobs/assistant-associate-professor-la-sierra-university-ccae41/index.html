Assistant/Associate Professor, Adventist Educational Administration Program LinkedIn Twitter Email Message Share Faculty Riverside, CA • ID: 2251-723 • Part-Time Job Description The School of Education at La Sierra University invites applications for a 20% part-time faculty position to oversee the University's Seventh-day Adventist Educational Administration Program, including the Principal Endorsement and

Certificate Program. The position is expected to begin on a mutually agreed-upon date. This is a non-tenure-track, part-time (20%) faculty appointment. The initial appointment will be for two years, subject to annual performance reviews and program needs. The contract may be renewed or extended by mutual agreement and continued institutional need. This position is primarily administrative and instructional, focusing

on leadership and oversight of the Adventist Educational Administration Program and related certificate offerings. This position is primarily online. The successful candidate will provide leadership for the Adventist Educational Administration Program, oversee program operations, advise students, coordinate course offerings and instructors, support recruitment efforts, and maintain strong relationships with Adventist

schools, conferences, unions, and educational organizations. The School of Education offers graduate programs in educational leadership and administration, providing professional preparation for educators seeking leadership roles within Seventh-day Adventist schools and organizations. Students are drawn from across North America and from international Adventist educational systems. Responsibilities The successful

applicant will: Provide leadership and oversight for the Seventh-day Adventist Educational Administration Program. Coordinate and administer the Adventist Principal Endorsement and Certificate Program. Teach selected online graduate courses in educational leadership, administration, or related areas. Advise and mentor students pursuing Adventist educational leadership credentials. Collaborate with conferences,

unions, and Adventist educational organizations to support recruitment, enrollment, and program development. Coordinate course scheduling and collaborate with adjunct faculty and program instructors. Assist with curriculum review, program assessment, accreditation requirements, and continuous improvement efforts. Support marketing, recruitment, and retention efforts for Adventist educational leadership programs.
