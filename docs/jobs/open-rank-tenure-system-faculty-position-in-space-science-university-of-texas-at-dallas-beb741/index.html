Location Richardson Position End Date (if temporary) Posting Open Date 10/27/2026 Posting Close Date 05/31/2027 Open Until Filled No Desired Start Date 08/01/2027 Job Summary Position Description The Department of Physics in the School of Natural Sciences and Mathematics (NSM) at The University of Texas at Dallas (UT Dallas) seeks a visionary, collaborative individual for an open rank tenure system faculty position

in space sciences. Although open rank, the preferred appointment would be at the associate or full professor level with tenure. We invite applications from candidates who are highly accomplished scholars and academic leaders with experience in spacecraft instrument development and management, and an established record in ionosphere-thermosphere-magnetosphere (ITM) system research. The selected candidate will be a

faculty member in the Department of Physics and affiliated with UT Dallas’s W. B. Hanson Center for Space Sciences (CSS), which has developed and flown over 60 spacecraft and rocket instruments during its >60-year history. Currently, nine satellites carry CSS instruments in Earth orbit, and the planned addition of GDC Trailblazer in 2027 will further expand CSS-based measurements relevant to ITM science. In addition

to projects related to spacecraft instrumentation, the CSS has a vigorous research program associated with fundamental and applied studies of the Earth’s ITM system using a wide variety of ground-based instrumentation, advanced numerical models, and data analyses. The successful candidate will be expected to contribute new externally funded projects that leverage the engineering expertise and spacecraft

instrumentation development resources available at the CSS. Additionally, the candidate is expected to engage with space science faculty and staff in synergistic efforts towards strengthening the CSS. The candidate is expected to lead space instrumentation efforts at the center into the future. The appointment commences for the fall 2027 semester. Qualifications Minimum Education and Experience: A PhD or equivalent
