POSITION SPECIFICS The Department of Biochemistry and

Molecular Biology at Penn State , University Park campus, invites applications for a tenure-track faculty position at the rank of Assistant or Associate Professor . The successful applicant will join the highly collaborative Penn State Center for Eukaryotic Gene Regulation . The search is open to any area of eukaryotic gene regulation. Of particular interest for this year’s search is the use of biochemical methods to

investigate eukaryotic gene regulation. The anticipated starting date for this appointment is summer or fall of 2027 or spring of 2028. The duties include research, teaching, supervision of student research at both the undergraduate and graduate levels, and service to the university and the greater professional community. Candidates are expected to demonstrate interest in and capacity for developing a strong,

independent research program, and teaching and mentorship excellence at both the undergraduate and graduate levels.
