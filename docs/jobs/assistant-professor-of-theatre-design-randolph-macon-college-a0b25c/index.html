Position summary

Position Details : Randolph-Macon College invites applications for a full-time tenure-track position of Assistant Professor of Theatre Design in the Department of Arts to begin September 1, 2027. Institution Information: Randolph-Macon College is a nationally recognized residential institution of about 1800 students located in Ashland, Virginia, just north of Richmond. Founded in 1830, the college offers a broad

range of academic programs, including a graduate program in PA studies. The RMC curriculum, grounded in a liberal arts core, fosters intellectual growth and personal development, with a steadfast focus on helping students find and navigate pathways through college and to successful outcomes. With a commitment to inclusivity and hands-on learning, Randolph-Macon College provides an extraordinary educational experience

&ndash; enhanced by exceptional athletic and extracurricular activities and supportive alumni &ndash; and prepares graduates for success in their chosen fields and in their lives. The growing Theatre Program at Randolph-Macon College&mdash;recently relocated to newly constructed, state-of-the-art facilities&mdash;emphasizes a balance between history, literature, theory, and creating for the stage. Prospective faculty

will have the opportunity to realize a visionary restructuring of the program. Students participate directly in collaborative theatre-making practices, with numerous opportunities for hands-on experiential learning in an environment that nurtures creativity. Students also understand how their learning and skills can apply to a wide variety of professions and industries. The program is also strengthened by its

proximity to the vibrant theatre communities in Richmond, Washington, D.C., and the mid-Atlantic region. Major/Essential Functions: Randolph-Macon College faculty teach, advise, conduct research and engage in scholarship, and serve the college and department. Primary teaching responsibilities will include teaching courses in the Theatre Program&rsquo;s curriculum to majors and non-majors, designing theatre

productions, and contributions to general education. Theatre faculty are expected to engage actively in recruiting prospective students to the program and the College. Minimum Qualifications: MFA in Theatre Design, Theatre Technology or other technical or design related concentration to be completed at time of appointment, September 1, 2027.
