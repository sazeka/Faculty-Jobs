Career Opportunities at Ohio Northern University Job Summary: The Department of Art & Communication in the Getty College of Arts & Sciences at Ohio Northern University welcomes applicants for a tenure-track, assistant professor position in Communication, with a specialization in Interpersonal and/or Organizational Communication. We seek a talented individual who is (1) passionate about teaching that bridges

theoretical foundations in Communication with real-world practice, (2) committed to interdisciplinary scholarship that involves undergraduates, and (3) invested in student success. If you desire to make a difference in the lives of students, we invite you to apply for a tenure-track faculty position to begin August 17, 2027. Review of applications will begin October 22, 2026 and continue until the position is filled.

A Ph.D. in Communication Studies or a related discipline is required. ABD applicants will be considered as long as all degree requirements are completed by September 1, 2027. The successful candidate will demonstrate evidence of teaching effectiveness, a promising research agenda, and the ability to contribute meaningfully to the Interpersonal/Organizational Communication (I/O) pathway of the department’s major in

Communication & Media. The I/O pathway is one of three within the Communication & Media major, alongside Media, Culture & Society and Digital Communication & Content Creation. Candidates with expertise in the following specializations of Interpersonal and/or Organizational communication are encouraged to apply: communication in the workplace, relational communication, sports communication, or

human-machine/AI-mediated communication. Job Description : The successful candidate will teach a variety of undergraduate courses such as Interpersonal Communication, Organizational Communication, Group Dynamics, Presentational Speaking, and courses in the candidate’s area of expertise, strategically developed in response to student interest and evolving professional needs. Ohio Northern faculty in the College of

Arts & Sciences teach 23-25 credit hours per year. Additional responsibilities include advising undergraduate students, scholarship, and service. In your statement of teaching philosophy, please discuss how you would or have supported a classroom of students with a broad cross-section of differing levels of academic preparation.
