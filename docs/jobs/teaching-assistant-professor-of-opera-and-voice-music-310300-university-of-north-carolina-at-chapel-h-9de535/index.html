The Department of Music at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill invites applications for a Teaching Assistant Professor of Music with expertise in Opera and Voice. We seek a pedagogically innovative and collaborative colleague who will direct UNC Opera and teach studio voice within the context of a broad liberal arts

curriculum. This position is particularly well suited to a singer-teacher and opera director who is passionate about working with singers in a performance program that is undergraduate-only and welcomes students with a wide range of musical backgrounds, interests, and levels of prior experience. UNC Opera typically performs a scenes program in the fall and a full production in the spring. This course emphasizes the

musical and dramatic aspects of role preparation and staging, rather than high-concept productions. Candidates with experience teaching across musical genres and styles, including classical, musical theatre, and contemporary vocal repertoire, are especially encouraged to apply. Primary Purpose of Organizational Unit The Department of Music at UNC-Chapel Hill offers a BMus and BA in Music for undergraduate students
