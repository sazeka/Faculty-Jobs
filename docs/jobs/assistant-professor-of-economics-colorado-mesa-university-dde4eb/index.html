ANNOUNCMENT OF POSITION VACANCY ABOUT THIS POSITION Summary: The Davis School of Business at Colorado Mesa University is seeking candidates for the rank of Assistant Professor of Economics with their primary focus in high-quality instruction and curricular innovation. The position is full-time, tenure-track starting January 2027 (preferred) or August 2027. Colorado Mesa University (CMU) is a state-funded institution

that emphasizes quality teaching and research, having embraced the teacher-scholar model. The standard teaching load is 12 credit hours per semester; courses are delivered on-campus, online, and in hybrid format, though our main focus is on in-person learning on a dynamic residential campus. Davis School of Business is AACSB accredited and the qualified candidate will be expected to maintain Scholarly Academic

status. In addition to teaching, scholarship within your discipline is expected along with advising students and service at the school and university level. There may also be potential opportunity to work on technical reports and economic updates. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES include the following. Other related duties may be assigned. Candidates may come from diverse backgrounds, with a range of

experiences, achievements, and leadership roles, either within or outside academia. Candidates should have significant industry, business, or corporate experience relevant to economics. As a member of the Davis School of Business, you will be expected to teach four classes per semester in a variety of undergraduate and graduate courses such as Principles of Micro and/or Macro Economics, Intermediate Microeconomic

Theory, Managerial Economics (graduate), and Labor Economics. Salary: $92,000 - $98,500 Commensurate with education and experience. Excellent health and retirement benefits package: Click here for more information!
