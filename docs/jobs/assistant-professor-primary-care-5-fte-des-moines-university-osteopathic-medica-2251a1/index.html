Job Search Subscribe to jobs Assistant Professor Primary Care (.5 FTE) Apply now Job no: 493018 Work type: PT Location: West Des Moines Categories: Faculty Des Moines University Medicine and Health Sciences is seeking a passionate and collaborative Academic Internal Medicine Faculty Member to join our Primary Care–Academic Department in a part-time role averaging 20 hours per week (0.5 FTE). This is an exciting

opportunity for a physician who is enthusiastic about medical education and committed to shaping the future of healthcare through innovation, mentorship, and student engagement. Reporting to the Chair of the Primary Care–Academic Department, this faculty member will play an integral role in delivering a dynamic and learner-centered educational experience. Working alongside a dedicated team of Primary Care faculty,

the successful candidate will contribute to classroom instruction, develop and facilitate laboratories and clinical skills experiences, and support a variety of educational initiatives that advance the mission and priorities of both the department and the University. If you are energized by teaching, value academic collaboration, and enjoy inspiring learners as they prepare for careers in medicine, we invite you to

bring your expertise and enthusiasm to DMU. What you’ll do: Deliver engaging medical education by planning, facilitating, and assessing a variety of learning experiences, including classroom lectures, small-group instruction, simulation activities, and clinical clerkship didactics across DMU's health sciences programs. Support curricular excellence and innovation through active participation in curriculum

development, continuous improvement initiatives, student advising and mentorship, admissions interviews, and fostering an inclusive learning environment that promotes student success. Contribute to the University and broader community by serving on committees, supporting student organizations, participating in outreach efforts, completing required training, and collaborating on special projects that advance the

mission of DMU and the profession. What we are looking for: D.O. degree from a COCA accredited college of osteopathic medicine or an M.D. degree from an LCME accredited college of medicine. Board-eligible/certified by the American Osteopathic Board of Internal Medicine or board eligible/certified by American Board of Internal Medicine.
