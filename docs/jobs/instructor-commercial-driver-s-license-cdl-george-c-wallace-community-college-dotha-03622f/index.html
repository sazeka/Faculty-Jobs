Summary The Instructor coordinates, schedules, and supports the operation of Wallace Community College’s Commercial Driver’s License (CDL) training programs. The position serves as the primary operational liaison among students, CDL instructors, Workforce Development personnel, Alabama Law Enforcement Agency (ALEA) testing personnel, the Training Provider Registry (TPR), employers, vendors, and other community

partners. The position is responsible for coordinating course schedules, student onboarding, testing, equipment, facilities, compliance documentation, recruitment activities, and day-to-day program operations. The position also performs CDL instructional duties, including classroom, range, and behind-the-wheel training, student evaluation, safety and compliance, and documentation.POSTING DATE: NOVEMBER 13,
