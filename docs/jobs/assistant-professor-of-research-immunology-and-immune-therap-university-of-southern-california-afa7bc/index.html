Assistant Professor of Research Immunology and Immune Therapeutics Los Angeles, CA Apply Current employees apply here Job ID REQ20178705 Category Faculty Helpful Information for Applicants External Applicants Internal Applicants (Current USC Employees) The Department of Immunology and Immune Therapeutics at the Keck School of Medicine of the University of Southern California in Los Angeles, California invites

applicants for a full-time non-tenure-track Assistant Professor of Research position in the field of immunometabolism. We seek an outstanding scientist with deep expertise in mass spectrometry-based metabolomics and to support and advance a highly collaborative immunometabolism research community.The successful candidate will provide scientific leadership in the application and development of state-of-the-art

metabolomic technologies to investigate cellular and tissue metabolism in the context of immune responses. This position will play a central role in supporting faculty studying immunometabolism across diverse biological systems, including sterile inflammation, cancer, infectious diseases, and other immune-mediated disorders.ResponsibilitiesThe successful candidate will:• Lead the development, implementation, and

optimization of mass spectrometry-based metabolomic and lipidomic workflows for cellular and tissue analyses.• Collaborate with investigators across the department to design, execute, and interpret metabolomic studies that address fundamental questions in immunometabolism.• Develop and apply advanced analytical approaches for metabolic flux analysis, including stable isotope tracer experiments and quantitative

interpretation of isotopic labeling data.• Integrate metabolomic and lipidomic datasets with complementary multiomic platforms to generate mechanistic insights into immune cell fate and function.• Provide expertise in experimental design, quality control, data analysis, and interpretation for collaborative research projects.• Participate in the development of new methodologies and technologies that enhance the

department's metabolomics capabilities.• Contribute to grant applications, collaborative publications, and the training of graduate students, postdoctoral fellows, and research staff in metabolomics methodologies.Required Qualifications• Ph.D., M.D., or equivalent doctoral degree in biochemistry, analytical chemistry, metabolism, immunology, systems biology, or a related discipline.• Demonstrated expertise in mass
