Position summary

Position Title: Director of Experiential Education, Assistant/Associate Professor Description: Larkin University (LU) College of Pharmacy (COP) is seeking a full-time Director of Experiential Education. The Director of Experiential Education works closely with the Assistant Dean for Practice and Professional Affairs and Dean in ensuring the integrity of the University and the College of Pharmacy (COP) are in line

with the founding principles, values, mission, and goals and department specific policies and procedures and works collaboratively with the Professional Affairs staff, other administrators, faculty and staff for the continued development of the Professional Affairs curriculum. The faculty will have responsibilities in different aspects of pharmacy academia. The Director reports directly to the Assistant Dean for

Practice and Professional Affairs.LUCOP’s accelerated Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) program is fully accredited by the Accreditation Council for Pharmacy Education (ACPE) and is an integrated three-year curriculum. LU is a private not-for-profit 501(c)(3) university and consists of the College of Pharmacy, College of Biomedical Sciences, School of Physician Assistant Studies, and School of Dental Medicine. More

information about LUCOP is available at: https://www.larkin.edu/academics/college-of-pharmacy/ Key responsibilities include, but are not limited to, the following:Recruit, establish, train and maintain licensed pharmacists and/or other practitioners (as applicable) who satisfy the criteria for preceptors to meet the needs and practice interests of students while developing additional geographic regions and preparing

for colligate growth.Creates, identifies, and leads activities related to community engagement and professional events.Serves as a liaison for community partners and in community-based initiatives.Implements a progressive plan that includes innovative clinical practice initiatives with partners and preceptors and strengthens community engagement with alumni preceptors.Assist in updating experiential policies and

procedures, manuals, assignments, newsletters, and syllabi for the introductory (IPPE) and advanced pharmacy practice experience (APPE) program.Assist in overseeing the experiential program and practice programs including capacity, student schedules, evaluations of students, preceptors, committees, newsletters, and sites in close collaboration with the Office of Professional Affairs staff and experiential management
