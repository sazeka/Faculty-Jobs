Assistant Professor of Scenic Design 510032 Amherst, Massachusetts, United States College of Humanities & Fine Arts Full Time Tenure Track Faculty Opening on: Oct 14 2026 Closing on: Feb 14 2027 Title: Assistant Professor of Scenic Design Executive Area: Academic Affairs College/School/MBU: Humanities & Fine Arts Department: Theater Work Location: Amherst Schedule: Full Time Work Arrangement: Onsite Job Summary The

University of Massachusetts Amherst Department of Theater seeks a full-time tenure-track Assistant Professor of Scenic Design, with the position slated to begin September 1, 2027. The successful candidate will be an artist and teacher whose primary specialty is scenic design, and as a multi-disciplinary artist, has experience and expertise in some other area of theater (for example, other design areas, directing,

puppetry, etc.). Reflecting the needs and advances of the industry, a candidate with experience in projection design or other technologies is desirable.The strong candidate will be committed to a student-centered, mentorship model of teaching and learning, with the ability to teach foundational courses, advanced topics (such as computer assisted drafting), and courses that can appeal to the general UMass student

body. They should also have significant industry connections that can be an important part of student learning and experiences.The Department seeks a colleague who will contribute to the research and creative mission of a flagship R1 university through an active program of creative and/or scholarly achievement. The successful candidate will demonstrate outstanding potential to establish a sustained record of

nationally recognized creative work, research, and professional engagement that will lead to a national and international reputation in scenic design and related fields. We seek an artist-scholar whose professional practice advances the discipline, contributes to contemporary conversations in theater and performance, and creates opportunities for collaboration, visibility, and impact beyond the university. The

Department depends on its faculty and staff being flexible, collaborative, communicative, and adventurous, and able to model these traits to the students. We are particularly interested in artists who have diverse expertise or experience in theater-making, adaptable to a range of budgets and spaces, as well as possible experience in other creative industries.
