Requisition Begin Date 07/20/2026 Position Qualification Position Summary Position Summary The Dean of Graduate Studies serves as the campus leader for graduate education at the University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC) and as UNMC’s representative within the University of Nebraska Graduate College. The Dean is administratively responsible to the Chancellor of UNMC and works in close partnership with the Dean of

the Graduate College, who provides leadership for the University-wide Graduate College. In this role, the Dean collaborates with graduate deans and academic leaders across the University of Nebraska system to advance graduate education as a shared, system-wide enterprise. The Dean is a member of the UNMC leadership team and works closely with the Chancellor, college deans, institute directors, and research leadership

to align graduate education with institutional priorities in research, education, and workforce development. The Dean provides senior leadership for the planning, development, coordination, assessment, and continuous improvement of graduate programs. The position works with faculty, program directors, and academic leadership to support the establishment, modification, review, and enhancement of graduate degree and

certificate programs, ensuring alignment with institutional priorities, accreditation requirements, and evolving workforce needs. As a member of the UNMC leadership team, the Dean oversees the Office of Graduate Studies and provides leadership for graduate student recruitment, admissions, enrollment, retention, degree completion, student support services, and professional development initiatives.
