Assistant Professor – Law and Society Program (Department of Political Science) FACULTY VACANCY ANNOUNCEMENT ABOUT THE COLLEGE John Jay College of Criminal Justice is a senior college of the City University of New York (CUNY) and an internationally recognized leader in educating for justice. Led by President Karol V. Mason, John Jay is a federally designated Hispanic-serving institution, it is ranked third in the

nation in Black student success, and it is a top ten institution for promoting student social mobility. John Jay is proud to serve a diverse and dynamic student body of 15,000 students that includes nearly fifty percent students who are first in their family to attend college as well as students who are immigrants, from low-income families, or from other historically underrepresented groups in higher education. The

College participates in the doctoral programs of the Graduate Center of CUNY, and offers bachelor’s and master’s degrees both in traditional criminal justice-related fields of study as well as in a robust portfolio of liberal arts and sciences programs that highlight themes of justice across the arts, sciences, humanities, and social sciences. The College seeks staff and faculty members who thrive in multicultural

academic environments and are committed to access and excellence in higher education. ABOUT JOHN JAY’S DEPARTMENT OF POLITICAL SCIENCE The Department of Political Science has 26 full-time faculty members with a strong commitment to research excellence and student success. The department offers a major and a minor in Political Science, as well as a major in Law and Society. We serve over 1000 students across the two

majors. Many of our faculty also teach in master’s programs offered through John Jay and the political science and other doctoral programs offered through CUNY’s Graduate Center. LAW AND SOCIETY PROGRAM AT JOHN JAY The Law and Society major at John Jay is a globally recognized leader in Law and Society scholarship and undergraduate education. With over 850 students and a vibrant and award-winning faculty, this

interdisciplinary major is housed within the Department of Political Science. The major curriculum integrates with many of the Liberal Arts programs across John Jay College. We see our students as carriers of important knowledge about the impact of sociolegal processes in their own communities and work to incorporate that knowledge in our curriculum and teaching.
