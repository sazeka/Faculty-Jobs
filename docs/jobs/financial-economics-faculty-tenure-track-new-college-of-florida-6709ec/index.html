We strongly

prefer broadly trained candidates who are interested in teaching finance in a multidisciplinary, social sciences and pluralistic setting. The successful candidate should be prepared to teach a range of finance-related courses, including Investment Analysis, Corporate Finance, Fixed Income Analysis, and Introduction to Accounting and Finance, as well as additional electives in their area of expertise. Depending on our

needs, we might also consider a second candidate who could teach Intermediate Microeconomics and Micro-econometrics, and potentially contribute to the financial economics program. The successful candidate will also oversee the Bloomberg Finance Lab, including management of the Bloomberg Terminal facility, integration of Bloomberg resources into the curriculum, and support for student training, research, and Bloomberg

Market Concepts (BMC) certification. We are open to applications from mid-career professionals from the financial industry who hold a suitable Ph.D. and are eager to transition to academia in a liberal arts college. The successful candidate is expected to maintain a program of research by publishing in reputable peer-reviewed journals and book chapters.
