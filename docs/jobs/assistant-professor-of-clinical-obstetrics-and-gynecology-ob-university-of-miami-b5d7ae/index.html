The Department of Obstetrics, Gynecology and Reproductive Sciences at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine is seeking an Assistant

Professor of Clinical to join the Division of Ob/Gyn Generalists. This faculty position includes clinical responsibilities within the Department of Obstetrics, Gynecology & Reproductive Sciences and will serve as an attending physician with a focus on the care and management of obstetric patients. Members of the Division work collaboratively with allied health professionals, specialists, and subspecialists to provide

a full spectrum of women's healthcare services. CORE JOB FUNCTIONS Active participation in the inpatient and outpatient clinical practice of Obstetrics and Gynecology at the University of Miami Kendall Medical Center, Desai Sethi Institute, Doral Commons, University of Miami Hospital and Jackson Memorial Hospital. Participation in the advancement and education of “OB/GYN” medical students, residents, and fellows.

Areas of specialty care include uterine fibroids, sexual health, pelvic pain, vulvar disorders, family planning, preconception counseling, cervical dysplasia, and anal dysplasia. Develops and carries out a comprehensive medical treatment plan addressing biological, psychological, and sociocultural domains. Performs physical assessment of patients in a logical and respectful manner. Prescribes medical treatment and

clinical drugs to patients. Consultation for hospitalized patients on L&D service. Consultation for nonservice unit patients by telephone. Inpatient L&D at Jackson Holtz Women’s & Children’s Hospital Required to cover 12 hours shifts on L&D, 6 shifts per month Gathers essential and accurate information through the review of pertinent records and interviews with the patient, family members, caregivers, and other

health professionals. Reports to the responsible physician and complies with policy co-sig. Clinical investigation includes a broad range of domains, including but not limited to patient oriented, translational, epidemiologic, behavioral, outcomes, implementation or health services research. Will need to use EPIC at UHealth Clinics & Cerner at Jackson Health for documentation and Mobile Charge for billing. Reports
