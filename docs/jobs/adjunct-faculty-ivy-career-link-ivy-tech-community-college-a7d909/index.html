Adjunct Faculty- Ivy+ Career Link

Ivy Tech Community College  ·  Anderson, IN
AdjunctNon-Tenure

Position summary

The Ivy+ Career Link team provides quality customized and open enrollment training opportunities for clients and community members in our campus service area who are seeking to learn more about specialized topics and skills. Qualified instructors are needed to develop and deliver various contract and open enrollment trainings. This position is specifically for courses that will be conducted at Amplify in Hancock

County. Classes may be offered in a continuing education classroom environment, live online, or at a client site and may be scheduled during morning, afternoon, or evening sessions on weekdays or weekends. Ivy+ Career Link instructors for this position should be prepared to create and deliver engaging course content. A variety of topics and subject areas will be offered to support both personal and professional

enrichment. Instructors are expected to provide high-quality teaching materials, including documentation that substantiates their expertise and course content. Ivy Tech Community College-Anderson is seeking non-credit course instructors with proficiency in a subject area as demonstrated through a completed higher education program, industry certifications, and/or equivalent professional or volunteer experience

reflected in a professional resume. Successful candidates will deliver courses at Amplify in Hancock County and help support workforce and community education initiatives in the region. Duties and Responsibilities Deliver non-credit, skills-based classes as assigned. Providing a learning environment that supports student success. Collaborate and cooperate with the Ivy+ Career Link Employer Consultant to confirm dates

and times, content, pricing, and planning for courses. Teach and train classes as assigned in workplace and campus locations within programming designed to meet specific company learning objectives. Teaching load is dictated by project, or projects, not credit hour production. Work a flexible schedule as dictated by the client company training demand. Maintain records on student attendance, grades, and other

information. Develop materials as necessary for the effective delivery of curriculum. Initiate and follow through on evaluation processes that determine the viability of each and every training program delivered. Assist in the identification of facility and equipment needs in the classroom. Participate in training and professional development as needed.
