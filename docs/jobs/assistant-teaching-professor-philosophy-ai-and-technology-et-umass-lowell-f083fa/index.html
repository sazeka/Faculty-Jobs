Applications Open: Nov 04 2026 Applications Close: Open until filled General Summary of Position: The Department of Philosophy at the University of Massachusetts Lowell invites applications for a full-time, promotion-eligible position at the rank of Assistant Teaching

Professor, beginning in the 2027–28 academic year. The position is part of a university-hiring initiative focused on artificial intelligence and emerging technologies. We seek a collaborative educator with expertise in the ethical, social, and philosophical dimensions of artificial intelligence and emerging technologies. This is a teaching and service-focused position with no research expectations. The successful

candidate will teach three undergraduate courses per semester, with areas of teaching competence in Engineering Ethics, AI Ethics, Philosophy of AI, and Introduction to Ethics, and will undertake significant service responsibilities within the Department and across the University. The successful candidate will contribute to interdisciplinary teaching and curriculum development at the intersection of ethics,

technology, and society. This work will build on the Department's existing strengths in social and political philosophy, philosophy of science, and American pragmatism, as well as the University's strengths in engineering, computing, and technological innovation. The successful candidate will also contribute to university-wide efforts to integrate AI into the curriculum, working with faculty and academic leaders,

including the University's Director of AI Learning & Career Innovation. The University of Massachusetts Lowell is an R1 research university offering a competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package. The Department of Philosophy is a collegial department committed to undergraduate teaching and to making philosophical inquiry accessible to students from a wide range of backgrounds and disciplines.
