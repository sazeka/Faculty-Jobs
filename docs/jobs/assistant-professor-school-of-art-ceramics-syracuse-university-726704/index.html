FLSA Status Exempt Hours Determined by the School Director or DeanOffice hours required Job Type Full-time Rank Assistant Unionized Position Code Not Applicable Job Description The School of Art, within the College of Visual and Performing Arts, at Syracuse University invites applications for a tenure-track assistant professor of ceramics to begin fall 2027.

Responsibilities include teaching undergraduate and graduate courses—from introductory through advanced levels, including wheel throwing, hand-building, sculpture, and slip casting—while guiding students in material knowledge, technical skill, and rigorous studio practice. Candidates should have in-depth knowledge of ceramic art’s processes, histories, and materials, grounded in tradition while engaging contemporary

We are especially interested in candidates working at the intersection of ceramics and digital practice—digital modeling, scanning, clay printing, computational/AI-assisted processes, and fabrication—built upon strong material fundamentals rather than replacing them.Syracuse University has one of the country’s longest-standing traditions in ceramics
