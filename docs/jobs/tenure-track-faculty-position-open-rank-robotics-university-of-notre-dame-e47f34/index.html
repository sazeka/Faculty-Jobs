University of Notre Dame: College of Engineering: Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering Location Notre Dame Main Campus Open Date Sep 03, 2026 Deadline Dec 01, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time Description The Department of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering at the University of Notre Dame invites applications for a tenure-track faculty position in robotics. This search is part of sustained growth in robotics at Notre

Dame. Recent investments include the Physical AI and Robotics (PAIR) initiative, a cross-departmental effort that connects Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering with Computer Science and Engineering and with Electrical Engineering. Robotics at Notre Dame is organized around a distinctive identity in human-centered robotics, built on established strengths in mechanics, modeling, control, and experimentally grounded

robotic systems. While applications are welcome from all areas of robotics, the Department is especially interested in candidates working in one or more of the following areas: ● Sensing, actuation, and robotic materials, including soft, multifunctional, and biohybrid systems ● Dexterous manipulation and physical interaction with complex or unstructured environments ● Wearable, assistive, and rehabilitation robotics,

with particular interest in upper limbs ● Robotic mobility for terrestrial, aerial, and aquatic systems operating autonomously in extreme environments ● Integrated design, modeling, control, and estimation for emerging robotic systems Across these areas, the Department is particularly interested in candidates who advance robot learning and data-driven reasoning in ways that are grounded in the physics, mechanics,

sensing, and control of real robotic systems and demonstrated through meaningful experimental deployment. The successful candidate will join an interconnected robotics community with active collaborations across engineering, computing, the health sciences, and industry, and with shared experimental facilities and testbeds that support work at the intersection of hardware, control, and learning. Additional information

about robotics at Notre Dame is available at robotics.nd.edu. Qualifications The Department seeks to attract, develop, and retain exceptional faculty members with strong records of achievement and significant future potential.
