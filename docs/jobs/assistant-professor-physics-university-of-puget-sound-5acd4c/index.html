Responsibilities:The Department of Physics at the University of Puget Sound, a predominantly undergraduate liberal arts

college in Tacoma, Washington, seeks to recruit a tenure-line Assistant Professor. We welcome applicants from any field of physics whose methods include computational physics, analysis of large data sets, or substantial computer-based analysis of data. This will be a joint appointment, but the faculty member's home department will be Physics. Most courses taught will be part of the physics major, and 1-2 courses each

year will also contribute to the Computer Science Department or the Data Analytics Program. The successful candidate will develop an active research program involving undergraduates and will have the ability to teach across the undergraduate physics curriculum as well as courses in either the Data Analytics program or Computer Science Department. A typical semester teaching load consists of two courses and two

laboratory sections. We expect the incumbent to show promise and enthusiasm to become a skilled teacher. Qualifications:PhD in physics, astronomy, or closely related field by the time of appointment and demonstrated commitments to undergraduate teaching, research mentorship, and inclusive pedagogy are required. Postdoctoral experience preferred. Application Deadline: Review of applications begins October 16th, 2026.
