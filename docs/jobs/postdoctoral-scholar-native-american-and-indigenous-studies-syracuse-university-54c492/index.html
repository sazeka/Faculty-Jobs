Job Type Full-time Rank Post Doc Unionized Position Code PD Job Description The Center for Global Indigenous Cultures and Environmental Justice (CGIC), located on the ancestral homelands of the Onondaga Nation and within the broader Haudenosaunee Confederacy, invites applications

for a one-year postdoctoral scholar appointment, offered in collaboration with the Native American and Indigenous Studies Program at Syracuse University. The postdoctoral scholar will join a cross-campus network of faculty and graduate students—Indigenous and ally alike—whose research and teaching engage topics of importance to Indigenous peoples and who are affiliated with CGIC while housed in departments across the

university.
