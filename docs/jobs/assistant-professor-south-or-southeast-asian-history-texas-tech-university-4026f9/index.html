Assistant Professor - South or Southeast Asian History 46074BR History Position Description The Department of History in the College of Arts & Sciences at Texas Tech University invites applications for a full-time, 9-month, tenure-track Assistant Professor in the history of Modern South or Southeast Asia to begin September 1, 2027. The area of specialization is open, but we will prioritize candidates working on the

post-1800 period who are well suited to our department’s needs. The successful candidate will be expected to publish peer-reviewed research and teach World History survey courses, as well as advanced undergraduate and graduate courses in their area of expertise.'
