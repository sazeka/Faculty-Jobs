ABOUT POSITION DESCRIPTIONWe are seeking a knowledgeable and motivated individual to serve as a part-time instructor and curriculum developer for our new Forklift Technician training program, based on the nationally recognized Manufacturing Skill Standards Council (MSSC) Certified Forklift Technician (CFT) standards. This role is responsible for helping to

develop, implement, and deliver classroom and hands-on training for students preparing to become certified forklift technicians. The instructor will work on an as-needed basis to support program development, instruction, and assessment.*Continuing Education Instructors are subject to an 18-hour work week. WHAT YOU WILL DO ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS and DUTIESCollaborate with program leadership and LTS Material Handling to

develop and refine the Forklift Technician curriculum in alignment with MSSC CFT standardsDeliver engaging, instructor-led online and hands-on training sessions covering forklift maintenance, service, and repairPrepare students for the Certified Forklift Associate (CFA) and Certified Forklift Technician (CFT) assessmentsEvaluate student performance through both online and hands-on assessments, ensuring mastery of the

20 key mechanical work functions outlined by MSSCAssist in the setup and maintenance of training equipment and materialsStay current with industry standards, safety regulations, and best practices in forklift technology and technician trainingParticipate in required MSSC instructor authorization training to become an Authorized CFT Instructor WHO YOU ARE COMPETENCIESKnowledge, Skills, and Abilities•Strong knowledge
