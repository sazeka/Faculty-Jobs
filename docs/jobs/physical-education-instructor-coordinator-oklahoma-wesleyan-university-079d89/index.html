Physical Education Instructor/Coordinator January 7, 2025 Apply Now Description: This position is responsible for the instruction and supervision of Physical Education students in the School of Education & Humanities. This position requires the integration of faith and learning with a biblical worldview. A successful candidate must demonstrate excellence in pedagogy and planning and have expertise in the supervision

of educational practitioners. A successful candidate must be able to work independently and collaboratively on individual and school goals. Evidence of commitment to the integration of Christian faith and living with the theory and practice of education is expected. A personal and vital Christian faith is required; a connection to The Wesleyan Church or one of the many denominations comprising the historic

Wesleyan-Armenian tradition is preferred. Supervision Given or Received: Work is performed under the general supervision of the Dean of the School of Education & Humanities and ultimately reports to the Provost. Position Summary: The School of Education & Humanities is currently seeking candidates for the position of Physical Education Instructor/Coordinator. This position would work to prepare students for health

and physical education teaching careers in a school setting. Essential Duties & Responsibilities: Protect and advance the mission and vision of OKWU; Teach courses at the undergraduate level in physical education; Coordinate, assign, and supervise student practicums and internships as necessary; Collaborate with university members to recruit, retain, and refine students in the field of Physical Education; Advise

undergraduate physical education students; Attend professional development opportunities and university community service opportunities; Represent the needs of the department by serving on committees; Maintain office hours to engage with students and advisees; Write curriculum and develop programs in Physical Education; Other duties assigned by the dean. Minimum Qualifications: Candidates must have an earned Master’s

degree in Health and Physical Education or other related degree from an accredited institution. Current Oklahoma Health and Physical Education certification is required. Preferred Qualifications: Candidates with an earned terminal degree in the above-listed fields will be given priority. Higher education experience is desired, but not required, for this position.
