Position description The Department of Chemical and Environmental Engineering at the University of California, Riverside

invites applications for a full-time Postdoctoral Scholar position in Dr. Michael Zachariah’s lab to conduct research on the development and implementation of in operando diagnostic tools to understand reaction dynamics at high temperatures. Most specifically the implementation of high heating rate mass spectrometry to obtain kinetic rates and mechanism for decomposition of polymers and composite solid-state fuel

systems. This is an open research area position, and the department welcomes applicants from all areas of chemical and environmental engineering who demonstrates strong potential for impactful research in the Zachariah Lab. Applicants must hold a PhD in Chemistry, Chemical or Mechanical Engineering or a closely related discipline. The candidate should have knowledge of mass spectrometry or a related spectroscopy with

an interest in modifying either the instrument or the software. Ideally the candidate will have a background in chemical kinetics measurement or combustion diagnostics. A record of publication indicating the candidate has experience in the relevant scientific disciplines consistent with the research being undertaking in the Zachariah lab. The initial appointment will be for two years, with the possibility of renewal

based on performance and availability of funding. Salary will range from $71,491 to $85,736 annually, commensurate with qualifications and experience, in accordance with University of California guidelines see: UCOP Compensation Salary Scales.
