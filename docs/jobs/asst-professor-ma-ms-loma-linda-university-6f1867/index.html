The Assistant Professor position

provides academic and whole-student support through coaching, mentoring, and remediation for individual students and small groups. The Assistant Professor collaborates with departmental and School of Nursing faculty to enhance student learning outcomes and

This position fosters an inclusive, supportive learning environment that promotes student retention, academic achievement, professional growth, and overall student success.
