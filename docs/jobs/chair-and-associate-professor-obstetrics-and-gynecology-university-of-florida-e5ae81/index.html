The Department The Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology is a dynamic and growing academic department committed to excellence in patient care, education, research, and community engagement. The department is staffed by University of Florida physicians and encompasses specialty divisions in Maternal-Fetal Medicine,

Female Pelvic Medicine and Reconstructive Surgery, Gynecologic Oncology, and General Obstetrics and Gynecology. The department provides comprehensive women’s health services across Northeast Florida through a growing network of clinical locations, including UF Health Jacksonville, the region’s premier academic health center and tertiary referral hospital; UF Health North, a full-service medical campus in North

Jacksonville offering inpatient and outpatient women’s health services; and multiple ambulatory practices throughout the region. These include the Emerson Women’s Health Center, the Elizabeth G. Means Center, Generation-to-Generation Women’s Care in Orange Park, and the expanding UF Health St. Johns (Durbin Park) campus, serving one of the fastest-growing communities in Northeast Florida.
