Cybersecurity Faculty Institutional Overview Capitol Technology University is a career-focused university located in Laurel, Maryland, between Baltimore and Washington, D.C. It is a fully-accredited non-profit private university that was started in 1927 by a Navy veteran to teach students how to build radios. The University offers unique commitments to on-campus students that include a salary guarantee and a Chargers

Assistance Program that helps students pay back their loans if they do not find full-time work within 90 days of graduation.
