Allied Health Faculty Print Allied Health Faculty Salary $40,000.00 - $45,000.00 Annually Location South Charleston, WV Job Type Faculty Job Number 430 Division Academic Affairs Opening Date 07/21/2026 Description This 9-month full-time faculty position supports the Allied Health program at BridgeValley Community and Technical College, delivering high-quality instruction in foundational and program-specific courses

designed to prepare students for entry-level healthcare roles. Course assignments may include medical terminology, pharmacology, community health, allied health skills, phlebotomy, EKG, and other courses depending on departmental needs. The faculty member will teach 15 credit hours (15 contact hours) per semester, advise students, participate in curriculum development, and contribute to program and institutional

initiatives. Salary offer will be commensurate with education and experience. Examples of Duties Prepare for and deliver instruction in both traditional classroom and online/distance learning formats.Supervise laboratory, simulation, and skills-based instructional activities in Allied Health learning environments.Provide instruction in phlebotomy techniques, safety procedures, specimen handling, and regulatory

standards.Maintain accurate and timely instructional records and documentation to support institutional effectiveness, accreditation reviews, and program approvals.Collaborate with clinical partners and assist with coordination or evaluation of clinical/field placements, including phlebotomy clinical experiences where applicable.Use inclusive teaching strategies that support diverse student populations and promote

equitable student success.Remain current with best practices, standards, and emerging trends in the Allied Health and broader healthcare fields.Maintain regular availability to students for academic advising, mentoring, tutoring, and course support.Participate in department, division, and faculty meetings; attend professional development workshops and in-service training.Contribute to curriculum design, course

review, and systematic assessment processes supporting BridgeValley’s continuous improvement model.Perform other duties as assigned within the scope of the position. Qualifications MinimumBachelor’s degree in a health-related or allied health field from an accredited institution.Current national certification as a Phlebotomist (e.g., ASCP, NHA, AMT) is required or willingness to become nationally certified.Experience
