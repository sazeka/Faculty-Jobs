Position Title: Assistant Teaching Professor of Chemistry Position Type: Full time Location: Robert L Rooke Science Center - Chemistry Categories: Faculty Exempt Job Summary: Job Duties: The Department of Chemistry at Bucknell University invites applications for a Professional Track faculty position to begin August 1, 2027. The successful candidate will teach courses in general chemistry and may contribute to other

subjects depending on background, interests, and the needs of the Department. The teaching load is six courses per year with typical class sizes of around 30 students. Candidates will also be expected to participate in academic advising and University service. The initial appointment is for three years, with the possibility of reappointment contingent upon satisfactory performance. Excellent teaching is the primary

criterion for reappointment and promotion. This track includes three successive ranks. Faculty members become eligible for promotion to the next rank after six years of service at their current rank. Prior equivalent service may be applied toward promotion. Although this position will not be eligible for tenure, Bucknell values professional track colleagues as full voting members of the faculty and provides extensive

resources to support their growth. This includes access to a robust Teaching & Learning Center and funding for professional development and scholarly engagement. Although there is no scholarship requirement for this position, opportunities for mentoring undergraduate research students are available. The Department of Chemistry is committed to delivering student-centered, active educational experiences for our

students within one of the nation’s foremost liberal arts universities. Our department places a strong emphasis on high-impact educational practices, including the use of active learning, student-centered experiences in novel chemical research, and hands-on access to a uniquely strong collection of chemical instruments. A strong Cell Biology/Biochemistry Program is jointly provided by faculty across the Chemistry and

Biology departments. We seek candidates who are committed to creating a climate that fosters the growth and development of a diverse student body. We encourage applications from members of groups that have been historically underrepresented in higher education.
