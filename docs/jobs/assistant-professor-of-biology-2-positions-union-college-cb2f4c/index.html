Assistant ProfessorTenure-TrackBiology

Position summary

Union College invites applications for two tenure-track positions: one in Microbiology and the other in Immunology, beginning September 1, 2027. Applicants must have a PhD in the respective field. Post-doctoral or comparable professional work is desirable. Review of applications will begin on October 1, 2026 and continue until the position is filled. Applications received by October 1, 2026 will receive priority

consideration. Position descriptions: We seek two biologists whose research addresses fundamental questions in, respectively, microbiology or immunology using cellular, molecular, and/or genetic approaches. We encourage all applicants with appropriate background and expertise to apply, particularly those candidates whose research investigates the cell/molecular mechanisms underlying microbial biology, immune

function, or host-associated microbiome biology using modern and/or innovative experimental approaches ( e.g. , single-cell technologies, functional genomics, gene-editing, systems-level analyses, and/or bioinformatics techniques). Befitting the teacher-scholar model, our goal is to hire two outstanding colleagues with demonstrated commitments to both teaching and developing a successful research program that

incorporates our students. Teaching responsibilities include an upper-level microbiology or immunology course with labs, contribution to the biology core curriculum at both the first and second-year level, and a course of their choice. About the Biology Department: The Department of Biological Sciences at Union College is committed to providing exceptional academic experiences and training for undergraduates seeking

to understand what creates, sustains, and changes ourselves, other organisms, communities, and the environment. We contribute to numerous interdisciplinary programs including Biochemistry, Biomedical Engineering, Environmental Science and Policy, Environmental Engineering, Public Health, Nanotechnology, and Neuroscience ( https://www.union.edu/interdisciplinary-studies ).
