Tenure-Track and Tenured Faculty Positions in Electrical and Computer Engineering, Fall 2027Apply now DescriptionThe Chandra Family Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Texas Austin has multiple faculty openings with a start date of Fall 2027 for tenure-track and tenured faculty positions. Candidates in all areas of electrical and computer engineering will be considered for

tenure-track positions only, unless explicitly stated otherwise, especially as they align with the strategic focus of the department in: (i) Core/foundational AI; (ii) computer systems (tenured faculty candidates will also be considered for this area); and (iii) robotics and physical AI. We are particularly interested in candidates who will contribute to supporting our student body through their teaching, research,

and service. Successful candidates are also expected to contribute to and benefit from the department’s mission and values: be the driving force for education enabling individual student success, scientific discovery, and technical innovation in the service of social good; and be bold, innovative, and collaborative in their aims and endeavors, while valuing, appreciating, and welcoming individuals of all backgrounds

and worldviews. The Chandra Family Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Texas at Austin is also committed to addressing the family needs of faculty, including dual-career couples, families with children, and single parents.QualificationsApplicants should have received or expect to receive a doctoral degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering, computer science, or a

related discipline prior to August 2027. Successful candidates are expected to mentor graduate students, teach our undergraduate and graduate students, develop an externally sponsored research program, collaborate with other faculty in a wide range of fields, and be involved in service to the university and profession. Application InstructionsApplications must be submitted electronically via UT Austiin Interfolio. We

will begin evaluation of applications immediately and will continue throughout the academic year until positions are filled. Submission of applications by October 15, 2026, is strongly encouraged. Applications received after November 15, 2026 may not be given equal priority.
