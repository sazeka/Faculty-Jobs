Position Summary: Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry invites applications for a full-time faculty position as Program Director/Course Director in the Department of Periodontics. We are seeking an enthusiastic, dedicated, and experienced Periodontist to lead and actively participate in both predoctoral and postdoctoral education, including the Advanced Dental Education Program in Periodontics. The successful

candidate will provide leadership in clinical and didactic education, curriculum development, accreditation compliance, and program administration. This role requires a strong commitment to academic excellence, patient-centered care, and the advancement of oral health equity. The candidate will also engage in scholarly activity, mentorship, and institutional service. Essential Functions (Principal Duties and Job

Responsibilities): Serve as Program Director for the Advanced Dental Education Program in Periodontics, ensuring compliance with CODA standards and institutional policies. Serve as a clinical and didactic faculty member in the Department of Periodontics for both predoctoral and postdoctoral programs. Supervise and instruct dental students and residents in periodontal diagnosis, treatment planning, and non-surgical

and surgical periodontal therapy. Provide instruction in dental implantology, laser dentistry, and dental sedation. Lead curriculum development, course management, and continuous program evaluation and improvement. Prepare and deliver lectures, laboratory sessions, and clinical demonstrations. Mentor students and residents in clinical competency, professionalism, and scholarly activity. Promote and participate in

research and scholarly activities aligned with institutional priorities. Participate in departmental, school, and institutional committees and strategic initiatives. Support outcomes assessment, accreditation processes, and quality improvement efforts. Foster an inclusive learning environment that supports diverse learners and promotes health equity. Perform other related duties as assigned. Knowledge, Skills and

Abilities Required: Demonstrated expertise in clinical periodontics, including advanced surgical techniques and implant placement. Strong teaching, communication, and organizational skills with experience in academic dentistry. Ability to lead an advanced education program, including administration, accreditation, and faculty coordination. Commitment to working effectively with diverse students, faculty, and patient
