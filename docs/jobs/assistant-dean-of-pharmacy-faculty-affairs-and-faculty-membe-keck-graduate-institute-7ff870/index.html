Position summary

Job Posting Title: Assistant Dean of Pharmacy Faculty Affairs and Faculty Member (Assistant/Associate/Full Professor), School of Pharmacy Job Description: Position Overview: The Assistant Dean of Pharmacy Faculty Affairs will lead efforts to advance the school’s tradition of innovation in teaching, learning, research, scholarship, and patient- and client-centered care. The Assistant Dean will provide academic and

administrative leadership for faculty in the PharmD program, including those in the foundational sciences; clinical sciences; and social, behavioral, and administrative sciences. Reporting directly to the Dean, the Assistant Dean will be responsible for faculty oversight, programmatic leadership, faculty assignments within the curriculum, and accreditation support. This position offers an outstanding opportunity for

a highly collaborative, forward-thinking, and enthusiastic leader to inspire a close-knit community of faculty, staff, and students and guide the PharmD program in its continued pursuit of excellence in education and the development of creative and effective leaders dedicated to ethical conduct, interprofessional collaboration, and service to others. Faculty rank will be commensurate with the candidate’s education,

training, and experience. Keck Graduate Institute (KGI) was founded in 1997 as the first higher education institution in the United States dedicated exclusively to education and research related to the applied life sciences. KGI offers innovative postgraduate degrees and certificates that integrate life and health sciences, business, pharmacy, engineering, and genetics, focusing on industry projects, hands-on

industry experiences, and team collaborations. KGI employs an entrepreneurial approach and industry connections that allow students to become leaders in healthcare and the applied life sciences. Located in beautiful Claremont, California, KGI is a member of the Claremont Colleges, a consortium of five undergraduate liberal arts colleges, two graduate institutions, and The Claremont Colleges Services, which provides

shared institutional support services. Each nationally recognized college has its own campus, students and faculty, and distinctive mission, and each offers top-notch curricula, small classes, distinguished professors, and personalized instruction. The Claremont Colleges consistently place at the top of national college rankings, including Forbes, Money and U.S. News and World Report, and the Princeton Review.
