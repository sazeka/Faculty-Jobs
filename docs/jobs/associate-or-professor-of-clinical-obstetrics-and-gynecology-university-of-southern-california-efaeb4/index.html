The Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology at the Keck

School of Medicine of the University of Southern California is seeking a Division Chief of Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery who is a fellowship trained, board certified academic urogynecologist at the level of associate or professor of Obstetrics and Gynecology. This position is ideally suited to a mid-career faculty member.We are seeking a highly motivated individual who has demonstrated clinical

excellence and whose primary responsibility will be the expansion of clinical services. This physician leader must also have a dedication for teaching and training future gynecologists and urogynecologists as part of our fellowship program.
