Minimum Qualifications Master’s degree in Physician Assistant Studies

or a related field. Minimum of three years of clinical practice experience as an Advanced Practice Provider. Current NCCPA certification (for PA applicants). Current Michigan state licensure or eligibility for licensure. Strong written and oral communication skills. Preferred Qualifications: Prior teaching experience in graduate medical education. Scholarly activity and/or research experience in academic or clinical

settings. Participation in state, regional, or national professional organizations that support the PA profession. Interest in contributing to the Physician Assistant Studies academic program in a leadership capacity. applicants for admission to the MPAS program; evaluate MPAS program outcomes; assist in developing, upholding, and refining program policies and procedures.
