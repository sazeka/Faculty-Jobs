Position summary

Job Description The Department of Sociology and Anthropology and the Health, Medicine, and Society Program invite applications for a joint, tenure-track faculty position as Assistant Professor of Sociology, with a specialization in medical sociology, and an anticipated start date of July 1, 2027.The individual hired will be expected to teach undergraduate courses in quantitative methods, introduction to sociology

(taught periodically as part of a rotational schedule), and upper-division sociology electives, as well as offering interdisciplinary courses in Lehigh’s Health, Medicine, and Society Program, including both the core course Medicine & Society and electives for advanced undergraduates. The individual hired must maintain an active program of research and participate in departmental, college, and university service. The

selected candidate is expected to carry a standard teaching load of 2/2 and to demonstrate a commitment to evidence-based, inclusive teaching practices.The Department of Sociology and Anthropology has 14 faculty members and offers undergraduate majors and minors in anthropology and sociology. Department faculty are active researchers and dedicated teachers who collaborate in a variety of interdisciplinary academic

programs. The Health, Medicine, and Society Program has 13 faculty members representing multiple disciplines. The program offers an undergraduate major and minor and is jointly administered by the College of Arts and Sciences and the College of Health.Founded in 1865, Lehigh University has combined outstanding academic and learning opportunities with leadership in fostering innovative research. Recognized among the

nation's highly ranked research universities, Lehigh offers a rigorous academic community for over 7,500 students. Lehigh University has some 5,800 undergraduates, 1,800 graduate students, and nearly 600 full-time faculty members. Lehigh University is located in Bethlehem, PA, a vibrant and historic area. Over 840,000 people live in the Lehigh Valley, which is in close proximity to New York City and

Philadelphia.QualificationsCandidates must have an earned Ph.D. in Sociology by the date of employment. Candidates must also have at least one scholarly work accepted for publication or have secured extra-institutional research funding.Application InstructionsReview of applications will begin on September 29th and will continue until the position is filled.
