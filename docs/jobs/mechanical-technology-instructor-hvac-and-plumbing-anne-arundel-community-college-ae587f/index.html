Work Schedule: Daytime hours/8am-4pm Position Type: Part-Time Faculty, Exempt Position Summary Part time faculty position located in the Clauson Center for Innovation and Skilled Trades on the Arnold campus. Instructor is responsible for classroom and lab instruction in HVAC and Plumbing trades. The instructor is expected follow the provided curriculum outline and learning expectations in

regular increments and timeline. Content for course is both lab based and theoretical. Instructional guidelines, learning materials, and content are hosted in on-line learning platforms. Training and support for new instructors is provided before and during instructional terms. Instructors must have expertise in the trade and the ability to effectively manage a group training/learning environment. Some basic computer

experience (email, web browsing, basic communication and organization) is also required. Prior teaching experience is preferred but not required. A desire to share your knowledge of the industry with future skilled trades workers is a must! Instructors will be required to adhere to Anne Arundel Community College’s Part Time Faculty Instructional policies and procedures. Job Duties and Responsibilities • Prepare

materials needed for course instruction, labs, testing, supplies;• Provide basic lab maintenance and track inventory of equipment and tools utilized;• Relate instruction and material to course objectives;• Familiar with NEC code book as appropriate;• Discuss and demonstrate workplace safety, personal safety and power/hand tool safety;• Communicate effectively and efficiently with students, other faculty and staff;•

Submit grades, attendance, reports and records in a timely manner Required Qualifications: • Journeyman/Master license or Journeyman level of knowledge in HVAC and Plumbing trade;• Safety Awareness and knowledge of NEC Code book as appropriate;• Thorough knowledge of the principles, terminology, tools and equipment used in the trade• High School degree or equivalent• 5 years of related experience Preferred
