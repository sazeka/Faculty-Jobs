Position summary

The Mechanical Engineering Program at Ave Maria University invites applications for a full-time, open rank, non-tenured faculty position to start in the 2027-2028 academic year. The position requires both research and teaching of Mechanical Engineering subject matters at the undergraduate level in the following areas: - Machine Design and Material Sciences - Thermal Sciences Preference will be given to candidates

with relevant industry and research experience having earned a doctorate degree in Mechanical Engineering or a closely related field. Applicants must be authorized to work in the United States for full consideration. The following documents must be submitted: - Cover Letter - Curriculum Vitae and List of Publications - Graduate and Undergraduate University Transcripts - Teaching Philosophy as it Relates to the

Apostolic Constitution Ex Corde Ecclesiae - Undergraduate Research Statement - Three Letters of Recommendation Review of applications will begin in the fall of 2026 and will continue until the position is filled. Rank and salary will be commensurate with the candidate&rsquo;s credentials.
