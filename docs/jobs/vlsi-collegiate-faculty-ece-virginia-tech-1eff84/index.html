VLSI Collegiate Faculty - ECE 538472 Blacksburg, Virginia Electrical and ComputerEngineering Teaching & Research Faculty College of Engineering Job Description The Bradley Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) at Virginia Tech seeks a collegiate faculty member to contribute to the department's undergraduate instructional mission through excellence in teaching, with an emphasis on Very Large Scale

Integration (VLSI) and related areas of electrical and computer engineering.The successful candidate will teach undergraduate electrical and computer engineering courses, including core and advanced VLSI topics, and contribute to curriculum development, student mentoring, and hands-on learning experiences. Candidates with expertise in related areas, including computer architecture, memory systems, semiconductor

technologies, and digital and analog/mixed-signal circuit design, are encouraged to apply.The faculty member will also engage in research and scholarly activities within their area of expertise and collaborate with ECE faculty, students, and industry partners to support the department's instructional and research missions. Required Qualifications •Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a closely

related field, completed prior to the appointment start date.•Previous teaching experience in electrical engineering, computer engineering, or a related discipline at the college or university level.•Experience collaborating with faculty, researchers, or other professionals in academic, research, or interdisciplinary settings.•Demonstrated experience or engagement in interdisciplinary research or instruction.

Preferred Qualifications •Experience teaching or demonstrated expertise in VLSI, computer architecture, memory systems, or hardware design.•Academic or industrial experience in semiconductor theory, digital and analog/mixed-signal circuit design, or device modeling.•Experience mentoring undergraduate or graduate students.•Experience developing or delivering online, distance, or technology-enhanced

instruction.•Experience collaborating with industry partners on research, educational initiatives, or student projects.•Experience supporting externally funded research or industry-sponsored student projects.•Experience communicating technical concepts through teaching, research presentations, scholarly publications, or other professional activities. Overtime Status Exempt: Not eligible for overtime Appointment Type
