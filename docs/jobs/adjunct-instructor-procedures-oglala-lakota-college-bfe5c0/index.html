Adjunct Instructor Procedures Open Positions and Application Procedures for Adjunct Instructors. Application Procedures For Adjunct Instructor Positions The Oglala Lakota College (OLC) accepts applications for Adjunct Instructors year-round. All applicants seeking to teach must submit their documentation at least four weeks before the start of the semester. It is the responsibility of the applicant to ensure that the

Adjunct Application is complete and OLC receives the official transcripts. Incomplete applications will not be considered for review and will be returned to the applicant after they have been processed as incomplete. Please send the 1) Completed Adjunct Instructor Application form 2) ALL official transcripts used to determine qualification to teach 3) additional certifications, licensure, etc. 4) Resume or CV

(although redundant, this will help reviewers determine if the applicant has extensive experience with scholarly work, publications, presentations, additional work experience) (Additional work experience in other areas may be considered for additional course approval) to: For further information, call, write, or email: Jonathan DerbyOglala Lakota College Office of the Vice President for InstructionP.O.
