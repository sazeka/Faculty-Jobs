Chair of Herr School of Accountancy, Associate or Full Professor 499426 Grand Forks, North Dakota, United States Full-time Faculty Salary/Position Classification $170,000+ commensurate with rank and experience, based on a 9-month contract, with additional compensation provided for department chair responsibilities. 40 hours per week This position will work onsite at UND Grand Forks, ND campus Purpose of Position The

Nistler College of Business and Public Administration seeks to hire an accomplished academic to serve as Department Chair in the Herr School of Accountancy. The ideal candidate will pair a strategic vision with a collegial approach to leadership and demonstrate a track record of excellence in teaching and research. The ability to work with and support faculty at different ranks and with varying balances of teaching

and research responsibilities is essential, as is a desire to be actively engaged in the Herr School's students, faculty, and staff. Reporting directly to the Dean of the Nistler College of Business & Public Administration, the Chair will work with students, faculty, and staff as well as institutional stakeholders to further develop and enhance the Herr School’s programs and reputation. The Chair is also responsible

for adapting and expanding the curriculum to be responsive to changes in the discipline and the field, including the evolution of technology and skill sets graduates need for employment. Other key responsibilities of the Chair include staffing and scheduling courses, evaluating faculty, representing the Herr School at the college’s leadership committee, and engaging with external stakeholders. A successful Chair will

possess excellent administrative leadership skills that will support the continued success and growth of the Herr School. Additionally, the Chair will contribute to the teaching and research mission of the Herr School. The Chair should be able to constructively engage colleagues, students, and strategic partners within and outside of the Herr School. A successful chair will possess excellent written, oral, and

interpersonal communication skills and be able to build relationships with students, coworkers, and various external groups. Of particular importance is the ability to maintain and foster relationships with Accounting firms and other student employers and with Herr School alumni.
