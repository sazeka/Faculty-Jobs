Adjunct Faculty: MBA Pro+ (Online) Faculty in Finance

Position summary

Overview Have you ever wondered why higher education feels stuck? Or imagined rebuilding it from the ground up? Imagine a university where advanced artificial intelligence enhances every student's learning experience through personalized, adaptive, and real-world education. San Francisco Bay University (SFBU), an innovative, nonprofit, WASC-accredited university located in the heart of Silicon Valley, seeks a

forward-thinking and experienced Online Adjunct Professor of Finance to facilitate graduate Finance courses within our MBA Pro+ program. Working alongside AI-enabled instructional technologies, the faculty member will guide graduate students through engaging asynchronous learning experiences in FIN 501 - Financial Management and FIN 568 - Corporate Finance, providing the mentorship, coaching, and academic oversight

that only expert faculty can deliver. The MBA Pro+ program prepares current and aspiring business leaders with the financial knowledge, analytical skills, and strategic decision-making capabilities needed to thrive in today's technology-driven global economy. Finance is a cornerstone of organizational success, enabling leaders to evaluate investments, optimize capital allocation, manage financial risk, and drive

sustainable growth. Students learn to analyze financial statements, assess investment opportunities, apply financial modeling techniques, evaluate business performance, develop strategic financial solutions, and leverage emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, to enhance financial analysis and decision-making. Through executive case studies, simulations, and applied consulting projects, students

develop the expertise necessary to make data-informed financial decisions and create long-term organizational value. What You Bring We are looking for educators who are actively experimenting with new forms of assessment and innovative teaching practices in an AI-enabled learning environment. Ideal candidates have designed, tested, and refined engaging, technology-enhanced learning experiences that reflect the

evolving needs of graduate business education. They understand the changing relationship between human expertise and artificial intelligence, are comfortable discussing both successful and unsuccessful instructional innovations, and are excited to contribute to a culture of continuous improvement within the MBA Pro+ program.
