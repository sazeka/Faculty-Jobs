Job Description DEPARTMENT/SCHOOL/COLLEGE : Department of English and Department of Global Cultural Studies/School of for the Humanities/College of Humanities and Social Sciences POSITION DESCRIPTION: The Department of English and the Department of Global Cultural Studies at Montclair State University, a Hispanic-Serving Institution just 14 miles outside of New York City, are

offering a joint tenure-track position in Latinx Studies and Creative Writing at the rank of Assistant Professor beginning August 2027. We invite PhD and MFA holders to send applications. We are especially interested in candidates capable of teaching creative writing workshops, courses in our Latin American and Latino Studies program, and English courses. The candidate’s area of expertise should be informed by

immigration from the Caribbean and/or Latin America, dealing with such concepts as bilingualism, borders, and social justice. The candidate must have the oral and written proficiency necessary to teach in both English and Spanish and demonstrate a robust record or exceptional promise in terms of publishing academic articles, poetry, fiction, or other genres in Spanish. The successful candidate may also be asked to

collaborate with other departments’ cross-listed courses and to lead interdisciplinary initiatives. Teaching : The standard annual teaching load for tenure-track faculty is 12 teaching credit hours (TCH) a semester, with additional TCH reassigned time (course release) available for faculty with additional responsibilities such as mentoring doctoral faculty or administering programs.
