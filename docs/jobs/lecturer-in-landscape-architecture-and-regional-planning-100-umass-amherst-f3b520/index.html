Landscape Architecture programs. Teaching responsibilities include a 3:3 teaching load. We are particularly interested in applicants capable of teaching a variety of undergraduate landscape architecture studios as well as any one of several possible mixed undergraduate/graduate seminars -- such as a professional practice seminar, graphics/digital technologies, landscape history & theory, construction/ materials, or

LARP faculty also mentor graduate and honors students on their capstone projects and serve as guest critics in other studios. All classes are taught in-person at our flagship campus in Amherst.The selected candidate will also serve as director and primary advisor for students in our Bachelor of Science in Landscape Architecture (BSLA)

Workload and course assignments will be adjusted to commensurate with service responsibilities associated with directing the

BSLA undergraduate program. Should those administrative responsibilities change or be reassigned, the teaching load will return to the standard 3:3.
