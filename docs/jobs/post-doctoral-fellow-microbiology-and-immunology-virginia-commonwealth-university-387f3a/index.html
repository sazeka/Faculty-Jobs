Position Summary: The Post-Doctoral Fellow will assist the Principal Investigator with

funded research projects investigating interactions among sensory neurons, immune cells, and epithelial cells in the skin. The goals of the research projects will be: To define how sensory neurons and their mediators modulate T cells in the context of psoriasis. To study how sensory neurons and immune cells promote protection against skin-invading parasites. To determine the actions of parasitic helminths on neurons

that evoke itch and pain. The Post-Doctoral Fellow will also assist with scientific manuscript preparation, protocol standardization, mouse colony management, and training of graduate students and laboratory technicians.
