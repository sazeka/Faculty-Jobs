Remote or hybrid work is not available for this position.JOB SUMMARYThe Center for Translational Research within the Research Institute Health and Wellness Center (RIHWC) at ACHE is seeking a Postdoctoral Research

Associate dedicated to the NIH R01: 1R01DK148868-01, Novel Mechanisms Promoting Energy Intake Compensation in Response to Aerobic and Resistance Exercise. The Postdoctoral Research Associate will work closely with PI, Kyle Flack, PhD, RD, Director of Translational Research, to provide subject recruitment, care, and data collection procedures in adherence with the study protocol and in accordance with good clinical

practice principles. Novel Mechanisms Promoting Energy Intake Compensation in Response to Aerobic and Resistance Exercise is a 5-year clinical trial including up to 120 participants randomized into two different supervised exercise interventions. Outcomes include changes in body composition (DXA), total energy intake and expenditure, hormonal mediators of hunger, cardiometabolic parameters, behavioral mechanisms

promoting energy intake, and functional outcomes such as muscle strength and fitness.
