Tenure-Track Faculty - Assistant Professor in Marketing

Northeastern Illinois University  ·  Marketing  ·  Chicago, IL
Assistant ProfessorTenure-TrackMarketing

Position summary

The College of Business and Technology at Northeastern Illinois University (NEIU) invites applications for one tenure-track faculty position at the Assistant Professor rank in Marketing. This position will start in Fall 2027, pending final funding approval. Minimum Requirements Applicants should have received a Ph.D./D.B.A degree in Marketing or a closely related field from an AACSB-accredited school by the starting

date of appointment. ABD’s will be considered for a one-year, non-tenure-track initial appointment. This appointment can be converted to tenure-track if the required degree is completed within the initial year. Qualifications The successful candidate should be able to teach undergraduate and graduate courses in Marketing Research, Marketing Analytics, International Marketing, Personal Selling, Sales Management and

other marketing courses in our programs; conduct quality research; and participate in service activities to the NEIU community. Demonstrated potential for excellence in teaching and research is required. Relevant business experience will also be helpful. Application Send an application letter, vitae, copy of transcripts, the names and contact information of at least three professional references, most recent student

evaluations, and sample research work electronically (via email: MGT-MKTG@Neiu.edu) to Dr. Charles Funk, Chair, Department of Management and Marketing, College of Business and Technology, Northeastern Illinois University, 5500 North St. Louis Avenue, Chicago, IL 60625. While unofficial transcripts are accepted, official transcripts for all earned degrees are required of candidates selected for final interviews. The

position also requires a background check. Review of applications will begin immediately and continue until the position is filled. AMA Conference Interviews Members of the search committee will be attending the American Marketing Association Summer Academic Conference, July 24-26, 2026 in Denver, Colorado. We will be conducting informational interviews on-site (in-person) during the conference. Interested candidates

should submit their application materials early to be considered for an on-site interview. Please indicate in your cover letter if you plan to attend the conference. Salary and Benefits The salary range for this position is $130,000 - $140,000. University employees may be eligible for a variety of State of Illinois benefits. These benefits are administered through the Illinois Department of Central Management Service
