Studies Primary Purpose of Organizational Unit The Department of Natural Sciences, through innovative, hands-on and technology-rich learning experiences, prepares students for competitive STEM career opportunities as well as graduate and professional programs. The department fosters a transformative learning experience by promoting active learning, scientific reasoning and critical thinking while maintaining a

rigorous academic environment that meets the students where they are, while helping them reach their full potential. Through undergraduate research, traditional graduate and an Accelerated BS-MS programmatic linkage, the department prepares students for and expands access to graduate level education. Description of Work This role plays a pivotal part in advancing our commitment to sustainability education and

fostering a culture of academic excellence. This role places a primary emphasis on teaching at the undergraduate level. The presence of the position on the faculty will provide increased intellectual depth and further enhance the quality of teaching and research already underway at ECSU by enhancing active and experiential learning. OR The Department of Natural Sciences at ECSU is actively seeking a dedicated

candidate for the position of Professor of Sustainability, focusing on Sustainable Agriculture, Sustainable Energy (hydro, wind, solar), and/or Environmental Science. Responsibilities include shaping the sustainability curriculum with a specific focus on agricultural practices, renewable energy technologies, and environmental science topics such as coastal erosion, conservation, and GIS studies.
