Assistant Professor of Film and Media Arts Apply now Job no: 50000561 Work type: Full time Location: Virginia Beach Categories: School of Communications & the Arts Regent University’s School of Communication and the Arts invites applications for a full-time, tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Film and Media Arts. This faculty member will teach across the film curriculum, with a special focus on

screenwriting and fiction film production. The successful candidate will hold an MFA in film production or a closely related filmmaking discipline by the start date and maintain an active and ongoing creative practice. That practice must show an established or emerging record of creative scholarship peer-reviewed through festivals and other juried or curated forums. Candidates must have strengths in fiction

filmmaking, including screenwriting, as well as a craft specialization in one or more of the following: sound design, producing, or post-production. We are especially interested in candidates conversant in one or more contemporary areas of practice: writing for episodic and streaming platforms and the contemporary writers’ room; independent and micro-budget feature development; and the craft implications of

generative AI and virtual production for how stories are written and made. We welcome strong artists working in any active area of the field. Regent University is one of the nation’s leading academic centers for Christian thought and action, offering over 150 associate, bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral degrees and enrolling more than 13,000 students worldwide. Regent offers high-quality, in-demand, and affordable

degree programs while remaining committed to its foundational mission to educate Christian leaders to change the world. The salary range for this position is $70,000 to $80,000 annually, posted in good faith based on the budgeted amount available for the position. Rank and salary within the range are commensurate with academic experience, a record of creative work adjudicated in juried, curated, or professionally

selective forums, and demonstrated capacity to teach and serve across the curriculum. Work Location: Virginia Beach, VA Expectations include 1) a dedication to Christ-centered teaching and learning; 2) an active and ongoing program of creative practice in the discipline; 3) the ability to teach graduate and undergraduate-level courses; 4) facility with online instructional technology and demonstrated success teaching
