POSTDOCTORAL FELLOWSHIPS IN JAPANESE STUDIES Applicant Deadline: Friday, December 18, 2026, 5:00PM ESTRecommender Deadline: Friday, January 8, 2027, 5:00PM ESTHarvard University will offer two types of postdoctoral fellowships in Japanese Studies: Reischauer Institute Postdoctoral FellowshipThe Edwin O. Reischauer Institute of Japanese Studies (RIJS) at Harvard University will offer Postdoctoral Fellowships in

Japanese Studies to recent Ph.D. graduates of exceptional promise, in any area of the humanities or social sciences, to provide the opportunity for postdoctoral fellows to turn their dissertations into publishable manuscripts and to continue their research in Japanese Studies. U.S.-Japan Program Postdoctoral FellowshipThe Program on U.S.-Japan Relations (USJP) at the Weatherhead Center for International Affairs will

offer Postdoctoral Fellowships to recent Ph.D. graduates in anthropology, business, economics, history, international relations, law, political science, psychology, public health, public policy, and sociology, among other fields. Scholars may examine domestic issues that bear on Japan’s external relations or problems that it shares with other countries, and the Program encourages projects that compare Japan’s

experience cross-nationally. Please submit only ONE application. Applications for the RIJS and USJP Postdoctoral Fellowships will be submitted through a single platform (ARIeS). Applicants will be asked to indicate whether they wish to be considered for the RIJS Fellowship, the USJP Fellowship, or both. —- APPOINTMENT TERM:Year-long appointments, commencing August or September 2027.FELLOWSHIP GRANT: Stipend of
