Position description The Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology invites applications for a faculty position in the Health Sciences Clinical Series (non‑tenure track) at the level of Assistant Professor or higher, commensurate with qualifications. This position is within the Division of General Obstetrics and Gynecology and is dedicated to providing exceptional clinical and

surgical care to our diverse patient population. The Division of General Obstetrics and Gynecology provides the full spectrum of Women’s Reproductive Health Care including low-risk obstetrics, ambulatory gynecology, and benign gynecologic surgery, as well as supervise residents and medical students in the only academic medical school in Orange County, California. Responsibilities include providing the full spectrum

of obstetrical and gynecologic care within the UCI faculty practice, including GYN inpatient care, along with dedicated time supervising resident continuity clinics at UCI’s Federally Qualified Health Centers. The role also involves supervising and teaching trainees at all levels, participating in scholarly activities, and maintaining a private patient practice. Additional duties include educating medical students,

residents, and fellows. We seek individuals who are enthusiastic about contributing to our fourfold mission to discover, teach, heal, and serve. Candidates must hold an MD or equivalent, possess a California medical license and Drug Enforcement Agency certificate by the start date, and be eligible for or certified by the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology.
