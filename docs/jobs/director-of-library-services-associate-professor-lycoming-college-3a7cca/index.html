Position summary

Lycoming College is hiring a Director of Library Services, Associate Professor to work within Snowden Library. Lycoming College, one of the nation’s oldest residential liberal arts and sciences colleges, invites applications for the position of Director of the Library. This full-time, twelve-month administrative executive and library faculty position provides strategic leadership for Snowden Library and advances

library services that support student success, teaching and learning, faculty and student scholarship, information literacy, and campus engagement.The Director of the Library reports to the Associate Provost for Academic Excellence and is responsible for leading library administration, operations, instruction, assessment, collections, technologies, facilities, budget planning, policy development, and the College

Archives.
