Explore Academic Positions at Harvard Postdoctoral Fellow in Chemistry and Chemical Biology (Mason Lab) Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Position Details Title Postdoctoral Fellow in Chemistry and Chemical Biology (Mason Lab) School Faculty of Arts and Sciences Department/Area Chemistry and Chemical Biology Position Description The Mason Lab invites applications for a Postdoctoral Fellow

position. The Mason group applies the tools of synthetic chemistry and nanoscience to design materials that address basic science challenges in energy and medicine, with a particular emphasis on the development of chemical strategies to manipulate entropic effects, phase transitions, and porosity. The Postdoctoral Fellow will be responsible for leading new research directions in the development of microporous

nanomaterials for biomedical applications.The initial appointment is for one year, with the possibility of renewal up to three years, contingent upon funding and satisfactory performance. Responsibilities:1. Conduct research in materials chemistry.2. Publish and present research findings in high-quality scientific journals and conferences.3. Collaborate with faculty, researchers, and students on interdisciplinary

research projects.4. Contribute to the preparation of research proposals.5. Participate in teaching and mentoring activities for undergraduate and graduate students. Basic Qualifications 1. Applicants must hold a Ph.D. in chemistry, materials science, chemical engineering, or a closely related field by the appointment start date.2. Demonstrated record of research achievement.3. Strong written and oral communication

skills.4. Ability to work independently and as part of a team. Additional Qualifications Special Instructions Interested candidates should submit: a cover letter a CV a brief research proposal, typically 0.5 – 2 pages in length the names and contact information of two to three professional references. A minimum of two recommendation letters are required for this position. All recommendation letters should be from

experts at the ladder faculty ranks. References will receive a system-generated request to upload a letter.
