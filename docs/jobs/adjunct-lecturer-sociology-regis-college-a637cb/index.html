Position summary

Position Summary Regis College is seeking a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP) or Adult-Gerontology Nurse Practitioner (AGNP) to join the Center for Health and Wellness. This is a part-time position working 8 hours each Friday during the academic year (August through May). Reporting to the Director of the Center for Health and Wellness, the Nurse Practitioner provides comprehensive, high-quality health

care to Regis College students. The NP practices with a high degree of autonomy and clinical judgment while collaborating with other members of the Center for Health and Wellness team and community health care providers. The ideal candidate will demonstrate strong clinical assessment and decision-making skills, excellent communication, and a commitment to providing accessible, compassionate, and evidence-based care

within a diverse college community. Essential Responsibilities Provide comprehensive primary and episodic health care services to students. Assess, diagnose, and treat acute and chronic health conditions within the scope of NP practice. Perform comprehensive and focused health assessments, including evaluation of physical, psychological, emotional, social, cultural, and environmental factors affecting health.

Develop, implement, and evaluate individualized plans of care using sound clinical judgment and current evidence-based practice. Prescribe medications and treatments in accordance with Massachusetts law, professional standards, and applicable clinical protocols. Provide preventive health services, health education, and counseling. Educate students regarding diagnoses, treatment plans, medications, preventive

measures, and appropriate use of the health care system. Promote student responsibility for illness prevention, health maintenance, and restoration of health. Provide continuity of care and appropriate follow-up for students receiving services. Maintain timely, accurate, complete, and confidential electronic medical records. Refer students to specialists, emergency services, or other health care providers when

clinically appropriate and ensure appropriate transfer of clinical information. Collaborate and consult with other members of the Center for Health and Wellness team and outside health care professionals as needed. Utilize appropriate resources within Regis College and the surrounding community to support student health and wellness. Develop and maintain collegial relationships with clinical staff and community
