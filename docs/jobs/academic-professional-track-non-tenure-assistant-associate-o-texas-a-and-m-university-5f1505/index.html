Back to Listings Academic Professional Track (Non-Tenure): Clinical Assistant Professor or Clinical Associate Professor Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Location Dallas, Texas Open Date 4/1/2026 Position ID 179558 Description The Texas A&M University College of Dentistry, Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (OMS), invites applications for a full-time Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon, 100% FTE, academic

professional track (non-tenure) faculty position, with a 11-month service period. The start date is July 1, 2026, or negotiable. Applicants will be considered for the faculty titles of Clinical Assistant Professor or Clinical Associate Professor, as commensurate on review of qualifications. Responsibilities include didactic/clinical teaching at the post-doctoral level, supervision of OMS residents, providing direct

patient care as part of the faculty practice, including on-call coverage, participation in research, and other collaborative activities. Candidate must possess excellent interpersonal and communication skills, display initiative, a positive attitude, flexibility, demonstrate a commitment to the Department's mission, and hold themselves to the highest standards of ethical and professional conduct. Qualifications

Required qualifications: Applicants must have a DDS/DMD degree or equivalent degree, Completed an ADA-accredited OMS residency program Board certified or board eligible Eligible for a Texas Dental License. Preferred qualifications: Focus in Head and Neck Oncology and/or Craniomaxillofacial Reconstructive Surgery. Application Instructions Interested applicants must apply through the Texas A&M University Faculty Job

Board, hosted by Interfolio at apply.interfolio.com/183029, and upload the following: 1) Curriculum Vitae 2) Cover Letter 3) List of three references with full contact information including email addresses 4) Personal Statement include philosophy and plans for research, teaching, and service, as applicable For questions related to this position please contact Rebecca Davis, via email, at rdav17@tamu.edu Equal

Opportunity/Veterans/Disability Employer. The University is aware that attracting and retaining exceptional faculty often depends on meeting the needs of two careers and therefore has a Dual Career Program.
