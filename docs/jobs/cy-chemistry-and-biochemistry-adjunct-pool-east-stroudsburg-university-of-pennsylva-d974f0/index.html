Apply Now Refer Job: Add to Saved Jobs Back East Stroudsburg University’s Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry is looking for Adjunct Instructors to teach laboratories in general and organic chemistry and non-majors courses. Located in the beautiful Pocono Mountains of Pennsylvania, ESU’s

proximity to New York City and Philadelphia provides convenient access to internships, careers and social activities. Just minutes from campus are the country’s largest water parks, scenic Delaware Water Gap Recreation Area, Appalachian Trail, and other opportunities for recreational fun. Responsibilities include teaching college chemistry and biochemistry general education courses and laboratories. ESU's Chemistry

and Biochemistry Department offers undergraduate programs of study that lead to Bachelor’s degrees in Chemistry, Biochemistry and Chemical Biotechnology. The program offers a comprehensive foundation of courses and an opportunity for students to select elective courses within specific areas of Chemistry and Biochemistry to prepare students to excel in careers in industry, professional medical areas, or graduate

programs.The ESU Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry programs are accredited by the American Chemical Society (ACS) and the American Society for Biochemistry and Molecular Biology (ASBMB). The department is housed in the Hoeffner Science and Technology center populating 13 dedicated teaching and research laboratories and a cold room, in addition to modern faculty offices. The department maintains an impressive

suite of instrumentation highlighted by a Bruker 400Mhz NMR, Agilent LC/Q-TOF Mass Spec, GC/MS, GC, Eclipse Fluorescence Spec and HPLC. A Perkin Elmer AAS, NexION ICP-MS, Thermo Nicolet FTIR and a Kodak gel imaging system complement a host of other instrumentation including several UV/Vis Specs and centrifuges.Details can be found at https://www.esu.edu/chemistry_biochemistry/index.cfmQualifications Successful

candidates will possess a minimum of a master’s degree in Chemistry, Biochemistry, or closely related area.
