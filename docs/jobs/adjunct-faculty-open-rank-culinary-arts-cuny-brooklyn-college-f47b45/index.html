Adjunct Faculty Open Rank - Culinary Arts FACULTY VACANCY ANNOUNCEMENT The Culinary Arts Program at Kingsborough Community College seeks adjunct faculty with a history of significant industry and teaching experience to join our team. We are looking for talented professionals who have a passion to teach and want to share their knowledge with students. Successful candidates will be committed to teaching in a

multicultural environment and welcome the opportunity to work with students with diverse learning needs. The Kingsborough Culinary Arts Program is recognized for its leadership in Culinary Arts study and workforce training in the New York Metropolitan area. Kingsborough Community College is located in Manhattan Beach, Brooklyn, adjacent to Sheepshead Bay with a diverse student population. Duties Include: Teach both

lab and lecture courses within the Culinary Arts curriculum, including, but not limited to introductory and advanced culinary arts, catering, pastry arts, food service management, food safety and sanitation, and dining room management. Create, develop and, present curricula and course materials including lectures and assignments. Administer and evaluate student work, including assignments, exams, and lab performance.

Participate in adjunct faculty meetings and trainings, as required. Day, evening, and weekend teaching may be required based upon the needs of the Program. Ideal candidates should have: Strong fundamental and technical culinary skills The ability to communicate professionally and effectively, both verbally and electronically, with colleagues, students, and the community Excellent organization, time management, and

interpersonal skills The ability to teach both lecture and lab courses Visa sponsorship is not available for this position. QUALIFICATIONS Bachelor’s degree in area (s) of expertise, and the ability to teach successfully. Applicants should have a minimum of five (5) years of post-degree employment in Culinary Arts. Higher education teaching experience, with a strong record of teaching is strongly preferred. Current

NYC Food Handler’s License and Serve-Safe Food Safety Certificate is required. COMPENSATION Adjunct Lecturer - $112.50 per hour ($116.44 starting 09/01/26) Adjunct Assistant Professor - $120.83 per hour ($125.06 starting 09/01/26) Adjunct Associate Professor - $129.17-$129.75 per hour ($133.69-$134.29 starting 09/01/26) Adjunct Professor - $137.50-$142.94 per hour ($142.31-$147.94 starting 09/01/26) Salary
