Position : Assistant or Associate Professor Faculty Position in Biology Faculty Unit : Faculty of Science Program : Biology Closing Date : Review of applications will begin November 1, 2026 Job Type : Full-time Job Category : Faculty Status : Open Mission BYU–Hawaii is part of the Church Educational System of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints BYU–Hawaii’s mission is to prepare disciples of Jesus Christ

who will live and lead in Oceania and the Asian Rim ( https://about.byuh.edu/mission.html ) BYU–Hawaii is an undergraduate teaching institution with a strong emphasis on excellence in teaching and student learning. ( https://clt.byuh.edu/framework-for-learning ). Its distinctive student body provides faculty an exceptional opportunity to teach and mentor students who will serve and lead throughout Oceania, the Asian

Rim, the United States, and other parts of the world Candidates for this position should be academically, professionally, and intellectually prepared to excel in their discipline and deeply committed to advancing the mission of BYU–Hawaii through their teaching, scholarship, mentoring, service, and example Responsibilities Teaching requirement is typically 30 semester credit hours per year over three semesters in

some (but not all) of the following subject areas/courses: Introductory Biology or Chemistry, Human Anatomy or Physiology, Ecology or Marine Biology, Statistics, Cell Biology. Scholarship: Faculty are expected to remain professionally and academically engaged through research or creative endeavors and on-going professional development. Citizenship: Faculty are expected to contribute actively to the university

community and their profession. Qualifications A doctorate in Biology or related field is required. Foreign equivalent degree from a nationally accredited institution accepted. Specializing in areas such as: Human Anatomy or Physiology, Ecology or Marine Biology, Cell Biology, Environmental Science. ABD near completion will also be considered (must complete degree by start date). As an educational institution

affiliated with The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (Church), BYU–Hawaii prefers to hire qualified members of the Church in good standing, as authorized under 41 C.F.R. § 60-1.5 (a)(6). To be eligible for employment, applicants who are members of the Church must hold and be worthy to hold a current temple recommend and receive a clearance from the Church’s Ecclesiastical Clearance Office.
