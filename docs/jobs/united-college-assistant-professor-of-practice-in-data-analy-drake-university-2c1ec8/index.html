United College: Assistant Professor of Practice in Data Analytics (Teaching in China) United College Faculty, non-tenure track Title: United College: Assistant Professor of Practice in Data Analytics Position Number: Q99997 Unit/Department: Drake United College Salary: Commensurate with experience and qualifications of the selected candidate Department Summary: QU – Drake United College, a unique joint degree program

between Drake University in Des Moines, Iowa, and Qingdao University in Qingdao, China, invites applications for multiple faculty positions to start Spring 2026 or Fall 2026. The QU-Drake United College is collaborative initiative between Drake University and Qingdao University that allows undergraduate students to earn both a Qingdao University Degree and a Drake University Degree through a jointly developed

curriculum taught in Qingdao. The dual degree programs, offered in Biology and Data Analytics, enable students to complete four years of full-time study in Qingdao University. Students take six credits of ESL/Public Speaking over two semesters. Qingdao University is located in Shandong, an eastern Chinese province on the Yellow Sea. Qingdao is a vibrant coastal city with a population of over 7 million, known for its

temperate climate, rich history, and popularity as a travel destination. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs. Qingdao has been ranked among the world’s top 500 universities in the Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU). Job Summary: Faculty will teach fall and spring semesters onsite in Qingdao, with a teaching load of four (4) sections per semester. All instruction will

be in English. Faculty will also spend time in Des Moines, Iowa for training and support before and after teaching abroad. Candidates should be able to teach a variety of courses in programming, spreadsheets, statistics, machine learning, data mining, generalized linear models, regression, time series, databases, cloud computing, or digital ethics. The positions are renewable for multiple years with the possibility

of conversion to tenure-track. Minimum Qualifications: A Ph.D., or near completion in Computer Science, Information Systems, Statistics, Data Science or a related area is required. Salary and Benefits: Salary is competitive with peer schools and will be commensurate with qualifications and experience of the selected candidate.
