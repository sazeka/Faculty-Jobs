is CSWE accredited and is currently developing a fully online option. The successful candidate will assist in the development and delivery of the online option and ensure CSWE standards are maintained across the curriculum. Candidates should demonstrate a student-centered professional focus, a strong commitment to social work education, and dedication to social work values and ethics. The area of specialization is

open. The position includes teaching undergraduate Social Work general practice courses in both face-to-face and online formats at the undergraduate level. Candidate appointed at the rank

of assistant professor is expected to maintain an active research agenda and demonstrate scholarly productivity within the field of social work.
