Position summary

The Medical College of Wisconsin’s Division of Geriatric & Palliative Medicine is seeking a Faculty Geriatrician to provide geriatric primary care & consultative memory care . This position will be located in Milwaukee and greater Milwaukee area . Our geriatrics clinical practice spans geriatrics specialty care, memory care, and geriatrics primary care in the ambulatory, inpatient, and post-acute/long-term care

setting. The focus will be on providing comprehensive, longitudinal care to older adults in 2 ambulatory settings: the MCW Geriatrics Memory Disorders Clinic (on-campus), and one of Froedtert & MCW's clinics in the greater Milwaukee area (off-campus). Position Highlights: Academic position with a rich & flexible mix of teaching, patient care, research/scholarly activity, and administrative engagement tailored to the

interests & career goals of the individual faculty Primarily a clinical role initially, with opportunities and a focus to expand academically with time Access to robust mentorship in memory care, including involvement with cutting-edge diagnostic & therapeutic approaches Evaluate & manage patients with memory disorders while helping advance the delivery of comprehensive, evidence-based care Exceptional opportunity

for faculty eager to grow as clinician-educators & investigators while contributing to the rapidly evolving field of dementia care Opportunity to build new geriatrics community practice Extensive opportunities for mentorship & professional exploration including the potential for additional clinical training in neurologic memory disorders with potential of dedicating portion of on-campus clinical time to the Neurology

clinic Outstanding nursing support in clinic Schedule: Full time days Eight 1/2 days of clinic per week with dedicated academic time Night, weekend, & holiday call for Froedtert Hospital and the ZVAMC in rotation with other faculty: 5-6 weekends per year and ~1 holiday per year Department/Division Highlights: Expanding division with highly collaborative & mission-driven faculty Faculty enjoy a diverse & rewarding
