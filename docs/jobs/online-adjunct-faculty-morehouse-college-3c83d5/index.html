Required Qualifications A Master’s degree in Management, Business Administration (MBA), Organizational Leadership,

Preferred Qualifications A Doctorate degree (Ph.D., DBA, or Ed.D.) in the teaching discipline or a related field. Prior experience facilitating online higher education courses using both synchronous and asynchronous delivery

Preferred Education/Experience Required Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities Deep understanding of pedagogical strategies and able to assess how students collaborate in a digital workspace. Commitment to the mission of Morehouse College, fostering leadership, critical thinking,

and ethical decision-making. Ability to actively engage students through collaborative online discussions and interactive coursework. Functional knowledge and comfortable operating within standard higher education Learning Management Systems (LMS)—specifically Canvas—along with proficiency in MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook), Banner, Starfish, and Zoom. Strong written, oral, and listening communication

skills, with a proven ability to convey complex academic and professional ideas clearly and effectively in a virtual environment.
