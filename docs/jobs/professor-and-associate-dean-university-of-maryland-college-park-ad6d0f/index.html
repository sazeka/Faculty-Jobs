Job Description Summary Organization Summary Statement Professor and Associate Dean for Academic Programs and Faculty Affairs College of Agriculture and Natural Resources University of Maryland, College Park The College of Agriculture and Natural Resources at the University of Maryland, College Park, invites applications and nominations for the position of Professor and Associate Dean for Academic Programs and

Faculty Affairs. The University of Maryland is a Land-Grant Institution, a Sea Grant Institution, a Carnegie Research Extensive University, and a flagship of the state University System of Maryland. The University has a strong commitment to excellence in teaching, research, and extension. Degree programs in the College are offered in Agricultural and Resources Economics, Animal and Avian Sciences, Environmental

Science and Technology, Environmental Science and Policy, Nutrition and Food Science, and Plant Science and Landscape Architecture, as well as a two-year certificate program in the Institute of Applied Agriculture. The college also offers M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Veterinary Medicine. Currently, the college enrollment includes 1100 undergraduates, approximately 300 graduate students seeking M.S. or Ph.D. degrees, and

120 students in the Institute of Applied Agriculture. The College has 292 faculty FTEs and 305 staff, on- and off-campus. University of Maryland Extension has 24 county/city offices located throughout the state and is jointly administered with the University of Maryland Eastern Shore, our 1890 Land-Grant partner. The Maryland Agricultural Experiment Station has 8 statewide research facilities. Additional information

about the College is available at its website: www.agnr.umd.edu. Through its associate deans, the College of Agriculture and Natural Resources fosters a close collaborative working relationship between instruction, research, and extension activities. Position Description The Associate Dean is responsible to the Dean of the College for meeting the mission and goals of the college’s undergraduate instructional

programs. In addition, the Associate Dean undertakes faculty affairs responsibilities, which involve coordinating policies and procedures related to college and departmental tenure and promotion, mentoring faculty, and resolving faculty issues.
