The successful candidate will support undergraduate aerospace programs through classroom and simulator

instruction, student mentoring, curriculum development, assessment, and departmental service. This position reports to the Professional Pilot Chair within the Aerospace Sciences Institute. SUMMARY Provide classroom and simulator instruction in support of Aerospace Sciences Institute academic programs. Responsible for course development and delivery, student assessment, academic advising, curriculum improvement,

student recruitment and retention activities, and participation in departmental, university, and industry initiatives. Ensure compliance with university and accreditation requirements, support departmental assessment activities, promote aviation safety, and contribute to the continued growth and success of the Aerospace Sciences Institute. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Teach undergraduate aerospace and

aviation courses in classroom and flight simulator environments as assigned. Courses may include professional flight operations, aircraft systems, aerodynamics, aviation safety, crew resource management, meteorology, and related disciplines. Develop syllabi, lesson plans, instructional materials, assignments, examinations, and other course resources consistent with departmental, university, and accreditation

requirements. Evaluate student performance and provide timely, accurate, and constructive feedback regarding academic progress and achievement. Maintain regular office hours and provide academic advising, mentoring, and career guidance to students pursuing aviation-related professions. Participate in curriculum review, revision, and continuous improvement activities to enhance program effectiveness and student

learning outcomes. Support departmental assessment initiatives and assist in collecting and maintaining documentation related to academic outcomes and accreditation requirements, including AABI compliance activities. Participate in student recruitment, retention, outreach, and promotional activities supporting the Aerospace Sciences Institute.
