Assistant Professor, Transplant Hepatology - Internal Medicine, McGovern Medical School

The University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston  ·  Internal Medicine, McGovern Medical School  ·  Texas, TX
Assistant ProfessorTransplant Hepatology - Internal Medicine

Position summary

JOB DESCRIPTION The Division of Gastroenterology, Hepatology, and Nutrition, McGovern Medical School at UTHealth Houston is recruiting an Assistant Professor, focusing on Transplant Hepatology. Position Key Accountabilities: Develop and evolve the growth of our liver disease clinical program which in turn will expand the liver transplant pool and support clinical investigative programs within the Ertan Digestive

Disease Center at Memorial Hermann-Texas Medical Center and UTHealth Houston. Develop relationships and clinical activities in partner facilities within the Memorial Hermann system with a concentration on providing Hepatology expertise and transplant evaluations in areas where we currently do not have a clinical presence.Develop between two to three new transplant hepatology clinics at target locations in the Texas

Medical Center and the Houston area. Qualifications: M.D. degreeBoard-certified or board-eligible, or possess equivalent qualifications through an approved pathway toward U.S. board certificationEligible for licensure in the State of TexasCompletion of an ACGME-accredited fellowship program, or equivalent advanced GI/hepatology training from a recognized international program Qualified applicants must demonstrate a

commitment to excellence, discovery, and service, have the potential to be outstanding in their field of expertise, and share an interest in fulfilling the common goals of McGovern Medical School at UTHealth Houston.
