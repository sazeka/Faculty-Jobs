Ideal candidates for this position will have both professional experience in the commercial real estate field and teaching experience at the university level.

Preference for this position will be given to candidates who can demonstrate teaching/professional experience in courses such as Real Estate Principles, Real Estate Finance and Investment, Real Estate Development, and Real Estate Asset Management. community; and participate in innovative curricular development activities. The position is a two-year, ten-month, non-tenure track appointment, with eligibility for reappointment based on established NTTP procedures.
