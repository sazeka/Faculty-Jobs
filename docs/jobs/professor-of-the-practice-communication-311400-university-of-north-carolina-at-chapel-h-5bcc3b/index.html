The Department of Communication at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill seeks to hire a Professor of the Practice (teaching track) to join our expanding

Screenwriting Minor. We are seeking a dynamic and engaged teacher who is equally confident in teaching introductory and specialized upper-level courses. A master’s degree in screenwriting or another discipline, or the equivalent in professional

experience, is required. The successful candidate will teach a 3/3 course load, including at least four sections of Introduction to Writing for Film and Television, as well as intermediate and advanced screenwriting electives.
