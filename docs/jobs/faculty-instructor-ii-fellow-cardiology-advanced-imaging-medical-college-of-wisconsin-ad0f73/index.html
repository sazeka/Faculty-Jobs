Advanced Cardiac Imaging Fellowship – Faculty Instructor – Froedtert & MCW Position Summary: The Medical College of Wisconsin Division of Cardiovascular Medicine is seeking a physician that has completed an ACGME-accredited fellowship in cardiovascular disease, and that is board certified or board eligible in cardiovascular disease for an advanced cardiac imaging fellowship as a faculty instructor. This one-year

program will focus on advanced echocardiographic experience (including intra-procedural TEE for structural heart cases such as left atrial appendage occluders, mitral and tricuspid transcatheter edge-to-edge repair, transcatheter tricuspid and mitral valve replacement, PFO/ASD/VSD closure, etc.) and cardiac MRI imaging with options for increased nuclear and computerized tomography exposure. Position Highlights:

One-year program with research opportunities included Teaching faculty includes both cardiologists and radiologists Upon completion, the fellow will be considered Level III in both echocardiography and cardiac MRI as delineated in the ACC/AHA COCATS 4 document (with optional additional focus in CT or nuclear) Active role in cardiology fellow and resident training Participate in didactic teaching sessions with the

cardiology fellowship to provide a comprehensive review of multimodality cardiac imaging Opportunity to complete coursework for Master’s degree in Clinical and Translational sciences Department/Division Highlights: Nationally Recognized, High‑Performing Heart & Vascular Care Program State-of-the-Art Equipment & New Facilities Comprehensive Cardiac Program including Full Complement of Complex & Structural

Interventions, Advanced Heart Failure with vibrant Heart Transplant, MCS, ECMO and Shock programs, and complex state-of-the-art EP program Superior Program Leadership Academic Practice of over 60 Cardiologists Cutting‑Edge Technology, Innovation, & Academic Medical setting Cardiovascular Medicine | Medical College of Wisconsin Minimum Qualifications: ACGME-accredited cardiovascular disease (general cardiology)
