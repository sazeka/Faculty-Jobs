Aviation - Assistant Professor (Full Time Faculty) Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Details Position Information Position Title Aviation - Assistant Professor (Full Time Faculty) Position Summary The Department of Aviation Science at California Baptist University invites applicants capable of teaching courses in Aviation Flight and/or Aviation Management. Pay Range Qualifications Those

desiring to teach courses in Aviation Flight must possess FAA Advanced Ground Instructor (AGI) Certificate or Certified Flight Instructor. Qualified applicants for either major must have a master’s degree in Aviation/Aeronautics or related field and some degree of aviation industry experience. Successful candidates should have a desire to work in an entrepreneurial setting within a growing aviation program. It is

expected that all faculty members are professing Christians who are actively involved in a church. Teaching Responsibilities Teaching responsibilities in the undergraduate program of Aviation. Specific course assignments will be dependent upon qualifications and availability. Nondiscrimination Statement State and Federal law permit California Baptist University to discriminate on the basis of religion in order to

fulfill its purpose. The University does not discriminate contrary to either State or Federal law.
