This position serves as the full time Massage Therapy Instructor for the program and coordinates the daily operation of the student massage therapy clinic. The Instructor does not supervise personnel or perform departmental management functions but serves as the primary faculty resource for students and adjunct

instructors regarding instructional matters and clinic operations.The Instructor teaches didactic, laboratory, and clinical courses; maintains office hours for tutoring, advising, and attendance management; supports student academic success; and ensures the student clinic operates in accordance with North Carolina Board of Massage and Bodywork Therapy (NCBMBT) requirements, North Carolina Community College System

(NCCCS) standards, and Alamance Community College policies.This is a full-time, 12-month instructional staff position. The Massage Therapy Instructor is expected to deliver high-quality instruction while maintaining instructional materials, supporting students and other faculty, and coordinating the Student Clinic.
