Assistant or Associate Professor - Critical Minerals and Materials Circularity for Reliable Energy Technologies and AI Infrastructure 496817 Full-Time Civil Engineering Department: Civil Engineering Salary: Commensurate with Experience Description: The Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering (CEE) in the Cullen College of Engineering at the University of Houston invites applications for a full-time,

tenure-track faculty position in the broad area of Critical Minerals and Sustainable Materials for Reliable Energy Technologies and AI Infrastructure. This search is part of the University's Presidential Frontier Faculty initiative, which is a university-wide interdisciplinary hiring campaign aimed at enhancing the UH research enterprise by integrating the strengths of existing faculty and infrastructure with strong

early career new faculty hires. All qualified candidates are invited to apply at the Assistant or Associate Professor rank, with particular encouragement for candidates with multidisciplinary research interests. Areas of interest include, but are not limited to, critical mineral recovery and resource extraction from primary, secondary, and nontraditional sources; separation, processing, and refining; recycling,

reuse, and materials circularity; sustainable and functional materials chemistry and discovery; environmental and systems-level assessment, including LCA, TEA, and supply-chain risk analysis; AI/ML-enabled materials and process discovery and optimization; and critical materials for reliable energy technologies, semiconductors, advanced computing, and AI infrastructure.QualificationsThe applicant must have an earned

Ph.D. in civil and environmental engineering, chemical engineering, materials science and engineering, mechanical engineering, chemistry, or a closely related field. Candidates should demonstrate a strong record, or clear potential, for developing an externally funded research program in critical minerals, sustainable materials, or closely related areas that align with emerging national priorities in energy, advanced

manufacturing, AI infrastructure, and resilient domestic supply chains.
