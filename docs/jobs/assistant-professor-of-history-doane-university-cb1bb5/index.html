Position Details Position Information Position Title Assistant Professor of History Unit History Job Location Crete, NE (68333) Description of Work Doane University, a nationally recognized

liberal arts and sciences university in Crete, Nebraska, invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor of History starting in the 2027-2028 academic year. This position begins August 16, 2027. Responsibilities include teaching 12 credit hours of on-ground undergraduate courses each semester. The position may also teach select courses online, subject to the needs of the institution. The department is

seeking a generalist with the ability to teach World and European history courses. The area of specialization is open. This position will be responsible for introductory courses in world history and advanced courses with a focus on topics such as the history of the ancient world, military history, and country-specific, regional, or broader world history. Candidates may develop courses in their area of expertise as

well. The opportunity to teach the program’s gateway and capstone courses, Introduction to Historical Methods and the Writing and Research Seminar, is also possible. During the Fall semester, the position has the opportunity to develop a course in Doane’s LAR 101 First Year Seminar program, with the topic to be chosen by the faculty member.Doane University actively works to create and sustain a welcoming learning

community for all members to fulfill their potential, feel valued, and make meaningful contributions to the University. Doane University is an equal-opportunity employer and seeks candidates who reflect the diversity of our society. Applicants must be currently authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis. Doane University does not provide employment visa sponsorship for this position. Minimum
