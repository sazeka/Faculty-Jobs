Attending Clinical Open Rank Faculty, Pediatric Dentistry

High Point University  ·  Pediatric Dentistry  ·  High Point, NC
FacultyNon-TenurePediatric Dentistry

Position summary

Are you a compassionate, mission-driven pediatric dentist ready to transform lives through education and care? Join High Point University’s Workman School of Dental Medicine, home to one of the nation’s most advanced new dental schools and a network of cutting-edge clinical hubs throughout the Triad region. We are disrupting traditional dental education with an integrated, real-world private practice model grounded

in innovation, collaboration, and purpose. This is more than a job; it is a unique opportunity to shape the future of pediatric dental education and deliver care in an area of strong economic growth with unmet needs for specialized dental care. You will also have the opportunity to help build and potentially lead our future Pediatric Dentistry Residency Program. POSITION SUMMARY: HPU Health- Oral Health Network is a

dental network that provides primary and specialty oral health care services, part of the High Point University Workman School of Dental Medicine (WSDM). This is a full-time, 12-month, non-tenure track faculty appointment with duties in the High Point University Workman School of Dental (WSDM), which includes the Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) and post-doctoral programs such as Advanced Education in General

Dentistry, Digital Dentistry Fellowship, and Oral Oncology Fellowship. Professional rank will be commensurate with the candidate’s level of experience and qualifications at the time of hiring. We are seeking a full-time Pediatric Dentist to serve in clinical and academic roles, educating future dentists while shaping pediatric protocols across our facilities. QUALIFICATIONS: Education DDS or DMD degree (or foreign

equivalent), active full dental licensure in the state of North Carolina (or eligible for North Carolina dental licensure) Completion of a CODA-accredited pediatric dentistry program in the US. Licensures/ Certifications Board-certified or board-eligible in Pediatric Dentistry required Experience and Training 5 years of experience in clinical practice of dentistry is preferred. Leadership experience and program

building is preferrable. Proficiency in providing pediatric dental care in an ambulatory, inpatient, operating room, and urgent care setting. Demonstrated passion for treatment and clinical supervision. Strong interest in residency program development is highly encouraged. Knowledge, Skills, Ability: Robust verbal and written communication skills Exceptional receptiveness and approachability Poised and professional
