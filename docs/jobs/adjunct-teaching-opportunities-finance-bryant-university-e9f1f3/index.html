Details Position Information Job Title Adjunct Teaching Opportunities - Finance Full Time / Part Time Part Time Job Type Adjunct Reports To Dean, College of Business Position Summary Bryant University is seeking an Adjunct Professor of Finance for the Spring 2027 semester. The successful candidate will have a strong commitment to excellence in teaching and to bringing their experience to the classroom. We have a

particular need for an individual who could teach one or two of the following undergraduate courses: Financial Management, Financial Institutions and Markets, Intermediate Corporate Finance, Financial Statement Analysis, or Real Estate Finance. Descriptions of these courses can be accessed via the Course Catalogue on the Bryant website. Bryant is recognized as a top 1 percent national leader in student education

outcomes and ROI and regularly receives high rankings from U.S. News and World Report, QS Stars, Money, Bloomberg Businessweek, Wall Street Journal, and College Factual. Bryant’s strategic plan, Vision 2030, redefines higher education through a commitment to providing students with a transformational learning experience. Vision 2030 accelerates advancements in academic innovation, experiential learning, vibrant

student life, Division I athletics, and a connected community that fosters belonging and success. Principal Accountabilities This position includes teaching at the undergraduate level as well as contributing to student growth and development through active involvement in student related co-curricular activities. Qualifications Candidates must have at least a Master’s Degree in Finance or a related field from an

AACSB-International or EQUIS accredited institution. Relevant industry or regulatory experience in finance or prior teaching experience at a business school is required. Working Environment Bryant University provides an inclusive and welcoming educational and working environment for all members of its community.
