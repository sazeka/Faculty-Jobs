Position summary

Job Title: Assistant Professor of Music Department: Music Job Description: The Rhodes College Department of Music invites applications for an Assistant Professor of Music (choral) to begin August 2027. Rhodes hosts a thriving music community at this small liberal arts college with big-city opportunities. It is the mission of the Department of Music to create a welcoming and collaborative undergraduate community that

combines the best of the classroom, the practice room, and the real world through performing, internships, service, research, and other opportunities in Memphis and beyond. Our students participate in all aspects of the music program while pursuing any major at Rhodes. The successful candidate will be a dynamic choral director who is an expert in and advocate for broad and diverse choral works, including but not

limited to non-Western music, the music of traditionally marginalized groups, and/or World music. This faculty member will be expected to bring this expertise to Rhodes College’s flagship concert choir, Rhodes Singers, and continue to enrich the College’s tradition of excellence in the fine arts by teaching conducting as well as courses in an area of specialization (e.g., history, theory, electronic music production,

record engineering, composition, computer music) to complete a 3/2 teaching load each academic year. Special consideration would be given to candidates whose courses might also contribute to Rhodes College’s interdisciplinary programs (e.g., Africana Studies, Asian and Asian American Studies, Environmental Studies and Sciences, Gender and Sexuality Studies, Jewish/Islamic/Middle Eastern Studies, Search

[Interdisciplinary Humanities], Urban Studies). The successful candidate will have evidence of a developing trajectory for research and/or creative activity. Rhodes College is an accredited institutional member of the National Association of Schools of Music (NASM). Details: Start date: August 9, 2027 Requirements: 1. Doctorate in Music 2. Evidence of experience and success in directing choirs and in higher education

teaching 3. Evidence of teaching conducting 4. Ability to teach courses in other (interdisciplinary) areas 5. Excellent written and oral communication skills Preferred Qualifications (not required but to be considered) 1. College-level teaching experience 2. Relevant industry and practical experience Rhodes is a nationally ranked residential college committed to the liberal arts and sciences. Our highest priorities
