Byrd Institute - RC1511 Job Description The Marshall Advanced Manufacturing Center is accepting resumes for an

Industrial Maintenance Instructor/Trainer that will be based at MAMC Huntington.Duties: Designing and providing customized training to meet industry demand; assisting with instruction of Advanced Manufacturing Program including skill sets in Industrial Maintenance, Process Controls, Electronics and Electrical options. Responsible for teaching the installation, inspection, and maintenance of equipment in a typical

manufacturing environment along with basic machine processing concepts. Lead in maintaining a safe and clean facility, assuring all safety procedures are followed. Travel will be required, and the candidate may split time at classroom locations within the service district. Additional Responsibilities: Duties also include student pre-testing, interviews, and selection; developing lesson plans; maintaining discipline

in the classroom and lab; instructing students on a day-to-day basis; evaluating student performance; and maintaining attendance and test records. Performing preventive maintenance on shop equipment; maintaining inventory of supplies; working with the program Industry Advisory Council to maintain industry standards. Include day-to-day interaction with all partner sites, pertinent day-to-day interaction with

management, intense day-to-day interaction with local and regional customers, ensure partner and industry satisfaction, lead in developing and implementing appropriate procedures and processes to ensure the latest technologies are available. Location OTHR - Other Off-Campus Location Salary Range Salary is commensurate upon education, experience and internal equity Required Qualifications Education An Associate degree

in a related technical field is required. Experience Must have a minimum of five years’ work experience manufacturing equipment including hydraulics, pneumatics, motors/controls, control panel, and other automation/processing equipment.Must be proficient in diagnosis and troubleshooting of equipment and the ability to maintain equipment.
