Assistant Professor - Institute for Health & Humanity

Medical College of Wisconsin  ·  Institute for Health & Humanity  ·  Milwaukee, WI
Assistant ProfessorHealth Policy, Economics & Data Analytics

Position summary

Position Summary: The Institute for Health & Humanity's Division of Health Policy, Economics & Data Analytics at the Medical College of Wisconsin invites applications for an endowed faculty position at the Assistant Professor level. This endowed faculty position supports the development of an innovative, externally funded research program in health economics and health policy. The successful candidate will contribute

to a growing portfolio of research addressing the economics of healthcare delivery, financing, and policy, while dedicating a significant portion of their research effort to breast cancer prevention, diagnosis, treatment, survivorship, and outcomes. The position offers exceptional opportunities to develop both disease-specific and broader health economics research through collaborations with nationally recognized

investigators across MCW and its extensive clinical, academic, and community partnerships. Position Highlights: The successful candidate will: Develop and sustain an innovative research program in health economics with a special focus on breast cancer care. Conduct rigorous analyses of healthcare utilization, costs, value, quality, access, reimbursement, and health policy, with a particular emphasis on breast cancer

and oncology. Pursue competitive external funding from agencies such as the National Institutes of Health (NIH), Department of Defense (DoD), Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute (PCORI), American Cancer Society (ACS), and other public and private sponsors. Collaborate with clinicians, epidemiologists, biostatisticians, implementation scientists, public health researchers, and community partners to conduct

multidisciplinary research. Publish high-impact scholarly work in peer-reviewed journals and present findings at national and international scientific meetings. Mentor graduate and medical students, postdoctoral fellows, residents, and junior clinical investigators. Contribute to graduate and professional education through teaching and curriculum development, as appropriate. Participate in departmental,

institutional, and professional service activities. Research Areas of Interest Applicants with expertise in one or more of the following areas are encouraged to apply: Health economics and health policy with a special focus on cancer research Causal inference and policy evaluation Healthcare markets, provider behavior, and provider incentives Value-based payment and alternative reimbursement models Health insurance
