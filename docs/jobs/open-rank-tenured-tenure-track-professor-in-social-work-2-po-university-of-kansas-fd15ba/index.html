Open Rank, Tenured/Tenure Track Professor in Social Work - 2 positions Position Overview The University of Kansas School of Social Welfare (KUSSW) invites applications for two open-rank, tenure-track or tenured professor positions beginning in Fall 2027. As part of a strategic investment in strengthening the KUSSW’s quantitative and data-informed research enterprise, we seek scholars with a strong, externally

fundable research agenda who use quantitative, computational, or other data-informed methods to address pressing social challenges. We are particularly interested in candidates whose scholarship advances racial, social, economic, and environmental justice through rigorous empirical research, including the analysis of social systems, policy and program evaluation, and population-level outcomes using large

administrative or other complex data sets. Applicants must hold a Ph.D. in Social Work or an M.S.W. with a Ph.D. in a closely related field by the time of appointment (August 2027).Successful candidates will contribute to the KUSSW’s nationally recognized educational programs through excellence in teaching and mentoring. We seek faculty whose scholarly expertise and teaching interests align with macro social work

practice, social policy, community practice, program evaluation, and closely related areas. Faculty teach across the School’s B.S.W., M.S.W., Ph.D., and D.S.W. programs, which are offered through in-person, online, evening, and weekend formats to serve students throughout Kansas and beyond.KUSSW is committed to fostering a collaborative and supportive educational environment for students, faculty, and staff. We seek

colleagues whose research, teaching, and service reflect the values and ethics of the social work profession and align with the School’s Vision, Mission, and Guiding Principles.This position is subject to a collective bargaining agreement between The University of Kansas and United Academics, United Academics of KU, AAUP, or AFT. UAKU is the exclusive representative. As the State of Kansas is a right to work state,

you have the right to be a member or not be a member of a union without fear of personal or professional repercussions.About The KUSSWThe KUSSW is situated in the beautiful, historic, and culturally vibrant cities spanning Lawrence and the Kansas City metropolitan area, with satellite partnerships across the state of Kansas.
