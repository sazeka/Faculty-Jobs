Postdoctoral Scholar - Nutrient Profiling, Food Quality, and Artificial Intelligence

Tufts University  ·  Nutrient Profiling, Food Quality, and Artificial Intelligence  ·  Boston, MA
PostdoctoralNutrition

Position summary

Overview The Food is Medicine Institute at the Friedman School of Nutrition Science and Policy at Tufts University (the Institute) seeks an outstanding postdoctoral fellow to work on an original nutrient profiling system, Food Compass , under the mentorship and guidance of Institute Director Dr. Dariush Mozaffarian . Bringing together research, training, patient services, policy development, and community engagement,

the Institute aims to serve as a catalyst to drive change, improve health, reduce health disparities, and create a more equitable and resilient healthcare system that recognizes the power of nourishing food. Its efforts are centered in health equity to work collaboratively with and improve the lives of traditionally marginalized, vulnerable populations. The Institute also hosts and builds networks with partnerships

with stakeholders within and beyond healthcare to ensure that efforts advance well-being and policy broadly and reach the people who need them most. What You'll Do This is a full-time (12 months per year) position with an anticipated duration of 2-3 years, subject to annual review and renewal based on performance and available funding. The position is based in Boston, MA, and requires regular in-person work. Flexible

and hybrid arrangements (e.g., 1–2 days per week remote) may be considered; fully remote work is not available. Applicants must be authorized to work in the United States. The start date is negotiable. This position offers a competitive salary, excellent benefits, and opportunities for professional development, travel, and collaboration with leading researchers and partners. The successful candidate will meaningfully

contribute to advancing the development, expansion, and application of Food Compass across various partnerships.
