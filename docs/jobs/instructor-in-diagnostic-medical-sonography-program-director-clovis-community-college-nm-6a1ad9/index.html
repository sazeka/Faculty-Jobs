Position summary

Compensation: MA - $75,679 - $78.737 PhD - $79,510 - $82,722 Compensation Type: Salary Employment Type: Grade: E09 Department: Instruction Position Summary This is an 11 month faculty contract. Compensation is issued over a 12 month period. Full-time faculty positions at Clovis Community College are not remote positions and require a physical presence on campus to fulfill teaching duties, conduct office hours, and

fulfill other college-related responsibilities. The instructor in Diagnostic Medical Sonography/Program Director is a member of a faculty team, providing instruction leading to Diagnostic Medical Sonography certificate. Is responsible for the overall direction of the Clovis Community College School of Diagnostic Medical Sonography. Provide theory instruction as well as supervision of clinical experiences of students.

Duties are performed in the context of a comprehensive community college; understanding of the community college mission and philosophy will be critical to success. Duties & Responsibilities Prepare and teach courses in Diagnostic Medical Sonography. Teach 12 credit hours each Fall and Spring semester. May include day, evening, and online. Teaching assignment may include courses provided for high school students

under the provisions of the dual credit policy of the state of New Mexico. The instructor may teach additional courses, including courses for area high school students as part of the contract or on an overload basis, by agreement with the Division Chair and Executive Vice President. Consult with students during office hours and participate in orientation, registration, committees, councils, in- service, student

advisement, and professional development activities. In collaboration with other members of the division, determine course content and curricula. Fulfill all functions of a faculty member, including those related to teaching, faculty service, assessment of student academic achievement, and professional development. Participate in college-wide studies and projects, providing information as requested. Demonstrate or

actively work to attain proficiency with such technologies as may be useful in instruction, including personal computers, presentation software, computer-generated tutorials, and learning management systems. The Instructor in Diagnostic Medical Sonography will have tasks including, but not limited to, the correlation of didactic and clinical instruction, supervision and evaluation of Diagnostic Medical Sonography
