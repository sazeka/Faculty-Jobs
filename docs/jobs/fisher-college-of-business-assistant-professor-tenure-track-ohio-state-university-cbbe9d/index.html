Job Title: Fisher College of Business, Assistant Professor (Tenure-Track) Department: Fisher College | Operations and Business Analytics The Department of Operations and Business

Analytics at the Fisher College of Business, The Ohio State University, invites applications for a tenure-track faculty position at the Assistant Professor rank in Operations Management. We seek candidates who are committed to developing a leading research program in Operations Management and who have the potential to become internationally recognized scholars in the field. We welcome applicants who pursue

phenomenon-driven, problem-centered research that rigorously addresses important real-world operations management challenges. Candidates who employ multiple methodological approaches to data collection and analysis and investigate important operations management questions (e.g., operational excellence, supply chain management, service operations, sustainability, project management, etc.) are encouraged to apply.
