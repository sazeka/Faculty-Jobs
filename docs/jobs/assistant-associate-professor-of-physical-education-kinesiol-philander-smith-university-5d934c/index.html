Job Summary: Philander Smith University invites applications for a full-time faculty member in Physical Education, Kinesiology, and Human Performance. The successful candidate will be responsible for teaching undergraduate courses, engaging in scholarly activity, advising students, and contributing to departmental and institutional service. This position supports the institution&rsquo;s mission of academic

excellence, student success, and community engagement. Duties and Responsibilities: Teaching & Instruction Teach a range of undergraduate courses in physical education, kinesiology, and human performance (e.g., Exercise Physiology, Motor Learning, Fitness & Wellness, Strength & Conditioning). Deliver instruction in multiple modalities (in-person, hybrid, and/or online). Develop and update course materials, syllabi,

and assessments aligned with program and accreditation standards. Integrate technology and innovative teaching strategies to enhance student learning. Student Engagement & Advising Advise and mentor students in academic and career planning. Support student success initiatives, including retention and completion efforts. Supervise internships, practicums, and/or student research projects as applicable. Scholarship &

Professional Development Engage in scholarly activity, research, or creative work appropriate to the discipline. Maintain professional credentials and stay current with trends in kinesiology and human performance. Present research and/or participate in professional conferences. Service & Institutional Support Participate in departmental, college, and university committees. Contribute to program development,

assessment, and accreditation processes. Engage in community outreach and partnerships related to health, fitness, and human performance.
