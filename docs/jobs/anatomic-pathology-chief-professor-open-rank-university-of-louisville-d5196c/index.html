Anatomic Pathology Chief/Professor (Open Rank)

University of Louisville  ·  Health Sciences Center, KY
Assistant ProfessorAssociate ProfessorFull ProfessorNon-TenurePathology

Position summary

Department: Pathology Location: Health Sciences Center Time Type: Full time Worker Type: Regular Term (Fixed Term) Job Req ID: R108217 Position Description: The Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine at the University of Louisville invites applications for an innovative, collaborative, and visionary leader to serve as Director and Chief of Anatomic Pathology . The successful candidate will provide strategic,

clinical, and academic leadership for all anatomic pathology services across multiple clinical sites within a large, complex academic health system. This individual will oversee anatomic pathology operations; advance subspecialty sign-out, integrated diagnostics, and precision medicine initiatives; and lead departmental efforts in quality, safety, and continuous improvement. The Chief of Anatomic Pathology will play

a key role in mentoring and professional development for anatomic pathology faculty, with particular emphasis on colleagues early in their careers. The department is a recognized leader in digital pathology , operating a fully digital clinical workflow that supports remote access and innovative educational models, and is supported by state-of-the-art diagnostic and research laboratories, including robust core

facilities and advanced molecular diagnostics capabilities. Working closely with departmental leadership, hospital administration, and clinical partners, the Chief will help foster a culture of excellence, collaboration, and innovation. The successful candidate will demonstrate strong administrative and interpersonal skills, experience navigating a highly matrixed environment, and a clear commitment to the academic

mission, including scholarship and medical student, resident, and fellow education. Applicants must be board certified in Anatomic Pathology or AP/CP and eligible for an unrestricted medical license in the Commonwealth of Kentucky. Academic rank, tenure status, and compensation will be commensurate with qualifications, experience, and scholarly accomplishments; the compensation package is highly competitive.

Louisville—home of the Kentucky Derby—is a vibrant and affordable city offering world-class cultural amenities, extensive parks and recreational opportunities, a nationally recognized food scene, and a high quality of life. Interested candidates should submit a curriculum vitae, a statement of interest, and the names of four references with their application. Equal Employment Opportunity The University of Louisville
