POSITION SPECIFICS The Computer Science and Software Engineering Department at Penn State Behrend invites applications for a non-tenure-line faculty position to begin in Fall 2027. education and who will actively contribute to the academic mission of our programs. Candidates should demonstrate a strong commitment to high-quality teaching and student engagement, with the potential to contribute to research and applied projects.
