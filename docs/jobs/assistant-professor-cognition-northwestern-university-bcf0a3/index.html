Previous JobJob TitleAssistant Professor, Cognition Next JobJob ID54611 LocationEvanston, Illinois Add to My Favorite JobsEmail this Job Department: Education & Social PolicyThe Learning Sciences (LS) program at Northwestern University’s School of Education and Social Policy (SESP) invites applications for a faculty position (assistant professor level) whose work advances understanding of cognition and learning

broadly construed within and across contexts. SESP is an interdisciplinary school focused on understanding teaching, learning, development, and policy across the lifespan and institutions. The successful candidate will maintain a rigorous, externally funded research program on cognition and learning. We welcome applicants trained in learning sciences, psychology, education, cognition, and related disciplines who

bring theoretical and empirical depth to these issues. Candidates must have the capacity to teach and advise both undergraduate (especially in the Learning Sciences major) and graduate students in broad survey courses related to conceptual development and knowledge representations. Applicants with expertise in quantitative and/or qualitative approaches are welcome and we are especially interested in scholars who

apply computational methods in their research.Applicants should submit via the online system, a letter outlining their research program and teaching experience, a current CV, representative papers, and three contacts for letters of reference. Letters can be addressed to Carrie Tzou, Search Committee Chair. Questions can be directed to Jigbie Aguirre jigbie.aguirre@northwestern.edu.Review of materials will begin on

November 9, 2026. Applicants are strongly encouraged to submit their materials before that date. For more information about the School of Education and Social Policy, please visit http://www.sesp.northwestern.edu/.The expected base pay range for this position is $105,000 - $120,000This base pay range is for a nine-month academic appointment, does not include summer salary, and is subject to negotiation. Research

discretionary funding will also be provided. Summer salary, including from sponsored projects, may also be available.Northwestern University has provided a pay range representing its good faith estimate of what the university reasonably expects to pay for the position.
