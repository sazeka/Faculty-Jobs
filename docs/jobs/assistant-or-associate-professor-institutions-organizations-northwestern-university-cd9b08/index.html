Previous JobJob TitleAssistant or Associate Professor, Institutions, Organizations, & Policy Next JobJob ID54612 LocationEvanston, Illinois Add to My Favorite JobsEmail this Job Department: Education & Social PolicyThe Human Development and Social Policy (HDSP) program at Northwestern University’s School of Education and Social Policy (SESP) invites applications for a faculty position (assistant or associate level)

whose work advances understanding of institutions, organizations, and social policy as these multi-level dynamics of emergence, implementation, persistence and change shape human development and well-being across the lifespan. SESP is an interdisciplinary school focused on understanding teaching, learning, development, and policy throughout systems and across the lifespan. The successful candidate will maintain a

rigorous research program on organizational and institutional dynamics related to social policy and the relationship between and among these dynamics to human development. We are particularly interested in scholars whose work engages one or more of the following:• Policy design, emergence, implementation, or institutionalization, and their effects on individuals, families, communities, professions, or societal

sectors. • Organizational dynamics within and among sectors that shape human development and well-being across the life-span, such as schools and education systems, human service organizations and related systems, housing, or other societal domains• How organizations and institutions shape social and structural influences on health, education, and human development.• How interpersonal, social, or organizational

networks emerge, shape and/or influence individual and organizational outcomes within social or policy relevant geographies or domains.We welcome applicants trained in sociology, economics, history, political science, social policy, organizational behavior, education, and related disciplines who bring theoretical and empirical depth to these issues. Successful candidates will be expected to teach and advise both

undergraduate and graduate students in broad survey courses related to organizations, institutions, and social policy.
