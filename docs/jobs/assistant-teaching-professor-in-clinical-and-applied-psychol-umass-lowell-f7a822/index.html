Applications Open: Nov 09 2026 Applications Close: Open until filled General Summary of Position: The Department of Psychology at the University of Massachusetts Lowell invites applications for a full-time Assistant Teaching Professor position in Clinical & Applied Psychology to begin

September 1, 2027. We welcome applicants with expertise in clinical psychology or related applied areas, including behavior analysis, health psychology, social work, counseling psychology, and community-based practice, who can contribute to undergraduate and graduate education through teaching, mentorship, supervision, and experiential learning. We are particularly interested in applicants with expertise in one or

more of the following areas: mental health and wellbeing, behavioral interventions, behavioral medicine, health promotion, clinical and community-based practice, and the prevention or treatment of psychological and behavioral health concerns. Additional areas of interest include interventions addressing mental and physical health, health disparities, substance use, chronic health conditions, and the needs of

underserved populations. Applicants with other areas of applied expertise that complement the Department's existing strengths and curricular needs are also encouraged to apply. Applicants should demonstrate a strong commitment to undergraduate and graduate teaching, student mentorship, and experiential learning. We are especially interested in candidates with experience in clinical, healthcare, community, or social

service settings who can connect psychological science with professional practice. The successful candidate will teach courses in their areas of expertise and may contribute to student supervision and experiential learning through community service learning, directed studies, honors theses, graduate fieldwork, and master's projects or theses. Opportunities may also include developing and coordinating applied training
