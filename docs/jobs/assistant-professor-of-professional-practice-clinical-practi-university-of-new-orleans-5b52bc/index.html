Leadership, Counseling and Foundations Job Summary Job Description The Counselor Education Program within the LSU New Orleans School of Education seeks applicants for a non-tenure track full-time Assistant Professor of Professional Practice, which is equivalent to a clinical faculty position. The Assistant Professor of Professional Practice will also serve as the Clinical Practicum & Internship Coordinator for the

CACREP accredited graduate programs in counseling: M.Ed. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, M.Ed. in School Counseling, and Ph.D. in Counselor Education. The successful candidate will have established teaching, clinical, and supervision philosophies, a preference for collaborative work, a strong commitment to mentoring counseling students, and established clinical experience. This is an in-person position that is

responsible for 12 academic units per semester (4/4 – with the expectation of a release for administrative responsibilities associated with the Clinical Practicum & Internship Coordinator position) Example of Duties: Serving as the Clinical Practicum & Internship coordinator for the program; responsible for the coordination of practicum and internship experiences in Clinical Mental Health and School Counseling
