Position Title: Assistant Teaching Professor in Music, Media, Production Compensation: $55,000 - $60,000 Department: School of Music Start Date: 08/16/2027 About this opportunity: The School of Music invites applications for a non-tenure line faculty position in Music Media Production with an emphasis on live sound, recording technology, and professional audio-visual systems. The successful candidate will bring

substantial industry experience and demonstrated expertise in contemporary recording, live production, and AV workflows. Responsibilities will include teaching undergraduate courses in recording technology, live sound, audio systems, and related production areas, as well as supervising senior projects. We seek a practitioner-educator who can provide authentic, hands-on instruction and prepare students for

professional work across recording, live sound, AV systems integration, and related areas of the audio industry. Our dynamic faculty function as collaborative, innovative, and adaptive members of the College of Fine Arts community, both within and beyond the School of Music, dedicated as teachers, productive in creative and/or scholarly work, engaged in public service activities, and contributing to the Ball State

and Muncie communities and beyond. Additionally, lifelong learning at Ball State University extends educational engagement beyond the classroom and includes learning for professional development, personal enrichment, or other goals throughout one’s lifetime. Faculty will be expected to participate in the department’s, college’s, and university’s lifelong learning opportunities when available and may be assigned

teaching responsibility in this area as part of their overall responsibilities. Candidates must have current authorization to be employed in the US without employer sponsorship. production areas, as well as supervising senior projects.
