employed by the university. Basic Responsibilities A. Provide high-quality instruction, direction and support for students in the classroom environment. B. Essential Duties Provide high quality instruction, direction and support for students in the classroom environment. 1.

Facilitate a range of class sizes, up to 25 students, which may include dividing the class into sections for discussion and group activities. 2. Provide rich and timely feedback on student work, being both encouraging and constructively critical. 3.
