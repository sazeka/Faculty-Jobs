Assistant Professor, College of Dental Medicine

Roseman University of Health Sciences  ·  College of Dental Medicine  ·  South Jordan, NV
Assistant ProfessorDentistry

Position summary

Why Join Roseman? Because this is your chance to grow with a team—and a University—that’s going places. We are a private, nonprofit institution preparing the next generation of healthcare leaders through innovation, compassion, and academic rigor. And we believe those same values should guide how we treat our employees. Job Description: The Role at a Glance As an Assistant Professor in the College of Dental Medicine,

you will contribute to the academic and professional development of dental students through instruction, assessment, and mentorship across a variety of educational settings. Working closely with students and faculty, you will help bridge foundational knowledge with clinical application while promoting evidence-based practice and a culture of continuous learning. This is an excellent opportunity for an educator who is

passionate about student success and the future of dental healthcare. What You’ll Do and Why You’ll Love This Role: Inspire and develop future dental professionals through engaging classroom, simulation, and case-based learning experiences that help students build confidence and clinical competency. Make a meaningful impact on student success by mentoring, assessing, and guiding learners as they progress toward

becoming skilled, compassionate practitioners. Help shape innovative dental education by integrating evidence-based practice, biomedical sciences, and modern teaching approaches into the curriculum. Collaborate with dedicated faculty and community partners to support curriculum alignment, public health initiatives, and exceptional student learning outcomes. Stay at the forefront of the profession by utilizing

emerging technologies, advancing your expertise through calibration and training, and contributing to a culture of lifelong learning. What You'll Bring (Qualifications): DDS/DMD degree from a CODA accredited US or Canadian dental school or equivalent Teaching experience at the pre-doctoral level is preferred What Makes You Successful: You are someone who: ✅ Is passionate about teaching, mentoring, and helping

students build the skills and confidence needed to succeed in dental practice. ✅ Brings a commitment to evidence-based education and continuous learning while embracing innovative teaching methods and emerging technologies. ✅ Builds strong, collaborative relationships with students, faculty, and staff to create a positive and engaging learning environment. ✅ Communicates effectively and enjoys guiding students
