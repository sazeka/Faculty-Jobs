Position summary

Adjunct in Chemistry Adjunct Professor - Chemistry Lab Angola, Indiana Summary Teaching responsibilities will focus on general chemistry. Salary is based on the number of credit hours taught. Candidates must have earned a Bachelor's Degree in Chemistry with teaching experience preferred. The Department of Science at Trine University is a part of the Rinker-Ross School of Health Sciences, and offers B.S degrees in

chemistry, biochemistry, biology, and forensic science. The department is housed in the Best Hall of Science and has recently moved into a new $6.6 million, 26,000 square-foot addition. The addition includes new biochemistry, organic, and physical/inorganic chemistry laboratories as well as space for student research. The department is well equipped with modern instrumentation for both teaching and research, with

significant additions including: three GCs, GCMS, HPLC, microwave plasma spectrometer, FTIR, and NMR (60 MHz) with multi-element probe, having been made within the last five years. Applicants should send a detailed CV, a letter describing their qualifications for teaching laboratories and copies of undergraduate and graduate transcripts. Required Education Candidates must have teaching experience and have completed

graduate work in chemistry. Salary Salary is based upon the number of credit hours taught and the number of students registered for the class being taught. Are you ready to join Trine?
