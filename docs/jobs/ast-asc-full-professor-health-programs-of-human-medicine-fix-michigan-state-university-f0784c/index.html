Family Medicine Geriatrics Division provide comprehensive care to geriatric patients along the continuum of care, including outpatient, assisted living and skilled nursing facilities, located throughout the Greater Lansing area. students, residents, and fellows during their clinical rotations. All faculty are engaged in medical education and physician faculty have opportunities to engage in research and scholarship, either independently or in collaboration with experienced research

faculty. The Department collaborates with a statewide network of residency programs and health systems, including rural and underserved populations.
