Reporting to the

Associate Dean for Effectiveness and Accountability, this role partners with departments and school leadership to support ABET and SACSCOC compliance, strengthen continuous improvement practices, and translate assessment and enrollment data into actionable insight. The Assistant Dean plays a critical advisory role in aligning ECS growth, quality, and operational capacity. Minimum Education and Experience Minimum of a

Graduate degree in a discipline related to the job function; minimum of five years of management experience in an academic environment or equivalent corporate environment. If this position has direct instructional responsibilities it will require a doctorate degree. Preferred Education and Experience We are seeking candidates who can effectively manage and work collaboratively in teams and with units comprised of

individuals with a range of backgrounds, skills, and perspectives, contributing to a campus environment of respect and appreciation for differences. The successful candidate will possess excellent organization, communication, problem-solving, decision-making, and management skills and will demonstrate a strong commitment to strategic planning, forecasting, and data-driven decision making. Doctoral degree in higher

education, statistics, or related field. Seven or more years of experience in academic assessment, enrollment management analytics, institutional research, or decision support within higher education. Demonstrated experience supporting ABET and SACSCOC processes, standards, and reporting expectations.
