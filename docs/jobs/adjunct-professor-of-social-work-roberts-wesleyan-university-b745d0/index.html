Adjunct Professor of Social WorkBachelor of Social Work ProgramRoberts Wesleyan University invites candidates to apply for an adjunct faculty position in the online-asynchronous Bachelor of Social Work Program. We seek energetic and engaged adjunct instructors for generalist social work courses in a growing BSW Program. This Roberts degree-completion program provides a flexible format conducive to working adults.

Courses are offered in an online-asynchronous format. Courses are eight weeks in duration.The BSW programs in the Department of Social Work are housed in the School of Education and Social Work on our park-like campus in Rochester, New York, providing the program with the advantages of a suburban setting within a vibrant metropolitan area.Roberts Wesleyan University is a comprehensive, Christian university of the

liberal arts, sciences, and professions serving approximately 1,750 traditional and nontraditional students at the undergraduate and graduate levels. The University is committed to integrating a Christian worldview with learning in order to prepare thoughtful, spiritually mature, service-oriented people who will help transform society. Roberts believes that all people are created in the image of God and therefore
