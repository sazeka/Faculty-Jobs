General Functions The Assistant/Associate Professor of

Management will: Promote excellence in teaching, research, and service, supporting the mission of Langston University as an HBCU. Foster an inclusive learning environment that inspires students from diverse backgrounds. Collaborate with faculty, staff, and external stakeholders to advance the strategic goals of the School of Business. Participate actively in faculty meetings, curriculum planning, and accreditation

processes. Serving as a role model for ethical behavior, integrity, and professionalism reflects Langston’s values. Engage in professional development to stay current with advancements in supply chain management, data analytics, and business education. Specific Responsibilities Teach undergraduate and graduate courses in Supply Chain Management and Data Analytics, with a standard teaching load of 5/4 courses per

semester or 27 credit hours annually. Courses will be offered in face-to-face, hybrid, and online formats. Engage in innovative and original scholarly research contributing to supply chain management and data analytics. Develop and implement micro-credentialing and certificate programs in supply chain management and data analytics to align with industry needs. Advise and mentor students. Actively participate in

recruiting efforts to attract students to the business program and the School of Business. Represent Langston University as a media and community supply chain management and data analytics expert. Support curriculum development efforts to maintain ACBSP accreditation standards. Reports to the Dean of the Business School and may coordinate with other departmental advisors and faculty.
