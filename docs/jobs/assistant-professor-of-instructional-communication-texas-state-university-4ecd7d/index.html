Job Opportunities at Texas State University Assistant Professor of Instructional Communication Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Information Posting Information Position Title Assistant Professor of Instructional Communication Job Posting Number 2027013TTL Job Location San Marcos Department Department of Communication Studies Position Description The Department of Communication Studies

at Texas State University invites applications for an Assistant Professor of Instructional Communication. Candidates’ teaching and research expertise should include instructional communication, and we welcome candidates employing a range of methodological approaches. This tenure-track position will involve teaching, research, and service responsibilities, primarily in-person at the San Marcos campus. Employment will

begin in August 2027. Required Qualifications A PhD in Communication Studies or a communication-related field. Specialization in instructional communication. Preparedness to teach instructional communication and other courses in complementary areas at the university level (listed below). A record of departmental, university, professional, or community service. Preferred Qualifications Sustained and impactful

peer-reviewed, published scholarship. Evidence of preparation to teach graduate-level research methods (e.g., quantitative, qualitative, rhetorical, etc.) Teaching and/or research expertise in one or more complementary areas, such as interpersonal communication, nonverbal communication, small group communication, service-learning, or applied communication. Experience with or potential for external grant funding.

Application Procedures Only applications submitted through the Texas State University website will be accepted and considered: https://jobs.hr.txstate.edu/postings/58328.To assure full consideration, please submit the following by October 5, 2026: CV (including list of courses taught).
