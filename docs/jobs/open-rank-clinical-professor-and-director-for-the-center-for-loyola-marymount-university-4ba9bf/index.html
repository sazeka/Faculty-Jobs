Position summary

Who We Are The School of Education (SOE) at Loyola Marymount University (LMU), a Carnegie-classified R2 institution in the mainstream of American Catholic higher education, seeks outstanding applicants for a clinical faculty position in Catholic Education who value its mission and share its commitment to inclusive excellence and the building of a just society. LMU is federally recognized as a Hispanic-Serving

Institution (HSI) and an Asian American and Native American Pacific Islander-Serving Institution (AANAPISI) and is recognized by the State of California as a Black-Serving Institution (BSI). These designations reflect the university's continuing commitment to supporting student success, belonging, and thriving within a diverse academic community. Position Summary Reporting to the Dean of the School of Education

(SOE), the Director provides strategic leadership and oversees all operational and financial aspects of the organization while advancing its mission and strategic plan. The Director will have a Clinical Faculty appointment in the Department that best fits their qualifications, rank commensurate with higher education teaching experience. They build relationships with external partners and funders to ensure the unit’s

economic independence and sustainability. They will create and support an inclusive, collaborative organizational culture and cultivate conditions for thriving and innovation. The Center Summary The Center is a self-funded unit of the School of Education, established to promote and support a rich tradition of Catholic education through professional preparation, research, and programs. The Center is a leader in

innovation and creative solutions to problems of practice and governance in Catholic education. The Center serves as a scholarly and professional thought partner for Catholic schools and supporting organizations in Southern California and beyond. It brings together a broad range of successful programs for the preparation and development of educators and leaders in the School of Education. Appointment Type and Details

The position is a one and a half-year appointment starting in Spring 2026 though academic year 2027-2028. This is a clinical faculty appointment with Director responsibilities. Applicants must hold a Ph.D. or Ed.D. in Education or a closely related field before the position start date of January 2027. This is a term position for which annual renewal is contingent on the programmatic and budgetary needs of the School
