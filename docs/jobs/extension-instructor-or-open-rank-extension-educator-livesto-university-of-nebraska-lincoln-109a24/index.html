This is a Livestock Systems extension professional position that will focus on all facets of an integrated livestock system and will develop co-learning engagements for clientele

and stakeholders in the beef and/or small ruminant industry. The agriculture sector is the greatest economic driver in Nebraska. Of the agriculture sector in Nebraska, beef cattle are the largest economic driver. Communities across Nebraska thrive when there is a robust, profitable economic livestock sector. Nebraska Extension’s role is to catalyze opportunities for the livestock sector to make informed decisions

that are supported by research. Because Nebraska Extension is a solution-centric organization, the ability to engage producers and allied industry and commodity groups in learning and sharing opportunities is core to this role.
