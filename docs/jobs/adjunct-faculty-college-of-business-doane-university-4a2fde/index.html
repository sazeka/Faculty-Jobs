of Business Job Location Crete, NE (68333) Description of Work Doane University, a nationally recognized liberal arts and sciences university in Nebraska, invites applications for Adjunct Faculty in the College of Business. The newly-formed College of Business offers undergraduate programs in the following areas: Accounting, Agribusiness, Business Administration, Economics, and Leadership. We offer minors in each of

these areas, as well as in Finance, Human Resource Management, Management, and Marketing. At the graduate level, we offer an MBA and a Master of Arts in Leadership. The College of Business offers classes in Crete, Lincoln, and online. Our residential campus is located in Crete, with classes offered during the day in 16-week semesters. Most students in Crete are recent high school graduates. The Lincoln campus is

geared toward working adults, with classes offered one night a week in the evening during 8-week terms. Most classes meet in person, but a few meet via distance learning. We also offer asynchronous, online classes during the 8-week terms. Candidates will only be considered for academic areas that they are qualified to teach according to our accrediting bodies.For further inquiries on the position, please email

accept work in the United States. Doane is unable to sponsor applicants for work visas.
