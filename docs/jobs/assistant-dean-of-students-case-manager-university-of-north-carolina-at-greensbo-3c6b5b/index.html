Primary Purpose of the Organizational Unit The mission of the Dean of Students Office is to initiate learner-centered programs and services that foster a culture of care conducive to academic and personal development. Position Summary This Assistant Dean of Students (Case Manager) position is primarily responsible for the case management and outreach functions of both the Behavioral

Assessment Team (BAT) and the Erase Hate campaign. The multidisciplinary Behavioral Assessment Team focuses on early intervention strategies for students who may be at risk of harming themselves or pose a significant disruption to the living, learning, or work environment. This position serves on the Behavioral Assessment Team and is responsible for managing case intake information, engaging in ongoing case

evaluations, coordinating follow-up with active and inactive cases, providing updates regarding concerning and disruptive behavior, soliciting pertinent information from campus partners to support informed decision making, and maintaining continuity of care across service providers.
