Welcoming Seven New Faculty Members

Stephens College  ·  Stephens College, MO

Position summary

Stephens College is proud to welcome a dynamic cohort of new faculty members this year. Bringing expertise from the Broadway Stage, the Disney executive suite, the front lines of nursing and the cutting edge of molecular biology, these educators embody the Stephens spirit of innovation and heart. Corey Wright | Assistant Professor of Dance | Head of the Dance Department Corey Wright joins the Conservatory of the

Performing Arts as a multidisciplinary director and choreographer with credits ranging from Disney to UCLA. For Corey, joining Stephens feels “inspiring, purposeful and right.” This year, he is launching a K-Pop class to explore the intersection of social dance and musical theatre, starting a Conservatory podcast, and seeking out the perfect local coffee shop. Beyond the studio, Corey is finishing his MS in Clinical

Mental Health Counseling, a pursuit that informs his goal of helping performers maintain their mental well-being. He lives for the “aha!” moments, when a student proves their own perceived limits wrong! Brian Guidry LeTraunik | Assistant Professor of Acting With over forty years of experience across film, television, and theaters nationwide, Brian Guidry LeTraunik brings a master-level perspective to the Conservatory

for Performing Arts. An actor, director, historian, and fight director, Brian is a Certified Teacher with the Society of American Fight Directors (SAFD) and literally wrote the book on the subject—A History of Contemporary Stage Combat. Whether he is directing classics like Hamlet or contemporary premieres, Brian’s multidisciplinary background as a SAG-AFTRA member and Registered Yoga Teacher ensures our theatre

students receive a holistic, professional-grade education rooted in both history and physical craft. Sumee Chang | Assistant Professor of Equestrian Studies Sumee Chang’s arrival at Stephens is a true homecoming; she rode her first horse at the Stephens Stables at just three years old. After a 25-year career as an executive at The Walt Disney Company and founding the American Mustang Project, she is “elated to return

to Columbia. Sumee is dedicated to fostering a sense of discovery, helping students understand the boundless nature of the horse-human connection.
