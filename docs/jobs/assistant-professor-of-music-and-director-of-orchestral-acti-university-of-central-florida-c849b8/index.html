Assistant Professor of Music and Director of Orchestral Activities R114064 Main Campus, Orlando, Florida Faculty Full time School of Performing Arts - Academic Instruction The Opportunity:The University of Central Florida (UCF) School of Performing Arts invites applications for a 9-month, tenure-earning Assistant Professor of Music, with the internal university title of Program Director of Orchestral Activities. This

position is located at the UCF main campus with an anticipated start date of August 8, 2027. The successful candidate will provide artistic, curricular, and programmatic leadership for UCF’s orchestral studies program and will join the school’s string faculty as an active and collaborative colleague.The school seeks a dynamic educator-conductor and accomplished string musician with a compelling vision for the

continued growth and development of orchestral and string education at UCF. The successful candidate will demonstrate excellence in musicianship, teaching, recruitment, and ensemble leadership; inspire high levels of artistic achievement among students; and contribute meaningfully to the academic and artistic life of the School of Performing Arts.The ideal candidate will be an engaged collaborator who values strong

relationships across the string area, with other ensemble directors, and throughout the broader music program. The position also carries an important role in strengthening UCF’s connections with public and private school music educators, supporting student recruitment, and representing the program within the state, regional, and national music education and string communities. This position reports to the Director of

the School of Performing Arts.The Department of Music within the School of Performing Arts offers B.A., B.M., B.M.E., and M.A. degrees and is part of the University of Central Florida, a Carnegie R1 research university and the largest university in Florida by enrollment. UCF serves more than 70,000 students, offers 250-degree programs, and supports a dynamic environment in which teaching, creative activity, research,

and community engagement intersect.Located in one of the nation’s most active metropolitan areas, UCF offers exceptional opportunities for a musician and educator to build professional and community connections beyond the university.
