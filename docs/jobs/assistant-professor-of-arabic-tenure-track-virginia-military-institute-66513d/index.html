Assistant Professor of Arabic- Tenure Track

Virginia Military Institute  ·  Arabic- Tenure Track  ·  Lexington, VA
Assistant ProfessorTenure-TrackArabic

Position summary

Apply for this position » Hiring Range: $76,000 to $83,000* Pay Band: UG Job Duties This full-time, in-person position includes the responsibilities of teaching all levels of Arabic (3-3), scholarship, professional citizenship activities, and mentoring students. Minimum Qualifications Minimum requirements include a Ph.D. in Arabic, Arabic Studies, or a related field. Ph.D. must be in hand by the start of the

appointment, and a native or near-native fluency in Arabic (MSA + a dialect) and English. Specialization is open but preference will be given to Arabic pedagogy, Arabic linguistics, and Arabic literature. Additional Considerations This is a tenure-track position at the rank of assistant professor with a start date of 1 August 2027. We are seeking an enthusiastic, innovative teacher-scholar, who embraces the

integrated approach of Arabic pedagogy, to join our departmental focus of developing linguistically proficient and culturally competent learners. Willingness to participate in summer study abroad programs with students is a plus.*Salary commensurate with experience for the assistant professor position in Arabic.Faculty members wear uniforms and adhere to military customs, but military experience among the faculty is

neither required nor expected. For more information about VMI and the department of Modern Languages and Cultures, please visit our website. Special Instructions You will be provided a confirmation of receipt when your application and/or résumé is submitted successfully. MLJobSearch@vmi.edu.Review of applications will begin immediately and continue until the position is filled. Preliminary interviews will be conducted virtually and on a rolling basis.Background check is required for final candidate.
