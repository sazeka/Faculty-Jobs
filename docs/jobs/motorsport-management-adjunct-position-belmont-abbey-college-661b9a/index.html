Home > About Us > Employment > Motorsport Management Adjunct Position Motorsport Management Adjunct PositionPosition Summary We are seeking highly qualified adjunct instructors to join our newly announced Master of Arts in Motorsport Management degree. Belmont Abbey College is now accepting applications for the Fall 2025 semester and future terms for the Master of Arts in Motorsport Management, which has received

full approval from the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools Commission on Colleges (SACSCOC). Job Responsibilities Candidates should have a proven track record of mentoring and developing emerging professionals, as well as strong research skills applicable to the field. Experience in directing student research projects or contributing to academic inquiry is highly valued. The role requires individuals who can

integrate practical industry knowledge with academic rigor to provide students with a comprehensive and engaging learning experience. Qualifications Ideal candidates will possess a minimum of a graduate degree in business, sport management, or motorsport management related academic field. Ideal candidates would have 10 years of professional experience in the motorsport industry, demonstrating significant expertise in

business-related roles and the management of teams, brands, or racing organizations. Application Process Fill out the following form to apply for one of Belmont Abbey College’s available academic positions. APPLY NOW Review of applications will begin immediately and continue until the search is completed. Please apply by email to TreyCunningham@bac.edu using the subject line “Motorsport Management Adjunct.” Include a

letter of application, vita, the names and contact information of at least three references, and a written response in which you explain how you might contribute to the College’s Catholic, Benedictine liberal arts mission, how your work engages with the Catholic intellectual tradition, or how your own faith tradition informs your vocation as teacher and scholar. Official transcripts are optional at this stage but are

required for the interview.
