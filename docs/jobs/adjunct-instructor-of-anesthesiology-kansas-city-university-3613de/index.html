Adjunct Instructor of Anesthesiology

Kansas City University  ·  Anesthesiology  ·  Joplin Campus, MO
AdjunctAnesthesiology

Position summary

Job Type: Faculty Job Description: Kansas City University seeks adjunct faculty for a new Master of Health Science in Anesthesiologist Assistant Program in Joplin, Missouri. Faculty members play a critical role in the implementation of the program curriculum. Responsibilities include teaching course material in various settings, including the high-fidelity human-patient simulation lab, classroom (in person and

synchronous virtual), and small group discussion. Faculty participate in curriculum improvements and student assessments to support student success and professional growth. Faculty also provide student advisement and hold regular office hours for academic support. Choice of topics include task trainer and high-fidelity simulation, POCUS, anesthesia monitors and machines, applied physics, preanesthetic e valuation and

examination, cardiovascular and respiratory physiology, anesthesia pharmacology, sub-specialty anesthesia and surgery-specific protocols, quality improvement and patient safety. Time commitment is collaboratively negotiated between the program director and faculty, ranging from 0.2 to 1.0 FTE. Essential Duties and Responsibilities: The faculty member will be responsible for , but not limited to the following:

Develop, implement, deliver and evaluate instructional content and practical sessions for assigned courses by utilizing traditional and interactive teaching methods Maintain course syllabi Create and grade exams, quizzes and other assessments Serve as an advisor, coach and/or mentor to MHS-AA students guiding their professional development Participate in developing the goals, objectives , and policies and procedures

for the Master of Health Science in Anesthesiologist Assistant program specifically, and as a collaborator with other KCU programs Participate in scholarly activities and service in support of the department, with emphasis on annual accreditation requirements Attend various university ceremonies, including white coating ceremony, graduations, and other special events Participate in KCU meetings, as requested

Represent Kansas City University favorably and in accordance with the established Core Values and expectations Perform other duties as assigned by supervising faculty or Program Director Candidates must demonstrate the ability to contribute to and participate in a humanistic environment of learning and discovery.
