Associate Clinical Professor (NTT) or Assistant Professor (TT) of Dental HygieneThe UMKC Division of Dental Hygiene prepares students to become competent, adaptable oral health professionals who actively contribute to improving the public’s oral health. As the only program in Missouri to award a baccalaureate degree in dental hygiene, and part of our state’s only public dental school, we offer a long-standing,

community-focused education that equips graduates to plan, implement, and evaluate dental hygiene care for populations across the lifespan. Our graduates are prepared for success in clinical practice, education, public health, hospital programs, and school-based settings, and they join an alumni network that includes roughly half of all practicing dentists in Missouri. With strong regional impact, a commitment to

serving states without their own dental schools, and a public clinic that provides care across the Kansas City metro, UMKC fosters meaningful partnerships that support improved oral health and quality of life for the communities we serve.About Us - UMKC School of Dentistry | University of Missouri-Kansas CityDental Hygiene - Academic Programs - UMKC School of Dentistry | University of Missouri-Kansas City Job

Description UMKC is seeking applications for a 12-month full-time faculty member in the Division of Dental Hygiene. The position is a 100% benefit eligible, full time, ranked non-tenure track (NTT) OR tenure-track (TT) position at the rank of Clinical Assistant Professor/Clinical Associate Professor (NTT) or Assistant Professor (TT). Consideration for tenure-track requires a demonstrated record of scholarly activity

within the application materials. Qualified candidates should exhibit commitment and contributions to clinical and didactic teaching, scholarship, and professional service. Specific responsibilities include, but are not limited to, classroom, laboratory, and clinical teaching and evaluation in the clinical entry level program and service on School of Dentistry Committees.
