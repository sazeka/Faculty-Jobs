DESCRIPTION of SUZUKI PROGRAMThe Amarillo College Suzuki Program, founded in 1977, is a well-established program in the panhandle of Texas teaching students ages 3-18 on violin, viola, cello, flute, and guitar. Amarillo is a town rich in arts and culture, with possibilities to perform with a professional symphony, opera, several theater outlets and

numerous church and other performance venues. Please visit www.actx.edu/suzuki for more information about our program. Qualifications EDUCATIONRequired: Completion of the Suzuki Associate of Americas (SAA), Completion of Suzuki Every Child Can course and commitment to continue training. Preferred: Bachelor’s Degree, Registered Suzuki Associate of Americas (SAA) and Suzuki training unit levels 1-4 or beyond (may be

completed after application). EXPERIENCERequired: One (1) year of performance or teaching experience and instrument proficiency to be accepted for SAA Teacher Training. Preferred: Trained in the Suzuki Method beyond unit 4, with instrument proficiency.Experience teaching students, Pre-K – 12th grade. Job Duties & Responsibilities Instruct private lessons and group instruction using the Suzuki teaching method and

adopted AC Suzuki terminology and practices.Teach private lessons, up to 2 group classes per week and assist in leading play ins, workshop sessions and community performances as needed.Attend solo recitals, workshops, concerts, fundraising and community outreach events.Teach day and/or evening courses as required.Attend briefings on procedures, advising, registration, and general policy.Assist with registration.
