Job Posting Title Assistant Professor of Chemistry Job Description Summary Faculty Job Description The Chemistry Department of Wellesley College invites applications for a

tenure-track position in synthetic organic chemistry at the rank of Assistant Professor beginning in the Fall of 2027. Candidates with a strong research background in chemical synthesis, catalysis, or the development of new synthetic methods are highly encouraged to apply . Applicants should be prepared to teach introductory chemistry, organic chemistry lectures and laboratories, and advanced seminar-style courses.

The successful candidate will have a commitment to curricular/lab development and pedagogical effectiveness in undergraduate education. They will also have a well-developed plan to lead a sustainable, productive research program that actively involves undergraduates. The position offers a competitive start-up package, ongoing support for research, mentoring, and access to faculty housing . A Ph.D. or equivalent

degree is required by the time of appointment; candidates who are ABD at the time of application are welcome to apply.
