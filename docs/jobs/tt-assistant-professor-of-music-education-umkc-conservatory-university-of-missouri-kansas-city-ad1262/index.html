Job Description Assistant Professor of Music EducationThe UMKC Conservatory invites applications for a tenure-track position in Music Education at the rank of Assistant Professor, beginning in Fall 2027. We are seeking a music educator with documented achievements in teaching, music performance, and music education research. The ideal

candidate will maintain a balanced activity profile that includes accomplishments across all these areas and will collaborate with colleagues to promote a vision that enriches the training and growth of future music educators. Additionally, we seek a teacher-scholar who will consider the philosophy and approaches of music teacher preparation in an era when technology can provide information and instruction, while

sustaining a focus on the essential human connections of music making.Primary ResponsibilitiesTeach undergraduate and graduate courses in music education according to candidate’s specializations and needs of the Conservatory. Collaborate with music education faculty to support field experiences and student teaching, while cultivating and maintaining strong partnerships with area schools and practicing music
