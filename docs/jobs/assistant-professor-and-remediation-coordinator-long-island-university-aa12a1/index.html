Basic Function and Scope of Responsibilities: Coordination of enhancing student success by adapting to the classroom and testing demands of LIU’s Department of Nursing. The role involves developing and running academic support sessions that focus on test-taking strategies, the nursing process, nursing content, and study skills. Sessions may range in numbers depending on student learning needs. Frequent one-to- one

sessions may be required to address content learning issues and/or problems with test-taking anxiety.
