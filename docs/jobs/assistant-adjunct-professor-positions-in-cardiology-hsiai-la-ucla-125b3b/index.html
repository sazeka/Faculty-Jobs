Position description The Division of Cardiology, Department of Medicine at the VA Greater Los Angeles Healthcare System is seeking candidates for Assistant Adjunct Professor

positions. The candidates are expected to participate in research on the neuro-immune-vascular interface in arterial stiffness. The candidates will also work in collaboration with Tzung Hsiai, M.D., Ph.D. Designing and performing experiments,
