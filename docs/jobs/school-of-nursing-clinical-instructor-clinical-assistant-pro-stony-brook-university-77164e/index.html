Job Description: The Stony Brook University School of Nursing invites applications for a 0.8–1.0 FTE faculty appointment within our Graduate Program, along with the opportunity to serve as Program Director of the Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP) program. This is an exceptional chance for an experienced Family Nurse Practitioner to play a key role in educating the next generation of advanced practice nurses while

contributing to the continued development and growth of an established graduate program within a nationally recognized academic health center. We seek a dynamic clinician–educator with recent family practice experience who demonstrates excellence in advanced practice and a commitment to graduate nursing education. Candidates with experience precepting nurse practitioner students, teaching in upper division or

graduate nursing programs, and/or serving in leadership or supervisory roles are strongly encouraged to apply. Faculty in the School of Nursing are part of a collaborative academic community committed to advancing health, preparing the next generation of nurse leaders, and improving healthcare through excellence in education, scholarship, and clinical practice.
