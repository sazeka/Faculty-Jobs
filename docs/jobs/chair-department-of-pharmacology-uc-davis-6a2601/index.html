Position description Reporting directly to the Dean of the UC Davis School of Medicine, the Chair of Pharmacology will work closely with the Vice Dean for Basic Sciences to advance a strategic vision to create an institutional ecosystem which enables truly innovative,

high-risk yet high-impact, ground-breaking discovery across the School of Medicine (SOM)’s basic sciences and pre-clinical departments. The incoming Chair will also work closely with the Vice Dean for Research and Vice Dean for Basic Sciences in aligning departmental research support and coordinating the department’s research goals with the SOM’s overarching research strategy. In support of the SOM and Department’s

strong educational programs, the Chair will partner with the Vice Dean for Medical Education to ensure alignment of preclinical curriculum design, faculty teaching loads, and student competencies with institutional standards. In addition to these key relationships, the Chair will also work closely with other institutional leaders across UC Davis SOM's basic science and clinical departments, centers and institutes,

and other UC Davis schools and colleges, such as the School of Veterinary Medicine, the College of Biological Sciences, the College of Letters and Science, the College of Engineering, and more. Across all missions of the Department, the Chair will serve as the senior strategic, scientific and operational leader. They will lead the team’s educational and research programmatic growth, crafting a compelling scientific

vision and strategy for the Department. The Chair is currently supported by a Department Executive Advisory Group which consists of the Chief Administrative Officer, Vice Chair for Education, Vice Chair of Academic Development, and Vice Chair for Research and Administration who work together closely when making any major departmental decisions.
