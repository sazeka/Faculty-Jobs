the Director provides strategic, academic, and operational leadership for Bryant University’s Center for Applied Artificial Intelligence (the “Center”). The Director translates Bryant’s AI vision into practice by strengthening AI-infused education and credentials, expanding applied research and external partnerships, and promoting the responsible, ethical, and secure use of AI across the University. The Center

operates in a hub-and-spoke model. As the institutional “hub” leader, the Director coordinates with cross-campus partners—particularly deans and program directors, the Center for Teaching Excellence, the Sprague Center for Entrepreneurship and Design Thinking, the Sales Institute, the Executive Education & Career Accelerator, and the Office of Information Services—to ensure that Bryant’s AI strategy is integrated,

scalable, and measurable.Key partners and stakeholders Provost, deans, academic leaders, and faculty across Bryant’s colleges Center for Teaching Excellence (faculty development, pedagogy, course design) Sprague Center for Entrepreneurship and Design Thinking (programming, prototyping, venture support) Bryant University Sales Institute and the Hauck Sales Performance Lab (experiential learning, competitions, employer

engagement) Executive Education & Career Accelerator (certificates, corporate training, revenue-generating programs) Office of Information Services (data governance, privacy and security, platforms, IT enablement) University Advancement (fundraising strategy, stewardship, and impact reporting) Corporate and community partners (workforce readiness, internships, employer engagement) This position may be appointed as

either a Staff Director or a Faculty Director. The specific appointment will be based on the selected candidate’s qualifications, academic preparation, and relevant experience.
