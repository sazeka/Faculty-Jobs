Physics Faculty (Open Rank) JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY Florida Polytechnic University invites applications for a tenure-equivalent faculty position in Physics at the rank of Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, or Professor, beginning August 2027. Physics underpins every engineering and science degree at Florida Poly. This hire is part of a strategic expansion focused on three goals: excellent instruction in

Physics 1 and 2, a stronger B.S. in Physics, and a larger research presence. You will teach students across the University and mentor physics majors through a curriculum that includes a sophomore and junior design sequence. JOB DESCRIPTION Responsibilities Teach introductory physics (Physics 1 and 2) and courses at all levels of the physics curriculum Build a theoretical or experimental research program that involves

undergraduates Advise and mentor physics majors Contribute to department and University service Minimum qualifications Ph.D. in Physics or a closely related field, completed before the start date Associate Professor: six years at the rank of Assistant Professor or equivalent Professor: six years at the rank of Associate Professor or equivalent Preferred qualifications Evidence of effective undergraduate teaching,

especially in introductory physics Theoretical or experimental expertise that complements current faculty strengths Relevant research or industry experience Record or clear potential of externally funded, independent, or interdisciplinary applied research Experience advising and mentoring undergraduates Active participation in professional organizations Key dates Start date: August 2027 Full consideration:
