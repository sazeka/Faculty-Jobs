About this Position Langston University invites applications and nominations for the position of Dean of the School of Nursing and Health Professions. Reporting directly to the Vice President for Academic Affairs, the Dean serves as the chief academic and

administrative officer for the School and provides visionary leadership for nursing and allied health education, workforce development, clinical partnerships, student success, accreditation, and community engagement. The Dean will lead a dynamic and growing academic unit dedicated to preparing highly skilled, compassionate, and culturally responsive healthcare professionals. The successful candidate will advance

innovative academic programming, strengthen healthcare partnerships, support student achievement, and position the School as a leader in addressing healthcare workforce shortages across Oklahoma and beyond. This is an exceptional opportunity for a collaborative leader committed to excellence in nursing and health professions education, clinical partnerships, faculty development, research, and public service. Required

Qualifications: Ph.D./Ed.D. Earned doctorate in nursing, nursing education, healthcare administration, public health, or a closely related field from an accredited institution. Master’s degree in nursing from an accredited institution. Experience: 10 years of progressive administrative experience. Experience with ACEN, or other nursing accreditation processes. Experience leading nursing or allied health programs at

an HBCU or public university. Demonstrated success in clinical partnership development and healthcare workforce initiatives. Experience with grant-funded initiatives, simulation laboratories, online learning, and interprofessional education. Evidence of successful fundraising and external engagement.
