The Welding instructor is responsible for delivering

hands-on, industry-focused instruction to students preparing for employment in welding. The instructor will create an engaging learning environment that emphasizes practical skills, workplace safety, technical knowledge, and industry-recognized competencies. More specifically the instructor is responsible for providing hands-on instruction and classroom training to students enrolled in a non-degree welding

certificate, workforce development, or vocational training program. The instructor will teach fundamental and advanced welding techniques, safe equipment operation, welding procedures, and industry standards to prepare students for employment and career advancement in the welding industry.
