Posting Details Posting Details Title Assistant Professor of Dance Posting Number F00304JP Department Theater and Dance Position Summary Bowdoin

College’s Department of Theater and Dance invites applications for a full-time tenure-track faculty appointment in Dance at the Assistant Professor level, beginning July 1, 2027. The position subfield is contemporary modern dance with preference given to candidates who also offer an additional technique, especially one that expands our curricular offerings beyond the traditional Euro-American canon. The teaching load

is two courses per semester. Bowdoin is particularly interested in candidates dedicated to undergraduate teaching who show promise of ongoing and impactful creative work. The successful candidate will have a strong technique and studio teaching practice and the ability to make technique accessible to students of all levels, mentor student choreographers, and set original work on students each semester. Candidates

whose courses may cross-list with other campus departments and/or programs are especially encouraged to apply. An MFA in Dance is expected by the date of the appointment. Bowdoin College offers opportunities for professional development, a fully-funded, year-long pre-tenure sabbatical leave (after three years of teaching and successful reappointment), and regular, generously funded, post-tenure sabbaticals. Support

for teaching, grant submission, artificial intelligence initiatives, computing software, and community partnerships in courses is available. The College also offers robust programming and resources for establishing and enhancing mentoring networks.
