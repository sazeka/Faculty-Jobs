Position summary

Job Description Summary MUSC seeks a doctoral-level trainee for a structured HLA Laboratory Director-in-Training Fellowship in the HLA and Tissue Typing Laboratory. The program provides supervised clinical, technical, quality, regulatory, consultative, and leadership experience in histocompatibility and immunogenetics and supports preparation for a future application to the American College of Histocompatibility and

Immunogenetics (ACHI) Laboratory Director certification pathway. The fellow will train in a high-complexity academic laboratory supporting adult and pediatric solid-organ transplantation, hematopoietic cell transplantation, living-donor and paired-exchange programs, and organ-donor services in collaboration with Sharing Hope SC.
