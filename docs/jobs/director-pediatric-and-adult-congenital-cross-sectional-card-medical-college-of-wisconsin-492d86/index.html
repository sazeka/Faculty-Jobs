Position summary

Position Summary: The Department of Pediatrics , Division of Cardiology at the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW) and Children’s Wisconsin (CW), seeks a board certified / board eligible physician to join our expanding cross-sectional imaging team as Director of Pediatric and Adult Congenital Cardiac Cross-sectional Imaging. The Director of Cross-Sectional Imaging will be recognized as a clinical, academic, and

administrative leader who advances the mission of Herma Heart Institute through exceptional patient care, high-quality imaging services, innovation in cross-sectional imaging, impactful scholarly contributions, faculty and trainee development, and strategic program growth. As a leader, you will drive the cardiac MRI (CMR) and cardiac CT (CCT) programs through a transformative phase of expansion, enhancing care for

children and families. . We are comprised of nationally and internationally renowned, dedicated group of faculty and staff with a strong commitment to clinical care. Position Highlights: Provide strategic vision, leadership, and oversight for a nationally recognized pediatric and congenital cardiac imaging program encompassing CMR, CCT, echocardiography, and advanced 3D imaging technologies. Join one of the nation's

leading congenital heart programs within the Herma Heart Institute, supported by a large multidisciplinary team, a thriving research enterprise, and substantial institutional commitment to innovation and growth. Lead the continued expansion and innovation of the Herma Heart Institute's CMR, CCT, and advanced visualization programs during a transformative period of growth and investment. Deliver expert clinical care

and interpretation of advanced non-invasive cardiac imaging studies for patients with simple and complex congenital and acquired heart disease. Ensure the accuracy, quality, safety, and appropriateness of all cardiac imaging studies through comprehensive quality assurance, accreditation, and performance improvement initiatives. Develop and execute strategic plans for program growth in partnership with Cardiology,

Radiology, Heart Center, and hospital leadership, including new service development, enhancing technology, outreach initiatives, and regional expansion opportunities. Drive operational excellence by leading initiatives that improve imaging performance, patient outcomes, efficiency, innovation, and regulatory compliance.
