Job Opportunities at Texas State University Assistant Professor in Urban and Regional Planning Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Information Posting Information Position Title Assistant Professor in Urban and Regional Planning Job Posting Number 2027021TTL Job Location San Marcos Department Department of Geography and Environmental Studies Position Description The Department of

Geography and Environmental Studies at Texas State University invites applications for a tenure-track assistant professor position in Urban and Regional Planning to begin Fall 2027. We seek an urban geographer or planner whose research will expand and complement the department’s existing strengths in urban and regional planning, applied environmental geography, and GIScience. Areas of specialization are open, but may

include urban infrastructure systems, planning and public policy, comparative urbanization, housing and finance, or related areas. The successful candidate will be expected to establish an active, externally visible research program; seek external research support; publish in high-quality scholarly outlets; teach undergraduate and graduate courses in urban geography and related areas; mentor undergraduate and

graduate students; and contribute service to the department, university, discipline, and broader community. Required Qualifications A Ph.D. degree in geography, urban and regional planning, or urban studies by time of appointment. A demonstrated record of peer-reviewed scholarship. Demonstrated or potential ability to develop a strong research agenda. Demonstrated or potential ability to teach urban geography courses

at undergraduate and graduate levels. Preferred Qualifications Applied research interests in urban planning, urban infrastructure management, comparative urbanization, or housing and finance. Demonstrated record of securing external research funding. Demonstrated ability to advise graduate students in urban geography or urban planning. Application Procedures To be considered in the initial pool of candidates,

complete applications must be received no later than November 22, 2026. Complete applications received after this date will only be considered if no viable candidates are identified and hired from the initial pool. This position will remain open until filled. All positions are subject to availability of funds. Only applications submitted through the Texas State University website will be accepted and considered:
