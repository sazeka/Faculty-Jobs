Position description The School of Public Health (SPH) at the University of California, Berkeley (UCB) invites applicants to a pool of qualified temporary Lecturers to teach

courses in online education formats and Lecturers in lieu of GSIs to support courses in online education formats. The teaching includes fall, spring, and summer online course instruction in the Online Master of Public Health program. Screening of applicants is ongoing, and the number of available positions varies throughout the year. To receive full consideration for any openings, please submit a complete application

with your area(s) of Specializations indicated. Only completed applications are reviewed, and the hiring committee will contact candidates under consideration. Lecturer duties: In addition to teaching responsibilities, the instructor of record is responsible for the overall governance of the course. Duties include preparing course materials (e.g., syllabi, instructional videos, coordinating with guest lecturers,

ensuring learning outcomes and assessments align with CEPH requirements), holding office/discussion hours, assessing student work and assigning grades, responding to email, coordinating the teaching team (e.g., supervising academic student employees), meeting with program directors for course support and evaluation, and maintaining the course website in partnership with an instructional designer.
