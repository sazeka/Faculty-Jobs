Job Summary The Faculty and Student Liaison serves as the central point of support for faculty,

staff, administration, and students. This position ensures the smooth operation and scheduling of classrooms, skills labs, simulation areas, and faculty support services, while also providing students with guidance and referrals to the appropriate resources. The Liaison collaborates closely with IT, the Library, and the Nursing Department to address technology and physical resource needs, manages the Nursing iPads

and acts as the point person.
