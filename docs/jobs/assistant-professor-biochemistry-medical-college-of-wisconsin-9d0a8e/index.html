Position summary

Position Summary: The Department of Biochemistry at the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW) , invites applications for a Tenure-Track Faculty appointment in its Program in Chemical Biology . Appointments at the Assistant Professor or Associate Professor rank will be considered. We seek individuals who will develop and apply integrative methods in chemical biology and structural biology to understand the molecular

basis of human health and advance therapeutic approaches to cure chronic diseases, particularly cancer, inflammation and neurodegeneration. Candidates with interests in epigenetics, GPCR signaling, glycobiology, or metabolic disorders are encouraged to apply. MCW is committed to biomedical research excellence. It offers competitive salary support, generous benefits and start-up packages with continued support beyond

the start-up phase, as well as outstanding opportunities for collaboration with basic, translational, and clinical investigators. Position Highlights: Develop and apply integrative methods in chemical biology and structural biology research. Investigate the molecular mechanisms that influence human health and disease Advance therapeutic strategies aimed at treating chronic diseases, including cancer, inflammation,

and neurodegenerative disorders. Conduct innovative research in areas such as epigenetics, GPCR signaling, glycobiology, and metabolic disorders. Collaborate with basic science, translational, and clinical investigators across disciplines. Contribute to the institution's mission of excellence in biomedical research. Secure and maintain an independent, externally funded research program. Mentor and support students,

trainees, and junior researchers. ​ Department/Division Highlights: The Department Biochemistry at MCW has a long history of outstanding research. Areas of research strength include cancer biology, cell signaling, glycobiology structural biology, and metabolism, supported by exceptional structural biology, chemical biology, and therapeutics development programs. country.
