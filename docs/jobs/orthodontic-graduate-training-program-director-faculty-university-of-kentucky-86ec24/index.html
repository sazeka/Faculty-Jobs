Job Summary The Department of Oral Health Science in the College of Dentistry is seeking an innovative leader to serve as our Orthodontic Training Program Director with a tenure eligible Assistant, Associate or Full Professor appointment within the College. The Program Director will serve

under the Division Chief of Orthodontics and will be responsible for providing guidance and leadership for the graduate training program within the Division. They will assure that all Commission on Dental Accreditation standards (CODA) will be appropriately maintained to allow full accreditation of the program. In addition, the Program Director will be responsible for overseeing the academic and clinical training for

all graduate students in the program and will work in collaboration with staff members that have their main functions related to the graduate training program. The successful candidate will have completed a CODA approved orthodontic graduate program, be certified by the American Board of Orthodontics and eligible for academic dental license from the Kentucky Board of Dentistry. Ideal candidates would have a record of

excellence in dental education, research, mentoring students and have a clear vision for the program. Responsibilities The Distribution of Effort (DOE) for this position will include; 40-45% teaching, 20% administration, 15% research/scholarship /creative activities, 20% patient care services, and 5% personal/professional development.
