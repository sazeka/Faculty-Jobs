Position summary

Oakwood University invites applications for a full-time faculty position in the Department of Social Work. Appointment rank (Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, or Professor) will be based on academic credentials, teaching experience, scholarship, and professional accomplishments. Applicants must be an enthusiastic and student-centered educator committed to excellence in teaching, scholarship, service, and the

integration of faith and learning. The successful candidate will contribute to the preparation of competent, ethical, and culturally responsive social work professionals who are committed to service, advocacy, social justice, and community engagement. Essential Responsibilities Teach undergraduate social work courses in assigned areas of expertise. Advise and mentor students regarding academic progress, professional

development, and career opportunities. Participate in curriculum development, assessment, and continuous program improvement initiatives. Support field education activities and collaborate with community agencies and organizations. Maintain scholarly and professional engagement through research, publication, presentations, grant development, or other professional contributions. Participate in departmental,

university, and community service activities. Assist with student recruitment, retention, and accreditation-related activities. Sponsor departmental organizations/clubs Integrate Seventh-day Adventist values and principles into teaching and student engagement. Required Qualifications Assistant Professor Master of Social Work (MSW) degree from a CSWE-accredited institution. Minimum of two years of post-MSW

professional social work practice experience. Evidence of teaching effectiveness or potential for excellence in teaching. Eligibility for social work licensure, where applicable. Associate Professor Earned doctorate in Social Work or a closely related field from an accredited institution. Significant record of successful college-level teaching. Demonstrated scholarly achievement and professional service. Evidence of

leadership within the profession and academic community. Professor Earned doctorate in Social Work or a closely related field from an accredited institution. Distinguished record of teaching, scholarship, service, and professional leadership. Demonstrated contributions to the advancement of social work education and practice. Strong record of mentorship, research, and institutional service. Preferred Qualifications
