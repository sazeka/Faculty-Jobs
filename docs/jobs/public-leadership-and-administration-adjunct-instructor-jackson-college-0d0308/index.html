Public Leadership & Administration Adjunct Instructor Print Public Leadership & Administration Adjunct Instructor Salary $49.00 - $56.00 Hourly Location Multiple Campus Locations, MI Job Type Adjunct Instructor Department Public Leadership & Administration Opening Date 03/28/2024 Description Benefits Questions Description Teach courses in Public Leadership and Administration.Adjunct teaching positions available at

the following Correctional Education Program locations: Women's Huron Valley in Ypsilanti, MI, St. Louis Correctional Facility in St. Louis, MI, Milan Federal Prison in Milan, MI, and Gus Harrison Correctional Facility in Adrian, MI. These classes are conducted in person. Adjunct instructor positions are part-time. Teaching assignments will vary from semester to semester and are based on enrollment. Therefore, there

may be semesters where no assignments are available. Essential Functions Evaluate and grade students' class work, assignments and papers. Prepare and deliver instructional materials (i.e. syllabi, homework assignments, and handouts) in person and/or on-line. Initiate, facilitate, and moderate classroom discussions. Compile, administer, and grade examinations. Participate in required reporting processes (i.e. grades,

participation, etc.). Assist students in connecting to college resources. Minimum Qualifications Master's degree or higher in Public Leadership & Administration or subfield OR Master's degree or higher in another field with 18 graduate credits in Public Leadership & Administration or subfield.
