Job Requisition: JR100975 Adjunct Faculty - Online Counseling Program-1 (Open) Job Posting Title: Adjunct Faculty - Online Counseling Program-1 Department: CC00182 WM001 | PROV | Education Operations Job Family: Faculty - Adjunct Job Requisition Primary Location: School Of Education Primary Job Posting Location: School Of Education Job Description Summary: The department of School Psychology and Counselor Education

is recruiting a pool of persons for the Fall 2025, Spring 2026, and Summer 2026 semesters to be considered for possible adjunct teaching positions in our online counseling program. This is a fully online, remote position. This pool will be accessed ONLY when an opening arises and is based on funding. Only those selected for an interview will be contacted. The review of applications is ongoing. Official transcripts

will be required from candidates upon hire. We will not sponsor applicants for work visas for this vacancy. Position responsibilities include, but are not limited to, the following: •Maintain frequent and open communication with William & Mary faculty and staff through email, mail, telephone, and any other medium deemed appropriate. •Review course content, activities, and assignments prior to the term start date.

•Meet all designated course instruction guidelines. •Provide students with timely and specific feedback. •Grade assignments and exams in a timely manner based on the instructions/rubrics provided (2-3 business days). •Respond to student inquiries within 1-2 business days. •Monitor discussion boards and participate as applicable. •Hold weekly virtual office hours for individual or group consultation. •Submit final

grades according to William & Mary policy and the Online Programs calendar. •Submit end-of-semester feedback and reflection form. Job Description: Required Qualifications: Successful completion of a terminal degree in counselor education or related field or qualifications matching those enumerated in the Faculty Qualifications Manual for Alternate Methods of Qualifying Faculty to Teach. Preferred Qualifications:

Online university teaching experience in counseling, counselor education, or related field. Please indicate in your cover letter the program(s) in which you have expertise.
