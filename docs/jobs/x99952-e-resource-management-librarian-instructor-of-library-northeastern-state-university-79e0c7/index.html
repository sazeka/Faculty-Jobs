Sign InCreate ProfileEnglish (US)X99952 E-Resource Management Librarian/Instructor of Library ServicesTahlequah, OK, United States|req5921Posted on: 9/3/2026 Back to SearchE-Resource Management Librarian/Instructor of Library Services JOB SUMMARYE-Resource Management Librarian provides leadership and expertise in managing the full lifecycle of the NSU Libraries’ electronic resources, including online databases,

e-journals, e-books, and streaming media. This position is essential for ensuring seamless discovery, access, and maintenance of digital content for the NSU community. The successful candidate will combine technical skills with strong negotiation and communication abilities to support the research and learning needs of NSU students and faculty. This position will be based in either Tahlequah or Broken Arrow. MAJOR

DUTIES●Resource Management: Manage the entire lifecycle of electronic resources, from trial and acquisition to renewal or cancellation.●Access & Troubleshooting: Ensure reliable access to electronic materials for both on and off-campus users. Configures the Library’s subscription databases and collections on vendor platforms. Ensure that library online resources are discoverable by maintaining proxy stanzas and IP

information. Act as the primary technical liaison for access issues and broken links, working with vendors, library IT, and users to resolve problems promptly.●Licensing & Negotiation: Coordinate the review and negotiation of license agreements and contracts with vendors and publishers, ensuring compliance with legal standards and university policies. Facilitate database trials and evaluate resources for compliance

with accessibility standards. ●Data Analysis & Assessment: Collect, analyze, and interpret usage statistics (e.g., COUNTER and SUSHI data) to inform collection development decisions, track cost-per-use, and support budget allocation.●Systems Management: Utilize and maintain the Discovery and Electronic Resource Management (ERM) systems in the library management system (LMS). Activate and deactivate collections and

records as needed. Maintains information about licensed electronic resources in the LMS.●Collaboration & Training: Work collaboratively with departmental and other library staff. Provide training and support to library staff and, at times, faculty and students on the effective use and management of electronic resources. Works as a member of the Technical Services Department and reports to the Director of Library
