Position description Part-Time Artificial Intelligence Instructor University of California, Riverside - University Professional and Global Education *ABOUT UCR PROFESSIONAL AND GLOBAL EDUCATION UCR PGE is the continuing and professional education division of the University of

POSITION OVERVIEW Position Title: Artificial Intelligence Instructor Salary Range: $100 per Instructional Hour Anticipated Start: Winter 2027 POSITION DESCRIPTION UCR PGE is currently in search of artificial intelligence practitioners to deliver university-developed curriculum in AI on a part-time basis. Extension

offers a Professional Certificate in Applied Artificial Intelligence and a Postgraduate Diploma in Applied Artificial Intelligence. Our courses focus on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning. Concepts covered include data preparation techniques, including data collection, cleaning, and feature engineering. Learners have an opportunity to apply AI concepts to train, validate, and fine-tune AI

models, including large language models. The applied capstone course provides hands-on experience in deploying and monitoring AI models in real-world applications. Our audience includes both adult learners (domestic and international) as well as traditional college-aged students from abroad who enroll in our visiting-student program. There are opportunities to teach our courses in both online and classroom settings.

However, this posting is geared for In-person classroom instruction from our Riverside location. UCR is on the quarter system, and courses are generally 10-12 weeks in length. We also have opportunities to teach in our short-term courses. Courses include: • Artificial Intelligence: Principles and Techniques • Data and Data Preparation for AI • AI Practicum (project-based) • Artificial Intelligence and Innovation
