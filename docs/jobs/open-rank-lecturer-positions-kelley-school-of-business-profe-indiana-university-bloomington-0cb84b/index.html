Location Bloomington Position Summary The Kelley School of Business at Indiana University Bloomington (IUB) invites applications for full-time, open-rank Lecturer positions in Professional Skills beginning Fall 2027. These are 10-month, non-tenure-track appointments with an 18-credit-hour annual teaching assignment.Professional Skills faculty teach primarily in Kelley Compass, a required three-course

professional-development sequence for Kelley undergraduates. The sequence integrates career development with professional communication, leadership, teamwork, and professional identity development. Faculty typically have a primary assignment in one of the three Compass courses, with opportunities to teach across the sequence. Professional Skills Courses Compass 1: Professional Identity and Career Exploration (1.5

credits) Compass 1 introduces students to career development and professional identity. Students explore their values, interests, personality, and skills; investigate majors and career possibilities; learn to articulate their professional identity and value to professional audiences; and develop professional networking skills. Compass 2: Career Readiness and Job Search (1.5 credits) Compass 2 develops students’

career-readiness and recruiting skills. Course content includes employer research and career strategy, resumes, interviewing, professional correspondence and networking, giving and receiving actionable feedback, cultural competence, and emotional intelligence.
