Position summary

The Department of Otolaryngology and Communication Sciences at the Medical College of Wisconsin (MCW) is seeking a Board Certified or Board Eligible Fellowship trained Rhinologist to join our expanding team of subspecialists. This is an exciting opportunity to build a busy clinical practice focused on disorders of the nose and paranasal sinuses, while contributing to the academic mission of one of the nation’s

leading academic medical centers. Opportunity Highlights: Join a collaborative and collegial team of Otolaryngology faculty with a strong culture of mentorship and support. Practice at Froedtert Hospital and affiliated sites , a nationally recognized academic medical center and MCW’s primary adult hospital partner. Opportunities for clinical and translational research, quality improvement initiatives, and resident

and medical student education. Participate in multidisciplinary care of patients with sinonasal tumors, skull base pathology, and allergic/inflammatory conditions. Competitive compensation and benefits package with protected academic time and support for research and professional development. Schedule: Full-time faculty position at the Assistant or Associate Professor level. Provide coverage during core business

hours with some flexibility required, as necessary, to accommodate business needs. Department Highlights: Largest otolaryngology group in Wisconsin, featuring nationally and internationally recognized, fellowship-trained physicians. Comprehensive subspecialty expertise across all major areas of otolaryngology, providing exceptional opportunities for clinical collaboration. Strong clinical partnerships with Froedtert

Hospital, Children’s Wisconsin, and the Clement J. Zablocki VA Medical Center. Advanced surgical programs specializing in rhinology, anterior skull base surgery, head and neck oncology, and complex reconstructive procedures. Established neurotology and otology programs offering advanced hearing restoration procedures, including cochlear implantation. Multidisciplinary approach to patient care, collaborating with

neurosurgery, oncology, audiology, speech-language pathology, and other specialties. Two NIH-funded research education programs supporting the development of academic surgeon-scientists and future faculty leaders. Extensive clinical, translational, and basic science research opportunities supported by federal and industry funding.
