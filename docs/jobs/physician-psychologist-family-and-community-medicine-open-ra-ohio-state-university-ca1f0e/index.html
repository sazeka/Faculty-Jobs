Job Title: Physician – Psychologist, Family and Community Medicine (Open Rank/Track Faculty) Department: The Department of Family and Community Medicine at The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center is seeking a Clinical Psychologist to join our growing academic healthcare system. This

faculty position offers the opportunity to provide evidence-based psychological assessment and treatment within a nationally recognized academic medical center while contributing to education, research, and interdisciplinary patient care. The successful candidate will provide outpatient clinical services, collaborate with multidisciplinary healthcare teams, and participate in the academic mission of The Ohio State

University College of Medicine. Faculty rank and track will be commensurate with experience and qualifications. The Department of Family and Community Medicine offers patient-centered personalized medical services through The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center at our family medicine centers located throughout Columbus and Franklin County. These centers feature family medicine teams of physicians, residents,

nurse care coordinators, nurse practitioners, pharmacists, dietitians, medical assistants/technicians, and mental health experts who work together to ensure our patients receive excellent, personalized healthcare.
