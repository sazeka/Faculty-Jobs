Position Details Position Information Winthrop Position Title Assistant/Associate Professor, Interior Design Job Description Winthrop University, a highly ranked, public,

comprehensive university, announces a search for an Assistant/Associate Professor of Interior Design. This is a full-time, 9-month, tenure-track appointment in the Department of Design within the College of Visual and Performing Arts. Our campus is in Rock Hill, South Carolina approximately 30 minutes from Charlotte, NC which has a thriving design market and international airport. The successful candidate will have

the opportunity to work in and develop our new state of the art facilities for Interior Design at The Thread, a dynamic and flexible hub for learning, collaboration, and creativity, representing a transformative initiative aimed at enhancing the educational environment for students, faculty, and the wider community. This position has an August 2027 start date.Winthrop University seeks an experienced interior designer

and/or architect with a terminal degree in their field, and a proven record of relevant professional experience that aligns with contemporary principles and practices. The ideal candidate will have a strong emphasis on human-centered design methodologies.
