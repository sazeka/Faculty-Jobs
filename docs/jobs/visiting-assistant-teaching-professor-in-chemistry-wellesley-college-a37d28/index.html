Job Posting Title Visiting Assistant Teaching Professor in Chemistry Job Description Summary Faculty Job Description The Chemistry Department of Wellesley College invites

applications for a Visiting Assistant Teaching Professor position in Chemistry for the Fall 2026 semester. The successful applicant would teach two or three biochemistry labs and one introductory chemistry lab, in coordination with respective lecture instructors, for a total of three or four labs in the Fall. If the candidate desires, they may be able to also teach two labs in the Spring 2027 semester; the yearlong

position would be benefits-eligible. In addition to class meeting times, the candidate will hold regular office hours in person, promptly respond to student inquiries throughout the semester, and attend relevant teaching team and departmental meetings. There are no expectations for this position regarding formal academic advising. The candidate will be expected to engage in professional development and departmental

service as outlined in the collective bargaining agreement. Applicants should have a broad background in chemistry or biochemistry, experience teaching undergraduates, and strong teaching, writing, and interpersonal skills.
