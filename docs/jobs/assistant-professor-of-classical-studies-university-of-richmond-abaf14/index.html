Assistant Professor of Classical Studies

University of Richmond  ·  Classical Studies  ·  Richmond, VA
Assistant ProfessorClassical Studies

Position summary

The Department of Classical Studies in the School of Arts and Sciences at the University of Richmond invites applications for a full-time tenure-track position at the rank of Assistant Professor to begin in the 2027-2028 academic year. We are seeking a scholar with an active program of research in ancient medicine and a strong commitment to undergraduate teaching and mentorship. Areas of expertise in which we are

particularly interested include, but are not limited to: Hellenistic Egypt, religious ritual and magic, gender and sexuality, race and ethnic identity, disability studies, reception of Hippocratic Corpus and Galen, and papyrology. Research programs involving digital humanities are especially welcome. In addition to a course focused on the history of ancient medicine (CLSC 210), the successful candidate will teach

Greek and Latin language courses and mentor undergraduate student research. The candidate will be able to develop new courses that enhance the department’s existing curricular offerings and can further support our General Education curriculum, the Web of Inquiry , including opportunities to contribute to the University’s interdisciplinary programs. The regular teaching load will be five courses per academic year

across two semesters. The Department of Classical Studies strives to sustain an engaging and collaborative environment that welcomes students from all backgrounds into the study of the languages, material cultures, histories, and receptions of the ancient Mediterranean world, with special emphasis on ancient Greek and Latin. The successful candidate will broaden the range of scholarly specialties, viewpoints, and

experiences represented within the department. We are interested in applicants who have a commitment to creating inclusive learning environments, mentoring students from a range of backgrounds and experiences, and generating student interest in Classics. A Ph.D. in Classics or a related field is required by the time of appointment. Applicants should apply online at https://jobs.richmond.edu and submit as separate

.pdf files: a curriculum vitae, cover letter (two pages maximum), teaching portfolio, research statement, and a sample or excerpt of published or unpublished scholarship (15-25 pages).
