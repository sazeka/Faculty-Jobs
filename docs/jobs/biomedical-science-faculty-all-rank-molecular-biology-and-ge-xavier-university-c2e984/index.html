The 12-month positions beginning in June of 2027. Tenure

and non-tenure track will be considered. These positions are involved in the creation, development, and delivery of the basic science medical curriculum, the creation and evaluation of assessments, pursue scholarly activities in their area of expertise, and provide mentorship and guidance for medical students. The biomedical science faculty report to the Associate Dean of Faculty and Medical Programs. XUCOM is the

first osteopathic medical school at a Jesuit Catholic University. To fulfill its mission, XUCOM will integrate Jesuit Catholic traditions and values with Osteopathic Principles and Practice; our mission is infused in all that we do. Xavier is an EOE. JOB RESPONSIBILITIES: Designs and delivers curricular content within the College of Osteopathic Medicine Involved in the creation of assessments within the curriculum of

the College of Osteopathic Medicine Advises and mentors students of XUCOM Contributes to the mission of the college through teaching, scholarship and service Other duties as assigned QUALIFICATIONS: Required: Terminal degree in discipline related to medical education (PhD, MD, DO) Evidence of scholarly activity in genetics or molecular biology Preferred: 1-3 years teaching experience in medical education or graduate

education Evidence of student mentoring POSITION: Twelve-month, non-tenure and tenure track will be considered RANK/SALARY: Rank and salary commensurate with qualifications and experience Xavier University in Cincinnati, Ohio is part of the 500-year-old Jesuit Catholic tradition of academic excellence in the liberal art. We strive for a climate of respect and inclusiveness that welcomes and supports members from

diverse backgrounds and life experiences, is committed unreservedly to open and free inquiry, and deliberately seeks out multiple perspectives. These values are central to our mission . HOW TO APPLY: To ensure consideration, qualified/interested applicants must submit a cover letter, resume, teaching philosophy and the names and contact information of three professional references on Xavier University's website.
