Number 201104663 Position Title Assistant/Associate/Full Clinical Professor of Law State WI Employment Status Full Time Position Status Limited Term If Limited Term (End Date of Assignment, Project, or Grant) 06/30/2031 Position Type Faculty Job Family Faculty # Hours Per Week 37.5 Position Overview Marquette University Law School invites applications to join our faculty. This clinical position will be part of the

Law and Entrepreneurship Clinic (LEC), a transactional legal clinic that provides pro bono legal services to entrepreneurs, small businesses, and nonprofit organizations in southeastern Wisconsin, with a focus on clients who cannot afford counsel. The position will help expand the scope of the LEC to include a focus on real estate transactions. The position will educate and supervise students in such work. The

position will also teach other courses and provide additional educational activities in the area of real estate and related areas. This position will serve as a point of contact for the Law School with the Vieth Institute for Real Estate Leadership (VIREL) and work to integrate law students in the LEC with the Vieth Institute’s initiatives. Marquette University, an equal opportunity employer (EOE), is a Catholic,

Jesuit university with a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs. We seek candidates who understand and will advance the University’s mission, which is stated at https://www.marquette.edu/about/mission.php.
