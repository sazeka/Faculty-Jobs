Position Title: Assistant Professor of Nursing Compensation: $75,000 - $85,000 Department: School of Nursing Start Date: 08/16/2027 About this opportunity: The Ball State University School of Nursing invites applications for a full-time, tenure-line faculty position at the rank of Assistant Professor. The successful candidate will contribute to the School's teaching, scholarship, and service missions and will have an

appropriate nursing specialization to teach in undergraduate and/or graduate nursing programs. Undergraduate teaching is primarily campus-based and may include classroom, simulation, laboratory, and clinical instruction. Graduate teaching may include online instruction. Teaching assignments and locations may vary. This is not a remote position. Employer will consider sponsorship. What you'll do and what you will

bring: Duties and Responsibilities: · Teach undergraduate and/or graduate nursing courses consistent with academic preparation, clinical expertise, and program needs. Teaching assignments may include classroom, online, simulation, laboratory, and clinical instruction or supervision. · Incorporate current evidence, research, professional standards, and best practices into teaching and learning activities. · Evaluate

student learning and competency in classroom, simulation, laboratory, and clinical settings, as applicable. · Participate in course and curriculum development, implementation, evaluation, and continuous improvement. · Maintain an active program of scholarship appropriate to a tenure-line faculty appointment, including conducting research and disseminating scholarly work through peer-reviewed publications and

presentations at state, national, and/or international conferences. · Seek internal and/or external funding to support scholarship and professional activities, as appropriate. · Mentor students regarding academic progress, professional development, and related activities. · Contribute to the mission and professional life of the School of Nursing, College of Health, Ball State University, community, and nursing

profession. · Participate in School, College, and/or University committees and shared governance activities. · Engage in professional development and maintain currency in nursing practice, nursing education, and area of expertise. · Perform other duties consistent with the faculty role as assigned by the Director of the School of Nursing.
