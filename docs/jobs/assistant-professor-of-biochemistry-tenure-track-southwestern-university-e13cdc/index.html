POSITION OVERVIEW The Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry at Southwestern University invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor position with expertise in biochemistry

beginning in August of 2027. We seek a dedicated biochemist committed to innovative, student-centered education who is enthusiastic about mentoring undergraduates in both classroom and research settings. The successful candidate will have opportunities to contribute to the University’s developing interdisciplinary neuroscience program and general education curriculum.The successful candidate will teach five courses

per academic year in our American Chemical Society (ACS) approved department. These courses will include undergraduate upper-level biochemistry lectures and laboratories, general chemistry, and specialty courses in the candidate’s area of expertise. Additional responsibilities include maintaining an active scholarly agenda, academic advising, and departmental and university service. The department encourages

applications from candidates with a demonstrated interest in fostering inclusive excellence in the classroom, curricular innovation, and pedagogical creativity.Additional information about the Chemistry and Biochemistry Department is available at www.southwestern.edu/chemistry-and-biochemistry/.
