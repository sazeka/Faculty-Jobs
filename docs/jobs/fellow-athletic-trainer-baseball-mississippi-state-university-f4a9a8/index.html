Must be a BOC Certified Athletic

Trainer or eligible to sit for the BOC Exam. Salary Grade: 09 Please see Staff Compensation Structure for salary ranges. Essential Duties and Responsibilities: 1. Assist Sports Medicine Staff with the athletic training needs for sports as assigned. This includes direct team and sport coverage (home and away). 2. Assist in the day-to-day operations of the athletic training room. Ensure proper use of equipment and

facilities by student-athletes, including adherence to athletic training room time schedules and proper operating procedures. 3. Assist the Sr. Associate Athletic Director for Sports Medicine with the prevention and care of student-athlete injuries and illnesses. 4. includes ensuring compliance with team physician instructions. 6. Obtain required continuing education requirements to fulfill and maintain state and BOC certifications. 7.
