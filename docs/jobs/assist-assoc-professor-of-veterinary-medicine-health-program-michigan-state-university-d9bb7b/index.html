College Of Veterinary Medicine 1169565 Working/Functional Title Assist/Assoc Professor Health Programs Fixed Term Position Summary The Department of Small Animal Clinical Sciences includes over 40 faculty with a state-of-the art Veterinary Medical Center (VMC). The VMC supports outstanding patient care with a superb team of highly qualified veterinary technicians to support the general and specialty services. It is

equipped with GI, pulmonary, and genitourinary endoscopic equipment, laparoscopy, 64 slice helical CT, digital cinefluoroscopy, 3.0 Tesla MRI, nuclear medicine, and a linear accelerator, amongst others. We are well-represented in supporting specialty services including 24/7 emergency and critical care, urgent care, community medicine, anesthesiology, cardiology, primary care, diagnostic imaging, interventional

radiology, sports medicine and rehabilitation, ophthalmology, orthopedic surgery, soft tissue surgery, medical oncology, theriogenology, and dermatology. The Internal Medicine service is focused on exceptional instruction and patient care, supported by a busy caseload and experienced faculty. The successful candidate’s responsibilities will include management of referral cases, supervision and clinical instruction of

house officers and students, and a lesser commitment to scholarly contributions and teaching (both pre-clinical and clinical) in the candidate’s area of interest or expertise. Our Health Programs (HP) appointment system is dynamic and generally associated with a heavier clinical commitment than tenure stream faculty while still maintaining ample time for teaching and scholarly interests. The extent of participation

in the pre-clinical teaching curriculum as well as expected contributions to specialty service leadership is dependent on the interests and strengths of the applicant. Health Programs faculty have full college voting rights and the main difference from tenure stream faculty is that a collaborative research focus is acceptable whereas independent research programs are expected for tenure stream faculty.
