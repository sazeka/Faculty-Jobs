Assistant/Associate Professor of Christian Ministry & Formation

MidAmerica Nazarene University  ·  Olathe, KS
Assistant ProfessorAssociate ProfessorChristian Ministry & Formation

Position summary

MidAmerica Nazarene University invites applications for multiple full-time faculty positions in the Department of Christian Ministry and Formation. For each open position, the department seeks a faithful Christ-follower, gifted teacher, mentor, scholar, and ordained minister who will contribute to the intellectual, spiritual, and vocational formation of undergraduate students preparing to serve Christ, the church,

and the world. Initial review of applicants will begin on December 1st, with interviews planned for January 2027. The intended start date for all positions is August 1, 2027. Candidates with expertise in the following fields are encouraged to apply: biblical studies (including biblical language proficiency), theology, practical theology, Christian education, philosophy, and intercultural studies. Successful

candidates will demonstrate the ability to teach broadly within the department&rsquo;s undergraduate curriculum and to support programs that prepare students for ministry, service, and ordination in the Church of the Nazarene and in the church universal. The Department of Christian Ministry and Formation seeks to form students intellectually and spiritually through a Christlike, in-person learning community. The

faculty in the department help majors grow to be rooted, mature, contemplative, and missional servants who participate in God&rsquo;s redemptive work in a variety of ministry settings. The faculty also serve a vital role in the general student population through teaching courses in Christian scripture and tradition to all students. Successful candidates will embrace these commitments and be eager to invest in

students through excellent teaching, advising, mentoring, scholarship, and participation in the life of the university and church. Primary Responsibilities The faculty member will be expected to: Teach undergraduate courses in Christian Ministry and Formation, with teaching assignments based on departmental need. Mentor and advise students as they discern and prepare for ministry, service, and vocation, including

students pursuing ordination in the Church of the Nazarene. Contribute to curriculum development, assessment, and program improvement. Participate actively in departmental, college, and university service. Apply teaching, mentoring, and scholarly expertise in regular service to the Church of the Nazarene and its mission (local, district, regional, global).
