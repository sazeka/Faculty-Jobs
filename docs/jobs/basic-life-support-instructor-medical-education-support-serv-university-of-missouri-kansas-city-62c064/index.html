Under the supervision of the simulation leadership, the BLS Instructor delivers standardized, high-quality CPR and emergency cardiovascular care instruction in compliance with

current AHA curriculum guidelines. This role supports the UMKC SOM mission to provide competency-based training to healthcare learners across the UMKC SOM enterprise.Knowledge, Skills & AbilitiesAbility to deliver clear, effective instruction to diverse learner populations including medical students, nurses, residents, and allied health professionals.
