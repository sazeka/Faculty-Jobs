is a family nurse practitioner (FNP). This is an open rank position at either the assistant, associate, or full clinical professor rank based on experience in higher education. This position requires an on-campus presence. We seek applicants recognized for teaching expertise, clinical scholarship, ability to work with diverse populations, and excellent oral and written communication skills in English. This is a

9-month, full-time benefited position. A growing, dynamic, and well-regarded school established in 1968, the Solomont School of Nursing enrolls over 550 nursing students in BS, RN to BS, Master's, Doctor of Nursing Practice, and PhD in Nursing programs and has 20 full-time faculty members. NCLEX-RN pass rates and NP certification exam pass rates consistently exceed 90%. The School's diverse research portfolio

includes research in health disparities, correctional worker health, occupational health, pediatrics, and women's health. Clinical faculty are expected to teach 9 credits per semester. Faculty in the School often teach across programs, including in the undergraduate and graduate programs. The SSON has two tracks in the nurse practitioner program, including family nurse practitioner and adult gerontologic primary

care. Faculty also participate in the supervision of Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) projects. All faculty in the School are expected to engage in service to the School, College, and University, commensurate with rank.
