Position summary

Advertised Position Title: Applicant Pool for Adjunct Faculty, Lab Assistant, Emergency Services Technology Job Description: The Lab Assistant is an important part of the EMM educational team. The assistant will work under the supervision of the Clinical Coordinator and Instructors teaching, demonstrating and assessing lab skills of students. The lab assistant can only work up to his/her certification level.

Knowledge, Skills & Abilities: Working knowledge of the National Registry of EMT testing skills. Knowledge of prehospital care as delivered by EMT -Basic, Intermediates, or Paramedic level. Must have good verbal communication skills and technical skills consistent with Emergency Medical Care, Ohio Department of EMS and all accrediting bodies. Must be able to identify and correct weaknesses in student performance of

skill delivery Essential Functions: Assist in setting up skill labs for the Emergency Services Technology students. Monitor the students progress by observation and mentoring in the completion of skill lab. Assist Instructor explaining, demonstrating and assessing students in the lab setting. Assist in cleaning and replacing items used in the skill labs as directed by the Lab Coordinator Provide testing monitoring

for the students as they complete the National registry skills varifiaction test completed at the end of each semester Other Characteristics: Outgoing, Good Attitude, Leadership Skills and attention to detail Minimum Education/Experience: High School Diploma or equivalent (G.E.D.) Two years field experience or equivalent as a State of Ohio Certified Basic EMT , Intermediate EMT , or Paramedic Union Position: Job
