Rice University: Wiess School of Natural Sciences: Kinesiology Location Houston, Texas Open Date Sep 17, 2026 Description Tenured and Tenure-Track Faculty in Environmental Health Rice University invites applications for multiple tenured and tenure-track positions at the senior and junior levels in Environmental Health, with preferred focus areas in environmental and occupational epidemiology, environmental exposures

and sustainability, environmental risk assessment, toxicology, infectious diseases, global environmental health, and climate impacts on health, as well as the intersection of these areas with environmental justice. The anticipated start date is July 1, 2027. The ideal candidate(s) would bring an interdisciplinary approach, leveraging quantitative training in biostatistics, epidemiological modeling, spatial analytics

(GIS), or AI/data science, with experience analyzing clinical (e.g., electronic medical records), registry (e.g., Texas Cancer Registry), or administrative (e.g., American Community Survey) health data. Primary academic appointment will be in the Department of Kinesiology, ranked #1 nationally by the popular Niche™ rankings and home to Rice’s Public Health Sciences and Sports Medicine and Exercise Physiology

undergraduate degrees. These positions are part of an initiative to support interdisciplinary environmental health scholarship, including a new Climate + Health Excellence Center (FORECAST) funded by the Burroughs Wellcome Fund. They will come with robust joint appointments in Departments of BioSciences, Chemistry, and Earth, Environmental and Planetary Sciences as most appropriate. The successful candidate(s) will

be expected to establish a vibrant, externally funded research program, collaborate across disciplines, and teach at the undergraduate and graduate levels. We especially encourage applications from scientists addressing climate impacts on human health and infectious disease, and contributing to broad themes of sustainability or One Health principles. Potential to connect to exercise science, the department’s other

major intellectual theme, will also be explored, such as through interests in impacts of extreme heat, air quality, and toxicants on human physiology and performance. Candidates must have a PhD, DrPH, or equivalent degree in public health science or a relevant field of natural science or engineering and have completed the requirements for the degree by July 1, 2027.
