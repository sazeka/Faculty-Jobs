Department Head and Associate Professor/Professor of Accounting or Finance Accounting and Finance Full Time Permanent - Faculty Unclassified Department Head and Associate Professor/Professor, Department of Accounting & Finance Tommy & Victoria Baker School of Business The Citadel – Charleston, South Carolina The Tommy & Victoria Baker School of Business at The Citadel invites applications and nominations for the

position of Department Head and Associate Professor/Professor in the Department of Accounting & Finance. This is an outstanding opportunity to lead a thriving undergraduate department of approximately 300 students and 10 full-time faculty members within an AACSB-accredited business school committed to academic excellence, principled leadership, and continuous improvement. The successful candidate will serve as

Department Head while holding a faculty appointment in either Accounting or Finance at the rank of Associate Professor or Professor, commensurate with qualifications and experience. The Department Head reports directly to the Associate Dean of the Tommy & Victoria Baker School of Business. The Opportunity The Department of Accounting & Finance seeks a collaborative, visionary, and student-centered academic leader who

will build upon a strong foundation while helping shape the department's future. The successful candidate will join a collegial faculty dedicated to outstanding teaching, meaningful scholarship and professional engagement, and preparing graduates for successful careers in accounting, finance, and related professions. Working collaboratively with faculty, the Associate Dean, and other university leaders, the

Department Head will strengthen academic programs, recruit and mentor outstanding faculty, enhance student engagement and career readiness, foster innovation in teaching and learning, and cultivate relationships with alumni, employers, and the regional business community. This position offers the opportunity to lead through collaboration, develop both people and programs, and advance a department that values

excellence, integrity, continuous improvement, and shared governance. About the Department The Department of Accounting & Finance offers Bachelor of Science in Business Administration degree programs in Accounting and Finance and supports the Tommy & Victoria Baker School of Business through teaching in the Master of Business Administration program.
