college-age students to adult learners. The college offers associate of arts, associate of fine arts, associate of science and associate of applied science degrees, professional and skills certificates, and noncredit courses and programs, and workforce development training in a 14-county area in central Missouri with locations in Sedalia, Boonville, Clinton, Lake of the Ozarks, Whiteman Air Force Base, Jefferson City

Position Purpose: We are looking for qualified candidates to teach various classes within the Industrial, Manufacturing & Construction Technology Pathway during future semesters either online or at the main campus in Sedalia. each semester with no benefits. Programs under this category include Agriculture Mechanics, Automotive Technology, Construction Management, Engineering Design Technology, HVAC, Industrial Electrical Maintenance, Precision Machining, Technical Math, Applied Science, and Welding. Responsibilities will include:Deliver quality instruction that incorporates active learning and promotes student engagement; facilitate

Provide appropriate academic support and guidance to students and timely responses to student inquiries within 24

business hours.Meet academic reporting and final grade entry deadlines.Assess student learning through a variety of methods, including exams, quizzes, papers, and projects.
