Sussex County Community College is seeking a highly qualified, enthusiastic and student-focused Full Time Accounting and Business Faculty Instructor. This full-time tenure track Accounting and Business Faculty position is a teaching and discipline leadership role in the Professional Studies Department and collaborates with both the Dean of Faculty/Associate Vice President of Academic Affairs and the Department

members. This faculty member must have a strong demonstrated background and expertise in the accounting and business areas and will contribute to a program that includes degrees and certificates in Business Administration, Business Management, Accounting, Marketing, International Business, Entrepreneurship, Supervision, Communications, Supply Chain, and Economics. assigned. The faculty member will contribute to student success by advising students and providing accounting and business expertise.
