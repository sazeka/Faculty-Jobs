They are

envisioned to be equivalent to the tenure-track faculty position without requiring the responsibility of classroom teaching, or service and engagement, and without eligibility for tenure. Research Faculty are responsible for complying with all policies and procedures that relate to their employment and to their use of/access to university facilities and resources, both those now in effect and those adopted from time

to time by the University. Colleges (including schools/departments and centers/institutes housed in colleges) that report to a dean and Discovery Park District (DPD) centers/institutes that report to the Executive Vice President for Research may employ Research Faculty in accordance with this policy.
