Commercial Music (Voice).Union University is accredited by the National Association of Schools of Music. About Union University Founded in 1823, Union University is a private, coeducational liberal arts-based university offering bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral degrees, with a total enrollment of about 2,600. The main campus is located in Jackson, Tenn., with additional campuses in Memphis and Hendersonville. A

national leader among colleges and universities in promoting a Christian worldview and a Christ-centered approach to education, Union is consistently ranked among the nation’s premier Christian universities. Essential Job Duties Teaching responsibilities include primary and secondary applied voice for students in Music Education, Commercial Music, and Worship Leadership. Applied lessons for minors and elective voice

students. Additional teaching opportunities may include commercial vocal techniques, commercial vocal styles pedagogy, recording studio singing techniques, and vocal improvisation techniques. This position requires the ability to perform and teach various styles including Classical, Commercial, Gospel, Jazz, Musical Theatre, and Contemporary Worship music. The candidate should demonstrate ability in teaching and

modeling belt singing technique. The ability to accompany students on piano from written music and charts/Nashville numbers is desired. Opportunities to provide coaching, direction, and supervision for vocal ensembles may be available. Perform in Faculty Concerts and University ensembles as directed. Supervise Student Junior and Senior Recitals and Projects in voice. Other courses and additional duties may be

assigned predicated on the interests, qualifications, and experiences of the applicant as well as on the departmental needs.Additional responsibilities encompass service to the department, university, and community along with appropriate scholarly activities.
