Assistant Professor of Music & Assistant Band Director

Lincoln University (MO)  ·  Music & Assistant Band Director  ·  Jefferson City, MO
Assistant ProfessorMusic

Position summary

Job Summary: The Assistant Professor of Music/Assistant Band Director serves as a faculty member and administrative leader within the Division of Student Affairs and Division of Academic Affairs, supporting the artistic, educational, and operational excellence of Lincoln University's band program. Reporting to the Director of Bands, this position provides leadership in the instruction, administration, recruitment,

performance, and continued development of the University's band ensembles while contributing to the University's mission of student success, leadership development, and community engagement. The Assistant Professor/Assistant Band Director assists with all aspects of the Marching Musical Storm, concert bands, pep bands, jazz ensembles, athletic bands, and other musical groups as assigned. The position collaborates

with students, faculty, staff, alumni, community partners, and prospective students to cultivate an environment rooted in excellence, accountability, discipline, and pride. As a faculty member, the Assistant Professor teaches undergraduate music courses consistent with departmental needs while advising students, supporting assessment efforts, participating in institutional service, and contributing to curriculum

development. Within Student Affairs, the position plays an important role in fostering student engagement, belonging, retention, leadership development, and school spirit through musical performance and campus traditions. Through collaborative leadership, innovative recruitment strategies, and student-centered mentorship, the Assistant Professor/Assistant Band Director advances the University's commitment to

developing musicians, scholars, and leaders who positively represent Lincoln University throughout Missouri and beyond. Essential Job Functions: Assist the Director of Bands with the artistic, administrative, and operational leadership of all university band ensembles. Conduct rehearsals and performances for the Marching Musical Storm and other instrumental ensembles as assigned. Serve as primary conductor for

designated ensembles in the absence of the Director of Bands. Teach undergraduate music courses consistent with academic qualifications and departmental needs. Assist with planning, coordinating, and executing all marching band performances, athletic events, concerts, ceremonies, community performances, and university special events.
