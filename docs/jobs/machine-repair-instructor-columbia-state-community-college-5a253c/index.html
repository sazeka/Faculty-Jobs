Position Summary The Machine Repair Instructor is responsible for delivering daily classroom and laboratory instruction in support of a registered apprenticeship program for a large,

advanced manufacturing partner. Instruction is aligned with a structured Related Technical Instruction (RTI) curriculum and supported by faculty within the Engineering Systems Technology program. This role focuses on developing apprentice competencies in applied mathematics, Blueprint Reading and CAD Fundamentals, industrial maintenance, machining, and machine repair through applied, hands-on instruction and

theory-based learning.
