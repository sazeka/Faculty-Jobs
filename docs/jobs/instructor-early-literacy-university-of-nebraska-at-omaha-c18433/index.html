Posting Details Position Information Job Title Instructor - Early Literacy Department Teacher Education Essential Functions This instructor position provides instruction and facilitation support to the Teacher Education Department, specifically special projects related to the UNO Early Literacy Workshop. This position requires strong curriculum and instructional

experience; independent judgment; ability to prepare documents with minimal instruction; make decisions based on broad general guidelines related to project instruction and facilitation; micro and macro-level communication with private, state, and campus entities related to the project, and other responsibilities as assigned. Primary responsibilities include development and facilitation of curriculum associated with

a statewide literacy teacher training workshop/course offered through the Teacher Education Department: one-on-one contact with Project Director (faculty) to support facilitation and development of curriculum, train new facilitators around the state on curriculum model and implementation practices; problem solve to meet deadlines of project training materials and communication; Coordination and organization of

workshop schedules and statewide facilitator trainings; provide inclusive, teacher-centered adult learning opportunities in responsive cohort training model.This is a full-time, benefits-eligible nine (9) month position with an anticipated start date of August 17, 2026.
