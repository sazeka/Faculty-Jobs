Assistant or Associate Professor of Psychology 501242 Faculty Full-time Opening on: Aug 19 2026 Closing on: Oct 1 2026 College of Science & Engineering Application Deadline: 10/1/2026 Position Details: HEALTH PSYCHOLOGY FACULTY POSITION AVAILABLE This full-time faculty position is on-campus and in-person. The TCU Department of Psychology is hiring a tenure-track Assistant or Associate Professor beginning August 2027.

This position is part of a broader strategic initiative to build a nationally recognized research program focused on health and well-being. Primary consideration will be given to candidates who have completed a Ph.D. in any area of Psychology. We are particularly interested in health psychology research focused on addiction science, digital technologies and artificial intelligence (AI), intervention/implementation

science, aging, behavioral science/medicine, or biomedical approaches, but we welcome applications from all areas of psychology. Applicants are expected to complement and extend the existing strengths of the department and institutes, collaborate across the department and university, and help shape the future of health psychology at TCU. Applicants must have a well-developed funding trajectory aligned with external

funding mechanisms (NIH, NSF, DoD, state, or foundation money). At the Associate level, the candidate must have active, transferable funding and/or Notice of Award (NOA) and strong potential for continued support. The successful candidate will be expected to (1) teach relevant undergraduate and graduate coursework in their area of expertise; (2) maintain an active, externally-funded research program involving

undergraduate and graduate students; (3) collaborate with other faculty members within and external to TCU; and (4) provide service to the college and university.
