Assistant Professor of Practice in Dance (Professional Track)Apply now DescriptionThe Department of Theatre and Dance at The University of Texas at Austin invites applications for an Assistant Professor of Practice in Dance (non-tenure track). The successful candidate will have demonstrated expertise in ballet and the ability to build and teach curriculum in/across one or more of the following areas: contemporary

and/or traditional dance forms, somatic practices, performance and repertory, dance studies, choreography, and/or movement improvisation. The ideal applicant will capably teach all skill levels and confidently address multiple levels of ability within a single class. This position teaches courses for non-majors, dance majors (BFA and BA), and dance minors and contributes administration, expertise, and leadership

through university, college, department, and area service commitments. The department encourages balancing professional rigor with student-centered pedagogy consistent with the expectations of a public university. This position is part of the Dance Area, which offers an MFA in Dance, BA in Theatre and Dance, BFA with a Performance and Choreography emphasis, BFA with a Dance Education emphasis, and a Dance minor. The

Dance Area’s priority is to cultivate well-rounded artist citizens with a broad range of skills who can compete in and contribute to the shifting landscape of the performing arts and entertainment fields with agency and innovation. The faculty encourage students to develop concrete skills in dance with roots in live performance while simultaneously emphasizing the application of those skills to a variety of contexts

and disciplines. The Dance Area supports and is supported by programs in Acting, Directing and Playwriting, Drama and Theatre for Youth and Communities/Theatre Education, Live Design and Production, and Performance as Public Practice. This position requires relocation to Austin, TX. As an integral part of the thriving creative culture of Austin, Texas, the Department of Theatre and Dance offers an array of programs

to prepare artists, scholars, and teachers to make profound contributions to theatre and dance through engaging study, interdisciplinary collaboration, and exceptional performance experiences.
