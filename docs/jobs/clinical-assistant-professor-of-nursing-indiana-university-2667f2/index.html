Appointment Status Non-Tenure Track Department IU Southeast School of Nursing Location Southeast Position Summary Indiana University Southeast, one of eight campuses of Indiana University, is seeking dynamic and innovative educators to fill a faculty position in the School of Nursing. Located in historic New Albany, Indiana, our beautiful 180-acre campus is only 10 minutes from vibrant, downtown Louisville, Kentucky.

IU Southeast serves students enrolled in more than fifty undergraduate and graduate degree programs. IU Southeast is a student-centered and community engaged campus where faculty and students enjoy a small college environment alongside the many benefits of being connected to the larger, nationally ranked IU School of Nursing. Pre-licensure Baccalaureate, undergraduate, and RN-BSN programs at IU Southeast have an

outstanding reputation in the region and are accredited by the CCNE through 2030. MSN programs in nursing administration and nursing education are offered via an IU Regional Consortium and are accredited by the CCNE through 2035.We are excited to invite applications for a Clinical Assistant Professor to join our dynamic undergraduate nursing program. This full‑time, on‑site faculty role offers the opportunity to

shape the next generation of nurses through engaging clinical instruction, impactful classroom teaching, and innovative simulation‑based learning.Qualified candidates will hold an earned MSN and be eligible for RN licensure in Indiana.
