Adjunct Instructor of Nursing (Clinical or Didactic)

Position summary

University of West Alabama: Academic Affairs: College of Nursing Location University of West Alabama Open Date Apr 15, 2026 Description Assist faculty to: Develop, implement, and evaluate the program of learning within the framework of the mission of the University and the philosophy and goals of the College of Nursing. Plan, implement, and evaluate policies of the University and of the College of Nursing. Provide

instruction for students as assigned by the program or course coordinator(s) of the College of Nursing. Teach courses as assigned by the program coordinator(s) or the dean of the College of Nursing. Assist other faculty in selecting health care settings for nursing students’ clinical laboratory experiences. Coordinate and assume responsibility and accountability for clinical supervision of students. Meet all agency

requirements for clinical supervision, including, but not limited to, current CPR certification; negative TB skin testing or chest x-ray; drug and criminal background screening; Hepatitis B immunization; and liability insurance. Assist other faculty in the simulation or skills laboratory as assigned by the program or course coordinator. Professional: Maintain membership in professional organizations. Participate in

professional organization activities. Develop professionally through attendance at professional workshops or enrollment in formal education courses. Serve as a professional role model for peers and students. Qualifications Education: A minimum of a master’s degree in nursing is preferred.* Experience: A minimum of one year of clinical experience. Licensure: Current unencumbered Alabama RN licensure. * In exceptional

cases, an associate or baccalaureate degree is acceptable for temporary adjunct clinical instruction. Terms and Conditions of Employment: The terms and conditions of employment are cited in the annual contract between the University and the employed faculty member. The position is based upon twelve months of employment for each academic year. Application Instructions To Apply: When you select "Apply Now," you will be

prompted to provide the following: Cover Letter. A current resume or CV. Transcripts of all college work. Unofficial transcripts will suffice for application. Official transcripts must be submitted immediately upon employment. Email and phone number of three professional references. Reference letters may be requested later.
