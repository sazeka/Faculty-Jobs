Job Title: Physician – Internal Medicine, Hospital Medicine, Hospital Medicine (Open Rank/Track Faculty) Department: The Ohio State University College of Medicine and the Wexner Medical Center seek Hospitalist to join the Department of Internal Medicine Academic rank and track commensurate with

academic record and experience. Position Overview Physician serving as a member of the Division of Hospital Medicine, within the Department of Internal Medicine; directs and provides direct and indirect patient care services in an inpatient setting; interviews, obtains and reviews medical history of patients; performs physical examinations to assess medical needs; orders procedures, diagnostic testing, and other

ancillary services; determines, prescribes and implements treatment plans and/or medications required; documents all services and instructions provided in medical record; educates patients and promotes wellness, prevention and early detection by providing materials, resources and services to patient and their families; directs and participates in quality assessment and improvement activities; serves as preceptor and

provides bedside training of residents, fellows, medical students, undergraduate & graduate students, nursing staff, and other allied medical professionals; ensures continuing medical education requirements are met; participates in department, college, medical center and university committees and other service activities. Department/Division Overview The Division of Hospital Medicine at The Ohio State University
