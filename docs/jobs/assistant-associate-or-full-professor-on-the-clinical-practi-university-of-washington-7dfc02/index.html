Assistant, Associate, or full Professor, on the Clinical Practice Track, General Cardiologist, VA Puget Sound Health Care System Position Overview School / Campus / College: School of Medicine Organization: Medicine, Cardiology Title: Assistant, Associate, or full Professor, on the Clinical Practice Track, General Cardiologist, VA Puget Sound Health Care System Position Details Position Description The Division of

Cardiology, Department of Medicine at the University of Washington invites applications for a general cardiologist in a clinical practice faculty position at the rank of Associate, or Professor of Clinical Practice. We seek applicants who demonstrate commitment to excellence in teaching and service in a diverse and inclusive academic community. The anticipated start date is February 1, 2027. Specific rank will be

commensurate with experience and qualifications as generally described in UW’s Faculty Code Section 24-34. We seek applicants who demonstrate commitment to excellence in research, teaching, and service in a diverse and inclusive academic community. The successful candidate will join our faculty on a full-time basis for a twelve-month service period (July 1- June 30). This position is paid directly by VA Puget Sound

Health Care System (VAPSHCS) and receives no UW compensation. Please refer to VA Puget Sound Health Care System (VAPSHCS) for information about compensation for this position. This position is for a highly motivated general cardiologist for the Puget Sound Veterans Association Medical Center to become director for the Medical Specialty Care Oversight Taskforce (MASCOT) program. The primary objective of this position

is to lead a team in creating a hub and spoke program aimed at establishing a cardiology network across Washington, Oregon, Idaho, and Alaska, specifically supporting veterans who live in areas with limited or no access to cardiology care. Successful candidates will have a keen interest in growing a strong telehealth presence in the Veterans Integrated Service Network (VISN 20) and developing key relationships with

partners to build the region’s cardiology infrastructure. Candidates will be expected to have a track record of success in program building and managing personnel, and be committed to the VA’s mission, vision, and value of providing the highest excellence of care to our veterans.
