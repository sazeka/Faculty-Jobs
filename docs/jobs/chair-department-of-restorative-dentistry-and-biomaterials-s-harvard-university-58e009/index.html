We are dedicated to creating a welcoming environment where everyone can thrive to advance education, discovery and clinical innovation.OPPORTUNITY AND RESPONSIBILITIESHarvard School of Dental Medicine (HSDM) invites nominations and applications for

the next Chair of the Department of Restorative Dentistry and Biomaterials Sciences. This is a full-time 12-month appointment at the rank of associate professor or full professor, based upon credentials and qualifications. In partnership with the Harvard John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS), the ideal candidate will be eligible for a joint faculty appointment in the Department of

Bioengineering. The Chair will lead strategic planning in alignment with the School’s vision and mission. The successful candidate will maintain and advance a high-impact research program while providing strategic leadership for the department’s broader research portfolio, spanning preventive and restorative dentistry, digital technologies, bioengineering, artificial intelligence, and regenerative, implant, and

biomaterials sciences. The Chair will contribute to teaching across dental and medical students, graduate students, and postdoctoral fellows, and will serve as a core member of the HSDM Leadership Team. The Chair will also provide mentorship, career development guidance, and broad faculty support. The successful candidate should have academic experience, a national reputation for excellence, documented success in

strategic change leadership, and a strong record of scholarship and research. The Chair provides leadership for the vision, strategic direction, and long-term growth of the department in alignment with the mission and priorities of the HSDM and SEAS.
