Job Description: The Academic & Faculty Affairs Support Coordinator provides comprehensive administrative, operational, and programmatic support for faculty affairs, academic operations, clinical education, and instructional programs within the College of Nursing. This position serves as a central point

of coordination among faculty, academic leadership, students, clinical partners, and university stakeholders to ensure the effective delivery of academic programs, seamless clinical placement processes, and compliance with college, university, regulatory, and accreditation standards. The coordinator supports a broad range of academic and faculty-related functions, including faculty workload administration, course and

instructional scheduling, clinical education coordination, faculty appointment and evaluation processes, recognition and promotion activities, accreditation support, and data management. The role also contributes to continuous quality improvement initiatives and operational excellence that advance the College's educational, clinical, and strategic mission.
