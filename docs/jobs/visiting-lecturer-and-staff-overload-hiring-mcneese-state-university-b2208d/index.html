Office of Human Resources and Student Employment Visiting Lecturer and Staff Overload Hiring The University complies with the University of Louisiana System Board of Supervisors Policy and Procedures Memorandum FS.III.II.B.C.D-1. The steps below outline the process for hiring new and returning visiting lecturers (VLs) and unclassified administrative staff who are hired in adjunct positions (i.e., staff teaching

overload). This is not the process for faculty overload. The hiring department must review the following policies prior to beginning either process: Assessment and Certification of Faculty English Proficiency Policy Faculty Appointment and Credentialing Policy The hiring department should also refer to the detailed instructions for completing the Letter of Intent to Employ (VL/Staff OL) form in Dynamic Forms:

Instructions for Letter of Intent to Employ VL & Staff OL Dynamic Form Download Notice of Deadline Effective immediately, the Office of Human Resource and Student Employment, in conjunction with the Office of Institutional Research and Effectiveness, is imposing a deadline of two weeks prior to the start of the appointment term for the Letter of Intent to Employ (VL/Staff OL) form to be submitted by the preparer in

Dynamic Forms. This deadline is necessary to permit enough time for the appointment to be approved and the appointee to be set up in Banner and given access to email, Canvas, etc. Note that this deadline applies regardless of whether the appointee is teaching courses offered in a regular term or part of term, and the deadline will be enforced by either the Office of Human Resources and Student Employment or the

Office of Institutional Research and Effectiveness through the form’s approval workflow. Exceptions to this deadline will be granted on a case-by-case basis and only after the hiring department provides adequate justification via a comment entered by the preparer on the Letter of Intent to Employ (VL/Staff OL) form.
