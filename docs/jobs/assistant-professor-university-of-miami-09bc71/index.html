Studies and Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer CenterThe University of Miami School of Nursing and Health Studies and the Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center invite applications for a tenure-track faculty position. We seek a candidate interested in joining a transformative, collaborative initiative between the cancer center and the nursing school, with research interests and expertise in cancer survivorship and

population sciences.We are particularly interested in a highly collaborative team scientist with expertise in one or more of the following areas: cancer and aging; dissemination and implementation science; and AI/ML or big data analytics. These areas would complement existing and developing research programs in symptom and toxicity measurement and management; patient-reported outcomes (PROs); technology-supported

behavioral and psychosocial interventions; biobehavioral mechanisms in cancer control; electronic health records (EHR), bioinformatics, and value-based models of cancer survivorship care; health disparities in specific survivorship subgroups (e.g., LGBT, Hispanic, elderly populations); cardiometabolic health in cancer survivors; epidemiological cohort studies; and lifestyle medicine and translational science

research.The School of Nursing and Health Studies and Sylvester, an NCI-designated cancer center, provide an excellent platform for research and education, with a broad and diverse scientific portfolio spanning cancer prevention, control, survivorship, and population sciences.This position will be housed in the School of Nursing, which is closely aligned with the cancer center’s mission and the Sylvester

Comprehensive Cancer Center, led by nationally and internationally recognized experts in nursing and cancer control sciences.
