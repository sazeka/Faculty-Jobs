Position summary

Classification Fixed Term (Fixed Term) Minimum Pay $0.00 Compensation will be commensurate with the level of the position, education, and experience. JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY Music Instructor: MCC’s Continuing Education Division is seeking enthusiastic, knowledgeable, and experienced Music Instructors to provide engaging and enriching learning opportunities for members of our community. This position will offer

various teaching opportunities throughout the year and will be responsible for developing and delivering accessible, engaging, and practical non-credit music courses for learners of varying ages and experience levels. Music Instructors may teach courses in areas such as instrumental or vocal instruction, music appreciation, songwriting, music theory, performance, composition, or other music-related topics.

Instructors will create a supportive and inclusive learning environment that encourages participants to explore their musical interests, develop new skills, build confidence, and enhance their personal enjoyment and appreciation of music. Preferred Qualifications Demonstrated experience teaching music in a classroom, private lesson, workshop, community education, or other instructional setting. Strong knowledge and

experience in one or more areas of music, such as vocal or instrumental performance, music theory, composition, songwriting, or music appreciation. Proficiency in one or more musical instruments and/or vocal performance. Experience developing engaging, hands-on, and accessible learning experiences for students with varying skill levels. Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to explain

musical concepts clearly and effectively. Experience working with adult learners and diverse community audiences. Formal education, professional training, certifications, or demonstrated expertise in music or a related field preferred. Active involvement in the local or professional music community is a plus. Ability to create a welcoming, inclusive, and supportive learning environment. Commitment to providing

practical, enjoyable, and engaging educational experiences that encourage lifelong learning and personal enrichment. This position will have random teaching opportunities throughout the year. MCC holds classes at multiple locations throughout the Metro area including Fremont and Blair. Most classes are held weeknights and weekends.
