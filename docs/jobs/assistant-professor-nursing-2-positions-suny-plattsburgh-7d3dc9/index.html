Academic Department Nursing About The Position: Expected to begin in Fall 2026, these 10-month, tenure-track positions are unique opportunities to join a faculty who encourages educational mobility and supports a mission that emphasizes excellence in teaching, scholarship, and service. Duties are expected to include teaching courses such as Nursing Foundations, Health Assessment, OB, Pediatrics, and/or Care of

Adults. Clinical supervision of students in a hospital and/or lab setting is also included.
