The Learning Lab Instructor shoulders the pivotal role of guiding adult

learners through GED preparation, high school completion, and remedial education. Their mission is to empower students to unlock their full potential by delivering engaging coursework and fostering a creative learning environment. In the multifaceted role of a Learning Lab Instructor, key responsibilities encompass facilitating student enrollment by conducting transcript evaluations and aiding in the selection of

appropriate coursework. They diligently oversee and support students in their journey through GED preparation and adult education, fostering both academic and social growth. Maintaining meticulous records, they ensure the confidentiality and accuracy of student progress documentation. Through comprehensive assessment and testing, they gauge student proficiency in relevant areas and provide timely interventions for

those who require additional support. Additionally, they guide students towards ancillary opportunities such as work-study programs, equipping them with the necessary information to make informed decisions about post-secondary education and potential scholarships. When necessary, they adeptly refer students to external agencies for specialized assistance, ensuring holistic support throughout their educational
