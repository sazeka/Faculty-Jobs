This is a 1.0 FTE faculty position on the LCOM Clinical

Scholar Pathway in the Department of Neurological Sciences with attending privileges at CVPH. CVPH is a network hospital in the North Country of Upstate New York located around 1 hour from the Larner College of Medicine in Burlington, Vermont.
