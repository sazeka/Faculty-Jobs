Saint Anselm College  ·  Department of Philosophy  ·  Manchester, NH

Position summary

The Department of Philosophy at Saint Anselm College, a Roman Catholic liberal arts college in the Benedictine tradition in Manchester, NH invites applications for a three-year Assistant Professor position to begin August 2027. Area of Specialization (AOS): Ethics with a secondary specialization in Technology Ethics, Business Ethics, or Bioethics; Area Of Concentation (AOC): Metaphysics and Epistemology. The

successful candidate will have a sympathy for and understanding of the Catholic philosophical tradition, will be grounded in the history of philosophy, will be able to teach PH 205: Nature and the Human Person, and will teach other courses throughout the curriculum that fulfill the two philosophy core learning outcomes: theoretical and ethical reasoning. The successful candidate will also have demonstrated engagement

with Ethics-related programming beyond the classroom and will desire to take the lead on Ethics initiatives on and off campus. Interested applicants should submit a letter of interest, curriculum vitae, evidence of teaching ability (including sample syllabi and a statement of teaching philosophy), evidence of research potential (samples of scholarship, articles, or conference papers), and academic transcripts to the

attention of Dr. Kyle Hubbard, Search Committee Chair, via the college website . Three letters of recommendation are required and should be submitted in PDF format via email to the department faculty assistant, Kate Jensik at kjensik@anselm.edu . Applications should be received by December 15, 2026 for full consideration. Required Skills and Experience: A Ph.D. in Philosophy by May 2027 is required. Candidates should

demonstrate a strong commitment to excellence in teaching at the undergraduate level as well as scholarly research. Applicants must be authorized to work for any employer in the United States without current or future visa sponsorship (e.g., H-1B visa). Inclusivity Statement: In order to realize our mission as a Catholic, Benedictine, liberal arts college, Saint Anselm College is committed to sustaining an

intentionally inclusive environment that will benefit all constituencies. In particular, the college is committed to providing students with the richest opportunities to learn, both in the classroom and within the environment of the college community as a whole.
