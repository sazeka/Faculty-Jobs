POSITION: Computer Information Systems Instructor, Full-Time LOCATION: Swainsboro Campus POSITION DESCRIPTION: Full-time twelve (12) month faculty position that requires teaching responsibilities for credit courses within the Information Technology Professional and Cybersecurity program areas. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS: Master’s degree in Computer Information Systems or Computer Science, or master’s degree in a related

field with 18 graduate semester hours in the teaching discipline from an accredited institution. Must have three years of in-field experience within the last 7 years. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS: · Prior teaching experience (Cisco Network Academy teaching experience desired) SALARY/BENEFITS: Salary is to be commensurate with education and work experience.
