Description Benefits Questions Position Announcement The Department of Health Care Diagnostics and Therapeutics at Utah Tech University (UT) in St. George, Utah, invites applicants for a full-time professional-track Instructor of the Practice or tenure-track Assistant Professor of Paramedicine faculty position, to begin Spring Semester 2027. The Paramedic Program is located at the University's Taylor Health Sciences

building and is designed to prepare students for career opportunities in pre-hospital emergency care, such as ambulance, fire department, search and rescue, law enforcement, and volunteer service. Information about the programs can be found online at https://health.utahtech.edu/emergency-medical-services/. Responsibilities Provide Paramedic course instruction and clinical supervision.Assist in the development of

curriculum for the paramedic program, including course design and materials.Ensure the program meets all regulatory and CoAEMSP accreditation requirements.Assist in recruitment and training of program faculty and staff to ensure effective delivery of the program curriculum.Monitor student progress, provide feedback and support, and facilitate remediation as needed.Collaborate with other educational institutions,

healthcare providers, and industry partners to maintain current knowledge and best practices in the field of emergency medical services.Collaborate with other UT programs, faculty, and staff to implement interprofessional experiences among programs and students.Establish and maintain relationships with regulatory agencies, accrediting bodies, and other stakeholders to ensure compliance with all regulations and

standards, including those related to CoAEMSP accreditation.Prepare for and participate in CoAEMSP accreditation site visits and assessments, and implement changes as directed by program director.Ensure that program evaluations and assessments are completed on a regular basis, and implement changes as needed.Maintain accurate student records and ensure that they are kept confidential.Participate in community outreach
