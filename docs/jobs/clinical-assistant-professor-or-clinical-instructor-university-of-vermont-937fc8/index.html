Advertising Copy Position Overview The Department of Nursing in the College of Nursing and Health Sciences (CNHS) at the University of Vermont (UVM) invites applications for two full-time, 9- month clinical faculty position at the rank of of Clinical Instructor or,

Clinical Assistant or Associate Professor. The primary responsibility of this position is to deliver high quality instruction in the undergraduate and/or graduate Nursing programs. Additional responsibilities include academic advising and evaluation of student learning outcomes, contributing to service at the program, college, and/or university levels, and maintaining clinical practice as appropriate. Both positions

will support UVM’s commitment to high-quality, student-centered education. Required Qualifications Minimum of Master’s Degree in Nursing or Masters in progress Demonstrated competence in clinical practice or specialization by attaining national nursing specialty certification and/or advanced practice licensure.
