Department of Computer Science in Mercer University’s College of Liberal Arts and Sciences is accepting applications for a tenure track Assistant Professor position. Teaching responsibilities can include undergraduate courses in computer science, data science, information

technology, and cybersecurity. Occasionally, teaching responsibilities could include graduate courses in Software Systems, or Cybersecurity. involved are expected. Qualifications : Applicants should hold a PhD in computer science or a closely related field from an accredited college/university. Applicants who are ABD will be considered at the rank of Instructor until completion of the PhD; however, the PhD must be completed no later than December 31, 2027.
