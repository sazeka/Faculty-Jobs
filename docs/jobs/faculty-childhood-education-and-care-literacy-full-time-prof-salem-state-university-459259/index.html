Faculty, Childhood Education and Care (Literacy), Full-Time, Professor of Practice 492985 443 - Childhood Education and Care Faculty: Non-Tenured Not On Track Professor of Practice On-site $80,000-$85,000 (for the rank of assistant professor) Overview The Professor of Practice, Literacy is a non-tenure track faculty position focused on strengthening literacy instruction and clinical practice within educator

preparation programs at Salem State. In addition to teaching literacy courses, this role centers on supervising teacher candidates, training and coaching other supervisors, and ensuring high-quality practicum experiences are aligned with state standards, with a particular emphasis on literacy across Early Childhood, Elementary, and Moderate Disabilities (PK–8) licensure programs. This position begins in Fall 2027.

Salem State University is where scholarship meets purpose.
