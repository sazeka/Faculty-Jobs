Position summary

Position Title: Assistant Professor of Science Education Compensation: $65,000 - $72,000 Department: Biology Start Date: 08/16/2027 About this opportunity: The Ball State University Department of Biology, in collaboration with the STEM Education Center, is seeking applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor position in Science Education beginning August 2027. This is a full-time, on-campus, nine-month,

benefit-eligible position offering a competitive salary. Employer will consider sponsorship. What you'll do and what you will bring: Position Function: The successful candidate will teach undergraduate courses within science education, including secondary and elementary methods courses as well as a biology content course for elementary educators. There may be opportunities for teaching other undergraduate and

graduate courses, supervising student teachers, or supporting programs in local school districts through our professional development school relationships. The successful candidate will also develop an externally funded, science education research program, ideally engaging undergraduate and graduate students while complementing and collaborating with current faculty in the Department of Biology and the STEM Education

Center. The strongest candidates will bring accomplished K-12 experience, engagement with local schools, and a record of peer-reviewed publications supporting a well-defined science education research program with strong potential for external funding. Duties and Responsibilities: Will teach undergraduate courses in science education for secondary and/or elementary teachers. The teaching assignment may include:

Introduction to Science Teaching, Methods of Secondary Science Teaching, Curriculum & Research, and Using Science Methods and Materials. Methods courses are paired with field experiences, and address planning, instruction, and assessment in the science classroom, among other topics. Typical load is 3 courses (9 credit hours) per semester. Department, Facilities, and Support The Department of Biology comprises 30

full-time faculty, including multiple faculty focused on science education, and offers the BS in Biology, the MS in Biology, and the PhD in Environmental Sciences. The Ball State STEM Education Center is the home of the interdisciplinary Teaching Major in Science, with concentrations in middle school science, chemistry, Earth/space science, life science, and physics.
