Location ST. LOUIS, MO 63110&#xa;&#xa; Position Summary Postdoctoral Research Associate positions are available in the Shmuylovich Laboratory at WashU Medicine. Our lab develops and translates novel optical imaging technologies for noninvasive diagnosis and monitoring of human disease, with a particular focus on skin imaging across the full spectrum of skin pigmentation. We combine biomedical optics, multispectral

and infrared imaging, spectroscopy, image processing, computational analysis, and clinical investigation to develop technologies that can improve diagnostic accuracy and reduce disparities in care. Current projects span visible, near-infrared, and short-wave infrared imaging and include applications in dermatology, vascular disease, tissue injury, and physiologic monitoring. The laboratory works at the intersection

of engineering, physics, computation, and clinical medicine, with opportunities to participate in device development, optical system design, image and signal analysis, translational studies, and human-subject research. Postdoctoral researchers will have the opportunity to lead independent projects while working closely with a multidisciplinary team of clinicians, engineers, physicists, and collaborators across WashU

and other institutions. We are particularly interested in creative and highly motivated scientists who enjoy solving technically challenging problems with a clear path toward clinical translation. Trainees will be encouraged to develop their own research ideas, publish their work, present at national meetings, and pursue independent funding and career-development opportunities. The Shmuylovich Laboratory is based

within the Department of Dermatology at WashU Medicine in St. Louis and benefits from the institution’s extensive resources in biomedical engineering, imaging, clinical research, and translational medicine.
