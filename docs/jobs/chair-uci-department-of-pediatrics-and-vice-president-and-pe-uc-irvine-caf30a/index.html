Position description Role Summary UCI School of Medicine and Rady Children's Health Orange County (RCHOC) seek a nationally recognized physician leader to serve simultaneously as Chair of the Department of Pediatrics at UCI and Vice President & Pediatrician-in-Chief (PIC) at RCHOC. This dual academic-clinical appointment carries responsibility for the strategic

direction of one of the region's leading academic pediatric departments and for its full integration with a premier children's hospital. As part of the Susan and Henry Samueli College of Health Sciences, the UCI School of Medicine advances medical knowledge and clinical practice through its tripartite mission to Discover, Teach, and Heal. The Department of Pediatrics is home to a community of faculty that includes

over 400 UCI and RCHOC physicians and scientists organized across 20 divisions and centers. The Department spans the full spectrum of pediatric disease, bringing academic scientists and clinicians together to generate new knowledge and translate it into improved outcomes for children. The Department is home to several nationally recognized academic and clinical programs, including the Center for Autism &

Neurodevelopmental Disorders, the Conte Center on Brain Programming in Adolescent Vulnerabilities, the Research Center for Exercise Medicine and Sleep (RCEMS), and the UCI Health Pediatric Gender Diversity Program.
