Na covers music ranging from Baroque to Contemporary with her powerful interpretations and vibrant stage presence as a soloist and guest artist. She made her Carnegie Hall debut as the

in Vocal Performance and Literature from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. She teaches music at Bunker Hill Community College and voice at Northeastern University.
