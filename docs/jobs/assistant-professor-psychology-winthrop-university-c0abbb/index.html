Position Details Position Information Winthrop Position Title Assistant Professor, Psychology Job Description The Department of Psychology at Winthrop

University invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor position specializing in developmental psychology or a closely related area. The successful candidate will teach, supervise, and advise psychology students while maintaining a student-centered research program.Specializations may include aging, adulthood, adolescence, childhood or development across the lifespan. We’re particularly interested in

candidates whose teaching and scholarship will expand the department’s developmental psychology offerings and support students pursuing careers or graduate study in fields such as gerontology, healthcare, behavioral health, counseling, education, child life, and occupational therapy. Position Responsibilities Teach undergraduate courses across the discipline that include introductory psychology, research methods, and

statistics for psychology majors. Teach additional undergraduate psychology courses including developmental psychology and other courses within the candidate’s area of expertise Establish a vibrant research program that involves undergraduates Support the program by engaging in curriculum design and student advising Engage in professional stewardship across the campus to improve the overall Winthrop community About
