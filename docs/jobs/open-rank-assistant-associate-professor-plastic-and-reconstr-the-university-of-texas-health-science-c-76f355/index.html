Position summary

Open Rank (Assistant/Associate Professor) – Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Division of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery UT Health San Antonio The Department of Surgery in the Joe R. and Teresa Lozano School of Medicine at UT Health San Antonio is seeking a clinically focused plastic surgeon to join our busy and growing academic practice. This full-time faculty position is available at the Assistant or Associate

Professor level (non-tenure track), depending on experience and qualifications. Position Overview We are looking for a surgeon with strong clinical training and a commitment to delivering high-quality patient care in both reconstructive and aesthetic surgery. The ideal candidate will have completed an approved microsurgery fellowship, be board certified or board eligible in plastic surgery, and be able to obtain a

full Texas medical license. The position includes active participation in a busy elective surgical practice and will involve hand surgery and craniofacial trauma call. The role is ideal for a candidate interested in working in a collaborative, multidisciplinary academic environment with opportunities for hands-on teaching and clinical mentorship of residents and fellows. The position includes opportunities in

oncologic reconstruction, as well as general elective reconstructive and aesthetic surgery. The successful candidate will be able to develop programs at expanding clinical sites, including the new Multispecialty Research Hospital and Kyle Seale Parkway Ambulatory Surgical Center as well as new University Hospital sites including Retama Parkway and in South San Antonio Responsibilities • Provide comprehensive clinical

care in general plastic surgery, including microsurgical reconstruction, aesthetic surgery, and trauma-related procedures. • Participate in hand surgery and craniofacial trauma call rotations as part of a shared faculty schedule. • Serve as a clinical educator for residents and medical students in both inpatient and outpatient settings. • Collaborate with faculty across surgical and medical specialties to ensure

optimal patient outcomes. • Engage in quality improvement, practice development, and patient safety initiatives.
