Advertising Copy The Robert Larner, M.D., College of Medicine (LCOM) at the University of Vermont (UVM) seeks an accomplished, engaging and visionary physician leader with a record of significant contributions in academic medicine to serve as its next Chair of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine. The incoming Chair

will also serve as the Chair of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine for University of Vermont Health (UVM Health), which provides pathology and laboratory services across Vermont and Northern New York. The LCOM collaborates closely with the University of Vermont Medical Center (UVMMC), the state’s only tertiary academic medical center. Together, they offer leading clinical, research, advocacy and educational programs.
