Associate or Full Professor in Advertising Apply now Job no: 541227 Work type: Tenure-Track Faculty Location: Main Campus (Gainesville, FL) Categories: Communications/Public Relations/Marketing, Education/Training/Instructional Design Department:23020000 - CJC-ADVERTISING Classification Title: ASO PROF Classification Minimum Requirements: Applicants must have a Ph.D. or be on track to have one by October 15, 2026, in

advertising, communication, marketing, information science, human-computer interaction, computer science, data analytics, or related field. A background teaching advertising classes at the undergraduate or graduate level and some professional experience is desirable. Job Description: The Opportunity The Department of Advertising at the University of Florida College of Journalism and Communications invites

applications for a full-time, nine-month scholar at the rank of Associate or Full Professor in Advertising. Position Description The Department of Advertising seeks to hire an associate or full professor engaged in scholarly research and grant acquisitions in the areas of health communication, science communication, environmental communication, or risk communication. Scholars should emphasize mediated or mass

communication aspects of these specialties. The successful candidate has built a national or international reputation, via an established research record with publications in top-tier journals and success in securing extramural funding. They are also expected to mentor junior faculty, contribute to the department’s leadership, advance the research agenda, and foster collaborations across the College and University

and with other national or international institutions. In addition to scholarship, the ideal candidate will bring extensive experience in teaching and mentoring undergraduate and graduate students, especially doctoral students, on topics related to the faculty member’s expertise. We seek candidates that can teach undergraduate advertising courses and graduate-level mass communication courses. Preferably, they will

demonstrate competency in teaching one or more courses from the core curriculum for the advertising major, especially courses in advertising strategy and advertising campaigns. Core courses for the undergraduate major include but are not limited to Principles of Advertising, Advertising Strategy, Copy and Visualization, Digital Insights, Media Planning, and Advertising Campaigns.
