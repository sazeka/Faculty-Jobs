Scientist for a two-year position supported by a collaborative National Science Foundation grant. The successful candidate will join the P-MixSwitch project, a collaboration between Susquehanna University and the University of Arizona, investigating the physiological and genetic mechanisms that enable small marine mixoplankton to switch between photosynthetic and predatory lifestyles.The Postdoctoral Scientist will

work with investigators and research personnel at both institutions in an interdisciplinary research environment integrating microbial ecology, physiology, molecular biology, and bioinformatics. The position will involve laboratory experiments, molecular and transcriptomic analyses, bioinformatics, manuscript preparation, conference presentations, and mentoring undergraduate researchers. This position is defined as

an exempt position under FLSA regulations. transcriptomic datasets using bioinformatics approaches.Contribute to manuscript preparation and presentation of research findings at scientific conferences.Assist in mentoring undergraduate researchers.
