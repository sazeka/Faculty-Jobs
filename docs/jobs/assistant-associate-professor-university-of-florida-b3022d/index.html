FLORIDA LEVIN COLLEGE OF LAW seeks to hire several professors, across a variety of fields, as part of the College’s pursuit of excellence in legal education and scholarship. The University of Florida, located in Gainesville, FL, is among the nation’s leading public research institutions and the flagship university of the third largest state. The Levin College of Law seeks highly qualified candidates for tenure-track

positions. The Appointments Committee welcomes applications from entry-level, pre-tenure lateral, and recently tenured candidates writing in all areas of law. Successful candidates will have publication records, strong scholarly potential, commitment to excellence in teaching, and enthusiasm for creating a welcoming environment for all students. Candidates must also have a JD or equivalent academic degree. In

reviewing applications, the Appointments Committee will consider long-term teaching needs in required and large enrollment classes, including but not limited to property and taxation (corporate, partnership, and/or international). Expected Salary: Commensurate upon experience Required Qualifications: Candidates must have a JD or equivalent academic degree Preferred: Special Instructions to Applicants: To apply for a

position or to request further information, applicants should contact the Appointments Committee’s chair Annie Brett at brett@law.ufl.edu. Application materials should include a cover letter, a resume with at least three references, a list of publications, and up to three pieces of scholarly work. Materials should also be uploaded at http://jobs.ufl.edu. This position is open until filled. Review of application
