Position End Date (if temporary) Posting Open Date 10/19/2026 Posting Close Date 04/30/2027 Open Until Filled No Desired Start Date 08/01/2027 Job Summary Position Description The Department of Neuroscience at The University of Texas at Dallas seeks to hire two tenure-system faculty members (open rank) with expertise in receptor pharmacology. The goal of this hiring initiative is to develop an internationally

recognized neurotherapeutics research and educational program at UT Dallas that leverages and extends existing University expertise in pain, migraine, and peripheral neuropathy research as well as a growing emphasis in affective disorders, addiction, and/or neurodegeneration. The ideal candidate would conduct highly collaborative receptor pharmacology (which can include GPCRs, ligand-gated ion channels, RTKs, or

other receptors) and drug development research in areas of focus aligned with departmental strengths in pain, peripheral neuropathy, migraine, affective disorders, addiction, and/or neurodegeneration. The successful candidate will have a documented history of scholarly work in pharmacology, neuroscience, and/or drug development and will have experience in advancing therapeutics to development stage. Faculty will be

expected to collaborate directly with UT Dallas faculty working on identifying targets for therapeutic development but external collaborations are also encouraged. Faculty would be expected to teach undergraduate and graduate courses related to pharmacology, neuroscience, and/or development of therapeutics. Faculty will have an opportunity to play a key role in entrepreneurial activities emerging from the Center for

Advanced Pain Studies and from other Departments and Centers at UT Dallas. The appointment commences for the fall 2027 semester. Qualifications Minimum Education and Experience: A PhD in pharmacology, neuroscience, or a related discipline is required prior to employment. Candidates should demonstrate commitment to excellence in research, teaching, and service and evidence of an innovative and productive research
