Cybersecurity Researcher- Adjunct

University of New Hampshire System  ·  Durham, NH
Cybersecurity

Position summary

USNH Employees should apply within Workday through the Jobs Hub app The Adjunct Cybersecurity Researcher will support research activities associated with an NIH R15-funded project focused on explainable artificial intelligence and intrusion detection for healthcare cybersecurity. The position will contribute to the design, implementation, evaluation, and documentation of machine learning and cybersecurity research

conducted within the Department of Computer Science. The researcher will work closely with the Principal Investigator and undergraduate research students, with responsibilities including machine learning model development, cybersecurity data analysis, experimental implementation, research documentation, and support for scholarly publications and presentations. This position is not considered essential staff.

Duties/Responsibilities: Machine Learning and Cybersecurity Research (60% ) Develop, implement, and evaluate machine learning and explainable AI models for healthcare cybersecurity applications. Conduct experiments involving intrusion detection, cybersecurity datasets, and related analytical methods. Perform data preprocessing, model evaluation, performance analysis, and interpretation of experimental results. Assist

with development and maintenance of research software and experimental workflows. Research Documentation and Dissemination (25%) Document research methods, experimental procedures, code, and results. Assist in preparing figures, tables, technical reports, manuscripts, posters, and conference presentations. Conduct literature reviews and contribute to the interpretation and communication of research findings.

Undergraduate Research Support and Project Coordination (15%) Assist the Principal Investigator in mentoring undergraduate students participating in the NIH R15 research project. Provide technical guidance on programming, machine learning, cybersecurity experiments, and research documentation. Participate in research meetings and coordinate project activities as needed. Requirements: Minimum Acceptable Education &

Experience : Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Computer Engineering, or a closely related field. Coursework, research, or project experience in machine learning, cybersecurity, programming, or data analysis is required Equivalent relevant experience may be considered. Knowledge, Skills & Abilities: Proficiency in programming, preferably Python. Knowledge of machine learning, data
