Assistant Professor and Lab Manager, EMS Programs

Santa Fe College  ·  Kirkpatrick Center - Gainesville, FL
Assistant ProfessorEmergency Medical Services

Position summary

Compensation $62,882.00 Annual Job Description Summary The application deadline for this position is 11:59pm EST on November 29, 2026. The Emergency Medical Services A.S. Program prepares students for promotions and advancement within the EMS industry. Students who are a state of the Florida certified as EMT and Paramedic, and graduated from a Florida accredited EMS Training Program, have already fulfilled the core

requirements and can earn an A.S. degree in as little as one or two semesters. With an A.S. degree in Emergency Medical Services, it is possible to move beyond providing care in the field and seek a position in management with a public safety agency or healthcare provider. Under general supervision, the Assistant Professor and Lab Manager for Emergency Medical Services (EMS) Programs, will develop, prepare, and teach

a variety of EMS courses, while also managing the EMS skills lab. Instructional activities will encompass more than 50 percent of total duty time. The Assistant Professor must have the flexibility to teach courses during the day, evening, or online, as well as taught using other distance learning formats. Job Description Responsibilities and Duties Include: Plans, organizes, and delivers instruction to didactic,

laboratory and/or classroom settings to promote student success within a community college setting. Supports and promotes the college and departmental missions centered on excellence in teaching and learning through quality instruction, curriculum enhancement, and timely service. Collaborates with the Associate Director, Chair, faculty, and peers to evaluate, revise, and develop curriculum that cultivates the mastery

of course content. Assists in the direction and supervision of EMS programs and personnel, including adjunct faculty and EMS clinical coordination. Demonstrates technological literacy and innovation in the delivery of instruction. Provides leadership, positive role modeling, and strong working relationships with students, public safety agencies, the EMS Advisory Committee, SF faculty and staff, and members of the

community. Works collaboratively as part of a cohesive academic team. Assists with the implementation of college policies, objectives, and functions in accordance with the SF philosophy, mission, strategic initiatives, and procedures. Participates in departmental meetings and collegewide committees as needed. Provides students with frequent, timely assessment and clear feedback regarding academic performance.
