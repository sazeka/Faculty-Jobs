Bus Driver - Adjunct

University of New Hampshire System  ·  Durham, NH
AdjunctNon-Tenure

Position summary

USNH Employees should apply within Workday through the Jobs Hub app Under the general supervision of a designated supervisor, provide safe, efficient, and on time service through the operation of motor vehicles/transit buses as assigned. Must make simple and complex decisions regarding emergency, traffic, and customer services situations quickly. Ability to deal with stressful situations and drive safely despite

distractions, including two-way radio conversations and passenger conversations/questions. Duties/Responsibilities Operates buses to transport passengers over established routes (transit routes, campus connector, Access, Medi-Cat, misc.) according to set time schedules as assigned. This includes: Stops at designated points to load and/or unload passengers. Safely load and unload passengers who use mobility devices,

using the wheelchair lift and tie-down securement system. Operates on-board computer for the destination signs and automatic stop announcements. Operates vehicles to transport groups (i.e. dignitaries) around campus as assigned. Utilize iPad to maintain statistical data for passenger counts, mileage and monitor fare boxes as required to ensure passengers are paying the proper bus fare. Notifies the

dispatcher/supervisor of detours, collisions, vehicle issues, or other incidents as needed. Complies with all UNH policies, procedures, and performance expectations, as well as all Department of Transportation (DOT) policies and procedures. Complies with all traffic laws and regulations. Meets and maintains UNH’s personal driving record requirements. Completes a thorough vehicle check pre- and post-trip. Checks

vehicle fluids (oil, coolant, fuel, etc.) as required. Fills out and submits the Driver Vehicle Inspection Report (DVIR) for every vehicle the driver operates. Completes and submits written reports concerning accidents, incidents, and any other miscellaneous reports as required. Depending on shift, may start up buses or close out buses, including fluid checks and reporting, fueling buses, cleaning interior and

exterior of bus, etc. as directed. Advises passengers of rules and regulations when necessary. Other duties as assigned. Requirements Minimum Acceptable Education & Experience: High School Diploma or Equivalent Required Licenses & Certifications: Must be at least 18 years of age and hold a valid drivers license for a minimum of 2 years. Must be able to earn and maintain a valid Commercial Driver’s License (CDL),
