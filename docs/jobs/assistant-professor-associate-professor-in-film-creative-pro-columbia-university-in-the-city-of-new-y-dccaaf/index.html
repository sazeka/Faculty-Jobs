This requires the producer to have both storytelling and business skills, the ability to build collaborative teams, and an understanding of rapidly changing

technologies and market conditions. &nbsp; Candidates must have a vibrant professional practice in producing, teaching experience, and the ability to teach and mentor students at the graduate level.
