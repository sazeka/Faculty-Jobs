Back to Listings Academic Professional Track (Non-Tenure): Clinical Assistant Professor or Clinical Associate Professor Public Health Sciences Location Dallas, TX Open Date 4/13/2026 Position ID 180866 Description The Department of Public Health Sciences in the College of Dentistry at Texas A&M University invites applications for a full time 100%FTE, academic professional track (non-tenure) faculty position with an

11-month service period; the start date is August, 2026, or negotiable. Applicants will be considered for the faculty titles of Clinical Assistant Professor or Clinical Associate Professor; rank commensurate on review of qualifications. All final candidates will be required to successfully pass a criminal background check prior to beginning employment. The Department of Public Health Sciences seeks to hire a faculty

member with PhD in behavioral science or related field. The faculty member will: Lead and contribute to the behavioral science curriculum across the DDS and dental hygiene programs, including instruction in health behavior theory, motivational interviewing, patient communication, cultural competence, and social determinants of oral health, etc. Establish a research program focused on community engagement and

implementation science and pursue extramural research funding. Participate in the College’s community outreach and engagement activities to enhance the College’s community impact. The Department of Public Health Sciences at Texas A&M University College of Dentistry is a recognized leader. More information can be found at dentistry.tamu.edu/departments/public-health. Qualifications Candidate must have a PhD in

behavioral science, implementation science, psychology, or closely related filed, and demonstrated the training and experience in behavioral science relevant to health or oral health. Application Instructions Interested applicants must apply through the Texas A&M University faculty job board hosted by Interfolio at and upload the following: 1) Curriculum Vitae, 2) Cover Letter 3) List of three references with full

contact information including email addresses. 4) Personal Statement include philosophy and plans for research, teaching, and service, as applicable. The application materials must be submitted online at: apply.interfolio.com/184188 Please forward questions regarding this position to Dr. Peggy Timothe at ptimothe@tamu.edu Incomplete and/or ineligible submission will not be considered.
