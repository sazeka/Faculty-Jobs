Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor - Emergency Medicine, Ultrasound Director 500920 Health Science Campus College Toledo, OH Academic Faculty Full-Time Healthcare Positions Faculty - Regular Job Description: UToledo Health and the University of Toledo College of Medicine and Life Sciences has a faculty position opening for a Ultrasound Director in the Department of Emergency Medicine. The Department of Emergency

Medicine is seeking a board certified or board eligible candidate who has completed residency training in Emergency Medicine as well as fellowship training in Emergency Ultrasound. This position offers a unique opportunity to build upon the academic affiliation of University of Toledo College of Medicine and Life Sciences (UT) with a large vibrant community hospital that has expanded our opportunities for our

fellows, residents, and students; to increase the number and expertise of the full-time faculty; and to enhance our growing research endeavors. The Department of Emergency Medicine is committed to excellence in clinical care, education, and research. The Ultrasound Director will play a key leadership role in advancing point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) education across both graduate and undergraduate medical education

programs, supporting innovation in curriculum development, competency assessment, and interdisciplinary collaboration. Responsibilities: Lead and oversee ultrasound education for Emergency Medicine residents and medical students, including didactic sessions, hands-on training, and competency assessments. Develop, implement, and manage ultrasound curriculum components, including resident rotations, orientation

programs, and longitudinal training experiences. Monitor and evaluate learner progress through scan tracking, proficiency assessments, and structured feedback, including remediation planning when needed. Collaborate with residency and medical school leadership to advance ultrasound education, including regular reporting to the Program Director and undergraduate medical education leadership. Serve as core faculty in

the Emergency Medicine residency, contributing to teaching, mentorship, conference leadership, bedside instruction, and scholarly activity. Minimum Qualifications: Doctor of Medicine or Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine required Board Certified or Board Eligible by the American Board of Emergency Medicine Successful completion of an Emergency Medicine residency; fellowship training in Emergency Ultrasound required
