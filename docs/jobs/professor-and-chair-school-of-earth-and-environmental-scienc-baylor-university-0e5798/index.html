Position summary

Baylor University seeks a visionary leader and accomplished scholar to serve as the inaugural Chair of the School of Earth and Environmental Sciences (SEES). The Chair will play a central role in developing and advancing an integrated vision for research, education, and program expansion aligned with Baylor’s goals as a nationally recognized R1 institution. The Chair will demonstrate a commitment to Baylor's mission

as a Christian research university. The new School, established in June 2026, is being formed through the integration of the Departments of Environmental Science and Geosciences and builds on established strengths across a broad range of earth and environmental systems research and teaching. These include environmental health and toxicology, environmental chemistry, ecosystem and atmospheric modeling, hydrological

and surface processes, biogeosciences, climate and paleoclimate, energy geosciences, environmental law and policy, and solid earth and planetary sciences.
