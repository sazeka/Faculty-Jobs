Position Details Position Information Position Title Visiting Assistant Professor of Communication Unit College of Arts & Sciences Job Location Crete, NE (68333)

Description of Work Doane University, a nationally recognized liberal arts and sciences university in Crete, Nebraska, invites applications for a one-year Visiting Assistant Professor of Communication for the 2026-2027 academic year. This position begins August 10, 2026.Responsibilities include teaching 9 to 12 credit hours of on-ground undergraduate courses each semester. Courses to be taught could include

Fundamentals of Communication, Writing for Social Media, Basic News Writing and Reporting, and/or other courses in Communication or the Doane Core. While teaching is the primary responsibility of this position, the successful candidate may also participate in service activities and assume limited administrative responsibilities, depending on the needs of the university and the candidate’s experience and

interests.Doane University actively works to create and sustain a welcoming learning community for all members to fulfill their potential, feel valued, and make meaningful contributions to the University. Doane University is an equal-opportunity employer and seeks candidates who reflect the diversity of our society. Applicants must be currently authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis. Doane

University does not provide employment visa sponsorship for this position. Minimum Required Qualifications Master’s degree in Communication or related field.A commitment to teaching in an undergraduate liberal arts university is expected from strong candidates. Preferred Qualifications Ph.D. or Advanced ABD in Communication or related field Required Licenses/Certifications Physical/Environmental/Working Conditions
