Position Title: Assistant Professor of Marketing Compensation: $132,000-$137,000 Department: Marketing Start Date: 08/01/2027 About this opportunity: The Department of Marketing in the Miller College of Business at Ball State University invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Marketing position beginning August 2027. We are a collaborative, student-centered, industry-connected, experientially

driven, and forward-looking department committed to preparing students for successful careers in a rapidly changing marketing profession. The successful candidate will join the department at an exciting point in its development. We are strengthening our curriculum, expanding applied learning and industry engagement, and creating more intentional connections across the student experience. The candidate will contribute

their own expertise and ideas while working with others to help shape what comes next. We value faculty interested in connecting classroom learning with meaningful applied and immersive experiences that help students develop the knowledge, skills, judgment, and professional readiness needed to succeed. We are seeking a candidate who is excited to contribute through excellent teaching, meaningful scholarship,

collaborative engagement, and a genuine investment in the continued growth of our students, programs, and department. The ideal candidate will demonstrate a strong commitment to helping students navigate emerging developments in marketing, including evolving technologies, digital platforms, data-informed decision making, and other innovations shaping the profession. Candidates with an interest and ability to teach

undergraduate and/or graduate marketing courses, particularly in one or more of the following areas are strongly encouraged to apply: digital marketing, marketing research, consumer behavior, and marketing analytics. Employer will consider sponsorship. What you'll do and what you will bring: Position Function Primary responsibilities include teaching undergraduate and/or graduate marketing courses, maintaining an

active program of scholarly research, and contributing to the continued success of the Department of Marketing through meaningful service, student engagement, and collaboration with colleagues. Duties and Responsibilities Teaching: Teach 9 semester credit hours of undergraduate and/or graduate marketing courses, with assignments based on departmental needs and the faculty member's expertise.
