position of Chair in the Department of Health Sciences (HSC). We are seeking a dynamic, collaborative chair to lead a wide range of programs in the department, which include undergraduate degree programs in Environmental Health and Sustainability, Health Informatics and Management, Health Promotion and Education, Medical Laboratory Science, and Occupational Safety and Health, a Master’s Degree in Public Health, and a

The Chair will encourage and support high quality research, teaching, and service in alignment

with the mission, vision, and goals of the department, college, and university. The preferred start date for this position is July 1, 2027. The position is a twelve-month tenure track appointment.
