Position summary

Butte College offers more than just a positive workplace, it offers a mission-driven community committed to equity, sustainability, and student success. Located on a stunning 928-acre wildlife refuge, we're a national leader in environmental responsibility. In addition to our main campus, Butte College serves students at our four satellite campuses throughout Butte and Glenn Counties. As a Hispanic Serving

Institution (HSI), Butte College is dedicated to advancing educational access and economic opportunity for our region’s diverse populations. Serving over 15,000 students each year, nearly half of our student body identifies as Latinx, Asian, African American, Native American, Pacific Islander, or multiracial. At Butte College, you’ll join a supportive campus culture that values continuous learning and invests in the

professional growth of faculty and staff. There are countless ways for employees to connect with the campus community beyond the classroom and office. Engage with students and colleagues across campus by participating in committees, campus-wide events, cultural celebrations, and professional development opportunities. We seek individuals who share our mission and values, especially our commitment to making a

difference in students’ lives by supporting initiatives that foster an inclusive environment. If you’re ready to work where your contributions matter, and where you’ll be inspired every day by the students you serve, Butte College is the place for you.
