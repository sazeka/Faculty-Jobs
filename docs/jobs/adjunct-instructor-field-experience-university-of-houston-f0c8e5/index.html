Adjunct, Instructor Field Experience 495315 Part-Time Closing on: Nov 25 2026 Urban Education Department: Urban Education Salary: Commensurate with Experience/Education Description: The University of Houston-Downtown Department of Urban Education is seeking applications for adjunct Field Experience Instructors. SAIS-Interdisciplinary Studies degree program (a 6-hour course load may be possible). The candidate is expected to participate in the department's field-based program supervising the fieldwork component for students in various certification programs (EC-6, 4-8, and 7-12). Candidates must have flexible daytime availability to conduct field-based observations.Duties Establishes and maintains relationships with campus
