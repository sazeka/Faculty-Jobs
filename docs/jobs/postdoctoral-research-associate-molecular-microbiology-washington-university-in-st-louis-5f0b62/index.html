Location SAINT LOUIS, MO 63110&#xa;&#xa; Position Summary The Kutluay Laboratory located at WashU Medicine in Saint Louis, MO, is seeking to hire a highly motivated postdoctoral researcher. HIV-1 continues to be a major global health problem without a cure or vaccine in sight. Our laboratory studies fundamental aspects of HIV-1 replication, virus-host interactions and innate immune defenses with particular emphasis

on the roles of viral and cellular RNA/DNA-binding proteins. To study these processes, we employ cutting-edge next-generation sequencing-based methodologies, tools of conventional molecular biology, biochemistry and molecular virology. Kutluay lab and WashU provide an enriching and rigorous training opportunity ensuring that trainees reach their diverse career goals. The city of Saint Louis has a rich history, is

highly affordable and family friendly. Qualified applicants will be invited for an in person visit to meet members of the lab and WashU research community. Job Description Primary Duties & Responsibilities: Information on being a postdoc at WashU in St. Louis can be found at https://postdoc.wustl.edu/prospective-postdocs-2/ . https://kutluaylab.wustl.edu/ . Trains under the supervision of a faculty mentor including (but not limited to): Assists with grant preparation and reporting. Prepares and submits papers on research. Assists in the design of research experiments. Evaluates research findings and assists in the reporting of the results. Conscientious discharge of their research responsibilities. Maintains conformity with ethical
