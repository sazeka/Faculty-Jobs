Overview: The University of Miami, UHealth is pleased to offer a one-year non-ACGME fellowship in Cardiovascular Advanced Imaging. This specialized

training program is designed for physicians who are looking to deepen their expertise in cardiovascular imaging techniques, with a particular focus on advanced imaging modalities used for the diagnosis and management of cardiovascular diseases. The program provides comprehensive hands-on training in state-of-the-art imaging technologies, including but not limited to cardiac MRI, cardiac CT, and echocardiography.

Objectives: The Cardiovascular Advanced Imaging Fellowship at UHealth aims to provide advanced training in the clinical and technical aspects of cardiovascular imaging. Fellows will gain experience in interpreting and performing imaging studies to enhance diagnostic and therapeutic decision-making for complex cardiovascular patients. Responsibilities: Clinical Training : Participate in daily clinical practice with a

multidisciplinary team, interpreting advanced cardiovascular imaging modalities including cardiac CT, MRI, echocardiography, and nuclear imaging. Advanced Imaging Techniques : Obtain expertise in advanced techniques such as myocardial perfusion imaging, coronary artery imaging, cardiac function evaluation, and tissue characterization using state-of-the-art imaging modalities. Research Opportunities : Engage in

clinical and translational research projects related to cardiovascular imaging. Fellows will have the opportunity to collaborate with faculty on ongoing studies and contribute to publications in high-impact journals. Collaboration with Specialists : Work closely with cardiologists, radiologists, and other specialists to develop integrated treatment strategies for patients with complex cardiovascular conditions.

Educational Activities : Participate in didactic teaching sessions, case discussions, and regular departmental conferences. Fellows will also be encouraged to present at local and national meetings. Quality Assurance and Protocol Development : Gain experience in quality assurance processes and protocol development to ensure accurate imaging results and effective clinical utilization.
