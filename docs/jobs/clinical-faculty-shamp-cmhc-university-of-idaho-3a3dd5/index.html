The faculty member will hold the rank of Clinical Assistant Professor, with an academic home

The position includes a 50% commitment to teaching, supervision, scholarship, and related faculty responsibilities in support of the Doctor of Psychology (PsyD) program and a 50% commitment to direct clinical service at CMHC. The faculty member will

operations and day-to-day service responsibilities at the Counseling and Mental Health Center (CMHC), the faculty member will work under the direction of the CMHC Director or their designee.
