Home Position: Full-Time Faculty of Practice: Music & Director of Choral Activities Location: St. Paul, MN Job Id: 100702* # of Openings: 1 Full-Time Faculty of Practice: Music & Director of Choral Activities Position Overview: Concordia University, St. Paul’s College of Fine & Performing Arts invites applications for a full-time faculty position in music and Director of Choral Activities to provide academic and

artistic leadership within the University’s music program and to support Concordia’s mission of Christian higher education in the Lutheran tradition. The successful candidate will contribute to curriculum and course management, lead and coordinate the team of music faculty, and assist with the recruitment and hiring of new part-time instructors. As Director of Choral Activities, the candidate will provide leadership

for the University’s choral program and direct the University’s choral ensembles. The position includes recruiting and mentoring students as they develop their musical, professional, and vocational gifts; supporting the role of music in the life of the church and broader community; and collaborating with colleagues in advancing Concordia’s mission of educating students for lives of service and leadership. As a

Christ-centered, Lutheran institution, we require the successful candidate to be an active member of a Christian church, though specific knowledge of Lutheran worship and hymnody is expected for this specific position. Applicants should be prepared to discuss how their faith informs their teaching, scholarship, and leadership. Essential Duties: Direct the universities choral ensemble(s). Lead the planning and

coordination of the university’s annual Christmas Concert. Coordinate and lead the annual High School Honor Choir. Plan and co-lead the annual Spring Music Tour. Teach courses and provide music instruction in areas of expertise. Oversee the maintenance and organization of music resources and equipment. Manage the budgets for the Christmas Concert and Christus Chorus. Coordinate individual music lessons and

faculty-taught music courses. Represent music programs at Admitted Student Day and other recruitment/outreach events. Coordinate music ensemble participation in chapel. Collaborate with Department of Fine & Performing Arts Chair. Schedule and lead regular music team meetings. Coordinate and monitor music participation scholarships. Perform other duties related to the position as assigned. Education & Experience:
