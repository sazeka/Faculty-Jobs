Position summary

JOB DESCRIPTION UTHealth School of Dentistry is seeking a highly qualified, full-time Associate Professor on the tenure or non-tenure track to serve as Program Director for our CODA-accredited Advanced Education Program in Pediatric Dentistry. This is a full-time faculty position with opportunities for academic advancement, scholarly activity, clinical practice, and educational leadership within a collaborative and

well-established department. The position is highly competitive in terms of salary. Academic rank and salary will be commensurate with qualifications and experience. The position is available immediately. Position Key Accountabilities: The Program Director will provide strategic leadership, administrative oversight, and academic guidance for the residency program. The ideal candidate will demonstrate a strong

commitment to excellence in graduate dental education, patient care, and faculty development, while upholding all standards required by the Commission on Dental Accreditation (CODA). Other responsibilities of the position include, but are not limited to, providing lectures and clinical instruction in pediatric dentistry to graduate and pre-doctoral students, conducting research, and providing service to the

university, such as serving on standing and ad-hoc dental school committees. An intramural faculty practice opportunity is available for eligible candidates. Research with extramural funding, while highly desired, is not required.
