Workforce Development (WFD) Instructor: OSHA 10 & 30 General Industry 494523 Harrisburg, PA, Pennsylvania Non exempt Job Summary: We are seeking an experienced, engaging Instructor of OSHA 10 & 30 General Industry to join our instructional team on a part time, as needed basis. In this role, you will deliver high quality, compliance focused safety training to adult learners across manufacturing, warehousing,

logistics, and industrial environments.This position plays a vital role in supporting high demand industry sectors by strengthening workplace safety culture, improving regulatory compliance, reducing incident risk, and preparing both new and incumbent workers with essential safety knowledge.The ideal candidate brings strong knowledge of OSHA standards, hazard recognition, and workplace safety practices within general

industry settings. This instructor serves as a key partner to employers seeking to meet compliance requirements while investing in the professional development and protection of their workforce. Minimum Qualifications: - OSHA Authorized Outreach Trainer status for General Industry required- Completion of OSHA 511 and OSHA 501- 5+ years of professional experience in manufacturing, industrial safety, logistics, or

related field Licensing Requirements: - Valid Driver's License (A valid driver’s license is required. Travel within the Central Pennsylvania region may be necessary.)- Please note that this position is required to comply with PA Act 15. You will need to present completed clearances and training certificates at the time of hire. Please visit https://www.hacc.edu/HumanResources/NewHire/index.cfm (and scroll to PA Act

15 Clearances) for more information. Hiring Range: Varies Preferred Qualifications: - 3+ years of experience teaching or training adult learners- Strong knowledge of OSHA regulations including hazard communication, lockout/tagout, machine guarding, PPE, electrical safety, and powered industrial truck standards Job Specific Task List: Facilitate Instruction - (Essential) Deliver engaging, high quality OSHA 10 and OSHA
