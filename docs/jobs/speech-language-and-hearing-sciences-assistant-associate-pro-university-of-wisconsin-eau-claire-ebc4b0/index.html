Position Title: Speech, Language and Hearing Sciences: Assistant/Associate Professor Job Category: Faculty Employment Type: Regular Job Profile: Assistant Professor Job Duties: POSITION : The Department of Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences is seeking a

tenure-track faculty member at the rank of assistant or associate professor. The department seeks a dynamic candidate with a strong interest in the academic and clinical instruction of undergraduate and master’s students in our CAA accredited program in speech-language pathology who is committed to teaching excellence and to maintaining an active program . This appointment carries an initial two-year probationary

appointment beginning with the 2027-28 academic year, August 16, 2027. Summer employment is possible. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS : Doctorate in Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences or a related field is required. ABD considered but completion of the doctorate will be required by June 1, 2028. Candidates with demonstrated potential for excellence in teaching and scholarship and commitment to support and enhance the

diversity and cultural and global awareness of students, faculty, and staff are encouraged to apply. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS : Prior university teaching and online teaching experience will be considered assets, as will expertise in any of the following areas or combination of areas: speech sound disorders, aural rehabilitation, or voice disorders. CCC-SLP/CCC-A is preferred. Demonstrated commitment to the principles

of communication as a fundamental human right (please see our department’s position statement ). DUTIES : Teach courses in the Department of Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences based on program needs and in accordance with specialization; provide academic advising to students as assigned; participate in scholarly activity, student research, and professional mentoring,; and engagement in service to the department,

college, university, and community. DEPARTMENT : The Department of Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences consists of nine faculty, seven clinical instructional faculty, and three adjunct instructors, approximately 300 undergraduate majors, and approximately 100 master’s students in residential and online programs. The department also has an active speech, language, and hearing clinic including 16 treatment rooms
