ranks The Department of Civil and Architectural Engineering (CAE) of the College of Engineering (CoE) at the University of Miami (UM) has an open position for a PROFESSOR IN THE PRACTICE OF CIVIL AND ARCHITECTURAL ENGINEERING at ALL ranks. Applications to fill this full-time, non-tenure track faculty position are being sought. The start date for this appointment is anticipated to be August 15, 2026. Minimum

qualifications for this position are earned BS, MS and PhD degrees in civil or architectural engineering or a closely related engineering discipline with a Professional Engineering license or ability to obtain Professional Engineering licensure within 2 years. Candidates with a minimum of 3 years of experience in industry and/or engineering education are preferred. Candidates with professional practice in thermal,

hydraulic and environmental systems in building sciences are encouraged to apply. Demonstrated ability in communication, interpersonal skills, collaboration, leadership, and student motivation are required attributes. The expectation of the candidate is to have a primary teaching role and in addition take part in departmental and University service. Individuals considering this position should have an interest and

ability to teach courses in undergraduate civil and architectural engineering, including senior design capstone courses. Candidates are expected to serve in departmental and/or university committees and professional societies.
