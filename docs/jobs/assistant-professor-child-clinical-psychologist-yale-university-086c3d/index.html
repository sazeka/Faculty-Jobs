Assistant Professor- Child Clinical Psychologist

Yale University  ·  New Haven, CT
Assistant ProfessorClinical Psychology (Child)

Position summary

Yale University: School of Medicine: Child Study Center Location New Haven, CT Open Date Nov 03, 2026 Deadline Nov 03, 2027 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time Description The Yale Child Study Center’s Clinical Services are comprised of a multidisciplinary faculty and a number of training programs, including psychology practicum, internship, and post-doctoral fellowship. Our services include child and adult treatment,

home-based services, and assessment services. Psychoeducational testing, developmental evaluations, and team evaluations are an important part of our array of services. The Child Study Center is committed to recruiting and supporting clinicians, researchers, and leaders representing diverse backgrounds today, as well as those in succeeding generations in the service of its clinical, research, training, policy, and

prevention agendas. Yale Child Study Center Clinical Services has an opening for full-time child psychologist dedicated to providing evaluation and treatment services to young children and their families. The successful candidate will be appointed as an Assistant Professor of Child Psychology on the Academic Clinician ladder track. A primary focus of this position is to provide developmental assessment and

evidence-based treatment for children under ten and their families. Additional job responsbilities include: · Conducting developmental and psychological assessments of children and adolescents · Providing evidence-based treatment to children and their caregivers, with a special emphasis on services for children aged 10 and younger and their families. · Providing Child Parent Psychotherapy (CPP) to young children who

have experienced traumatic events · Collaborating with members of clinical teams to inform comprehensive care plans · Participation in the psychology training faculty, including training and supervision of practicum students, psychology interns through the Yale Child Study Center’s APA-accredited internship, and/or post-doctoral fellows on the pediatric psychology track While not required on the Academic Clinician

track, research and quality improvement projects are available and encouraged as part of a collaborative team. The salary ranges listed below reflect the anticipated compensation range for all ranks included in this recruitment. $125,000-$150,000 Qualifications Successful candidates must possess an APA accredited doctoral degree in psychology, postdoctoral experience in clinical or health service psychology.
