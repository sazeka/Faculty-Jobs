Full-time Months per year 12 Primary Purpose of Department The College of Arts + Architecture was formed in 2008 with the aim of combining the arts disciplines and architecture under one roof, bringing a critical mass of artists and designers together to elevate the College’s voice. As the only college of its kind in the UNC System, the COAA demonstrates UNC Charlotte’s commitment to arts and culture on campus and in

the broader community, presenting exhibitions, lectures, and more than 100 performances each year.The College is home to three graduate degree programs, nine undergraduate programs, and certificate programs in Musical Theatre, Jazz, and Advanced Dance Studies, and a professional training certificate in dance in partnership with the Charlotte Ballet. The College is deeply committed to student retention and graduation

and provides extensive resources, including academic and career advising, internships, and scholarships. Students and faculty all benefit from the College’s commitment to personalized advising from specialized, full-time professional academic advisors who work with students one-on-one throughout their course of study. Vacancy Open to All Candidates Description of Work Assist the Dean in the oversight of various

duties, which may include but aren’t limited to, advancing research missions, supporting the undergraduate and graduate degree programs, faculty evaluation, faculty development, facilitate external support for undergraduate and graduate programs, curriculum development, course schedule planning, faculty/staff hiring and supervision, budget development, fiscal monitoring, and other related administrative activities

with and for the assigned program areas. Reports directly to the Dean and may assume some duties of the Dean in their absence. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Essential DutiesThe Associate Dean for Production Arts + Operations works with the Chairs of the Dance, Music and Theatre Departments to schedule all internal and external performances (as well as curricular and programmatic events).
