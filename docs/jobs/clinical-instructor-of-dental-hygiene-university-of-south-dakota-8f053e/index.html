The successful candidate will supervise dental hygiene students

during clinical education experiences, and deliver direct patient care during designated clinic sessions. This faculty member will play an integral role in preparing future dental hygienists while advancing access to quality oral healthcare throughout the region. Essential Responsibilities Teaching & Clinical Education Supervise dental hygiene students in the clinical setting while ensuring patient safety and

Ensure compliance with Commission on Dental Accreditation (CODA) standards, South Dakota Board of Dentistry regulations, and University

policies. Collaborate with Falls Community Health Dental Clinic staff to coordinate student schedules and clinical learning experiences.
