Position Overview The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center offers a unique environment for developing an innovative academic pain medicine practice. As one of the nation's first institutions to integrate Neurology, Neurosurgery, Neuroscience, Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation, and Psychiatry into a unified Neurological Institute,

Ohio State provides exceptional opportunities for multidisciplinary patient care, education, and clinical research. This position offers the opportunity to develop a comprehensive neurology-based pain medicine practice with outpatient, inpatient, infusion, and interventional treatment capabilities. The successful candidate will serve as a subspecialty expert in the diagnosis and management of acute and chronic pain

disorders, particularly those arising from diseases of the central and peripheral nervous systems. Clinical responsibilities include evaluation and treatment of neuropathic pain, headache and facial pain syndromes, radiculopathies, peripheral nerve disorders, complex regional pain syndrome, spinal pain, and other neurologic pain conditions.
