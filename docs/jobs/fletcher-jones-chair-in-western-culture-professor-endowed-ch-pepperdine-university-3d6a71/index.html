Position summary

Fletcher Jones Chair in Western Culture– Professor/Endowed Chair Malibu Campus Teaching Full-Time Seaver College Pepperdine University invites applications and nominations for the Fletcher Jones Chair of Western Civilization, a distinguished endowed faculty position dedicated to the study, teaching, and public advancement of the intellectual, cultural, political, and religious traditions that have shaped Western

civilization. The Chair will provide scholarly and pedagogical leadership within Seaver College, engaging students in the great texts, ideas, and historical developments that inform contemporary life, while contributing to Pepperdine's mission of integrating faith, learning, and scholarship. This is a senior appointment reserved for a scholar of national or international distinction whose research and teaching

illuminate the foundations of Western thought, including but not limited to classical philosophy, political theory, literature, history, and the Christian tradition, and whose work models rigorous, values-engaged scholarship consistent with Pepperdine's Christian mission. Pepperdine University is a Christian university committed to the highest standards of academic excellence and enduring spiritual values. Seaver

College, Pepperdine's undergraduate liberal arts college located on the Malibu campus, is known for its strong core curriculum, integration of faith and learning, and commitment to preparing students for purposeful lives of service and leadership. Duties Teaching undergraduate seminars in Great Books with the option of teaching courses aligned with disciplinary expertise. Pursue an active program of scholarly

research and publication that advances public and academic understanding of Western intellectual traditions Provide thought leadership through public lectures, symposia, and engagement with broader university and community audiences Mentor students and junior faculty, fostering intellectual formation consistent with Pepperdine's mission Contribute to curriculum development, particularly in the established and

evolving Great Books program Represent Pepperdine in relevant academic and public forums, enhancing the university's reputation in this field Participate in university service, including committee work and institutional initiatives related to the humanities The above information has been designed to indicate the general nature and level of work performed by employees within this classification.
