Position description Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor in the Health Sciences Clinical series The University of California, San Francisco School of Nursing seeks faculty experts for didactic and clinical teaching in the Doctor of Nursing Practice - Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP) programs. The FNP program offer both in-person and online coursework. This is a Full-time

position, including a required 25% time of clinical practice component, and candidates must maintain active clinical practice as a Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP). The faculty position will be appointed at the level of Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor in the Health Sciences Clinical series, depending on the qualifications and experience. Faculty will participate in the education and training of students at

Doctor of Nursing Practice graduate levels. Faculty will be appointed to the School of Nursing Department of Family Health Care Nursing. Faculty Position Responsibilities may include: Teaching didactic and skills development coursework via in-person, remote, and/or hybrid modalities; Curriculum design, development, implementation, and evaluation; Review curriculum to assure fidelity with current and future competency

and graduate nursing education guidelines from NACNS, ANCC, and other professional organizations. Academic advisement; Oversight of student development of clinical competencies and role development through conducting weekly clinical conferences, monitoring student performance in clinical settings, and/or evaluating clinical assignments; Collaborating and closely coordinating with academic and clinical teams across

multiple systems; Oversight and recruitment of clinical placements aligned with the specialty curriculum; Recruiting and retaining a talented student cohort; Participating in School of Nursing committees and in professional organizations.
