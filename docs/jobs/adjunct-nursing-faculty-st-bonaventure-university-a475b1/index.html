Adjunct Nursing Faculty Print Adjunct Nursing Faculty Salary $67.00 Hourly Location St. Bonaventure, NY Job Type Part Time Job Number 202200355 Division Academic Affairs Department DePerro School of Health Professions Opening Date 01/02/2024 Description Benefits Description Adjunct Nursing FacultySt. Bonaventure University (SBU) is seeking faculty for the Nursing Department to teach undergraduate and graduate nursing

curriculum. This is an adjunct part-time position. The main focus will be to teach undergraduate and graduate curriculum, oversee clinical experiences, or skills lab areas. Adjunct nursing faculty report to the Director of Nursing and will be part of an inter-disciplinary team within the DePerro School of Health Professions.Key Responsibilities will include:· Prepare and deliver assigned nursing curriculum for

successful student outcomes.· Coordinate and oversee clinical and skills lab experiences.· Uphold the mission, goals, and expected program outcomes of the nursing department and university.Successful candidates will have the following selected qualifications and skills:· Active, unencumbered Registered Nursing license in NYS or willingness to obtain one upon hire.· Earned graduate degree (nursing focus preferred);

doctorate (PhD, DNS, DNP, EdD) degree (completed or in progress) preferred.· Passion and expertise in psychiatric nursing care, obstetrics/women’s health, or critical care preferred.· Minimum of 3-5 years of nursing experience required.Applicants should attach a letter of interest, curriculum vitae/resume, and contact information for three references with their applications.Compensation$67/hour @ $2500/semester, with

increase as needed for # credit & contact hours The UniversitySt. Bonaventure University is located in the scenic Enchanted Mountain region of Western New York, approximately 75 miles south of Buffalo, New York, with easy access to major metropolitan areas. A close-knit and caring community guided by its values of compassion, wisdom and integrity, St. Bonaventure is dedicated to academic excellence as informed by its

Franciscan and liberal arts traditions. The university is committed to developing an inclusive community and preparing leaders dedicated to ethical conduct and compassionate service.The university currently enrolls approximately 2,000 undergraduate students and more than 1,000 graduate students in its schools of Arts and Sciences, Business, Education, Communication, and Health Professions.
