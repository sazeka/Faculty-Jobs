Smith College of Business Job Description The AACSB-accredited Ed G. Smith College of Business offers undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral degrees in a variety of business disciplines. Within the College, the

separately AACSB-accredited Accounting program offers a Bachelor of Science in Accounting and a Master of Accounting and supports DBA students emphasizing Accounting in their research. The Accounting Department is a collegial workplace comprised of faculty members with a variety of research and scholarship interests.Essential tasks that must be performed include: 1) conducting independent research in accounting and

publishing articles in peer-reviewed journals; 2) teaching graduate and undergraduate courses; and 3) providing service to the department, the College, the University, the accounting profession and/or the wider community. Qualifications Candidates must have (or expect to complete) a Ph.D. in Accounting from an AACSB-accredited university before the start date. We welcome candidates with research and teaching

interests in any area of accounting. We seek colleagues who will actively pursue scholarly research and enthusiastically approach teaching at both the undergraduate and graduate levels. Salary and benefits are competitive. Application Materials Application materials should include at least the following: a letter of application addressing the requirements of the position, a current curriculum vitae, names and contact

information of three professional references, and a research paper. Applicants must be able to start on campus by Fall 2027. If you have questions regarding this position, please contact Dr. Paul Ordyna (paul.ordyna@umsl.edu) or Dr. Michele Meckfessel (meckfesselm@umsl.edu) Application Deadline Applications will be reviewed beginning August 15, 2026 but will be accepted until the position is filled.
