Position summary

APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS: Interested applicants should submit the following materials with their application: Cover Letter Resume TYPE OF POSITION/EMPLOYMENT CATEGORY: Full-Time, Administrator DEPARTMENT: Clinical Learning Center SCHEDULE: Monday - Friday, Daily hours may vary between 7:30am to 8:00pm. Work location may be flexible under some circumstances: 2 lab sites. Evenings and occasional weekends REMOTE WORK

ELIGILITY: No HOURS/WEEK and MONTHS/YEAR: 35 hours/week and 52 weeks/year FLSA STATUS: E xempt, Salaried GENERAL PURPOSE Assist undergraduate and graduate nursing students with practice and competency evaluation of nursing skills in a supportive, nurturing, clinical environment. Participates in Simulation Based Education (SBE) across nursing curriculums. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Provide nursing students

with the support necessary to practice nursing skills. Administer competency evaluation of psychomotor skills for undergraduate students with reinforcement and remediation of nursing skills as warranted. Facilitate simulation experiences: setup, scenario competency, breakdown of environment, and debriefing. Assist with the day-to-day activities of the CLC. Assist with maintenance of laboratory environment, inventory

of supplies and equipment. REQUIRED MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS Bachelor's degree (Registered Nurse, B.S.N) 5 to 8 years of clinical experience Microsoft Office (Outlook, Excel, Word) RESPONSIBILITY FOR PUBLIC CONTACT Daily contact requiring courtesy, discretion, and sound judgment.
