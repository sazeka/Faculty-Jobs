Position summary

The Opportunity Lesley University always welcomes applications for Adjunct Faculty to teach upcoming classes in our Natural Sciences programs at the undergraduate level. These programs include classes in Biology, Chemistry, Earth & Environmental Science, Health Sciences, and Physics. Courses include a range of foundational, general education, and major-level classes. Most classes are taught in-person, with occasional

availability for remote courses. All courses with labs are taught on campus. Lesley University hires Adjunct Faculty on a regular cadence to support our Fall, Spring, and Summer semesters. By applying to an Evergreen position, your materials will remain on file and be reviewed on an as-needed basis throughout the upcoming semesters. As such, you will be put into consideration for current and future adjunct positions

within the Natural Sciences subject areas. The specific course hired for is subject to change depending on semester, course catalog, and department needs. About Liberal Arts & Business Lesley’s Liberal Arts & Business programs offer a broad foundation for all students. With 13 undergraduate majors across Business, Humanities, Natural Sciences, and Social Sciences, our graduates leave Lesley ready to take on diverse

careers in education, business, human services, science, the arts, and more. Anchored by our outcomes-driven CommonLynx general education curriculum, our programs feature ​​innovative role-play-based teaching, flexible specializations, and the opportunity to build skills and gain real-world experience through ​​guaranteed internships.
