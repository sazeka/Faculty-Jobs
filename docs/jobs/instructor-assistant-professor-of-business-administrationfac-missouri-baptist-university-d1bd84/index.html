Service to the institution. Student

mentoring. Business community partnership development. Minimum Education/Experience Earned Master’s degree in field of study with an undergraduate degree in the same or similar field of study Professional and scholarly accomplishments deemed exemplary and sufficient, or an individual who has an approved dissertation topic. Teaching experience at the higher education level. Business managerial experience. Business

experience. Preferred Education/Experience Earned doctorate in the desired business field of study including some economics with a master’s and undergraduate degree in the same or very similar field of study. Industry
