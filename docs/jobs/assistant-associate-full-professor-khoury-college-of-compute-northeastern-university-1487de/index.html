Position summary

About the Opportunity The Khoury College of Computer Sciences at Northeastern University has multiple tenure-track/tenured faculty positions at all ranks (Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, Full Professor), beginning academic year 2027-28 or 2028-29. The tenure home for this position will reside in the Khoury College of Computer Sciences, with a potential joint appointment in another Northeastern College

(Bouvé College of Health Sciences; College of Art, Media, and Design; College of Engineering; College of Science; College of Social Sciences and Humanities; D'More-McKim School of Business; Mills College; School of Law), to be determined in consultation with the successful candidate. Academic rank at the Associate Professor and Full Professor levels will be commensurate with experience and qualifications reflecting a

record of demonstrated teaching and scholarly excellence. We will consider candidates across all areas of computer science, whose research connects to the following sub-areas of AI: Physical AI: Designing AI systems that sense, reason and act within real-world environments. Areas of interest include machine perception and multimodal sensing, embodied and egocentric AI, world models and planning, wearable and

interactive systems, robot manipulation and mobility, multi-agent systems and safe human-robot collaboration, and assistive and rehabilitation technologies for clinical, home, and community settings. Human Centered AI: Designing, building, and evaluating interactive AI systems that augment human agency, creativity, collaboration, while accounting for their societal, cognitive, and economic impacts. Areas of interest

include human-AI interaction, co-creation and AI-supported work; human control over generative and agentic AI systems; human-centered explainability and decision-making; community-centered technologies as well as critical AI for the public good. We welcome a broad range of research approaches. AI and Software Engineering: Studying software engineering, programming languages, and formal methods for the robustness,

performance, correctness, and security of code generated by AI or interacting with AI. Areas of interest include the coordination and validation of concurrent AI agents acting on shared code at ecosystem scale, and the engineering of AI-based systems. AI Safety, Security and Alignment: Ensuring that increasingly capable AI systems remain safe, reliable, interpretable, and aligned with human intent. Areas of interest
