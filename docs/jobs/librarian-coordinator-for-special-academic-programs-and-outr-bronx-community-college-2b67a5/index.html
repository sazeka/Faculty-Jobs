Librarian Coordinator for Special Academic Programs and Outreach (Instructor or Assistant Professor) FACULTY VACANCY ANNOUNCEMENT The Librarian Coordinator for Special Academic Programs and Outreach will serve as the formal contact person between the library and special academic programs. The coordinator will report directly to the Chief Librarian and will be responsible for monitoring and improving the library’s

impact on the academic programs’ student learning and success. Hostos Community College has several special academic programs that address a variety of student needs. Examples include College Now, College Discovery, CUNY Start, CUNY Math, CUNY English, CLIP, ASAP, middle and high school programs. The primary role of the Coordinator is to foster communication between the library and the college’s academic programs.

The individual is expected to understand the information needs of each program and to represent those needs in appropriate forums within the library. At the same time, the Coordinator is expected to be fully knowledgeable about library resources, services, and technologies so as to represent these key resources effectively to the academic program constituents. As the Coordinator for Outreach, the individual will

bolster library engagement through programming and promoting library services to the Hostos campus community in support of student success and retention. The Librarian Coordinator will collaborate with teaching faculty, library staff and others in the design and delivery of program support that makes a direct and positive impact on students’ academic success and learning outcomes.
