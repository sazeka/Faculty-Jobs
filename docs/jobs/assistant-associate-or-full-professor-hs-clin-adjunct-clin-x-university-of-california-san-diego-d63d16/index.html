Position description The Department of Medicine at the University of California, San Diego is committed to academic excellence among its faculty, staff, and students. We are currently accepting applications for the Kagnoff Endowed Chair in the Division of Gastroenterology & Hepatology. This prestigious faculty position is intended to recruit an internationally

distinguished physician-scientist or scientist whose work is advancing the understanding, prevention, diagnosis, or treatment of gastrointestinal and liver diseases through innovative research, exceptional scholarship, and academic leadership. The Kagnoff Endowed Chair honors the clinical and scientific legacy of Dr. Martin F. Kagnoff, whose pioneering contributions transformed the fields of mucosal immunology,

host-microbe interactions, and gastrointestinal diseases, especially celiac disease. UC San Diego is home to one of the world's leading program in gastroenterology and hepatology. The Kagnoff Endowment provides dedicated support to accelerate scientific discovery and enhance research excellence. The Division of Gastroenterology and Hepatology is ranked number 1 in research and teaching among global universities, and

ranked number 27 in clinical care by the U.S. News & World Report. Faculty benefit from a highly collaborative scientific environment that includes partnerships across the School of Medicine, Moores Cancer Center, Altman Clinical and Translational Research Institute, Herbert Wertheim School of Public Health and Human Longevity Science, Sanford Stem Cell Institute, and numerous interdisciplinary research centers
