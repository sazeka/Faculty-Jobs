Business Analytics/Operations and Engineering Management Faculty (Open Rank) JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY Florida Polytechnic University invites applications for a tenure-equivalent faculty position in operations and engineering management at the rank of Instructor, Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, or Professor, beginning August 2027. We seek candidates who can contribute to teaching and, as appropriate to rank,

research in one or more areas such as supply chain management, logistics, engineering management, project management, or quality management and process improvement, with an emphasis on quantitative, analytical, and data-driven approaches. Our B.S. in Business Analytics and M.S. in Engineering Management emphasize quantitative, analytical, and computational approaches to organizational and technical challenges. This

position will strengthen that focus by helping students apply these methods to improve operations, manage projects and resources, and support data-driven decision-making. JOB DESCRIPTION Responsibilities Teach courses in one or more of the position’s areas of specialization to support the B.S. in Business Analytics and online M.S. in Engineering Management programs, and develop additional courses aligned with

departmental needs and the candidate’s expertise. Maintain a program of scholarship that leads to publication in the discipline (Assistant Professor and above). Instructors demonstrate a scholarly, intentional approach to pedagogy and curriculum instead. Contribute to department and University service. Areas of interest Areas of interest include, but are not limited to, supply chain analytics, logistics, operations

management, digital twin, engineering management, project management, and quality management and process improvement, including Six Sigma and statistical quality control. Candidates should bring expertise in one or more of these areas; expertise across all areas is not expected, and related specializations that complement the department’s STEM and analytics focus will also be considered. Minimum qualifications

Instructor: An earned master’s degree or higher in operations management, supply chain management, logistics, industrial engineering, engineering management, operations research, management science, project management, or a closely related field.
