Assistant Professor of Engineering

Illinois College  ·  Engineering  ·  Jacksonville, IL
Assistant ProfessorTenure-TrackMechanical Engineering

Position summary

Illinois College invites applications for a full-time tenure-track position in mechanical engineering to begin in fall 2027. This full-time faculty position requires a PhD or ABD in an engineering discipline. While we welcome candidates of a wide variety of expertise, preference will be given to those with skills or interest in teaching project-based undergraduate courses such as introductory engineering and capstone

design, as well as lab-based courses such as materials science or fluid dynamics. Illinois College Engineering is a young program that has recently received ABET accreditation in mechanical engineering. The major is embedded within a liberal-arts college, and so understanding and appreciation of the environment of a liberal arts college is essential. Additional duties include contribution to the ongoing development

of the engineering programs at the College, student advising and mentoring, and scholarly engagement. The successful candidate will be granted startup funds to help setup, including student-faculty research. This position resides within an interdisciplinary department including Physics, Mathematics, and Computer Science and hence presents an exciting opportunity for innovation and cross-disciplinary collaboration.

Moreover, there are plentiful opportunities for meaningful engagement with our various local engineering partners. Illinois College seeks candidates who have a commitment to working closely with a wide range of students. Currently, our population is approximately one-quarter students of color and 40% first-generation college students. Eagerness and ability to work with these populations is essential. Equally

important is excellence in pedagogy and an openness to student-centered teaching approaches. Founded by abolitionists, Illinois College was the first in Illinois to graduate students and hosts a chapter of Phi Beta Kappa. The College is student-focused, offering numerous opportunities for faculty-student interaction beyond the classroom, including collaborative research, international study-travel, and mentoring.

Located in historic Jacksonville, Illinois&mdash;just 30 miles from Springfield and 70 miles from St. Louis&mdash;Illinois College has been transforming lives since 1829.
