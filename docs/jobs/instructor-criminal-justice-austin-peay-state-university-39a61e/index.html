Instructor - Criminal Justice Clarksville, TN, United States Be the First to Apply Job Description The Department of Criminal Justice at Austin Peay State University invites applications for an on-ground instructor position starting July 30, 2027. This instructor position entails approximately nine months of full-time service and is a three-year, renewable position. The Department of Criminal Justice currently offers

bachelor's and master of science degrees in criminal justice, each with an optional concentration in homeland security. Additionally, the department houses a bachelor’s degree in national security studies. The program enrolls about 400 undergraduate majors per year and has approximately 60 in our graduate cohorts. The Department of Criminal Justice offers its courses through diverse delivery methods, including

face-to-face, online, and hybrid/blended formats. The successful candidate will be able to teach courses in all modalities. The Department of Criminal Justice is among the University’s fastest growing. The position’s responsibilities include teaching courses as assigned by the chair. Courses may require teaching at the Fort Campbell Center. Additionally, the successful candidate will advise students and participate

in relevant service to the university, community, and discipline. More information about the department is available at http://www.apsu.edu/criminal-justice. departmental, college, and/or university committee work• Faculty may teach during the summer semesters for additional compensation (subject to performance and availability) Required Qualifications:• One of the following: Ph.D. in criminal justice or criminology, a J.D., a Master’s degree in law/legal studies, or a Master’s degree in criminal justice with significant experience in courts/law.• A background search will
