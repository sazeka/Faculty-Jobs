Description The Department of Social Work at the

University of North Alabama invites applications for a tenure-track faculty position in Social Work to begin August 2027. Rank will be determined based on qualifications and experience. A doctoral degree in Social Work or a related field/ discipline is required (PhD or DSW degree). Also required is an MSW from a CSWE-accredited institution, with three or more years of post-MSW practice experience. Candidates with an

academic record of progressive scholarship/teaching/service and experience mentoring or teaching doctoral students in social work are strongly encouraged to apply. Experience with administration and/ or leadership of a doctoral program, CSWE accreditation, and teaching online graduate courses is preferred. Relocation to the North Alabama region will be required. Applications will be accepted until the position is

filled. Sponsorship is not available for this position. Essential Job Duties Essential duties of the position include teaching in the online DSW program, conducting research/scholarly activities, and doctoral student mentoring/advisement responsibilities. This will include chairing and serving on doctoral student capstone committees. Additional duties include maintaining office hours in accordance with University

policy, serving on Department, College, and University committees, functioning as a liaison between the Department of Social Work and the community, and performing other tasks essential to the Department.
