Posting Details Position Information Job Title Assistant/Associate Professor-Nonprofit Management Department School of Public Administration Essential Functions The School of Public Administration (SPA), University of Nebraska at Omaha (UNO), seeks applicants for a tenure-track faculty position at the rank of Assistant/Associate

Professor with an emphasis in Nonprofit Management, beginning January 2027 or August 2027. Candidates should hold a PhD in Public Administration, Public Affairs, Public Policy, Nonprofit Management, or a related field. We seek candidates who will make significant contributions to our doctoral and master’s programs, rigorous research, and engagement with the vibrant nonprofit and philanthropic community in Omaha.The

successful candidate must be able to teach in-person and online courses in nonprofit management at the master and doctoral level and other electives in public or nonprofit management or public policy; provide service on doctoral dissertation committees; and help supervise doctoral students teaching. The successful candidate must demonstrate promise of excellence in research and be committed to community engagement

and professional service. The typical teaching load for research-productive faculty is two courses per semester. On-campus presence is required.SPA has a vibrant, collaborative academic culture. It is home to one of the longest-accredited MPA programs in the country, offered both online and on campus.
