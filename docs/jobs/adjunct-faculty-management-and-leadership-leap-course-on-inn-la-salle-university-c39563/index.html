Position Description/Posting Details Posting Number A0142P Position Summary La Salle’s Early Achievement Program (LEAP) offers credit-bearing college-level courses to motivated students at partner high schools. The Management and Leadership Department is seeking instructors for a new course, ENT 170: Innovation and Entrepreneurship, that will be offered in Summer 2023. We are especially looking for instructors who

can bring energy, creativity, enthusiasm, support, and encouragement to high school students in this experiential, project-based course. Sections will meet in-person at La Salle’s campus on Tuesday and Thursday mornings (10 AM to noon) or afternoons (1 PM to 3 PM) for six weeks, from June 20 to July 27, 2023. Students will receive additional instruction through asynchronous online activities. The course will follow a

common syllabus, workbook, and structure, and a course coordinator will provide instructors with advance training and guidance.
