Boston College: Woods College of Advancing Studies Location Woods College- Chestnut Hill, MA Open Date Sep 01, 2026 Description The Woods College of Advancing Studies at Boston College seeks a Professor of the Practice to support the mission and continued growth of its academic programs. Woods College provides rigorous academic training in a collaborative learning environment that emphasizes applied learning,

innovation, and engagement with faculty and industry professionals. The Professor of the Practice will play a central role in teaching, advising, and supporting students within assigned academic programs. In addition to delivering high-quality instruction, the faculty member will contribute to program oversight through curriculum development, student advising, and collaboration with faculty and program leadership to

ensure the continued strength and relevance of program offerings. This position requires an innovative educator with strong disciplinary expertise and a commitment to applied teaching, student engagement, and academic excellence in a professional learning environment. The successful candidate will contribute to the continued growth and quality of the Applied Liberal Arts major through teaching, advising, curriculum

development, assessment activities, and collaboration with faculty and academic leadership. This is a 12-month faculty appointment with a normal workload equivalent to a 2-2-2 teaching load. Faculty are expected to teach two courses in the fall semester and two courses in the spring semester. Summer responsibilities include teaching one course and fulfilling the equivalent of one course release through assigned

programmatic responsibilities, which may include student advising, curriculum development, assessment, faculty collaboration, enrollment support, and other initiatives that advance the mission and strategic goals of the Applied Liberal Arts major and the Woods College. The expected salary range for this position is $150,000-160,000 for the Assistant Professor of the Practice rank, $160,00-170,000 for Associate

Professor of the Practice rank, and $170,000-180,000 for the Professor of the Practice Rank. The actual pay offered to the selected candidate will be determined based on factors such as, but not limited to, candidate qualifications and prior experience, scope of responsibilities, internal equity, and market considerations.
