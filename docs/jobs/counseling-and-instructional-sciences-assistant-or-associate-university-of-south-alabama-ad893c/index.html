DEPARTMENT OF COUNSELING AND INSTRUCTIONAL SCIENCES ASSISTANT/ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR OF INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT TENURE TRACK POSITION AVAILABLE AUGUST 15, 2027 THE DEPARTMENT OF COUNSELING AND INSTRUCTIONAL SCIENCES in the College of Education and Professional Studies at the University of South Alabama is seeking applicants for a full-time, tenure-track faculty position in Instructional Design and

Development at the rank of Assistant or Associate Professor. This appointment is a nine-month position beginning August 15, 2027. RESPONSIBILITIES include engaging in scholarly research and publication related to the candidate’s expertise in Instructional Design and Development (IDD); seeking external funding for research and/or training grants; teaching graduate courses online and on campus in IDD, as well as

service courses in educational technology, educational psychology, and/or educational research; supervising dissertations and advising students in IDD programs; engaging in professional and community service; working collaboratively with stakeholders across campus, business, and industry engaged in instructional design research and development; and working collaboratively with program faculty to promote, develop, and

grow the IDD programs. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS include: 1. an earned doctorate in instructional design and development (IDD), or a closely related discipline (ABDs will be considered as long as degree is earned by start date); 2. evidence of consistent and sustained scholarly productivity in peer-reviewed academic journals commensurate with rank; 3. evidence of or plan for seeking external funding for research or

training grants; 4. successful graduate-level instruction in IDD, or related area; 5. demonstrated ability to deliver instruction in face-to-face, hybrid, and fully online formats; 6. evidence of or potential for successfully advising graduate students to degree completion; 7. experience in or plan for leading or serving on dissertation committees; experience in supervising dissertations; and 8. demonstrated evidence

of collaborative partnerships within the community and with faculty peers across disciplines. For associate professor consideration: 1. evidence of receiving tenure at a regionally accredited institution of higher education; and 2. cohesive research agenda with clearly articulated path to seeking full professorship. 3. experience and skill in providing program leadership.
