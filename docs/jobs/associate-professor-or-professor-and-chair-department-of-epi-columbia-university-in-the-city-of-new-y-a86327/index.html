Associate Professor or Professor and Chair, Department of Epidemiology

Columbia University in the City of New York  ·  Department of Epidemiology  ·  New York, NY
Associate ProfessorEpidemiology

Position summary

The Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health is seeking applications and nominations for the position of Anna Cheskis Gelman and Murray Charles Gelman Professor and Chair of Epidemiology. The ideal candidate will have a national and global reputation as a top scientist, a leader in epidemiology, a distinguished educator, and a proven administrator and manager. This individual will possess a deep commitment

to producing world-class science with real-world impact and training the next generation of epidemiology leaders in research and practice. Reporting directly to the Dean of the School and serving as a key member of the School’s leadership team, the Chair will set a forward-looking and innovative vision for the Department of Epidemiology, leveraging its historic strengths and pioneering research to address

contemporary challenges in public health while at the same time working to prepare and adapt to the future of the field and the scientific and practical needs of the public. The Chair will play a primary role in supporting and expanding a dynamic faculty, many of whom are recognized as leaders or emerging leaders in epidemiology and related fields. This role will also involve fostering innovative educational programs

that prepare students to navigate and lead in the intricate landscape of science, public and community health, healthcare, government, and industry. The successful candidate will have a proven track record of academic achievement and reputation, substantial extramural grant support, and significant impact within public health. They will bring expertise in addressing both current and emerging challenges in

epidemiology, a clear vision for future advancements, and the ability to quickly cultivate new partnerships. Demonstrable leadership and management capabilities also are essential.
