The instructor is responsible for providing effective instruction in barbering courses and supporting students in developing the skills, knowledge, professionalism, and safety

practices required for the barbering profession. The instructor is supervised by the Program Coordinator or Program Director, the Division Director, and the Vice President of Academic & Student Affairs. The instructor has primary responsibility in academic matters for the content, quality, and effectiveness of the assigned curriculum and has the opportunity to participate in instructional governance and influence

institutional policy and practice through the role of instructor and regular committee assignments. Perform and carry out the teaching assignment in an effective and efficient manner. Teach assigned barbering courses using the approved course of study, established curriculum, textbooks, instructional materials, and applicable program requirements. Provide instruction in classroom, laboratory, shop, clinical, or other

learning environments as assigned. Establish and maintain appropriate office hours. Work individually and collaboratively with others to recruit new students and work to retain current students. professional societies and meetings where appropriate. Assist in advising and registration, as assigned.
