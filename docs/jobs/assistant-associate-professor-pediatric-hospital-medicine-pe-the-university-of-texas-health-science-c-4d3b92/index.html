Assistant/Associate Professor, Pediatric Hospital Medicine - Pediatrics, McGovern Medical School

Assistant ProfessorAssociate ProfessorPediatrics

Position summary

JOB DESCRIPTION McGovern Medical School at UTHealth Houston, Division of Pediatric Hospital Medicine (PHM), is actively seeking pediatric hospitalists to join our team at Children's Memorial Hermann Hospital in the Texas Medical Center. Our division's mission is to deliver high-quality, evidence-based, family-centered care to hospitalized pediatric patients at Children's Memorial Hermann Hospital in the Texas Medical

Center and Greater Houston. We prioritize effective communication, professionalism, excellent patient care, ongoing patient safety, and quality improvement initiatives. We also provide strong education for pediatricians, students, families, colleagues, and other medical professionals through curriculum development and educational research, 24-hour on-site clinical care, and supervision of residents and students.

Program Highlights: Academic program with a large pediatric residency program and a PHM fellowship. Children's Memorial Hermann Hospital is a 322-bed quaternary care women and children's hospital located within the Texas Medical Center with 138 Pediatric beds, including the first Level I pediatric trauma center in the region, 118 NICU beds, and 76 Women's Center beds Primary pediatric teaching site for UT McGovern

Medical School, the 9th largest medical school in the United States. Full complement of pediatric subspecialty services. Children's Memorial Hermann Hospital is Nationally Ranked in US News & World Report Best Children's Hospitals in Neurology and Neurosurgery, Cardiology and Heart Surgery, Gastroenterology and GI Surgery, Neonatology, and Orthopedics. No delivery or nursery responsibilities. Supportive mentorship

program. Growing Population Health, Advocacy, and Behavioral Health Care Initiatives. Collaborative work environment. Excellent work-life balance. Outstanding University of Texas System benefits. Competitive compensation package. Epic EHR. Nursing Magnet Status. Position Key Accountabilities: Average of 32 clinical hours per week. Primarily day shifts; 2-3 nights per month on average.
