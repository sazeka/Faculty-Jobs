UNIV - Open Rank Faculty - Maternal Fetal Medicine - Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology

Medical University of South Carolina  ·  Open Rank Faculty — Maternal Fetal Medicine — Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology  ·  Charleston, SC
Open RankMaternal Fetal Medicine

Position summary

Job Description Summary As a Maternal-Fetal Medicine sub-specialist, your clinical responsibilities will consist primarily of seeing high-risk OB/GYN patients in an outpatient setting, reading and performing ultrasounds, supervising genetic counseling, and performing appropriate MFM surgical and invasive procedures. In addition, you will provide admission, discharge, and rounding on inpatients on the obstetrics

(including labor and delivery) service. You will also be responsible for providing consultative services for OB/GYN patients for a wide variety of referring physicians from MUSC and throughout South Carolina. You will provide the above services Monday through Friday at the Prenatal Wellness Center or our outreach clinics. In addition, you will be expected to take MFM and OB/GYN call when assigned or as needed by the

Department. You may also be responsible for traveling to other parts of South Carolina to perform MFM services for contracts for your division.
