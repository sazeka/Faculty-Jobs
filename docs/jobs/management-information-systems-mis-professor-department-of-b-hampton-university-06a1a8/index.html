Management Information Systems (MIS) Professor – Department of Business Administration and Accounting Hampton University Faculty The Hampton University Department of Business Administration and Accounting invites nominations and applications for the position of Assistant Professor in Management Information Systems for Spring 2026. Founded in 1868, Hampton University is a leading historically black university (HBCU)

located on the Virginia Peninsula in the City of Hampton. It is a privately endowed, co-educational, nonsectarian institution. The five-year MBA program comprises 157 academic hours of coursework, including an embedded undergraduate degree in Business Administration (Bachelor of Science). Competencies in the five-year MBA are achieved through an interdisciplinary approach. This approach encompasses a liberal

education curriculum and a comprehensive background in accounting, technology, and engineering-based business. The program also includes structured and varied work experiences through internships. Professional application and refinement activities are required to ensure business sophistication, internalize professional and critical thinking skills, and build strong moral character.
