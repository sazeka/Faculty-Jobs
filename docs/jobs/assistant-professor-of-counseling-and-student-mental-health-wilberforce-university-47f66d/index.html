Position Summary Wilberforce University invites applications for a full-time Assistant Professor of Counseling and Student Mental Health Clinician to advance student well-being, personal growth, and academic success. This position offers a meaningful opportunity for a compassionate mental health professional to positively shape students’ lives through accessible, confidential, and culturally responsive counseling,

crisis intervention, and individualized support. The successful candidate will help students navigate personal challenges, develop resilience, and strengthen their sense of belonging while contributing to a campus environment where students can thrive. The position carries faculty status, with primary responsibility for direct, in-person clinical services and collaboration with campus and community partners.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities Clinical Counseling and Student Support Provide confidential, individual counseling and therapeutic support for students experiencing anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, adjustment difficulties, substance misuse, and other personal or emotional challenges. Conduct clinical assessments, develop individualized treatment plans, and provide appropriate short-term counseling and

follow-up services. Assist students in developing healthy coping strategies, emotional resilience, and skills that support personal well-being and academic success. Maintain accessible on-campus counseling hours and provide timely appointments for students seeking professional assistance. Coordinate referrals to specialized mental health, psychiatric, substance use treatment, and community support services when

appropriate. Maintain confidential clinical records in accordance with professional standards and applicable regulations. Crisis Intervention and Student Well-Being Provide professional assessment and intervention for students experiencing significant emotional distress, suicidal ideation, or other mental health crises. Collaborate with designated University personnel and community providers to ensure appropriate

crisis response, safety planning, and continuity of care. Assist in strengthening campus procedures for identifying, referring, and supporting students experiencing mental health challenges. Provide appropriate follow-up and support to students affected by traumatic events or campus emergencies.
