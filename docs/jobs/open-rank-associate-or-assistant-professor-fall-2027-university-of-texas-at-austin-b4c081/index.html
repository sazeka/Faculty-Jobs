Open Rank - Associate or Assistant Professor Fall 2027Apply now DescriptionThe Department of Mathematics at the University of Texas at Austin invites applications for two positions at the rank of tenure-track Assistant Professor or of Associate Professor with tenure, with the intent of appointing the two successful candidates as a Fellow of the Sid W. Richardson Foundation Regents Chair in Mathematics #2 and #3,

respectively. Highly qualified senior candidates at the full professor level are welcome to apply. Each position is a nine-month appointment with a start date of August 16, 2027. The department is interested in candidates who will contribute to excellence in mentoring through teaching, research, and service. The minimum annual 9-month salary is $120,000 with Health and Retirement Benefits. The salary can be

negotiated based on experience. The applications review process will start on October 19, 2026. Applications will be reviewed on a continuous basis until both positions are filled.QualificationsQualified candidates must have a Ph.D. degree and comparable experience with a strong research focus in mathematics and have publications in high-quality outlets, possess a research agenda that is well-defined and shows

promise for strong external funding potential. In addition, the candidates should have positive evidence of excellence in mathematics education. Preference will be given to applicants whose research interests can integrate and broaden the current research areas represented in the department. Curriculum Vitae Teaching statement Research statement Publication List Four signed letters of recommendation (to be submitted online by the reference writers at this site with one letter speaking

to the applicant’s teaching credentials) Background Checks: A criminal history background check will be required for finalist(s) under consideration for this position.
