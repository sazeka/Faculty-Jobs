Assistant Professor - Psychological Science and Counseling - PsyD Program Clarksville, TN, United States Be the First to Apply Job Description The Department of Psychological Science and Counseling at Austin Peay State University (APSU) seeks to fill two tenure-track faculty positions in Counseling Psychology with a start date of July 30, 2027. This position will serve our American Psychological Association (APA)

accredited PsyD program in Counseling Psychology. The program offers a generalist track as well as a concentration in Serving Military Personnel, Veterans, and their Families. The persons hired for this position will have a unique opportunity to help shape an innovative, forward-looking doctoral training program in health service psychology. Strong candidates will have a deep commitment to the foundational values

that characterize the field of counseling psychology: a holistic, contextual, strengths-based approach to intervention; an emphasis on integrating science and practice; and a steadfast commitment to culturally responsive practice and social justice. A doctorate (PsyD or PhD) in Counseling Psychology (preferred) or Clinical Psychology (acceptable) from an APA-accredited program is required. Preferred candidates will

be licensed as a psychologist HSP or be license-eligible in TN within 1 year of hire. Support is available for postdoctoral hours accrual. Clinical, teaching, and research areas are open, but interest and expertise in health psychology, consultation, ethics, geropsychology, cognitive and intellectual assessment, counseling children and adolescents, and/or counseling couples and families, as well as interest and/or

experience working with military populations is desirable but not required. All APSU faculty are expected to be engaged in university and community service, and scholarly activities. Faculty in the PsyD program also provide clinical supervision and consultation to doctoral students engaged in clinical work. The university has plentiful funding opportunities to help support research, teaching, and professional

development. Additional information about the position is found here: https://www.apsu.edu/psychology/psyd/. The program is housed in the new state-of-the-art Health Professions Building, which includes an in-house clinic that serves as both a training facility and a site for providing mental health services to the campus and community.
