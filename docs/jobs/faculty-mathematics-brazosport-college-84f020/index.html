Overview Level Experienced Position Type Full Time, Tenure Track Education Level Master's Degree Category Education Reports To Chair of the Division of Mathematics and Sciences Description Brazosport College, a two-time Top 10 finalist for the Aspen Prize for Community College Excellence and an Achieving the Dream Leader College, seeks a dynamic, student-centered, and innovative individual to join the Mathematics

faculty. This full-time, tenure-track faculty position will support instruction in developmental mathematics, core mathematics, and freshman and sophomore-level mathematics courses. The successful candidate will demonstrate a commitment to community college teaching, student success, academic rigor, and effective instruction across a variety of student populations and course modalities. This position will report to

the Division Chair for Mathematics and Sciences. Responsibilities Include Preparing curriculum, classroom instruction, assignments, and assessments for mathematics courses using a variety of traditional, active learning, and innovative teaching methods. Teaching a range of mathematics courses, which may include developmental mathematics, co-requisite mathematics support courses, College Algebra, Quantitative

Reasoning, Statistics, Precalculus, Calculus, and other freshman and sophomore-level mathematics courses as assigned. Teaching courses in multiple instructional formats, including face-to-face, blended, hybrid, online, dual credit, and other alternative delivery formats as needed. Teaching on campus and, when assigned, at offsite locations, including local high schools and dual credit partner sites. Supporting

student success through effective instruction, regular communication, timely feedback, academic support, and appropriate student advisement. Participating in curriculum development, course redesign, assessment of student learning outcomes, and continuous improvement efforts within the Mathematics department and Division. Collaborating with mathematics faculty, division leadership, advising, testing, and student

support areas to strengthen placement, persistence, completion, and transfer readiness for mathematics students. Maintaining scheduled office hours and campus presence in accordance with college expectations. Assisting in the development, review, and improvement of common course materials, LMS course shells (Brightspace Desire2Learn), shared assessments, and instructional resources as appropriate.
