Postdoctoral Research Fellows

Albert Einstein College of Medicine  ·  Bronx, NY
PostdoctoralNon-Tenure

Position summary

Postdoctoral Research Fellows Benefits Postdoctoral research fellows of the Albert Einstein College of Medicine are offered a bundled package of benefits that include medical, dental, vision and life insurance coverage. Postdoctoral research fellows not supported by an NRSA training grant, have the option to participate in several of the pre-tax programs offered by the College. Medical Plan We offer the choice of two

medical plans – The MontePrime EPO and MonteCare PPO Plans. Both plans utilize providers in the Montefiore network and the Anthem PPO network. Employee cost share will be higher for Anthem providers. Enrollment in the MontePrime EPO plan requires many of the services to be provided within the Montefiore network (except in the case of a bona fide emergency). The MonteCare PPO Plan provides the flexibility to use

providers and facilities outside of the Montefiore and Anthem networks, but the cost will be higher for healthcare services received out of network. Prescription Drug Benefits The prescription drug benefits are included with medical coverage and are administered by Anthem. In addition to the retail pharmacies in the Anthem network, and the Anthem home delivery services, employees can use the Montefiore Specialty

Pharmacy to fill their prescriptions. Prescriptions filled at the Montefiore Specialty Pharmacy are delivered to the Weiler Hospital Pharmacy on the Einstein Campus daily.The MontePrime EPO Plan provides prescription drug benefits only through the Montefiore Specialty Pharmacy. However, drugs purchased at a retail pharmacy may be eligible for reimbursement on an exception basis. Dental Benefits We offer the choice of

two dental plans administered by Cigna; the Cigna DHMO Dental Plan and Cigna PPO Dental plan. Vision Benefits We provide coverage in the high option vision plan administered by Spectera, UnitedHealth Care’s vision program. The plan provides benefits for routine eye exams, eyeglasses or contact lenses. Life Insurance Benefits Life insurance is designed to pay a benefit to an employee’s named beneficiary if the

postdoctoral research fellow dies from any cause while coverage is in effect. Einstein provides Basic Life insurance equal to $50,000. Disability Benefits Disability benefits continue part or all of a postdoctoral research fellow’s earnings if they are ill or injured and unable to work. Short term disability benefits include paid sick leave and supplemental sick pay for up to 26 weeks. Reimbursement Programs Flexible
