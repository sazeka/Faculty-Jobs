Position description UCSF School of Medicine and UCSF Benioff Children's Hospitals Division of Allergy, Immunology, Bone Marrow Transplantation Department of Pediatrics Adjunct, Clinical X, HS Clinical, and In Residence Faculty Series The Division of Allergy, Immunology, and Bone Marrow Transplantation within the Department of Pediatrics at the University of

California, San Francisco, and UCSF Benioff Children’s Hospitals is recruiting for faculty positions at the Instructor, Assistant, Associate, and Full Professor ranks in the Adjunct, Clinical X, HS Clinical, or In Residence faculty series, with a focus on Allergy/Immunology. Academic rank is commensurate with experience. Selected candidates will join a major academic medical center and established fellowship training

program. This position may have clinical responsibilities at both the San Francisco and Oakland campuses and would actively contribute to the Department's mission of excellence in research, education, and patient care. As part of our dynamic Division of Allergy, Immunology, and Bone Marrow Transplantation, you will have the opportunity to shape the future of pediatric allergy and immunology care while working

alongside world-class colleagues. Clinical Opportunities Include: Primary immunodeficiency diagnosis and management State-of-the-art cellular therapy treatment for inborn errors of immunity Comprehensive food allergy program including oral immunotherapy Drug allergy and desensitization services Multidisciplinary clinics in primary immune regulatory disease (PIRD) and eosinophilic gastrointestinal disease (EGID)

Specialized Facilities and Resources: State-of-the-art pediatric procedure clinic Advanced diagnostic immunology laboratories Dedicated infusion center for biologic therapy Robust collaborative research facilities and with academic and industry partners Primary Immune Deficiency Treatment Consortium (PIDTC) lead center Position Highlights: Faculty appointment at Instructor, Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor
