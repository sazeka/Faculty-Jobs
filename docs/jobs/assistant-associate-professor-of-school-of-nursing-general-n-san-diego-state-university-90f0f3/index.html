PTOC Assistant/Associate Professor of School of Nursing - General Nursing Apply now Job no: 562649 Work type: Instructional Faculty – Tenured/Tenure-Track Location: San Diego Categories: Unit 3 - CFA - California Faculty Association, Tenured/Tenure-Track, Full Time, Faculty - Health Sciences Position description The San Diego State University School of Nursing (SON) seeks to hire an open-rank, tenure-track assistant

or associate professor with expertise in the General Nursing Field. Applicants must hold an earned doctorate [PhD] in nursing or in a related field with a Master’s in Nursing. Apply via PageUp by November 30, 2026 to receive full consideration. The position will remain open until filled. To learn more, visit the SDSU School of Nursing website. The SON offers dynamic educational programs, employs creative and

innovative teaching methods, and provides faculty with opportunities to conduct research and pursue external funding. The SON offers prelicensure BSN and RN-to-BS programs and a Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) program with Family Nurse Practitioner, Adult-Gerontology Primary Care Nurse Practitioner, Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner, Adult-Gerontology Acute Care Clinical Nurse Specialist, and

post-master's pathways. The successful candidate will contribute to undergraduate and doctoral nursing education, maintain a program of research and scholarship, and engage in service to the School, University, profession, and community. The SON connects faculty and students with more than 100 community partners throughout San Diego and Imperial Valley Counties and beyond, providing strong opportunities for clinical

education, community engagement, interprofessional collaboration, and research.
