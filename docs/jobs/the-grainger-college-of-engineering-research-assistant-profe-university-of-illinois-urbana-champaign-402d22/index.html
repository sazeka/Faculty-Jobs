Projects in the group include those supported by The Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health

(ARPA-H), National Science Foundation (NSF), and National Institutes of Health (NIH). The successful candidate will carry out responsibilities related to research and public service. As part of the larger research group, they will carry out research and educational administration to support the entire team. They will be expected to also establish and maintain an active and independent research program. Research track

faculty in the Department of Bioengineering are expected to provide professional leadership within the department, other campus units, and the professional community. There is a strong track record of support for this research group from NSF, NIH and ARPA-H at Illinois, and we are seeking applications from individuals who are eager to engage with this collaborative community. Significant funding has resulted in a

demand for dedicated scientific leadership, and the individual in this role will provide leadership, project management, supervision of personnel, and sustained growth of the research enterprise which provide outstanding opportunities related to this research. This role is critical to the successful execution and long-term sustainability of funded research activities. This position joins a department that is

experiencing a period of programmatic growth, including significant increases in course offerings and student numbers in both graduate and undergraduate programs. With the completion of new facilities and strong partnerships with Carle Illinois College of Medicine and the Cancer Center at Illinois, there are numerous opportunities to impact valuable research.
