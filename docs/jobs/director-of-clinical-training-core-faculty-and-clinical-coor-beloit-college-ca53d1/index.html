Health Counseling (CMHC) program. This is an opportunity to build the program's clinical training infrastructure from the ground up and to establish the practicum and internship partnerships, structures, and culture that will define students' transition from classroom to practice at a liberal arts institution that genuinely values faculty expertise and intellectual vision. We are seeking a counselor educator who

understands that clinical training is where professional identity, ethical practice, and community-responsive counseling come together. This person will carry the responsibilities of a core faculty member and, in addition, lead the coordination of the program's practicum and internship experiences. The program's animating commitments include: social justice as a foundational orientation, embedded throughout the

curriculum and clinical training; attention to underserved communities and the mental health workforce needs of south-central Wisconsin and the broader region, including Chicago, Milwaukee, and Madison; interprofessional and interdisciplinary education, consistent with Beloit's institution-wide pedagogical identity; and community-based, experiential learning as a core program feature. About Beloit College Beloit

College is a selective liberal arts college in Beloit, Wisconsin. Nationally recognized for intellectual rigor and innovation, Beloit was ranked #18 among the nation's most innovative schools by U.S. News & World Report in 2026. Its graduates are known for their capacity to think across disciplines, engage with complexity, and connect knowledge to real-world impact. The School of Health Sciences, established in 2023,

reflects Beloit's commitment to preparing health professionals who are not only clinically excellent but broadly educated and community-minded. Interdisciplinary collaboration, experiential learning, and community-based practice are foundational to everything we do. The MA in Clinical Mental Health Counseling is the School's first graduate-level program, a significant milestone and a serious institutional commitment.
