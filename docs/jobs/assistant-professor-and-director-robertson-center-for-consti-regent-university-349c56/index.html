Assistant Professor & Director, Robertson Center for Constitutional Law Apply now Job no: 50000376 Work type: Full time Location: Virginia Beach Categories: Faculty & Adjunct, School of Law Regent University School of Law invites applications for the position of Director of the Robertson Center for Constitutional Law. The Robertson Center builds on that legacy, advancing first principles in constitutional law through scholarship, litigation, and public engagement.
