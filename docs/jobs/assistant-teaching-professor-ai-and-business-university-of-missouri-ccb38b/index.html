upon satisfactory performance, programmatic needs, and available funding. The start date of the appointment will be January 2027.This position is ideal for candidates who are passionate about applied artificial intelligence, enjoy teaching and mentoring, and are motivated by the opportunity to shape how future business leaders understand and use AI in organizational settings.Duties include teaching undergraduate- and

master’s-level courses focused on artificial intelligence and its applications in business, with an emphasis on experiential learning and hands-on use of contemporary AI tools and platforms. Courses are intended for business students and focus on managerial understanding and applied problem-solving, rather than technical model development. The successful candidate will have substantial autonomy in course design and

will be encouraged to continuously update course content to reflect the rapidly evolving AI landscape. of Business, supporting the integration of AI-related tools, assignments, and pedagogical approaches into existing courses. This may include consultations, workshops, and the development of shared instructional resources.
