Position Type: Full-time College Faculty appointment, open rank Start Date: Spring or Fall 2027 Position Summary The Department of Music at Abilene Christian University invites applications for a full-time College Faculty position in Collaborative Piano. The successful candidate will serve as a primary collaborative pianist for the Department of Music, working closely with students and faculty across vocal and

instrumental areas. The primary responsibility of this position is to provide high-level collaborative piano support for student instruction and performance. The position may also include limited classroom or applied teaching, such as class piano or other courses appropriate to the candidate’s expertise, as load and departmental needs allow. Responsibilities Provide collaborative piano support for applied lessons,

rehearsals, coachings, juries, pre-recital hearings, degree recitals, departmental recitals, auditions, and other student performances. Collaborate with vocalists and instrumentalists across the Department of Music. Serve as pianist and music director for opera productions and scenes. Accompany performance labs and other departmental activities as assigned. Coach students in musical preparation and effective

collaboration. Collaborate with faculty and guest artists as appropriate. Teach a limited number of courses, such as class piano or other courses appropriate to the candidate’s expertise, as load and departmental needs allow. Participate in recruitment and other activities supporting the life and mission of the Department of Music. Support the mission of ACU through Christ-centered teaching, service, and mentoring of

students. Required Qualifications Master’s degree in collaborative piano or a related field. High level of keyboard proficiency and musicianship. Significant experience collaborating with soloists and ensembles. Excellent sight-reading and score-reading skills. Ability to prepare a broad range of repertoire efficiently and professionally.
