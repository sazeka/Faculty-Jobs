Position Title: Race, Ethnicity, Gender, and Sexuality Studies: Visiting Assistant Professor in Critical Hmong Studies Job Category: Academic Staff Employment Type: Terminal (Fixed Term) Job Profile: Lecturer Job Duties: Position The Race, Ethnicity, Gender,

and Sexuality Studies (REGSS) Department in the College of Arts and Sciences at the University of Wisconsin-Eau Claire seeks to hire a Visiting Assistant Professor in Critical Hmong Studies. This two-year position begins on August 13, 2027. The position may be extended to a third year. We seek applicants whose research interests, qualifications, and/or experience relate to one or more of the following: Commitment to

an intersectional approach to Hmong and Hmong American issues, especially as they relate to race, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, social class, indigeneity and decolonization, disability, etc. Interest in the educational development and mentoring of Critical Hmong Studies students and students from affiliated organizations on campus. Required Qualifications Earned Ph.D. or Ed.D. in Asian American Studies, American

Studies, Ethnic Studies, Women’s, Gender, & Sexuality Studies, Education Studies, History, Sociology, or other closely related field; or MFA in writing, visual art, multimedia arts, and/or performing arts. ABD candidates will be considered if the doctorate is complete before the start of the contract. Excellence in undergraduate teaching. Preferred Qualifications Ability to communicate in Hmong and/or teach Hmong

language courses. Interest in doing outreach and working with Hmong American communities at the university and surrounding region. Interest in leading immersion trips, domestic and international, related to Critical Hmong Studies. Responsibilities The teaching load for this position includes 4 classes per semester totaling 12 credit hours. The successful candidate will teach Critical Hmong Studies courses in the

REGSS department, such as: Hmong American Experiences in the U.S.; and Intersectionality: Critical Approaches to Identity and Injustice. The successful candidate will also teach courses in departments or programs based on disciplinary expertise (e.g., LGBTQ Studies, Art and Design, Sociology, Music and Theatre, Languages, History, Anthropology).
