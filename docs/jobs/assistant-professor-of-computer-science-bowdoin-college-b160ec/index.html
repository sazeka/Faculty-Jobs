Posting Details Posting Details Title Assistant Professor of Computer Science Posting Number F00312JP Department Computer Science Position Summary Bowdoin College’s Department of Computer Science

invites applications for a full-time tenure-track faculty appointment in Computer Science at the Assistant Professor level, beginning July 1, 2027. We seek candidates whose research involves artificial intelligence (AI) or interdisciplinary work involving AI. We especially encourage those whose work intersects with trustworthy AI (e.g., explainability, interpretability, or privacy in AI models) or natural language

processing (NLP) to apply. The teaching load is two courses per semester. This hire will teach at all levels of the Computer Science curriculum, including introductory courses as well as advanced courses within the candidate’s area of expertise. Bowdoin is particularly interested in candidates dedicated to undergraduate teaching and with a promise of long-term successful research. A Ph.D. in Computer Science or a

related field is expected by the date of the appointment. Bowdoin’s Hastings Initiative for AI and Humanity aims to critically examine, thoughtfully utilize, and ethically guide AI’s trajectory. A $50 million dollar gift by Reed Hastings funds ten new faculty positions, including this position, across disciplines; supports faculty in AI-related teaching, research, and creative work; sponsors workshops, symposia, and

student research; and prepares the next generation of leaders to engage responsibly with the opportunities and challenges presented by artificial intelligence. Bowdoin College offers opportunities for professional development, a fully-funded, year-long pre-tenure sabbatical leave (after three years of teaching and successful reappointment), and regular, generously funded, post-tenure sabbaticals.
