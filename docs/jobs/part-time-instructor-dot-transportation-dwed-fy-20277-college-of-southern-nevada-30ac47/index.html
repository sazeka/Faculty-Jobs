Job Description Ensures public safety and regulatory compliance by inspecting commercial vehicles, transportation infrastructure, and operational practices. “Ensure public safety, infrastructure integrity, and compliance with DOT standards…”

Key Responsibilities Conduct inspections aligned with FMCSR, OSHA, and DOT regulations. “Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations (FMCSR)” Perform pre‑trip, en‑route, and post‑trip vehicle inspections. Verify Hours of Service, ELD data, permits, and hazardous materials placarding. Support roadways, pavement, and NBIS bridge inspection activities. “Bridge inspection standards (NBIS)” Document findings using digital

reporting systems. Communicate clearly with drivers and the public; apply de‑escalation techniques. Required Nevada Certifications These are the standard certifications for DOT‑aligned inspection roles in Nevada: Nevada Commercial Vehicle Safety Alliance (CVSA) Inspector Certification — Required for commercial motor vehicle enforcement and safety inspections. Nevada DOT Level I–III Inspector Credentials — Covers full

vehicle, driver, and hazardous materials inspections. Hazardous Materials (HazMat) Inspection Endorsement — Required for placarding and hazardous materials compliance checks. Hazardous material placarding NBIS Bridge Inspection Certification — Required for bridge and structural inspections. OSHA 10 or OSHA 30 — Required for safety compliance roles.
