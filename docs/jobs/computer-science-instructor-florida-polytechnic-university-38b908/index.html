Computer Science Instructor JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY Florida Polytechnic University invites applications for a full-time, tenure-equivalent Instructor of Computer Science, beginning August 2027. JOB DESCRIPTION Responsibilities Teach introductory programming courses (C, Python), Object-Oriented Programming (C++), Data Structures and Algorithms, with opportunities to teach other courses in computer science,

cybersecurity, and information technology curricula. Collaborate with colleagues in coordinated, multi-section courses, and coordinate a multi-section course when assigned. Maintain current course sites in Canvas and complete course-related administrative work on schedule. Teach sections scheduled across the full day, which may include 8:00 a.m. and evening sections. Contribute to department and University service.

Minimum qualifications Master's or doctoral degree in computer science, cybersecurity, information technology, or a closely related field, with at least 18 graduate credit hours in computer science. Experience teaching college-level computer science, cybersecurity, or information technology as the instructor of record. Evidence of effective college-level teaching. Excellent oral and written communication skills.

Preferred qualifications Experience teaching in or coordinating multi-section computer science, cybersecurity, or information technology courses. Use of active learning or other evidence-based teaching practices. Experience developing course materials, common assessments, or instructional resources. Experience supporting students who arrive with a wide range of background preparation in programming. Key dates Start

date: August 2027 Full consideration: applications received by January 11, 2027. Review continues until the positions are filled.
