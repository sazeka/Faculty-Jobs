Pulmonologist with an emphasis in Sleep Medicine. The ideal candidate would have an MD or MD/PhD, be board-certified or board eligible in Pediatric Pulmonology as well as sleep medicine, and qualify for a USC faculty appointment at the Assistant Professor level.The Division of Pediatric Pulmonology and Sleep Medicine has fifteen full-time and one part-time faculty members. Each faculty member has a clinical and

research profile that is tailored to their specific interests. The Division includes a Cystic Fibrosis Center, Pulmonary Function and Exercise Physiology Laboratory, Sleep Center, Infant Pulmonary Function Testing, Bronchoscopy Program, Home Mechanical Ventilation Program, Diaphragmatic Pacing Program, and is affiliated with a large chronic care facility. Our sleep center is accredited by the American Academy of

Sleep Medicine (AASM). We perform over 2,500 polysomnographies a year and maintain a sleep disorders consultation clinic. We currently have five triple boarded sleep specialists. We have an ACGME sleep collaboration with the KSOM LAC+USC/University Hospital sleep fellowship program with two fellows per year dividing their time between the two sites. The recruited faculty member would have specific responsibilities as

part of the sleep center team. The division has dedicated nurse specialists, social workers, dieticians, respiratory care practitioners, and research support staff. The Division has an ACGME accredited fellowship program with 6 post-graduate pulmonary fellows.
