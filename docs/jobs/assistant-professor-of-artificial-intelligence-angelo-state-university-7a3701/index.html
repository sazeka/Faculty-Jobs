Assistant Professor of Artificial Intelligence Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Details Position Information Job Title Assistant Professor of Artificial Intelligence Position Number 977278, 977301 Department Computer Science Salary Commensurate with qualifications and experience Remote No Job Summary The Department of Computer Science at Angelo State University invites applications for

two full-time, nine-month, tenure-track Assistant Professor positions in Artificial Intelligence, beginning in August 2027. The successful candidates will support undergraduate and graduate programs in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science through teaching, research, student mentoring, curriculum development, and service.Applications are encouraged from candidates across the full breadth of artificial

intelligence, including both software- and hardware-oriented areas. Relevant fields may include machine learning, generative AI, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, autonomous systems, edge and embedded AI, computer architecture, AI accelerators, neuromorphic computing, and hardware-software co-design.Each faculty member will normally teach three courses per semester, maintain an active research

agenda, pursue external funding, mentor students, and contribute to interdisciplinary AI initiatives across the university. The academic appointment, tenure home, and formal evaluation pathway are within the Department of Computer Science. Essential Duties Teach three undergraduate and/or graduate courses per semester in artificial intelligence, computer science, data science, computer engineering, or related areas,

as assigned by the Department Chair. Develop and continuously improve courses, laboratories, assignments, and other instructional materials that support Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science programs. Maintain an active research and scholarly agenda in artificial intelligence or a related field, including software-, systems-, or hardware-oriented research. Develop and submit proposals for external funding and

participate in externally funded research, educational, or workforce-development initiatives. Mentor undergraduate and graduate students in research, capstone projects, applied AI projects, professional development, and career preparation. Collaborate with faculty across Computer Science, Engineering, and other disciplines on AI-related research, curriculum, student projects, and institutional initiatives.
