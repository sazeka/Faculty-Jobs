Instructor of Radiologic Technology

University of Holy Cross  ·  New Orleans, LA
InstructorRadiologic Technology

Position summary

Instructor of Radiologic Technology Reports to:Provost and Vice-President for Academic Affairs, Dean of Academic Affairs, and Chair of the Department of Health Sciences; Directly to Radiologic Technology Program Director Supervises:Radiologic Technology Students Basic Function:Provides didactic, clinical/laboratory instruction and clinical supervision to current students. Provides evaluation of and advisement to

current students. Participates in periodic review and development of the program. Qualifications:Position requires a bachelor’s degree; a master’s degree is preferred. Must possess current, unrestricted certification with the American Registry of Radiologic Technologists and licensure from the Louisiana State Radiologic Technology Board of Examiners. Two years of full-time professional experience as a radiographer is

required. Major Duties: Foster the Mission of the University in teaching, research and service. Conduct oneself in a professional and respectful manner. Prepare and plan lecture and clinical/laboratory instruction in the assigned Radiologic Technology program courses in accordance with course curriculum and learning outcomes. Prepare and/or maintain current course syllabi for courses taught. Prepare and correct

student examinations and work assignments. Evaluate student performance in lecture and clinical/laboratory courses. Facilitate student clinical learning at clinical sites in accordance with accreditation requirements. Work with clinical preceptors and/or managers at clinical sites to create equitable learning opportunities for students, ensure clinical staff is knowledgeable of proper student supervision and

competency testing rules and foster a positive learning environment for all. Clinical teaching and supervision workload require duties of a radiologic technologist. Travel to educational facilities is required. Meet all regularly scheduled classes. Post and keep at least six regular office hours (Adjunct faculty should be available to students outside of class at mutually convenient times.). Provide student academic

advisement. Schedule student conferences to review individual student progress. Submit grades according to prescribed guidelines. Handle student documentation, evaluation and attendance records. Support Program Director in evaluation and revision of program curriculum, learning outcomes and program assessment. Assist in student recruitment, applicant and community activities.
