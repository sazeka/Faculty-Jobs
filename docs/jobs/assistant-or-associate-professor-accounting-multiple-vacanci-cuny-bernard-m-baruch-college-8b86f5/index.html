Assistant or Associate Professor- Accounting (Multiple Vacancies) FACULTY VACANCY ANNOUNCEMENT "At Baruch College we believe that student success is everyone’s responsibility” The Stan Ross Department of Accountancy is seeking applicants for two tenure-track Assistant Professor positions and one tenure-track Assistant or Associate Professor position in Accounting starting Fall 2027, subject to final budget approval.

The successful candidates will engage in high-quality scholarly research, teach undergraduate and graduate courses in accounting, mentor students (undergraduate, master’s, and Ph.D.), and share responsibility for committee and department assignments. QUALIFICATIONS Successful candidates must have a Ph.D. in Accounting or related fields. Candidates must also demonstrate an ability to conduct high-quality original

scholarly research in accounting, the ability to teach successfully, and to cooperate with colleagues for the good of the institution. Applicants should be able to teach in multiple modalities (in-person, online, hybrid) and participate in Zicklin programs offered both on campus and with our global partners. COMPENSATION Assistant Professor: $205,073- $220,073 Associate Professor: $225,000- $240,000 CUNY offers

faculty a competitive compensation and benefits package covering health insurance, pension and retirement benefits, paid parental leave, and savings programs. We also provide mentoring and support for research, scholarship, and publication as part of our commitment to ongoing faculty professional development. HOW TO APPLY Visit www.cuny.edu, access the employment page, log in or create a new user account, and search

for this vacancy using the Job ID or Title. Select "Apply Now" and provide the requested information. To ensure consideration, applications must be received via the official CUNY application system. Applicants are required to submit a philosophy of teaching that includes but is not limited to their commitment to and experience with teaching students in a campus environment with a substantial population of students

who are among the first-generation of their family to attend a college or university. Applicants are also required to submit a CV and a sample of the candidates academic writing (e.g., published papers, working papers etc) CLOSING DATE December 1, 2026 with review of resumes to begin September 10, 2026 JOB SEARCH CATEGORY CUNY Job Posting: Faculty EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY CUNY encourages people with disabilities,
