and academic leader to serve as Clinical Professor and Chief of Adult Psychiatry. This senior faculty position provides strategic, clinical, operational, and academic leadership for Adult Psychiatry programs across the UF Health system. The Chief of Adult Psychiatry will oversee a comprehensive portfolio of inpatient, outpatient, and specialty psychiatric services while advancing excellence in patient care,

education, research, faculty development, and program growth. In addition to divisional leadership responsibilities, and depending on interest and clinical area of

specialty, the incumbent may also serve as Chief of Inpatient Psychiatry and Medical Director of UF Health Shands Psychiatric Hospital, or as the Medical Director of the Adult Outpatient Psychiatry Clinic. Key Responsibilities Strategic and Administrative Leadership Provide strategic and operational leadership as Chief of Adult Psychiatry, overseeing adult psychiatric services across inpatient, outpatient,

Depending on expertise and interest, serve as Chief of Inpatient Psychiatry and Medical Director of UF Health Shands Psychiatric Hospital. Collaborate with

Recruit, mentor, and develop faculty and staff while fostering a culture of excellence, collaboration, and accountability.
