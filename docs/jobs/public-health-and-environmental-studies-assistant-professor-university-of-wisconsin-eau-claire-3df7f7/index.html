Position Title: Public Health and Environmental Studies: Assistant Professor in Environmental Public Health Job Category: Faculty Employment Type: Regular Job Profile: Assistant Professor Job Duties: POSITION A tenure-track faculty position at the rank of

assistant professor is available in the Department of Public Health and Environmental Studies in the College of Arts and Sciences. The department seeks a candidate with expertise in environmental health committed to teaching excellence and maintaining an active research and service program. This campus-based appointment carries an initial two-year probationary appointment beginning with the 2027/2028 academic year.

The starting salary for this position is $75,000. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS Earned doctorate in environmental health, public health, or related discipline. ABD applicants may be considered but must complete the terminal degree by August 1, 2027. Demonstrated potential to teach undergraduate environmental public health courses in both campus-based and online settings. Demonstrated potential for scholarship and

professional service. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS Record of professional experience in environmental health. Possession of CIH, CSP, RS/REHS or similar certification. Experience teaching and/or mentoring a broad range of students using high-impact practices, including laboratory and field work. Experience developing campus-based and online courses. Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Experience mentoring

and/or advising students in their academic and career development. Demonstrated commitment to values of accessibility and belonging. Ability to contribute to the department’s other programs is considered an asset. DUTIES Develop and teach a variety of undergraduate environmental public health courses. Example courses include Introduction to Environmental Health, Occupational Health and Safety, Hazardous and Solid

Waste, Toxicology and Risk Assessment, and Industrial Hygiene. Establish a program of scholarship that includes student collaborators. Engage in campus and community service such as extramural activities, departmental operations , and shared governance. Promote student development and academic achievement through advising, mentoring, service projects and internships. Collaborate with faculty, students, and community
