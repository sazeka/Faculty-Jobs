JobsLogin Back to all jobsVisiting Assistant Professor of Business and Psychology700 College Street, Beloit, WI, USAFull Time Email Me Similar Jobs Email Me This Job Beloit College invites applications from those with industrial-organizational psychology training and experience for a Visiting Assistant Professor of Business and Psychology position for the 2027-28 academic year. This is a benefits-eligible position

and has the possibility of renewal. The successful candidate will teach 6 courses (3 each semester) including Introduction to Psychology, a course in our Psychological Research Methods and Statistics sequence, and intermediate courses in their area of expertise, such as Behavioral Economics, Consumer Behavior and Marketing, Industrial-Organizational Psychology, Organizational Management, Organizational Leadership,

etc. We seek a colleague who has demonstrated excellence in or strong potential for teaching undergraduates, and whose pedagogies or research align with and engage our increasingly diverse student body and our interdisciplinary curriculum. A PhD in Industrial-Organizational Psychology or Applied Social Psychology, Organizational Behavior, Organizational Leadership, Program Evaluation, or a related field is preferred,

though applications from advanced ABD applicants will also be considered. Located in a diverse community close to Madison, Milwaukee, and Chicago, Beloit is a selective undergraduate liberal arts college that attracts students from across the United States and the world. The college emphasizes excellence in teaching, learning beyond the traditional classroom, international perspectives, and collaborative research

among students and faculty. It is recognized as one of the Colleges That Change Lives. Candidates should submit as one pdf an application that includes a cover letter, a statement of teaching philosophy, a CV, and a sample of teaching evaluations if there is prior teaching experience. In addition, candidates should have 3 letters of recommendation sent by recommenders to Diep Phan, Director of School of Business at

phand@beloit.edu. Review of complete applications will begin on October 16, 2026 and will continue until the position is filled. Questions about the position should be directed to Diep Phan, Director of School of Business: phand@beloit.edu or Suzanne Cox, Chair of Psychology: coxs@beloit.edu Apply Now with our quick 3 minute Application!
