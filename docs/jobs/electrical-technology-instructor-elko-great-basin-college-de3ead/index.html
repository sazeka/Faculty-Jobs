Job Description The Great Basin College School of Industrial Technology and Workforce Development

seeks a broadly trained Electrical Technology expert to complement its existing program. The position will be based in Elko, Nevada. This position will be responsible for teaching all aspects of the program. This position has an anticipated start date of October 1, 2026. The Electrical Systems Technology Instructor provides classroom, laboratory, and hands-on technical instruction to students preparing for careers in

the electrical field. The position combines technical instruction, student supervision, curriculum development, program support, industry engagement, and general faculty responsibilities. Qualifications Graduate of a certified electrical trade school is minimum, Associate’s degree or higher is preferred. Minimum of five (5) years’ experience in industrial electrical or similar Teaching experience in a community

college or technical field is preferred Responsibilities Teach all aspects of electrical program (for course list, please visit https://www.gbcnv.edu/catalog/current/programs/career-and-technical-education/elec-ct.html ) Teach and promote electrical safety Develop and teach courses in industrial and residential electrical technology, including electrical theory, electrical safety, National Electrical Code, AC/DC

circuits, motor controls, transformers, programmable logic controllers (PLCs), troubleshooting, and related electrical systems. Provide hands-on instruction and demonstrations using electrical equipment, tools, meters, motors, control systems, PLCs, and other laboratory equipment.
