SUMMARY Regis College seeks an experienced, collaborative, and mission-driven enrollment leader to serve as its next Vice President of Strategic Enrollment Management (VPSEM). The VPSEM will build upon the university's nearly century-long commitment to expanding opportunity, supporting student success, and preparing graduates for lives of purpose, leadership, and service. Founded by the Sisters of St. Joseph of

Boston in 1927, Regis has evolved into a vibrant, inclusive university known for its close-knit community, career-focused programs, and commitment to educating the whole person. Regis serves approximately 2,800 undergraduate, graduate and online learners through distinctive programs that prepare students to pursue excellence, become change agents in their communities, and serve as leaders in a more just and

compassionate society. As Regis celebrates its centennial year, the Vice President will join a dynamic and newly assembled senior leadership team led by recently inaugurated President Robin Cautin. This is a unique opportunity to contribute to a shared vision for the institution&rsquo;s second century, strengthening enrollment, advancing student success, and positioning Regis for continued growth and impact. THE

POSITION The Vice President of Strategic Enrollment Management (VPSEM) serves as the chief enrollment officer, providing strategic leadership and vision for centralized student recruitment, enrollment management, and retention. Reporting directly to the President and serving as a key member of the senior leadership team, the VPSEM will guide enrollment forecasting, financial aid and scholarship strategy, and tuition

discounting to support both access and long-term financial sustainability. The Vice President will bring a strong understanding of contemporary enrollment practices and will leverage analytics, market intelligence, and emerging technologies to strengthen recruitment, retention, and enrollment outcomes while advancing Regis College&rsquo;s mission in an increasingly competitive higher education environment. The VPSEM

oversees undergraduate, graduate, and adult student admission, financial aid, enrollment systems and operations, and enrollment communications.
