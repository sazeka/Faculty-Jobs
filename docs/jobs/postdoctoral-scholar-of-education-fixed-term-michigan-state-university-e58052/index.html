College Of Education 1172910 Working/Functional Title CTP Postdoctoral Scholar Position Summary This Postdoctoral Scholar will work under the supervision of Dr. Noreen Naseem Rodríguez to advance the work of the Creating Transformative Possibility through Asian American Studies (CTP) research team in the final two years of the project. This position offers opportunities to collaborate with dynamic education scholars

and contribute to innovative research on Asian American Studies and Ethnic Studies in K-12 schools. Successful candidates will support data collection and analysis, contribute to conference presentations and peer-reviewed publications, and organize team and research events. They will also work closely with faculty, educators, and graduate students as part of a collaborative research team, including established

partnerships with researchers at institutions across the nation. This role provides opportunities for scholarly growth, professional development and collaboration, and active engagement in a vibrant research community centered on Asian American Studies in K-12 contexts. Equal Employment Opportunity Statement All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion,

sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, citizenship, age, disability or protected veteran status.
