POSITION SPECIFICS We are searching for a decisive,

forward‑thinking Department Head to lead the Integrated Power Systems – Energy Conversion (IPS‑EC) Department within the Energy Science and Power Systems (ESPS) Division of the Undersea Systems Office (USO) at the Applied Research Laboratory at Penn State. The Department Head serves as the primary leadership interface between Division strategy and departmental execution. The role integrates people management,

technical excellence, and mission execution into a coherent unit aligned to USO and ARL goals. This is a key leadership role in a mission‑focused organization that develops next‑generation energy conversion technologies for critical national defense applications. This position may be filled as either a Research and Development Engineer or a non-tenure Research Faculty. The IPS‑EC Department Head is responsible for

the strategic direction, technical excellence, personnel development, and execution performance of multidisciplinary engineering programs. This position requires a leader capable of shaping advanced power and energy technologies while ensuring the highest standards of scientific rigor, safety, and programmatic success. Mission and Department Scope The IPS‑EC Department delivers cutting‑edge research, design,

analysis, and technology transition for energy conversion systems supporting thermal power plants for naval undersea platforms and other defense applications. The department’s core competencies include turbomachinery, heat exchangers, fluid systems, pressure vessels, structural components, heat‑engine subsystems, and compact integration of complete thermal engine systems.
