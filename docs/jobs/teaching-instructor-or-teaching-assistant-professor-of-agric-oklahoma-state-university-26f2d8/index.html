Teaching Instructor or Teaching Assistant Professor of Agricultural Education, Communications and Leadership/Biosystems and Agricultural Engineering BF9933 495375 Stillwater DIV AGRICULTRL SCI & NATURAL RESRCS (STW) Faculty Master's degree Teaching Instructor or Teaching Assistant Professor of Agricultural Education, Communications and Leadership/Biosystems and Agricultural Engineering POSITION DESCRIPTION This is an

11-month career-track, joint appointment in the departments of Agricultural Education, Communications and Leadership (AECL) and Biosystems and Agricultural Engineering (BAE) at Oklahoma State University at the rank of Instructor or Teaching Assistant Professor, depending on earned degree. This position is a full-time, 11-month appointment funded 100% Instruction with a salary commensurate with qualifications. The

start date is negotiable. MAJOR RESPONSIBILITIES The successful candidate will support the academic programs in both the AECL and BAE departments through classroom instruction, certification training, mentorship, and professional development. The candidate will be expected to support both school-based teacher preparation courses and student teacher supervision in agricultural education. The individual will be

expected to coordinate and teach the mechanization teaching modules associated with service courses provided through BAE for students in a variety of programs in and outside the Ferguson College of Agriculture. These courses include, but are not limited to, AST 3222 (Metals and Welding) and AST 3011 (Ag Structures), AST 3021 (Engines and Power), and AST 4101 (Ag Electrification). Instruction will require a hands-on

teaching approach in a laboratory intensive curriculum and coordination of teaching modules with BAE service courses involving metals and welding, field work skills, fabrication and construction, lab management, agricultural electrification, small engines, surveying, and agricultural structures. As part of instruction, it will be critical to ensure adequate technical skills associated with the certification program

tied to school-based agricultural education teachers, while also providing a level of knowledge consistent with a university-based education.
