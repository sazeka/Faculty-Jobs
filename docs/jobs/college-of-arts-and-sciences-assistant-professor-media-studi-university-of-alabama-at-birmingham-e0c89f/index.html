Status Faculty 9 Job Description The Department of Communication Studies in the College of Arts and Sciences at the University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB) invites applications for a full-time, tenure-track Assistant Professor in Media Studies to begin August 2027. We seek applications from candidates with a strong background in and commitment to critical media studies and the ability to teach multimedia production

(preferred areas include broadcasting, digital media, film, or multimedia journalism). The teaching load will be four courses per year over two semesters (2/2). Areas of specialization may include Digital Media, Advertising, Visual Storytelling, Interactive Digital Narrative, and related emerging media fields. Versatility in teaching media courses is desirable, as the successful candidate will support multiple

concentrations within the media curriculum.
