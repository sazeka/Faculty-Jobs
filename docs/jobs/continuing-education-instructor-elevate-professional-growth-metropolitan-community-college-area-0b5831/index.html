Continuing Education Instructor: Elevate Professional Growth and Leadership Program

Metropolitan Community College Area  ·  Continuing Education Division  ·  Area Wide, NE
Instructornull

Position summary

Classification Regular Minimum Pay $0.00 Compensation will be commensurate with the level of the position, education, and experience. JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY MCC’s Continuing Education Division is seeking enthusiastic and experienced non-credit instructors with leadership and management expertise to provide personal enrichment opportunities to members of our community for a new course series called Elevate. This

position will have various teaching opportunities throughout the year and will be responsible for developing and delivering engaging and practical course content that enhances students’ personal growth and enriches their lives. ABOUT ELEVATE Elevate is Metropolitan Community College's new professional growth and leadership development program designed to help individuals build the workplace skills needed to succeed

in today's evolving workforce. Through interactive workshops led by experienced industry professionals, participants strengthen their leadership capabilities, communication skills, critical thinking, workplace influence, and overall professional effectiveness. Multiple positions are available to teach for this program in a variety of subject areas focusing on developing soft skills for organizational success and

advancement. ESSENTIAL POSITION FUNCTIONS Develop and deliver engaging workshop curriculum and lesson plans aligned with Elevate's professional growth and leadership development objectives. Facilitate interactive learning experiences for adult learners in one or more professional development areas, including leadership, communication, critical thinking, emotional intelligence, workplace effectiveness,

problem-solving, strategic thinking, change management, professional presence, collaboration, project management, data analytics, and related topics. Create a positive and inclusive learning environment that encourages participation, collaboration, and skill development. Assess participant learning and provide constructive feedback to support professional growth and application of skills. Incorporate real-world

examples, practical exercises, case studies, and group activities to enhance learning outcomes. Adapt instructional methods to accommodate diverse learning styles, professional backgrounds, and experience levels. Collaborate with Continuing Education staff to support program quality, participant engagement, and continuous improvement initiatives.
