Number of Positions In Requisition 1&#xa;&#xa; Job Details The Department of Architecture at Thomas Jefferson University, together with the College of Architecture, Design, & Engineering (CADE), seeks candidates for the position of full-time (9-month) Assistant Professor of Architecture. Two positions are available on Tenure, Teacher Scholar, or Practice-Scholar tracks. Academic track and salary will be commensurate

with experience and qualifications. Candidates should have expertise in two or more of the following areas: ● comprehensive design ● building technology ● advanced fabrication and material technologies ● design computation Successful candidates will be expected to teach design studios, lecture courses, and seminars relating to their areas of expertise, while teaching at both undergraduate and graduate levels. We seek

applicants whose work and lived experiences will expand our intellectual diversity. We value candidates whose work elevates marginalized voices, embraces the humanistic dimensions of design, and engages their intersection with architecture. We encourage individuals from historically underrepresented communities including women, Native American peoples, Black and Indigenous peoples of color, Latinx, people who

identify as LGBTQIA+, veterans, and persons with disabilities to apply. Job Description Qualifications: Consideration will be given to applicants with experience in architectural practice, research, and teaching in the areas of comprehensive design, building technology, advanced fabrication, and design computation. Particular consideration will be given to those candidates who have demonstrated successful integration

of knowledge in building technology, advanced fabrication, and design computation within advanced architectural design studios. Candidates must possess all of following credentials: ● A professional degree in architecture (BArch or MArch) ● A master's degree in architecture or a related field (MArch, MS, etc.) ● Either professional licensure or a doctorate in architecture or a related field (advanced ABD candidates

will be considered.) Candidates must have a minimum of two years of professional experience in architecture. Foreign equivalents to U.S. degrees and licensure are acceptable. Uniquely qualified candidates without the minimum academic credentials who hold a professional license in architecture, with exceptional professional experience, and widely recognized professional work will also be considered.
