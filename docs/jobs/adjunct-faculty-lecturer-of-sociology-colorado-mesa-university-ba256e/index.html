Adjunct Faculty, Lecturer of Sociology

Position summary

ANNOUNCMENT OF POSITION VACANCY ABOUT THIS POSITION Summary: The Social and Behavioral Sciences department at Colorado Mesa University is seeking an adjunct instructor of sociology. Colorado Mesa University is a state-funded, largely undergraduate institution that emphasizes quality teaching. The standard course in sociology at CMU is a 3-credit hour class. The instructor will teach one course to start, with the

potential for future offerings. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES include the following. Other related duties may be assigned. Teaching introductory-level sociology courses such as Marriage and Families, General Sociology, and/or Social Problems. Teaching in other areas of specialization within the sociology discipline will be considered, requisite with the candidate’s experience and expertise. The ability to

teach in-person lecture format, as well as online. The ability to teach quality courses including delivering course content, facilitating student discussions and activities, assessing student learning, and providing timely and responsive communication and feedback to students. Salary: $600.00 - $850.00 per credit hour. Adjunct faculty are paid on a per credit hour basis, commensurate with education and experience. ‌

Minimum Qualifications Required: Master’s degree in Sociology. Experience teaching introductory-level sociology courses. Experience with basic classroom technology Preferred Qualifications: Candidates that can demonstrate excellence in teaching the required courses for this position will be given preference. Candidates with experience using the Desire 2 Learn (D2L) learning management system will be given preference.

Candidates with strong communication skills and demonstrated responsiveness to students will be given preference. DIRECT INQUIRIES CONTACT: Megan Henley, mhenley@coloradomesa.edu HOW TO APPLY: Applicants will be asked to complete an online application by creating a profile and then providing personal and demographic information. In addition, please be prepared to upload a: Cover letter Resume Copy of transcripts for

all degrees completed (if your transcripts include a social security number or birthdate, please redact that information before uploading the document. Official transcripts will be required upon hire) Colorado Mesa University is particularly interested in candidates who have experience working with students from diverse backgrounds and who have a demonstrated commitment to improving the levels of access and success
