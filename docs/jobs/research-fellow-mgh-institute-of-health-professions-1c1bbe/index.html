About the Vaccine and Immunotherapy Center The mission of the Vaccine and Immunotherapy Center (VIC) at Massachusetts General Hospital is to accelerate the development of novel, broadly applicable vaccines and immunotherapies aimed at eradicating

cancer, infectious diseases, and type I diabetes. VIC accomplishes this by: Bringing together scientists, physicians and business leaders experienced in transforming promising laboratory discoveries into therapies providing improved patient treatment. Funding selected projects and providing project management, technological transfer and practical assistance to ensure project success. Identifying high yield

translational medical research with a 2-3 year horizon for implementation in patients.
