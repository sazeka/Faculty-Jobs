Adjunct Chemistry Laboratory Faculty Print Adjunct Chemistry Laboratory Faculty Location 1 S Grove St, Westerville, OH Job Type Faculty-Part Time (Adjunct) Job Number 00506 Division Academic Affairs Department Chemistry Opening Date 07/14/2026 Description Benefits DESCRIPTION Otterbein University, Department of Chemistry is seeking applications for individuals to serve as adjunct (part-time) laboratory faculty for

the fall 2026 semester. RESPONSIBILITIES These individuals will provide instruction to undergraduate students in our non-majors’ introductory chemistry course, including basic experimentation, keeping lab notebooks, performing basic calculations and writing laboratory reports. These positions will have responsibilities that require the successful candidates to be on campus in Westerville, Ohio. These positions are

hired for about 4 hours per week for 16 weeks on Thursday afternoons from 2:40-5:35. The course runs from August 18th to December 3rd 2026. QUALIFICATIONS Minimum Required QualificationsBachelor's degree in a STEM field.Preferred QualificationsGraduate degree in chemistry or biochemistry.Compensation is a salaried rate for each semester commensurate with highest achieved degree.All applicants must apply online and

attach the required documents:CV or resume that show month and date for all education and all employment.Cover letter with personal non-work email address, US mailing address, and telephone number.Review of application materials will begin immediately, and the position will remain open until filled.Offers for the position Adjunct Laboratory Instructor are contingent on (1) a background check, and (2) receipt of an

official transcript of highest degree if not earned from Otterbein University.
