Joe R. and Teresa Lozano Long School of Medicine, UT Health San Antonio Department of Psychiatry General and Geriatric Psychiatrist The Department of Psychiatry in the Joe R. & Teresa Lozano Long School of Medicine at UT Health San Antonio is recruiting general and geriatric psychiatrists for our growing department. Candidates must be board-certified or board-eligible academic general psychiatrists to fill these

full-time Assistant Professor Non-Tenure Track positions. The faculty will be responsible for individual psychiatric clinical service duties within the outpatient UT Health faculty practice plan. Applicants must hold a valid Texas medical license. In addition, candidates must be eligible for clinical privileges with the University Health System under the appropriate staff category and must agree to abide by those

privileges as outlined by the current bylaws of the institution. Applications will be accepted until suitable qualified candidates are found. To apply for this position, please navigate to the UT Health Careers portal at: https://uthscsa.referrals.selectminds.com/faculty. Visit our website to learn more about UT Health San Antonio and the excellent benefits we offer at www.uthscsajobs.com . UT Health San Antonio is

an Equal Employment Opportunity/Affirmative Action Employer including protected veterans and persons with disabilities. All faculty appointments are designated as security sensitive positions.
