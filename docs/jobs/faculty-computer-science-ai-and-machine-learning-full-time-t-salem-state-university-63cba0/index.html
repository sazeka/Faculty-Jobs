Faculty: Computer Science, (AI and Machine Learning), Full-Time, Tenure-Track 493091 140 - Computer Science Dept Faculty: Tenured Tenure-Track On-site $90,000-$95,000 (for the rank of assistant professor) Overview The of Computer Science department at Salem State University invites applications for a full-time, tenure-track faculty position at the open rank, beginning in Fall 2027. We particularly welcome candidates

with expertise in one or more of the following areas: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Robotics. The successful candidate will demonstrate a forward-looking vision for the computing field, a strong commitment to effective teaching and student learning in a four-year undergraduate program, and excellent interpersonal and communication skills. Teaching responsibilities will include introductory,

intermediate, and advanced courses in computer science and information technology, including courses within one or more of the preferred areas of specialization. The candidate will also be expected to contribute to departmental and university service and to maintain an active program of scholarly or professional development appropriate to the position and rank. What makes Salem State University distinctive isn't just

what happens in our classrooms. It's who's in them. Our students are ambitious, diverse and often the first in their families to pursue higher education. Our faculty rise to meet that with rigorous scholarship, thoughtful teaching and a genuine investment in student growth. We're the Commonwealth's Civic Engagement University and a proud Hispanic and Minority-Serving Institution, designations we've earned and take

seriously. If you're looking for a collegial academic home where your work truly matters, Salem State delivers. Duties and Responsibilities Candidates are required to perform duties in accordance with the MSCA contract. Duties include teaching, advising, scholarship and contributions to continuously improve the department and university. The standard teaching load is 12 credit hours (approximately 4 lecture or lab

sections) per semester. The position is subject to available funding. Required Qualifications PhD in computer science or a closely related field. ABD with a master’s degree in computer science or a closely related field are welcome to apply. Conferral is required by May 2027.
