Overview The Department of Kinesiology is in the College of Health and Human Services at California State University, Fresno. The Department is one of the largest on campus and has approximately 1,000 undergraduate students pursuing Bachelor’s

degrees in Kinesiology. Undergraduate Kinesiology degrees include Exercise Science, Physical Education, and Sport Administration. In addition, the Department has approximately 75 graduate students pursuing Master’s degrees in either Athletic Training, Exercise Science, Sport and Performance Psychology, or Sport Administration. The faculty have been honored with numerous awards and recognitions in the areas of
