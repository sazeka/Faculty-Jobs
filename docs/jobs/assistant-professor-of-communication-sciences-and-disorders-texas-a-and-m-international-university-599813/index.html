Job Title Assistant Professor of Communication Sciences and Disorders Agency Texas A&M International University Department College Of Nursing & Health Sciences Proposed Minimum Salary Commensurate Job Location Laredo, Texas Job Type Faculty Job Description Texas A&M International University (TAMIU), a member of the Texas A&M University System, welcomes applicants for an Assistant Professor of Communication Sciences

and Disorders to begin Fall 2027 . TAMIU is a Hispanic Serving Institution with over 8,000 students located in Laredo, Texas, a vibrant and bicultural city with a population of about 250,000. The university advances knowledge through research and discovery, teaching and learning, community engagement and public service, and with an intentional focus on student success and research excellence. For more information,

please visit https://www.tamiu.edu/ This is a 9-month tenure-track position. Primary duties will include teaching assigned courses in Communication Sciences and Disorders program; evaluating students’ progress in coursework; completing the administrative aspects of assigned courses; supervising field-based students or student internship when needed; demonstrating scholarly productivity through publications in

referred journals and attendance at professional conferences and meetings; and assisting in the development of a Master's program. Duties also include providing service to the department, college and university. This position requires face-to-face interactions through a regular schedule of attendance on campus and in the workplace. The College of Nursing and Health Sciences prepares skilled professionals who promote

health, wellness, and quality of life. The College values excellence in teaching, scholarship, service, and community engagement. Required Qualifications The successful candidate must hold a doctorate in Communication Sciences and Disorders or related field from a regionally accredited institution. ABDs will also be considered but must have earned a doctorate before appointment. Candidates must demonstrate potential

for teaching, peer-reviewed scholarship, external funding, and supporting first-generation students, and hold or be eligible for Texas SLP licensure and/or ASHA CCC-SLP certification. We offer competitive salaries and a benefits package including health insurance, retirement benefits, and life insurance. Additional pay is available for summer teaching.
