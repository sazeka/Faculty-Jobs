Clinical Assistant/Associate Professor, Nursing

Plymouth State University  ·  Nursing  ·  Plymouth State University, NH
Open RankNursing

Position summary

USNH Employees should apply within Workday through the Jobs Hub app Plymouth State University invites dynamic applicants who are committed to academic excellence and devoted to teaching in a vibrant learning environment to apply for a Clinical Assistant or Clinical Associate Professor of Nursing (Non-Tenure-track). Applications are being accepted for a full-time, non-tenure track (9 month) Nursing faculty position

teaching pre-licensure undergraduate students in the classroom and clinical areas. The candidate will also be expected to advise undergraduate students and provide service to the university. Duties/Responsibilities Teaching Clinical Assistant or Clinical Associate Faculty are required to teach 20-21 credits per year. All credit hours will include any course/credit release . They teach undergraduate nursing courses

that incorporate current theory and research while employing contemporary instructional design. Areas of responsibility will vary according to the faculty member’s areas of expertise. All faculty participate in the development and delivery of interprofessional education. Clinical responsibilities Clinical Nursing faculty provide clinical instruction and/or oversight for students’ clinical experiences including

evaluation of student achievement and competence. They additionally direct and debrief students in clinical simulation and labs. Service Service is another important component of a faculty member’s workload. Service can be to the discipline, the School, the University, and the community and/or profession. Requirements: Minimum Acceptable Education & Experience: Master’s degree in Nursing Two years practice as a

registered nurse for the Clinical Assistant Four years of practice as a registered nurse for Clinical Associate Five years of relevant college/university level teaching experience for Clinical Associate Mental Health Nursing Background Desire to work as part of a team in the program, School and within the University Required Licenses & Certifications: Unencumbered RN license in the State of New Hampshire or

eligibility for licensing in New Hampshire Knowledge, Skills & Abilities: Excellent written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills Experience using instructional technology and/or developing and delivering effective online instruction Preferred Qualifications: Experience in curriculum development and program evaluation Simulation Experience Applicant Instructions: Review of applications will begin
