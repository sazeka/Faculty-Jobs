tenure-track faculty position in either Consumer Behavior or Quantitative Marketing with an expected start date of August 16, 2027. Applicants must hold a doctorate in Marketing or a closely related field or be ABD, with completion expected prior to joining. Candidates should provide evidence of outstanding research productivity and strong teaching ability, with a promise of continued productivity in the area of

marketing. Salary will be highly competitive and commensurate with qualifications.Qualified candidates should apply online and submit their application documents, including a detailed curriculum vitae or résumé; contact information for three recommendation letter writers; sample manuscripts, and other materials. Review of applications will begin in early August 2026.Salary is highly competitive and commensurate with

qualifications, and full-time faculty receive a robust employee benefits package. The annual base salary range for this position is $179,260-$276,156. productive in research. The Marshall School Dean is expanding the tenure-track faculty size. In line with this ambition, new assistant professors get reduced teaching loads, and research-active faculty are offered competitive research stipends and benefits.
