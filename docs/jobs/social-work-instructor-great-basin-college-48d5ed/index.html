Job Description Great Basin College is seeking a qualified candidate for a full-time, 9-month Social

Work Instructor and Practicum Coordinator position. This opportunity is available at any GBC campus location: Elko, Ely, Pahrump, or Winnemucca. Help build the future of social work education in rural Nevada! Great Basin College (GBC) is seeking a dedicated and collaborative Social Work Instructor to join our team and help launch our new Bachelor of Social Work (BSW) program. This is an exciting opportunity to

contribute to the development of a new academic program, shape curriculum, support accreditation efforts, and educate the next generation of social work professionals. The successful candidate will play an important role in developing and delivering a high-quality social work curriculum that prepares graduates to serve individuals, families, and communities, particularly in rural and frontier settings. The successful

candidate will work collaboratively with the Social Work Program Director and Dean of Health Sciences and Behavioral Health to develop and implement the new online program, contribute to curriculum development, support the Council on Social Work Education (CSWE) accreditation efforts, and teach undergraduate social work courses. Why Join Us? Help develop GBC’s new Bachelor of Social Work program, including curriculum

development, course design, and implementation. Prepare future social work professionals to address the needs of rural communities through accessible, high0quality education. Participate in developing program policies, assessment processes, and documentation to support CSWE accreditation.
