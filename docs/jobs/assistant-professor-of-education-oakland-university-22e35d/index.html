one semester and 2 courses in the other semester. candidate will contribute to the continued growth and quality of the ABA program, including support of ABAI Recognition and future accreditation efforts. Candidates with interests that complement existing faculty expertise and who demonstrate potential for future leadership within the program are especially encouraged to apply.
