Assistant Teaching Professor - Legal Analysis, Research, and Writing (LARW) Position Overview School / Campus / College: School of Law Organization: School of Law Title: Assistant Teaching Professor - Legal Analysis, Research, and Writing (LARW) Position Details Position Description Position Description The Legal Analysis, Research, and Writing (LARW) program at the University of Washington School of Law invites

applications for a teaching faculty position at the rank Assistant Professor (teaching track), with an anticipated start date of September 16, 2027. We seek applicants who demonstrate commitment to excellence in teaching and service in a diverse and inclusive academic community. The successful candidate will join our faculty on a full-time basis for a nine-month, 3-year appointment beginning September 16, 2027. The

base salary range for this position will be $11,300 - $13,000 per month, commensurate with experience and qualifications, or as mandated by a U.S. Department of Labor prevailing wage determination. The successful candidate will be expected to teach two sections of our first-year LARW course for the full academic year (3 quarters) with an anticipated class size of 17-20 students. Our innovative LARW course offers

first-year students seven credits of instruction in core lawyering skills including legal method, research, and writing. The successful candidate will mentor students and foster an inclusive environment. Legal writing courses at UW Law are taught by a team of dedicated and experienced faculty who stress collaboration and innovation. In collaboration with the LARW team, the successful candidate will develop curriculum

that is consistently improved with new discoveries and learning outcomes. Positive factors for consideration include, but are not limited to, candidates with admission to the Washington bar. Qualifications Applicants for this teaching faculty position must meet the following minimum qualifications: Juris Doctorate (J.D.) or foreign equivalent.
