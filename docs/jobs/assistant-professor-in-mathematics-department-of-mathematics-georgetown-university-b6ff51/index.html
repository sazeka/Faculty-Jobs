Georgetown University: Main Campus: College of Arts & Sciences: Mathematics and Statistics Location Washington, DC Open Date Sep 25, 2026 Deadline Nov 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time Description The Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Georgetown University (https://mathstat.georgetown.edu/) invites applications for a tenure-track position in mathematics at the rank of Assistant Professor to begin in August

2027. The department offers both undergraduate and graduate courses. Faculty members are expected to support all department programs. The teaching load for tenure-track faculty is two courses per semester. The successful candidate will have a strong commitment to both research and teaching. This search will focus on applied mathematics, broadly construed, with particular preference for computational mathematics. This

includes but is not limited to: mathematical foundations of AI and machine learning, inverse problems, optimization, numerical analysis. The projected base salary range is $100,000-$114,373 which represents the full range of anticipated compensation for this position. Compensation is determined by a number of factors including, but not limited to, the candidate’s individual qualifications, experience, education,

skills, and certifications, as well as the University’s business needs and external factors. Qualifications A Ph.D. in mathematics is required prior to the appointment start date; post-doctoral experience is preferred. Candidates must show a strong promise of excellence in research and teaching. Application Instructions Applicants should submit all materials electronically through Interfolio. Submit the following

required materials: Cover Letter CV Research Statement Teaching Statement Three (3) letters of recommendation (candidates must request confidential letters of recommendation to be uploaded in Interfolio by their letter writers) Provide Mathematics Subject Classification (MSC) code(s) in the required form Applications are due by November 15, 2026. For questions related to this position, please contact: Danny Cackley,

Department Administrator, dc1473@georgetown.edu. Application Process This institution is using Interfolio's Faculty Search to conduct this search. Applicants to this position receive a free Dossier account and can send all application materials, including confidential letters of recommendation, free of charge.
