Extension Faculty for Alaska Native Food Systems 533928 Fairbanks, Alaska, United States Faculty Full-time Hybrid On Campus Remote within Alaska UNAC Grade 30 UAF Institute of Agr Nat Res & Ext This position is located in Fairbanks, Alaska, and is a term-funded, 9-month Assistant Professor position with the UAF Cooperative Extension Service as part of a collaborative project between UAF Extension and the College of

Indigenous Studies. This position is funded for 3 years under a USDA ANNH grant. Faculty will have the opportunity to extend this through soliciting additional grant funds. Cooperative Extension faculty also have the opportunity to identify grant funding for up to 3 additional months each year. To thrive in this role, you must be ready to apply community-first relational approaches that center Alaska Native knowledge

and rural leaders as true co-producers of knowledge. You must be ready to apply deep listening, cultural humility, and long-term relationship development to the extension framework of nonformal education. Using proactive coalition-building, you should be ready to bridge traditional knowledge and research-based information to support food sovereignty, community resilience, and sustainable Arctic food systems.

Applicant Instructions: Please provide the following documents: Cover letter (2 page limit) CV/Resume (5 page limit) Minimum Qualifications: Master’s Degree in Food Systems, Sustainable Agriculture, Tribal Governance, Alaska Native Studies, Public Health, Community Health, Extension Education, Rural Development or relevant combination.Preferred certifications in food safety or food preservation. Preferred background

in Indigenous research methodologies, community-based participatory research, or nonformal adult education. Position Details: This position is located on the Troth Yeddha' campus in Fairbanks Alaska. This is a full-time, exempt faculty position complete with both a competitive salary and full employee benefits package. UA provides a generous compensation package that includes retirement options, annual leave, 12 paid

holidays per year, tuition waivers for employees and family members, and affordable medical, dental and vision care coverage. Salary is based on education and experience and negotiable.
