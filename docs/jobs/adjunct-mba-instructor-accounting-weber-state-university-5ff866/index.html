Position Information Position Title Adjunct (MBA) Instructor - Accounting Requisition Number A00118P Job Summary/Basic Function An adjunct instructor

is needed to teach a managerial accounting class in the MBA program. The class provides a general study of the use of accounting information by internal and external decision-makers, with an emphasis on its use by an entity’s managers. Through various methods, students will develop quantitative and qualitative skill sets to analyze accounting data and support strategic and operational business decision-making. Topics

covered include cost concepts, cost behavior, cost estimation, CVP (break-even) analysis, job-order costing, activity-based costing, relevant costs for decision-making, capital budgeting, and profit planning.**This is a pooled position. Applicants are selected from the pool as positions become available. After a period of time the pool will expire and applicants will be notified. Required Qualifications MBA or MAcc
