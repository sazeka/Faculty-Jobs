Lecturer/Artist-in-Residence in Music Business and Entrepreneurship Winter Park, FL, Florida Music Faculty (Non-Tenure Track) Opening on: Aug 14 2026 Academic Affairs Position Summary: The Department of Music at Rollins College is seeking a dynamic musician and music industry professional to join our faculty as a full-time Lecturer/Artist-in-Residence in Music Business and Entrepreneurship. The successful candidate

may teach undergraduate music major elective courses in areas such as commercial music, American popular music, music publishing, music business and marketing. The appointee will collaborate with the faculty in continuing to develop music business related courses. Additional responsibilities will include teaching general education courses to both music majors and non-majors, and may include applied lessons or

ensembles based on candidate experience.The appointee will also be expected to serve on departmental and faculty committees and participate in faculty governance as appropriate.Rollins College is a comprehensive liberal arts college located just north of Orlando, FL, a diverse metropolitan community with a thriving economic and cultural scene. Nearby Orlando International Airport provides easy access to U.S. and

international destinations. The college emphasizes innovative and quality teaching in small classes and ranks number one among 121 Southern master’s-level universities in the annual rankings of “America’s Best Colleges,” released by U.S. News & World Report. Please visit the college website at www.rollins.edu. Minimum Qualifications and Education: Master’s degree in music or equivalent professional experience.

Experience in the following areas: marketing, artist management, music publishing and licensing, music in media, media composition, entrepreneurship, and copyright. Demonstrated record as an innovative classroom teacher. Preferred Qualifications: At least 2 years of college-level teaching. Terminal degree. Documented experience in research and publication. Demonstrated record of music performance Instructions to

Curriculum vita Statement of teaching philosophy that presents the candidate's commitment to the liberal arts and to student development Contact information for three references
