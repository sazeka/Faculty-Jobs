Assistant, Associate or Full Professor - Physical Therapy FACULTY VACANCY ANNOUNCEMENT The Division of Science and Technology's Department of Physical Therapy at the College of Staten Island (CSI) is excited to announce the tenure-track open rank Faculty position for the Doctor of Physical Therapy in Staten Island, New York. This unique opportunity is for a collaborative faculty member who can teach clinical or

foundation courses or specializes in any of the major physical therapy specializations (OCS, ECS, SCS, etc.) and is committed to the profession of Physical Therapy and the inclusive excellence of our students. As a faculty member of the DPT Program, you will have a significant role in shaping the next generation of physical therapists. This full-time, 9 month, in-person faculty position involves expertise in

curriculum development, delivery, assessment, student advisement, leadership in service to the university and profession, community outreach, and scholarly engagement. New faculty for the DPT in CSI will join a well-established in-person program committed to providing evidence-based educational practice. Applicants should demonstrate a successfully teaching record. The successful candidate will be expected to develop

a well-defined research agenda and contributes to research and scholarly activity as required by the University and CAPTE. To develop, deliver and assess curriculum for DPT students. Advise and mentor students. Provide leadership in university and professional service and engage in community outreach efforts. The College of Staten Island is a four-year senior college of The City University of New York. It provides

exceptional opportunities to all its students and offers undergraduate and graduate degrees, certificates, and continuing education programs. CSI is accredited by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education. Completed in 1994, the 204-acre campus of CSI/CUNY is set on the largest physical New York City college site. Surrounded by a park-like landscape, the campus is centrally located on Staten Island. A rural

oasis in an urban setting, the campus includes 300 classrooms, laboratories and instructional space, study lounges, department and program offices, and faculty offices, along with extensive athletic facilities and an acclaimed Center for the Arts.
