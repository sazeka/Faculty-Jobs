Adjunct Engineering Instructors Print Adjunct Engineering Instructors Location Multiple Campuses Job Type Adjunct Faculty, Credit Job Number 000493-04 Department 1606 Engineering Opening Date 07/01/2025 Closing Date 12/31/2026 11:59 PM Eastern Description Benefits Questions Position Summary Delaware County Community College (DCCC), located outside of the Philadelphia Metro area, is looking for a talented and dynamic

Adjunct Instructor to join our team of faculty who are committed to student success. Adjunct Instructor who subscribes to the notion of meeting students where they are, DCCC has a place for you!In conjunction with Delaware County Community College's Mission and Values, Adjunct Engineering Instructors are responsible for providing quality instruction to students in Engineering courses at the College. It is the

responsibility of the instructors to be knowledgeable of the content they teach as well as able to convey that content in a manner that is accessible to the College's diverse student population. Along with learning and growing the College's culture, Adjunct Faculty are also responsible for providing students with the ability to take their knowledge and apply it to their future coursework and/or the career arc of

their choosing. Essential Functions Primary responsibilities include but not limited to: -Plan and provide content instruction to students enrolled in their section(s).Hold an office hour per week, per section taught, to meet with students concerning course content and other individual needsAssess the knowledge of students enrolled and submit grades representative of their knowledge and achievement on

assessmentsConnect students with the campus resources that will best supplement the needs of the students enrolled in your section(s)Supplement the material taught in lecture by leading hands-on and interactive labs Secondary Duties: Attend all professional development and training sessions offered to new and veteran Adjunct Professors at the CollegeMeet with Course Coordinators/Custodians to familiarize yourself

with the content you teach and how that fits into the larger academic programs your students are enrolled inMaintain relevant course materials and information (syllabus, assignments, grades, etc.) in CanvasAttend formal observation meetings with the Assistant Dean, STEM and/or Site Director to gain input on your teaching acumen and discuss ways in which you can improve.
