Position description This is a pool recruitment for academic, non-tenure track, limited-term appointments for part-time Lecturer positions. We are seeking qualified candidates with current knowledge and work experience who can create an interactive and engaging learning environment

for students. Lecturer positions are dependent upon instructional needs for the 2026-2027 academic year. To see possible course descriptions, visit: https://law.ucdavis.edu/students/registrar/course-descriptions Creating a new course is also a possibility. BASIC QUALIFICATIONS: Candidates must have a J.D. or equivalent degree and possess exemplary professional experience in the substantive area of law covered by the

course(s). TO APPLY: A complete application must include the following materials: Cover Letter – A concise description of the proposed course(s) you wish to teach, highlighting your qualifications to teach the relevant subject matter Current Curriculum Vitae (CV). Statement of Teaching (optional) – If you have prior teaching experience, include a statement detailing the course(s) you have taught. Sample Student

Evaluation(s) (optional) – If applicable, provide at least one example of previous student evaluations. Three Professional References – Include names, mailing addresses, and email addresses for each reference. Authorization to Release Information Form – This form is required for all applicants to academic recruitments. Visit the Employment Disclosure Requirements webpage for details. Download, complete, sign, and

upload the form. SALARY: Commensurate with qualifications, education and experience. Salary will be prorated based on percent of appointment for part-time Lecturer positions. The percent of appointment depends on the type and number of courses assigned.
