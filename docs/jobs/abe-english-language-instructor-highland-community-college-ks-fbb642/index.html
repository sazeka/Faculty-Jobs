ABE English Language Instructor

Highland Community College (KS)  ·  Highland Community College (KS), KS
Instructor

Position summary

SUMMARY The Adult Education Local English Language Instructor is responsible for the support of the Adult Education Program at the assigned location. This position is funded through grant monies and is subject to the availability of funds. This position is 10 hours a week and 42 weeks a year. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES include the following. Other duties may be assigned. 1. Serve as an instructor by

performing the following activities: a. Hold pre-enrollment informational sessions and orientation activities for adult education classes b. Develop a plan of study with students based on skills, interests and goals; c. Provide quality instruction so as to provide the best opportunity for students to learn; d. Coordinate and deliver on-going professional development activities for staff as needed. 2. Use the adult

education data base to evaluate programs and make recommendations for program changes, professional development activities, a. Work closely with the Adult Education Director, Regional Coordinators, and Technical Center Director to provide guidance and collaborations necessary to meet goals; b. Work with Advisory committee and develop partnerships with business and industry that create opportunities for students and

awareness of programs. 3. Consult with the HCC Adult Education Program Director for questions related to the program. 4. Recruitment and Retention Activities: a. Provide ongoing transition services; c. Work with students who are not meeting academic and/or attendance requirements; d. Assist and refer students to financial, child care, transportation, and other resources as needed; e. Act as the advisor for students,

following HCC advising practices, and attend campus advising trainings. 5. Maintenance Activities: a. Keep accurate records and supply necessary information to the HCC Adult Education Program Director; c. Keep records of materials and information received from the Director and share with tutors and students; d. Perform other instructor-related duties as required. 6. Program Involvement and Development Activities: a.

Be a member of the Kansas Adult Education Association (KAEA); b. Attend Adult Education Staff Development on the state and local level; d. Help develop and promote ELA literacy at the local level;
