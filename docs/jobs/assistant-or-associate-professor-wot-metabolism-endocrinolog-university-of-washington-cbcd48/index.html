by a U.S. Department of Labor prevailing wage determination. Please note that only compensation provided by the University is included in University benefit determinations and calculations. Compensation provided by UW Physicians (UWP) practice plan may provide

eligibility for separate benefits offered by that organization. Other compensation associated with this position may include an administrative salary supplement (ADS).
