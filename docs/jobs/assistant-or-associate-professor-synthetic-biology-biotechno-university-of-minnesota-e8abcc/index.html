The new faculty will develop innovative, extramurally funded research programs using biochemical, computational and artificial intelligence tools to engineer proteins and enzymes, metabolism, and nucleic acids

Environmental applications of interest include but are not limited to carbon capture, circular bioeconomy, remediation, environmental engineering, renewable biomanufacturing, biosensors and monitoring, and waste biotechnology and resource recovery. New faculty will be supported by a competitive startup package, salary, and research space. They will contribute to undergraduate and graduate educational

programs, research mentorship, and academic and professional service.The BMBB Department is part of the College of Biological Sciences (CBS) and Medical School. BMBB has strengths in biochemistry, biotechnology, synthetic biology, metabolism, proteomics, molecular genetics, signaling, and structural biology. BMBB supports an undergraduate biochemistry major and doctoral program. The Department has multiple core

facilities including cryo-electron microscopy, nuclear magnetic resonance, X-ray crystallography, metabolomics and proteomics. BMBB and CBS interface with university-wide initiatives in biotechnology, supercomputing, biomanufacturing, genomics, structural biology, field stations, and advanced imaging that empower research exploration. The University offers a collaborative, world-class research environment embedded in

a region renowned for innovation. For more information about BMBB and CBS, please visit the department’s website: https://cbs.umn.edu/bmbb.
