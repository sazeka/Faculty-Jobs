Primary Location: Integrated Science Center Primary Job Posting Location: Posting Location - William & Mary Job Description Summary: The Department of Computer Science at William & Mary, a public university of the Commonwealth of Virginia, invites applications for a postdoctoral fellow position in the Robust and Adaptive Intelligence Laboratory, expected to begin September 10, 2026. We seek an individual to

contribute to two intertwined threads of research: (i) AI-based approaches for routing under uncertainty, including dynamic dispatch and stochastic and online vehicle routing at scale; and (ii) explainability of AI-based sequential decision-making, combining formal logic, large language models, and planning so that decisions produced by complex systems can be understood, audited, and trusted. The appointed researcher

will publish in top venues in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and operations research; contribute to open-source releases; and work closely with graduate students in the lab. The initial appointment is for one year, with the possibility of renewal based on performance and funding availability. Job Description: Required Qualifications: Applicant must hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science, or a related field, at

the time appointment begins (expected to begin September 10, 2026). Preferred Qualifications: Experience in Sequential Decision-Making under Uncertainty, Planning, and Machine Learning. Strong software-engineering skills and a track record of publishing at top AI/ML/OR venues or of deploying research systems in real-world settings are welcome. Applicants must apply online at https://www.wm.edu/offices/uhr/careers/ .

Submit a curriculum vitae, a cover letter, two names for letters of references, and a statement of research. For full consideration, submit application materials by the review begin date: August 10, 2026 . Applications received after the review date will be considered if needed. Information on the Department of Computer Science may be found at https://cdsp.wm.edu/computerscience/ . Posting Range: $70,000 - $75,000,
