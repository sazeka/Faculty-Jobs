Graduate Studies Dean/Education Department Chair Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Details Position Information Position Title Graduate Studies Dean/Education Department Chair Job Description The Dean of Graduate Studies is a senior academic leader responsible for overseeing all graduate-level programs within the institution. This role provides strategic vision, academic leadership, and

administrative oversight to ensure the quality, integrity, and growth of graduate education. The Dean works collaboratively with faculty, department heads, and university leadership to enhance student success, research excellence, and program innovation. The Dean will also serve as Education Department Chair, whose role includes oversight of curriculum, faculty, student success, accreditation, and partnerships with

PK–12 school systems to ensure high-quality educator preparation.Essential Responsibilities Include:Academic Leadership and Program Development Develop and implement strategic academic plans aligned with institutional goals and accreditation standards. Lead curriculum development aligned with Mississippi educator licensure requirements and MS CCRS. Promote innovative and technology-enhanced instructional delivery.

Ensure continuous program assessment and improvement. Program Oversight and Administration Oversee graduate education programs for quality and compliance. Monitor enrollment, retention, and licensure pass rates. Develop and enforce academic policies. Evaluate programs and recommend improvements or changes. Faculty Leadership and Development Recruit, mentor, and evaluate faculty. Ensure faculty credentials align with

SACSCOC, ACBSP, CSWE, and EHAC standards. Promote scholarship, research, and professional development. Accreditation and Compliance Ensure compliance with SACSCOC, CAEP, MDE, ACSBP, CSWE and EHAC requirements. Lead accreditation reporting and continuous improvement efforts. Required Qualifications Earned Ph.D. or other terminal degree with demonstrated excellence in teaching, research and service; qualified to teach

in education, as well as other disciplines; experience with two or more program accrediting bodies, scholarly research productivity; must be highly motivated and able to work independently and as a team member; must have excellent interpersonal skills and meticulous in performing job duties Preferred Qualifications Earned PhD, strong evaluation and assessment skills, graduate degree or 18 graduate hours in 2 or more
