Director and Clinical Faculty, Graduate Program in Environmental Science and Natural Resource Journalism

University of Montana  ·  Missoula, MT
Clinical FacultyEnvironmental Science and Natural Resource Journalism

Position summary

The School of Journalism at the University of Montana seeks a director for its master’s program in Environmental Science and Natural Resource Journalism . The two-year interdisciplinary degree combines journalism and science coursework to prepare students to report on environmental and natural resource issues across print, photo, video and multimedia platforms. The director will provide strategic leadership and

implement a vision for how the program will continue to prepare journalists to define coverage on a beat that impacts all facets of society and our world. They will lead recruitment and admissions, advise students through the curriculum, oversee the program's signature field projects, including the Crown Reporting Project and the Story Lab course, and represent the program to university leadership, donors and the

professional journalism community. This is a full-time, benefited clinical faculty position that combines teaching, student mentorship and program administration.
