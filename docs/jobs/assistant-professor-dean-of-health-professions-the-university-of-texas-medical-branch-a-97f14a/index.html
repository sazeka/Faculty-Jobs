Assistant Professor - Dean of Health Professions

The University of Texas Medical Branch at Galveston  ·  Dean of Health Professions  ·  Galveston, TX
Assistant ProfessorHealth Administration

Position summary

JOB DESCRIPTION: The UTMB School of Health Professions invites applications for a full-time Master of Health Administration (MHA) Program Director at the Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, or Professor rank. We seek an accomplished academic leader to refine, validate, and operationalize a foundational program proposal into a fully accredited, elite MHA program. This individual will lead the translation of

existing business plans into an active academic infrastructure and drive the program through the CAHME candidacy and initial accreditation phases. ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS: • Curriculum Validation & Operationalization: Translate existing programmatic proposals and business plans into a functional, CAHME-compliant academic program; validate and refine all proposed coursework against a formal, stakeholder-driven

competency model. • Accreditation Leadership: Serve as the primary author and strategist for the CAHME eligibility and candidacy applications; establish the data collection systems and frameworks required to prove the program meets initial accreditation standards. • Network & Partnership Building: Establish foundational relationships with industry leaders, regional health systems, and the ACHE network to secure

future residency, fellowship, capstone, and clinical education placements. • Policy & Infrastructure Development: Finalize programmatic policies, admissions criteria, student handbooks, and public-facing informational materials compliant with both UTMB institutional rules and accreditor standards. • Faculty Recruitment: Collaborate with leadership to identify, recruit, and onboard foundational core and adjunct

faculty; establish standards for teaching excellence within the newly operationalized curriculum. • Teaching & Scholarship: Deliver core graduate coursework within the MHA curriculum; maintain an active portfolio of scholarship and professional service commensurate with academic rank. • Student Recruitment Support: Partner with enrollment marketing to promote the new program, evaluate inaugural applicant pools, and

oversee initial student admissions and advising. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS: • Doctoral degree (D.H.A., Ph.D., Dr.P.H., or equivalent) in health administration, public health, health policy, business administration, or a closely related field. • Master's degree in health administration, public health, or a related discipline. • Minimum 5 years of progressive leadership experience in healthcare administration or higher
