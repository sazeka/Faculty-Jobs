Position summary

Assistant Dean of Students 497200 Full-Time Dean of Students Department: Dean of Students Salary: Commensurate with education and experience. Description: The Assistant Dean of Students provides leadership of programs, initiatives, services, and resources for students grappling with their mental health, students who are pregnant and/or parenting, and students with experience in foster care. Responsible for

assessment, development, implementation, and evaluation of campus and basic needs services and resources that support students navigating critical situations that impact their academic and personal experience. Serves as the primary CARE case manager for students. Supports student crises and emergencies through critical intervention and referrals, including facilitation of the campus-wide CARE Team, oversight of the

Hawk Market, and management of both the Hawk Emergency Fund and RISE Scholars Program. Serves as the designated university representative for the FamilyU initiative as well as the university liaison for both students who are pregnant and/or parenting, and students with experience in the foster care system. Serves as a CSA. Duties: Serves as a case manager for students in need. This includes, but is not limited to,

chairing the Crisis Awareness Response Emergency Team; responding to and managing student care/basic needs insecurities/issues; conducting one-on-one meetings with students to identify concerns, basic needs, priorities, and barriers; providing individualized support and follow-up with students; connecting students to on and off-campus services and resources; providing ongoing case management for students; and

documenting all case records, notes, referrals, and communications in the appropriate software. Develops and manages a strategic vision and comprehensive basic needs support model and resources for students in crisis situations, which includes Hawk Emergency Grant distribution, Hark Market oversight, RISE Scholars Program management, and other basic needs initiatives. Creates and maintains strategic partnerships with

on and off-campus partners. Oversees campus-wide family programming, initiatives, and resources. This includes serving as the FamilyU representative for the university and serving as both the university liaison for students who are pregnant and/or parenting, and students who have experience in foster care. Supervises, trains, coaches, and evaluates full-time staff and graduate assistants as assigned. Indirectly
