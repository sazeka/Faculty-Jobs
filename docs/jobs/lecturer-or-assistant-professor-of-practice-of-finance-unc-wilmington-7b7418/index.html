Workplace Main UNCW Campus Brief Summary of Work for this Position The Department of Economics and Finance at the University of North Carolina Wilmington invites applications for a nine-month, non-tenure-track faculty position in Finance at the rank of Lecturer or Assistant Professor of Practice. We seek collegial and engaged scholars who will contribute to the department’s mission through excellence in research,

teaching, and service. The anticipated start date is August 2027. These positions are appointed to an initial one-year term with no renewal limits.The successful Lecturer candidate will have an emphasis on teaching while also contributing to meaningful service for the department, college, and university. The teaching load is eight courses annually (4:4 load).The successful Assistant Professor of Practice candidate

will demonstrate a strong commitment to high-quality, engaging instruction and continued scholarly activity. The teaching load is seven courses annually (3:4 or 4:3 load). In addition to teaching responsibilities, the successful candidate will be expected to contribute to the intellectual life of the department through an active research agenda and meaningful service to the department, college, and university.Both

positions may include teaching of undergraduate introductory and upper-level Finance courses, as well as graduate courses in the Master of Business Administration (MBA), International MBA (IMBA), and Master of Science in Finance (MSF) programs.Service activities may include advising and supporting student organizations, participating in departmental initiatives, and engaging in other activities that advance the

department’s mission for both Lecturer and Assistant Professor of Practice positions. Minimum Education and Experience Requirements 1. Applicants for appointment at the rank of lecturer must have at least a master’s in finance (or related discipline) with one year of work experience.2. Applicants for the Assistant Professor of Practice position must have a Ph.D., DBA in Finance, Real Estate, or an equivalent terminal
