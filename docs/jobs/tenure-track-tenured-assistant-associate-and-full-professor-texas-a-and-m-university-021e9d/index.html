levels (Assistant, Associate, and Full Professor) with a nine-month academic appointment, and the possibility of an additional summer appointment contingent upon the need and availability of funds, beginning fall of 2027. Strong candidates in core, multi-disciplinary, or emerging areas of computing and AI are encouraged to apply. Particular areas of interest are (but not limited to) cybersecurity/privacy, database

systems, formal methods, health computing, quantum computing, and responsible/human-centered AI. The department may hire multiple faculty in one or more of these areas to continue growing strong research clusters. The successful applicant will be required to teach, advise, and mentor graduate and undergraduate students; develop and maintain an independent, externally funded research program with strong visibility

through high-impact publications; participate in all aspects of the department’s activities; and serve the department, college, university, and/or profession, as applicable. Strong written and verbal communication skills are required. Applicants should visit http://www.cse.tamu.edu to review our academic and research programs. The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Texas A&M is currently one of

15 departments in the College of Engineering. Many of the 57 tenured/tenure-track faculty and 28 teaching-focused faculty hold numerous national distinctions, including AAAI, ACM, IEEE, AAAS, SIAM Fellows, and ACM Distinguished Scientists and Engineers. The department has a strong and vibrant research program, with half the faculty having received NSF CAREER awards. CSE faculty have strong collaborations across Texas

A&M in data science, health, cybersecurity, and national security, including through the Institute of Data Science, the Center for Remote Health Technologies and Systems, the Global Cyber Research Institute, the Cybersecurity Center, and the Bush Combat Development Complex.
