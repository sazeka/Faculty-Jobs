Posting Details Posting Summary Position Title Assistant Professor of Theology FLSA Department Department Of Theology Job Summary The

Theology Department at Xavier University of Louisiana, a Catholic and Historically Black University, invites applications for a tenure-track position in Catholic Studies at the assistant professor level beginning in fall 2027. This position is an opportunity for a teacher and scholar with demonstrated expertise in Catholic studies to contribute to a growing department and undergraduate core curriculum. Scholars whose

research and/or teaching connects Catholic studies with history, theology, biblical studies, psychology, ethics, decolonial thought, womanist thought, feminist thought, interreligious dialogue, science-engaged theology, or other interdisciplinary approaches to Catholic intellectual and religious traditions are especially encouraged to apply. The department values diversity, equity, and inclusion and is particularly

interested in candidates who can effectively teach in a diverse university environment and demonstrate a commitment to supporting the success of students from varied backgrounds. Job Duties Teach undergraduate-level courses in theology, including introductory courses and more specialized courses in Catholic studies and their area(s) of specialization. Maintain an active research agenda and a record of scholarly,

peer-reviewed publications. Mentor students minoring and majoring in theology. Engage in service to the department, college, and university. The teaching load is 12 credit hours per semester.About the DepartmentThe Department of Theology serves the University’s core curriculum and a growing group of Theology majors and minors.
