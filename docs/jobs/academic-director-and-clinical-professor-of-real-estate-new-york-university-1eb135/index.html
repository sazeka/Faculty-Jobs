New York University: NYU - NY: School of Professional Studies Location New York, NY Open Date Oct 28, 2026 Description ACADEMIC DIRECTOR AND CLINICAL PROFESSOR OF REAL ESTATE The NYU School of Professional Studies seeks distinguished academics with exemplary experience in commercial real estate finance and development for the role of Academic Director and Clinical Professor of Real Estate for the Schack Institute of

Real Estate, with an anticipated start date of Fall 2027. Ideal candidates will have experience with utilizing and teaching the latest technologies related to artificial intelligence, data analysis and property technology specifically focused on applications for real estate professionals. Faculty candidates should also have experience working with a wide range of professionals and have experience or evidence of the

potential for success in teaching a broad population of students both graduate and undergraduate, including first generation students and international students, as well as those new to the field of real estate. Reporting to the Associate Dean of the Schack Institute of Real Estate, the successful candidate will work closely with the Chair to implement the vision for expanding the Schack Institute of Real Estate’s

programs and initiatives, and to increase the Schack Institute of Real Estate’s profile and visibility locally, nationally and internationally. The successful candidate will teach within the graduate and undergraduate programs; may also teach within corporate and executive education programs; develop courses; advise students; collaborate with other faculty and industry partners; and engage with the broader NYU SPS

academic community, as appropriate. Primary Responsibilities: In consultation with departmental leadership, the Academic Director coordinates daily and academic operations related to the unit, including oversight of the curriculum, student advising, adjunct faculty, and program staff.
