Indiana University South Bend invites applications for Tenure-Track faculty in printmaking. The applicant should be an artist-scholar specializing in printmaking. The candidate should understand aesthetics, visual literacy, the history of art, and contemporary issues in art as related to printmaking and fine art practices. Each semester, the candidate will teach two printmaking classes and one course in the

department’s general education curriculum.The full-time load (100% FTE) is 12 credit hours (typically four courses) each fall and spring semester, with one course reassigned time for scholarly and creative activities following university & campus policies. The successful candidate will teach three courses per semester, contribute to service, engage in scholarly/creative activities, mentor students, and use their

expertise to teach fine art students, particularly those students with a concentration or minor in printmaking. The faculty member will also manage and maintain the printmaking studio. This position begins on August 1, 2027. The candidate will have the opportunity to apply for tenure in their 6th year of service.
