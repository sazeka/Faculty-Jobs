Workforce Solutions Job Summary The part-time Lineman Instructor will provide direction and instruction for the Lineman Training Program on MCTC's Rowan Campus. This individual will assist in the teaching of the program's students, ensuring alignment with the KCTCS approved curriculum and the latest industry updates. They will also be responsible for assistance in the recruitment of students by attending networking

meeting, holding seats on job-related committees, and attending career fairs for secondary institutions and adults. They will also work with MCTC's Lineman Program Contact to order materials and supplies needed for the program. This position will also build and retain strong employer partnerships through advisory boards, guest instructors, and student employment opportunities after completion of the training.

Managing and tracking student placements, maintaining a safe learning environment, and ensuring security of all inventory tools and material for the program is essential as well. This is an in-person position and not eligible for remote. Schedule is Monday through Friday 8-4:30 for 7 weeks per class cohort. Additional sessions as need is present.
