Adjunct Faculty Day, World Lang & Cultures and Education, AY 2026-2027 493079 177 - World Languages / Cultures Faculty: Adjunct Part-Time Hybrid $1,000 - $2,253.60 per Semester Hour Credit of Instruction Salem State University invites applications for a temporary Adjunct Professor of Spanish Education with a joint appointment in the Department of World Languages and Cultures and the McKeown School of Education for

spring 2027. This position plays a central leadership role in the university’s Spanish teacher licensure programs. The candidate will teach Spanish teacher licensure courses for the 4+1 BA/MEd with Initial Licensure and the Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT) in Spanish. These programs are jointly supported by the Department of World Languages and Cultures and the McKeown School of Education. Teaching Responsibilities

Teaching responsibilities will be distributed across the two units and will focus primarily on licensure-related coursework, including but not limited to: • Spanish Methods I and II courses with integrated, co-requisite field experiences • Student teaching seminar and practicum • Advanced-track courses such as Action Research and Clinical sequences Service & Professional Engagement The candidate will be expected to:

• Engage in service activities in both the Department of World Languages and Cultures and the McKeown School of Education • Collaborate across disciplines to align licensure curricula, assessment practices, and clinical expectations • Participate actively in state, regional, and national professional organizations related to world language education Working Location: This is an onsite position in Salem, MA with some

on-campus, in-person responsibilities. Application Instructions: Please apply online and attach CV, cover letter, and official transcripts. To ensure you are assigned the highest eligible rank, you should provide the total sum of credits you have taught at the college/university level. This information can be added to your CV, cover letter, or a separate document. Employment is contingent upon a completed background

and CORI check satisfactory to the university. This position is not eligible for H1B visa sponsorship. EEO Statement: The university considers all qualified candidates without regard to race, color, religion, sex, age, national origin, disability status, veteran status, gender identity, sexual orientation, genetic information, pregnancy or pregnancy-related condition or any other characteristic protected by law.
