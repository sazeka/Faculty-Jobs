Job Openings >> Adjunct Faculty, Applied Electricity and Electronics, Manufacturing Engineering Technology Program Adjunct Faculty, Applied Electricity and Electronics, Manufacturing Engineering Technology Program SummaryTitle:Adjunct Faculty, Applied Electricity and Electronics, Manufacturing Engineering Technology Program ID:1419Department:Business and Data ScienceDescriptionLaGrange College seeks to fill an

adjunct teaching position in applied electricity and electronics in its new manufacturing engineering technology program. The course is expected to be offered in Spring 2027, and the need will be ongoing. The course description and number of credit hours are listed below. METC 3222 Applied Electricity and Electronics - Fundamentals of electricity and electronics, basic circuits, motors, generators, and power

distribution, advanced electronic circuits. (3 credits) Preference will be given to those who can teach in person, although applications from remote instructors will be considered. A minimum of a master’s degree in a relevant engineering field is preferred, although those with significant professional experience will be considered. LaGrange College seeks candidates with varied backgrounds and perspectives. If you

embrace our values of excellence, service, civility, and citizenship, and are committed to modeling those attributes in our community, we encourage you to submit your application for consideration. Interested candidates should visit https://lagrangecollege.applicantstack.com/x/openings to apply for the position and to view other positions available at the college. Required documents include: A letter of interest

describing the alignment of candidate qualifications with the position requirements Current curriculum vitae/resume Graduate and undergraduate transcripts (unofficial copies are fine at this point) Contact information (only) for three professional references The review of applications will continue until the position is filled.
