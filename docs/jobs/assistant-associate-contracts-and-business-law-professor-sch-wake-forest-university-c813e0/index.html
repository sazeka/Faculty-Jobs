Job Description Summary Wake Forest University School of Law invites applications from entry-level candidates as well as from pre-tenure lateral candidates for a tenure-track professor to teach contracts and business law. Job Description: Job

Qualifications Applicants should possess a J.D. from an accredited U.S. law school. The potential to engage in high quality research and teaching is required. Practice experience is preferred. Lateral applicants should hold a tenured/tenure-track position at an accredited U.S. law school. Application Instructions Review of applications will begin August 1, 2025, and continue until the position is filled. Applications
