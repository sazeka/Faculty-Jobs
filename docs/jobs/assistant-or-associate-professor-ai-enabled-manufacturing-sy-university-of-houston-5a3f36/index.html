Assistant or Associate Professor - AI-Enabled Manufacturing Systems 497100 Full-Time Industrial Engineering Department: Industrial and Systems Engineering Salary: Commensurate with experience Description: The Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISE) in the Cullen College of Engineering at the University of Houston invites applications for a Presidential Frontier Faculty (PFF) position at the Assistant

or Associate Professor rank in AI-Enabled Manufacturing Systems. We seek candidates who will advance the design and operation of intelligent, automated, resilient, and human-centered manufacturing systems through cutting-edge interdisciplinary research, innovative teaching, and student mentorship. The PFF program is a university-wide interdisciplinary hiring initiative overseen by university leadership to recruit a

large cohort of convergence research faculty working in areas related to energy, health, sustainability, and security. Qualifications: Applicants must hold an earned Ph.D. in industrial engineering or a closely related field by the time of appointment. We are especially interested in candidates whose research explores how artificial intelligence, quantum computing, digital twins, human judgment, and operational

decision-making can be integrated to create reliable, scalable, and resilient manufacturing systems. Successful candidates must demonstrate strong potential for, or an established record of, developing a well-funded nationally recognized research program, publishing peer-reviewed scholarship, securing external funding, advising students, and contributing to teaching innovation, with accomplishments commensurate with

the rank sought. Rank and salary will be commensurate with experience. The University: The University of Houston is a Carnegie-designated Tier One public research university and the flagship institution of the University of Houston System. With more than 49,000 students, UH is recognized for its commitment to academic excellence, research innovation, and community engagement. Faculty have access to outstanding

research facilities and strong institutional support for pursuing competitive external funding. In particular, advanced materials and manufacturing is a major institutional research priority at UH, supported by a growing ecosystem of interdisciplinary centers, shared facilities, computational infrastructure, and industry partnerships.
