The Department The Department of Surgery is a dynamic and nationally recognized academic department committed to excellence in patient care, education, research, and service to the community. The department provides a broad range of surgical services and serves as a major referral resource for patients throughout

Northeast Florida and Southeast Georgia. The department encompasses seven major divisions: Acute Care Surgery, Cardiothoracic Surgery, General and Gastrointestinal Surgery, Surgical Oncology, Otorhinolaryngology, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, and Vascular Surgery. With expertise spanning general and specialty surgery, trauma, surgical oncology, and complex surgical care, the department is well positioned for

continued growth and innovation across its clinical, academic, and research missions. The department provides inpatient and outpatient surgical services at multiple locations and is closely integrated with UF Health Jacksonville, the region’s academic health center and tertiary referral hospital.
