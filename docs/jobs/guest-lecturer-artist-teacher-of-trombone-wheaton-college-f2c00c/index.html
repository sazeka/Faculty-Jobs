Guest Lecturer - Artist Teacher of Trombone Wheaton, IL, United States Job Description Position: Guest Lecturer – Artist Teacher of Trombone The Conservatory of Music at Wheaton College (Illinois) invites applications for a part-time guest lecturer position beginning August 26, 2026. Responsibilities include teaching applied trombone lessons and studio classes, recruitment and offering of “trial lessons,” as well as

adjudicating trombone auditions and performance juries. Compensation for guest lecturer positions range from $60 to more than $90 per instructional hour, dependent on qualifications and experience. All candidates should have evidence of successful applied teaching experience and a minimum of an earned master’s degree in music. Priority will be given to applicants with a record of strong teaching, accomplished

performance, and Christian faith. Start Date: August 26, 2026 Application deadline: Until Filled WHEATON COLLEGE is an evangelical Protestant Christian liberal arts college whose faculty and staff affirm a Statement of Faith and adhere to lifestyle expectations of the Wheaton College Community Covenant. Wheaton is an equal opportunity employer committed to recruiting and retaining a diverse and talented workforce and

student body. We encourage the expression of multiple perspectives within a Christian worldview, inside and outside the classroom. For more information about Wheaton College visit www.wheaton.edu. Additionally, Wheaton College aspires to provide a physically safe environment for students, staff, and faculty to learn and work. In support of this objective, the College requires background checks for final candidates,

after an employment offer is extended. Employment is contingent upon successful completion of a background check.
