Full-Time Position Assistant Professor of Biology Union Commonwealth University invites applications for a full-time Assistant Professor of Biology beginning August 11, 2026. The successful candidate will demonstrate a strong commitment to excellence in undergraduate teaching, student mentoring, academic advising, curriculum development, recruitment and retention, scholarly and professional development appropriate to

the appointment, and service to the Department, School, and University. Applicants from diverse academic and professional backgrounds are encouraged to apply, including those with expertise in the biological sciences, biomedical sciences, medicine, nursing, and other health-related disciplines. Applicants with an earned terminal degree (Ph.D. or equivalent) in Biology or a closely related discipline, as outlined in

the Qualifications for Tenure-Track Appointment section below, are eligible for appointment to a tenure-track position. Candidates with a master's degree in Biology or a related field may be considered for a full-time, non-tenure-track appointment. Additionally, individuals with appropriate graduate credentials and substantial professional experience in a health-related discipline, including medicine, nursing,

physician assistant studies, or allied health, who are academically qualified to teach biology courses, are encouraged to apply and may also be considered for a full-time, non-tenure-track appointment. such as General Biology, Human Biology, Anatomy and Physiology, Microbiology, Cell Biology, Genetics, Ecology, Evolution, and upper-level biology courses as assigned.  Develop engaging learning experiences that integrate scientific principles with practical applications and current biological research.  Foster critical thinking, scientific inquiry, laboratory skills, and evidence-based decision making.  Advise and

mentor biology majors and students pursuing careers in healthcare, medicine, nursing, education, research, environmental sciences, and other life science professions.  Participate in curriculum assessment, program development, student recruitment, and academic advising.  Contribute to departmental, university, and professional service activities.  Maintain an active program of professional development,
