Message to Students: Welcome to the 2024-2025 Academic Year! Newileng (welcome) — I would like to take this opportunity to welcome our new and returning students to Northwest Indian College. Hy’shqe (thank

you) for choosing NWIC as a step forward in your academic pathway. NWIC embraces place-based and Indigenous knowledge in alignment with its institutional vision and mission. My hope is for you to continue taking steps that unite your personal curiosity with the noble purpose of making lasting differences in your respective communities. Welcome to Northwest Indian College. Dean of Academics — Rudy Vendiola (yǝˀqʷin)

Rudy Vendiola has served as the interim Dean of Academics since Jan. 2023 and became the permanent Dean of Academics in Dec. 2023. Rudy is an enrolled member of the Swinomish Tribe and of Lhaq’temish and Visayan descent. He has a Master’s degree in Adult Education and Bachelor’s degree in Communications, both from Western Washington University, and an AAS in Business from Skagit Valley College. Rudy has worked in

Indian education for over 35 years, including serving as the Indian Education Coordinator in the Ferndale School District for 17 years. Associate Dean of Two-Year Programs — Julia Orloff Julia Orloff (Sugpiaq (Alutiiq/Aleut through her paternal family) is the Associate Dean of Two-Year Programs for NWIC. She is a proud alumna, earning her Bachelor of Science in Native Environmental Science from NWIC in 2015. Julia

brings a varied background to her work, including experience in organized labor and commercial fishing with her father in Bristol Bay, Alaska. Julia has worked at NWIC for the past decade, where she’s served as a Native Environmental Science advisor and as the BSNES STEM Alumni & Internship Coordinator. She is deeply thankful for the education NWIC has provided and for the Lummi beliefs and values that have helped

strengthen her personal and Tribal identity. Julia looks forward to continuing to serve the NWIC community and supporting students as they prepare to become the next generation of Tribal and community leaders.
