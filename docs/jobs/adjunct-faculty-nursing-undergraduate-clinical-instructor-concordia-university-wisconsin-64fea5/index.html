Position Summary

Concordia University is seeking part-time adjunct faculty in Clinical Teaching for the Undergraduate Nursing Program on the Mequon, WI campus. The successful candidate will be a registered professional nurse with expertise and experience in OB, Med-surg, or Community Health, who is responsible for supervising students in the clinical area. This contract is renewed on a semester-by-semester basis. Job Duties &
