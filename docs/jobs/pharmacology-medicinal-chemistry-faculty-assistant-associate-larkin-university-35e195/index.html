Position Title: Assistant/Associate Professor of Pharmacology/Medicinal Chemistry Description: Larkin University (LU) College of Pharmacy (COP) is seeking a full-time faculty for the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences. The faculty will have responsibilities in different aspects of pharmacy academia, including but not limited to didactic teaching, laboratory teaching, research, student advising, and service to the

institution and profession.LUCOP’s accelerated Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) program is fully accredited by the Accreditation Council for Pharmacy Education (ACPE) and is a three-year curriculum. LUCOP is planning to offer a Master’s program in Pharmaceutical Sciences starting Fall 2027. Larkin University is a private not-for-profit 501(c)(3) university and consists of the College of Pharmacy, College of Biomedical

Sciences, School of Physician Assistant Studies, and School of Dental Medicine. More information about LUCOP is available at: https://www.larkin.edu/academics/college-of-pharmacy/LU maintains advanced and functional research capabilities in different areas of pharmaceutical sciences. flash chromatography system, SpectraMax multi-mode microplate reader platform, Zetasizer (dynamic light scattering), dissolution system, mixer, homogenizer, cell culture facility, real-time PCR, Western blotting, and FluorChem FC3 protein imager.
