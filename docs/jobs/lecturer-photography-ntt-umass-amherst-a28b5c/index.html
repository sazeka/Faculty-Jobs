Lecturer Photography NTT 510138 Amherst, Massachusetts, United States College of Humanities & Fine Arts Full Time Lecture & Non Tenure-Track On Site Faculty Opening on: Oct 27 2026 Closing on: Jan 27 2027 Title: Lecturer Photography NTT Executive Area: Academic Affairs College/School/MBU: Humanities & Fine Arts Department: Art Work Location: Amherst Schedule: Full Time Work Arrangement: Onsite Job Summary The

Department of Art at the University of Massachusetts Amherst seeks applicants for a full-time academic-year (nine-month) non-tenure track Lecturer position in Photography. This is a full-time, benefited position with an initial appointment of two years. Reappointments in the first four years are at the Dean’s discretion and will be contingent upon both availability of funding and satisfactory performance. Expected

start date: September 1, 2027. Essential Functions The successful candidate will be an experienced photographer or an artist who uses lens-based media and an educator who is committed to teaching. They will be able to teach all levels of analog processes and digital photography. Additional expertise in other media and/or experimental, cross-disciplinary approaches to photography are welcome. The successful candidate

will demonstrate an expertise in traditional and innovative approaches to photography, with the ability to apply skills and concepts in interdisciplinary contexts. They will stay current with trends and practices in the areas of photography, and work to develop foundational and advanced courses in photography and lens-based media. They will also mentor students in preparing for careers in photography-related fields,

including developing strong portfolios and other professional materials. The person in this position will initially teach three courses per semester and participate in service to the department for the academic year. The core courses this position delivers will be in photography, but the position may also teach interdisciplinary introductory, upper-level and graduate seminar courses, rotated among art department

faculty. Other teaching responsibilities include serving on BFA and MFA thesis committees, student advising, and helping to oversee photography facilities and equipment. A commitment to the academic success of all students and strong departmental citizenship is essential to a successful candidacy.
