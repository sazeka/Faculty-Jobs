Position summary

Medical Professions Instructor Arkansas State University Mid-South, a comprehensive community college located in West Memphis, Arkansas, seeks a full-time Allied Health Instructor to prepare students seeking to pursue a career in the medical professions. The successful candidate will be learner-focused, technology savvy, knowledgeable about the various fields in the healthcare setting, and excited about working with

ASU Mid-South in its Medical Professions program. The Medical Professions Instructor reports to the Dean of Allied Health and Sciences with responsibility for the instruction of secondary center and college-level courses, student advising, student retention, assessment of student learning, service on college committees, and other duties as assigned. Work assignments may include evening and/or weekend hours. This is a

10-month, 30 hour per week position with full college benefits. Required Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in any healthcare related subject, with appropriate professional certifications preferred; associate degree may be considered* Excellent spoken and written communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively with colleagues Preference will be given to candidates with prior teaching experience. *A

compelling combination of educational background, work experience, and professional credentials may be considered. Desired Qualifications: Teaching and/or other position-related work experience preferably at a community college Demonstrated understanding of academic needs of underprepared, low income, first generation, and traditionally underrepresented students Evidence of the ability to work effectively to meet the

educational needs of a diverse student body Preference will be given to candidates who understand active learning and divergent learning styles Essential Duties and Responsibilities: Teach courses for the Medical Professions program during the fall and spring semesters. Deliver instruction to secondary and postsecondary students that aligns with approved ASU Mid-South syllabi and academic standards, supporting

student attainment of course-and program-level learning outcomes. Support development of curriculum to align with initiatives in the Medical Professions program. Participate in medical professions and related program advisory committees. Maintain accurate attendance and grade records using the College’s LMS.
