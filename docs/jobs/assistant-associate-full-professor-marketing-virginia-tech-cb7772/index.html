Assistant/Associate/Full Professor - Marketing 533529 Blacksburg, Virginia Marketing Teaching & Research Faculty College of Business Job Description Open rank tenure track faculty position. Responsibilities will include teaching undergraduate and graduate courses offered by the Department of Marketing, advising undergraduate and graduate students, pursuing a program of research leading to publication in major

academic journals in marketing and allied fields, and university service including committee and outreach activities as appropriate to the faculty member’s rank and expertise. Required Qualifications Doctorate in marketing or related area (or in the final stages of completing such a doctorate) or doctoral level degree and demonstrated academic accomplishments in marketing. Instructional capabilities at undergraduate

and graduate levels. Assistant professor applicants must show potential for excellence in scholarship in marketing and related areas. Applicants at the associate professor or higher rank must have a demonstrated record of excellence in scholarship. Preferred Qualifications Preference will be given to candidates whose research and teaching interests focus on quantitative empirical modeling. Overtime Status Exempt: Not

eligible for overtime Appointment Type Regular Salary Information Hours per week 40 Review Date 07/15/2025 and to continue until filled.
