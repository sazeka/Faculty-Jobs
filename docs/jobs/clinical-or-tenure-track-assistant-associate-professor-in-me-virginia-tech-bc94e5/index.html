Oncology. The final track will be offered based on the qualifications and preferences of the successful candidate.This is a rare and exciting opportunity to join a collaborative, multi-disciplinary oncology team within the Animal Cancer Care & Research Center (ACCRC), part of the Virginia Tech Carilion Research Institute’s biomedical research complex in Roanoke, VA. dedicated team that includes a medical oncology hospitalist, two radiation oncologists, one surgical oncologist (with a second position in negotiation), and specialty interns.Advanced Facilities & Technology:The ACCRC is equipped with a USP800-compliant chemotherapy suite, a 64-slice CT scanner (Siemens SOMATOM Confidence), digital radiography, ultrasonography, three modern operating rooms, and a state-of-the-art

Varian Edge linear accelerator. It is a premier small animal cancer research hospital delivering exceptional, comprehensive, and

compassionate cancer care. collaboration and professional growth.Life in the Roanoke Valley:Roanoke, the largest city in southwest Virginia, offers an exceptional quality of life with a rare combination of natural beauty, outdoor adventure, and low cost of living.
