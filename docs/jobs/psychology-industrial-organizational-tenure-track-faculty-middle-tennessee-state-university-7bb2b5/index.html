Job Summary/Basic Function The Department of Psychology at Middle Tennessee State University invites applicants for a full-time, tenure-track faculty position (#120490) at the rank of assistant or associate professor in industrial-organizational

psychology. The start date for the position is August 1, 2027. The successful candidate will teach undergraduate and graduate I-O courses, including courses that contribute to the department's broader teaching needs (e.g., general psychology, basic statistics, social psychology, or research methods), supervise student research and theses, and develop a research program. Areas of I-O specialization are open. We have

the #1 nationally ranked M.A. program in Industrial-Organizational Psychology and a strong undergraduate major in I-O psychology. Additionally, the successful candidate will be able to support the MTSU Center for Organizational and Human Resource Effectiveness. Required Education A terminal degree in industrial-organizational psychology or a closely related field with at least eighteen (18) graduate hours in

psychology is required by the appointment date. Other Desirables Applicants with evidence of effective teaching, strong quantitative skills, evidence of an active research program, and applied experience will be given special consideration. Documents Needed to Apply To successfully apply, applicants must include a cover letter, curriculum vitae, statement of teaching philosophy, and statement of research or creative

activities (as appropriate to the discipline) as separate attachments when submitting the application. Special Instruction to Applicants If you need help with the application, please contact Mitzi Dunkley, Faculty Recruitment Specialist, at (615) 898-5128.
