Adjunct Faculty - Criminal Justice Apply now Job no: 50000318 Work type: Part time Location: Virginia Beach Categories: Faculty & Adjunct, College of Arts & Sciences Regent University’s College of Arts and Sciences is seeking adjunct faculty to teach a variety of courses in criminal justice. The ideal candidate will be able to meaningfully integrate their Christian faith into teaching in their discipline. The

applicant will be expected to teach both on-campus and online courses. An earned Ph.D. in Criminal Justice; Juris Doctorate; combination of a PhD in Criminal Justice and Juris Doctorate; or a master's level degree and five or more years of specialized, professional experience in criminal justice is required. Practical criminal justice and/or law practice experience and previous undergraduate teaching experience is

preferred. The candidate must have excellent research, communication, mentoring, and organizational skills, as well as a knowledge of online learning platforms and Microsoft office programs. Expectations include 1) a dedication to Christ-centered teaching and learning; 2) a record of or potential for academic scholarship; 3) the ability to teach undergraduate-level courses; 4) facility with online instructional

technology; and 5) a willingness to embrace the College and University Identity and Mission statements and subscribe to a statement of Christian faith. Salary: $100/credit hour/student up to $3,000 per course Please submit CV, cover letter, and unofficial transcripts along with your application.
