Assistant Teaching Professor, Natural Sciences

Babson College  ·  Wellesley MA, MA
Assistant ProfessorNon-TenureNatural Sciences

Position summary

THE OPPORTUNITY The Mathematics, Analytics, Science and Technology (MAST) Division at Babson College is seeking a full-time teaching professor in the natural sciences, with an environmental science focus. The position is non-tenure track, at the Assistant level and the start date is Fall 2027. A successful applicant will have a passion for undergraduate teaching of non-science majors, as Babson only confers business

degrees. The successful applicant will also be active in some form of research or applied work, whether traditional discipline-specific research, pedagogical research or interdisciplinary research or applications. Finally, a successful applicant will also demonstrate a desire to contribute to Babson’s mission through service on campus. The MAST Division includes nineteen full-time faculty and several part-time

instructors. WHAT YOU WILL DO Teach natural science to undergraduates. Teaching responsibilities will vary and may include the foundation science courses (multi-science discipline courses with hands-on labs) and our innovative intermediate Socio-Ecological Systems courses , which pair a natural scientist with a social scientist to co-teach integrated sustainability and systems thinking. Work as a team player and

willing to collaborate with faculty across disciplines, both in integrated team teaching and in college service. Commit to working in ways that will support and enhance the College’s commitment to teaching in an aligned, engaged, and inclusive manner. Assume additional responsibilities as required. WHAT EDUCATION AND SKILLS YOU WILL NEED Ph.D. (or an equivalent terminal degree) in a natural science discipline with a

strong connection to environmental science, climate science, ecology, environmental studies, and/or energy systems. We encourage applications from faculty of all backgrounds and career stages. Passionate about teaching and innovative curriculum development in the undergraduate setting. Demonstrated competency in traditional academic research, scholarship of teaching and learning, or engagement in practice. A team

player, willing to lead and collaborate with faculty from multiple disciplines to co-design and co-teach courses in an integrated, cross-disciplinary context. Aligned with our values of teaching excellence, collaboration, and inclusion. Embraces Babson’s mission to educate entrepreneurial leaders who create great economic and social value everywhere.
