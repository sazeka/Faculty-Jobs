Núria Rodríguez-Planas is Named a Distinguished Professor

CUNY Queens College  ·  School of Business  ·  CUNY Queens College, NY
Full ProfessorEconomics

Position summary

FacebookTwitterLinkedIn Queens College Faculty Member Named City University of New York Distinguished Professor of Economics —Núria Rodríguez-Planas examines how gender norms, public policy, and economic institutions shape opportunities and outcomes over the life course— Flushing, NY, July 14, 2026—Queens College faculty member Núria Rodríguez-Planas has been elevated to the rank of distinguished professor of

economics. Rodríguez-Planas teaches in the college’s school of business and at the City University of New York (CUNY) Graduate Center. The appointment was approved by the CUNY Board of Trustees in June, bringing the college’s total number of distinguished professors to 15. The title of distinguished professor is the highest academic honor that CUNY can offer its faculty, conferring it in recognition of exceptional

scholarly achievement. It is reserved for faculty with records of outstanding performance by national and international standards of excellence in their field. A renowned scholar in labor economics, gender economics, and the economics of education, Rodríguez-Planas examines how gender norms, public policy, and economic institutions shape opportunities and outcomes over the course of people’s lives. Her scholarship

has produced foundational evidence on the role of gender norms in shaping educational achievement, labor force participation, financial literacy, risky behaviors, and intimate partner violence. Her work has also informed policy debates on unemployment insurance, family-friendly labor market policies, educational interventions, and social protection programs in both the United States and Europe. Rodríguez-Planas has

served on the Queens College faculty since 2014. Before joining CUNY, she was senior research associate at the Institute for the Study of Labor (IZA) in Bonn, Germany. Previously, she was a faculty member in the Department of Economics and Economic History at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, in Barcelona, Spain. Earlier in her career, Rodríguez-Planas served as an economist at Mathematica Policy Research, the

Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, and the Brookings Institution in Washington, D.C. She has published nearly 50 peer-reviewed articles that have appeared in such prestigious journals as the American Economic Review, American Economic Journal: Applied Economics, Journal of Labor Economics, Journal of Human Resources, Journal of Public Economics, and Economics of Education Review.
