View all jobsSocial Work Director of Field Education Faculty PositionPortland, OregonEducationPosition Summary The Social Work Director of Field Education provides academic and administrative leadership for the social work field education program. The position coordinates students’ supervised field practicum experiences, develops and maintains agency partnerships, supports field instructors and students, and ensures

that field education meets accreditation, institutional, and professional standards. The Director of Field Education is a full-time faculty member who contributes to teaching, student advising, curriculum development, program assessment, service, and scholarly or professional activities consistent with the institution’s appointment expectations. The position is a ranked faculty member at the assistant or associate

rank depending on education and years of experience. The position is located in the School of Social Science and Social Work. Essential Responsibilities Direct and administer the undergraduate social work field education program. Develop, evaluate, and maintain field placement opportunities that fulfill social work program learning outcomes and students’ professional development. Recruit, screen, orient, and evaluate

field agencies and field instructors. Match students with appropriate field placements based on their educational needs, goals, availability, accessibility, and agency capacity. Coordinate and direct field placements for social work major and social work dual degree majors (psychology, criminology and justice), collaborating with faculty colleagues in School of Social Science and Social Work. Provide orientation,

consultation, and ongoing support to students, agency field instructors, agency-based supervisors, and WPU faculty liaisons. Monitor student progress and address performance, ethical, safety, and/or placement concerns. Coordinate placement planning, contracts, documentation, evaluations, and CSWE accreditation compliance. Teach field education seminars, practice courses, and/or other courses as assigned. Advise and

mentor students regarding field requirements, professional development, licensure pathways, and academic progress. Collaborate with faculty to integrate classroom learning with field experiences. Ensure field education policies and practices that align with applicable accreditation standards, institutional practices and policies, and social work ethical standards. Gather field education data for social work program
