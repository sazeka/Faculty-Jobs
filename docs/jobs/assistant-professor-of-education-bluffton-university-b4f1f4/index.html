POSITION SUMMARY: Faculty typically teach 23-25 credits per academic year and engage in advising, service, and professional activities

consistent with Bluffton University expectations. Appointments may include additional teaching and/or administrative responsibilities as appropriate. Specific appointment terms, workload expectations, and related policies are defined at the time of appointment. The Assistant Professor of Education is a tenure track position. The successful candidate will teach education courses and general curriculum courses in the

undergraduate and graduate programs. Expertise/experience in mathematics curriculum and instruction is preferred but not required. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES: Teach in the undergraduate, graduate education and general education program. Supervise student teachers.
