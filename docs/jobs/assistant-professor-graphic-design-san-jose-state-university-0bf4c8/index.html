Position Description The Department of Design at San José State University invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor position in Graphic Design, beginning in Fall 2027. This is a nine-month, academic-year appointment. We seek a designer, educator, and researcher whose practice bridges analog and digital forms, and who can teach

across the undergraduate and graduate graphic design curriculum, including foundational studios, typography, visual systems, interaction design, and advanced electives aligned with the candidate's expertise. The ideal candidate will bring a strong foundation in typography, visual systems, and critical and experimental approaches to design-making, with agility in contemporary digital tools, technologies, and emerging

practices. We are particularly interested in designers who expand the possibilities of graphic design through experimentation, systems thinking, collaboration, and engagement with contemporary social and technological questions. The ideal candidate demonstrates formal rigor, conceptual depth, and an active practice that contributes new knowledge through creative practice, design research, scholarship, or public

engagement. The department recognizes multiple forms of scholarly and creative activity, including peer-reviewed publications, exhibitions, commissioned work, funded research, public scholarship, and other contributions that advance the discipline.
