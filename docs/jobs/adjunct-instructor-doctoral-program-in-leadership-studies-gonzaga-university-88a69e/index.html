Job Purpose: invites applications for its Adjunct Instructor Pool. This pool focuses on teaching doctoral-level research courses, including classes such as Principles of

Research and Quantitative Research Methods. Depending on qualifications, instructors may also be asked to teach elective courses currently offered in the program. We will contact candidates as courses become available. Generally, this will be between 1-3 courses in an academic year. Courses are delivered online (Canvas) in either 8 (summer) or 16-week formats and may include a required in-person campus immersion

component. Successful candidates will demonstrate a commitment to mentoring and supporting graduate students as they develop the knowledge, skills, and dispositions needed to conduct doctoral-level social science research effectively. Our program ethos is to support students in developing their own research agenda and focus. Adjunct faculty are responsible for teaching developed hybrid and/or online courses, teaching

immersion class sessions, evaluating assignments, submitting grades, and being available to meet with students as needed. Adjuncts are also expected to communicate with the departmental chairperson regarding student concerns, departmental expectations, and other areas of student engagement and success. We seek candidates with graduate-level teaching experience and demonstrated expertise in social science, leadership,

or closely related fields. Evidence of continued research activity beyond the dissertation is desirable. Gonzaga University is committed to cultivating an intellectually vibrant and culturally inclusive community for students, faculty, and staff.
