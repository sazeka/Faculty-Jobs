Surgical Technology Adjunct Faculty Job Category Part-Time/Adjunct Faculty Division Health Professions Department Main Campus Job Description Plan, prepare, implement, teach, and evaluate classroom, laboratory, and clinical instruction for the Surgical Technology program. Participate in program development and evaluation as needed. Participate in program-related activities as needed. Required Qualifications MINIMUM

REQUIREMENTSThree years of experience, either in the operating room scrub role or as an instructor in surgical technology, or a combination of both, within the past five years. REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, AND ABILITIES Ability to
