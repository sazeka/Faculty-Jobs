Back to Listings Tenure-Track: Assistant Professor Hospitality, Hotel Management and Tourism Location College Station Open Date 8/20/2026 Position ID 190205 Description The recently renamed and relaunched Arch H. Aplin III ’80 Department of Hospitality, Hotel Management and Tourism has a prominent 60-year history at Texas A&M University, a leading and well-resourced R1 land-grant institution. Applications are

welcomed for a full-time tenure-track appointment beginning Fall 2027. This is a 9-month academic position, with additional opportunities for summer appointments. The faculty title of Assistant Professor is dependent on qualifications. The position will be based at the University’s flagship College Station campus. We seek candidates who can contribute to the department’s growing strengths in hospitality, hotel

management, tourism, and related service industries through innovative, impactful, and industry-relevant scholarship. Areas of interest are open and may include, but are not limited to, hospitality and tourism management, technology-enabled service innovation, data-informed decision-making, complex service systems, and other emerging topics relevant to hospitality, tourism, and related service industries.

Applications are invited from candidates who bring innovative perspectives - theoretical, methodological, and professional. We welcome candidates whose work demonstrates strong potential to advance hospitality and tourism scholarship, and who can contribute to high-quality teaching, communicate effectively with academic and professional audiences, and engage meaningfully with industry, community, and

interdisciplinary partners. The successful candidate will be expected to contribute to teaching excellence at the undergraduate and graduate levels to; engage in student mentorship; develop a productive and externally visible research program; and engage in service (to the department, university, profession, and/or industry and community partners). The department holds consistently high ratings in global rankings of

university hospitality, tourism and recreation programs and has an established record of accomplishment in teaching, research and public service. Our distinguished, multi-disciplinary faculty has authored thousands of impactful journal articles, books, and technical reports, and have provided intellectual leadership internationally in hospitality, tourism, events and recreation.
