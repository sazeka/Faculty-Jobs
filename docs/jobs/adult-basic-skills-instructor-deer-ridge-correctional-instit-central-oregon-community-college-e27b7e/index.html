Adult Basic Skills instruction to incarcerated adults in Reading, Language Arts, Writing, Social Studies, Science, and Math. Curriculum delivery includes recognition of the many student learning styles, measurement of student progress and flexibility in meeting individual student needs. Performs all aspects of the position within the rules and constraints of the corrections environment.The successful candidate must

pass the security clearance and be approved to enter Oregon Department of Corrections facilities; in addition to all College policies and the terms of the COCC/ABS collective bargaining agreement, this position is subject to the rules and regulations of the sponsoring agency. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Provide supervision and support of AIC (Inmate) students and tutors in the ABE/GED classroom. Create

individualized student education goals and plans for students based on student interview and pretest data. Assign appropriate curriculum to student needs. (Not SPED IEPs) Coordinate students with the appropriate tutor. Train and advise tutors working with GED students. Work with individual and small groups of students.
