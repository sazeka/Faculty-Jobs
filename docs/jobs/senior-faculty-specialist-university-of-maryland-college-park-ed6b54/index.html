Job Description Summary Organization's Summary Statement: The Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering in the A. James Clark School of Engineering is seeking a Senior Systems Administrator to support enterprise IT operations at the Laboratory for Physical Sciences (LPS). Since 1956, LPS, in partnership with the University of Maryland, has been advancing the physics and engineering behind information science

and technology. LPS is a unique organization with a model of basic and applied research at the intersection of academic research and national security, supporting over 400 users across multiple sites. The Senior Systems Administrator will serve as a senior member of a small, collaborative IT team responsible for maintaining core IT infrastructure, improving production system reliability and security, and providing

hands-on support to researchers and staff across multiple sites. The ideal candidate is a versatile generalist with strong fundamentals in systems and network administration, a methodical approach to problem-solving, and a record of delivering dependable IT services in mission-critical environments. The A. James Clark School of Engineering at the University of Maryland serves as a catalyst for high-quality research,

innovation, and learning, preparing our students to develop innovations that address the 21st-century Grand Challenges (e.g., energy, environment, security, and human health) and improve the human condition. The Clark School is dedicated to leading and transforming the engineering discipline and profession, accelerating entrepreneurship, and transforming research and learning activities into innovations that benefit

millions. Minimum Qualifications Education: Bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field. Experience: Six (6) years of professional systems administration experience in a team-based IT organization, including hands-on management of Windows Server and/or Linux environments supporting a substantial user base.
