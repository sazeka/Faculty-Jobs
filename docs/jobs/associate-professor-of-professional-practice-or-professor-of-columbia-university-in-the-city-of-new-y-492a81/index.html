Associate Professor of Professional Practice or Professor of Professional Practice in Actuarial Science

Columbia University in the City of New York  ·  New York, NY
Associate ProfessorActuarial Science

Position summary

The School of Professional Studies (SPS) at Columbia University invites applications for an Associate Professor of Professional Practice or Professor of Professional Practice to serve as a core faculty member in SPS's M.S. program in Actuarial Science . This is a full-time appointment with multi-year renewal contingent on successful review. This position is expected to begin January 1, 2027. A later start date of

July 1, 2027 may be considered. &nbsp; JOB RESPONSIBILITIES The role involves the following main activities: 1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Teaching: Teaching a minimum of four (4) core courses per academic year: two (2) in each of the fall and spring semesters. 2.&nbsp;&nbsp; Service: Serving the Actuarial Program, through such activities as: Continually evaluating and enhancing curriculum to maintain academic rigor,

professional relevance, and alignment with developments in actuarial practice; Recruiting, onboarding, developing, and evaluating adjunct faculty; Supporting admissions and enrollment activities, including promoting the program to prospective students and participating in application review; Advising students on academic planning, course selection, actuarial professional development, and career paths; Supporting

students and graduates in career development and employment outcomes; Developing co-curricular programming; and Fostering engagement with actuarial employers, practitioners, and professional organizations. 3.&nbsp;&nbsp; Eminence: Serving the actuarial profession as a thought leader, through such activities as: Publishing articles in industry journals; Authoring books or book chapters; Publishing research studies;

Speaking at industry conferences; Serving on industry committees; and Holding leadership roles in relevant professional organizations. Review of applications will begin immediately and will continue until the position is filled.
