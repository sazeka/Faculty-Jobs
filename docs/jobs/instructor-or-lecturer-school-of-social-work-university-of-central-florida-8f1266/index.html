Instructor or Lecturer, School of Social Work R114116 Main Campus, Orlando, Florida Faculty Full time Social Work - Academic Administration The OpportunityThe School of Social Work in the College of Health Professions and Sciences at the University of Central Florida invites applications for a 12-month, non-tenure earning, instructor or lecturer position. The selected candidate will be expected to teach and field

placement in the BSW and MSW programs (both campus based and online); and participate in meaningful service at the school, college, university, and community levels. The selected candidate will be assigned to the main Orlando campus, with an anticipated start date of Summer 2027.The School of Social Work is recognized regionally and nationally for the superior quality of its educational offerings and its

contributions to clinical social work, health care, scholarship, and service to our community. The school has 34 full-time faculty, 10 staff, numerous adjunct faculty, and over 900 students. We offer a BSW, and an MSW program. There are various campus base and online tracks in the MSW program. The College of Health Professions and Sciences includes three schools, one department, and six centers and institutes and is

a core member of the UCF Academic Health Sciences Center, alongside the College of Nursing and College of Medicine. The college is committed to interdisciplinary collaboration and community impact.Minimum Qualifications: For instructor rank, candidates must hold an MSW from a CSWE -accredited program awarded by an accredited institution at the time of application. For lecturer rank, candidates must hold an MSW from a

CSWE-accredited program awarded by an accredited institution and a Ph.D./DSW in Social Work or related field from an accredited institution at the time of application. At least one year of experience with field social work education as a teacher/instructor, field instructor or supervisor. Two years of post MSW social work practice experience. Preferred Qualifications: Teaching experience in social work; both online

and campus based. Active status as a qualified supervisor for registered social work interns. Additional Application Materials Required:UCF requires all applications and supporting documents to be submitted electronically through the Human Resources employment opportunities website, https://www.ucf.edu/jobs/. In addition to the online application, interested candidates should upload the following: A curriculum vitae.
