School of Architecture (SoA) within the University of Utah’s College of Architecture + Planning invites applications for an Assistant Professor (Tenure-Track) position in any of the following three content areas: Advanced Building Systems and Technologies; Architectural Design + Historic Preservation, Rehabilitation, and/or GIS; or Human Interactions and Big Data. SoA is highly interested in candidates with expertise

in contemporary technologies, transformative processes, and approaches in integrating interdisciplinary collaboration to advance innovative design, research, or creative work, and teaching. This is a full-time, nine-month appointment (1.0 FTE) beginning July 1, 2027. The School of Architecture (SoA) has a long history of excellence in education, research, and societal impact, with our original architecture degree

program dating back over 75 years. As home to the only professionally accredited Master of Architecture (M.Arch) degree in the state, the school is known for leading transformative changes, most recently through an integrated curriculum, award-winning design-build programs, and sustained enrollment growth. During the 2025 NAAB Accreditation Site Visit for the M.Arch program, the school excelled, meeting all criteria

and achieving 100% compliance. The school aims to be a national leader in architectural education, research, and practice, strongly aligned with the University of Utah’s goals.
