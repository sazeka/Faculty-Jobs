Number of Positions In Requisition 1&#xa;&#xa; Job Details Thomas Jefferson University invites applications for a full-time, nine-month faculty position in Landscape Architecture at the rank of Assistant Professor. The appointment will be on the Teacher-Scholar, or Practice-Scholar track, depending on the candidate’s qualifications and experience. Salary will be commensurate with experience and qualifications. The

successful candidate will teach across the undergraduate landscape architecture curriculum, including design studios, lecture courses, and seminars. We seek an individual with a strong foundation in landscape architectural design who can help students connect design fundamentals with interdisciplinary approaches to complex environmental and societal challenges. Responsibilities include contributing to curriculum

development and collaborating with colleagues across the College to strengthen transdisciplinary teaching and learning. The candidate will join a collegial faculty and pursue research/scholarship or professional practice appropriate to the appointment track. We seek applicants whose work and experiences will expand our intellectual diversity. We value applicants whose work elevates marginalized voices, embraces the

humanistic dimensions of design, and engages their intersection with technology. We encourage individuals from historically underrepresented communities including women, Native American peoples, Black and Indigenous peoples of color, Latinx, people who identify as LGBTQIA+, veterans, and persons with disabilities to apply. Job Description Qualifications: Candidates must possess one of the following categories of

acceptable academic credentials: • A combination of a professional bachelor’s and a master’s degree; or • A terminal professional master’s degree; The professional degree must be in Landscape Architecture. Foreign equivalents to U.S. degrees are acceptable. Professional Credentials: Candidates must hold registration as a landscape architect or be actively working toward registration. Applicants pursuing registration

must have completed the required experience hours necessary to qualify for the licensing exam. Successful candidates who are not yet registered must achieve licensure within two years of their contract date. Desired Qualifications: We will consider applicants with degrees and experience in practice, research, and teaching in landscape architecture.
