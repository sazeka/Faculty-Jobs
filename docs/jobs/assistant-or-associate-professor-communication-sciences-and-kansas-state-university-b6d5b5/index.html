Assistant or Associate Professor, Communication Sciences & Disorders The School of Health Sciences, within the College of Health and Human Sciences, is looking to hire two 9-month faculty members at the rank of Assistant or Associate Professor (tenure-track) to join their Communication Sciences and Disorders (CSD) program. 522150 Manhattan School of Health Sciences Academic / Faculty - 9 month Closing at: Oct 20 2026

- 23:55 CDT On-site About This Role The School of Health Sciences, within the College of Health and Human Sciences, is looking to hire two 9-month faculty members at the rank of Assistant or Associate Professor (tenure-track) to join their Communication Sciences and Disorders (CSD) program. We welcome candidates with expertise in any area of speech-language pathology, although candidates with a background in

dysphagia, adult neurogenic communication disorders, and/or neuroanatomy are especially encouraged to apply, as these areas align with current curriculum priorities. Successful candidates will join a program with an active interprofessional education initiative, an on-campus speech and hearing clinic, strong connections to the local healthcare and education communities, and a school-level commitment to translational

and community-engaged scholarship. Responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Establish and maintain an active, externally funded program of research, with dissemination through peer-reviewed outlets Teach undergraduate and/or graduate courses aligned with area/s of expertise and program need Advise graduate students Provide service to the program, school, college, and university Engage in interdisciplinary

and collaborative scholarship opportunities within the school The selected candidates will be expected to maintain a 2:2 teaching load across the academic year, equivalent to 0.4 FTE, with allocation of remaining tenths related to research and service being negotiable. These 9-month positions at the rank of assistant or associate professor have a preferred starting date of August 2027, with a competitive salary

commensurate with qualifications and experience. Spring or summer 2027 start dates may be possible upon request. Individuals with a clinical doctorate may be considered for a non-tenure track faculty position. For more information, please reach out to Dr. Bob Garcia at rgarcia@ksu.edu. About Us The Communication Sciences and Disorders (CSD) program has been a nationally recognized presence at Kansas State University
