Position description The University of California, Riverside School of Medicine invites nominations and applications for the position of Senior Associate Dean for Student Affairs. Reporting

directly to the Vice Chancellor for Health Sciences and Dean, the Senior Associate Dean serves as the School’s chief student affairs officer and provides strategic leadership for admissions, student success, academic support, wellness, career advising, student engagement, pathway programs, and student life. The incumbent will have a concurrent academic appointment in an appropriate department in the School of

Medicine. Successful candidates must also have clear potential or demonstrated ability to work successfully with and benefit a diverse student body. As a member of the School’s senior leadership team, the Senior Associate Dean will advance innovative, learner-centered programs that support medical students from recruitment through graduation and residency placement. The position also provides executive oversight for

student information management, data governance, regulatory compliance, and accreditation-related student affairs activities.
