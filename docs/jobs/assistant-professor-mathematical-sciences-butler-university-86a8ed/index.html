Assistant Professor, Mathematical Sciences IN, United States (On-site) Be the First to Apply Job Description The Department of Mathematical Sciences at Butler University invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor position starting in August 2027. We welcome applicants in Statistics or closely related fields, but we will consider applicants from all mathematical fields willing to teach courses in our

Statistics Major. Other teaching responsibilities may include courses in our Actuarial Science and Data Science Majors, introductory courses in our Mathematics Major, and courses for non-majors. The typical teaching load is three courses (9 credit hours) per semester. Questions about this position may be directed to Dr. Scott Kaschner (skaschne@butler.edu).Applicants should have:Candidates must have a PhD by the time

of appointment. The ideal candidate will have a demonstrated commitment to evidence-based and engaged-learning pedagogies as well as to building an inclusive classroom environment for an increasingly diverse student body. We value candidates who share our commitment to engagement and inclusion of students, faculty, and staff with diverse backgrounds, perspectives, and experiences.Successful candidates will be

expected to sustain a research program involving undergraduates and show a commitment to working with students on undergraduate theses and other independent projects. Butler has an established undergraduate research program supported by institutional funds, including internal research funds, the Butler Summer Institute to support summer student research, and the annual Undergraduate Research Conference. There are

also funds available to support faculty and student travel to conferences. Start-up funds are available for this position. To apply, applicants should submit the following: a cover letter discussing qualifications for and interest in the position.
