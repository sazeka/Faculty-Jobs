UNIV - Assistant Professor - Department of Pediatrics

Medical University of South Carolina  ·  Department of Pediatrics  ·  Charleston, SC
Assistant ProfessorPediatrics

Position summary

Job Description Summary Incumbent will provide clinical care of complex pediatric oncology, hematology, and cellular therapy patients. This includes the design of treatment regimens and preparative regimens for autologous stem cell transplant, allogeneic stem cell transplant (includes haploidentical donor, mismatched unrelated donors, matched unrelated donors, matched related donors and cord blood transplants), CAR-T

cell therapy, and gene therapy for sickle cell disease. Incumbent will also supervise clinical education in the pediatric hematology, oncology, and cellular therapy inpatient and outpatient units and participate in didactic teaching and clinic training of medical students, pediatric residents, and pediatric hematology/oncology fellows. Degree required: MD, DO or MBBS degree and completion of 36 months of residency

training in pediatrics and 36 months of training in pediatric hematology/oncology; must be eligible for South Carolina Medical License and must be eligible for board certification in pediatric hematology/oncology. Foreign educational equivalency acceptable. Hours: 7am to 7pm, 7 days on/7 days off. Resumes can be submitted by mail to: Attention: Timothy Leonard Stroman, Administrative Mannager II, 125 Doughty Street
