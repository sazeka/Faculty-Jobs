Dean of the School of Technology and Design POSITION DETAILS New York City College of Technology (City Tech or the College) of the City University of New York (CUNY) is a comprehensive college in downtown Brooklyn, offering associate and baccalaureate degrees in technology and health related degree programs, other career-oriented degrees and liberal arts transfer degrees in its Schools of Arts and Sciences,

Technology and Design, and Professional Studies. We are the largest public college of technology in New York State, and exceptional in that we go beyond traditional class offerings to create a high-tech, hands-on educational experience for our students in order to prepare them for a rapidly changing world. City Tech is one of ten CUNY colleges designated as leading research institutions in 2025’s Carnegie

Classifications of Institutions of Higher Education. It was ranked 22 overall and ranked number 15 as a top performer on social mobility in US News and World Report’s Best College Rankings (within Regional Colleges North). City Tech seeks an exceptional leader to serve as Dean of the School of Technology and Design. The School of Technology & Design blends theory and practice to prepare students for careers in

engineering, design, media technologies, architecture, construction, and related fields. With a strong emphasis on digital modeling, production tools, systems integration, and real-world materials and processes, the School serves sectors ranging from communication design and new media to facilities management, telecommunications, robotics, and environmental systems. The School is organized into the following

departments: Architecture Communication Design Computer Engineering Technology Computer Systems Technology Construction Management / Civil Engineering Technology Electrical and Telecommunications Engineering Technology Entertainment Technology Environmental Control Technology Mechanical Engineering Technology Reporting to the Provost, and serving as a member of the President’s Cabinet, the Dean provides strategic

vision and operational leadership to all aspects of the School’s academic and scholarly programs.
