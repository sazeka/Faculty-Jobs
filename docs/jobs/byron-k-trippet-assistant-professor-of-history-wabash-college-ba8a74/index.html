Byron K. Trippet Assistant Professor of History

Wabash College  ·  History  ·  Crawfordsville, IN
Assistant ProfessorTenure-TrackHistory

Position summary

The Wabash College History Department invites applications for a full-time tenure-track position in Medieval history to begin July 1, 2027, at the rank of Assistant Professor. A Ph.D. in History or a related field is required by the start date. The successful candidate will demonstrate a strong commitment to teaching and research in an undergraduate liberal arts setting. Teaching responsibilities consist of three

courses per semester, including introductory world history courses, all-college courses, and advanced courses in non-Atlantic regional history, such as the Middle East, North Africa, or other related regions. The History Department at Wabash College currently offers courses in World, U.S., Latin American, and European history. This position is intended to extend the department’s coverage of medieval and non-Atlantic

history while expanding the range of topics currently offered in advanced courses and seminars for majors and minors. Further information about the History Department is available at https://www.wabash.edu/academics/history. Wabash College offers competitive salaries and benefits with substantial support for professional travel and research as well as continued professional development orientations and workshops for

all faculty. In the pre-tenure years, this position’s appointment is as a Byron K. Trippet professor, which provides additional teaching and research support. About Wabash College Founded in 1832, Wabash College is a private, independent, residential four-year liberal arts college with a mission to “educate men to think critically, act responsibly, lead effectively, and live humanely.” Its faculty is ranked No. 9

nationally for accessibility by the Princeton Review. The College invests significant resources to ensure students can participate in off-campus and international immersion learning experiences. Our 92-acre wooded campus is located in Crawfordsville, Indiana. Crawfordsville is a growing, vibrant community with easy access to outdoor recreation and facilities. Within 50 minutes of Indianapolis, Crawfordsville is close

to an array of nationally recognized museums and sports facilities, including those housing the WNBA Fever, NBA Pacers, and NFL Colts. In the midst of a strategic transformation to advance the success of first-generation students and students from low-income families, Wabash College is committed to attracting and effectively supporting faculty and staff who are committed to student success.
