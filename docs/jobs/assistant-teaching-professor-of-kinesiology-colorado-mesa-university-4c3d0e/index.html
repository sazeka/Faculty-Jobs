Colorado Mesa University  ·  Grand Junction, CO

Position summary

ANNOUNCEMENT OF POSITION VACANCY ABOUT THIS POSITION Summary: Colorado Mesa University invites applications for an Assistant Teaching Professor of Kinesiology to begin Fall 2027. The chosen candidate will be responsible for assisting with the teaching and advising needs of the undergraduate Kinesiology programs. The standard teaching load is 12 credit hours per semester; courses will be assigned as in-person.

Involvement with advising and service to the department, university, and profession are expected. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES include the following. Other related duties may be assigned. Teach undergraduate Kinesiology courses including: Activity courses Health and Wellness Foundations of Kinesiology Applications of Physical Fitness Methods of Group Exercise Instruction Health and Fitness Assessment

Exercise Physiology Labs Provide academic advisement to students in the Kinesiology programs. Provide service to the department, university, community, and profession. EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE: REQUIRED: Masters in Kinesiology-related field. Excellent oral, written, and interpersonal communication skills. Undergraduate teaching experience. Certification through the National Strength and Conditioning Association

(NSCA; CSCS, NSCA-CPT, CSPS, CPSS, and/or TSAC-F) or the American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM; ACSM-CPT, ACSM-EP, ACSM-GEI, ACSM-CEP) PREFERRED: Doctorate degree in Kinesiology-related field Experience advising undergraduate students Experience working as a professional in the field of Kinesiology, with emphasis on personal training, strength and conditioning, group exercise Interest in teaching in a CASCE

accredited program Salary: $55,000 - $61,500 Commensurate with education and experience. Excellent health and retirement benefits package: Click here for more information! HOW TO APPLY: Applicants will be asked to complete an online application by creating a profile and then providing personal and demographic information.
