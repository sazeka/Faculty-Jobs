candidates for two full-time, non-tenure track Legal Writing Professors of Law, beginning in the 2026-2027 academic year. These are 10-month contract position starting on August 1, 2027.The positions lead to a presumptively renewable five-year appointment after a probationary period of five one-year appointments. Lateral candidates will also receive consideration. Legal Writing professors at Cleveland State may apply

for promotion to Senior Legal Writing Professor three or more years after attaining the first five-year appointment. Salary will be competitive and commensurate with experience. Faculty hired for this position will teach upper-level writing courses focused on skills tested in the NextGen bar exam. They may also teach in our first-year Legal Writing program or teach other upper-level courses that fit their expertise

and the law school’s needs.Legal Writing professors may also advise students undertaking independent research projects and have other administrative and advising duties within the College of Law. Additionally, they may choose to participate in the broader legal writing community through scholarship and service.Legal Writing professors at Cleveland State vote on all matters except those concerning the hiring,

promotion, and tenure of tenure-track faculty. They participate in law school and university governance, serve on and chair faculty committees, and otherwise contribute as valued members of the Cleveland State community. Legal Writing professors are eligible to apply for summer teaching and research grants as well as funding for academic travel. This position requires teaching on-campus courses. In addition, you may

be assigned to teach off campus, evenings, weekends, and via distance learning technology, depending upon student need for the delivery of instructional services. CSU College of Law offers a comprehensive legal writing curriculum taught by experienced legal writing faculty.
