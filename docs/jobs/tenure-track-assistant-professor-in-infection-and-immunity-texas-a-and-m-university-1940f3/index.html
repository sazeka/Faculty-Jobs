Back to Listings Tenure-Track: Assistant Professor in Infection and Immunity Veterinary Pathobiology Location College Station Open Date 7/21/2026 Position ID 186646 Description The Department of Veterinary Pathobiology (VTPB) in the College of Veterinary Medicine & Biomedical Sciences (VMBS) (http://vetmed.tamu.edu/) at Texas A&M University (TAMU) invites applications from researchers in infectious disease and

immunity for one (1) full-time, 9-month appointment faculty position with an expected start date of August 1, 2027 as part of a cluster hire initiative for Translational Research in Infection and Immunity. The selected candidate will be expected to adhere to the College’s code of professional conduct. A competitive start-up package will be provided to the successful applicant. This position includes an effort

distribution of 70% research and scholarship, 20% teaching, and 10% service. The successful candidate will be expected to develop and sustain a vigorous, externally funded research program and to contribute to collaborative, interdisciplinary scholarship. Research areas of interest include, but are not limited to, emerging, re-emerging, and zoonotic pathogens—including viral, bacterial, fungal, and parasitic

agents—as well as host immunity to infection and immune modulation to advance health. Candidates whose work incorporates interdisciplinary perspectives and collaborative approaches are especially encouraged to apply. Teaching responsibilities will primarily include courses in the Biomedical Sciences (BIMS) Undergraduate and Graduate Programs, with opportunities for qualified candidates to contribute to the

professional DVM curriculum. The BIMS Graduate Program offers a rich environment for graduate training, mentorship, and student recruitment, and currently enrolls 124 M.S. and 133 Ph.D. students. In addition, the Department of Veterinary Pathobiology is home to an NIH-funded T32 training program that supports veterinarians pursuing advanced research training in biomedical sciences. Service responsibilities include

contributions to the Department, College, and University, with additional encouragement to engage in professional organizations and community service. Salary will be commensurate with qualifications and experience. TAMU offers a comprehensive benefits package that includes multiple options for life and health insurance, retirement plans, and sick leave.
