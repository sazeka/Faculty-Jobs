Position summary

Job Description The Opportunity The Applied Aviation Sciences Department of the College of Aviation at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU) in Daytona Beach, FL invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Meteorology position. This role requires a candidate with funded-research and teaching experience in dynamic/synoptic meteorology. In addition to expectations of pursuing funded research,

the role offers the opportunity to teach undergraduate dynamic/synoptic meteorology courses directly supporting the B.S. in Meteorology program as well as the opportunity to teach additional upper- and lower-level meteorology courses supporting other programs within the College of Aviation. Basic teaching requirements are nine undergraduate credit hours (three courses) per semester with opportunities for course

reductions with significant funded research. Faculty members will also be expected to provide modest service to the University and the meteorology profession through a variety of opportunities (e.g., committee work, faculty senate, student organization advisement, outreach, professional society support) About us For over 100 years, Embry-Riddle has been the world's leading aviation and aerospace university, educating

more than 30,000 students across residential campuses in Daytona Beach, Florida, and Prescott, Arizona, as well as through a global network of Worldwide locations and online programs. With more than 165,000 alumni around the world, our impact reaches every corner of the aerospace, aviation, defense and technology industries. Today, our faculty, researchers and professional staff are advancing innovation across

aviation, space, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, autonomous systems and next-generation engineering. Working alongside industry leaders, government agencies and innovators, they are solving real-world challenges and shaping the technologies that will define tomorrow. Whether you are an educator, researcher, administrator, technologist or industry professional, your work at Embry-Riddle will contribute to
