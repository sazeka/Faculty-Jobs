Teaching Instructor/Assistant Professor, Apparel Design & Tech AF7738 495456 Stillwater EDUCATION & HUMAN SCIENCES (STW) Faculty Master's degree Career-track Instructor or Teaching Assistant Professor in Apparel Design and Technology Department of Design and Merchandising Start Date: August 2027 POSITION: The Department of Design and Merchandising (DM) in the College of Education and Human Sciences at Oklahoma State

University welcomes applications for a full-time, nine-month, Career-track Instructor or Teaching Assistant Professor position, 95% Teaching (21-24 credit hours over the academic year) and 5% Service, in Apparel Design and Technology beginning August 2027. The Apparel Design and Technology program prepares students for careers in apparel design, product development, technical design, 3D digital product creation, and

apparel production. We seek a highly engaged educator who is deeply committed to undergraduate teaching, student mentoring and professional development, and service to the program, department, university, and profession. RESPONSIBILITIES: The successful candidate will teach undergraduate courses in apparel design and production. Teaching responsibilities may include one or more of the following: apparel construction,

advanced apparel design, technical design, apparel product development and production, and 3D digital product creation, depending on program needs and the candidate’s areas of expertise. The candidate is expected to demonstrate familiarity with apparel production equipment and digital design technologies, provide basic troubleshooting support, and promote safe and effective use of laboratory equipment and software.

The candidate will contribute to maintaining contemporary, industry-relevant curriculum and integrating emerging technologies and industry-standard practices into teaching and student learning. The candidate is expected to maintain professional currency through ongoing professional development, including participation in professional organizations, workshops, industry activities, and training in emerging apparel

design technologies and practices. The candidate will mentor students in developing professional portfolios, preparing for internships and employment, participating in design competitions and exhibitions, and building connections with apparel industry professionals.
