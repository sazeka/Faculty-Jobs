joining our team of dedicated faculty, you will be invited to participate in innovative research opportunities and scholarly activities and will contribute to the high-quality, innovative education and training of students and pediatric and psychiatry residents and fellows in a diverse clinical learning environment that will ultimately improve the health of the children in Southern Nevada.Profile of the Kirk
