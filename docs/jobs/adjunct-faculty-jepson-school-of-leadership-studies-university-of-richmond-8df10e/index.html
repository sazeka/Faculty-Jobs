Adjunct Faculty - Jepson School of Leadership Studies This temporary posting is for the sole purpose of receiving applications for adjunct faculty already identified by University of Richmond’s Jepson School of Leadership. We are not currently recruiting for these roles and you should only apply if you have been specifically instructed to by either University of Richmond’s Human Resource Department or

the Jepson School of Leadership . Requirements for the position: Ph.D. strongly preferred; prior teaching experience at the undergraduate level; knowledge of subject matter; and ability to communicate effectively with undergraduate students required. Adjunct faculty are expected to meet classes as scheduled, strive to improve teaching effectiveness, take a sympathetic interest in the progress and development of each

student, keep accurate records of the academic standing of each student, and hand in promptly all reports of grades and other information required by the deans, the Registrar, the Provost, or the President. The Law School also cultivates a genuinely collegial community marked by engagement, civility, inclusivity, and concern for others and the greater good.
