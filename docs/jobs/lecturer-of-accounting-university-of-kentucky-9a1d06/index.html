accredited institution Required Related Experience n/a Required License/Registration/Certification n/a Job Summary The Von Allmen School of Accountancy at the University of Kentucky in Lexington, KY invites applications for multiple Lecturer of Accountancy positions with a possible start date of January 1, 2027 or August 1, 2027. The selected candidates will be appointed to a full-time 9-month, non-tenure eligible

faculty position for an initial two-year period that has the potential for renewal and for promotion to Senior Lecturer after five years. Applicants must have a master’s degree in accounting or a related field from an accredited institution. Professional licensure/certification (e.g., CPA, CMA, CFA), significant accounting-related work experience, a doctoral degree (e.g., PhD, DBA), and prior teaching experience are

preferred qualifications. The primary job responsibilities include teaching up to four courses per semester in accounting and fulfilling the service needs related to the various programs in the school. While applications from all teaching areas will be considered, preference will be given to candidates with the ability to teach both introductory and advanced courses. The Director will determine the number of courses

and other assigned duties based on the candidate’s experience and areas of expertise. The anticipated initial Distribution of Effort will be 90% teaching and 10% service. Applications must include the following: a letter of interest (upload under cover letter) and a curriculum vitae. In addition, please provide the names and contact information for at least three references when prompted in the application. This

information may be utilized to solicit recommendation letters from your references within the employment system. Applications will be evaluated on a rolling basis and will be accepted until the positions are filled. For additional information about this search, please contact Stephen Lusch (stephen.lusch@uky.edu), Interim Director of the Von Allmen School of Accountancy.Salary is competitive and will be commensurate
