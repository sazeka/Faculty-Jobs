Examples of Duties and Responsibilities Helena College University of Montana, a comprehensive two-year college, supports our diverse community by providing the paths and tools necessary to assist learners in

The Aviation Maintenance program is seeking qualified applicants who wish to become part of this dynamic and creative team to serve our students as a part-time instructor for the 2026-2027 academic year. This is a temporary part-time, non-benefited position. Aviation Maintenance classes are scheduled Monday—Friday from 7:00am-1:00pm. Curriculum covers the Airman

Certification Standards outlined in the FAA CFR 147, including classroom lectures and written tests as well as hands-on learning in a lab setting. Adjunct instructor pay is $850 per class credit and each class is 6 or 7 credits. Minimum Qualifications Airframe and Powerplant Certification.Five (5) years of aviation-related work experience.Effective oral and communication skills. Additional Information Please email

your resume, with a cover letter, college transcripts (if applicable), and copy of current A&P certification, to Stephanie Hunthausen, Executive Director of Career Technical Education & Dual Enrollment, stephanie.hunthausen@helenacollege.edu.
