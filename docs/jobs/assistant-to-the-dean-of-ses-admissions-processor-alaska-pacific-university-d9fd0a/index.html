Employment < All Careers Assistant to the Dean of SES/Admissions Processor Date Posted: July 30, 2026 This position is responsible for supporting the Dean of Student Enrollment Services in the daily operational functions for Alaska Pacific University REPRESENTATIVE DUTIES Department Operations and Management Ensures integrity of Admissions processes and workflows, including established communications plans and

internal/interdepartmental processes for new students. Supports the promotion and implementation of departmental policies and procedures for the equitable and consistent administration of the University’s academic policies and procedures. Supervises Student Enrollment Services Data Specialist and Front Desk Coordinator Hires, trains, and supervises student workers. Assists Admission Counselors/Staff Assistants in

operational areas. Works directly and collaboratively with the Student Enrollment Service Team (Admissions, Registrar’s Office, Financial Aid Office), Dean of Students, Campus Life, and the APU faculty to coordinate and expedite the process of admission and matriculation. Acts as primary resource for collecting and disseminating institutional data pertinent to the recruitment effort, including data sets and reports

for the Dean of Student Enrollment Services. Provides and promotes the use of regular reporting and data analysis for recruitment, retention, and resources management. Oversees the department’s use of all SIS systems and the processing of prospective and student information data, ensuring that data entered into the system are accurate and timely for use by various departments across the university. Serves as backup

budget officer and provides other management support in absence of the Dean Outreach and Recruitment Assists the Dean in developing strategic recruitment plans, including outreach throughout Alaska, nationally, and internationally and with Tribal organizations and Indigenous communities. Plans and implements recruitment and school/organization partner events in collaboration with Dean of Student Enrollment Services.

Coordinates implementation of the communication plan for all prospects, applicants, and admitted students; collaborates with Dean of Student Enrollment Services to determine new communication sequences for new developing markets. Counsels freshmen, undergraduate transfer, graduate & international applicants regarding the admission process, department requirements, deadlines, processing, financial aid, and housing
