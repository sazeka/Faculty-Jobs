About this Position The Michael and Anne Greenwood School of Music (GSM) seeks applications for four adjunct positions of Visiting Artist in Residence – Collaborative Piano with a start date of August 2026. Responsibilities: Oklahoma State University invites applications for Collaborative Pianists in the Greenwood School of Music. This is a nine-month adjunct

position (0.5 FTE) with a base salary to provide collaborative services for degree and faculty recitals and ensembles. Positions are available with focus areas in vocal/opera coaching and/or instrumental collaboration. Additional duties may include accompanying instrumental and vocal lessons, student juries, GSM auditions, masterclasses, and search candidate recitals, as well as other collaborative engagements within

the Greenwood School of Music. This appointment is eligible for renewal. Workload expectation is 20 contact hours per week. The Greenwood School of Music maintains an active performance environment with numerous recitals, ensemble performances, and guest artist events throughout the academic year. Collaborative pianists are valued members of the performance and instructional community and work closely with faculty

and students in a collaborative, artist-centered environment. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a dynamic and evolving program while supporting the development of emerging musicians. Please note that this position does not include benefits.
