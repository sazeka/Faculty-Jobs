Dean of the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences Posted On: November 16, 2026 Hours: Full Time Description: The Cooper Union for the Advancement of Science and Art seeks outstanding applicants for the position of Dean of the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences (HSS) to begin in August 2027. Located in the East Village, Cooper Union has played an important role in higher education since its founding by

Peter Cooper in 1859, providing education without tuition to all qualified students. Today, the college rigorously selects students of outstanding potential, while sustaining Peter Cooper’s radical commitment to diversity and to his foundational belief that fair access to a free education is necessary for a more just world. The Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences is the intellectual common ground of the Cooper

Union. Students earn their degrees in the Irwin S. Chanin School of Architecture, the School of Art, or the Albert Nerken School of Engineering. HSS gives Cooper Union’s architects, artists, and engineers a shared foundation in reading, writing, research, and the ethical, social, and civic questions that shape their professional work. Every student completes the four-semester HSS core as well as electives; many go on

to pursue HSS minors. The Dean leads a faculty of full- and part-time scholars and teachers and oversees the core curriculum, electives, and minors. The Dean also oversees the Center for Writing and Learning, which supports students and faculty across all disciplines, and the Cooper Union Library, including its Archives and Special Collections. The Dean is both the steward of a distinguished faculty and a leader of

the academic infrastructure that shapes every Cooper Union student’s education. This important administrative position requires vision and leadership in the areas of pedagogy and curriculum while addressing urgent ethical, cultural, and environmental issues. It also requires supporting and championing the centrality of humanistic inquiry in the development of all Cooper students and being the public face of HSS

within Cooper Union. The Dean leads a small front office as well as a team of full- and part-time faculty. Full-time and part-time faculty are unionized, as is the staff of the Cooper Union; teaching is the college’s highest priority.
