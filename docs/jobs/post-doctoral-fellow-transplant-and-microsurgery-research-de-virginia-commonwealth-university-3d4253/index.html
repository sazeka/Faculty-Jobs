This shared position will play a key role in advancing translational research focused liver transplantation, advanced microsurgical techniques, and transplant-related outcomes. The postdoctoral fellow will provide advanced training and research experience in experimental transplantation models, operative technique development,

laboratory investigation, and scientific dissemination. The primary responsibilities will be transplant-related research, ex vivo organ perfusion research, data collection and analysis, and supervised training and participation in organ perfusion pump-related activities. The Postdoctoral Fellow will work collaboratively with transplant surgeons, faculty investigators, research personnel, and other members of the

multidisciplinary research team to advance the Division's research objectives and contribute to the development of innovative approaches to transplantation science. The ideal candidate will demonstrate strong organizational and communication skills, the ability to work independently across two research teams, and a commitment to scientific rigor and collaboration. Prior experience in an academic or biomedical

research setting is highly preferred.
