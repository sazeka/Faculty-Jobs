The successful candidate will be an

accomplished scholar, an experienced academic administrator, and an exceptional relationship builder who leads with integrity, humility, and purpose. This individual will understand the evolving landscape of higher education and business education while demonstrating the judgment, curiosity, and courage necessary to guide the College through future opportunities and challenges. The next dean will be expected to

foster a culture of collaboration, innovation, accountability, and continuous improvement while serving as both the chief advocate for the College of Business and a trusted institutional leader Additional Information The dean leads in the context of shared governance, working collaboratively with faculty, staff, students, alumni, employers, corporate partners, community leaders, and University colleagues to shape the

future of business education. The successful dean will balance academic excellence with operational effectiveness, entrepreneurial thinking with thoughtful stewardship, and innovation with a deep respect for the college’s traditions and values. While the dean serves as the chief advocate for the College of Business, Illinois State also expects its deans to serve as institutional leaders. The next dean will contribute

to university-wide initiatives, collaborate across colleges and divisions, and help strengthen Illinois State as a whole. Opportunities for collaboration include partnerships with the new College of Engineering, the Katie School of Insurance and Risk Management and its connections to actuarial science, and the School of Information Technology in the College of Applied Science and Technology. These partnerships offer

opportunities to advance interdisciplinary initiatives in areas such as data analytics, artificial intelligence, risk management, and innovation. The University is not seeking a leader who simply manages an excellent college. We seek a leader who will elevate it.
