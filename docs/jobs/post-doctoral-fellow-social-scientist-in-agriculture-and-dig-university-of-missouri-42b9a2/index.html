This postdoc will play a key role in a NIFA-AFRI Social Implications of Digital Technologies funded grant focused on the socio-economic impact and adoption behavior of virtual fencing technologies for livestock in Missouri and Colorado (Note: the postdoc will be based in Columbia, Missouri)This position offers a unique opportunity to contribute to a farmer-focused applied research project on an interdisciplinary team

and contribute to other Center for Regenerative Agriculture research and extension projects. You will work under the supervision of the PI, Dr. Kelly Wilson, Assistant Professor in School of Natural Resources, and the Associate Director of the Center for Regenerative Agriculture. Key duties and responsibilitiesCo-lead design and implementation of producer surveys, including survey development, administration, and

monitoring response rates to reduce nonresponse. Manage, clean, and analyze quantitative datasets using reproducible workflows, including statistical analyses. Provide quantitative methodological expertise to the research team, including advising on survey measurement, selection of statistical analytical approaches, and interpretation of findingsApply social science theoretical frameworks (e.g., diffusion of

innovations, technology acceptance, behavioral models) to research design, measurement, and interpretationIntegrate survey findings with qualitative data from farmer interviews in a mixed methods designSupport qualitative data collection and analysis with farmers and farm partners as neededLiaise with technology development collaborators including virtual fencing companies and project partners. Lead or co-lead
