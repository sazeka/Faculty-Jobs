Job Description POSITION DESCRIPTION: The Feliciano School of Business at Montclair State University invites applications for a two-year renewable, full-time non-tenure-track Business Statistics faculty position starting September 1, 2027. Ideal candidates for this position will have both professional experience in industry and teaching experience at the university level.

Preference for this position will be given to candidates who can demonstrate teaching/professional experience in Business Statistics. The candidate selected will be expected to teach courses at undergraduate and graduate levels in both face-to-face and online delivery modes. Position is a two-year, ten-month, non-tenure track appointment, with eligibility for reappointment based on established NTTP procedures.

Teaching Faculty members are expected to contribute to the University's mission through excellence in teaching and service. Teaching: The standard annual teaching load for 10-month teaching faculty is 12 teaching credit hours (TCH) a semester, with an additional 6 TCH in the summer for 12-month faculty. Reassigned time (course release) is available for faculty with additional responsibilities such as administering

programs. Teaching Faculty are expected to center student success and measurable learning outcomes through clear course design, inclusive pedagogies, and the integration of emerging disciplinary and pedagogical developments.
