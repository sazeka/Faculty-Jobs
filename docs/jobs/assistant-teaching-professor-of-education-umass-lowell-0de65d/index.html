Leadership in

Schooling program. The successful candidate will contribute to the preparation and professional development of educational leaders through high-quality teaching, doctoral mentoring, program development, and service. Depending on program needs and the candidate’s expertise, the successful candidate may also contribute to teaching and other instructional activities in the School of Education’s undergraduate Bachelor of

Education (BAEd) program. The Ed.D. is a practitioner-based program designed to support the ongoing professional needs of educational leaders in PK–12 contexts. The program is anchored in the belief that educational leaders can make a substantial impact on students, families, schools, and communities by integrating professional knowledge, practical experience, research evidence, and systematic inquiry. The School of

Education at the University of Massachusetts Lowell prepares educators, educational leaders, and researchers through undergraduate, master’s, doctoral, and professional learning programs. The School of Education faculty are engaged in teaching, scholarship, program development, and partnerships with schools and communities. Faculty collaborate to prepare educators and educational leaders who can use research,

evidence, professional knowledge, and reflective practice to improve educational opportunities and outcomes. The School values collaboration, excellence, inclusion, service, and strong partnerships with educational communities.
