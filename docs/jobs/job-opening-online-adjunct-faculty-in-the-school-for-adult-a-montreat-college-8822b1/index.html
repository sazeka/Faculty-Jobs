Job Opening: Online Adjunct Faculty in the School for Adult and Graduate Studies Location: Remote Summary Adjunct faculty teaching opportunities are available for undergraduate and graduate online programs. The disciplines with the greatest need include: Clinical Mental Health Counseling Marriage and Family Therapy Speech Language Pathology Statistics Biology *Please note that we are not accepting applications for

Business adjunct teaching at the time. Successful candidate will affirm and support the Mission, Vision, and Statement of Faith of Montreat College. Education Applicants must hold an earned doctorate in the field to teach in a master’s level program. A minimum of a master’s degree in the field is required to teach undergraduate courses. Online teaching experience is preferred. Supervision Associate Dean of Adult and

Graduate Studies Application Requirements/How to Apply Candidates are requested to send the following materials to the Office of the Provost: Complete, Online Adjunct Application Faculty selecting Provost’s Office from the supervisor drop-down Letter of Intent Résumé or Curriculum Vita Copies of Institutional Transcripts Statement of Personal Faith Statement of Christian Teaching Philosophy Two Professional

References About Montreat College Montreat College is a Christian liberal arts college accredited by the Commission on Colleges of the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools to offer master’s, bachelor’s, and associate degrees. The main campus is located in the Blue Ridge Mountains fifteen miles east of Asheville, North Carolina, a region recognized as one of the most attractive living environments in the

United States. The College is committed to Christ-centered teaching and learning and is a member of the Council for Christian Colleges & Universities.
