POSITION SPECIFICS We are seeking a Department Head for

the Engineering and Laboratory Services Department of the Applied Research Laboratory (ARL) at Penn State. The Engineering and Laboratory Services Department, located in Warminster, PA, focuses on 1) Supporting other departments in the research, design, development and transition of the electrical, mechanical, and software components and systems utilized in navigation applications, and 2) Assisting with laboratory

and in-field testing. This position may be filled as either a Research and Development Engineer or a non-tenure Research Faculty. You will: Lead a multi-disciplinary, diverse staff in the development, integration, test of high performance navigation systems and sensors Plan, staff, and execute programs to ensure all departmental staff are appropriately and sufficiently tasked at all times Recruit, retain, and define

performance expectations of department staff Develop, maintain, and utilize department staff and resources Mentorship, professional, and career development of department staff Evaluate professional growth through performance reviews Foster an environment of collaboration across the department, division, office, and ARL Support the execution of the department programs Preparation and execution of department budgets

Proposal and project development Support strategic planning initiatives Interact and collaborate regularly with program management, program sponsors and stakeholders Perform engineering work related to navigation integration and test Faculty rank will be commensurate with education and experience. Minimally requires a Master's degree in an engineering or science discipline and 1 year related experience. Required
