Position Details Title Postdoctoral Fellowship in Political Science School Faculty of Arts and Sciences Department/Area Postdocs - Social Science Position Description The Davis Center Postdoctoral Fellowship program offers comprehensive research, training and professional development opportunities for scholars advancing their careers in political science. Fellows pursue their research with support from

an interdisciplinary community of experts and with access to world-class resources. The program provides scholars with experiences and connections that endure well beyond their fellowship year.About this position:• 9-month, full time, in-residence fellowship (September 1, 2027– May 31, 2028)• Stipend of at least $60,000 for 9 months (based on an annual stipend of at least $80,000 )• Additional research funding of at

least $5,000• Eligible for employee benefits, including subsidized health insurance• The fellow may be invited to teach a Harvard class during the course of their appointment• Citizens of all countries may applyThe Davis Center will award one Postdoctoral Fellowship in Political Science. Research proposals must be on topics related to political science in Russia or Eurasia. The Davis Center defines “Eurasia” as

consisting of the following regions: the Baltics, Eastern Europe, Southeastern Europe, the Caucasus, and Central Asia. Basic Qualifications This position is for junior scholars who will have completed the Ph.D. or equivalent by September 2027, but no earlier than September 2022.Citizens of all countries may apply. Additional Qualifications The Postdoctoral Fellowship is a 9-month, in-residence fellowship.

Postdoctoral Fellows are in residence full-time at the Davis Center from September 1, 2027 – May 31, 2028. Special Instructions Applications must be submitted no later than January 6, 2027.Letters of recommendation (minimum of 2, maximum of 3) are due no later than 11:59 p.m. EST on January 13, 2027. Late submissions will not be considered. More details about eligibility, benefits, expectations, and application
