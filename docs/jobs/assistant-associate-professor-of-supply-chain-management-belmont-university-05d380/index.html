that will be given preference for this position is Supply Chain Management. Applicants will be expected to have expertise in an SCM-related function (e.g., logistics, operations management, planning, purchasing, and strategy) and demonstrate a proven ability to engage in an environment that emphasizes cross-functional interaction and integration, both within and outside traditional supply chain management areas.

The candidate will be expected to teach in undergraduate and graduate courses offered in the College. high-quality, peer-reviewed journals in supply chain management. This position includes an external-facing role that requires the ability to effectively build partnerships with industry. Additional responsibilities include academic advising and service to the college and university. A terminal degree in Supply Chain Management or a related field from an accredited university is required. ABD candidates will also be

considered.
