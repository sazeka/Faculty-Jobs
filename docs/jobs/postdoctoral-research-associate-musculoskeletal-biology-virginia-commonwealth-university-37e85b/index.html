Position Responsibilities: The Biomedical Engineering Department at Virginia Commonwealth University invites applications from qualified candidates for post-doctoral positions working in the laboratory of Henry J. Donahue, Ph.D. Candidates

should have a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering, Biomedical Sciences, Mechanical Engineering or a related discipline. Our laboratory examines, using both in vitro and in vivo models, the cellular and molecular mechanism by which musculoskeletal tissues adapt to the mechanical environment, the role of genetic variability in this process and the effect of metabolic disease on this process. We are most interested in

candidates with experience in one or more of the following areas: cell and molecular biology, in vitro and in vivo imaging, especially micro CT, transgenic animal models, and bioinformatics. Salaries for these post-doctoral positions will be at the NIH scale.
