Undergraduate Nursing FacultyThe University of Mary in Bismarck, ND invites applications for a full-time ongoing faculty appointment for the onsite baccalaureate program. The successful candidate will have a strong ability and desire to teach with established nursing expertise and current practice experience. Primary responsibilities include classroom and lab/clinical instruction. Salary is commensurate with

qualifications, experience, and rank.
