Position summary

Employment Opportunity Detail Adjunct Foundation Studio Instructors The Department of Art is seeking adjunct instructors to teach Foundation Studio courses. Potential teaching assignments include Introduction to Studio Art (non-major), Introduction to Art History (non-major), 2D Design, and all levels of Drawing. Requirements: Applicants should have at least a graduate degree (MA or MFA) in Studio Art or a closely

related field. Teaching and/or professional studio experience is preferred. The classes are typically held in-person with schedules based on applicant availability and department needs. Application Process: Please complete the application process and submit a current C.V via https://www.evansville.edu/offices/hr/employment-application-form-faculty.cfm This is an open application for future semesters and program

needs. Contact Information: If you have any questions, please contact Todd Matteson at tm8@evansville.edu. Information about the department and our course offerings can be found at https://www.evansville.edu/majors/art/index.cfm Return to the list of Employment Opportunities Office Phone 812-488-2943 Office Email hr@evansville.edu Office Location Room 118, Olmsted Administration Hall
