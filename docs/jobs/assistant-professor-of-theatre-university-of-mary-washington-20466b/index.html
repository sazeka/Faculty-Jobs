Job Summary/Basic Functions The Department of Theatre and Dance at the University of Mary Washington is now accepting applications for a full time, tenure track position of Assistant Professor of Scenic Design beginning August of 2027. The successful candidate will contribute to the department’s dynamic production program through scenic design, technical direction, and construction of departmental

productions in conjunction and collaboration with fellow faculty designers and directors, students, guest artists, and support staff. Additionally, the successful candidate will be expected to contribute to the department through curriculum development, effective teaching, recruitment, advising, student mentorship, and service to the university, college, and department. MFA in scenic design, or equivalent

professional training and experience is required. Facility with Vectorworks or AutoCAD is required. Maintaining understanding in contemporary professional standards and practices is expected. Candidates must be committed to excellence in undergraduate teaching at the introductory level, both within the discipline and general education curriculum, as well as in upper-level design and technology classes. The Department

is particularly interested in candidates who recognize the unique opportunities of a design position in a liberal arts environment and who are excited to contribute to a vibrant program with strong production values. Candidates should be able to demonstrate a high level of dedication to effective teaching and high-impact learning in an inclusive environment that embraces diverse talents and backgrounds. The

successful candidate can look forward to joining a department recognized by the Princeton Review as one of the nation’s best with a growing number of majors and a vibrant, respected production program, and which is building a new multi-million dollar, state-of-the-art theatre facility.
