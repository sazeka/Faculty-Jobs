Details CLINICAL SCIENTIST SPECIALIZING IN DISSEMINATION & IMPLEMENTATION SCIENCE. The Department of Psychology at the University of Utah invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor faculty position in our Clinical Science program. We are seeking applicants with research and teaching interests in Dissemination and Implementation (D&I) Science.We are particularly interested in candidates with expertise

in implementation science approaches that seek to improve access to evidence-based behavioral health interventions, reduce health disparities, and strengthen healthcare systems. Examples of research emphases that would fit the position include the development, dissemination, implementation, adaptation, scaling-up, or sustainment of evidence-based psychological and behavioral interventions across clinical, healthcare,

educational, community, and/or public health settings. We particularly welcome candidates whose work also focuses on rural, frontier, youth, and/or underserved communities. Applicants with a strong research record, high-quality clinical training from a clinical science perspective, and a commitment to community-engaged translational research are especially encouraged to apply. License eligibility, skills in

quantitative methods, and experience securing or demonstrated potential for external funding are highly valued. The successful candidate will be expected to develop and maintain an active research program, contribute to graduate and undergraduate teaching, and help develop curriculum, training opportunities, and community partnerships that support community-engaged research.
