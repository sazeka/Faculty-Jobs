Human Neuroscience at the University of Utah The Department of Psychology at the University of Utah invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant Professor position in Human Neuroscience. We seek an outstanding scientist with a strong, theory-driven research program that advances our understanding of the human brain through innovative neuroscience research. We welcome applications from researchers across all

areas of human neuroscience whose work addresses fundamental questions about brain function, cognition, development, learning, health, or disease. We are particularly interested in candidates who combine important theoretical questions with innovative computational, quantitative, or methodological approaches. Applicants employing computational modeling, advanced statistical and quantitative methods, multimodal

neuroimaging, machine learning, artificial intelligence, or related approaches are encouraged to apply, particularly where these methods provide new insights into human brain function and behavior. The Department seeks to expand its research strengths at the intersection of human neuroscience, developmental science, and computational approaches. We therefore welcome applicants whose research addresses questions

related to brain development across the lifespan, learning, adaptation, brain health, resilience, translational neuroscience, or related areas. We are especially excited by scholars whose work integrates innovative computational or quantitative methods with important questions in human neuroscience and who will help strengthen connections among the department’s existing expertise in human cognitive neuroscience,
