Position summary

The Center for Advanced Research Technologies (CART) at the University of Rochester Medical Center (URMC) seeks an experienced and highly motivated Technical Director to lead the operations of the Cryo-Electron Microscopy (Cryo-EM) Resource Center. This individual will provide scientific, technical, and operational leadership for a growing cryo-EM facility that supports a vibrant research community. &nbsp; The

Cryo-EM Resource Center is in the process of installing a Thermo Fisher Scientific Glacios 3 cryo-TEM, equipped with a Selectris energy filter and Falcon 4i direct electron detector. The Technical Director will play a central role in overseeing instrument commissioning and maintenance, method development, user training, and day-to-day operational management. The successful candidate must be highly responsible,

reliable, and technically oriented, with excellent interpersonal skills. &nbsp; The Technical Director will have the opportunity to shape the direction of a growing cryo-EM program and play a key role in advancing cutting-edge research. This role offers a supportive, collaborative environment and excellent opportunities for career advancement. &nbsp; Key Responsibilities &nbsp; Oversee the installation, calibration,

commissioning, and ongoing optimization of the Glacios cryo-EM platform with Selectris energy filter and Falcon 4i detector, along with additional planned instrumentation acquisitions. Develop, validate, and maintain cryo-EM workflows for sample preparation, data acquisition, and data quality assessment. Collaborate effectively with the Director of CART, the Scientific Director of the Cryo-EM Resource Center, and

adjacent CART core facility leaders. Provide expert consultation to investigators on experimental design, sample suitability, data collection strategies, and optimization for cryo-EM and cryo‑ET applications.
