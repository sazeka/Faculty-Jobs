Position summary

Location St. Louis, MO 63110&#xa;&#xa; Scheduled Hours 40 Position Summary The Chief Quality and Safety Officer is the principal physician executive for quality, patient safety, high reliability, and performance improvement at Barnes Jewish Hospital and WashU Medicine. Working in close partnership with the Barnes-Jewish Hospital President and executive team, BJC HealthCare leaders, and WashU Medicine clinical and

academic leadership, the CQO develops and executes a unified quality and safety agenda that advances safe, equitable, evidence-based, and patient-centered care. The CQO will translate the strengths of the WashU Medicine and Barnes-Jewish Hospital partnership into measurable improvement. This includes creating shared priorities, clarifying joint accountability, accelerating adoption of best practices, and ensuring

that quality and safety initiatives are integrated into clinical operations, faculty practice, education, research, and strategic planning. The role has dual reporting relationships to the BJH President and the WashU Medicine Vice Chancellor for Clinical Affairs. Job Description Primary Duties & Responsibilities: Barnes-Jewish Hospital Serve as the senior WashU Medicine physician partner to Barnes-Jewish Hospital

executive, clinical, nursing, and operational leaders for quality, safety, and high-reliability performance. Co-develop and execute a single, integrated quality and patient safety strategy for Barnes-Jewish Hospital in partnership with BJC HealthCare and WashU Medicine leadership. Establish shared priorities, governance, decision rights, and accountability across Barnes-Jewish Hospital and WashU Medicine to ensure

coordinated execution and sustained results. Serve as a primary liaison to Barnes-Jewish Hospital quality and safety committees, medical staff leadership, and executive governance forums, promoting transparency, timely escalation, and alignment. Build strong working relationships with department chairs, division chiefs, service line leaders, nursing leaders, and hospital operations to embed quality and safety into

daily clinical practice. Partner with Barnes-Jewish Hospital leadership in BJC system quality initiatives, helping translate system standards into local action and share successful practices across the system.
