description The Department of Theater and Dance at the University of California, Santa Barbara, invites applications for a qualified lecturer to teach Introduction to Stage Management during the 2026–2027 Academic Year. This undergraduate course explores the foundational duties and responsibilities of a stage manager, with instruction focused on practices across pre-production, rehearsals, performance runs, and

post-production processes. Areas of expertise may include production paperwork and organization, rehearsal and performance coordination, communication and leadership within creative teams, and show calling. Work will be in person on the UCSB campus. Reimbursement for travel and housing expenses will not be provided.
