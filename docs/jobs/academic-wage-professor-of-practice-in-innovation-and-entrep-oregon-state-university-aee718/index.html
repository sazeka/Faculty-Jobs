The Department of Chemistry is seeking a fixed-term, salaried Academic Wage: Professor of Practice in Innovation and Entrepreneurship. This is a part-time (.10 FTE), 12-month position. The PoP is expected to contribute to teaching and research in the Department of Chemistry and co-teach one graduate or combined

graduate/undergraduate level course in the foundations of innovations with applications to climate and energy ventures. This position supports OSU’s mission to foster innovation, collaboration, and societal impact while preparing students for leadership in the emerging climate and energy economy. The courses will serve both senior undergraduate and graduate students in Science, Engineering, Business and other

colleges at OSU that are interested in Innovation and Entrepreneurship. The PoP in the Department of Chemistry performs duties in professionally related instruction and service with expectations in scholarly activity.
