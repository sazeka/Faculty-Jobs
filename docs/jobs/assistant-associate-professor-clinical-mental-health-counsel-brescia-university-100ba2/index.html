Position summary

Brescia University Our Mission: We are a Catholic Ursuline university forming compassionate, critical thinkers through a holistic and deeply personal education. Assistant or Associate Professor for the Master’s of Science in the Clinical Mental Health Counseling program Brescia University seeks full-time faculty members whose teaching philosophy aligns with our mission and who are passionate about high-quality

teaching, student mentorship, and inclusive learning environments. We are hiring core faculty at the Assistant or Associate Professor level for the Master’s of Science in Clinical Mental Health Counseling program for June 2027. This is a full time, tenure track position required to teach 18 credit hours over the academic year, including summer courses. Remote candidates will be considered. Experience and Education

requirements: • Doctoral degree in counselor education preferably from a CACREP-accredited program; or • A related doctoral degree and have been employed as a full-time faculty member in a counselor education program for a minimum of one full academic year before July 1, 2013; or • Graduation from a rehabilitation doctoral program prior to January 1, 2018. • Demonstrated commitment to community engagement, academic

advising, and collegial service. Responsibilities are, but not limited to: • For remote candidates, traveling to campus to participate in residency teaching, program, and university meetings, and one commencement ceremony. Currently, we expect this to happen 1-3 times per academic year. • Teach 6 of the 8 CACREP core areas and 3 of the 9 CACREP concentration/specialty areas • Participate in student advising, student

counseling practicum supervision, curriculum development, and program assessment. • Serve on university committees and contribute to the life of the department and institution. • Uphold and advance Brescia’s mission to offer education of the whole person through a commitment to academic excellence, service, and values-based leadership. To apply, please submit the following electronically to HR@Brescia.edu • A cover

letter describing your teaching philosophy, disciplinary expertise, and alignment with Brescia’s mission • A current curriculum vitae • Unofficial graduate transcripts • Contact information for three professional references; one must be from a current/former supervisor or higher ed administrator familiar with your role as a professor BRESCIA UNIVERSITY IS AN EQUAL OPPORTUNITY EMPLOYER This employer participates in
