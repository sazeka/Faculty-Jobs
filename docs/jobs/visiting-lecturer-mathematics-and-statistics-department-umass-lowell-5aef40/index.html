Applications Open: Oct 28 2026 Applications Close: Open until filled General Summary of Position: The Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Massachusetts Lowell invites applications for a full-time, non-tenure-track faculty position at the rank of Visiting

Lecturer to start on September 1, 2027. The primary responsibility will be to provide high-quality teaching of undergraduate courses in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics. This position is renewable annually, for a maximum of four years. UMass Lowell is located about 25 miles northwest of Boston in the high-tech corridor of Massachusetts. As a Visiting Lecturer, the successful candidate will teach four

classes per semester (12 credits) at the undergraduate level. Courses taught by department faculty can be found at: https://www.uml.edu/Catalog/Undergraduate/Sciences/Departments/Mathematical-Sciences/Course-Listing.aspx Applicants may apply separately to both our Assistant Teaching Professor and Visiting Lecturer posts if they meet the requirements of each. Minimum Qualifications (Required): Earned doctoral or

master’s degree in mathematics, statistics, or another field that provides substantial preparation for teaching undergraduate mathematics or statistics. Evidence of successful teaching at the college or university level, including at least two years of teaching experience for doctoral degree holders or five years for master's degree holders. Relevant professional experience involving substantial use, communication,

or application of mathematics or statistics may be considered in lieu of a portion of the teaching experience requirement. Excellent written and oral communication skills. Additional Considerations: Demonstrated success teaching undergraduate mathematics or statistics courses, or equivalent evidence of the ability to communicate mathematical ideas effectively to diverse student populations.
