Description CCBC is the college of choice for over 50,000 students and 200 businesses each year — all with unique goals, strengths and requirements. By offering a holistic learning environment that is both accepting and challenging, we meet students where they are and take them where they want to go. We offer the region’s most expansive selection of degree, certificate and workplace certification programs at 3

campuses plus 3 convenient CCBC centers along with off-site community locations.The part time adjunct Senior Programs Instructor teaches college level non-credit courses to students 60+ that focus on art, computers, history, politics and events, humanities and culture, languages, and writing. These courses are opportunities for social and cultural enrichment and have no homework and testing requirements.Classes are

offered during the day at Catonsville, Dundalk, Essex, Owings Mills and Hunt Valley campuses along with senior centers and retirement communities throughout Baltimore County. Courses are offered primarily in-person along with some Zoom classes.Pay rates for non-credit courses in Continuing Education are commensurate with education. Applicants must provide a summary of what they would like to teach along with the area

of the county where they would be willing to work and preferred teaching modality. Minimum Requirements Instructors should have experience teaching and have an expert knowledge of the topic they are proposing to teach. Instructors will be required to complete all forms and documentation related to the course and all documentation required by the college. Must possess a High School diploma or GED.Academic degrees or

professional credentials in a related discipline preferred.Must be a subject matter expert.Computer proficiency preferred. Experience teaching or training in an adult setting preferred.
