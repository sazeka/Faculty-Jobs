The selected candidate will be appointed to a full-time 9-month, non-tenure eligible faculty position for an initial two-year period that has the potential for renewal and, for faculty hired at the rank of Lecturer, for promotion to Senior Lecturer after five years. Lecturers contribute to the institution’s mission and the vitality of its undergraduate program by providing

high-quality instruction and fulfilling department and college service obligations. The successful candidate will have a primary emphasis on teaching while also contributing meaningful service to the department, college, and university. Teaching responsibilities total 9 to 12 credit hours per semester (depending on class size). Candidates should have experience teaching, or willingness to teach, large-lecture

sections in one or more of the following: Microeconomic Principles, Macroeconomic Principles, and Business Statistics. Additional teaching may include smaller classes in the candidate’s field of interest as a lower or upper-division undergraduate course. Minimum qualifications are: Ph.D. in Economics at beginning of appointment, prior college/university teaching experience, and a demonstrated potential for quality

teaching, especially in a large lecture format. Evidence of experience or interest in teaching on-line and distance instruction is valued.
