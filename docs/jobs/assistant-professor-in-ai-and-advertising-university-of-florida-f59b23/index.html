Job Description: The Department of Advertising in the College of Journalism and Communications (CJC) at the University of Florida invites applications for a nine-month tenure-track Assistant Professor in AI and Advertising position to begin in Fall 2027. Candidates must have completed a Ph.D. in communication, mass communication,

advertising, marketing, or a related field by the time of appointment. We seek a scholar whose research makes a strong contribution to the field of advertising, with a focus on artificial intelligence and its implications for the industry, professional practice, and pedagogy. Relevant areas for research specialization include but are not limited to AI-mediated advertising and persuasion, human-AI interaction,

generative AI, algorithmic and automated communication, AI-driven social media and consumer insights, and the broader implications of emerging technologies for consumers, brands, media, and society. This faculty member will work in partnership with UF’s Consortium on Trust in Media and Technology, which is based at CJC. The Consortium connects scholars and thought leaders across disciplines to develop applications

supporting media and technology that is credible and equitable. The Consortium’s work is part of UF’s commitment to artificial intelligence as the centerpiece of a major, long-term initiative combining world-class research infrastructure, cutting-edge research, and a transformational approach to curriculum. The successful candidate also will have opportunities to collaborate across the College and University on

research related to artificial intelligence, emerging media technologies, consumer behavior, and strategic communication. The successful candidate will be expected to teach undergraduate courses in the advertising curriculum and established graduate courses approved by CJC’s Graduate Division.
