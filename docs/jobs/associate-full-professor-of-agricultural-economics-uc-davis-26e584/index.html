Position description The Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics in

the College of Agricultural and Environmental Sciences at the University of California, Davis is recruiting an Associate/Full Professor of Agricultural Economics. This is an academic year (9-month), tenured Associate/Full Professor position with a 20% Instruction and Research (I&R), and 80% Agricultural Experiment Station (AES) appointment. The appointee is expected to establish an outreach and research program in

agricultural economics, with a focus on the issues facing California’s farm economy. The appointee will bolster our applied research footprint across the state and help meet the needs of California's agricultural sector through applied research on agricultural production costs and returns, farm-level costs and impacts of regulation, and of management strategies that strengthen profitability and long-run economic

viability. The successful candidate is expected to oversee UC Davis ARE's world-renowned Cost and Return studies program (Cost and Return Studies). This will involve supervising research staff, engaging with funders and external stakeholders, as well as maintaining an extramural granting program to support core programmatic activities and methodological improvements. Teaching responsibilities will consist of one to

one-and-a-half quarter-long courses per year. The courses to be taught by the appointee will be set by the department chair based upon departmental needs and the successful candidate’s interests/expertise. The appointee’s primary teaching responsibilities are likely to include ARE 120 Agricultural Policy, ARE 138 International Commodity & Resource Markets, 139 Futures & Options Markets, and/or ARE 155 Operations
