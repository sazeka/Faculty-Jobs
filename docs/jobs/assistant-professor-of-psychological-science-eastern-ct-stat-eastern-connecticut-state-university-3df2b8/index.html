Position summary

Assistant Professor of Psychological Science Industrial-Organizational Psychology Department: Psychological Science Eastern Connecticut State University is seeking a qualified candidate to fill the Assistant Professor of Psychological Science in Industrial-Organizational Psychology position. Eastern is Connecticut’s Public Liberal Arts University and is located in Willimantic, Connecticut which is 1.5 hours from

Boston and 2.5 hours from New York City. Eastern is one of four universities in the Connecticut State College and University System (CSCU). The position also offers substantial health and retirement benefits. Qualifications: PhD in Industrial-Organizational Psychology, or related field (ABD considered but PhD needs to be in hand by August 2027). Position Description: The Department of Psychological Science is pleased

to announce a tenure-track Assistant Professor position beginning in August 2027. Eastern is Connecticut’s only public liberal arts university, and Psychology is among the largest majors on campus. This position requires teaching courses in our Industrial-Organizational Psychology Concentration (e.g., Industrial-Organizational Psychology, Groups & Teams, Leadership), the department's Research Methods sequence

(Behavioral Science Statistics, Research Methods I & II) as well as meeting other departmental needs (e.g., General Psychology, courses in the Eastern’s Liberal Arts Core, and courses in the candidate’s specialty area). This position will support the Physical Education program through our Groups & Teams course and our graduate program in HR Management. There is potential for the candidate to contribute to development

of stackable Graduate Certificate Programs in Management and related fields/specializations (e.g. Healthcare, HR).Candidates with research and practice interests involving AI implementation will be given preference, but candidates from all areas of I-O will be considered. Candidates should demonstrate potential for teaching excellence at the undergraduate level, especially with first-generation students. Those

willing to mentor students in their research are especially encouraged to apply.
