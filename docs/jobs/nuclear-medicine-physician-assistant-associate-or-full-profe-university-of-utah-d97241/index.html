Qualifications New Position to Begin 12/01/2026 Details The Nuclear Medicine Section in the Department of Radiology and Imaging Sciences at the University of Utah seeks a faculty member at the Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor rank to join our growing clinical and academic program. With new PET and theranostics suites under construction, this position offers an opportunity to help shape the next phase of

molecular imaging and targeted radionuclide therapy at an academic medical center. We welcome applicants committed to excellent patient care, education, and academic collaboration. Academic rank will be commensurate with qualifications and experience. Clinical PracticeFaculty responsibilities encompass a broad range of diagnostic and therapeutic nuclear medicine services, including: Supervising the administration of

standard-of-care lutetium-177, radium-223, and iodine-131 therapies, as well as investigational radionuclide therapies. Interpreting oncologic, neurologic, and cardiac PET/CT, general nuclear medicine examinations, and bone densitometry studies. Participating in multidisciplinary treatment planning conferences. Sharing after-hours home pager call responsibilities with other nuclear medicine faculty. The position is

based at the University’s academic health campus in Salt Lake City, Utah. Home workstations are available for selected remote diagnostic shifts and call responsibilities. Education and MentorshipFaculty play an active role in educating diagnostic radiology residents, nuclear radiology fellows, and rotating medical students.
