Visiting Assistant Professor in Environmental Studies

Loyola Marymount University  ·  Environmental Studies  ·  Westchester Campus, CA
VisitingEnvironmental Studies

Position summary

Who We Are The Department of Urban and Environmental Studies at Loyola Marymount University (LMU), a Carnegie-classified R2 institution in the mainstream of American Catholic higher education, seeks applicants for a one-semester (Spring 2027) Visiting Assistant Professor of Environmental Studies who value its mission and share its commitment to inclusive excellence and the building of a just society. LMU is federally

recognized as a Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) and an Asian American and Native American Pacific Islander-Serving Institution (AANAPISI) and is recognized by the State of California as a Black-Serving Institution (BSI). These designations reflect the university's continuing commitment to supporting student success, belonging, and thriving within a diverse academic community. As a Catholic, Jesuit, and Marymount

university, LMU values faculty who engage students as whole persons and pursue scholarly work that promotes justice, equity, and the Common Good. Appointment Type and Details This position is a one-semester (Spring 2027) full-time appointment with a 3-course teaching load. Applicants must hold a Ph.D. in environmental studies, geography or a closely related social science field by the position start date of January

2027. ABD applicants who anticipate completion of the doctorate by December 2026 will also be considered. Position Responsibilities The Department of Urban and Environmental Studies at Loyola Marymount University invites applications for a one-semester (Spring 2027) Visiting Assistant Professor position in Environmental Studies, beginning January 2027. The successful candidate will teach EVST 1000 Introduction to

Environmental Studies; EVST 3998 Race, Body, and Environment; and a First-Year Seminar (FFYS 1000) based on the candidate’s expertise. Our department values collaborative, interdisciplinary, and community-engaged approaches to teaching and research. We seek candidates who are excited to teach and mentor a diverse student body in Los Angeles and who will contribute to LMU's teacher-scholar model through excellent

undergraduate teaching. How To Apply The application deadline is September 30 . Questions regarding this position may be directed to Tyler Harlan, Chair, Department of Urban and Environmental Studies, at tyler.harlan@lmu.edu .
