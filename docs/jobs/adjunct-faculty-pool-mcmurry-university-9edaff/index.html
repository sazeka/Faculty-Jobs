Privacy PolicyJob OpeningsAdjunct Faculty PoolHuman Resources - Abilene, Texas (Hybrid)Position Summary McMurry University invites applications for adjunct faculty positions to support instructional needs across academic programs. This posting is used to build a pool of qualified candidates for part-time, temporary teaching assignments as they become available. Appointments are made on a course-by-course basis and

are dependent upon departmental needs, enrollment, and funding. Adjunct faculty members play a vital role in delivering high-quality instruction and supporting student success. Selected candidates may teach undergraduate and/or graduate courses in their area of expertise, either in-person, online, or hybrid formats. Key Areas of interest (but not limited to): Chemistry Business History Political Science Key

Responsibilities Teach assigned courses in alignment with approved curriculum and learning outcomes Prepare course materials, syllabi, and instructional content Engage students through effective teaching strategies and timely communication Assess student learning and provide meaningful feedback Maintain accurate records of attendance, grades, and course activities Adhere to university policies and academic standards

Minimum Qualifications Master’s degree in the teaching discipline or a related field (minimum 18 graduate hours in the discipline) Relevant professional or teaching experience Strong communication and organizational skills Preferred Qualifications Prior college-level teaching experience Experience with online or hybrid instruction Terminal degree in the discipline (where applicable) Application Requirements Completed

application Curriculum Vitae (CV) or resume Unofficial transcripts (official transcripts required upon hire) Additional Information This is a continuous posting used to establish a pool of qualified applicants Applications are reviewed on an as-needed basis Submission of an application does not guarantee employment Adjunct faculty are hired on a per-course, part-time basis Equal Employment Opportunity/Affirmative
