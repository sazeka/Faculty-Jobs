Clinical Instructor, Community & Behavioral Health TN, United States Be the First to Apply Job Description Job Summary The Clinical Instructor serves the Department of Community and Behavioral Health by providing high-quality instruction, student mentorship, and community-engaged learning experiences across undergraduate, master's, and doctoral public health programs. The position is a nine-month, teaching-focused

faculty appointment that supports undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral education while providing operational leadership for Project EARTH. Responsibilities include classroom and online instruction, dissertation committee service, community engagement activities, student mentoring, experiential learning initiatives, and support for student organizations and academic outreach efforts. The Clinical Instructor will

support recruitment and retention efforts, and enhance the department's capacity to meet increasing instructional and doctoral education demands. Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities Demonstrated ability to teach effectively in undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral-level public health programs. Knowledge of public health principles, program planning, evaluation, and community health practice. Ability to develop and

facilitate experiential and service-learning opportunities. Ability to teach in face-to-face, hybrid, and online environments. Ability to mentor undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students. Working knowledge of low-resource and community-based health settings. Strong written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills. Ability to collaborate with faculty, students, community partners, and external

stakeholders. Ability to support student organizations, recruitment activities, and community engagement initiatives. Ability to serve on doctoral dissertation committees and mentor graduate students. Required Qualifications Doctor of Public Health (DrPH) or closely related terminal degree from an accredited institution. Demonstrated history of experiential learning and community-engaged education. Working knowledge

of low-resource health settings. Experience supporting extracurricular student activities and student engagement initiatives. Ability to teach across undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral programs. Eligibility for graduate faculty status. Preferred Qualifications Experience in public health higher education. Experience with online instruction and course delivery. Experience serving on graduate thesis or dissertation
