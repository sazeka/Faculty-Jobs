the Department of Plant Biology at Michigan State University (MSU) invites applications for a fixed-term, postdoctoral scholar in plant systematics and phylogenomics. We seek a botanist who combines strong bioinformatics skills with an interest in using plant collections to address evolutionary questions. Research will build on our phylogenomic studies of Castilleja (the paintbrushes, Orobanchaceae) and explore the

role of hybridization and introgression in plant diversification. Potential directions include determining how introgression varies across species and geographic regions, investigating the persistence of species boundaries in the face of ongoing gene flow, and integrating genomic, morphological, and ecological evidence to understand evolutionary history. There will also be opportunities to contribute to ongoing

phylogenomic projects in other plant groups, particularly within Orobanchaceae. Specific questions, projects, and approaches will be developed collaboratively with the successful candidate, based on their interests and expertise. The postdoc will have substantial scope to shape the research and develop independent projects in other plant groups that address complementary questions. The lab is closely connected with

the MSU Herbarium, and we are particularly interested in applicants who want to connect herbaria with modern collections-based research. Related opportunities include Michigan and Great Lakes floristics and development of infrastructure linking specimens with genomic and other biodiversity data. The postdoc will join MSU's Plant Sciences and Ecology, Evolution, and Behavior communities, with opportunities for

collaboration, student mentoring, and professional development aligned with their career goals. Duties and responsibilities - -Develop and carry out research drawing on existing lab collections and datasets and herbarium specimens, with potential opportunities for targeted fieldwork. -Generate and analyze molecular data.
