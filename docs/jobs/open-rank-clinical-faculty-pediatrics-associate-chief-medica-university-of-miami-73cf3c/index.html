Description of the service The Holtz Children’s Hospital and Jackson Women’s Hospital Associate Chief Medical Officer ( ACMO ) will assist with the

oversight of the Hospital’s medical practice to ensure and promote high quality and cost-effective medical care. The ACMO serves as a liaison between medical staff and facility administration. The ACMO will report to the Vice President and Chief Medical Officer for the Jackson Memorial Hospital with a dotted line to the Vice President Holtz Children’s Hospital and Jackson Women’s Hospital. The ACMO provides

leadership and expertise for hospital quality and clinical effectiveness. The ACMO will evaluate and optimize the care management approach, process of disease management, patient satisfaction, patient safety and develop processes to assure appropriateness of care including length of stay and ancillary resource utilization. Provides an environment of quality, and cost improvement that is data driven and develops

systems to review utilization of resources and objectively measure outcomes of care in the inpatient and outpatient settings. The ACMO will monitor physician and group patterns, present data, analysis and interpretation to physicians and physician and hospital committees for review. The ACMO assures that quality management programs are carried out in all clinical areas through the development and implementation of

effective disease management programs, clinical protocols and guidelines, other decision tools, and review of the outcomes. Develops a performance database incorporating JCAHO and other key quality indicators to provide leadership necessary to meet regulatory requirements and guidelines. Conducts a quality improvement program including Peer Review process; reviews charts of providers, developing and communicating

corrective action plans to improve physician quality and behavior. Provides strategic leadership by focusing teams and organizational units on visions and distinctive strategies that result in excellent short and long-term performance in clinical, service and financial outcomes. Integrate evidence-based medicine practices wherever possible, building this into new and existing patient care delivery systems. Promotes
