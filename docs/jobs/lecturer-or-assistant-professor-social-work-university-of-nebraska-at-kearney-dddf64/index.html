University of Nebraska at Kearney Lecturer or Assistant Professor, Social Work Job Description Job Summary: The Department of Social Work at the University of Nebraska at Kearney invites applications for a faculty position at the rank of Lecturer or Assistant Professor. The Assistant Professor appointment is tenure-track. The Social Work program is committed to excellence in undergraduate teaching, scholarship,

community engagement, and meaningful faculty-student interactions. The BSW degree is offered in both on-campus and fully online formats and serves more than 130 students. Candidates whose research and/or practice interests broaden the department’s expertise and who have experience working with diverse student populations are especially encouraged to apply. Responsibilities: Both ranks include teaching undergraduate

courses in online and face-to-face formats, participating in student recruitment and advisement, serving on departmental and university committees, and contributing to program development and assessment. Assistant Professor Teaches three courses per semester and is expected to maintain an active program of research and scholarship and attain graduate faculty status. Lecturer Teaches four courses per semester.

Research and graduate faculty status are not required. Required Qualifications: Assistant Professor Doctorate in Social Work or a related field and an MSW from a CSWE-accredited institution; advanced ABD candidates may be considered. Minimum of two years of post-MSW practice experience. Lecturer Master of Social Work (MSW) from a CSWE-accredited institution. Minimum of two years of post-MSW practice experience

Preferred Qualifications: Lecturer Previous teaching experience or professional experience relevant to teaching. Assistant Professor Experience teaching diverse student populations in varied formats; demonstrated commitment to research and publication; and effective collaboration skills About Us: The University of Nebraska at Kearney (UNK) is a public university committed to excellence in teaching, research, and

community engagement. As part of the University of Nebraska system, UNK offers a supportive, student-focused environment with the resources of a larger institution. Located in central Nebraska, UNK serves around 6,000 students and is known for its strong academics, welcoming campus culture, and dedication to student success.
