Policy & ManagementThe USC Sol Price School of Public Policy is seeking a working professional with expertise and a primary position in legal and regulatory issues in health care. The candidate will be able to teach graduate-level course related to one or more of the following

areas: Theories and practices of financial accounting with emphasis on healthcare, accounting as a management tool; accounting systems; basic accounting concepts and procedures; budgets; financial statements. Preference will be given to candidates with prior teaching experience and excellent teaching evaluations.PPD 516 – Financial Accounting for Health Care Organizations. Course learning objectives include:

understanding various accounting systems, and financial accounting theories with emphasis on health care matters, understanding and communicating basic financial accounting terms, and analyzing financial statements and performance.
