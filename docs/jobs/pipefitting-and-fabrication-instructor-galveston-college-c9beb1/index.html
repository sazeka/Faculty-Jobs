Pipefitting and Fabrication Instructor

Galveston College  ·  Galveston, TX
InstructorTenure-TrackPipfitting and Fabrication

Position summary

Duties: Provide classroom and hands-on instruction in all areas of pipefitting, including layout, assembly, installation, maintenance, and repair of piping systems. Teach proper use of hand tools, power tools, and equipment associated with the pipefitting trade. Instruct students in reading blueprint, isometric and drawings, interpreting specifications, and following industry codes and standards (e.g., ASME, OSHA,

NCCER). Emphasize safety practices and ensure compliance with all safety regulations in the classroom and lab settings. Develop lesson plans, instructional materials, and lab exercises aligned with curriculum standards and industry best practices. Evaluate student progress through written exams, practical tests, and hands-on demonstrations. Maintain accurate attendance and grading records. Provide guidance,

mentorship, and support to students pursuing trade certifications or employment. Inspect, maintain, and request repair or replacement of training equipment and materials as needed. Collaborate with department leadership, advisory committees, and industry partners to ensure curriculum relevancy. Participate in faculty meetings, professional development, and program improvement activities Required Qualifications: High

school diploma or GED required, associate degree or technical certificate in a related field preferred. Minimum of 5 years of verifiable experience as a journeyman pipefitter in commercial, industrial, or construction settings. Previous teaching or training experience preferred (college, apprenticeship program, or on-the-job instruction Preferred Qualifications: Journeyman License preferred -Texas. NCCER Instructor

Certification or willingness to obtain certification upon hire. OSHA 10 or 30-Hour Safety Certification preferred. Current First Aid/CPR certification desirable.
