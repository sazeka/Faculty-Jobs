Class schedule is flexible based on training cohorts and employer demand (evening and/or weekends possible). POSITION SUMMARY: York County Community College (YCCC) is seeking a qualified

and enthusiastic Contract Instructor to deliver short-term, industry-aligned training in Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T), Precision Measurement, and Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM) Layout . engineering drawings, apply GD&T principles, and perform accurate dimensional inspections using both traditional precision measurement tools and CMM technologies. practical training that meets industry standards and supports student success.
