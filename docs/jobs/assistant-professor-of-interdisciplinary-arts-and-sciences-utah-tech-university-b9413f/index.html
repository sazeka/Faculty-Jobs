Position Announcement The Department of Interdisciplinary Arts & Sciences at Utah Tech University (UT) in St. George, Utah invites applications for a full-time tenure-track Assistant Professor faculty position, starting Fall Semester 2026. The department, located within the College of Education, serves as a hub for interdisciplinary activities on campus and is the home for both the Integrated Studies (INTS) and the

Individualized Studies (INDS) baccalaureate degree programs and the Master of Science in Leadership Studies (MSLS, a forthcoming program). Most information about the department and its associated programs and faculty can be found online at https://ias.utahtech.edu/. courses.
