Associate Director of Training, PsyD Program (core faculty)

Adler University  ·  Adler University, IL
Psychology

Position summary

Adler University invites applications for a full-time Associate Director of Training (ADOT) and core faculty position in the doctoral psychology (PsyD) program of the Chicago Campus. Adler University is the leading academic institution advancing socially responsible practice, healthy communities, and a more just society. We are committed to quality through continuous process improvement and personal accountability.

As a result, we seek professionals, who embrace life-long learning, present proactive solutions, and drive results to socially responsible clinical training practices. Position Summary The Associate Director of Training – PsyD (ADOT) serves as a core faculty member of the Psychology Department’s Doctor of Psychology (PsyD) in Clinical Psychology Program and will be joining a team of training faculty that consists of

one Director of Training and one other Associate Director of Training. The ADOT works with the training team to oversee clinical training placements throughout students’ graduate careers. ADOTs act as secondary advisers to students related to professional goals and development, as well as interfacing with practicum and internship supervisors and academic advisers on training-related matters. The ADOT engages in

practicum site visits and site development and serves as a point-of-contact for practicum training sites for concerns with students and to gather information on the provision of clinical training. This position reports to the Director of Training – Psy.D. and is accountable through the Director of Training to the Department Chair of Clinical Psychology. The ADOT is a member of the Clinical Psychology core program

faculty and teaches seminars and courses at the discretion of the Department Chair. Education/Experience Licensed Clinical Psychologist or in the process of obtaining licensure in Illinois Doctorate in Clinical or Counseling Psychology (Psy.D., Ph.D., or Ed.D.) Valid Illinois driver’s license and access to a car to visit training sites as needed Preference will be given to applicants with prior experience in clinical

training and/or teaching at the graduate level Essential Duties & Responsibilities Serves as an administrative point-person to all students in the Clinical PsyD Program for clinical training-related matters Develops and updates policies and procedures to ensure that the Clinical PsyD Program is maintaining best practices related to clinical training Assists students in site selection and review of application
