Minimum Quantifications and Other Credentials: A PhD in Materials Science and Engineering, Physics, Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field by the time of appointment A robust research and publication record of accomplishment Rank/Title:Assistant or Associate Professor Information on Position:The Department of Materials Science and

Engineering (MSE) at Northwestern University, Evanston, IL, invites applications for a full-time tenure-track faculty position to be filled at the Assistant or Associate Professor level, with the appointment rank commensurate with qualifications and experience, beginning September 1, 2027. Candidates are sought who develop and apply multimodal, in situ and operando X-ray characterization methods, including

diffraction, spectroscopy, microscopy, and coherence-based techniques; outstanding candidates advancing complementary electron-based characterization methods will also be considered. We seek researchers developing frontier X-ray science methods to probe material structure and behavior across length and time scales under realistic operating conditions, with applications including human health, energy conversion and

storage, and national security. Of particular interest are experimental programs that leverage advances in physics-informed artificial intelligence, scientific machine learning, autonomous control, or real-time feedback to guide experiments and enable the co-design of materials and manufacturing processes. Strong interest in and evidence of collaboration with national laboratories and/or industry partners is also

desirable. The successful candidate will be responsible for carrying out research, teaching, and service while building a vibrant research program that contributes to the department’s mission.The first academic department of its kind in the world, the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Northwestern University leads the field in materials innovation and education.
