Sign InCreate ProfileEnglish (US)Tenure Track Assistant/Associate Professor of Analytical ChemistryUnited States|req8212Posted on: 8/21/2026 Back to SearchThe Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry at East Stroudsburg University (ESU) invites applications for a tenure-track Assistant/Associate Professor with a specialty in analytical chemistry starting in August 2027. Appointment at the Assistant or Associate

Professor level will be commensurate with qualifications and experience. For full consideration, applications will begin on September 28, 2026. The successful candidate will demonstrate highly effective teaching and enthusiasm for advising and mentoring a diverse population of undergraduate students. Primary responsibilities include teaching analytical chemistry and associated laboratory courses. Additional teaching

responsibilities may include general chemistry, non-majors' courses, and upper-division offerings in the candidate’s area of expertise. An active research program involving undergraduate students is expected. Faculty contribute to university service by participating in activities including committee memberships, curriculum development, and outreach. The Department's Chemistry and Biochemistry programs are accredited

by the American Chemical Society (ACS) and the American Society for Biochemistry and Molecular Biology (ASBMB). The department is located in the Hoeffner Science and Technology Center and houses 13 dedicated teaching and research laboratories, a cold room, and modern faculty offices. The department maintains an impressive suite of instrumentation including a Bruker 400 MHz NMR, Agilent LC/Q-TOF Mass Spectrometer,

GC/MS, HPLCs, IR, UV/Vis, and fluorescence spectrometer.
