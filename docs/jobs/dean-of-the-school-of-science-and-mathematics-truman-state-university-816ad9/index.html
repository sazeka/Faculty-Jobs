Posting Details Position Information Position Title Dean of the School of Science and Mathematics Job Responsibilities Truman State University — Missouri’s premier, highly selective, public liberal arts and sciences university — seeks a strategic and innovative administrative professional to serve as the next Dean for the School of Science and Mathematics. The new Dean will be a confident leader with a positive

attitude, strong communication skills, and a collaborative spirit. The Position: The Dean for the School of Science and Mathematics provides leadership for the School and works with other deans, department chairs, faculty, and staff to achieve the goals of the School and the University. The Dean is committed to helping students achieve essential learning outcomes in all aspects of their education. They champion the

value of a liberal arts and sciences education as the strongest preparation for students to productively contribute to society. They also work collaboratively with chairs and faculty to articulate a School vision that is consistent with the University mission. The Dean must display a high standard of personal and professional ethics and integrity and will guide and evaluate the performance of chairs and faculty

within the School. The Dean will promote teamwork and continuous improvement among the School’s students, faculty, and staff as well as provide intellectual leadership. The Dean will promote assessment to measure progress toward strategic goals, display fiscal responsibility, and develop strategies to secure external resources to support School and University programs.
