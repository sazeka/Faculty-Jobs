Position summary

Faculty Opportunities Assistant / Associate Professor of Counseling Education & Practicum Coordinator Goldey-Beacom College invites applications for an Assistant/Associate Professor of Counseling Education and Practicum Coordinator. This individual will teach psychology and counseling courses and coordinate practicum sites with supervisors, students, faculty, and staff. We seek an organized individual with a strong

background in program counselor education and practicum site management. Key Responsibilities: As a member of the GBC faculty, you would: Teach a 3/3 course load cross undergraduate and graduate programs in counseling and psychology, ensuring relevance and rigor across all content areas Coordinate all practicum and internship experiences in accordance with CACREP Standards, including site development, supervisor

liaising, and student support Establish and maintain partnerships with industry to support internships, practicums, and other hands-on learning experiences for students Participate in department and college assessment activities Demonstrate sustained engagement with the profession through participation in professional activities, scholarly activities, and service to the profession Why Join Goldey-Beacom College? At

GBC, faculty work closely with motivated students in small classes that emphasize applied learning and professional readiness. Faculty have the opportunity to shape programs and curricula in a supportive, collegial environment. With a small enrollment and a large endowment, the College offers a stable place to foster student success and to grow as a practitioner and scholar. Faculty at Goldey-Beacom College receive

annual stipends for conference travel, research, and workshops for their ongoing professional growth. Located in beautiful, suburban Pike Creek, Delaware, Goldey-Beacom offers a range of degrees and attracts a diverse student body from 22 states and over 60 nations. Our mission is to empower learners to achieve excellence by linking knowledge to practice. Compensation: Assistant Professor: $64,000 – $68,000 Associate

Professor: $68,000 – $75,000 Reports To: Vice President for Academic Affairs/ Provost Months per Year: 10.5 months Benefits: Goldey-Beacom College offers an excellent benefits package that includes: Medical, Dental, & Vision Retirement Plan (403b) in which College contributes 5.5% Life & Disability Insurances Education benefits for employees, spouses and dependents Paid Holidays Free weekly meals in dining hall
