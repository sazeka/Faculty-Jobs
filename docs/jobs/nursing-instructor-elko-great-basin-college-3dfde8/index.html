Job Description Great Basin College is hiring for a full-time, 9-month tenure-track nursing faculty

Shape Future Nurses – Teach in GBC’s Associate of Applied Science (AAS) Nursing Program and contribute

to RN-BSN coursework. Diverse Teaching Opportunities – Engage students through classroom, online, simulation, and clinical instruction. Make a Difference – Play a key role in developing skilled, compassionate nurses for the healthcare workforce. Qualifications: Education: Master’s in Nursing (MSN) OR a BSN with

enrollment in an MSN program within one year of hire. Degrees must be from an ACEN or CCNE-accredited program. Prior teaching experience is a plus, but not required. Skills & Attributes: Passion for teaching and mentoring students.

Strong organizational and communication skills. Ability to oversee students in clinical settings. Experience with distance learning and online instruction is desirable.
