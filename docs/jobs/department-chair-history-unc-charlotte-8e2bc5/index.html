competitive and commensurate with experience.Application Instructions:Interested candidates should submit a cover letter describing how they meet the qualifications listed above, including their administrative experience and approach to leadership, scholarship, and teaching; a current curriculum vitae; and the names and contact information of three references.Applications must be received by October 9, 2026.For more

Studies, Capitalism Studies, the David R. Ravin School of Architecture, Global Studies, Latin American Studies, Religious Studies, and Women’s and Gender Studies.
