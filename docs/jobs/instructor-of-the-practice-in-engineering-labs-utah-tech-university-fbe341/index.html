College of Science, Engineering, and Technology at Utah Tech University (UT) in St. George, Utah, invites applications for a full-time Instructor of the Practice in Engineering Labs position to begin Spring Semester (January 2027). Come join an active group of educators dedicated to offering students immersive experiences with lab courses, facilitating UTXplore projects, and equipment training. More information about

the Engineering Department can be found online at https://mech.utahtech.edu/. Responsibilities Teach 30 credits of laboratory and/or lecture courses in the Engineering Department each academic year.Maintain labs and lab equipment within the Engineering Department.Ensure engineering labs are compliant with university, state, and federal safety regulations.Provide prototyping guidance to students involved in UTXplore,

capstone, design practicum, and research projects.Attend department, college, and division faculty meetings; work with other faculty in program management and adhere to University policy and procedures.Perform defined campus-wide duties, including holding office hours, functioning as an advisor to students, and performing university service and scholarship as defined by academic policy.Perform other duties as

assigned. Qualifications M.S. in an engineering discipline, or related field, required. Candidates with a B.S. degree in an engineering discipline that have relevant work experience and/or a M.S. degree in a non-related field will also be considered.Relevant industry experience is preferred.Familiarity with equipment, whether machining, controllers, or electronic testing equipment, is preferred.A strong prototyping

background is preferred. Prior teaching experience in engineering or related courses preferred.A strong commitment to and evidence of potential for excellence in teaching.Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a commitment to collegiality and teamwork.An interest in active learning, experiential learning, and student engagement is desirable.
