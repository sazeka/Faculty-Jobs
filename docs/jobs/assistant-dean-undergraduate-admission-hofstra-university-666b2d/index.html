Position Title Assistant Dean, Undergraduate Admission Position Number 896672, 897652 Position Category Administration School/Division Enrollment Management Department Full-Time or Part-Time

Full-Time Description Reporting to the Director of Undergraduate Admission, the Assistant Dean handles all aspects of admissions and recruiting for Undergraduate Admission in the Office of Enrollment Management. Candidates must be willing and able to organize and handle all aspects of administration and adhere to the rules and regulations of Hofstra University, the Colonial Athletic Conference, and the NCAA.

Additional hours required in the evenings and weekends in support of special projects, travel, and events. Responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Travels domestically and internationally to visit high schools and attend college day/night programs. Conducts interviews with prospective students.
