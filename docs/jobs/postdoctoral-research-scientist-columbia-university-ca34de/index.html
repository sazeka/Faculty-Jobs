Postdoctoral Research Scientist

Columbia University  ·  Columbia University, NY
PostdoctoralNeuromuscular Disorders

Position summary

The laboratory of Dr. Michio Hirano, in the H. Houston Merritt Neuromuscular Research Center at Columbia University, is seeking a talented and highly motivated Postdoctoral Research Scientist. The successful candidate will perform cell culture studies to assess candidate pharmacological therapies for a rare neuromuscular genetic disorder due to a HSPB8 pathogenic variant.&nbsp; &nbsp; In addition, the candidate will

be responsible for the following:&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; Culture primary human fibroblasts - &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Continue on-going medium throughput drug screening of cultured human fibroblasts from patients with HSPB8 pathogenic variants. - &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Immunocytochemistry of patients’ and control fibroblasts - &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Immunoblot quantification of

autophagic markers - &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Work closely to prepare data for future drug efficacy assessment/review.&nbsp; - &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Potential single molecule characterization of HSPB8 wild-type vs. mutant proteins The Merritt Neuromuscular Research Center is comprised of a diverse and collaborative group of scientists and neurologists, with an invaluable connection between

the laboratory and clinic.&nbsp; Our research into the underlying molecular pathomechanisms of mitochondrial neurogastrointestinal encephalomyopathy (MNGIE) and thymidine kinase 2 deficiency (TK2d) has directly has led to our development of clinical trials for these devastating diseases.&nbsp; The group’s pre-clinical studies have led to pioneering the use of nucleoside supplementation to treat TK2d. &nbsp;&nbsp;
