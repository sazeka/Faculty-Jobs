Position description Health Sciences Clinical Professor Series, Open Ranks Department of Dermatology, UC Irvine The Department of Dermatology at the University of California, Irvine is recruiting outstanding applicants for a Dermatologist at the Assistant, Associate or full Professor level. Responsibilities include patient care, teaching, and academic activities. The successful candidate will work with a

collaborative group of 23 faculty, 16 residents, 1 fellow, and dedicated staff. The UCI Skin Club has in excess of 30 members across the campus in basic sciences and other disciplines. HS Clinical Professor Series - see: http://www.ucop.edu/academic-personnel-programs/_files/apm/apm-278.pdf Requirements: Applicant must possess a M.D., Ph.D., or M.D./PhD degree in the field of Dermatology or equivalent, and should be

board-certified or board-eligible in Dermatology. In addition, should be eligible for California licensure. Candidates with demonstrated capabilities for teaching, professional competence/activity, and University/public service suitable for the HS Clinical Professor series at the UCI Department of Dermatology will be considered. Positions are dependent upon funding. Rank will be determined based on qualifications and

experience. Initial appointments are for one year and renewal is based on availability of support. Salary is commensurate with qualifications and experience. What We Offer: • A competitive compensation package commensurate with experience. • Comprehensive benefits including medical, dental, & vision. • Generous vacation time and professional development support. • A desirable location in beautiful Orange County.
