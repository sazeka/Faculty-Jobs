Academic Affairs · Business, Science, Tech and Math · Knight Campus (Warwick) Assistant Professor, Computer Science and Cybersecurity JOB SUMMARY:The Department of Computer Science and Cybersecurity seeks a dynamic tenure-track faculty member to teach undergraduate courses across the computer science curriculum. The department prepares students for successful transfer opportunities and careers in computer science,

software engineering, cybersecurity, cloud computing, data analytics, networking, artificial intelligence, and related computing disciplines. As a teaching-focused institution, CCRI values excellence in instruction, student engagement, advising, and curriculum development.Teaching responsibilities will be based on departmental needs and the successful candidate's qualifications. Full-time faculty are expected to

teach a full instructional workload consistent with the CCRI Faculty Association Agreement, advise and mentor students, participate in curriculum development and assessment, and contribute to departmental and college initiatives. Course assignments may include programming, software engineering, scripting, operating systems, cybersecurity, cloud computing, data analytics, artificial intelligence, and other computer

science disciplines.This position will begin with the Spring 2027 semester.ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:Commitment to CCRI's Mission:Support the philosophy and mission of a comprehensive community college.Foster a diverse, inclusive, equitable, and student-centered learning environment through collaboration with students, faculty, staff, and community partners.Effective Teaching:Maintain current expertise

through continuous professional development in computer science, software engineering, cybersecurity, instructional technologies, and emerging computing trends, incorporating new developments into instruction where appropriate.Deliver high-quality, student-centered instruction using evidence-based teaching practices that promote critical thinking, problem solving, and student success.Develop, implement, and assess

course competencies, learning outcomes, and student performance using equitable and effective assessment practices.Adapt instructional strategies based on assessment results, student feedback, and diverse learning needs to continuously improve student learning.Instructional Skills:Provide high-quality instruction in face-to-face, hybrid, synchronous online, asynchronous online, and laboratory learning
