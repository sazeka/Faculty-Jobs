Position description Neil and MJ Kelly Presidential Chair in Vitreoretinal Science Department of Ophthalmology and Vision Science University of California, Davis - Internal

Applicants Only The holder of the Neil and MJ Kelly Presidential Chair in Vitreoretinal Science will be a senior faculty member in the Department of Ophthalmology and Vision Science whose specialty is in the area of Vitreoretinal Surgery and whose distinguished scholarship is nationally recognized. The initial term of the Neil and MJ Kelly Presidential Chair in Vitreoretinal Science is for five years, with the option

of reappointment per regularly established University procedures for endowed chair/professorship appointments (APM 191, UCD 191). It is expected that the holder of the endowed chair will advance customarily through the faculty ranks on the basis of his/her scholarly activities, teaching, University and public service. In addition, as the holder of the Neil and MJ Kelly Presidential Chair in Vitreoretinal Science is

expected to: Engage in teaching and scholarly activities focused in the area of Vitreoretinal Surgery. Teach ophthalmology residents, fellows, and medical students in both clinical care and research in the Department of Ophthalmology and Vision Science and other departments as appropriate. Conduct and support research related to ophthalmic clinical care and perform service to the Department of Ophthalmology and

Vision Science and the broader community in which it is situated. Provide leadership in the area of Vitreoretinal Science and Surgery and be fully engaged in activities in the Department of Ophthalmology and Vision Science.
