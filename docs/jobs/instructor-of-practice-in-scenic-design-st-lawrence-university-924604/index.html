Posting Details Position Information Position Title Instructor of Practice in Scenic Design FLSA Exempt

Primary Responsibilities St. Lawrence University invites applications for an Instructor of Practice in Scenic Design in our Performance and Communication Arts department. We seek artist-teachers committed to inclusive teaching in the liberal arts to join and expand our transdisciplinary curriculum that emphasizes theatre, performance, rhetoric, and communication. Possible courses include scenic design, scenic

painting, props design, stagecraft, our Introduction to Theatre course (taught in rotation with colleagues), and other areas of the candidate’s specialty. The successful candidate will be required to teach 3 courses per academic year (organized as a 2-1 or 1-2 load based on department and university theatre needs). In addition to teaching, the successful candidate will serve as the resident scenic designer for

University Theatre, assist the production manager/technical director in the scene shop during afternoon lab hours, and actively help supervise student crews. Other duties include maintaining the scenic painting and props areas and leading student workers, serving as a mentor to student-designers and technicians, and participating in recruitment and retention efforts. A successful applicant will actively participate

in the life of the department through department meetings and professional development opportunities. This 10-month position begins the fall semester of 2027 (August 2027).
