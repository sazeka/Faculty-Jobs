Assistant Professor of Video Journalism, School of Journalism

University of Montana  ·  School of Journalism  ·  Missoula, MT
Assistant ProfessorTenure-TrackJournalism

Position summary

The School of Journalism at the University of Montana seeks a tenure-track assistant professor to teach and help redesign the undergraduate multiplatform video storytelling track within the Bachelor of Arts in Journalism. This position will teach video reporting, interviewing, shooting, scripting, and editing. The position combines a full teaching load with responsibility for helping to design the future of video

journalism. The successful candidate will teach courses across our program, with an emphasis on video courses and skills. They will work with school leadership and faculty to define the video competencies all graduates should develop and will map them across courses that include lectures and hands-on learning and production. The successful candidate will help assess and develop the track's coverage of contemporary

platforms, production practices, and emerging tools, including social and web video. The successful candidate will know and teach evolving digital technologies, with an eye toward guiding how those can be strengthened within course offerings. The Assistant Professor will expand and strengthen the track's applied learning opportunities, including the Student Documentary Unit and projects distributed through Montana

PBS and other broadcasters. The successful candidate will also advise students, participate in school, college and university governance and service, and maintain an active record of professional practice, scholarship or creative work appropriate to a journalism faculty appointment.
