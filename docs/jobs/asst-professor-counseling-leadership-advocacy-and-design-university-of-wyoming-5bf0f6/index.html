JOB TITLE: Assistant Professor JOB

PURPOSE: The faculty member will provide methodological support for graduate students in the college. In addition, the successful candidate will maintain a scholarly research agenda and provide service to the program, school, college, university, and the profession. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES: The faculty member will be expected to teach master's and doctoral level courses in Educational Research, to

include both qualitative and quantitative research methods. Furthermore, the faculty member will be expected to support graduate students throughout the college by serving on student committees. In the area of research, the faculty member will be expected to maintain an active publication agenda to include publications such as peer reviewed journal articles, book chapters, and/or presentations at conference venues
