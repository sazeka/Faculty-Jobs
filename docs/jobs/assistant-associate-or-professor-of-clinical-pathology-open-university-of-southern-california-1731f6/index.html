Faculty members in Transfusion Medicine provide clinical service to three hospitals located on the Health Science Campus (Keck Hospital, Norris Cancer Hospital, and Los Angeles General Medical Center) and participate in teaching in the medical school and residency/fellowship programs associated with an academic medical center. Candidates should have a demonstrated commitment to teaching and clinical

service.Candidates for appointment must hold an M.D. or D.O. degree, be eligible for a California Medical license, be certified in Clinical Pathology and be certified or board eligible in Transfusion Medicine. Interest in general laboratory medicine along with hemostasis and the diagnosis and management of coagulopathies is desirable. The annual base salary range for this position is $250,000-$450,000.
