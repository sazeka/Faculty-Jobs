Carnegie Mellon University’s department of Mechanical Engineering is searching for a Postdoctoral Research Associate to join their team. This is an exciting opportunity for someone who thrives in an interesting and challenging work environment. You will contribute to the department by focusing on the interface between engineered

mammalian cells and implantable device environments, including studies of cell adaptation, longevity, functional stability, optical readout compatibility, and integrated system behavior. This role will emphasize assessment of engineered cells in configurations relevant to device integration. The postdoctoral associate will design and execute experiments to understand how engineered cells perform over time in

controlled in vitro settings, biomaterial-supported culture environments, and prototype device-relevant configurations. The position requires a technically mature researcher who can work across cell biology, biomaterials, device testing, quantitative imaging, and experimental systems engineering. Core Responsibilities: Lead cell-device integration experiments supporting project milestones. Develop test plans for

evaluating engineered cells under implant-relevant in vitro conditions. Collaborate with cell-engineering, device, optics, tissue-engineering, and systems- integration teams. Establish reproducible protocols for longitudinal culture, functional testing, and data collection.
