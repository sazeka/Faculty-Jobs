Applications Open: Oct 28 2026 Applications Close: Open until filled General Summary of Position: The Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Massachusetts Lowell invites applications for a full-time, non-tenure-track faculty position at

the rank of Assistant Teaching Professor to start on September 1, 2027. The primary responsibilities will be to teach undergraduate courses in Statistics or related fields, and to coordinate our Undergraduate BS Statistics and BA Actuarial Studies programs. This position is renewable annually, potentially leading to an appointment as Associate Teaching Professor following six consecutive years of outstanding

performance evaluations. UMass Lowell is located about 25 miles northwest of Boston in the high-tech corridor of Massachusetts. As an Assistant Teaching Professor, the successful candidate’s responsibilities include both teaching and service. Teaching will consist of three classes per semester (9 credits) at the undergraduate level. The primary service responsibility will be to provide leadership for the Statistics

and Actuarial Studies programs, including academic advising, curriculum assessment and development, coordination with faculty teaching in the programs, and outreach to industry and community partners. Information about our degree programs can be found at: https://www.uml.edu/catalog/undergraduate/sciences/departments/mathematical-sciences/mathematical-sciences-major.aspx Courses taught by department faculty can be
