About this Position This Clinical Assistant/Associate/Professor of Forensic Science position serves in the School of Forensic Sciences at the OSU Center for Health Sciences (OSU-CHS) in Tulsa, Oklahoma. The position is focused on forensic behavioral health and related areas. This position

serves in the Forensic Sciences Graduate Certificate, M.S., D.F.S., and Ph.D. programs and is actively engaged in both teaching and laboratory functions. The position will work collaboratively with faculty members in the School of Forensic Sciences, as well as local and state agencies. The position serves a multidisciplinary role encompassing teaching, research, and applied forensic analysis. This position supports

the academic mission of the institution through instruction in forensic behavioral health, applied forensic genealogy, and the advancement of forensic mental health policy and evaluation research. The faculty member contributes to the development of future forensic professionals while advancing scholarship and innovation in forensic mental health assessment and investigative methodologies.
