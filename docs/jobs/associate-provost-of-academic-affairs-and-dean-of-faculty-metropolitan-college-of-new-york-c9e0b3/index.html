Position summary

Metropolitan College of New York (MCNY) is a unique and vibrant learning institution. It is a non-profit, regionally accredited college offering associate, bachelor's and master's degrees, with state-of-the-art campus in the Hub district of the South Bronx. For over 50 years, MCNY has offered highly motivated learners a powerful model of Purpose-Centered Education that promotes personal transformation and

constructive change in workplaces and communities. MCNY enrolls approximately 1,000 students annually into its accelerated degree programs with convenient onsite and remote schedules for added flexibility. Position Overview Metropolitan College of New York (MCNY) seeks an accomplished and collaborative academic leader to serve as Associate Provost of Academic Affairs & Dean of Faculty. Reporting to the Executive Vice

President & Provost, the Associate Provost & Dean of the Faculty provides strategic and operational leadership for academic affairs, curriculum quality, assessment, and faculty affairs. The successful candidate will help advance academic excellence, student success, and continuous improvement across the College while ensuring that academic policies, programs, and faculty practices align with institutional priorities

and applicable state and federal standards. Key Responsibilities Academic leadership: Partner with the EVP & Provost, school leaders, faculty, and staff to implement MCNY’s academic strategy and strengthen the quality, relevance, and coherence of academic programs. Curriculum development and review: Lead and coordinate the design, revision, approval, implementation, and periodic review of curricula, academic

programs, and related policies. Assessment and continuous improvement: Oversee institution-wide academic assessment, including program learning outcomes, curriculum mapping, use of evidence, and improvement planning. Faculty affairs: Provide leadership for faculty recruitment, appointment, orientation, workload, evaluation, development, promotion, retention, and related policies and processes. Shared governance: Work

productively with faculty governance bodies and academic committees; support transparent, inclusive, and timely decision-making. Accreditation and compliance: Coordinate academic contributions to accreditation, program review, regulatory reporting, and other quality-assurance requirements.
