Cypress College Professor Foster Stanback Named 2027 Orange County Teacher of the Year Nominee

Cypress College  ·  Cypress, CA
Full ProfessorAviation

Position summary

Cypress College Professor Foster Stanback Named 2027 Orange County Teacher of the Year Nominee by Campus Communications on August 28, 2026 Cypress College aviation professor Clarence Foster Stanback has been nominated for the 2027 Orange County Teacher of the Year award, recognizing an exceptional educator whose work reaches far beyond the classroom. Stanback is known for helping students understand complex aviation

concepts while also preparing them for the realities of entering the very demanding and often costly field. Through mentoring, career guidance, scholarship assistance, and connections to employers, he helps students build a path from the classroom to a career. Students have described Stanback as a passionate and approachable instructor who takes time to learn their names, interests, and career goals. The Cypress

College dean of Career Technical Education wrote in support of Stanback’s nomination that students view him as one of the most influential educators and mentors they have encountered. In the classroom, Stanback teaches both foundational and advanced aviation topics using historical context, real-world examples, current research and emerging technology. In addition to quizzes and exams, students complete simulations,

role-playing exercises, and other hands-on assignments. He also sets clear learning goals and lets students help establish expectations for the class. Stanback is involved in his students’ careers, helping them prepare by connecting them with internships, job opportunities and industry contacts. His network includes the Civil Air Patrol, the U.S. Air Force Academy, flight schools and other aviation organizations.

Retired Capt. Ed Valdez, Cypress College Aviation and Travel Careers department coordinator and professor, credited Stanback with helping many students pursue careers in aviation through education, mentorship and access to opportunities. “Foster Stanback is very deserving of the OCDE Teacher of the Year Award,” Valdez wrote. “He is one of the most impactful educators at Cypress College.” Stanback remains active in

industry meetings, college events and department activities while continuing his own professional development. Colleagues say his professionalism, vast knowledge and respect for students make him a strong representative of Cypress College among the 2027 Orange County Teacher of the Year nominees.
