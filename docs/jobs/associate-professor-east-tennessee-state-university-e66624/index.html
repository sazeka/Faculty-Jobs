Associate Professor TN, United States Be the First to Apply Job Description Job Summary The Department of Communication Studies and Storytelling at East Tennessee State University seeks an Associate Professor of Communication Studies. This position carries full-time, tenure-track status beginning August 15, 2027, as an academic year appointment pending continued availability of funds. We seek a colleague with a

demonstrated emphasis in Organizational Communication, and a record of teaching, service, and research appropriate for the rank of Associate Professor. We are especially interested in candidates who have a secondary interest in one or more of the following areas: Crisis and Risk Communication, Social Media & Digital Communication, Applied Communication, or Political Communication. We are open to candidates who use

any Communication Studies research methodology. Our new colleague must demonstrate respect and value for the use of narrative and storytelling in teaching and scholarship, although their research may or may not incorporate narrative. Teaching (60%)—The expected teaching load is a 3/3. This faculty member will primarily teach in our undergraduate Communication Studies major and minor programs but may have

opportunities to teach graduate courses for our Graduate Certificate in Storytelling. They will be expected to teach existing undergraduate courses in Organizational Communication, Business and Professional Communication, and Communicating in Groups. The successful candidate will also have the ability to teach one or more of the following undergraduate courses currently in our catalog: Communication Theory,

Professional Communication & Technology, Advanced Public Speaking, Speaking for Social Change, and/or Communication Ethics. We are especially interested in candidates who are willing to develop new courses in Organizational Communication and at least one of the following areas: Crisis and Risk Communication, Social Media and Digital Communication, Applied Communication, and/or Political Communication. Our new

colleague will serve on undergraduate honors thesis committees and hold regular oﬃce hours for students. Teaching formats may include in-person, hybrid, and online teaching. Summer and winter teaching opportunities are optional and available depending on need.
