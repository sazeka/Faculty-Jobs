Program Director and Associate Professor/Associate Clinical Professor or Professor/Clinical Professor Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Posting Details Position Information Job Title Program Director and Associate Professor/Associate Clinical Professor or Professor/Clinical Professor Position Number 998404 Department Physical Therapy Salary $95,000-$110,000 Remote No Job Summary The Department

of Physical Therapy at Angelo State University invites applications for a faculty position as Program Director of the Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) program. The successful candidate will provide academic and operational leadership for the program, with primary responsibility for ensuring ongoing compliance with Commission on Accreditation in Physical Therapy Education (CAPTE) standards and accreditation

requirements. The Program Director will lead and support faculty and students in achieving program goals while fostering a collaborative academic environment. The position will promote faculty development, student success, curricular excellence, and continuous quality improvement. Working closely with the Department Chair, faculty, and university administration, the Program Director will support the day-to-day

operations of the program and contribute to departmental and institutional strategic initiatives. The Program Director will also contribute to the department’s mission through teaching, scholarship, and service. Essential Duties Provide leadership and oversight to ensure ongoing compliance with all applicable CAPTE Standards and Required Elements Provide effective academic and administrative leadership for the DPT

program consistent with departmental, college, and university missions and strategic priorities Provide leadership for the ongoing development, implementation, assessment, and revision of the DPT curriculum to ensure alignment with CAPTE requirements, contemporary evidence, and physical therapist practice Teach in the Doctor of Physical Therapy curriculum in assigned areas of expertise Engage in scholarly activity

consistent with program and institutional expectations, including research, publication, and professional presentations Contribute to service activities within the program, department, college, university, and professional community Knowledge, Skills and Abilities Knowledge CAPTE accreditation standards, compliance requirements, and accreditation processes applicable to Doctor of Physical Therapy education
