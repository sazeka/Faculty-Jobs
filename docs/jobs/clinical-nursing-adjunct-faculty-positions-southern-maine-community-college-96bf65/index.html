Clinical Nursing (Adjunct) Faculty Positions

Southern Maine Community College  ·  Southern Maine Community College, ME
AdjunctNursing

Position summary

PROJECTED START DATE : August, 2026 RESPONSIBILITIES: SMCC is seeking adjunct clinical nursing faculty (approximately 9-12 clinical hours/week per semester) for the Brunswick and South Portland campuses. Responsibilities include the supervision/instruction of associate degree nursing students in hospital medical/surgical and/or skilled nursing settings. Adjunct faculty are responsible for assigning patients who meet

the educational and clinical experience requirements. Clinical faculty plan pre- and post-conferences and provide regular feedback to students for performance improvement and review. Final clinical evaluations are the responsibility of the clinical faculty. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS: BSN (Master&rsquo;s degree in Nursing preferred) Current Maine RN licensure Recent Acute Care and/or Skilled Nursing Care Experience Must

meet vaccine and regulatory requirements of clinical sites Must be able to meet the functional requirements of a nurse (ex. lift up to 50lbs) PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS: Experience in a lab setting Experience in Skilled Nursing Care settings EMPLOYMENT ELIGIBILITY : In compliance with federal law, all persons hired will be required to verify identity and eligibility to work in the United States and to complete the
