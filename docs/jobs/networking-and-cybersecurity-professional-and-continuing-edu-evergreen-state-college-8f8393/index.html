Networking & Cybersecurity (Professional and Continuing Education) (Tenure Track Faculty) Bookmark this Posting Print Preview | Apply for this Job Position Details Position Information Position Title Networking & Cybersecurity (Professional and Continuing Education) (Tenure Track Faculty) Job Summary The Evergreen State College seeks a broadly trained regular* faculty member eager to develop innovative curriculum in

cybersecurity, networking, and computing infrastructure at an experimental liberal arts college. We are particularly interested in candidates who are excited to explore the unique possibilities that networking, cybersecurity, systems administration, and computing infrastructure offer for problem solving, critical thinking, and digital citizenship within an interdisciplinary, team-taught curriculum. We are seeking a

practitioner-to-educator whose professional experience has been built in network administration, IT systems, cybersecurity operations, or related infrastructure fields and who is motivated to prepare the next generation of technology professionals. Core teaching responsibilities include IT foundations, networking concepts and protocols, network administration, systems administration, and the bridge into cybersecurity

practice. This is not a programming or software engineering position; rather, it focuses on the infrastructure that enables modern computing systems to function securely, reliably, and at scale. Industry certifications such as CompTIA Network+, Security+, CCNA, or equivalent, together with significant professional experience are valued alongside or in place of a traditional academic profile. The successful candidate

will help design and teach courses that contribute to certificate programs as well as degree completion pathways for returning adult students. The faculty member will also serve as the coordinator of the College’s Cybersecurity Certificate, providing academic leadership for the program, supporting curriculum development and continuous improvement, advising students, and ensuring the certificate remains aligned with

current industry practices and workforce needs. We are interested in candidates who have experience or interest in using innovative pedagogy to engage students in developing their conceptual understanding of computation.
