Assistant Athletic Trainer / Instructor of Athletic Training

University of Evansville  ·  Athletic Training  ·  University of Evansville, IN
InstructorAthletic Training

Position summary

Employment Opportunity Detail Assistant Athletic Trainer / Instructor of Athletic Training The University of Evansville Department of Athletics is seeking a dedicated, student-centered, and collaborative athletic training professional to join our Sports Medicine team. This position will assist the Associate Athletic Director for Sports Medicine in providing comprehensive healthcare services for University

student-athletes while also supporting the School of Health Sciences Athletic Training Program through classroom instruction and clinical education. The successful candidate will serve as the primary athletic trainer for either baseball or softball and may be assigned additional sport coverage as determined by the Associate Athletic Director for Sports Medicine. This position combines clinical athletic training

responsibilities with teaching and clinical supervision duties, providing a unique opportunity to contribute to both student-athlete health and athletic training education. Key Responsibilities The Assistant Athletic Trainer / Instructor will balance athletic training services, educational responsibilities, and administrative duties. Primary responsibilities include: Athletic Training & Sports Medicine Provide

prevention, evaluation, treatment, and rehabilitation of athletic injuries and illnesses for assigned student-athletes. Develop and implement injury prevention and rehabilitation programs in collaboration with the Sports Medicine team. Deliver healthcare education and counseling to student-athletes. Communicate effectively with student-athletes, physicians, coaching staff, and parents, when appropriate, regarding

injury status and treatment plans. Coordinate and provide medical coverage for practices, competitions, and team travel. Collaborate with physicians and allied healthcare professionals to ensure comprehensive care for student-athletes. Maintain accurate electronic medical records and injury documentation. Assist with insurance claim processing and related administrative functions. Support scheduling and coordination

of medical coverage for home and away athletic events. Teaching & Clinical Education Provide instruction within the School of Health Sciences Athletic Training Program. Serve as a clinical preceptor and mentor for Athletic Training students. Supervise, educate, and evaluate Athletic Training students and athletic training interns. Assist with recruitment and retention efforts for the Athletic Training Program.
