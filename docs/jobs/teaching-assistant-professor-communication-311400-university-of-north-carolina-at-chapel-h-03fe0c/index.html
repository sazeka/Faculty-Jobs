The Department of Communication at The University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill invites applications for the position of Teaching Assistant Professor (fixed term, non-tenure track) in Interpersonal Communication or Organizational Communication. This is a full-time three-year appointment

that is renewable and eligible for promotion in rank. The position carries a 3/3 teaching load, with 80% of time devoted to teaching and 20% to service. This colleague will lead a core foundational course for the

Department of Communication—Introduction to Interpersonal and Organizational Communication/COMM120 (https://catalog.unc.edu/courses/comm/)—which introduces undergraduates to key frameworks, research, and skills in interpersonal communication.
