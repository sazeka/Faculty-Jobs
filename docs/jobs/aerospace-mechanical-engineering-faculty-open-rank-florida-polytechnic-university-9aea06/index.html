Aerospace/Mechanical Engineering Faculty (Open Rank) JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY Florida Polytechnic University invites applications for a tenure-equivalent faculty position in Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering at the rank of Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, or Professor, beginning August 2027. Florida Poly launched its B.S. in Aerospace Engineering in Fall 2026, building on a long-running Aerospace

concentration in Mechanical Engineering. This hire will help build the program’s propulsion and aerodynamics coursework and laboratories from the ground up, while also teaching in the thermal-fluids courses shared by both degrees. JOB DESCRIPTION Responsibilities Teach aerospace courses such as Principles of Flight Vehicle Propulsion, Rocket Propulsion, and Fundamentals of Aerodynamics, along with Mechanical

Engineering courses in thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and heat transfer Help develop aerodynamics and propulsion laboratory instruction and equipment Mentor aerospace design and senior capstone teams Build a research program that involves undergraduate and M.S. students Contribute to department and University service Areas of interest We welcome applicants from all areas of aerospace engineering.
