Faculty Services Assistant Los Angeles, California, United States Social Sciences Regular Title: Faculty Services Assistant Department: Social Sciences Duration: Indefinite - 11 Months Employment Status: Non-Exempt, Full-Time, 30 hours per week Job Summary Reporting to the Senior Department Services Coordinator, the Faculty Services Assistant provides a wide range of highly organized, accurate, and effective

administrative support to faculty in assigned academic departments. The Faculty Services Assistant supports the teaching, academic, and professional work of department faculty and contributes to an effective learning environment for students. The Faculty Services Assistant receives directions from department chairs, faculty, and the Office of the Dean of the College, particularly the Academic Support Administrator.

The Faculty Services Assistant assists with daily office operations, events, and program management.This position is represented for purposes of collective bargaining by SEIU. Job Duties 35% - Provide Administrative Support to Department Chairs and Faculty - Provide administrative support to department chairs, faculty, and the Senior Department Services Coordinator with routine departmental operations and academic

activities. Prepare and organize course materials; maintain faculty and staff contact lists; and compile and post faculty teaching schedules and office hours. Support faculty with course-related logistics, including ordering desk copies of course books, printing and copying, reserving classrooms and meeting spaces, arranging audio-visual setup, and organizing class trips and academic events. Assist with logistics for

program reviews, faculty searches, and the welcoming and orientation of new faculty and adjunct faculty. Support the Office of the Dean of the College and faculty with projects and initiatives, including grant-funded programs and symposia. Perform other duties and projects assigned by department chairs or the Office of the Dean, including temporary support for other departments or programs within Academic Affairs as

needed. - (Essential) 20% - Support Departmental Events and Programs - Assist with planning and publicizing departmental events, including guest speakers, seminar series, discussion groups, panels, and social events.
