Position summary

Overview Have you ever wondered why higher education feels stuck? Or imagined rebuilding it from the ground up? Imagine a university where advanced technology enhances every student's learning experience through personalized, adaptive, and real-world education. San Francisco Bay University (SFBU), an innovative, nonprofit, WASC-accredited university located in the heart of Silicon Valley, seeks a forward-thinking and

experienced Online Adjunct Professor of Accounting to teach ACC 501 Managerial Accounting within our MBA Pro+ program. Working alongside innovative instructional technologies, including artificial intelligence-enhanced learning tools, the faculty member will guide graduate students through engaging asynchronous learning experiences while providing the mentorship, coaching, and academic oversight that only expert

faculty can deliver. The MBA Pro+ program prepares current and aspiring business leaders with the financial and managerial knowledge needed to succeed in today's technology-driven global economy. Accounting serves as the foundation for sound business decision-making, while emerging technologies and artificial intelligence continue to transform financial reporting, auditing, compliance, and business analytics.

Students learn to interpret financial statements, apply accounting principles, evaluate financial performance, analyze managerial accounting information, understand auditing and regulatory requirements, and leverage modern accounting technologies to solve complex business challenges. Through executive case studies, simulations, and applied consulting projects, graduates develop the analytical, ethical, and strategic

skills necessary to support organizational accountability and long-term success. What You Bring We are looking for educators who are passionate about graduate business education and committed to preparing the next generation of accounting professionals. Ideal candidates have experience designing engaging, student-centered learning experiences that foster analytical thinking, ethical decision-making, and practical

application of accounting principles. Candidates should be interested in exploring how artificial intelligence and emerging technologies are reshaping accounting practice and business education and be excited to contribute to a culture of continuous innovation within the MBA Pro+ program.
