Department: Accountancy Location: Belknap Campus Time Type: Full time Worker Type: Regular Job Req ID: R109474 Position Description: The School of Accountancy (SOA) in the College of Business at the University of Louisville (UofL) invites applications and nominations for the Anderson Endowed Chair in Accountancy at the rank of Professor with tenure. The Anderson Endowed Chair will be an exceptional scholar-teacher

who will contribute to the SOA’s scholarly excellence, educational mission, and national profile. The successful candidate will have the opportunity to help shape the School of Accountancy’s next stage of scholarly and educational development. Working collaboratively with the School Director and faculty, the Anderson Chair will provide intellectual leadership to strengthen the School’s research culture, mentor and

develop faculty talent, advance curricular innovation, and expand the School’s national visibility. Key Responsibiliti es Scholarly Leadership: Maintain an active, high-impact research agenda with regular publication in leading accounting journals. Teaching Excellence: Deliver high-quality instruction primarily through face-to-face undergraduate and graduate courses, including courses in the BSBA in Accountancy and

Master of Science in Accounting and Analytics (MSAA) programs. The expected teaching load is 9 credit hours per academic year. Faculty Mentorship: Actively mentor junior faculty and support their development toward tenure, promotion, and sustained scholarly productivity. Service: Contribute to curriculum development, assurance of learning processes, college and university committees, and broader academic and

professional service. Required Qualifications Earned doctoral degree (Ph.D.) in accounting from an AACSB-accredited institution. Qualifications commensurate with appointment at the rank of Professor with tenure at the time of hire. A sustained and distinguished record of publication in leading accounting and business academic journals. Evidence of an active research agenda with the potential for continued scholarly

productivity and impact. Demonstrated excellence in teaching at the undergraduate and/or graduate level. Proven track record of mentoring junior faculty and supporting their professional development toward tenure and promotion. Ability to work collegially and collaboratively across academic and professional communities. Preferred Qualifications A research and teaching specialization in financial accounting,
