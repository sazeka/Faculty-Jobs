Position description The Department of Neurology, Division of Neuropsychology, invites applications for a position in clinical neuropsychology. The position will be at the rank of Assistant or Associate Professor in the Department of Neurology, School of Medicine, University of California, Irvine. The

Division of Neuropsychology consists of four adult neuropsychologists, who conduct neuropsychological evaluations of adults with a wide range of neurological conditions. Ideal candidates for this Clinical Faculty position will be interested in working with adults with diverse conditions, including but not limited to: memory disorders, movement disorders (including DBS evaluations), stroke, traumatic brain injury,

oncology, multiple sclerosis, epilepsy, and psychiatric conditions. This position is ideal for a generalist, who can provide evaluations of a wide variety of disorders without specialized focused in any one particular area or condition. Clinics are located in Orange and Irvine and this position will primarily involve working out of UCI Medical Center in Orange. Qualifications include a PhD or PsyD in Clinical

Psychology and completion of a two-year postdoctoral fellowship in neuropsychology. Candidate must be eligible for California licensure or licensed to practice in the state of California. Candidates should have experience conducting neuropsychological assessments of adults. In addition to direct patient assessment, this position includes supervision of neuropsychology practicum students and postdoctoral fellows.
