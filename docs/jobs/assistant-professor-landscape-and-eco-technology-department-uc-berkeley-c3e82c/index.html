Position description The Department of Landscape Architecture and Environmental Planning (LAEP) at UC Berkeley seeks to fill a tenure-track position at the Assistant Professor level.

The successful candidate is expected to contribute to contemporary design discourse on “Landscape and Eco-Technology” through disciplinary engagement, teaching, research and/or practice. This position will allow the department to carry forward its legacy of leadership in service of the State of California, the nation, and the wider world. It also will further the department’s disciplinary knowledge on public

infrastructure through teaching, design, and/or research in material, vegetal, topographical, and hydrologic landscape systems. We seek a colleague whose approach encompasses cultural meaning and critical discourse as well as performance-based assessment and design engagement. The Department of Landscape Architecture and Environmental Planning at UC Berkeley is California’s flagship program. As an accredited

professional program in an R1 institution, the department expects its faculty to carry out research and/or creative practice that is recognized nationally and internationally and to support pedagogy that critically advances the role of Eco-Technology in contemporary design, praxis, and landscape performance. Landscape architecture has a long and distinguished history at Berkeley, starting in 1913 and boasting a

renowned cast of alumni and faculty. Today, we offer degree programs at the undergraduate, Masters, and PhD levels. As one of the founding departments in the College of Environmental Design, established in 1959, interdisciplinarity is part of our ethos and public engagement of our mission. Building on the legacy of environmental design, we tackle contemporary challenges from climate adaptation to societal crises
