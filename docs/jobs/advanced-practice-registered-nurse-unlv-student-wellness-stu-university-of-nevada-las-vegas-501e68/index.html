applications for *REVISED* Advanced Practice Registered Nurse, UNLV Student Wellness, Student Health and Faculty Staff Treatment [R0148940]*REVISED* ROLE of the POSITIONThe Advanced Practice Registered Nurse (APRN) serves as a clinical healthcare provider for a diverse population on UNLV’s Maryland Parkway campus and if indicated, at the Las Vegas Medical District. As a provider in the Student Health Center (SHC) and

Faculty and Staff Treatment (FAST) Center, the APRN acts as a healthcare team member and provides direct patient care, in person or via telehealth, to students, faculty, staff, and dependents, including initiation of treatment, evaluation of responses to treatments, referral to appropriate specialists when indicated, and accurate and timely documentation of all findings concerning the patient in the electronic health

record (EHR). The APRN acknowledges the unique characteristics of each patient and provides respectful, culturally competent, and professional clinical services. This APRN position supports the health and mental health of UNLV students and employees, thereby allowing them to succeed in their academic pursuits and/or employment. The SHC and FAST Center are accredited by the Accreditation Association for Ambulatory

Health Care (AAAHC) and are part of the Student Wellness Unit at UNLV, which includes the Student Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS), Care Center, Wellness Promotion, Disability Resource Center,*REVISED* MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONSThis position requires a Master’s degree from an accredited college or university as recognized by the United States Department of Education and/or the Council on Higher Education
