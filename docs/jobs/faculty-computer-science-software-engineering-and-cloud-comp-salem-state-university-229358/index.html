Faculty: Computer Science, (Software Engineering and Cloud Computing), Full-Time, Tenure-Track 493094 140 - Computer Science Dept Faculty: Tenured Tenure-Track On-site $80,000 - $85,000 Overview The Department of Computer Science at Salem State University invites applications for a full-time, tenure-track faculty position at the open rank, beginning in Fall 2027. We particularly welcome candidates with expertise in

one or more of the following areas: cloud computing, software engineering, and databases. The successful candidate will demonstrate a forward-looking vision for the computing field, a strong commitment to effective teaching and student learning in a four-year undergraduate program, and excellent interpersonal and communication skills. Teaching responsibilities will include introductory, intermediate, and advanced

courses in computer science and information technology, including courses within one or more of the preferred areas of specialization. The candidate will also be expected to contribute to departmental and university service and to maintain an active program of scholarly or professional development appropriate to the position and rank. Salem State University is where scholarship meets purpose. As the Commonwealth's

Civic Engagement University, we believe education matters beyond the classroom, and our faculty are central to that mission. They bring intellectual curiosity and a genuine commitment to student success, teaching students who will go on to shape their communities, many of whom are the first in their families to earn a college degree. We value collaboration, academic rigor, and the kind of civic-minded engagement that

makes a real difference. If you're driven by meaningful work and want to be part of something bigger than any single course or semester, Salem State is your place. Duties and Responsibilities Candidates are required to perform duties in accordance with the MSCA contract. Duties include teaching, advising, scholarship, and contributions to the continuous improvement of the department and university. The standard

teaching load is 12 credit hours per semester. The position is subject to available funding. Required Qualifications: PhD in computer science or a closely related field. ABD with a master’s degree in computer science or a closely related field are welcome to apply. Conferral is required by May 2027. Experience in teaching introductory, intermediate-level, and advanced-level computer science and information technology
