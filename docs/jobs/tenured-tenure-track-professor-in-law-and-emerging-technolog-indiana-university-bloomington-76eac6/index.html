University Maurer School of Law in Bloomington is seeking outstanding lateral tenured or tenure-track applicants in the regulation of artificial intelligence, robotics, and other emerging technologies. The position is part of Indiana University’s Faculty 100 (F100) initiative. The initiative is a campus-wide investment to recruit 100 new tenured/tenure-track faculty whose work strengthens IU’s research capacity in

strategically important and rapidly growing areas. IU Bloomington has significant cross-campus strength in technology, informatics, business, labor, public affairs, ethics, and social science. The strongest candidate would be a scholar with a record or emerging agenda in one or more of the following areas: law and artificial intelligence, responsible AI, robotics, workforce transformation, human-centered technology,

public-sector innovation, and technology’s social impacts. A law scholar in this area could contribute regulatory analysis, institutional design, and policy-relevant research pertaining to labor-market governance, tax and public-finance analysis, law and political economy, and/or normative frameworks for evaluating the distributional effects of technological change. We encourage applications from scholars who apply

interdisciplinary perspectives and share important research overlap with current IUB scholars, while also offering new dimensions that will expand and strengthen the IU’s broader AI and emerging technologies research program.
