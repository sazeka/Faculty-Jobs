Position summary

Assistant/Associate Professor — Master of Physician Assistant Studies Program Murphy Deming College of Health Sciences at Mary Baldwin University invites applications for a 12-month full-time, renewable (Clinical or Scholar Pathway) Physician Assistant Faculty position beginning in September 2026. Position Summary: The Physician Assistant (MSPA) Program is fully accredited, and the dynamic faculty teaches within a

unique interprofessional curriculum. We invite individuals with exceptional skills and experience in contemporary medical practice, teaching, and administration to apply. Primary responsibilities include guiding students through the PA program through teaching, advising, and mentorship. Teaching load will be determined according to expertise and program/ college needs. Candidates should be dynamic and dedicated

educators who will facilitate classroom and applied learning activities while fostering an inclusive and caring learning environment. Additional responsibilities include academic advising, university, community, and professional service, and supporting the interprofessional education curriculum. Required Qualifications Master’s degree required. Must possess an unencumbered PA, NP, DO, or MD license to practice in the

Commonwealth of Virginia.
