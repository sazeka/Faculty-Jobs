JOB SUMMARY The College of Agriculture, Family Sciences and Technology (CAFST) seeks an energetic and student-focused faculty member with expertise in Agricultural Engineering Technology (AET)

for a 9-month, tenure-track Assistant Professor position. This is a dual appointment shared between the Departments of Agricultural Sciences (25% teaching) and Agricultural Research (75%). The successful candidate will be responsible for teaching undergraduate and graduate courses in Agricultural Engineering Technology as determined by departmental needs. Specific teaching assignments will be made by the Department

Chair. The faculty member will also plan an important role in student advising and mentorship, including providing guidance and support to related student organizations. The successful candidate will assist in the recruitment of students into the Agricultural Engineering Technology program. The position includes a strong research expectation. The successful candidate will be expected to develop a robust research

program in discipline and collaborate with colleagues in the Cooperative Extension program.
