description UCSF School of Medicine and UCSF Benioff Children's Hospitals Department of Pediatrics HS Clinical Faculty The Department of Pediatrics at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF), and UCSF Benioff Children’s Hospitals (BCH) are recruiting for the next Executive Medical Director of Quality & Safety (EMD for Q&S). The successful candidate will be appointed at the Assistant, Associate, or Full

Professor rank in the Health Sciences (HS) Clinical faculty series, commensurate with experience. The selected candidate will serve as the Executive Medical Director of Quality & Safety for UCSF Benioff Children’s Hospitals, the senior clinical executive responsible for designing and advancing a unified, high-reliability pediatric quality and safety strategy across BCH. The EMD for Q&S will integrate deep pediatric

clinical expertise with strategic, system-level leadership to advance equitable, high-value care, reduce preventable harm, and strengthen a culture of safety and continuous improvement across all BCH sites. The EMD for Q&S will partner closely with BCH executive leadership and leaders across UCSF Health and the UCSF School of Medicine to translate institutional priorities into measurable outcomes and ensure the

consistent delivery of excellent, patient-centered, and equitable care across the pediatric enterprise. The selected candidate will join a major academic medical center and established training program and will actively contribute to the Department of Pediatrics’ mission of excellence in research, education, and patient care. The position may include clinical responsibilities at both the San Francisco and Oakland

campuses. Strategic Leadership & Impact The EMD provides enterprise‑level vision and direction for BCH’s quality and safety agenda, ensuring alignment with UCSF Health’s strategic goals and national pediatric standards.
