The department offers majors and minors in Management Information Systems as well as Finance, Risk Management and a minor in Economics. While being the second

largest university in Houston, the University of Houston-Downtown (UHD) provides life-changing educational access and opportunities to individuals living in one of the most diverse metropolitan areas in Texas. The ideal candidate should be student-centered, utilizing pedagogical approaches that emphasize practical application. discipline and a willingness to work with others in promoting the advancement of the university including serving on department, college, and university-wide committees as well as to the profession and the community. Minimum Qualifications: Applicants for the Assistant Professor position should have a terminal degree in Management Information Systems or related field from an AACSB accredited college or should be

Demonstrated quality teaching at the college level that recognizes and adopts best practices in pedagogy and embraces a desire to serve the needs of a mobile student body. Demonstrated research ability commensurate with the timeline of obtaining a terminal
