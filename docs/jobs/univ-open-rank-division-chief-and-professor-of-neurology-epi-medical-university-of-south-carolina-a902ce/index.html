Position summary

Job Description Summary The Department of Neurology at the Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC) seeks an accomplished, board-certified neurologist with fellowship training in Epilepsy to serve as Division Chief of Epilepsy at the rank of Professor of Neurology. The successful candidate will provide strategic, academic, clinical, and administrative leadership for a well-established and growing epilepsy program

within a nationally recognized academic medical center. The Division Chief will be responsible for defining and executing the Division’s strategic direction, expanding clinical programs and patient access, recruiting and developing faculty, strengthening education and research, and ensuring the long-term operational and financial sustainability of the Division. The successful candidate will maintain an appropriate

clinical practice and scholarly portfolio while fostering a collaborative, accountable, and inclusive culture across the Division and its multidisciplinary partners.
