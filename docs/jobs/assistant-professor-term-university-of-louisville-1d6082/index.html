Department: Computer Science & Engineering Location: Belknap Campus Time Type: Full time Worker Type: Regular Term (Fixed Term) Job Req ID: R108898 Position Description: The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Louisville invites applications for a full-time Teaching Assistant Professor (Term) position (non-tenure track), beginning in Spring 2027. This position offers an exciting

opportunity to join a dynamic, student-focused department within a thriving urban research university. Ideal candidates will hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a closely related field; demonstrate a strong foundation in artificial intelligence and one or more core areas of computer science or computer engineering; possess outstanding communication skills; and show a passion for making

meaningful, positive contributions to students’ educational experience. Relevant research, industry, or professional experience in AI, computer science, or computer engineering is preferred, as is an undergraduate degree in one of these fields. Because this is a teaching-focused position, candidates should be prepared to teach under-graduate and graduate courses in artificial intelligence as well as core areas of

computer science and engineering. Courses may include, but are not limited to: Artificial Intelligence, AI Literacy/Fluency, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Python Programming, Java/OOP, Software Engineering, Computer Networks, Computer Architecture and Organization, C/C++, Algorithms, Data Structures, Cybersecurity, Embedded Systems, Data-bases, and Operating Systems. The successful candidate will play an important

role in expanding the department's rapidly growing AI curriculum, including AI literacy and fluency, hands-on AI tools, machine learning, generative AI, and other AI-related courses, while also contributing to instruction in core computer science and engineering areas as needed. The University of Louisville is a Carnegie R1 research institution and a nationally recognized Metropolitan Research University located in

Kentucky’s largest and most diverse city. Louisville has a metropolitan population of over 1.2 million and is within a two-hour drive of Cincinnati, Indianapolis, and Nashville. The Department of Computer Science and Engineering is strongly committed to excellence in teaching, research, and service. Faculty enjoy opportunities for cross-disciplinary collaboration, engagement with industry partners, and participation
