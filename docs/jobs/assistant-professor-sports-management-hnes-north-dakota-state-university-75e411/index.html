prioritizes experiential learning through national trips and active learning in the classroom. The Sport Management program has established industry partnerships connecting students with working professionals. The HNES department is housed within the College of Health and Human Sciences and includes three undergraduate

programs, four graduate programs, and 15 faculty members.
