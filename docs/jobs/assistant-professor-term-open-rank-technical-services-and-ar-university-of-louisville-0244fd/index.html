Assistant Professor Term (Open Rank) - Technical Services and Archives Cataloger

University of Louisville  ·  Technical Services and Archives Cataloger  ·  Health Sciences Center, KY
Assistant ProfessorTechnical Services and Archives Cataloger

Position summary

Department: Location: Health Sciences Center Time Type: Full time Worker Type: Regular Term (Fixed Term) Job Req ID: R109285 Position Description: The University of Louisville seeks an energetic, service-oriented librarian who is skilled and interested in technical services and archives within an academic library setting. Reporting to the Associate Director/Collections, the position is responsible for cataloging

materials in all formats for the Kornhauser Health Sciences Library/Medical Archives according to national and local standards, authority control, materials processing, and management and maintenance of the library’s online catalog. This position also assists with the management and organization of the Medical Archive collection, develops and implements ongoing metadata updates, performs clean-up projects, and makes

improvements to the integrated library system. Primary Responsibilities Perform original and copy cataloging for library materials in all formats. Apply cataloging standards including Resource Description and Access (RDA), Anglo-American Cataloging Rules (AACR2), MARC21, Library of Congress Subject Headings (LCSH), Medical Subject Headings (MeSH), Library of Congress and NLM Classification, and OCLC MARC format.

Oversee and create records in OCLC WMS and DOCLINE (NLM) to ensure data accuracy for all print and electronic resources. Verify discrepancies between DOCLINE and OCLC WMS holdings information, ensuring data consistency and reliability. Utilize and assist with updating and maintaining technical services cataloging procedures. Assist with management and organization of the Medical Archive collection. Aid in making

Archives accessible to the University of Louisville, the greater Louisville community, and other external users. Act as the primary liaison for library donations. Work within a team environment in support of departmental projects and the University Libraries’ strategic plan. Strengthen partnerships between the library and other campus units through participation in student success related activities. Engage in

research, scholarly activity, and service to the university, community, and profession. Required Qualifications Master’s degree in library/information science from an ALA-accredited program (or international equivalent). Experience cataloging, with familiarity with standard tools such as RDA, AACR2, MARC21, MeSH, LCSH, and NLM Classification. Experience working in academic libraries, archives, museums, or cultural
