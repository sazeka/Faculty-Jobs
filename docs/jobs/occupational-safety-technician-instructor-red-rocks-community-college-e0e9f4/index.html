Job Summary: The part-time Occupational Safety (OSH) Instructor is responsible for providing high-quality instruction within the Occupational Safety program at Red Rocks Community College. This position primarily teaches OSHA 30-Hour Construction Industry

Standards (OSH 1311) and related occupational safety courses that prepare students for safe work practices and employment in construction and other skilled trades industries. Under the direction of the Director of Skilled Trades, the instructor delivers classroom, hybrid, online, and field-based instruction in accordance with OSHA Outreach Training Program requirements, approved curriculum, established course

outcomes, Colorado Community College System and Red Rocks Community College policies and procedures, Colorado Career and Technical Education standards, and current industry practices. The instructor fosters an inclusive learning environment that promotes student engagement, workplace safety, regulatory compliance, and workforce readiness. Responsibilities include preparing and delivering instruction; assessing and

evaluating student learning; maintaining accurate academic records; providing timely feedback and final grades; utilizing the College's learning management system and instructional technologies; and ensuring course materials and syllabi meet institutional requirements. The instructor collaborates with Skilled Trades leadership to promote consistent course delivery, continuous program improvement, and student success
