Position summary

Automation & Advanced Manufacturing Instructor Primary Purpose: Provide high-quality instruction and workforce training in automation and advanced manufacturing, with primary responsibility for training in battery construction and assembly and the automated manufacturing of parts. The instructor will develop and deliver classroom, laboratory, and hands-on learning experiences that prepare students and incumbent

workers for modern manufacturing environments supporting regional industry partners. Qualifications: Education Associate degree in Advanced Manufacturing, Automation, Mechatronics, Industrial Technology, Engineering Technology, Electronics Technology, or a closely related field required. A bachelor’s degree in a related field is preferred. Relevant industry certifications in manufacturing, automation, robotics, PLCs,

CNC, additive manufacturing, safety, quality, or Lean/Six Sigma are preferred. Special Knowledge/Skills Knowledge of automated manufacturing systems, industrial controls, robotics, programmable logic controllers, CNC machining, additive manufacturing, and production processes. Knowledge of battery components, battery construction, battery pack assembly, manufacturing safety, quality control, and inspection practices.

Ability to operate, demonstrate, maintain, and troubleshoot manufacturing and automation equipment safely. Ability to develop competency-based curriculum, laboratory activities, assessments, and instructional materials. Excellent oral and written communication skills and the ability to explain technical concepts to students and incumbent workers with varied experience levels. Ability to use instructional technology,

manufacturing software, and general computer applications. Strong organizational, planning, recordkeeping, and problem-solving skills. Ability to work effectively with college personnel, students, grant stakeholders, employers, and community partners. Experience Five years of directly related, current work experience in advanced manufacturing, industrial automation, robotics, battery production, mechatronics,

machining, industrial maintenance, or a related technical field. Previous teaching, technical training, apprenticeship, or workforce development experience is preferred. Experience in defense, aerospace, automotive, energy storage, battery manufacturing, or another advanced manufacturing environment is preferred.
