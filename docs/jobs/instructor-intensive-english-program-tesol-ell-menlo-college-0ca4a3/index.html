Intensive English Program / TESOL / ELL Instruction Menlo College is seeking an adjunct instructor to teach English language learners within its Intensive English Program. To be considered for a teaching position, a candidate must meet the following qualifications: Have at least a master’s degree from an accredited institution of higher learning in English or TESOL or a related discipline Demonstrate previous

college-level experience working with English language learners and have knowledge of the concepts and practices of TESOL Be committed to providing excellence in education and fostering a dynamic and welcoming learning environment Have the ability to work closely with a diverse student body and the IEP team of instructors About Menlo College Menlo College is a diverse and tight-knit community of exceptional, involved

people. Our mission is to: “…ignite potential and educate students to make meaningful contributions in the innovation economy.” We attract students from all over the world who are committed to learning, playing intercollegiate sports, starting and leading organizations, and making an impact in their communities. Continuing an unbroken streak for more than a decade, Menlo College was named to the most recent Princeton

Review “Best Colleges” list, recognition given to only 12% of all colleges and universities in the U.S. Menlo College was also ranked #11 in Money for Best Business Colleges in the U.S. Menlo College is designated as both a Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) and an Asian American and Native American, Pacific Islander-Serving Institution (AANAPISI). Only 1% of all colleges and universities in the U.S. achieve this

dual recognition. Our campus is located 30 miles south of San Francisco, in Silicon Valley – one of the world’s most entrepreneurial and innovative locations. Silicon Valley is renowned as an innovation incubator with exceptional opportunities for experience-based learning. Menlo College’s School of Business offers bachelor’s degree programs, ten majors, and two graduate degree programs. Menlo College is accredited

by AACSB and WSCUC. Application Process Applications will be screened by the Office of Academic Affairs, and qualified applicants who best meet the needs of the College will be contacted for an interview.
