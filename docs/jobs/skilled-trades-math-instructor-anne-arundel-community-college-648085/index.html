Mornings Position Type: Hourly and Temporary, Exempt Position Summary Anne Arundel Community College is seeking a part-time Skilled Trades Math Instructor to support students in the Mechanical Technology training program. approach that connects directly to electrical and mechanical trades. Working in collaboration with the trades instructor, the math instructor will help align instruction with program outcomes, reinforce technical skills, and support student success in an Integrated Education and Training (IET) model.Course content is housed in Canvas, and instructors are expected to use the learning management system to deliver

instruction, communicate with students, and support an engaging online learning environment. Candidates invited for an interview will be asked to provide a short teaching demonstration.
