Positions College of Allied Health - CFIT. Seeking qualified applicants for a 10-month principal faculty position for the Harding department of Cardiac Function & Interventional Technology (CFIT); rank will be commensurate with applicant experience. Responsibilities will include working with existing faculty to develop cardiac device continuing education program, teach in the program's core on-ground, and on-line

curriculum, advising and mentoring students, assisting with the technology lab, conducting scholarly activity in the faculty's area of expertise, and engaging in service to the college, university, and community. Qualified applicants will be expected to have three plus years’ experience working with cardiac electrophysiology (EP) mapping systems, possess a minimum of a master's degree, a minimum of three years of

clinical experience; prior teaching experience and IBHRE certification is preferred. Interested individuals should apply online. Inquiries for additional information may be sent to Kenneth Turley, CFIT director and professor, krturley@harding.edu, or Box 12281, 915 E. Market Ave, Searcy, AR 72149-5615.
