Population Health PharmD Fellow/Instructor - School of Pharmacy

Medical College of Wisconsin  ·  School of Pharmacy  ·  Milwaukee, WI
InstructorPharmacy

Position summary

Position Summary: MCW School of Pharmacy is seeking applications for a Population Health Fellow. This individual will be appointed as a full-time, year-round non-tenure track Clinical Instructor. This position is a 1-year post-graduate opportunity, designed to prepare pharmacists for a career in population health management program design, implementation, and maintenance with an anticipated start date of 7/1/2027.

This position reports to the Population Health Fellowship Director in the School of Pharmacy. Individuals that have completed PGY1 or PGY2 training program are encouraged to apply. The training year will consist of approximately 40% clinical practice in Population Health Management at Froedtert & 60% scholarship, teaching, and other academic-related duties. The fellow will have a longitudinal academia pharmacy

experience, exposing them to a variety of teaching responsibilities, including lecturing, facilitating small group-based learning, course coordination and student assessment. Highlights of the training program include working with a grant implementation team on the design, implementation, clinical practice, and continuous quality improvement of a population health management program in hypertension. At the end of the

training program, academic fellows will be well prepared for a career in population health management in an academic medical center.
