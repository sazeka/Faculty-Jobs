Job Details: The McAfee School of Theology is searching for a faculty of New Testament Studies on the Atlanta, Georgia campus. Responsibilities : McAfee offers courses in fully residential, concurrent learning, and

fully online formats in its degree programs that include MDiv, MACM, MTS, and DMin. serve on McAfee and University committees as assigned by the Dean. Qualifications : Applicants must have a PhD from an accredited college/university in a related field with a concentration in New Testament Studies by the start of the appointment.
