Asst Professor of Sculpture 510136 Amherst, Massachusetts, United States College of Humanities & Fine Arts Full Time On Site Tenure Track Faculty Opening on: Oct 28 2026 Closing on: Jan 28 2027 Title: Asst Professor of Sculpture Executive Area: Academic Affairs College/School/MBU: Humanities & Fine Arts Department: Art Work Location: Amherst Schedule: Full Time Work Arrangement: Onsite Job Summary The Department of

Art at the University of Massachusetts Amherst invites applications for a full-time tenure-track Assistant Professor of Sculpture to begin September 1, 2027. Essential Functions The successful candidate will lead the department's sculpture and 3D program, demonstrate an active and evolving creative practice, advise students, serve on BFA and MFA thesis committees, and provide service to the department, university,

and community. Additionally, the position requires maintaining safe and compliant operation of sculpture facilities in collaboration with Environmental Health and Safety. Teaching responsibilities will include Introduction to Sculpture, Moldmaking, and electives serving the Sculpture area or additional courses serving the broader department, such as Professional Practices and Graduate Seminar. The position also

offers an opportunity to expand instruction in the growing demand for design-oriented applications of sculptural production methods, such as prototyping and fabrication. Successful candidates must be able to maintain, operate, and provide instruction in the woodshop, digital fabrication (laser cutter and CNC router), moldmaking, and metalwork areas. The teaching load consists of four courses per academic year. The

candidate must maintain an active exhibition and research record commensurate with expectations at an R1 research university, with clear potential for continued artistic growth and recognition at regional, national, and international levels. A commitment to the academic success of all students and strong departmental citizenship is essential to a successful candidacy. Minimum Qualifications MFA or similar terminal

degree in Art, Sculpture, or related field from an accredited institution.At least 2 years of experience teaching college-level courses beyond graduate school. Demonstrated record or strong potential for creative scholarship appropriate to a tenure-track appointment, including an active and ongoing studio practice and a record of exhibition or other professional creative activity.
