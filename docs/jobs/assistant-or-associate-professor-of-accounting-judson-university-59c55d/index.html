Assistant or Associate Professor of Accounting

Judson University  ·  Accounting  ·  Elgin, IL
Assistant ProfessorAssociate ProfessorAccounting

Position summary

Job Summary A full-time Assistant or Associate Professor level position in Accounting, beginning in August 2026. The faculty member will teach in the Department of Business. Primary Responsibilities Teach a full course-load in undergraduate accounting including Cost Accounting, Intermediate Accounting, Managerial Accounting, Individual and Corporate Tax Accounting, and Auditing Assist Advisees in registering for

courses and serve on committees as assigned. Provide ongoing consultation to the Registrar’s Office regarding student placement and course transfer equivalencies. Maintain ongoing assessment of the Business major and of the service courses in accounting in consultation with other disciplinary areas and the General Education program. Qualifications An earned Masters in Accounting, MBA or higher. CPA preferred.

Demonstrate an active and growing Christian faith Commitment to a Christian liberal arts education with an ability to integrate faith in the discipline in an engaging and compelling manner Possess collaborative skills to work with colleagues across the Business Department and throughout the university. Teaching will be in-person on the Judson’s Elgin campus, so candidate must be within commutable distance to Judson.

Salary/Benefits The University offers a comprehensive benefits package, including medical, dental, and retirement plans, a tuition waiver program, as well as opportunities for professional development and growth. Salary range: $55,000 – $70,000, with room for negotiations. Rank and salary based on qualifications, education, and work experience. Timeline Review of applications will begin immediately. The position will

remain open until filled. The appointment will begin August 1, 2026.
