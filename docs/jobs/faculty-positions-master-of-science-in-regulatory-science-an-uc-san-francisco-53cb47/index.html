description Faculty Positions – Master of Science in Regulatory Science and Digital Innovation (RSDI) Department of Bioengineering and Therapeutic Sciences School of Pharmacy University of California, San Francisco The Department of Bioengineering and Therapeutic Sciences (BTS) at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) invites applications for full-time faculty positions in the Adjunct Professor series at

the Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor rank. These positions will support the newly established Master of Science in Regulatory Science and Digital Innovation (RSDI), an innovative graduate program launching in Summer 2027. The program is designed to prepare future leaders in regulatory science, digital health, artificial intelligence, medical product development, and global regulatory strategy through two

academically equivalent pathways: a full-time Foundational Track and a hybrid Executive Track. The RSDI program was developed in collaboration with FDA scientists, industry leaders, and UCSF faculty and represents a major institutional investment in the future of regulatory science education. We seek candidates with expertise in one or more of the following areas: Product Development, Entrepreneurship, and Leadership

Digital Regulatory Science, including AI/ML-enabled medical products, Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), digital health, regulatory informatics, and real-world evidence Global Regulatory Strategy and Quality Systems, including multinational regulatory submissions, international harmonization frameworks, the WHO prequalification program, and quality management systems. Successful candidates will contribute to

graduate education through course leadership, curriculum development, academic advising, and program service. Faculty members will serve as course directors, supervise capstone projects and master's theses, participate in admissions and curriculum committees, and collaborate with academic, industry, healthcare, and regulatory partners.
