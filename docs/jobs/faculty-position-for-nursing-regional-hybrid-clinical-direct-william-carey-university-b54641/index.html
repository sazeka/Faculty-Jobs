Faculty Position for Nursing, Regional Hybrid Clinical Director

William Carey University  ·  Regional Hybrid Clinical Director  ·  William Carey University, MS
Nursing

Position summary

Job Summary The Regional Hybrid Clinical Director oversees the clinical components of the hybrid nursing curriculum for the assigned region. This role provides leadership and operational support to faculty teaching hybrid clinical courses, coordinates clinical placements for students, and seeks new clinical agency partnerships within the assigned region. The Regional Hybrid Clinical Director is responsible for

evaluating clinical faculty performance within their assigned region, ensuring effective instruction, and promoting continuous improvement. The role requires travel to remote clinical sites as assigned. Duties and Responsibilities This job description does not state or imply that these are the only duties to be performed by the employee occupying this position. Duties and responsibilities listed are essential job

functions and exclude functions that are incidental to the performance of fundamental job duties. Assigned Region Meet with faculty of record for each hybrid clinical courses to plan clinical calendar and experiences. Facilitate student clinical orientations including the technology requirements for the facility and providing student assistance when technical problems occur. Regularly evaluate the performance of

clinical faculty through clinical observations, student feedback, and peer reviews. Routinely travel to clinical sites in assigned region. Collaborate with the School of Nursing leadership to develop and implement strategic recruitment efforts for clinical faculty positions of assigned region. Assist in the onboarding process for new hybrid clinical faculty, ensuring they are adequately oriented to the program and

institutional policies of assigned region. Lead targeted recruitment events and efforts in assigned region for the COHS and Hybrid Nursing Programs. Identify and develop new clinical partnerships to enhance student learning opportunities in assigned region. Evaluate clinical sites to confirm student learning opportunities, ensuring the quality of patient care and educational experiences of assigned region. Cultivate

and maintain relationships with clinical agencies, negotiating memoranda of understanding (MOUs) and contracts as necessary in assigned region.
