TELL Instructor Onboarding & Training Coordinator

Martin Luther College  ·  New Ulm, MN
InstructorInstructional Design and Technology

Position summary

SUMMARY Own the process of recruiting and preparing TELL Network instructors to teach confidently and consistently while assisting with student and class administration as needed. This position takes prospective instructors from initial interest through screening, approval handoff, onboarding, training, technical readiness, and ongoing support. The position also supports communication and administrative workflow

between students and instructors to help classes operate efficiently. This role maintains instructor-facing content and digital resources by updating training presentations, instructional materials, and selected website content as program needs evolve while supporting student operations. The result is a dependable pool of trained instructors and well-supported students progressing through the TELL program toward

church planting. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Instructor Recruitment and Onboarding Recruit prospective TELL Network English instructors through approved campaigns, ministry networks, referrals, and follow-up activities. Serve as the primary contact for prospective and newly approved instructors throughout recruitment and onboarding. Conduct initial screening and route candidates through the established

approval process; clearly document who has been approved to proceed. Guide approved instructors through a consistent onboarding process covering the TELL Network program, student path, LIVE Class expectations, instructor responsibilities, and communication standards. Maintain an onboarding checklist and track each instructor from initial interest through ready-to-teach status. Confirm when an instructor has completed

readiness requirements and communicate that status promptly to the Student Operations & Scheduling Coordinator. Training and Teaching Readiness Train instructors in Zoom, class-flow expectations, WhatsApp communication, attendance procedures, student follow-up, safeguarding of student information, and other tools or processes used in LIVE Classes. Maintain and improve instructor training materials, quick-reference

guides, checklists, FAQs, and other support resources. Update instructor-facing PowerPoint presentations and other digital teaching resources to reflect approved curriculum, process, and program changes. Make routine content updates to TELL Network website pages related to instructor resources, training, events, and other assigned program information; coordinate platform, design, integration, or significant website
