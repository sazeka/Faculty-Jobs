Atlanta’s John Marshall Law School Welcomes Alicia Hughes as Associate Dean of Student Affairs

Atlanta's John Marshall Law School  ·  Atlanta, GA
Facultynull

Position summary

September 11, 2026 Atlanta’s John Marshall Law School (AJMLS) is pleased to welcome Alicia Hughes as the new Associate Dean of Student Affairs. As an attorney, educator, higher education leader, and former public official, Hughes brings a distinguished career spanning legal practice, public service, academia, and student development. Dean Hughes will work directly with Dr. Harrison, who has entered her new role as

the Senior Associate Dean and Chief of Student Affairs at AJMLS. Hughes began her academic career at Texas Southern University, where she earned a B.S. in Biology with dual minors in Chemistry and Biomedical Engineering. As an undergraduate, she was a Douglass Honors Institute Scholar, international champion debater, Student Government Association President, and National Institutes of Health undergraduate researcher

at Baylor College of Medicine. She later earned her Juris Doctor from the University of Miami School of Law, where she held several leadership and academic positions, including serving as President of the Black Law Students Association. Her career has since taken her across law, government, public service, and higher education. After practicing law and completing a federal judicial clerkship with the U.S. District

Court for the Middle District of Alabama, Hughes went on to serve as a congressional staffer, elected city councilor, governor-appointed commissioner, and Chief Policy Advisor of the National Bar Association. Hughes has also built a significant career in legal education. She has taught at the University of Texas at Austin, Widener University’s Delaware Law School, Boston University School of Law, and Emory University

School of Law, where she primarily taught and advised students in patent law. At Emory, she also served as the inaugural Executive Director of the Center on Civil Rights and Social Justice. Her academic and leadership experience includes fellowships with the University of Virginia’s Sorensen Institute for Political Leadership and the Aspen Institute’s Rodel Fellowship in Public Leadership. She has also completed

doctoral coursework toward an Executive Ed.D. in Higher Education Management at the University of Pennsylvania. Now at AJMLS, Hughes is focused on building upon the strong foundation already established within Student Affairs while helping students navigate the many dimensions of their law school experience.
