Assistant Professor, Clinical Psychology R114205 Main Campus, Orlando, Florida Faculty Full time Closing at: Nov 21 2026 - 12:00am EST Psychology - Academic Instruction The OpportunityThe Department of Psychology in the College of Sciences (COS) at the University of Central Florida (UCF) invites applications for one full-time, 9-month, tenure track assistant professor position in Clinical Psychology, anticipated to

begin in August 2027. We seek dedicated and collegial educators/scientists who can establish collaborative relationships within the department/college as well as with other colleges at the university and within the community. The successful candidate will be expected to teach undergraduate and graduate courses in different modalities, including face-to-face and online. The instructional duties and services

assignments are in accordance with the department’s current equitable workload policy.Area of research specialization within health services psychology is open. We are interested in collaborative candidates with programs of research that can contribute to and complement program, department, and university strengths (see faculty areas of expertise here: https://sciences.ucf.edu/psychology/people/).The Department of

Psychology is one of the largest departments by enrollment at UCF, with more than 50 full-time faculty and approximately 4,000 undergraduate majors. The department has three broad areas of emphasis in clinical psychology, human factors and cognitive psychology, and industrial and organizational psychology. UCF’s online bachelor’s degree in Psychology is ranked #3 in the nation by U.S. News and World Report. All

members of the department share a common goal of understanding behavior through empirical investigation, which is reflected in teaching and research. The department is home to two terminal master's programs in Clinical Psychology and I/O Psychology, and three Ph.D. programs in Clinical Psychology, I/O Psychology, and Human Factors and Cognitive Psychology.The Clinical Psychology Ph.D. Program has maintained American

Psychological Association (APA) accreditation since 2003, and in 2023 received the maximum re-accreditation period of ten years. The program houses a training clinic, which serves the UCF and surrounding communities.To learn more about the Clinical Psychology program, please see our website at https://sciences.ucf.edu/psychology/graduate/ph-d-clinical/.
