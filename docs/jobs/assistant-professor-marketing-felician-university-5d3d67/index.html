Position summary

The School of Business and Information Sciences of Felician University is presently offering an exciting opportunity for an academically qualified, full-time Marketing faculty to develop and deliver undergraduate courses, integrating analytics and using simulations. The Assistant Professor will be responsible to teach, administer, and develop curriculum across its undergraduate and graduate business programs, and

will fulfill all responsibilities of a full-time faculty member in the areas of Teaching Effectiveness, Scholarship, and Service as outlined in the Felician University Faculty Handbook. Felician University is committed to maintaining a learning and employment environment based upon our core values of Respect for Human Dignity, Compassion, Transformation, Justice and Peace, and Solidarity with People in Need. We offer

a choice of medical, dental, vision, and life insurance benefits, as well as a retirement plan (403B), tuition remission, and generous time off benefits. engaging learning experiences across traditional, online and hybrid environments; ability and willingness to teach at off-sites campus sites as needed; demonstrated familiarity with assessment and willingness to participate in assessment training; comprehensive knowledge of marketing, data analytics, simulation software and emerging industry trends; ability to design and implement courses that integrate data
