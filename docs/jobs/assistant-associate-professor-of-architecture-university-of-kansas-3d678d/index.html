Assistant/Associate Professor of Architecture Position Overview The School of Architecture & Design at the University of Kansas invites applications for a tenure track or tenured position as Assistant or Associate Professor of Architecture, with an emphasis in Computational/Parametric Design and Advanced Manufacturing Technologies.The appointment is expected to begin in the spring semester of 2027.Architects are

increasingly expected to integrate advanced computational design technologies, digital workflows, and research into the design, coordination, fabrication, and performance of buildings. This expertise spans the integration of building systems and programs through computational and parametric design, artificial intelligence, building performance analysis, advanced digital fabrication, and industrialized construction

processes.This position will play a central role in developing and implementing coursework across the professional and graduate curricula in these areas, fostering externally funded research in computational design, digital fabrication, and emerging design technologies, and coordinating professional and academic programs that support certifications and advanced credentials in automated design processes and digital

practice.The successful candidate should demonstrate expertise in one or more of the following areas: Computational and parametric design, advanced digital fabrication, industrialized construction, building performance technologies, artificial intelligence integration in architectural design, or related emerging digital technologies that advance architectural practice and research.The faculty is committed to

excellence in applying its knowledge and experience to educating the next generation of architects. The Department seeks candidates who will contribute to this mission through innovative teaching, impactful scholarship, interdisciplinary collaboration, and engagement with the evolving profession.KU’s excellence is a result of the rich tapestry of experiences, perspectives, and backgrounds of our faculty, staff,

students, and colleagues from across our nation and the globe. At KU, we invest in continuous learning and growth by creating a climate where people engage in respectful dialogue and debate and support each other’s success.
