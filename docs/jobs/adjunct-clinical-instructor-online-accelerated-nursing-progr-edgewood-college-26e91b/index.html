Adjunct Clinical Instructor – Online Accelerated Nursing Program DESCRIPTION OF JOB: Edgewood University is seeking part-time Adjunct Clinical Instructors for our Online Accelerated Nursing Program. We are looking for individuals with expertise in one or more of the following areas: Medical-Surgical Nursing Maternal-Child Health Psychosocial/Behavioral Health Public Health Adult Health Perinatal & Pediatric Nursing

Pharmacology Leadership & Healthcare Organizations Evidence-Based Practice Responsibilities: Adjunct Clinical Instructors are nursing professionals with the responsibility of providing a quality learning experience for Edgewood University students with hospitals and clinical sites. The relationship of the adjunct faculty member to the student is one of teacher and facilitator of learning. Each adjunct is hired to

guide students and facilitate learning in the clinical setting. Supervise a group of 7 Edgewood ABSN students onsite in the clinical setting Clinical hours are typically 8-10 hours per day Create a supportive learning environment conducive to attainment of clinical objectives Demonstrate professionalism, integrity, and ethics in the clinical setting Promote evidence-based practice Evaluate students’ written work as

assigned Hold minimum of 1 virtual office hour each week Collaborate with faculty and program leadership to ensure course objectives are met. Provide timely and constructive feedback to support student development. JOB QUALIFICATIONS Necessary Education or Work Experience: Master of Science in Nursing (MSN) or current enrollment in an MSN program. Unencumbered Wisconsin RN license (or eligibility for licensure). At

least two years of full-time (or equivalent) direct patient care experience. Willingness to obtain additional RN licensure in approved states where the program operates. Edgewood University will assist in the licensure process. Preferred Knowledge and Skills: Previous teaching experience in an academic or professional setting (online or in-person). Experience in online education and synchronous teaching

methodologies. Strong multicultural competence and commitment to fostering an inclusive learning environment. Other Qualifications: Edgewood University, a Catholic college sponsored by the Dominican Sisters of Sinsinawa, is an Equal Opportunity Employer, committed to academic excellence through diversity in its faculty, staff, and students.
