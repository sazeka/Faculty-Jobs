Demonstrated leadership and administrative experience in higher education. Job Description: The Department of Economics at the University of Florida

invites applications for the position of Department Chair. This is a full-time, nine-month, tenure-accruing faculty appointment. The successful candidate will be appointed at the rank of Associate Professor or Professor and will be eligible for tenure upon appointment, subject to the University's approval process. The Chair appointment is expected to be a three- to five-year term, with the possibility of

reappointment. In addition to providing leadership and oversight of the Department's academic, administrative, and financial operations, the successful candidate will demonstrate a vision for advancing research excellence, strengthening the national and international reputation of the Department's doctoral program, and effectively advocating for strategic faculty growth. The Department welcomes applications from

candidates in all fields of economics. The Department of Economics comprises 22 faculty members, including 5 instructional faculty, 10 Assistant Professors, and 7 Full Professors. The Department has experienced substantial growth in recent years across its research, graduate, and undergraduate missions and is poised for continued advancement. The Department's established strengths in Industrial Organization,

International Trade, Macroeconomics, and Public Economics provide a strong foundation for future growth. In addition, a talented group of junior faculty is rapidly expanding the Department's research profile through innovative scholarship and publications in leading economics journals. The Department offers doctoral, master’s, and undergraduate programs: Ph.D.: A small program admits 10 students every two years.
