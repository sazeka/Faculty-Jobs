Summary Missouri Baptist University is seeking to hire a full-time Instructor or Assistant Professor in Marketing, Management, or Business Administration to serve in the School of Management and Marketing. Faculty are expected to prepare and deliver instruction grounded in a Biblical worldview that advances student learning outcomes, cultivates intellectual
