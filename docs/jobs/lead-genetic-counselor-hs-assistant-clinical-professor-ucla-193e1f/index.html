Position description The Department of OB/GYN is seeking a lead Genetic Counselor with experience in reproductive genetics and clinical research for a Health Sciences Assistant Clinical Professor faculty

appointment. The role combines clinical genetic counseling expertise with responsibilities supporting the research mission of the department as well as participating in the education of genetic counselors through UCLA Master’s program and OB/GYN trainees. Applicants must possess an M.S. degree in Genetic Counseling to apply. Candidates must also be ABGC board certified and eligible to obtain a California Genetic

Counselor License. We seek candidates who are enthusiastic about engaging in clinical care, academically productive scientific inquiry, educating genetic counseling students and serving the university community. Academic appointment level will be commensurate with qualifications and experience. The selected candidate will join an established team with expertise in OB/GYN, maternal fetal medicine, reproductive

genetics and reproductive endocrinology and infertility, clinical genetics, genomic medicine, and precision health. The role offers opportunities for clinical practice, academic engagement, research collaboration, and teaching within a dynamic academic environment. In addition to the Department of OB/GYN, the Department of Human Genetics hosts an ABMGG-accredited Medical Genetics residency, a T32 training grant, a

Masters in Genetic Counseling program, and multiple ABMGG-accredited Laboratory Genetics training programs. Opportunities are available for clinical research, clinical trials, and collaboration with basic science researchers. Faculty members have numerous opportunities for teaching across a variety of academic programs. Teaching and supervision of medical students, residents, fellows, and genetic counseling trainees
