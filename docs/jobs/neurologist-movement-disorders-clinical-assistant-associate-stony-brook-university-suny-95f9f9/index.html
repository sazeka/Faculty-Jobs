Position summary

The Department of Neurology in the Renaissance School of Medicine is seeking movement disorders neurologists to join our outstanding Parkinson's and Movement Disorders Center.&nbsp;&nbsp; Stony Brook is home to the only DBS program, Tics and Tourette's Disorder TAA Center of Excellence and Huntington's Disease HDSA Center of Excellence in Suffolk County, Long Island, NY.&nbsp; Suffolk County has a catchment area of

1.5 million residents, of which nearly a quarter of a million are geriatric.&nbsp; We partner with Stony Brook Southampton Hospital, which offers numerous wellness programs with support of APDA.&nbsp; Center for Parkinson's Disease | Stony Brook Southampton Hospital Stony Brook University Hospital is a 624-bed tertiary care university hospital.&nbsp; Its academic affiliate, Stony Brook University, is ranked #1 among

New York state public universities.&nbsp; It has a state-of-the-art PET-MRI and other neuroimaging with a cyclotron.&nbsp; We are members of the Parkinson Study and Huntington Study Groups, with active clinical trials and investigator-initiated research. Responsibilities include: Management of patients with Movement Disorders, including inpatient Movement Disorders consultations. Commitment to community engagements

and education. Coverage of the general neurology or&nbsp;consult services. Favorable weekend and overnight call responsibility shared equally among&nbsp;faculty. Supervision of neurology residents and medical students. This represents an exciting opportunity for a Movement Disorders neurologist interested in building an outpatient movement disorders practice at the region's premier academic medical center.&nbsp;

Candidates will have the opportunity to develop innovative clinical, educational, and research programs to suit their unique academic interests. Faculty can also take advantage of an exceptional retirement plan offered through the New York State Pension plan and 457(b), 401(a) and 403(b).
