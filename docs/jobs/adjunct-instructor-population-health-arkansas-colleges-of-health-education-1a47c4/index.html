The primary focus is on

delivering coursework through Learning Management Systems (LMS) with an emphasis on nutritional, rural, and indigenous health offerings. Adjunct Faculty engage(s) in teaching and assessment activities that support the mission of a student-centered, equity-minded institution. They will assist with educational activities across ACHE’s School of Population Health MPH and DrPH Programs as appropriate.ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS
