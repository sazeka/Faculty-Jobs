(Rolling Pool) English Language Program Adjunct Instructor - Global Education Office 45002735 907 Floyd Avenue, VCU Main Campus, Virginia, United States, 23284 Faculty Adjunct Global Education Office MBU The English Language Program (ELP) in the Global Education Office at Virginia Commonwealth University seeks qualified part-time adjunct faculty to teach in its intensive English language program. The ELP provides

English language support to students who are academically admissible to VCU undergraduate and graduate programs but who need additional English language proficiency to succeed in an academic environment. The program also serves individuals seeking to improve their English language skills for educational, professional, and personal purposes, including prospective students who may later apply to VCU academic programs.

Reporting to the Director of the English Language Program, adjunct faculty play an important role in supporting student language development and academic success while contributing to a welcoming and inclusive learning environment. Applicants will be considered on a rolling basis and based on program teaching needs. Responsibilities Teach one or more English language courses. Evaluate student performance and submit

grades in accordance with program requirements. Hold scheduled office hours and meet with students as needed. Participate in program and team meetings. Foster a supportive and engaging classroom environment that promotes student learning and success. Required Qualifications Master's degree in TESOL or a related field, or a master's degree plus a TESOL certificate. Experience teaching English as a Second Language

(ESL) at an accredited college or university. Demonstrated English language proficiency. Evidence of cross-cultural competence and sensitivity. Strong interpersonal and organizational skills. Demonstrated experience working in and fostering an environment of respect, professionalism and civility with a population of faculty, staff, and students from various backgrounds and experiences, or a commitment to do so as a

member of the VCU community. Preferred Qualifications Two or more years of experience teaching in an intensive university ESL program in the United States. Experience teaching a variety of language skills and proficiency levels. Experience with language assessment, placement testing, or curriculum development. Technology proficiency relevant to language instruction. Position Details:Salary Range: $862 per ELP
